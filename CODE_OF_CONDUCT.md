# Code of conduct

Dot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in its issues, pull requests, discussions and every other place of the project.

In short:

- Be kind and patient, and assume that others mean well.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, no sexual language or imagery, and no one's private information made
  public without their consent.

## Reporting

Report what breaks this code of conduct with **Report content** in the menu of the comment, issue or pull request.
The maintainer may edit or remove what was posted, and warn, block or ban whoever posted it.
