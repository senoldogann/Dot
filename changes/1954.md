  reviewed, only the files of that diff, and checks that the earlier findings were fixed. A change that touches a path
  that always needs a human goes straight to the second review model (Claude's refused such diffs), and so does every
  review while your Claude plan's five-hour window is 80 % used or spent. The other model is told to make at most 40 tool calls
  and to give its verdict in time. `dotx review plan` shows the reason (`route`). Applies once your own
  checkout has pulled the hook.
