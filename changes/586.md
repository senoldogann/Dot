- Reactions on Dot's messages: under each answer, and under what Dot said in the middle of a run, a button opens a
  palette of seven (👍 good, ❤️ love it, 👎 not right, 🔁 do it again, 📌 remember this, ❓ explain, ✋ hold on); the ones
  that stand are chips beside it, a second press takes one back, and they are there after a reload and on your other
  screens. While a run works, a reaction on one of its messages reaches it at its next step as a note from Dot: a 👎
  makes it reconsider and change course, a 🔁 do the last part again another way, a ❓ explain, a 👍 or ❤️ keep going,
  and a ✋ holds it before its next step until you write to it (or take the ✋ back; a run held for an hour with nobody
  writing stops). A reaction never vouches for what the run read: it clears none of its marks of untrusted content, is
  no instruction to the review model and approves no step. After the run ended, a 👎, a 🔁 and a ❓ start a follow-up run
  in the same chat, as your own message would ("My reaction 👎 to your answer above: …"; queued behind a run at work,
  and as untrusted as the run it is about), at most one for a message and a reaction and 20 an hour; a 📌 keeps the
  message as a note, marked as read from outside content when the run read some, and never one that holds a key or a
  secret; a 👍 and a ❤️ are kept as feedback. Settings › General › Reactions chooses, for each reaction, whether it
  starts a follow-up run or is only feedback, and has a switch that turns the follow-ups off (`[reactions]`).
  `POST /api/runs/{id}/messages/{message}/reactions` and its siblings, and the events `reaction_added` and
  `reaction_removed` (item 586).
