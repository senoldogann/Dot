- Dot can trade crypto on Coinbase (off by default, docs/trading.md): a paper account of its own at Coinbase's real
  prices, or your Coinbase account with a key that may view and trade and never transfer. Your limits (one order, a
  day's orders, a day's loss, one product held, the products allowed) are held in code in every mode, yolo's too; a live
  order waits for your yes in every mode but yolo, and the emergency stop stops every order (item 763).
