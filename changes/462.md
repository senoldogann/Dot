- Schedules: a schedule can run as one of your agents (its **Agent**). Its runs carry the agent's instructions and its
  tools only, wear its face, and take the agent's model, reasoning level and approvals where the schedule leaves them
  on Default; a yolo schedule stays yolo. Deleting an agent switches its schedules off, and a cut-off run of an agent
  resumes as that agent (item 462).
