- Voice: where the browser has speech recognition (Safari, Chrome), the composer has a microphone that fills the box as
  you speak, the words the engine may still change drawn quieter, and stops on a second press, after a silence, when you
  type or send; nothing is sent by voice alone. Under each answer a speaker reads it aloud with the browser's own voice
  (one kept on this Mac when there is one for the language; a network voice, such as Chrome's "Google …" voices, sends
  the text it reads) and stops it. Settings › General › Voice chooses the language (the page's own, English, Türkçe and
  more) and has **Read answers aloud**, off by default, which reads an answer that comes in while you watch. Safari and
  Chrome may send your voice to Apple's or Google's servers to recognize it; Dot sends nothing itself (item 574).
