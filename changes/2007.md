- A trial, off by default (item 2007): `[desktop] background_driver = "cua"` sends the clicks, typing, keys and
  scrolls of `desktop_act` to the application's own window in the background through cua-driver 0.28.2, which Dot
  starts as its own child, so they go while you are at the Mac, your pointer, keyboard and front window left alone.
  Each step is judged and recorded as before; hover, drag, a click with a key held and `activate` still wait for you
  to step away. Dot never installs the driver: `dotx doctor` says whether it is there and the command that installs it.
