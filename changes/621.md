- Dot keeps logins, cards and contact details of its own, in **Settings › Vault**: tabs with their counts (All,
  Logins, Cards, Contacts), a search, and a **New item** menu (Login, Card, Contact, Import from CSV…), each in a
  dialog whose Save stays off until what you wrote is good (a card's number is checked as you type: its check digit,
  its brand, the expiry as month and year). A card can be read from a photo on this Mac, which sends the picture
  nowhere. Passwords, card numbers, expiry dates, security codes and the notes of a login are kept only in your Mac's
  Keychain; Dot's database holds a login's site and username, a card's brand, last four digits and nickname, and your
  contact details. A task is told what the vault holds by label and never sees a password, a card number, an expiry or
  a security code: `browser_fill_login` types a login of the vault (beside 1Password's) into a page of its own site,
  asking you first in every mode but `yolo` (also when 1Password is connected and cannot be read: the logins of the
  vault are listed and filled all the same), and `vault_contacts` lists your contact details for a task to type where
  a form asks. A card is kept and shown to you by the eye, and Dot fills it into no page for now: that is for another
  item, which needs more than this one holds. The field a login was typed into shows as `[hidden]` in every view of
  the page in every mode, also in a page the tab goes back to, and a screenshot covers it; Chrome keeps none of it in
  its session or its autofill files (the body of a form a page sends by POST is deleted when Dot's Chrome closes). A
  form with no method, which a browser sends by GET and so puts what is typed in the address, now gets no password
  from 1Password or the vault. A request that a page sends is refused when its address holds the password, and only a
  request of the site the password was typed into, in the tab it was typed into, where no script of a task runs; the
  addresses a task opens itself, and the requests of a script a task runs in any other tab, are judged by the rules
  on addresses alone, so that a refusal never tells a task whether its guess is the password. Nothing is refused or
  decided differently for a password a vault filled (a step of a task that read outside content, a note a task
  saves and how long it may be, a habit, an agent's file, what one of your rules asks about a step), so that a task
  cannot learn it by trying words; it is masked where it is saved or shown. A shell command that names
  the vault's Keychain service asks first. A saved secret the eye shows is hidden again after twenty seconds and is no
  longer in the page's text, and for thirty seconds a screenshot of the Mac that a task takes leaves Dot's own windows
  out, and a look at them (`desktop_snapshot`, `desktop_read`) is refused, also at a window that is minimized or on
  another Space. A password is typed only into the field a ref names, also when the ref names an element inside the
  field's label, and never into a form that sends by GET. An import of the logins in a CSV export of Chrome, Edge,
  Safari, Firefox, 1Password or Bitwarden is read in memory and says how many went in, how many were left out and why,
  and to delete the file. The name on a card has an eye as its security code has, to read it, change it or remove it.
  An import whose notes are too long for the Keychain keeps the login without them and says so, and no longer stops
  half way (item 621).
