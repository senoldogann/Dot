- Dot drafts an email in your own Gmail or Outlook (`mail_draft`), a reply in the thread of a message it found or a
  new mail: the chat shows it as a draft card with the account it goes from and **Open in Gmail** / **Open in
  Outlook**, on the phone and the web. **Send email** on the card sends it through that account, **Discard** deletes
  it from the mailbox; nothing goes out before your press, in any mode, and a draft you changed in the mailbox is not
  written over. A sign-in still reads only: press **Allow drafts and sending** beside an account in Settings ›
  Connectors › Accounts (on the Mac) for the accounts Dot should draft in (Google: `gmail.compose`; Microsoft:
  `Mail.ReadWrite`, `Mail.Send`) (item 2047).
