- Videos you send from your iPhone are made smaller for their length instead of being cut at 20 MiB: a minute is about
  14 MB, so a video of up to about 15 minutes (200 MiB) goes whole, and the Mac writes it to disk as it arrives. This
  replaces the 20 MiB limit and the offer to trim past it that came with attaching videos from DotNext's + menu; the
  trim offer is now for a video over 200 MiB only. The web composer sends videos, sounds and large files the same way.
