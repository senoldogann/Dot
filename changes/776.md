- Dot can look at a site the way a security engineer starts (item 776): `recon` reads its addresses, its TLS
  certificate, its HTTP headers and the technology it names, and says what its owner should fix — a missing
  Content-Security-Policy or HSTS, a header that gives away a version, an open CORS policy, a certificate that is about
  to expire or does not cover the name. With `depth = "ports"` it also asks which of the common ports answer and reads
  their banners, on a host you listed in `[recon] owned_hosts`: your own, or one you may test. It sends no payload and
  tries no exploit ([Recon](docs/recon.md)).
