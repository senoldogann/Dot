- Campaigns: with `[policy] unanswered = "park"`, a session keeps for you, as any run does, a step handed to you or a
  question for your yes that nobody answered; the step is the campaign's, kept once for all its sessions. Your answer
  reaches the session while it works, and otherwise brings the next session forward, whose brief says what you
  answered (a step allowed once may then be done once); a session that kept a step still unanswered is followed after
  `campaign_wait_hours`, whatever it said it needs (item 484).
