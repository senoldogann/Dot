- Dot drives your own Safari the way it drives its Chrome: `safari_open`, `safari_read` and `safari_act` read a page as
  elements with refs and its text and act on them in batches inside the page, with no pointer, keeping your signed-in
  session and Safari's AutoFill. Its steps are judged exactly as Chrome's are (a payment, a message, a post, a
  password field). It needs Safari's "Allow JavaScript from Apple Events" on once (Settings › Developer); Dot says
  where and never turns it on itself (item 1959).
