- 1Password: a run signs in with your logins without its model ever seeing them. Connect it on Customize › Plugins ›
  1Password: a vault "Shared with Dot" and a service account that only reads it, made with your `op` through the
  1Password app, or a service account token of yours. A run lists the logins' titles and websites and names one with
  the refs of its fields, and Dot types the values itself. A login is filled only on its own site and a password only
  into a password field. Each fill asks you first in every mode but yolo, and in auto you answer it, not the review
  model. The browser tools mask the values, and no script runs in a page they were filled into (item 493).
