- A helper working in the background when Dot stops, restarts or crashes is no longer lost: the next start resumes it
  in its own chat from where it was cut, and its chat is told its report when it really ends, saying a restart cut it
  and Dot resumed it. One that cannot be resumed (resuming is off, it was cut too long ago, it was itself resumed, or a
  call that pays, sends or posts was cut) is told as cut off by the restart, not as failed, with why (item 1668).
