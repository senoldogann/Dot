- A morning brief (item 570): a card at the head of Schedules with a switch, a time (08:00), every day or the weekdays,
  and a box "Also tell me about…" for your own lines. On, Dot writes the brief at that time, and your phone gets the
  push of its first line, which opens it in its chat, "Morning brief", where each day is a new run. It is a schedule of
  your scheduler, labelled **Brief** in the list and switched off there too, and its run only reads (`research`): what
  waits for you, the runs that failed since the last brief, the campaigns at work, the schedules of the day, and, with
  a Google or Microsoft account signed in, today's calendar and the mail of the last day that needs you. What Dot tells
  you on its own goes through one function, `tell_owner` (`notify/owner.py`), an event the phone's push and the
  desktop's notifications both take. See [Dot comes to you](docs/proactive.md).
