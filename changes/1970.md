- Hooks: a service starts Dot's work by calling `POST /api/hooks/<id>`, signed with the hook's own secret (HMAC-SHA256
  of the timestamp and the body, 5 minutes either way, each call once). A hook starts a scheduled task or an agent with
  their own approvals and no more, tainted, the named fields of the call's JSON quoted and fenced as data. Settings ›
  Hooks and `dotx hooks list|add|rotate|remove` make, rotate, switch and delete them (the secret is shown once) and list
  the last calls; a hook answers on this Mac and the reach names only, unless `[server] hook_hosts` opens it to a tunnel
  (item 1970, [Hooks](docs/hooks.md)).
