- The composer's microphone on an iPhone or an iPad hears every time. Safari's own speech recognition there goes deaf
  after the first dictation of a page (WebKit bugs 317741 and 321436), so the page now records the microphone itself and
  sends the recording to your Mac, which writes it down on the device with macOS 26's own dictation: your voice goes to
  no one else, and the Mac keeps neither the recording nor the words. The words go into the box as before, and the
  recorder says "Writing down what you said…" meanwhile. It needs macOS 26 and Apple's developer tools on the Mac
  (`xcode-select --install`), with which the daemon builds the small program that writes down the first time it is used;
  the first time a language is used the Mac downloads its model from Apple. Where the Mac cannot, the iPhone keeps
  Safari's recognition and its toast says what the Mac lacks. A browser with no speech recognition (Firefox) gets the
  microphone this way too; a Mac's Safari or Chrome and Dot.app keep the browser's own recognition (item 626).
