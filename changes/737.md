- `[providers.openrouter] routing` sets, by model id, how OpenRouter chooses the provider that serves a model (`sort`,
  `order`, `only`, `ignore`, `allow_fallbacks`, `quantizations`, `max_price`); a variant (`:nitro`, `:floor`) takes its
  model's, and a 404 for a routing that leaves no provider says to loosen it (item 737).
