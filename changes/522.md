  is a payment, handed to you or held to the site's pre-approved caps. Before, "Pay" counted only as a whole label and

  such as "Order #4521" or "Order summary". A dialog that names an order and its amount asks you to pay, whatever its
  number. "Checkout" and "Continue to payment" stay plain clicks: the button that pays is the one handed to you
