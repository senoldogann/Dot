- Watchers (item 571): a **Watchers** panel under the morning brief on Schedules. A watcher looks every few minutes at new
  mail in a Google or Microsoft account you signed in, at the events about to start in its calendar, or at your GitHub
  notifications (a review asked of you, a mention, an assignment, a failing check), and tells you on your phone, and in
  a chat "Watchers" that keeps the lines, when something you care about comes. For mail you say what you want to hear of
  in your own words and the fast model reads each new mail's sender, subject and first lines against them; a mail it
  cannot judge is told, not dropped. A watcher can also start a run with your goal ("draft a reply in my voice and keep
  it as a draft"), which starts marked untrusted, in your default approval mode. The first look tells nothing, a
  restart neither repeats nor misses, at most 20 tells and 5 runs an hour come from one watcher (`[watchers]`), and a
  watcher that fails says why on its row. Nothing reaches the daemon from outside: it looks, there is no webhook. See
  [Dot comes to you](docs/proactive.md#watchers).
