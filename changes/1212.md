- Dot watches videos: give it a video file of the Mac or a link (YouTube, X, Instagram, Vimeo…) and `watch_video` gives
  the model frames of what it shows and a timestamped transcript of what is said, with any model. A link's own
  captions are used when it has some; otherwise the Mac's own dictation model writes the sound down, free and on the
  Mac. Needs `brew install ffmpeg yt-dlp` ([Watching videos](docs/video.md), item 1212).
