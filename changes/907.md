- Dot hands work to its agents as **work orders** (item 907): the order is recorded, the agent starts at once and nobody
  waits; when it ends, Dot checks the answer against what done means and accepts it, sends it back with notes, gives it
  to another agent, or asks you after three tries. Orders that stall, fail or run past their time are sent back to Dot
  for review, and the Agents page lists them under **Work orders**.
