- Your message ends a `wait_until` (item 1685): a run that waits for a condition (a check, an address, a file) stops
  waiting at its next check when you write to it, in Dot's own loop and on the Claude plan, and reads your message at
  once, where it used to wait up to an hour. A reaction, a helper's report or a work order does not end the wait
  ([Runs](docs/runs.md)).
