- The desktop app (item 2095) opens at login, in the menu bar with its window hidden (Settings › General › Desktop app
  › Open Dot.app at login, on by default, `[desktop_app] start_at_login`); its window opens where it was left; its menu,
  tip, down page and update dialogs speak the web UI's language (Turkish when the UI is Turkish); it looks for an update
  once a day while it runs, and the menu bar item shows how far Install and Restart has downloaded.
