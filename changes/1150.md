- In a Google Meet call, Dot answers in the call's chat whoever speaks to it ("Dot, …", "@Dot"), as Claude Meetings
  does, without asking you: from the call's own words only, never your files, mail or memory, one short line with no
  link, a few a minute at most. It tells the call it is there as soon as it is let in, and that its answers are not your
  word. A join a task asks for after reading a mail or a page asks you first. It reads the call four times as often, and
  the notes come every 3 minutes. `[meetings] answers = false` turns the answers off (item 1150).
