- Dot repairs its own code (items 919-921). It keeps a book of what goes wrong again and again in its own record, and
  each night hands the first problem to Claude Code, signed in on your Mac with your own plan, never an API key. The
  fix comes with a test and a pull request that Dot merges once every check passes, or leaves for you. A change to what
  needs your look always waits for you. Settings › Rules and memory › Self-repair shows the problems and the sessions.
  It is on once `[repair] repo` names Dot's checkout.
