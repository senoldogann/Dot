- Store: the daemon copies the database every hour while it runs, when it changed, without stopping the runs. The
  copies are checked, compressed and kept in `[store] backup_dir` (`<home>/backups` unless you name an external disk
  or iCloud Drive): the newest 24, and one of each of the last 14 days. `dotx backup now` makes one at once, `dotx
  backup list` lists them, and `dotx doctor` says how old the newest is (item 526).
