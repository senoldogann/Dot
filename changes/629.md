- A rule made from your words fits a word that starts or ends with a character such as `²` or `½`: a rule that should
  stop Dot at `x²` stopped it at nothing. A word that starts or ends with a circled letter or a vowel sign is a whole
  word now, as any other: it no longer fits inside a longer word, which also means that a rule that stops Dot at a
  word of a script written without spaces (Thai, Hindi) fits it only where it stands apart (item 629).
