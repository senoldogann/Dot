- Caps on what the tasks spend, set in Settings › Models › Caps (`[budget]`, `GET`/`PUT /api/budget`): tokens for one task
  with its helpers (500M), for all tasks of a day (4,000M) and for one source's tasks of a day (schedules and Dot's own work
  500M, code reviews 1,000M). A task past a cap waits and asks you, Continue (as many tokens again) or Stop, in every mode,
  Pay or send and free mode included, at your word; no answer stops it. The defaults are above everything measured on a busy
  Mac. A task's Cost card says from its first step what it and its helpers used against its cap and what tasks like it usually
  use, and the Usage page lists the week's costliest tasks. Tokens, not money: Dot keeps no price for a model (item 1731).
