- Settings › Models has an **Own work model**: the model that decides what Dot does on its own while the Mac is idle
  (until now only in `config.toml`), and the On its own card says which one it is (item 916).
- A try of Dot's own work that spends more than 5 million tokens and fails no longer lets a big thing go at once: the
  next try goes on from what it found, and only a second costly try in a row lets it go. Work that gets done is never
  cut for what it spends (item 916).
