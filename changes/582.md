- What Dot tells you on its own reaches iMessage too (item 582): with iMessage on and a number paired, the first line of
  the morning brief and each tell of a watcher come into your conversation with yourself as one message, marked `◆`,
  with the title, the text and the link to the page it opens, as they come to your phone's push. There is no new switch:
  the iMessage switch of Settings › Channels covers it. The message is masked of the secrets Dot holds, is never read
  back as a command, and a channel gets at most one in twenty seconds: a watcher that tells several things in a row is
  heard as its first one, and then one message, "and 3 more", that links to the newest. See
  [Channels](docs/channels.md) and [Dot comes to you](docs/proactive.md#telling-you).
