- Evals from your real runs: `dotx eval suite` builds a suite of your own goals (those that went wrong first), a few
  golden tasks and cases you write in `evals/real/owner.json`; `dotx eval real` plays it on a model and grades each
  attempt on the order of its tools and its ending; `dotx eval ablate` plays real goals with and without a skill, a
  part of it or a memory note and has a model pick the better answer blind, beside a noise band; `dotx eval costs`
  says what each tool and skill call cost in tokens per day (item 2091).
