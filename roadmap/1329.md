### 1329. The stage bar as the reference app's breadcrumb: the agent's badge, a slash and the chat's title, 52 px, a hairline only when the page scrolls, the same on a phone

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The owner's word of 2026-11-22: Dot's design is "completely like a toy"; make it plain and
professional like the reference app on this Mac, a phone and a Mac in one design language. Items 1327 and 1328 made the
sidebar; this is the bar above the page.

The bar was a pane of its own on a Mac (the face, the name, the panel's switch) and, on a phone, three panes of glass over
the conversation (item 1119, after Grok Bot's): a round way back, the name in a pill in the middle, a pill of tools, the
conversation passing under them. It is now one bar, the same on both:

- **52 px**, on the page's own ground, **sticky**, with no line under it until the page has scrolled under it: the window's
  own scroll on a Mac, the conversation's on a phone (`trackScrolled` in `layout.js`, `data-scrolled` on the bar, the
  hairline fades in). On a phone the bar no longer floats over the chat: the conversation begins under it.
- **At the left** the sidebar's button, which on a phone is the Back or the menu as before (items 338, 1079 and 1321: the edge
  swipe and the rules of Back are untouched), then the breadcrumb: the **badge** of the agent (two letters on a small square,
  no face), its name (a link to its page, item 1234), a slash and the **chat's title**, one line, the title cut with an
  ellipsis first. A delegated run keeps its parent's goal in the crumb and no second title. The model and the state that
  stood under the title on a phone are in the panel's Details.
- **At the right** the toggles of the panels, plain icon buttons: the panel's switch, and on a phone Dot's computer and
  the helpers; the daemon's pills after them.
- The face a page puts before an agent's name (a chat's, an agent's, a team's page) stays in the badge, out of sight, and its
  state is the badge's tint: the warning's ink while the run waits, the bad's once it failed, a step stronger while it works;
  no mascot stands in the bar (`stage.js`, `badge.js`: the same badge as the sidebar's rows).
- The curtain that stands while Dot opens (item 328) is drawn the same, with a place for the switch, so the breadcrumb
  does not move when the page lifts it; its end alone gives way to the tools.
