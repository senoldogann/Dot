### 1745. A plan step marked done is judged by the run's record

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Research M4 topic 1 ("verifier-owned completion"): the harness, not the model, says
whether a step is shown done.

**Measured before**, read-only in the owner's store, 14 days: 137 of 137 `mark_plan_step` done calls carried evidence
text, and all 263 done steps of the runs' last plans did (item 117 refuses a done mark without it). The evidence is the
model's own words: nothing tied it to a call the run made.

**What must hold.** No model call; a read of the run's own record (plans and tool results without their text, two
indexed queries); no new refusal and no new turn, so a run that finishes as before finishes as before; the result says
what was found, and the plan the model and the owner read says it.

**Built** (`agent/account.py`, `agent/plan.py`, `tools/meta.py`, `store/db.py`):

- `PlanItem.proof` (`shown`, `unverified`, `none`; empty when not judged) and `shown_by` (the ids of the newest three
  calls that show it), stored in the `plan` event (two defaulted fields of the event's data, no schema change).
- A step marked done, by `mark_plan_step` or written done by `update_plan`, is judged by the tool results of the time
  it was open (`opened_after`: the first plan of its newest open stretch; the run's start for a step written done at
  once). `step_proof`: none when no call that worked and is no bookkeeping came then; unverified when the newest
  browser or desktop act left a step not seen to hold and no read, look or command came after it; shown otherwise. A
  rewritten plan keeps how a done step stood; a step marked again is judged again; a helper's report is not judged.
- A step not shown reads `[done, not verified]` in the plan the model reads and "not verified" beside the step in the
  web UI's plan (`planbubble.js`, the Plan tab, the bubble and the phone's sheet), and the mark's result says why and
  to mark it again once a look shows it (a mark judges the step again). Finish is not refused for it: the run's
  account (item 1746) lists it.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_plan_proof.py`: a scripted model through the real loop, tools and store (a step a
listing showed is shown by it, a second step open as long is too, a step written after the last call is shown by
nothing and the result says so); `step_proof` and `opened_after` on their own; `tests/js/planbubble.test.js`. `dotx eval check`,
`tests/unit/test_freedom_contract.py`.
