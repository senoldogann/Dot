### 968. The tick of a dictation puts its words in the box at once

**Status: done (2026-11-03).** **Found** by the owner: the composer's voice message was slow to end, on the Mac, in a
browser and on the iPhone. With the browser's own recognition (the Mac's way), the tick asked the engine to stop and
the words went into the box only once it had settled them and ended: Safari's and Dot.app's engine often answers late
or not at all, and the page waited for it up to `SETTLE_MS`, three seconds, with the recorder on and its buttons off
(item 605). The iPhone's way, a recording the Mac writes down, was measured apart: macOS's dictation model on this Mac
writes 4.8 s of Turkish in 0.4 s to 0.9 s and 12 s in 0.7 s to 1.4 s (`transcribe` run by hand on a `say` recording);
the 3.04 s the daemon logged for 8 s today was taken while the Mac ran the browser tests. Not changed here.

**Built.** `microphone.js`: the tick puts the words heard into the box and closes the recorder at once (`closed`); the
engine settles what it heard meanwhile, and its settled words take their place when it ends, only while the box holds
what the tick put (`putAtTick`), never over what the owner wrote since; three seconds after the tick it is let go, as
before. A press of the microphone while one still settles drops that one and starts anew; typing drops it, and so does
a message sent.

**Not built.** The start: nothing of Dot's own stands between the press and the engine's start (a new recognition,
or for a recording the microphone and the audio worklet), and no measure of the owner's devices says where the wait
is; the owner is asked what they see.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_voice.py` (the tick's test rewritten: the words at once, the settled ones after, the
owner's own words kept), `npm run check`.
