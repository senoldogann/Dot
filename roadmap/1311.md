### 1311. A web push subscription is taken only at a browser maker's push service, and goes with the phone that registered it

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The review of the server found that `POST /api/push/subscriptions` took any `https://`
address. A phone's key or the installation's token could register `https://attacker.example/x` with keys of its own,
and the daemon then posted every push (up to 140 characters of a question, a draft's subject or a result) there, signed
with its VAPID key; it also made the Mac post to any https host. And a subscription was tied to no phone: removing a
phone deleted its APNs device but left the web push subscriptions its key had made.

A subscription is now taken only at a push service of `[notify] push_hosts`, whose packaged list is Apple's
(`web.push.apple.com`, `*.push.apple.com`), Google's (`fcm.googleapis.com` and `android.googleapis.com`, by name: any
`*.googleapis.com` entry, with or without a path, would take `<bucket>.storage.googleapis.com`, whose bucket the caller
names), Firefox's (`updates.push.services.mozilla.com`, `*.push.services.mozilla.com`) and
Windows' (`*.notify.windows.com`). A new service is one more line (the list replaces the packaged one). An address with a
sign-in part, a backslash, a space, a dot segment or a port that is not a number is refused as well, as what Dot checks
and what the HTTP client reaches could differ. The refusal is a 422 that names the host and the setting. A subscription
already stored at a host that is not on the list is not posted to (a warning in the log, `push_subscription_host_refused`)
and kept, so that the list stays the owner's to change; none is deleted by this.

`push_subscriptions` has a nullable `phone_id`, added at open with the other added columns (no new schema version: a
nullable column is the additive layer, a database from before it opens with every subscription's `phone_id` NULL and is
read by an older Dot as before). The route fills it from the phone state of a request that a phone's key let in, and the
installation's token leaves it NULL (those subscriptions keep working as before); the same endpoint registered again by
the token leaves the phone it belongs to. `DELETE /api/phone/phones/{id}` deletes the phone's subscriptions beside its
APNs device, and a phone removed while its registration is on the way takes the new row with it.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_phone_limits.py` (a foreign host is refused for a phone's key and the token, with its message;
Apple, FCM and Mozilla are taken; removing a phone removes its subscriptions and leaves the Mac's and another phone's),
`tests/unit/test_push_hosts.py` (what each default covers, and the spellings that must not pass), `tests/unit/test_push.py`
(a stored subscription at a foreign host is not posted to and the others are; `phone_id` kept and deleted by phone),
`tests/unit/test_store_schema.py` (a subscription from before the column stays, its digest pinned), and
`tests/smoke/test_phone.py` (the end-to-end push of a phone, now with its local push service on the list).
