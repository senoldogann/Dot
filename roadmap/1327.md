### 1327. The sidebar's chat rows as the reference app's: three quiet lines, no faces, Working and Settled in a dock at the foot

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The owner's word of 2026-11-22: Dot's design is "completely like a toy"; make it plain and
professional like the reference app on this Mac (open source, named in the owner's notes), on a phone and on a Mac in one design
language, every line and area one by one. This is the sidebar's list; its head, its agents and its foot are item 1328,
the stage bar item 1329 and the composer item 1330 (the tokens, the run page and the settings are other agents' work).

A chat's row had a creature's face, a loop mark that turned in a glow, a campaign mark on a gradient and, for what a chat
delegated, up to three small faces over one another. It now has three lines:

- the agent's badge, a 20 px square of the ink's tint with its two letters, in the smallest metadata size ("Code Reviewer" is CR, "Dot" is DO), its name
  in the muted ink and, at the right, the run's age, or Working and for how long (item 122), or Waiting and for how long
  (item 836, a pill with the warning's dot);
- the chat's title in the heavier weight, one line, cut with an ellipsis (it faded out before);
- a last line of the marks of its loop and its campaign (a small line icon each, no glow, no gradient), the team it is
  with, what it delegated as one count (the users mark and how many), its last words, and the dot of an unread chat.

The labels of the groups are one: Pinned, Today, Working, Settled and the sections of the channels (Dot's work, Agents'
work, Code reviews, iMessage) all in one small muted ink, and a group that folds has its count and a chevron at the right.

The list is arranged as the reference app's: the pinned chats first, then the days (Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days), and under
them a dock that keeps its place while the list scrolls: Working holds what waits for the owner and what works, with the
count of both and a fold that hides what works but never what waits for the owner (the step that needs an answer is not
folded out of sight), and Settled holds the chats that rest past a week, folded, with their count (they were the days'
Older). A search shows what it finds in a folded group. Their folds are kept under keys of their own (`dock`,
`settled`), so that a fold of `working` or `waiting` kept from before item 836 is never read as theirs.

A row whose agent's name arrives after the list (the agents are read apart) takes it in place, so that a menu a row opened
stays open.

The docs: `docs/daemon.md` (the rows and their marks, Working and Settled, the delegated count, the rows of the activity)
and `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` (a team's row) say what the rows are now; the pre-push review found the old faces still
there.

Not covered here: the agents' faces at the top of the sidebar and a phone's agent rows, which are item 1328's.
