### 809. Aggressive autonomy, corrected: keep the speed, remove the false success

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** The other agent's `feature/aggressive-autonomy` had the right direction and four unsafe
or unproven shortcuts. This keeps the aggressive parts and makes each one honest.

- **Speculative read-only work.** A read-only tool (`web_search`, `web_fetch`, `read_file`, `list_dir`,
  `search_files`, `find_files`, `browser_snapshot`, `browser_read_text`, `desktop_snapshot`, `desktop_read`) may skip
  the owner's **hold** (`wait_free`) in `auto`/`yolo`, so a takeover does not stop a lookup or a read. It does **not**
  skip the approval (`authorize_recorded`), and an acting tool always waits. The old claim that approval was skipped
  was wrong; the test now checks both.
- **Read-only retry instead of fake failover.** `browser_act → browser_find` and `desktop_act → desktop_snapshot`
  were reads pretending to be actions: a failed click became a successful "find". Removed. A read-only call that times
  out or fails is tried once more with the same arguments; an acting tool runs once and its failure is the answer.
  `RunCancelled` and `FailSafeTriggered` are re-raised, never retried, and the broad `except Exception` is gone.
- **Owner-urgent initiative.** `[URGENT]` still lets a thing start while another run is active or the Mac is in use,
  but only when its **source is `owner`**. A watcher's title carries a mail's subject, so a third party must not be
  able to jump the queue. The list is read only after the busy/idle decision (`_urgent_todo` is read-only), so a busy
  tick cannot drop or finish anything; `_next` sorts owner-urgent first.
- **Bounded outcome maximization.** The goal checker now asks for more only when the goal itself asks for the best,
  the cheapest, or a named target; it never asks for polish or a comparison the goal did not ask for.
- **Measured.** `tests/unit/test_aggressive_autonomy.py` (5), `tests/smoke/test_initiative.py` (21, including four new
  urgent cases), `tests/unit/test_request_gate.py`, `tests/unit/test_tool_errors.py`,
  `tests/smoke/test_context_budget.py` (16 together), plus the full unit suite. `ruff` and `mypy` clean.
