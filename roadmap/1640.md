### 1640. Dot for iPhone: the audit of everything that makes the app look childish

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The owner, after using the build on his phone "Proma" (2026-11-24, 11:20): "the design on the phone
must not be childish; watch these blues, these silly designs everywhere. In the simplest case the Queued window opens: the Next-up
background is black and so on, the Done button is purple; there are such absurdities everywhere. The UI/UX must be very professional
and good." Items 1640 to 1644 answer that; the rules they hold the app to are the standard the lead wrote that day
(`briefs-2026-11-24/UI-STANDARD.md`: one neutral graphite family, no accent hue, state small and honest, no tiles, no gradients, no
shadows on cards, one sheet recipe, measured contrast in both looks). This item changes no code: it is the list of what the standard
forbids, found in the source and on every screen before anything was changed.

**How it was looked at.** The same canned daemon (workspace `tools/ios_pro_daemon.py`: a run at work with three messages queued
and a loop, five helpers at work, an approval, a question, a hand-off, a secret, an away card, schedules, models) and the same
launch arguments (`tools/ios_pro_shots.py`, no taps) took 25 screens of the Debug build of `origin/main` (0ef172cf8) in dark and
light, at the default text size and at AX3, on an iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.5), and the lists and the sheets again on an iPad. They are
in workspace `ios-pro-shots/before/` (`<screen>-<look>-<size>.png`, `ipad-<screen>-<look>-large.png`). The launch arguments that
open a sheet without a tap (`-DotShowQueue 1`, `-DotShowStep 1`, `-DotShowInspector 2`, `-DotShowNewChat 1|2`) are Debug-only aids
added for this (a separate commit; the build the shots were taken from has them and no other change).
`grep -rn "\.tint(\|AccentColor\|accentFill\|Theme.accent\|Theme.link\|LinearGradient\|AngularGradient\|\.shadow(\|Color(\"" ios/Dot
ios/DotLiveActivity ios/DotShare` found what follows.

**What the standard forbids, where the source has it.**
- One cool blue is the accent of everything. `DotPalette.accent` (#6AA4FF dark, #2559C9 light), `accentFill` and its gradient
  `accentFillTop` (#3070E4 to #2762D8), `link` (#B8D6FF), the `AccentColor` asset (the same two blues: it is the system tint of
  every control that sets none) and `Theme.accent`, `Theme.accentFill`, `Theme.accentGradient`, `Theme.link`, `Theme.onAccent`,
  `Theme.good` (a green) read all over: the chat list (the "Working" header, the helpers line, the unread dot, the strip's
  "Working", the swipe "Read"), a chat (links, the tool line that runs, the send button's gradient, the dock's working mark, a
  step's glyph tile), the Run sheet (status tint, plan progress, plan marks, helper pills, schedule tiles and switches), the
  inbox ("Open chat"), Settings (switches, ticks, the connection pill), the model pickers' ticks, the microphone, the away card's
  title, the hand-off card's tile, the pairing and address screens (step discs, the glow under the mark).
- Every `.tint(...)` is one of three blues: `dotSheet()` and `RootView` set `Theme.accent`, three places set `Theme.accentFill`
  (a switch, a swipe action, the prominent glass button), `Theme.link` tints a message's text. Where nothing sets a tint the
  system's own applies (`AccentColor`): the "Done" of a sheet is blue text on grey glass in the large sheets and bare blue text
  in the medium ones (`confirmationAction` with no style), which on the owner's OLED reads as blue-violet.
- Gradients: `Theme.accentGradient` (the prominent button, the send button, the filled quiet button), `DotBackground` (an
  elliptical glow of the accent under the top of every screen), `DotMesh` (a mesh of blues and indigo and cyan behind the pairing
  and address screens and every empty state), the empty state's gradient ring, `CardRow`'s tile (`LinearGradient` of the tint),
  `StateRing`'s angular gradient, and the radial gradient of `DotGlyph` (Dot's mark is a luminous blue sphere) in the app and in
  the Live Activity.
- Shadows: the mark on the pairing and address screens (`shadow(color: accent, radius: 24)`), the amber ring's glow
  (`StateRing`).
- Coloured tiles: `CardRow` puts every icon on a 30 pt gradient square in a hue (Settings: five blues, a red, a teal, an indigo
  for "Open web UI"; the Run sheet: a blue and a grey one in one list; the Run status card: a 48 pt tinted square), the helper
  and the schedule rows (a tinted square), the inspector's step glyph in a tinted disc, the approval card's glyph in an amber
  disc, the hand-off card's in a blue one.
- Filled chips for a plain state: `StatusPill` (a capsule filled with 14 % of its tone) is the "Running" of every helper (blue),
  "Done" (grey), the "Connected" of Settings (green) and the gallery's four.
- A card darker than its ground: the Queued sheet (the card is `surface` #121214 on the system's glass, which the sheet is on
  iOS 26: the owner's "Next-up background is black"), and in the light look the same card is white on grey glass. Every sheet
  leaves the ground to the system's glass on iOS 26 (`dotScreenBackground(inSheet:)` and `dotSheet()` paint nothing there), so no
  card can know what it lies on.
- Two card languages: the Run sheet's status card is a gradient-free but tinted card (`statusTone.opacity(0.08)` over the
  surface, radius 16) beside flat cards of radius 12; the approval and the inbox cards tint their fill amber (7 to 9 %) and draw
  an amber border; the away and hand-off cards are plain. Radii of cards: 12 and 16.
- Three button styles in one row, and a different one in each sheet: the Queued sheet has a text "Send now", two chevron buttons
  and a red trash, the buttons of the approval card are glass (`glassProminent` blue, `glass`), the pairing field is glass, the
  model chip is glass. "Done" is a pill in the large sheets and bare text in the medium ones (the review's fix 10).
- Words in capitals: the inbox's "Recommended" is set `textCase(.uppercase)`.
- The Live Activity (`DotLiveActivity`): "Working" and the icons in `DotPalette.dark.accent`, "Done" in the green, the keyline in
  the accent, the avatar a blue radial gradient with two dark eyes. The share extension's own colours are the system's (its error
  text is `.red`).

**What the owner's screenshots and the shots show beyond the source.**
- Queued sheet (dark, light, Large): the black card; "Send now" in blue with an arrow; chevrons that are grey and then white;
  salmon trash icons on every row; "Done" in blue.
- Run sheet: the blue-tinted status card with a blue glyph tile; "Model for this chat" on a blue tile and "Default models…" on a
  grey one; five blue "Running" pills with a waveform; grey "Done" and "Cancelled" pills in the finished group; the blue ring of
  each helper's bot.
- Settings: a stack of coloured tiles (blue, deep blue, red, teal, indigo), blue switches, a green "Connected" pill on a green tile.
- Chat list: the blue glow under the title, Dot's blue sphere on every Dot row, blue icons in the glass pair at the top, "While you
  were away" in blue, a green tick and a red alert in the away card, an amber row tint on each "Needs you" row.
- Chat: the approval card's blue "Allow once" pill (2.8:1 on its own card, the review's fix 1), amber fill and border on the card.
- AX3: the capsule keeps "Working · 5m 56s" and shows the loop and the queue as a glyph and a number with no word (the dock is
  capped at a text size that cannot hold them); a tool line cuts "Opened pegasus.example" to "Opene…xample".
- The first-run screens (pairing, address): the blue mesh and glow, a blue sphere with a blue shadow, a glass field, step numbers in
  blue discs.
