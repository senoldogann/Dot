### 510. Teach a task writes its skill, and a chat's row is one line

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Reported by the owner.

- **Write the skill failed.** `POST /api/teach/{id}/draft` returned `TaughtSkill.model_dump()`, whose steps,
  parameters and shown lines are tuples. The route's `JsonValue` refused them (`ResponseValidationError`), so the panel
  said "Dot failed on an error it did not expect". It now returns `model_dump(mode="json")` (`server/routes_teach`).
- **Blank pages in the steps.** about:blank reports its origin as `null`, which a recording named "Opened ://nullblank",
  and the tab it started on was "Opened about://blank". A page that is no page of the web (`browser/teach.web_page`) is
  no step now, and a recording that starts on a blank tab goes to Google first (`BrowserSession.start_teaching`).
- **Tall chat rows.** Item 507 moved the ellipsis of a chat's last line onto the words inside it. Words put in the line
  bare, as a page of an earlier build did, wrapped to as many lines as they took. The line itself no longer wraps.

Checked by `tests/unit/test_teach.py` (blank pages are no steps, and are not where a recording starts), and by
rendering a row of either build's markup in Chromium and WebKit (62 px each).
