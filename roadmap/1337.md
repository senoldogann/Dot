### 1337. Schedules are Settings › Scheduled tasks: a list in rows with a switch, Run now, Edit and Delete, and a page for the form

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The owner's word of 2026-11-22: "Schedules must move into a submenu of the Settings page and
its icon be removed from the left menu."

The Schedules page is `#/settings/schedules` (the row Scheduled tasks of the Agents group). Its list is rows of a card: the
name with its badges (Brief, Loop, Taught task, the agent), one line of when it runs, when next and how the last went, and
at the right Run now, Edit, the switch (active / paused) and "…" for Delete; Details under a row still hold how its tasks
start, its goal and its history. The loops of chats (item 281) are a section of their own under the schedules, and
`#/settings/schedules?thread=<chat id>` shows the loops of that one chat alone, for the panel's "open all" link; the
Morning brief, the check-in, the Watchers and Dot's own initiative stand under them as before. The list is no longer a
box that scrolls inside past its third row (item 634): its rows are the page's.

New schedule and a schedule's Edit are pages, not a dialog (item 615): `#/settings/schedules/new` and
`#/settings/schedules/<id>`, the form as sections of the settings' rows (Task, When, How its tasks run, What its tasks
leave), the schedule's name in the breadcrumb, Run now and Delete in the head of an existing one, Cancel and the saving
button at the foot; saved, it goes back to the list. The brief is set by its own card, so its address leads to the list. The page keeps what
the dialog did for a changed form, and holds every change of the address inside Dot, not only a click on a link (review
round 2): the router (`main.js`, with `leaveguard.js`) asks a hook the page registers (`ctx.onBeforeLeave`, which returns
its disposer) before it swaps the page. "Discard your changes?" (Keep editing leaves it as it was) is asked for a link of
the app (Cancel, the nav, the breadcrumb), Back in a phone's bar (`history.back()` and `location.replace`), Enter in the
settings' search, Connect apps, the New chat and Appearance shortcuts, the browser's own Back and Forward, and Run now
(asked before the run starts, and not again on the way to its page). The hash has changed when `hashchange` comes, so the
router puts the change back at once and makes it again on Discard: with the Navigation API's entry index as `history.go`
walks (no entry is added or lost), and without it, or for a replaced entry, by writing the page's address over the entry
(`replaceState`, no event, no new entry), where a held browser Back leaves the page's address in the entry it walked to and
the next Back goes one entry further. The browser asks before the tab closes; a form nobody changed, once the models and
agents it lists were read, leaves without a question, as does one that was saved or whose schedule was deleted, and a
redirect of an old address (`MOVED`), which comes after the page has been left. Not held: an address typed in the bar that
loads the page anew (the browser's `beforeunload` question stands for that), and a change of the address made while the
question is open, which is put back as well.

The old addresses lead there (`#/schedules`, `#/schedules/<id>`, with `?schedule=<id>`), and the links of the interface
name the new ones: the notice of a schedule that did not run or was switched off, the line a run's schedule is made in, the
teach panel's "See it in Scheduled tasks", an agent's schedule row, System's usage line. The sidebar has no Schedules icon any more (item 1328), so no link of it
names the old address.
