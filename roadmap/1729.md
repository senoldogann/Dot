### 1729. A fact that changed replaces its older note: `memory_save` takes a subject, and the older note rests, kept

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The market research of 2026-11-24 (M4 topic 2 and top 10 no. 4; M2 idea 11, Graphiti's
validity windows and mem0's temporal retrieval) named it: notes were saved and ranked, never reconciled, so a note and
its correction both reached a run. Measured by item 1728 before this change: the outdated note was shown beside the
newest in 34 of 34 searches, and the newest was first in 2 (a floor, see 1728). The audit's own number says why the
model's follow-up is no answer: `memory_forget` was called once in 500 runs, though the answer of `memory_save` tells the
model to remove an outdated note.

What must hold: the owner's notes, pinned and corrected ones, a lesson the owner saved and a standing note are never
put aside by a run's save (item 1711: a run's change of one is a proposal); a note saved while its run read outside
content never puts aside a clean note (item 557, as the nightly check); nothing is deleted; a yolo or free run asks and
is denied nothing new (the save is the same call with one more optional argument).

What changed (`memory/supersede.py`, `tools/memory.py`, `memory/render.py`, `memory/service.py`, `store/db.py`,
`store/models.py`, `evals/memory_bench.py`):

- A nullable `subject` column on `memories` (added at open as the other columns are; the schema digest is pinned again;
  a trashed note restores with it) and `MemoryRecord.subject`. `memory_save` takes `subject`, a short name for the slot
  a fact fills; a recalled note shows it ("about user-city") so a run reuses it.
- `save_replacing` saves as before, then puts aside every awake older note of the same subject that a run may replace:
  `Store.retire_memory`, the call the nightly outdated check makes (tag `rest`, `replaced_by`, `rested_at`, which is
  when it stopped being true). Search, the notes a run starts with and the live notes already pass over a resting note,
  so recall prefers the valid note with no change to ranking; the Memory page already says "Replaced by another note" and
  "Resting since". The answer to the save lists what was replaced and what was left (the owner's, pinned, from before a
  page was read) and says to use `memory_update` for those.
- With no subject only a pure wording rule replaces, and only a note `similar_threshold` close: the same sentence with
  numbers swapped, or with a word of change added, at least three words shared (`wording_replaces`). A different
  subject on the older note stops it.

Design choices, with the evidence: (1) A cosine rule was rejected: `bge-m3` puts facts that changed at 0.68-0.97 and
facts that are both true at 0.68-0.84 (likes pizza/sushi 0.84, speaks Turkish/Finnish 0.84, a laptop that changed 0.85,
a phone number 0.76): at the 0.85 of `similar_threshold` all 8 true pairs stay apart (the closest is 0.838) but 9 of the
34 changes are missed, and a threshold low enough to catch them puts true facts aside. (2) A model call in
the save was not built: the nightly check already asks one about the notes that remain close, within a day, and the
saves with a close note are rare (18 pairs at 0.85 or more among the owner's 1,192 active notes, audit 2026-11-24); its cost and latency in every
run's save were not measured worth it. (3) A column, not a tag, so that the key does not compete for the six tags, does
not travel into a merge and does not show as a chip; additive and nullable. (4) Recency as a ranking tie-break was
left out: the tie that makes "first" 2 of 34 is the artefact of a store of two or three notes.

Measured with `dotx eval memory` (42 cases, scripted embeddings from bge-m3's measured cosines, and the real `bge-m3`: the
same numbers), before 1729 against after:

| group | cases | newest first | outdated also shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| facts that changed | 34 | 2 -> 25 | 34 -> 10 |
| saved with a subject | 18 | 1 -> 18 | 18 -> 0 |
| saved without one | 16 | 1 -> 7 | 16 -> 10 |
| old note handed to a run | 4 | 0 -> 3 | 4 -> 1 |
| facts that did not change (must stay) | 8 | 8 -> 8 | none put aside |

The 10 that remain are notes reworded into another sentence (U05, U06, U12), a name swapped (U01, U07, U10, C04, S04), a
version after a word that names a thing (U11, `invoice-v1.docx`, left out by the pre-push review's finding that `Invoice
1042 is paid` and `Invoice 1043 is paid` are two true facts, which the first cut of the rule replaced) and
a note below `similar_threshold` (U08); they wait for a subject or the night's model. The rule was written after the
cases, so "8 of 8 stayed" is what the rule was fitted to avoid (the traps `Nordic Pines pays within 14 days` against
`Blue Harbor pays within 30 days`, `4 CPU cores` against `16 CPU cores` of another server, `2 monitors` against `3 bikes`
are in `tests/unit/test_memory_supersede.py`), not a held-out rate.

Not done: no model judges in the save; nothing asks the owner; the owner's own saves (`dotx memory add`, the Memory
page) do not replace a run's older note; the lessons a reflection or a run's end writes do not take a subject; a search
does not offer the replaced notes as history; what a run does with the notes it reads was not measured (that takes a
model).

Checked: `tests/unit/test_memory_supersede.py` (the rule's pairs and traps, the numbers that name a thing among them; by subject, a chain, other subjects, the
same text again, the owner's/pinned/corrected notes kept, outside content, the cosine alone not replacing, no embeddings,
trash round trip; fails when the retire call or the protections are removed, each proved once),
`tests/unit/test_eval_memory.py` (the numbers above), `tests/smoke/test_memory_supersede.py` (the tool end to end: the
answer, a search, the starting notes, the policy unchanged), `tests/smoke/test_eval_memory_live.py` (`live`),
`tests/unit/test_store_schema.py` (digest pinned), `dotx eval check`, `tests/unit/test_freedom_contract.py`.
