### 631. A one-time schedule in the hour the clocks go back over is the one still to come

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** Item 625's agent ran the smoke tests at about 03:00 on 2026-10-25 in Helsinki, the night
the clocks went back (04:00 EEST became 03:00 EET), and five tests of schedules failed on its branch and on `main`. One
of them, run again at 03:37 EET, said:

    ToolInputError: 'at' is not in the future | context={"at": "2026-10-25T03:38:57", "now": "Sun 2026-10-25 03:37"}

**The defect.** `scheduler/timing.py` `local_moment` read a date and time without an offset in the zone with
`replace(tzinfo=zone)`, whose `fold` is 0: of the two moments of a time in the hour that happens twice, always the
first. After the change, 03:38:57 is both 00:38:57 UTC, an hour ago, and 01:38:57 UTC, a minute away. A one-time
schedule a minute ahead was refused as past (`plan_once`), and a run's or the owner's "at 03:45" then would have been
refused, or kept for the moment that had passed. The recurring schedules were right already: `firings` fires a fixed
time once on that night and runs a wildcard through the repeated hour
(`test_a_fixed_time_fires_once_on_the_day_the_clocks_go_back`,
`test_a_wildcard_schedule_runs_through_the_hour_that_repeats`).

**The fix.**
- `local_moments` gives both moments of such a time, the earlier first, and one for any other.
- `local_moment(at, zone, after)` takes the first of them later than `after`.
- `plan_once` gives it `now`.
- An edited schedule whose time is unchanged keeps the moment it had, whichever of the two it was (`scheduler/plans.py`).
- A time with an offset names one moment, and a time the clocks jump over is refused, as before.
- `docs/memory-and-schedules.md` says it.

**Tests.** `tests/unit/test_schedule_timing.py`, with the moments of that night written out:
- a time of the repeated hour asked for after the change is the second moment;
- asked for before the change, the first;
- between the two, the second;
- after both, refused as past;
- with an offset, the moment it names.
- It fails against the code before.
