### 1069. On a phone, Dot's icon is the two eyes in white on black

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** The owner, on 2026-11-09: "uygulama ikonunu mobilde grokbot benzeri yap": a flat icon,
one white sign on black, as the app they compare Dot with wears, not a lit ball with a gold rim on a gradient. Three
were drawn for them from Dot's own mark (no other app's sign): the eyes alone in white on black, a white ball with black
eyes, and a white ring with white eyes. They chose the first, the one recommended (the plainest, and the clearest at a
home screen's size).

Now the web app's icons (`icons/icon-{96,192,512}.png`, the maskable one) and the home screen's touch icons (and the
180 px files at the root iOS tries by name) are a tile of black with the eyes of the attentive pose in white, centred
(`desktop/icons/render.py` `phone_tile_svg`; the maskable one's eyes smaller, well inside the circle a phone may cut it
to). The favicon, the desktop app's icon and the menu bar's keep the ball.

A phone that added Dot to its home screen before shows the new icon once the shortcut is added again (iOS keeps the
icon it took).
