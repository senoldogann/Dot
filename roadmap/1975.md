### 1975. Typed memory: every note has a kind, and a run's starting notes are ranked by kind for where it came from

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The review of 2026-11-26 (`reports/review-2026-11-26/A-runs.md`, item 13) found that
the notes handed at a run's start rarely show in its work and that five pinned business and character notes went to
785 runs, every code review among them. The owner approved typed memory (GK4).

**Measured first** (read-only, `sqlite3 -readonly` on the owner's store, 2026-11-27, schema version 4):

- 1,307 notes: 1,055 tagged `lesson`, 213 resting, 32 merged away, 1,081 tainted; 1,094 live. Tags say little of a
  note's kind: after `lesson` (1,055) come `rest`, `core` (43), a site's tag, the owner's name (40), `gistsmith`.
- Recall had no notion of kind. Every run is handed the standing notes (tagged `core` and the owner's or pinned: 7)
  and every note the owner wrote (3), whatever its goal; then notes close to the goal.
- The 1.5 % echo of the review counts the hands-out from before item 1715 (2026-11-24 22:31), when no echo was
  recorded, as not echoed. Since then (`memory_uses.echoed` not NULL) the rough echo trace is 45.7 % for schedules,
  28.8 % for chats, 29.6 % for helpers and 29.4 % for team runs.
- Code reviews: 2,211 hands-out to 138 reviews on 11-22 to 11-24, none since item 1712 (11-24 21:09) made a review
  start with the notes about code alone. That part of the finding was already fixed.
- What was left: the 5 notes of the finding (3 owner-written `core,business` decisions, 2 pinned character rules)
  were each handed to 85 runs since 11-24 22:31, helpers and team runs among them; in the 56 stored helper runs, 3 of
  every 10 notes a helper started with were business notes.

**Approach.** A note has a `kind`: `profile` (the owner), `people` (another person), `workflow` (how something is
done), `rule` (a rule, preference or decision), `fact` (what is so now) or `lesson`, and `kind_by`, who told it:
`rule`, `model`, `run` or `owner`.

- Fixed rules first (`dotagent.store.kinds.rule_kind`, pure): the `lesson` and `journal` tags, then tags that say a
  kind (Turkish and English, folded), then words of the text ("kuralı", "kararı", "wants", "istiyor", "how to"). On
  the owner's store they tell 989 of the 1,094 live notes (90 %): 842 lessons, 64 rules, 50 facts, 17 workflows, 13
  profile, 3 people; 105 are left.
- Schema version 5 (`_tell_kinds`, item 1268's versioning): the columns come with the additive layer, the step gives
  every note the rules' kind once, after the copy every upgrade makes; a newer schema is refused as before. On a copy
  of the owner's 957 MB store the first open took 36 s, the copy (229 MB compressed) included.
- The store tells a new note's kind by the same rules as it writes it (and a merged note takes its kept note's).
  `memory_save` takes an optional `kind`, recorded as the run's on a note it created; a note saved before keeps its
  kind. The fast model tells what the rules cannot (`dotagent.memory.kinds`): the scheduler's pass, at most once an
  hour, asks one word a note, at most 40 a pass, and records it as the model's; a word that is no kind is logged
  (`memory_kind_not_told`) and asked again next pass, a provider's error ends the pass (`memory_kinds_failed`). No
  test calls a model: a stand-in answers.
- Recall ranks by kind for the run's source (`kind_plan`), and nothing else changes: a chat, a `dotx` command, a
  watcher's or a reply's run starts with the same notes as before (a test holds the two equal). A schedule puts
  workflows and rules first; a code review lessons and workflows first (its notes are still the ones about code); a
  helper (`delegate`, `team`) is handed a standing or owner-written note only when it fits its goal, as any note,
  since the run that gave it the work was handed them, and puts rules and workflows first. A note with no kind yet
  stands as before and comes after every kind; the `memories` event carries the plan and how many had no kind, so it
  is never silent.
