### 1015. A model's answer the checks refuse no longer fails the night

**Status: done (2026-11-07).** **Why.** On the owner's Dot 7 of the last 10 nights of learning failed (2026-10-28 to
11-06), every time on one answer of the proposer model (Kimi K3) that its checks refused after one more try: a tool
guidance of 1749 characters against 1500, a task's habit past 120 characters, a line that imitated a heading of the
prompt in a task, an experience's entry given as a sentence, a link in a moment, fields left out, a summary past 120.
The checks were right to refuse them, but the note that asked again said only what was wrong, and the one answer lost
the whole night: no lesson was ever kept.

**Built.** `agent/review.py`: the roles of the harness (proposer, composer, selector, task writer, experience writer)
are asked three times (`_HARNESS_ATTEMPTS`; `_decide_checked` takes the attempts, Dot's other questions keep two). The
note that asks again (`retry_note`) says what to fix: a `FixableAnswerError` carries it, from `length_fix` (the field,
its length, the bound and about how many words), `validation_fix` (each field's path and what to do, never a key the
model made up) and `problem_fix` (what to take out for a refusal of the gate; `harness/components.py`).
`harness/tasks.py`: `draft_problem` names where a refused text is (`in the text of file 1`). Each role's prompt states
the bounds of its own fields in characters and words. `harness/step.py`: a role's answer still unusable is recorded
(`unusable` in the record, `state_of`), the rest of the step goes on, nothing is adopted on its own; the step is
`unusable` when it made no change, `done` when it probed a version. `harness/weekly.py`: a week's proposal still
unusable keeps the night's digest, records the night `unusable` with `the proposal was unusable: <why>`, and leaves
the week unspent. `store/models.py`: the state `unusable`. The Self-improvement page: *answer unusable*, an amber cell
and its key in the legend, the reasons under the row. Docs: `docs/harness.md` (*When an answer stays unusable*).

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_harness_retry.py` (the words of a bound, the note, a component, a verdict and a gate
refusal told what to fix; a proposer asked three times reaching a usable answer on the third, and raising the last
problem when it does not); `tests/smoke/test_harness_week.py` (an unusable proposal keeps the digest, the night is
`unusable`, and the next night asks again and tries a proposal that fits); `tests/smoke/test_harness_step.py` (a
proposer unusable in one scope, in all, and a composer unusable: recorded, not failed, nothing adopted);
`tests/smoke/test_harness_page.py` (the night's pill, its amber cell, the key in the legend and the reason under
the row).
