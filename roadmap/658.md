### 658. A choice in a page answers with a tap

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner showed another agent asking to open a browser tab of mockups to choose from,
and asked whether Dot should do the same. Dot already draws such pages in the conversation (`show_page`), on the
phone too, kept with the chat. What it lacked is the rest of the owner's question, to which they said yes: the owner
had to type their choice, and Dot did not offer pictures on its own.

**The change.**
- The page's bootstrap (`routes_pages.py`): an element with `data-answer` that the user taps sends its words to the
  frame as MCP Apps' `ui/message` (`{"role": "user", "content": [{"type": "text", "text": ...}]}`). A page's own
  script may send the same.
- `pageframe.js` takes `ui/message` only after a click of the user's in that page, as it takes `ui/open-link`: the
  user's activation is live and the frame has the focus. It hands the words, up to 500 characters, to the page of the
  run (`dot:page-answer`), which cancels the event when it has a box to write in. The page is answered with an
  error otherwise, as on a delegated run's page, which has no box. A choice made full screen closes the dialog.
- `composer.js` `fill(text)` writes the words into the box, after what is there on a line of their own, keeps them as
  the draft and gives the box the focus. Nothing is sent: the owner reads, changes or sends.
- `prompt.py` (the pages section) and the tool's description (`tools/pages.py`) ask for options that are seen (a
  layout, a logo, a colour, a screen, a design, an animation, a diagram) drawn side by side on one page. Each option
  is labelled, tappable through `data-answer`, and Dot's pick is marked.
- `docs/runs.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_shown_pages.py`, at 1280 and 390, light and dark:
  - a tap on the page's option writes its words into the message box, which has the focus;
  - the `ui/message` the page's script sent by itself on load is refused.
- The unit tests of the prompt, the pages and the tools pass, and so does `dotx eval check`.
