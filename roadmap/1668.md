### 1668. A helper that a restart of Dot cut off is resumed at the next start and reports when it really ends

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Measured on 2026-11-24 in the owner's store: 10 helpers ended after the run that started
them, and 4 of them ended at the same second as their chat's own run, which came back 12 s later resumed: a stop of the
daemon cut them, and their chats were told they failed. A helper working in the background (item 1351) was told as ended
when the daemon came back, never resumed; and a run that the daemon's stop cut while it was still at work forgot the
helpers it had let go on, as a failed run does, so nothing was told of them at all.

What changed (`agent/manager.py`, `agent/resume.py`, `agent/helper_reports.py`):

- `tell_helper_reports` (the daemon's start) resumes a kept helper that the daemon's own end cut off (`cut_by_restart`:
  `daemon_shutdown` or `daemon_restarted`) with `_resume_helper`: a new helper run of the same parent in the helper's
  chat, with its goal, mode, model, effort, speed, approvals and agent, whose first message is the resume note of a root
  run (`resume_note`: how it was cut, the calls of its step and how each ended, its plan). It is kept in the store and
  followed in the old one's place (`DetachedHelper.resumed_from`), so that its chat is told its report when it ends; the
  parent's page gets a `helper_resumed` event; the report the chat reads says a restart cut the helper and Dot resumed
  it.
- The helper's parent has ended by then, and a helper takes its guards from its parent's gate (item 941). A resumed
  helper takes them from the parent's record instead, read as the parent's gate read them at its start: whether its chat
  is one of Dot's own initiative, one the owner opened and whether the owner is there (from the parent's chat and
  source), the chat whose "don't ask again" answers it, and the owner's goals of that chat up to the parent
  (`_instructions_before`). Its taint is the cut helper's (a resumed run's, item 243). It keeps no step for later
  (`keep_for(None)`), which refuses an unanswered step as before item 482: stricter, not looser.
- `helper_not_resumed` says why one is not: `[agent] resume` is `off`, it was cut longer ago than `resume_within_s`,
  another run went on in its chat, it was itself a resumption (no loop of restarts), or a call of an outward action (the
  policy's `owners_guard` flags: it pays, sends or posts; a unit test holds the two sets equal) was cut while it ran.
  The chat is then told, in a note of its own (`cut_report_note`), that a restart cut the helper off, that it did not
  fail, and why it was not resumed, so the run delegates the work again.
- A run that the daemon's stop cuts while it works waits for the helpers it let go on to end (item 197) and keeps them
  (`_wait_detached_of`) instead of forgetting them as a failed or stopped run does.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_helper_wake.py` (a helper cut by the daemon's stop is resumed at the next start, under the
same parent, with the `resumed` and `helper_resumed` events, and the chat is woken with the resumed helper's report; a
run cut with a helper in the background keeps it and the next start resumes it; with resuming off the note says the
restart cut it and why, not that it failed) and `tests/unit/test_resume.py` (each reason not to resume, the outward
class equal to the policy's). The two smoke tests fail with the resume or the keeping taken out.
Not done: a helper the run still waited on (`wait` true) when the daemon stopped is not resumed on its own: it ends with
its run, whose resume note names the cut `delegate` call. A crash (`daemon_restarted`) takes the same path as a stop and
is covered by the unit test only, not by a killed daemon.
