### 837. The pages' boxes and buttons are soft: no frames, a soft shadow, rounder corners

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner ("Sıradaki adımlar ile devam et. Amacımız Grok Bot kadar sade
fakat hoş tasarım"), with screenshots of that app. The first part of the fourth of the six stages of the design review
of 2026-10-30, the soft design language, as the owner was told: "daha yuvarlak köşeler, daha geniş boşluklar,
çerçevesiz kartlar, yumuşak düğmeler". Every box of Dot's pages had a hairline frame on a 12 px corner, and every
button one on an 8 px corner; the other app draws its boxes and buttons as soft fills.

- `app.css`: the radii rounder (`--radius-xs` 8, `--radius-sm` and `--radius-control` 10, `--radius` 16, `--radius-lg`
  24; they were 6, 8, 8, 12 and 20); a box of the pages (`.card`, a settings' group's body, Customize's tiles, the
  gallery's, Appearance's cards, a settings' card and tile, the memory's panel) is framed and lifted by two tokens,
  `--box-border` (none) and `--box-shadow` (`--shadow-card`: a hairline of shadow and a soft one round it); an account
  inside the card of the accounts is a soft fill, as a box within a box is, rather than a second shadow. A button is a
  soft fill with no frame (`--button-border`), a step darker under the pointer; the first of a page is ink as before
  and a quiet one has no fill. A chip has no frame. Inputs and selects keep their hairline, as what is typed in.
- `themes.css`: `--shadow-card`; with the contrast setting the boxes and the buttons are framed again in its stronger
  lines; the paper look takes the base radii rather than restating the old ones.

Kept as they were: the composer's own frame and lift (item 121), what floats (menus, dialogs), the inputs, every
layout; a box keeps the room of its frame, so nothing moves.

**Checked.** The owner's Dot read in a headless Chrome of its own with these sheets served in their place: Agents,
an agent, Schedules, Settings › General, Models, Appearance and Phone, Customize's connectors and plugins, Memory and a
chat, light and dark, before and after. Two looks were tried side by side first (no frame with a soft shadow, and no
frame on a darker ground); the first read as boxes on both schemes, the second did not. `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py`
holds the new values to the tokens. Of the browser tests of the screens touched and of every phone one (29 files), the
87 that launch Chrome passed on this Mac; the 41 that launch Playwright's own WebKit or headless shell cannot start
here, and CI runs them.
