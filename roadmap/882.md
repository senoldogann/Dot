### 882. A schedule's firing at a level its model does not offer runs at the nearest one, instead of failing to start

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-10-31, quoting their Dot: "Dot'un zamanlayıcısı her görevi
reasoning: medium ile kuruyor; bizim model (deepseek-v4.1-flash) yalnızca low/high/max destekliyor ... Test görevi
17:40'ta da başlamadı. Bu yüzden 5 görevi sildim"; "bu problemi düzeltir misin?").

What happened (the owner's database, read only): a chat whose runs start with no level chosen runs at `[models]
effort`, `medium`, which the run keeps; `schedule_create` makes its schedule with the chat's choices, so the schedule
kept `medium` as if it had been chosen. DeepSeek offers low, high and max, and `_launch_firing` checked the level as a
chat's choice is checked: every firing of three schedules (a test at 17:40 among them) ended "failed to start: the model
does not offer that reasoning effort" (`schedule_failures`), and the owner's Dot deleted five schedules. Chats on the
same model ran at `medium` all along: 39 of them in two days.

- `agent/manager.py`: `nearest_offered(effort, offered)`, the nearest level of the model's, the higher of two as near;
  `_firing_effort` keeps a schedule's level its model offers and moves one it does not to the nearest, logged as
  `firing_effort_not_offered` (a model with no choice runs at none). A level chosen for a chat's run is still refused.
- `docs/memory-and-schedules.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_firing_effort.py` (the nearest level; a firing at `medium` on a model of low, high and
max starts at high and ends done, and a chat's run at `medium` is refused), which fails before; the scheduler tests.
