### 615. Forms open in a dialog, not in the page

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The owner, with a screenshot of their coding app's "New task" dialog, given as an example
and not as a design to copy: "All forms should open as a pop-up. A form added inside the page makes the page longer;
opening it as a pop-up from a button saves room in many places." Schedules grew a form of thirteen fields under its
head, the Morning brief's settings opened under its line, a watcher or a note turned into its form in its row,
Self-improvement (and Rules, which is left as it is, see below) opened a card of fields over the list, and Connectors
kept a form at its foot: each made the page longer, and what stood under it went down.

What is taken from the example is its anatomy: a dialog over a dimmed page, a title and a line of what it does, the
fields with their labels, a foot of Cancel and the main action, and a cross at the top right.

**As built.**

- **One dialog for a form.** `formDialog({ title, description, body, submitLabel, onSubmit, danger, ... })` in `ui.js`,
  beside `confirmDialog` and `choiceDialog`, which share its frame (`dialogFrame`: title, line, what the dialog holds,
  foot; the question and the choice look as they did). `body` is the form as the page builds it. `onSubmit` is
  asynchronous: while it runs the main button is busy and Cancel, the cross and Escape do nothing; it resolves to close
  the dialog, resolves to `false` when a field the form needs is empty (the field has the focus and the dialog stays, as
  the in-page form did), and throws for a failed request: the reason shows inside the dialog, above the foot and outside
  the fields that scroll, the fields as they were, and the dialog stays. A second press while a request runs is refused.
- **Closing.** Cancel, the cross and Escape close it, asking first (*Discard your changes?*, Keep editing focused) when a
  field was changed; a field the page fills in later (the agents and the models of a schedule) does not count as a change
  (`ready`). A link inside the form that leads to another page (Add one, the brief's last run) asks first too, when a
  field was changed, and goes on once the owner said so; the dialog closes by itself, asking nothing, when the address
  changes by any other way (the browser's back button). A click on the dimmed page does not close it: a stray click
  must not drop a form. Nothing closes it while a submit runs. The key fields of a form (`type="password"`) are
  emptied as it closes.
- **Focus.** It goes to the first field on opening and back to the button that opened it on closing (to the one that a
  repainted list has now, through `returnFocus`: a schedule's Edit, a note's pencil).
- **A phone (up to 720 px).** A sheet of the window's width, round at the top, from the foot of what is seen (the
  keyboard lifts that foot, `layout.js`), its fields scrolling between the head and the foot; its sides are 16 px.
  The foot is one line of Cancel and the main button as wide as each other, with what the form does besides on a line
  above (Write one now); a button is never narrower than its words, so three that do not fit on a line (Cancel, Save
  for later and Save and use on a 320 px iPhone SE) go on to a second one.
- **The logic without a page.** `formflow.js` holds what needs no browser: whether a form was changed
  (`stateOfControls`), the first field to take the focus, and the busy, failed, done and asked states of the buttons
  (`createFormFlow`); `tests/js/formflow.test.js` tries them.
- **Forms moved** (their fields, rules, validation and requests are as they were; only where the form stands changed):
  - Schedules: **New schedule** and a schedule's pencil (the list no longer holds a form in a row, and a run starting or
    ending paints it behind the dialog without touching what is typed); a fresh form each time, so that the next one
    starts from the defaults (item 559).
  - The **Morning brief**: its line keeps its switch and has a **Settings** button (the arrow of item 609 is gone, and
    with it the fold kept in `dot.brief.open`); the settings, with Write one now beside Save, are in the dialog, and
    the pencil of the brief's row in the list opens it.
  - **Watchers**: New watcher and a watcher's pencil (done last; item 612's row on a phone and Check now are not
    touched, only where the form opens; the missing-account error is said in the dialog, as a toast would stand
    under it).
  - Settings › **Self-improvement**: **Write your own** opens the version's form, with Save for later beside Save and use
    at the foot and Start from an example, which fills the fields, under the form's intro; it starts from the version
    in use as the daemon last said it.
  - Customize › **Connectors**: **Add server** in the card of Your own MCP server (the form no longer stands at the
    foot of the page), a service's key (from its tile and from its page) and the service account token of 1Password.
  - Found by the search the item asked for, and moved too: Settings › **Providers**' Add key and Replace key (the key
    form grew the provider's panel), Settings › **Browser**'s Add profile, and Settings › **Memory**'s Edit of a note
    (its row turned into a form).
- **Left in place, and why.**
  - Settings › **Rules** (`views/rules.js`, its New rule form with the two ways to write a rule, the sentence of an
    approval card's link and the empty card's examples) stays as it was. The owner asked on 2026-10-24 for a new,
    simpler Rules page, one line (*When Dot wants to … / It should … / Add rule*), which will be an item of its own, and
    a dialog there would be thrown away. Until then New rule opens its card in the page, as it did.
  - Settings › Memory's **Remember** box and its **Your lines** box: the page's own input, as the composer is a chat's.
  - The composer and the boxes of a conversation (a question Dot asked, the answer to an approval).
  - Search boxes (chats, notes, models, connectors, skills, plugins, logs, licences).
  - One-field controls that act at once: switches, segmented choices, selects, a name saved as it is left (what Dot is
    called, what it calls you, the DevTools port, a font family), the rename of a chat in the sidebar, and the
    fallback-model select.
  - Settings › Channels: the number to pair and the code that comes back are one-field steps of a flow that act at
    once, and stand in the card all the time.
  - The theme editor: its colours are tried on the page live, which a dialog's backdrop would dim.
  - The **Agents** page's editors (New agent, an agent's Edit, New team and a team's Edit, in a card at the top of the
    page): item 610 rebuilt that page while this item was built, and the brief of this one left the page to it, so they
    are not touched here. They still grow in place and are the forms left of the kind.
- **Details the tests found.** A closed `<dialog>` leaves the accessibility tree at once and the document a moment
  later, when its answer is given (the `close` event): the helper of the tests waits for the document. The strip of
  the failure takes room from the fields: the field the owner pressed the button under stays in sight.

**Tests.**

  that trade values), the field to focus, and the flow: busy while it runs, a second press refused, a failure that
  leaves the dialog open and can be tried again, `false` that stays, any other value that closes, a close that asks
  (Keep editing and Discard), Escape pressed again while it asks, nothing closes a running submit, a closed form is
  closed, a page that goes away takes the dialog with it, and a failure that comes after it.
