### 2009. A bot's work lists the same on the phone and the web: handed-out time, judged by, finished objectives, hand-offs with their faces

**Status: in progress (2026-11-27).** The owner's rule of 2026-11-27: phone and desktop move together. Item 1995 put a bot's
work orders, objectives and hand-offs into DotNext's profile; reading the web's Agents page and Handoffs page beside it
showed where the two said or drew the same thing differently. This item settles those, on both.

What was compared (web: `workorders.js`, `workwords.js`, `views/team.js`; phone: `BotWorkPages.swift`) and what each side
does now:
- Work order row: the web said "handed out 2 h ago"; the phone's list did not (its page did). The phone's row says it.
- Objective row: the web said "Judged by: …"; the phone's list did not (its page did). The phone's row says it. A
  finished objective (done or let go) was hidden by the web and listed by the phone; the web now lists them last, under
  "Finished objectives", each with its state, as the phone's Finished section does. An active objective with no line of
  progress says "Next: <its next step>" on both.
- Hand-off row: the web drew the two faces, "From → To" and "<name> is working on it" / "No reply"; the phone drew none.
  The phone's row has the faces and the title "From → To", and says the same of a reply that is not there.
- The words "Written after Dot read content from outside" carry no full stop on the phone, as on the web.

What stays different, on purpose: the phone says bot and routines where the web says agent and schedules (the owner's
words on each); the phone's lists are scoped to the bot and the web's are not; the phone sorts hand-offs newest first in
two groups (received, sent) and the web as one thread oldest first; the phone has no status badge on a hand-off and no
links to the runs behind an order or a hand-off (it has no screen of a run by its id), and does not reload when a run
starts or ends (it reads when the page opens and when it is pulled).

How it was checked: `tests/smoke/test_work_orders_page.py` in Chrome (next-step line, finished objective last with its
badge, rows on one left edge and width, desktop and phone width; screenshots before and after), `tests/js/workwords.test.js`;
`BotsLiveUITests` on the throwaway daemon seeded with `--seed-work` (handed out on the order row, judged by and next step and
the finished objective on the objective rows, "From → To" and "<name> is working on it" on the hand-off rows; rows measured),
`check_strings.sh`, DotEngine `swift test`.
