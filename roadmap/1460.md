### 1460. Dot for iPhone: the ring of a working agent turns slowly

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The owner, after using the build on his phone "Proma" (2026-11-24, 08:47): "when Dot works,
the animation at the top spins far too fast". The ring round a working agent's avatar (item 1380's design, item 1394's fix that
it turns every time a chat goes back to work) turned once in 1.4 s, the pace of a busy indicator, on the agents strip and in
the chat list's rows, always in sight.

What changed: it turns once in 4 s, evenly. `Core/RingMotion.swift` holds the one number, `RingMotion.secondsPerTurn`, so that it
is one line to change again, and `RingMotion.turns(state:reduceMotion:)`, the rule that only a working ring turns and that Reduce
Motion leaves it drawn and still (an amber ring that waits for the owner never turns). `StateRing` (Features/Theme.swift) reads
both: the animation is `.linear(duration: RingMotion.secondsPerTurn).repeatForever(autoreverses: false)`, still driven from
`onAppear`, so a ring that left with the state and comes back starts again from the top (item 1394's fix stays), and it follows
Reduce Motion while it turns. The soft tail is the angular gradient the ring already had (transparent, 10 % and the full accent
over the circle). `ThreadState` and `ThreadSummary.state` moved to `Core/ThreadState.swift` so that the rule is pure.

Checked: `ios/DotTests/RingMotionTests.swift` (4 cases: four seconds a turn, only a working ring turns, Reduce Motion leaves it
still, a chat row stands as its run's status does). `xcodebuild test` on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5), the whole
suite: 377 tests, 0 failures, this item's 4 new cases among them (373 before it).
Not checked: the pace on a phone against the owner's eye (4 s is the brief's figure; the constant is one line); the Reduce Motion
case in the simulator's setting (the rule is tested, the view reads the environment value).
