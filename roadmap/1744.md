### 1744. A click that names nothing it expects is checked by whether the page reacted

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The market research of 2026-11-24 (M4, topics 1 and 3, its first of ten) and the
community's first complaint ("says it did it, didn't") ask that Dot, not the model, check what an acting step did.

**Measured before**, read-only in the owner's store, the 14 days to 2026-11-24: 1,150 browser steps went through
(`done`). 764 of them (66 %) carried `[not checked]`, 357 a verified check and 29 an uncertain one. By kind: 663 of
716 clicks (93 %) and 77 of 77 presses (100 %) were not checked, while what a step writes was (fill, insert, type: 2 to
6 % not checked, item 1145). The research's 79 % (227 of 286, 2026-11-18) is the same finding on an older window. No
`desktop_act` step ran in 30 days, so the desktop half is not measured and not changed here.

**What must hold.** No model call and no turn of the model for the check; no wait of its own (item 739 already watches
a click, a double click, a click or drag at a point and Enter for half a second at most, at 0.1 s polls, and returns at
the first change); a check never says more than what was seen; nothing a step expects or writes is judged otherwise
than before; the script retry of a click that changed nothing (item 781) still runs.

**Built** (`browser/session.py`, `core/outcomes.py`, `tools/browser.py`, `tools/desktop.py`, `tools/base.py`,
`agent/executor.py`):

- `_effect` returns what changed with its words (`_Effect`): `_what_moved` names the weightiest change of the same
  looks (a new address; a tab, dialog or download; a request; the page's content; for a click at a point the keyboard
  in a field), and a click on a field that keeps the keyboard is one too.
- A step with no check of its own (nothing written read back, nothing it expects) gets `the page reacted to the step`:
  verified with what changed, or uncertain when nothing did, never contradicted. A request or a new address seen after
  the half second (the step's own wait for its answer, item 1158) turns it verified. A step that named what it expects
  is judged by that alone, as item 1164 holds. The check does not hold a batch back (`holds_back` reads the step's own
  checks and sights as before).
- Item 1158 said that a 200 is no check, and it still is not: the check's condition is that the page reacted, and its
  evidence `it sent a request`, not that the order went through. Its smoke test now expects that check.
- A page that changes by itself is no witness (the pre-push review): Dot's watch of a page's changes now starts with
  each view it reads (`_capture`, beside the read, nothing kept) and keeps when the page last changed
  (`Pulse.quiet_ms`). A page that changed after Dot's last look (a view, or the end of a step's watch:
  `Tab.looked_at`) and before the step (`changed_by_itself`) has a change of its content or a request after the step
  judged uncertain; a new address, tab or dialog, the keyboard in a field, or a non-read request to its own site
  (item 1158) still counts.
- The tally counts steps, not checks: a step is verified when all its checks held, contradicted when one was refuted.
- The script retry skips a click only for a check other than the reaction.
- An act call's result carries `tally` (`StepTally`: verified, contradicted, uncertain, not checked, failed), stored in
  its `tool_result` event, for the run's account (item 1746). The desktop's act carries it too.

**Measured after**, on the same base commit, `main` against this branch, in throwaway homes with a headless Chrome:

- 40 clicks of each kind that name nothing they expect, as 93 % of the owner's clicks do: before, 160 of 160 not
  checked; after, 0 not checked (120 verified, the 40 that did nothing uncertain). Time of the act call, p50 / p95:
  a click that changes the page 135 / 214 ms before, 151 / 243 after; one that sends a request 135 / 239 and 147 / 249;
  one that does nothing 659 / 698 and 676 / 867; a field clicked 657 / 714 and 681 / 875. The added p50 is 12 to 24 ms
  and the added p95 10 to 169 ms, within the 30 and 300 ms the lead set; the change adds no wait or read of its own
  (the same looks are kept), so the difference is the Mac's load (other helpers ran their gates meanwhile), not the
  check.
- Again after the pre-push review's fix (the watch started with each view): 144 / 239, 141 / 253, 674 / 761 and
  682 / 785 ms, 5 to 25 ms more at p50 and 14 to 71 ms at p95 than `main`; the view's own time was not timed.
- The browser tasks of the eval sets (speed and state, 7 tasks, 3 rounds of their references by a scripted model):
  21 of 21 steps checked before and after, since every reference names what it expects; act p50 2.42 s before,
  1.91 s after (noise). Whether the check saves a model retries or re-reads cannot be measured with a scripted model,
  which sends the same calls whatever a result says.
- A field clicked takes 0.66 s before and after: item 739 looks at the field's keyboard only after the half second.
  Left as it was.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_browser_effect.py` (a click that changes the page, one that sends a request and a field
clicked are verified with what changed; one that does nothing is uncertain), `tests/smoke/test_browser_verified.py`
(item 1158's click carries the reaction; item 1164's steps carry none; a click on a page with a running clock is
uncertain), `tests/unit/test_outcomes.py` (the tally),
`dotx eval check`, `tests/unit/test_freedom_contract.py`.

**Not done.** The desktop: no act ran there in 30 days. A key other than Enter (Tab, Escape, arrows) stays unchecked:
item 739 does not watch it. Whether the check cuts a model's retries and re-reads needs runs of a real model; a scripted
model replays the same calls whatever the result says.
