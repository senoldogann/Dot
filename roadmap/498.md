### 498. Twelve more services in Connect apps

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Asked by the owner after Grok Bot's Connect apps, with items 492 and 493: more of the
services an owner works with, each added in one step.

**As built.**
- `mcp/catalogue.py` gains thirteen remote servers. Twelve sign in with the owner's account:
  - Mobbin, Semrush, Parallel Task and Plain;
  - MailerLite, Airtable, Klaviyo and Calendly;
  - Amplitude, Mixpanel, New Relic and PayPal.
  The thirteenth, Parallel Search, is open to all.
- Each one that signs in was checked on 2026-10-21 by GET alone, as Dot's client reads it. Its server's protected
  resource metadata and its authorization server's metadata offer dynamic client registration, a client without a
  secret (`none`) and PKCE with S256. Parallel's docs say its Search server needs no key.
- Left out:
  - Ahrefs: its metadata names no client without a secret.
  - Make and Pipedrive: only a client with a secret.
  - Datadog: its address is under `/api/unstable/` and serves one region.
  - Zoom: clients registered by hand, as its docs say.
  - Dropbox: registration for a few named clients only, as its docs say.
  - Plaid: a client-credentials token and no sign-in, as its docs say.
- `connections/directory.py` lists them in the sections where they belong:
  - code: New Relic;
  - design: Mobbin;
  - data: Airtable, Amplitude and Mixpanel;
  - research: Semrush, and Parallel with its two servers;
  - productivity: Calendly;
  - business: PayPal, Klaviyo and MailerLite;
  - support: Plain.
- The logos:
  - from gilbarbara/logos at the commit the others came from: Airtable, Amplitude, New Relic and PayPal;
  - Simple Icons' symbol in the brand's colour: Calendly, Mixpanel and Semrush;
  - letters for the rest.
  The folder's LICENSE names them.

**Tests.** The catalogue's and the directory's own: each listing's ways name catalogue entries, and each logo is a file
of the folder (`tests/unit/test_plugins_directory.py`). No sign-in was tried: that would register a client with each
service.
