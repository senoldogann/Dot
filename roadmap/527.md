### 527. Settings › Phone and Self-improvement read at a glance: a row of tiles, a bar of the steps done

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** Asked by the owner, with a screenshot of Settings › Phone: both pages were to look
tidier and nicer, Self-improvement as a better dashboard. The rules followed are the dataviz skill's: a row of stat
tiles (what it counts, the number, a line under it), and a status colour never alone, always with its words.

- **Self-improvement.** A row of four tiles leads the page (`overview` in `views/harness.js`, a group named
  *Overview*): what is in use (*Built-in*, or *A version* with its summary), the versions with how many are in each
  state, the nights of learning with the last night's outcome, when the next is or that the learning is off, over a
  strip of the last 14 nights (`nightStrip`: green a change, grey none, red a failure; a legend names each, a hover
  names the night and its outcome, and each cell is an image named so for a screen reader), and the lessons kept. The
  line under a number reads as two lines at most, and whole on a hover. The habits and the tool guidance in use stand
  side by side where the page is wide enough, *Versions* beside *Nightly learning*, the lessons in a grid; the form's
  fields stay folded until **Write a version** opens them (its button says whether they are, `aria-expanded`). On a
  phone the tiles go two to a row, and nothing reaches past the screen's edge.
- **Phone.** *Reach Dot from anywhere* has a bar of its three steps above them (a `progressbar`, *2 of 3 steps done
  on this Mac*); a step done has a tick and a green line down to the next, and the one to do now has its number marked
  in the accent. The keep-awake switch, a setting rather than a step, is set apart by a line. *Dot on your phone*
  numbers its steps in round marks, the pairing code is in a box of its own, and the QR codes sit on a white tile, so
  that a camera reads them in the dark theme too.
- No change to the API. `docs/harness.md` and `docs/phone.md` say what the pages show.

Checked by:
