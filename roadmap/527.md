### 527. Settings › Phone and Self-improvement read at a glance: a row of tiles, a bar of the steps done

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** Asked by the owner, with a screenshot of Settings › Phone: both pages were to look
tidier and nicer, Self-improvement as a better dashboard. The rules followed are the dataviz skill's: a row of stat
tiles (what it counts, the number, a line under it), and a status colour never alone, always with its words.

- **Self-improvement.** A row of four tiles leads the page (`overview` in `views/harness.js`, a group named
  *Overview*): what is in use (*Built-in*, or *A version* with its summary), the versions with how many are in each
  state, the steps of learning with the last one's outcome, when the next is or that the learning is off, over a
  strip of the newest 14 (`nightStrip`: green a change, grey none, red a failure; a legend names each, a hover names
  the step and its outcome, and the strip is a group whose cells are images named so for a screen reader), and the
  lessons kept. The line under a number reads as two lines at most, and whole on a hover. The habits and the tool
  guidance in use stand side by side where the page is wide enough, *Versions* beside *Nightly learning*, the lessons
  in a grid; the form's fields stay folded until **Write a version** opens them (its button says whether they are,
  `aria-expanded`). On a phone the tiles go two to a row.
- **Counted in all.** `/api/harness/steps` lists the newest 50 steps and `/experiences` the newest 200, so the tiles'
  numbers come from `GET /api/harness`, which counts them all (`steps_total`, `experiences_total`;
  `Store.count_harness_steps`, `count_harness_experiences`). A step does not say whether the night or `dotx harness
  step` started it, so the tile is *Learning steps*, not nights. The pre-push review found the tile stopped at 50.
- **Narrow phones.** A grid's least column width is `min(20rem, 100%)` (and 18rem, 12rem), as `app.css` already does
  elsewhere: a fixed 20rem reached past the edge of an iPhone mini (375 points) with a version in use, inside a pane
  that scrolls on its own, so the page's own width said nothing. The pre-push review found it.
- **Phone.** *Reach Dot from anywhere* has a bar of its three steps above them (a `progressbar`, *2 of 3 steps done
  on this Mac*); a step done has a tick and a green line down to the next, and the one to do now has its number marked
  in the accent. The keep-awake switch, a setting rather than a step, is set apart by a line. *Dot on your phone*
  numbers its steps in round marks, the pairing code is in a box of its own, and the QR codes sit on a white tile
  (`--mark-ground`), so that a camera reads them in the dark theme too.
- Every size, radius, spacing and colour is a token (`tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py`). `docs/harness.md` and
  `docs/phone.md` say what the pages show.
