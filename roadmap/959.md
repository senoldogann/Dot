### 959. The logos are drawn inline, and Connect apps wears four

**Status: done (2026-11-03).** **Asked** by the owner on 2026-11-03: "every key I press in the composer, the icons in
Connect apps render again; fix it, and show at least three or four logos there".

**Found.** Measured in WebKit (Dot.app's engine) and Chromium on a throwaway home, typing on a chat's page: the marks'
nodes did not change and their files were not asked for again; what a key changes is the composer's own height (its
`style`, twice a key), which lays the page out and repaints it. Since item 946 a logo was its file's shape as a CSS mask
(`mask: var(--logo)`), and WebKit draws an SVG mask anew on a repaint, which blinked.

**Built.** `inked.js` draws an SVG file of Dot's own inline, every paint of it `currentColor` (`fill="none"` kept),
keeping only the elements that draw a shape (`svg`, `g`, the shapes, `defs`, gradients and their stops) and no attribute
that runs, reaches out or styles (`on*`, `href`, `style`, `class`), each file read once a page and cloned for each mark.
`marks.js` `logoShape` draws a service's logo with it (the shape keeps its file in `data-logo`), and `providers.js`
`providerMark` a model provider's mark, which the composer's model pill, the model menu, the helpers' cards, the agents'
list and Settings › Models wear (the review of item 959: they were masks too, in the composer itself); `app.css` has no
mask for either, the Claude mark keeping its colour as its ink. `sidebar.js`: Connect apps wears four logos (`CONNECTED_MARKS`), the ready services' first and, while fewer are
ready, those of Google, GitHub, Slack and Notion (`OFFERED_LOGOS`).

**Checked.** A probe in WebKit: no change of the marks and no request of their files while typing. Screenshots of the
sidebar's foot and of Customize › Connectors in WebKit. `tests/smoke/test_customize_connectors.py` (a logo inline in the
tile's ink, no mask), `test_connect_apps.py` (four marks, the ready service's first), `test_plugin_gallery_page.py`,
`test_composer_picks.py`, `test_composer.py` (the model pill's mark inline, no mask), `test_providers.py`,
`test_model_picker.py`.
