### 563. Settings › Models and Providers follow the design

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** The first two screens of the owner's Claude Design redraw of the settings (item 562 is the
frame), drawn from the PDF's pages 1 and 2 with everything the tabs did before.

**As built.**
- **Models** (`views/system.js`). The head says "Which model does each job, and what a run falls back on when its model
  can't answer.", holds at its right how many providers and models there are (and the badges "N without a key" and "N
  unavailable") and the button **Ask providers again**. Three sections:
  - **Roles**: one card, a row for the default, fast, review and vision models (the title, the muted line, a select at the
    right in the mono face with the model's provider in its box, before the chevron: `.model-pick`). The selects, their
    fits and their pick-by-pick sending are as before; the rows keep their `models-default` and `models-role` classes.
  - **Fallbacks** (the standby models of item 478): a numbered row for each model in the order a run tries them (the
    model in the mono face, its provider, "no key" and "not listed now" badges, an up and a down arrow, ×), "None: a run
    whose model cannot answer ends with the error." when there are none, and the row **+ Add a fallback model** in the
    accent colour, with the select lying over it, clear, so that a press anywhere on the row opens its menu.
  - **Every model, by provider**: a search ("Search 13 models") over the folds of the providers, each fold a row with its
    models' count at its right; the table inside (context size, reasoning, the Why row, the asked model of
    `?model=<ref>`) is as before. A model the search leaves out is out of sight, and a provider with none; a fold that
    holds a match shows it while something is typed and goes back to how it was left once the search is cleared.
- **Providers** (`views/keys.js`). One card with the providers down its left side (each on a white tile with its logo, as
  the services' marks are, the tail of its key `••••VwcA`, a dot that says whether it is signed in) and the one chosen
  beside them: its tile, its name, "✓ Connected · the GPT models" in green (for an optional provider "Connected ·
  optional", and its longer line under **Key**), the check button. Sections **Key** (the page where a key is made at its
  right, the key in a box of its own: the variable, the tail, where it comes from at the right, or "not set"; **Replace
  key** or **Add key**, **Remove**) and **Models N** with a filter, the first eight models in the mono face and **Show
  all N**; **Typed judge** for TypeSafe as before. The two notes under the card wear a lock and an info mark.
- `settings.css`: `.model-pick`, the standby rows (`.standby-order`, `.standby-add`), the models' search and folds, the
  Providers card; a card's children that are not rows are parted by a hairline; the head's right side wraps on a narrow
  screen (a head with a meta and a button ran 104 px past a 320 px screen).
- `docs/models.md` and `docs/daemon.md` say it.

**Kept that the design does not draw:** the models' tables with their context-size and reasoning controls, the "Why" rows
and the measured evidence, the problem badges and notes of a provider (no key, key refused, access refused, not
installed), the deep link of the context meter, the typed judge's switch, the key check, the forms and flows of adding,
replacing and removing a key, the environment-key warning, and the real marks of the providers (the design draws
initials).

**Tests.** `test_standby_models.py`, `test_role_models.py`, `test_context_meter.py` and `test_providers.py` follow the new
words and classes (the card is gone; the fallbacks' select is named "Add a fallback model"; the key section is named
Key). Also run: `test_chat_choices.py`, `test_model_picker.py`, `test_provider_problems.py`, `test_setup_card.py`,
`test_context_sizes.py`, `test_judge_switch.py`, `test_tour.py`, `test_phone_fit.py` and the phone settings tests.
