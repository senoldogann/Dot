### 1801. DotNext's chat settings: the model, its level, fast mode, approvals and plan first, also before a new chat

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** The owner's shots of 2026-11-25 (PLAN §24, `owner-refs/ref-model-picker-1/2.png`) and his
test (§23: "choose the model and effort before sending"); §25: the composer's picker carries mode, effort and fast.

Before: the composer's chip was a menu of every model × level pair; a new chat could not choose anything and started on
the daemon's model, level and approvals (`POST /api/runs` with `mode` only).

What must hold: the picks reach the run, read in its record (`model`, `effort`, `speed`, `approvals`); for a chat that
exists they are its choices (`PATCH /api/threads/{id}`, item 199) as on the web; for a new chat they go with its first
message (`StartRunRequest`'s own fields, no daemon change); an outbox item kept by a build before this still reads.

What was built:
- `App/ChatSettingsSheet.swift`: the sheet "Chat settings" (✕ and ✓, a drag does not throw away picks): the providers as
  groups that fold, with their mark (the web's `static/icons/providers`, as template images) and their count when
  folded; each model a row with a **Default** badge (the daemon's main model, `GET /api/status`), a ✓ on the chosen one
  and a ☆ (favourites float up, kept on the phone as the web keeps them in the browser); a floating **Find a model**
  with a filter (all, favourites, free); **Options**: Reasoning (the model's levels, or its default), Fast mode (only on
  a model that has it), Approvals (Dot's setting, supervised, auto-accept edits, auto, ask only to pay or send; free mode
  is the owner's switch for all of Dot and is said so), Plan first (the next message); **Catalog**: Manage hidden
  models (an eye on each model). Only models that can call tools, as the web's picker.
- `Components/ModelCatalog.swift`: the app's one model catalog (the recent models, the caller's options, the folding
  provider groups, ✓, ☆, the eye of hidden models) and `ModelSearchBar`; every model picker is built on it (Settings ›
  Models' picker of helper S1, PR #1111, moves onto it once that is merged).
- `App/ChatSettingsRules.swift`: the pure rules (groups, order, hidden, search, filter, what a pick does to the level and
  the speed) with `Tests/ChatSettingsAndVoiceTests.swift`.
- The composer's chip opens the sheet; a new chat (`NewChatSheet`) is a clean canvas with the composer and the model in
  the bar and in the composer; its picks go with the first message.
- DotEngine: `ModelOption` reads `fast_mode`, `tools`, `free`; `ChatChoices.with(speed:)`, `with(approvals:)`;
  `ChatStart` and `NewChatMessage` carry model, effort, speed, approvals and plan first; `OutboxItem.newChat` holds
  them (`start`, nil in an older item); tests in `ChatStartTests.swift`.

Checked: the engine tests (the body of `POST /api/runs` with and without picks, an older outbox item); the fixture UI
tests (`ChatSettingsUITests`); `ChatListLiveUITests.testANewChatRunsOnTheSettingsPickedBeforeItsFirstMessage` against a
throwaway daemon on a real model (the owner's Claude plan), whose run record `e2e_chats.sh` reads.
