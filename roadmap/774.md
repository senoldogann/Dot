### 774. A wait for a text ends once the page stands still

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Measured** over 30 days of the owner's runs: 106 `browser_wait_text` calls, 64 saw
their text (half within 0.4 s, 60 within 5 s, none after 10 s) and 42 did not, lasting their whole time, 20 s at the
median and 899 s in all: mostly a thank-you text the model guessed after a form that had not gone (the review of
2026-10-27). The owner's run `run_mvrbj1il_f72dc4` lost 13 s so on two of them.

- A wait that has gone on for 5 s ends once the page has stood still for 3 s: no change in its documents (the change
  count of item 739, read in the first ten frames), no navigation under way, no request begun since the last look, none
  of its own requests (fetch, XHR, an EventSource's stream) under way and no WebSocket open, which the tab counts now
  (the pre-push review: a page that polls its server with short requests is rarely seen with one under way). It fails as
  a wait that timed out does, shows the page, and says why: "the page has not changed for 3 s and waits on nothing: as
  it stands the text is not coming". A page that keeps changing, waits on its server or polls it is waited for to the
  end; one whose changes cannot be read is never taken for still.
- The wait is made of half-second waits, each ending by its own time, so that none is left waiting in the page; whether
  a dialog was open is read once, at the start, so that one opening between two of them still ends the wait (item 698).
- The 42 waits of the measure would have lasted about 5 to 6 s each instead of 20.

**The change.** `browser/session.py` (`_text_or_still`), `browser/driver.py` (`no_dialog_open`, `watch_dialogs`),
`browser/pages.py` (`Tab.fetches`, `Tab.sockets`), `browser/types.py` (`TextWait.still_s`), `browser/render.py`,
docs/browser-and-computer-use.md.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_browser.py` (headless Chrome on a local site): a text that does not come on a still page
ends the wait after 5 to 7.5 s of its 20 and says why; a page that changes every 300 ms is waited for its whole 6.5 s,
one whose request the server answers after 7 s sees its text then, and one that asks its server every 1.5 s sees its
text at the fourth answer, after 6 s; a WebSocket is counted, not tried (the test site has none); the waits of items 698
and 554 as before.
