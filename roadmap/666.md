### 666. A task taught once does itself

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** The owner asked for the whole of "show a bot how it's done" as another agent app has it:
it follows along as the owner works once, saves that as a routine, and runs it on its own next time. Dot's Teach a
task (item 423) recorded the steps and wrote a skill, and Run it now ran it once; making it recur was a schedule the
owner set up apart, which knew nothing of the skill.

**The change.**
- A schedule's options carry `skills` (`store/models.py`, a `skills` column of `schedules`, a JSON list, none for every
  schedule before), and a firing's run starts with them picked (`scheduler/runner.py`, `agent/manager.py`
  `_launch_firing`), as a message with skills picked in the composer starts. An edit on the Schedules page, whose form
  has no field for them, keeps them (`routes_data.py`).
- `teach.py` `repeat_taught_skill` and `POST /api/teach/<chat>/repeat`: a task schedule in the chat, its goal the
  skill's line of what it does, the chat's choices as a schedule a run of the chat makes has them
  (`tools/schedule.py` `chat_options`), the skill picked. Only a skill the owner taught repeats so.
- The teach panel (`teach.js`): **Repeat it by itself** once the skill is saved, every day, on weekdays or every week on
  a day, at a time (`rhythm.js` `repeatCron`), and a line that says when in words and leads to the schedule
  (`#/schedules?schedule=ID`, item 665). The Schedules page marks such a row **Taught task**.
- `docs/browser-and-computer-use.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_taught_repeat.py`: the route makes the schedule in the chat with the skill and the chat's choices;
  an edit keeps the skill; its run's first request holds the skill's steps; an unknown task, a missing chat and a bad
  rhythm make nothing.
- `tests/smoke/test_teach_repeat_panel.py`: in Chrome, wide and at a phone's width, the saved task repeats every week on
  Monday at 08:30 from the panel, which says so and leads to the row marked Taught task.
- `tests/js/rhythm.test.js`: the crons of the rhythms, said back in words.
- `tests/smoke/test_teach.py` gives its stand-in roles `after_review` (item 649 asks it of them), which it lacked on
  `main`.
