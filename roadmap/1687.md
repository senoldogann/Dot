### 1687. Smoke tests of the phone's sync contract on a served daemon

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Item 1686's contract held end to end, on a real uvicorn server in a throw-away home with
the daemon's revision watch running and no model called (`tests/smoke/test_thread_sync.py`):

- A chat with a finished run, a helper at work in a chat of its own with a stored step and an ask, and a held queue with
  a message: its view holds each (the helper's title, provider, model, effort, step, stop route and that its parent
  waits; its ask, the queue, the newest run's page and events) at a
  revision, and the list's row has that revision and one ask. On the list's stream, a rename and a pin through `PATCH`,
  a rename written on a second connection to the database (as a `dotx` command writes, no bus of the daemon hears it)
  and the helper's end in its own chat each come as `thread_changed` of the chat with a larger revision; the view read
  after has the last one, the other process's title and no step for the helper. A chat deleted with `DELETE
  /api/threads` comes as `thread_changed` with `deleted: true`, and its view is 404.
- A chat's stream opened with a cursor sends what is past it and `synchronized` with the run's newest id; a run that
  starts later in the chat and the helper it starts in another chat come on the same stream, and so does an event
  stored first and told after a later event of the helper (an id lower than one sent); a rename comes as
  `thread_changed`. No id comes twice on one stream. After a break, while three events were stored (one told on no
  bus), the stream opened again from the client's cursors brings all three; what comes again is only what the client
  held; every stored event of the two runs and the helper came.
- A stream left unread while a run starts in the chat and starts a helper, and another chat's run fills its queue
  (the bus drops events, the starts among them): the run's and the helper's next steps still come on it. Pages of
  one run each, over three runs made at the same instant, hold each run once. Both failed before the second pre-push
  review's fixes.
