### 1288. A human check is found and passed in one call, and waited for no longer than it takes

**Status: done (2026-11-21).** The owner's word of 2026-11-21: "captcha ve diğer doğrulama geçişlerinde daha hızlı
olsun, ajan çok beklemeler yaşıyor; bunları daha akıllıca ve hızlı geçebilsin." The skill said to take a screenshot,
guess at the pixel of the checkbox in it, click and then sleep, all of it model round trips, and a site's own
"Press & Hold" was never passed at all: a click lets go before the hold has run, so the modal stays and the run
either clicked again or asked. A page that was still checking itself ("Just a moment...", Turnstile's spinner) was
waited for by a fixed sleep.

`browser/human_check.py` is one call that does what the three took. It reads the DOM of every frame (`frame.evaluate`,
so a cross-origin widget is read, not guessed at): the hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA and Cloudflare Turnstile anchors and their
`<iframe>`s, the challenge's own prompt, its grid and the `-33`/`-44` in its class that names the tile count, its
Verify / Next button, a site's own pressable button that says "Press & Hold", "I'm not a robot" or "Verify you are
human", and the site's token field (`g-recaptcha-response`, `h-captcha-response`, `cf-turnstile-response`) whose value
says the check is behind the page. A box is in CSS pixels of the main frame's viewport, which is what `click_at` and
`drag_at` take, and a frame's boxes are moved by where the frame sits.

What stands in front is one of five: a challenge (needs eyes), a hold (a button held down with the pointer, jiggled
once so the widget sees it move, and let go the moment the widget stops asking), a checkbox (one press, then the
state read again), a page still checking itself (polled), or nothing. The poll is every 120 ms - the page's own state,
not a sleep - and it ends the moment the check clears: the widget gone, its box checked, the token set, or the
widget's own words saying success. A check that needs eyes comes back as its own picture at **twice** its size
(`core/images.crop_scaled`, clipping a widget at the edge of the viewport), the box it came from, and the Verify box,
so the tile centres are arithmetic and no second screenshot is taken.

Every press the tool would make is weighed first, with the pixel it would land on, by the elements a snapshot shows
under it as a `click_at` of `browser_act` is (item 1252), by the names of those elements rather than by the typed judge
`browser_act` adds for a label no pattern knows - a check's own words explain the press. A payment button that a page
labels like a check is refused, and the run is told to press it with `browser_act`, which asks the user; the tool holds
the browser's lock while it works, so it may never wait for an approval inside it. A "Verify you are human" line does
not press the button drawn beside it, and only a hold, whose bar is often drawn beside its own words, takes a pressable
found there, and only one that says nothing of its own or says the same words. A widget's own in-progress word
("Holding...") still counts as the check asking, so a hold is not broken by the text it changes to. A widget someone
else draws (hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA, Turnstile) stands in front of a page's own words.

`browser/human_check.py` (`find_checks`, `pass_human_check`), `browser/types.py` (`CheckBox`, `HumanCheckOutcome`),
`browser/session.py` (`human_check`, which reads the page again after a pass), `core/images.py` (`crop_scaled`),
`tools/browser.py` (`browser_captcha`, `render_human_check`, `_refuse_a_flagged_press`, next to `browser_act` in the list), the
system prompt in both styles, `skills/builtin/captcha/SKILL.md`, `docs/captcha.md` and
`docs/browser-and-computer-use.md`. The context budget is raised by 300 and the tools' by 400, with their reasons.

Measured on a real headless Chrome against local pages: a "Press & Hold" passed in 1.6 s where it never passed
before, a cross-origin checkbox in 0.6 s, a page checking itself in 1.4 s instead of the fixed wait, an image
challenge handed back in 0.05 s with its picture at 620 by 620, and a page with no check answered in 0.00 s.
