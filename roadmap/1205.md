### 1205. The neutral look takes OpenMausBot's greys, rings instead of shadows, and its bubbles, composer and motion

**Status: done (2026-11-16).** The owner asked for OpenMausBot's design (an open Apache-2.0 clone of Grok's app) in
Dot. A comparison of the two (exact values, file and line in both) found that most of what the study of 2026-10-31
took had never landed: the neutral look was still warm (`#1b1a18`, an off-white close to cream in light), boxes and
the composer had shadows and a 3 px focus halo, bubbles were 20 px with no grouping, and the easing was Material's.
This item takes the CSS part of it (the report's items 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 12 and 16 in part); no brand (logo, name,
mascot) is taken.

- `css/themes.css`: the neutral look on Midnight's and Daylight's greys (dark `#121212` page, `#0e0e0e` sidebar,
  `#1f1f1f` surface; light `#fcfcfc`, `#f5f5f5`, white), neutral inks, `--edge` (a tenth of the text) and
  `--edge-strong`; a phone in the neutral look takes the phone's own dark greys (`#141414`, `#1c1c1e`, `#242426`…).
  Paper's faint ink is a step darker so that it reads at 4.5:1. The other looks keep their colours.
- `css/app.css`: a box is drawn by a ring of half a pixel rather than a shadow; the composer by a ring that grows to
  22 % under the pointer and in focus, with no halo, on 22 px that closes to 18 once its text wraps; your bubble on
  18 px with 7 by 12 px of room, two in a row on a touch screen joined at a 6 px corner and 4 px apart, a message just
  sent rising 4 px in 180 ms; one easing `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; a menu on 12 px (22 on a phone) that opens
  from 96 %, its rows 30 px on 6 px with icons of a 1.5 stroke (1.75 elsewhere); sheets rise 32 px in 260 ms; a
  phone's cards on 18 px and its sheets on 28; bold in an answer 600 and paragraphs with `text-wrap: pretty`. Reduced
  motion keeps only fades.
- `css/shell.css`: the open row of the sidebar and the open tab of Settings are a flat tint of the ink with no shadow;
  a chat's name and last line fade out over their last 28 px instead of an ellipsis.
- `css/run.css`: the phone's chat settings sheet (item 1202) rises as the message sheet does.
- `js/composer.js` marks the frame `data-multiline` when the box's text wraps; `js/messages.js` marks a message sent
  in the last 5 seconds `fresh`, so that one opened later stands still.
- `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py`: the muted and the faint tiers read at 4.5:1 on every surface (3:1 before), and the
  phone's greys are measured too. `docs/daemon.md` and `docs/phone.md` say so.

Not taken, and why: Dot's answers stay plain text on a Mac (item 854); the grouped corners only on a touch screen,
where a message's foot is hidden (a Mac shows each message's time and buttons under it, which part them); the
palettes of Settings › Appearance keep their quiet tiers at 3:1 (`appearance/tokens.js`), as raising them changes
every palette's colours and is a choice of its own; a phone's menu rows keep Dot's 36 px rather than 40; the
approval cards, the code block's copy plate, the quiet-run line and the gap stamps are the report's next items.

Checked: `tests/unit` (the contrast of every tier on every surface, the phone's greys too), `npm run check`, the
browser tests of the phone, the composer, the menus, the sidebar, the message sheet, the themes and the icons;
screenshots of the home, a chat, the composer with wrapped text, the sidebar or a chat's menu and Settings at 1280 px
in Chromium and 402 px in WebKit as a phone, dark and light, before and after.
