### 564. Settings › Browser and System follow the design

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** Pages 3 and 8 of the owner's Claude Design redraw of the settings (item 562 is the frame),
with everything the two tabs did before.

**As built.**
- **Browser** (`views/system.js`). The head says "Which Chrome Dot browses with, the pages it opens, and how it behaves
  while a run works." and holds **Look again**. Sections, each a name over a card of rows (`setSection`, `setRow`,
  `switchRowSetting`, `choiceRowSetting`):
  - **Where it browses**: Runs may use the browser (switch), Browse with (Own Chrome / My Chrome), Sign in to sites in
    Dot's Chrome (the button; the row shows in Own Chrome only).
  - **Profiles** (Own Chrome only): the lead, **+ Add profile** in the accent at the right of the name, and one card of
    rows: a dot (filled for the profile in use), the name, an "In use" pill (new `.badge.b-accent`), and Use, Clear or
    Delete at the right; Incognito says "Keeps nothing once Dot's Chrome closes" in its row.
  - **The page it opens** (size, appearance, zoom), **While a run works** (three switches) and **Developer** (the
    DevTools port in a small mono field, its explanation the row's line).
  - Kept below, as the design does not draw them: **How Chrome stands** (Chrome, remote debugging, agent connection,
    attach timeout) and **Let Dot join your Chrome** (the steps with the copyable `chrome://inspect` address). What Dot
    says of the moment (your Chrome's connection, the sign-in window, the wait for Allow, a refusal) stands over the
    sections while it holds (`.set-notices`, no room when it has nothing to say).
- **System** (`views/system.js`). The head keeps its line. At the top three tiles (`.set-tiles`): **Daemon** (a dot and
  "Running", "Up 3h 12m", the refresh button), **Permissions** (from the card below: "All granted" with what they are, or
  what is missing) and **Health** ("35 of 37 ok", "2 optional checks to look at", or the failing ones). The **Emergency
  stop** is a card of its own (the power mark in red, its state "Standby" or "Engaged", **Stop everything**, or
  **Release** once it is engaged). **Health checks** is a section: **Needs a look** first (name, what it found, the pill
  "optional" or "failing"), then the checks that passed in groups a fold each (setup, desktop control, providers and
  models, browser, memory, tools and plugins, other; the groups are told by the checks' names), the state of a group at
  its right ("✓ all ok", "N to look at"), a filter All / Needs a look and **Run again**. Below them, as before: the
  daemon's facts in four groups with Go on after a restart, the permissions (Ask macOS, the panes, Restart Dot) and About
  this Dot (Diagnostics and Open source licences), each a fold that stays as the reader left it.
- `settings.css`: the profiles, `.set-link`, `.set-notices`, the tiles, the emergency stop card, the health groups; a
  section's head wraps on a narrow screen.
- `docs/browser-and-computer-use.md` and `docs/daemon.md` say it.

**Tests.** `test_browser_settings.py` (the sections' names), `test_browser_attach.py`, `test_browser_sign_in.py` and
`test_devtools_port.py` (the page, not a card, holds the rows), and `test_about_pages.py` (the three folds that stay, the
emergency stop and the health checks as a card and a section, the tiles).

Not built: the health groups are told by the checks' names, so a check the doctor adds later lands in Other until it is
