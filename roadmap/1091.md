### 1091. A chat at work reads as a messaging app's on a phone

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** Asked by the owner, with screenshots of another assistant on the iPhone: its line under
the last message says what it does ("Dot reposunu arıyor"), "••• Yazıyor…" while it writes, the messages along the
way each in its own bubble, and the answer written in as it comes. The owner chose all four, the bubbles on a phone
only (item 854 had taken them away everywhere) and the dots in the face's place (item 971 had taken them from after
the words).

Before (screenshots of a scripted chat at 390 px): the line said "Thinking…", "Running a command…", "Working in the
terminal…"; the messages along the way were plain text; the answer already streamed with a caret.

Now (`workstatus.js`, `run.css`):
- A command, a file, a search or a page is said with what it is about, from the call's main argument ("Running gh repo
  view…", "Reading README.md…", "Searching the web for “Dot”…", "Reading github.com…"), one line cut at its end; other
  work keeps its phrases.
- While the run waits for its model (from its start, and once the last of a step's calls came back, its read-only calls
  running at once: `model_call` is told once an answer came) the line is three dots that rise in turn in the face's
  place and "Typing…"; the model's streamed thinking is "Thinking…" beside the face. A delta of spaces alone does not
  hide the line.
- On a phone's width (720 px or less) the answer and each line along the way sit in a soft bubble (`--msg-agent`); a
  Mac's window keeps them plain. A step whose model has sent only spaces holds no empty bubble: `timeline.js` marks it
  `data-blank` until it has a block (a CSS `:has()` inside a `:has()`, which browsers drop, was the pre-push review's
  find).

Checked: phone screenshots of a scripted chat before and after (typing, "Running sleep 4…", the bubble, the answer
growing word by word in its bubble), `tests/smoke/test_work_status.py` (the command's words, no dots while it runs, the
bubbles on a phone), the browser tests of the messages and the stream and `test_phone_*` (67 passed; the one failure,
`test_phone_return`'s copy of an earlier build, is the known flaky one), `npm run check`.
