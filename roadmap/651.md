### 651. A page a run shows is part of the answer

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner asked why the pages that the coding app they use beside Dot shows in a chat
look so good, and to take what fits. That app's own open code was read; it has:
- the same MCP Apps messages as Dot's `show_page` (item 193), the same base sheet and theme variables;
- a page shown with no title or box round it, on the thread's ground;
- heights measured ahead, so nothing jumps, and a page fitted whole up to 20,000 pixels;
- full screen inside the app;
- a contract for the agent: no outer card, inner boxes padded 16px with `var(--radius)`, no `vh`, and a screenshot
  before publishing.

Dot already had the frame. What it lacked is this item.

**The change.**
- `pageframe.js` and `app.css`:
  - the title row and the bordered box are gone, and the page sits on the conversation's ground, named by the card's
    `aria-label`;
  - Full screen and Open in the browser sit over its corner, on hover or focus, always on a touch screen, and hidden
    when the page was closed for loading another;
  - Full screen is a dialog of the UI, not a new tab, so an iPhone stays in Dot.
- The height a page took is kept in the browser, by page and by 40 px of width, for the last 100 pages. A page shown
  again stands at its height before it loads.
  - That app draws every page in headless Chrome at nine widths when it is published; Dot draws nothing more.
  - Nothing goes to the daemon, so no new route.
- A page still stops at 2,000 pixels and scrolls inside past them. Fitting a page whole up to 20,000 pixels, as that
  app does, was built and taken out after three reviews. A page in `vh` grows with its frame, and none of these could
  tell it from a page that builds itself without a case that went wrong (a frame out of sight, a phone under load):
  - a count of its growths;
  - their timing;
  - asking the page by growing the frame once.

  It waits for a signal of the page's own, such as the bootstrap saying the page's height follows its viewport.
- `tools/pages.py`: the tool tells the run the new contract:
  - part of the answer, with no title over it and no box;
  - no background, border or side padding outside;
  - 16 px padding and `var(--radius)` for a box of its own;
  - why a `vh` height grows the page;
  - to keep the top right corner free of controls, since Full screen and Open sit there, always on a phone.

  In place of that app's preview tool, it asks the run to open the page's address with its browser at a phone's width
  and the conversation's (item 601), and to show it again if something is cut.
- `docs/runs.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_shown_pages.py`, at 1280 and 390, light and dark:
  - the card has no caption and no border;
  - the browser keeps the height the frame took;
  - Full screen opens the page in a dialog, still reaching nothing of Dot's, and Escape closes it.
  - A page of 3,000 pixels and one of `100vh` both stop at 2,000.
- `tests/smoke/test_thread_turns.py`: an earlier turn's page is named by its card, has no caption and has Full
  screen. The review of the first push found it still looking for the title row.
  - The first run caught the corner's buttons covering Show it again.
