### 1285. A site whose certificate does not verify is not told as a failure in transit

**Status: done (2026-11-21).** Dot's own record showed `web_fetch: the request failed in transit` four times, in two
runs of 2026-11-20 that looked for events in Oulu. Each was a `ConnectError` from `SSLCertVerificationError`:
`startupoulu.com`, `www.startupoulu.com` and `duuniexpo.fi` give a certificate that does not cover their name (curl
refuses them alike). The sites are the cause, but the web client counted a certificate that does not verify among the
failures in transit (`WebTransportError`, "trying again can help"): the GET was sent a second time for nothing, and the
run was told it was a passing failure, so it tried the same host again under another name.

What changed: a connection whose certificate does not verify raises a plain `WebError` ("the site's TLS certificate
does not verify, so the page was not read; another try will not help"), with the TLS error in its context. It is not
retried and is not counted as a failure in transit. A handshake the site closes (item 960) and the other failures in
transit are unchanged.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_web_retry.py`, a GET whose connection fails on a hostname mismatch is sent once and raises a
`WebError` that is not a `WebTransportError` (it failed before the change); `tests/smoke/test_web.py` and
`tests/smoke/test_web_fetch.py` pass; a fetch of `https://duuniexpo.fi/` with the client now gives the new error at
once.

Log:
- 2026-11-21: a certificate that does not verify is its own error, not retried.
