### 1099. On a phone an answer's bubble grows with its words

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** Asked by the owner with a screenshot: on the phone an answer that was being written in
stood as a bubble most of the screen tall, "Token hazır —" and the caret at its top and nothing under them.

Cause: an answer that ends under the reader's eyes is written in a few words a frame (item 366), and kept the room it
will take from the start, so that the page lands on it as it will stand. That room was set on its words (`.prose`'s
`min-height`); since item 1091 the words are a bubble on a phone, so the bubble stood at its full height, empty, until
the last word came.

Now the room is set on the answer, which the bubble is in (`revealAnswer` in `timeline.js`), and taken off once the
words are all in: the page still lands where the answer will stand, and the bubble grows with its words.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_answer_streams.py`, a new test at 390 × 844 (the bubble's height while the words come is
under half its last; on the old code it was 733 px from the first word to the last), and the two tests before it.
