### 731. Dot's Chrome closes after half an hour unused

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** Item 454 made Dot's own Chrome able to close once unused (`[browser] idle_close_s`) and
left it off, the owner's to set; the owner's word then was that what Dot opens should close once the work is done.
Measured on the owner's Mac on 2026-10-27, with no run at work: Dot's Chrome held 14 processes and about 600 MB
resident (8 renderers).

**The change.** `idle_close_s` is 1800 by default, `tab_idle_s`: Chrome and its driver close once nothing used them for
half an hour, when the chats' own tabs close anyway, so no chat's page closes sooner than before; a page the owner
opened in Dot's Chrome and left behind its window now goes with it, unless that window is in front. The next call that needs it
starts it again on the same profile, signed in (item 453). 0 keeps it open as before.

**Checked.** `test_browser.py`'s test of item 454 (closing once unused, starting again on use).
