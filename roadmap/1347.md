### 1347. The line that says the task waits on its subagents is a pane in the dock, and a phone's way down no longer covers the dock

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** The owner's word of 2026-11-23, on his phone: the line "Waiting on 1 subagent …" (item
1331) seems to scroll with the history when he scrolls up the chat, and it must stay at the foot of the chat, over the
composer, like the composer's own status line.

It was never in the conversation: `capsule.wait` is the first child of the dock, outside the box that scrolls
(`run.js`: `dock = el("div", { class: "dock" }, capsule.wait, statusBar, …)`; measured in WebKit with an iPhone's touch at
390 by 844 and 430 by 932: the line's box is the same at the end, the middle and the top of the conversation). Two faults
made it read as part of it, both reproduced in WebKit (screenshots `shots/scroll/before-chat-*-scrolled-up.png`):

- **It had no ground.** On a phone the dock draws none (item 374), and the line, unlike the bar under it (item 1335), had
  no surface and no hairline, so the history showed through its words: "Waiting on 2 subagents" was written across a
  message and the messages went by behind it. On a wide window the dock's ground fades out over its top 28 %
  (`app.css .dock`), which is where the line stands, so it was half see-through there too. It now is the bar's pane:
  `var(--surface)`, a hairline, the bar's corner and 40 px, as wide as its words (`run.css .wait-line`).
- **The way down covered the dock.** On a touch screen an icon button draws a 4 px ring round itself for a finger
  (`app.css`, item 787: `.btn.icon-only::before { position: absolute; inset: -4px }`, placed from the button by
  `:where(.btn.icon-only) { position: relative }`). The way down (`.status-bar > .jump`) set `position: static`, which
  outweighs that `:where`, so its ring was placed from the dock instead and covered the dock's whole box, invisibly. The
  way down shows exactly when the reader has scrolled up, so from then on a tap or a swipe on the line (Details and Stop),
  the queue and the composer reached an invisible button, not what was drawn there. The jump is `position: relative` with
  its offsets cleared (`run.css .status-bar > .jump`), so the ring is the button's own.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_chat_one_scroller.py` (the line is in the dock, its ground opaque, its box unchanged at the end,
middle and top of a long conversation and while the conversation grows, and a hit test at its centre reaches it, at 390 and
430 wide; it fails without the change), `tests/smoke/test_subagent_rows.py` (the pane in Chrome and in WebKit),
`test_status_bar_phone.py`, `test_chat_scroll.py` (whose assertion that the composer is glass had been stale since item
1330: it says now that it is a plain box); `ruff`, `mypy`, `npm run check`.
