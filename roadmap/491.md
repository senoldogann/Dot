### 491. The view Use it here opens closes, and clears the traffic lights

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** A fix of item 475, reported by the owner: in the desktop app, after **Use it here** and
**Return control**, the view over the whole page stayed, showing "Dot has control", with no way back to the run's page.
Its close stood at the top left (item 298 placed it for a phone), under the window's traffic lights, which the system
draws over the page there, and the title beside it was under them too. **As built.** `computer.js` closes the view a
Use it here opened when the run is given back (Return control, a hand-back from elsewhere) and when the run ends; a view
opened only to watch, a phone's, stays until it is closed. In the desktop shell, `run.css` puts the close 96 px from
the left, on the lights' line as the bars do (item 134), and the title after it. Tests: `tests/smoke/test_takeover_input.py`
checks that Return control closes the view on a phone and in the desktop shell, and there that the close and the title
stand right of the lights; both fail without the fix.
