### 1349. A chat on a phone has one scroller that is as wide as the screen, and the dock lets a touch through

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** The owner's word of 2026-11-23, on his phone: however much he scrolls, no scroll bar moves
with the content, but a bar on the right edge moves up and down; the page does not scroll as he expects.

What was measured, WebKit with an iPhone's touch at 390 by 844 and 430 by 932, on the chat, the chats, Settings, Models and
Memory (`tests/smoke/test_chat_one_scroller.py`, `shots/scroll/`):

- No element of the app draws a bar at the right edge: no custom scroll indicator, no `::-webkit-scrollbar` on a phone (those
  rules are for a pointer, `app.css`), no fixed or sticky element narrower than 14 px; the elements outside the
  conversation do not move when it scrolls. The bar is the system's own, drawn at the edge of the box that scrolls.
- On the pages that scroll, the window is the one scroller, with nothing else scrolling, and no bounce
  (`html { overscroll-behavior: none }`): nothing to change, held by a test.
- **In a chat the box that scrolls was not as wide as the screen.** `main.page` has 12 px of padding on a phone and 16 px
  above its content, and the conversation (`.run-thread`) stood inside it: at 390 wide it was x 12 to 378, from 69 px down.
  A swipe, or a wheel, that began in those strips hit the page, which scrolls nothing, and the system's bar was drawn at
  the box's edge, 12 px in from the glass. The page's padding is now the conversation's own
  (`html.chat-shell .page[data-layout="run"] { padding: 0 }` and `.run-thread` padding of 16 px and 12 px).
- **A fifth of the screen scrolled nothing.** The dock (the bar, the queue, the composer) floats over the conversation's foot
  (item 374) and is as wide as the screen and as tall as its panes with the gaps between them, 180 px at 390 by 844 with the
  line of item 1347; a swipe that began in a gap, left or right of the bar, hit the dock and scrolled nothing, as the dock is
  not in the conversation. The dock has `pointer-events: none` and its panes `auto`, so a gap hands the touch to the
  conversation under it. Together with the invisible ring of item 1347 this was the dead glass over the foot of a chat.
- The queue's list, the one other scroller in the dock, holds its overscroll (item 1348).

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_chat_one_scroller.py` (the conversation is x 0 to the screen's width, the one scroller of the chat,
the window does not scroll and does not stretch, a hit test at the screen's two edges and in the dock's gaps reaches the
conversation; the four pages scroll in the window alone, with no bar at the right edge; at 390 and 430 wide; the first
test fails without the change), `test_chat_scroll.py`, `test_chat_switch_scroll.py`, `test_phone_*.py`,
`test_status_bar_phone.py`, `test_iphone.py`; `ruff`, `mypy`, `npm run check`.

Not verified: how the system draws the bar and its momentum on a real iPhone (Safari and the Home Screen app): WebKit on this
Mac does not draw the iOS overlay bar, and a swipe cannot be made in its touch emulation, only a wheel and a hit test.
