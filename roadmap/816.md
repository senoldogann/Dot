### 816. A connector that registers Dot with a secret is sent the secret

**Status: done (2026-10-29).** **Asked** by the owner ("connectors da hala left out problemi yaşıyoruz ... tüm plugins,
skills ve connectors sisteminin problemsiz ve sorunsuz olmasını istiyorum"), with a screenshot of Supabase on
Customize › Connectors: **Left out**, "signing in to the MCP server failed".

- The log said why: `OAuthTokenError: Token exchange failed (422): {"message":"Required parameter: client_secret"}`.
  Dot registers itself as a client without a secret (`token_endpoint_auth_method = "none"`). Supabase's authorization
  server lists only `client_secret_basic` and `client_secret_post`, registers the client with a secret all the same,
  and names no way of sending it. The MCP library sends no secret for a client that names no way, so the token
  exchange after the owner's sign-in was refused.
- `usable_client` (`dotagent.mcp.signin`): a client registered with a secret that names no way of sending it is sent
  the secret `client_secret_basic`, as RFC 7591 makes the way of a registration that names none, or
  `client_secret_post` when the server's metadata lists that and not basic. `FileTokenStorage` applies it to a client
  as it is registered (and hands it back to the library's flow, which asks for the token with the client it holds) and
  to one read back, so a client kept from before is renewed with its secret too. A client that names its way, or has no
  secret, is as it was.
- Read on the services on 2026-10-29, by Dot's own requests: of the 81 services of the catalogue that sign in, three
  list no client without a secret: Supabase, GitLab and Miro; Heroku lists no way at all. A registration of each, as
  Sign in makes one: GitLab, Miro and Heroku said `none` back with no secret and take a token request without one;
  Supabase gave a secret and named no way, refused a token request without it (422), and took the secret both ways
  (its answer to the made-up code was "Invalid or expired OAuth authorization"). The 17 services open to all answered
  Dot's own connection with their tools, and every service that signs in answered 401 to a start without a token.
- `tests/smoke/test_catalogue_reachable.py` had let Supabase list no `none` by its docs; its comment says now what it
  does. The catalogue's docstring and docs/extending.md say it too.
- Stripe, left out 18 times on 2026-10-27 and 28 with "the MCP server asks you to sign in again", was the sign-in kept
  from before item 795 (no token endpoint kept with it, so renewed at the wrong address); the fresh start of Dot's
  store took it out, and a sign-in made now keeps it.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_mcp_sign_in_secret.py`: the MCP library's own flow through Dot's sign-in against a
server answered in this process, as Supabase's answers: the token is asked for with the secret, by basic and by post
as the metadata lists, and kept so; a client kept from before with no way named is renewed with its secret; a client
that names its way or has no secret is not changed. Without the change all four fail, the first with the 422.
