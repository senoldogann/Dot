### 501. A chat's page opens on its own shape, not on an empty box

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Asked by the owner: the screen a chat shows while it loads is a bare box with a spinner
in it, and it reads as empty rather than as a chat opening. The page of a run is read in one go (item 454), so that box
stands there for as long as the daemon takes to answer.

**As built.**
- `js/panels.js`: `chatLoading()` draws what stands in a chat's place — Dot's mark, the line "Opening this chat", and
  the shape of the conversation to come: a message of yours at the right in the tint of a bubble and three lines of an
  answer under it. `js/views/run.js` draws it where the bare card stood.
- `css/run.css`: the composition is centred in the room under the bar, in the conversation's lane, and the page fades it
  in as it fades a page. Dot's mark is a ring of the look's ink turning once in six seconds around a dot of Dot's yellow
  that breathes, with a breath of that yellow behind it; the label takes the light the way a live thought's label does
  (item 374); each block carries a band of the look's ink that crosses it in its turn.
- `css/app.css`: two motion tokens, `--motion-turn` (the ring) and `--motion-shine` (the band of light), so the sheet
  names no duration of its own (item 73).
- Reduced motion: the mark and the blocks stand still and the band of light is not drawn, so the shape still reads.

**Checked.** `npm run check` green (42 tests); `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py` 36 passed (the two new tokens are named
everywhere, and the block does not step outside the palette). Clicked through the chat's page in Chrome at 1 280 and
390 px, light and dark, the run's read held back: the composition is centred with no page wider than the window, and two
shots 1.5 s apart differ (the ring turns, the bands cross), while the same pair with reduced motion is identical to the
byte.
