### 1965. DotNext's screens agree on which chats are left

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner, on build 8: after deleting every chat, Chats said "No chats yet" while Dot's
profile still listed a chat ("Komşumun Wi-Fi…", 23 Nov) under its Chats.

Causes, on the owner's store (read on a copy, never written): the chat was not stale on the Mac, it was never
deleted. The list holds the daemon's newest chats only; the three `cli` chats of 23 Nov lay below its window, so
Select › all never held them. Three things made the screens disagree:
- a chat the Mac said went left the list without the list being read again, so the window never refilled with the
  older chats and Chats said "No chats yet";
- the profile drew Dot's home chat from the agents read on their own (`GET /api/agents`), which nothing read again
  after a delete;
- All chats took the bot's chat ids when it opened and never again.

What was built: a deletion told on the list's stream reads the list again (its window refills); `bots(...)` drops a
home chat that is gone or going (`EngineSnapshot.goneChats`: deleted here, on the Mac or on another phone, or a delete
on its way), and the agents are read again when the list is told of a deletion (one read at a time, one more after it
when more deletions came meanwhile; none for the deletions a cold start's cache holds); a bot's All chats reads the bot's
chats from the bots each time. The other screens read the same list: a bot's unread mark and chat count, the home's
rows; the schedules on a profile are read again each time it shows.

Checked: `ReducerTests.testTheListDropsADeletedChatAndAnOlderViewDoesNotBringItBack` (the read after a deletion) and
`BotsTests` (a gone home is dropped), red without the change; `ChatListTests.testABotsScopeHoldsItsOwnChatsOnly`;
`BulkDeleteLiveUITests` (the profile empties after the delete).
