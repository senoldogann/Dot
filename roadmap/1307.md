### 1307. One rule for the nearest reasoning level, and a schedule keeps the level its firing ran at

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** Two functions chose the level a model takes for one it does not offer: `choose_effort` in
`llm/traits.py` (a run's calls, a standby model, the composer) took the lower of two as near, and `nearest_offered` in
`agent/manager.py` (a schedule's firing, item 882) the higher, and the docs said each its own way
(`memory-and-schedules.md`: the higher; `models.md`: only "the nearest"). The manager's also knew nothing of a model
whose levels are a plain switch (`off`, `on`), and ran such a firing at none. A schedule whose level was not offered said
so in the log at every firing, for as long as it lived.

- One function: `effective_effort` of `llm/traits.py` (it calls `choose_effort`) is what the manager uses;
  `nearest_offered` and its table are gone. Ties go lower, which is what `choose_effort` gives a chat's run on the same model (`medium` on
  DeepSeek's low, high, max is `low` there; read in the code, not measured on the owner's chats) and what costs less. A
  switch is a ladder like another: `medium` is `on`, `minimal` is `off`.
- The scheduler writes the level the firing ran at into the schedule (`Store.set_schedule_effort`), once, and the run
  gets an event `schedule_effort_changed` (`asked`, `kept`), which the timeline shows as a quiet line (English and Turkish).
  The next firing finds the level offered, so the log's warning (`schedule_effort_corrected`) comes once. A model with no
  choice leaves the schedule as it is and says nothing.
- An agent's schedule is judged against the model its run takes (`agent_bounds`: the schedule's own, else the agent's,
  else the main one), not the main model's, so that it is neither rewritten to the main model's level nor cleared when
  the main model has no choice; the event's `model` names that model.
- `memory-and-schedules.md` and `models.md` say the same rule.

A schedule whose `medium` ran at `high` on a model of low, high and max now runs at `low`: the firings of a schedule
already kept at `medium` change once, at its next firing, and are then as a chat's run is.
