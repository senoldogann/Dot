### 661. Dot added to an iPhone's Home Screen wears its icon again

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner's iPhone 17 Pro Max added Dot to the Home Screen with the letter D: the sheet
of Add to Home Screen (iOS 26, "Open as Web App" on) showed no icon. The share sheet showed Dot's mark, and every icon
answered whole over the owner's Tailscale address, with and without a session and with every fetch-metadata
combination tried.

**Why.** iOS 26 opens what is added to the Home Screen as a web app and takes the icon from the manifest. Dot's
manifest named `favicon.svg` of `sizes: any` beside its PNGs: as "any", it outranks every picture, and iOS draws no
SVG as a Home Screen icon. It has stood in the manifest since item 52. What changed is iOS, which used the touch
icons before.

**The change.**
- `manifest.webmanifest`: the SVG is left out, and the 180 px touch icon named, so the manifest's icons are PNGs of a
  size each: 180, 192, 512 and the maskable 512. The page's tab keeps the SVG favicon through its own link.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/unit/test_manifest.py`: every icon of the manifest is a PNG of a size, the 180 px one among them.
- On the owner's phone: once deployed, Safari reloaded and Dot added again.
