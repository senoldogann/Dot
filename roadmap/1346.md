### 1346. A link in a message is plain words in a brighter shade, with no chip, and a site is asked for no icon

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** The owner's word of 2026-11-23: the links inside chat messages (markdown links and
autolinks, in what Dot says and in what he wrote) lose every border and every fill, pill and chip; they read like the
words round them but a little brighter, with no line until hover, clear in dark and in light, and the keyboard focus ring
stays; the message bodies, tool cards, the queue and the side panel are checked for chips left over.

Item 1271 had drawn a link as a pill with the site's icon (`.link-pill`, a frame, a fill, a 16 px icon fetched from the
site by the daemon). Now `prose.js` draws it as `a.msg-link`: the label (the link's words; `#1001` for a GitHub pull
request or issue; the site's name for a bare address; `title` is the whole address) as text, no icon, no frame, no fill, no
padding, `display: inline`. Its colour is the sentence's ink tilted toward the accent by a mix the sheet writes
(`color-mix(in oklab, var(--accent-strong) 78%, var(--text))` in light, 58 % in dark, 45 % toward the bubble's own ink in
what he wrote): a shade brighter than the text in dark, a shade more saturated in light, no underline until the pointer
is on it or it has the focus, and the page's solid ring stays on the focus. The label shorter than the words written
(a bare address named by its site, long words cut at 48 characters) keeps them in `data-words`, so that a copy of the
conversation gives back what was written (`tests/smoke/test_markdown_copy.py` was failing on main for it since item 1271).

With no icon, nothing asks a site for one: `GET /api/favicons/{host}`, `server/favicons.py`, `routes_favicons.py`,
`faviconUrl` of `api.js` and the paragraph of `docs/safety.md` about the request to the linked sites (every host on the
way learned the Mac's address) are gone. Left over chips: the other links of the panel and the conversation are rows
(`lineage-row`, `context-row`, `source-link`, `automation-edit`), none with a frame or a fill at rest; the new browser test
walks every link of the messages, the timeline and the panel and fails on one that has either.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_message_links.py` (five links, markdown and bare, in his message and Dot's: no border, fill, icon
or padding, inline, no line, a colour other than the text's, the line under the pointer and with the focus and the ring,
opens in a new window with the address as its title, no link of the panel or conversation with a frame or fill; light and
dark, in Chrome and in WebKit), `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py::test_a_link_in_a_message_has_no_chip_and_reads_at_aa_on_every_ground_it_stands_on`
