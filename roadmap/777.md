### 777. Settings › Trading is a trading desk

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner: "trading sayfasını daha trading'e yakışır bir tasarım yap" (give
the trading page a design that suits trading). The page listed the settings first and the account last, as rows of
words: the worth, today's change and the holdings, with no price, no gain, no stop and nothing of what watches the
market between runs.

**The change.** `GET /api/trading` answers with `positions` (each open exit of the account with its buy's price, from
the new `Store.get_trading_order`, and the bid now, from what the account's holding is worth) and `watch` (the schedule
`[trading] wake_schedule` names, by the store, with whether it is on and when it fires next, or `schedule_name` null
when the id names no schedule; the day's wakes and the last one, as the market watch counted them; and the products a
run may trade at this moment, from Coinbase's list with `*`). `views/trading.js` draws, with trading on, top to bottom:

- the **Account** desk: the worth large, today's change and the change since the paper account's start with their
  share, what is in positions, and two meters of the day's limits (loss so far against `max_day_loss`, traded against
  `max_day`), green, amber past 60 %, red at the limit;
- **Open positions** as a table: size, entry, the bid now, worth, gain in money and share, and a scale from the stop to
  the target with the entry and the price marked and how far each end is (`positionFacts`);
- **Watching**: a line each, with its tone, for the stops, the market, the wake schedule (red when the id names none,
  amber when it is off: the pre-push review's nit of item 772, a wrong id woke nothing in silence) and the next shift
  (`watchLines`);
- **Holdings** and **Orders** as tables of figures that line up, a Buy green and a Sell red, the run's reason on the line
  under each order;
- under a line, **Settings**: the mode, the limits and the products, the Coinbase key, as before.

On a phone each table's row is a card of labelled lines. The colours are the themes' own (`--ok`, `--bad`, `--warn`);
the sizes and spaces the tokens (`tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py`). `orderWords`, which the old rows used, is gone.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_trading_settings.py`: an open position answered with its buy's price and the bid after
the price moved, the watch with the schedule's name, the day's two wakes and the two products past the floor of three, a
wake schedule naming no schedule said so, nothing watched while off. `tests/smoke/test_trading_settings_page.py`: the
desk's figures, the since-start change, the empty positions and the watch's line in Chrome, and the page inside a
phone's screen. `tests/js/tradingwords.test.js`: prices, shares, meters, a position's facts with and without a target or
a price, and the watch's lines and tones. Looked at in Chrome at 1280 px in light and dark and at 390 px with touch,
with a seeded paper account of two positions, one up and one down, and a failed order.
