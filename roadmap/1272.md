### 1272. On a phone an agent's page opens as a chat and goes back to the chats

**Status: done (2026-11-20).** The owner, on the phone: an agent opened from the list of chats went Back to the agents'
page (the office), not to the list; while it loaded it showed a card with a spinner that read as the office loading; its
face, name and box stood at the foot of the screen, not in the middle; and a swipe from the left edge in the iPhone app
went to another chat rather than to the list.

Found in WebKit at an iPhone's size against a served daemon:
- `/agents/<id>` had the agents' page as its parent, so a phone's Back from an agent with no chat yet (whose row opens
  its page) led to `/agents`.
- The page put `loadingPanel()`, a card with a spinner, where the agent would be.
- The page used the home's grid (item 124), whose upper row takes half of the room less `--home-start-h`, but nothing
  kept that height on the agent's page, so the block started half way down.
- The iPhone app's web view had `allowsBackForwardNavigationGestures` on: WebKit's swipe went back in the history (the
  chat opened before) while the page's own edge swipe (item 1079) opened the list.

Now:
- An agent's page has no page above it: its Back opens the list of chats, as a chat's does; opened by a link of the
  agents' page it still goes back there (`main.js`).
- While it is read it shows `chatLoading()`, Dot's three dots in the middle, as a chat does.
- `trackHeight(top, page, "--home-start-h")` keeps its face, name and box in the middle, on a phone and a Mac.
- A team's page, on the same grid, had the same card and the same place half way down: it loads and stands as an
  agent's does. Its Back is still the agents' page (no row of the sidebar opens a team).
- The iPhone app turns WebKit's back swipe off; the page's edge swipe is the one way back. It takes a new build of the
  app. In Safari's tab, Safari's own swipe still goes back in the history: a page cannot turn it off.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_agent_conversation.py::test_a_phone_opens_an_agent_as_a_chat_and_goes_back_to_the_chats`
(the three dots while the read is held, no card, the block's middle within 60 px of the room's, Back opening the list,
and Back from the agents' page returning there), and by screenshots at 430 px and 1280 px.
