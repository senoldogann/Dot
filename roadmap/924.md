### 924. A compaction keeps the ids of Dot's records a run named, and recall reads each back with its neighbours

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner, the fourth of the gaps they took from the DeepSeek Harness
"Super-agent" article (its Chain of Recall: each object recalled names the ids of the next, and context is widened
step by step). A compaction kept the goal, the plan, the owner's words, the newest actions and the files and addresses
touched, but no id of Dot's own records: a run, a chat, a thing to do, a work order named before the summary were gone
with it, and no tool read one back by its id. The check of a summary named `apv_` and `rule_`, prefixes Dot never
writes, so an approval's or a rule's id a summary made up went unseen.

- `agent/continuity.py`: `RunLedger.named`, the ids of runs, chats, things to do, work orders, schedules, approvals
  and objectives that the run's calls and their results named (the newest 60, one named again moving to the end),
  listed on one line of the continuity note with the tool that reads them back. The check's prefixes are Dot's own
  (`apr`, `rul`, `todo`, `wo`, `obj`, `agt`, `cmp`, `team` with `run`, `thr`, `mem`, `sch`).
- `tools/recall.py`: `recall(id)` reads one of those records with the ids next to it (a run's chat and parent, a
  chat's newest runs, a thing's last run, a work order's run, chat and objective); what a run that read content third
  parties can author wrote is fenced and marks the reader untrusted, as `read_agent_chat` does. It is in the core
  group and every agent keeps it.
- `docs/runs.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_continuity.py` (the ids kept from calls and results, the newest last, listed in the
note; the check knows an approval's id) and `tests/smoke/test_recall.py` (a run, its chat and a thing to do read back
with their neighbours; a run that read a page fenced and untrusted; an unknown id or kind refused).
