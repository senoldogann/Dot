### 709. The connection is said lost only when it is, on a phone and in Dot.app

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** `test_phone_return`'s network-wake test failed on main, and it was not the test:

- A break that began while the page was away (a phone put in a pocket) had its wait before the pill counted from when it
  began, so the pill said "Reconnecting" at once when the phone came back, inside the 8 seconds of grace a waking
  network is given (item 328).
- WebKit (Safari, the iPhone, Dot.app) hands the page a stream's answer only once its body begins. The lifecycle stream
  has nothing to say most of the time, so after every break the page read as not connected until its first keepalive,
  15 s on, and said "Reconnecting" for that long: with the network back, the pill stayed past the test's 13 s.

**The change.** The page counts its returns (`returns` in `state.js`); the shell hides the pill on a return and waits
again from it. Every event stream (`event_stream` in `server/stream.py`: the lifecycle, a run's, a screencast, a
teaching) opens with a comment line (`OPENING`), which every browser hands the page at once.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_phone_return.py` passes whole (the network-wake test failed on main at the pill shown
inside the grace, then, with the first fix alone, at the pill that stayed once the network was back: the stream's answer
came at 20.0 s with the opening line, not after the keepalive); the tests that read a stream's bytes expect the opening
line (`test_server.py`, `test_takeover.py`, `test_takeover_input.py`); every test that follows a stream.
