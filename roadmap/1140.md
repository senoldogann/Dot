### 1140. A note written beside a yes comes first

**Status: done (2026-11-12).** The owner: when Dot asked and they wrote a note instead of a plain yes, Dot took the step
as approved and read the note afterwards. Measured in their Dot on 2026-11-12: a mail's Send card was answered with
Approve and the note "Bir şey gönderme, bugün telefonda konuştuk ve DNA olayı kapandı"; the click ran, the mail went, and
the next step read the note.

What must hold: a yes that carries a note never takes the step in the same breath. The gate (`RunGate._ask_user`)
raises a refusal that tells the run the note, keeps the owner's words among the run's instructions, and remembers the
whole step (`ActionInfo`, not only what its card showed); the same step done again exactly so goes through once without
asking (`noted_yes_used`), anything else asks as before. A kept step's **Allow once** with a note (`allowed_text`,
`allowed_send_text`) puts the note before the step. The card's Approve says **Send note** while a note is written. A
yes with no note, a hand-off's "I did it" and a question's answer are unchanged.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_approval_modes.py` (a yes with a note waits, then goes once), `tests/unit/test_parking.py`,
the approval tests of `tests/smoke` (step decisions, peers, kept hand-offs, kept campaign, owner and initiative asks),
`npm run check`.
