### 461. A schedule says how the end of its runs is told

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The fifth of the nine ideas taken from OpenBot (item 449 has the plan), its third part:
"a notification level per schedule". Until now the end of every run was told on the phone and the desktop, a schedule's
as any other's, so a schedule that runs every hour told every hour. **As built.** A schedule has a level
(`ScheduleOptions.notify`, a column; its form's **Notifications**: Always, Only when it fails or needs me, Never; else
`[scheduler] notify`, `always`, which is how Dot worked). The level is read when its run starts and given to the run's
watch (`Scheduler._track`, `_watch`), so a schedule changed or deleted while its run works tells that run's end at the
level it had: never no notice, never two (the critique). At the end the watch writes the outcome and the row of failed
runs (item 458) and, when the level tells it (`scheduler/notices.py` `tells`: `always` every end; `problems` a run not
cancelled that ends with something it needs from the owner, failed or not, and of the other failed runs the first of a
row; `never` none), sends the live event `schedule_run_ended` (the run, how it ended, its answer), which the phone
(`notify/push.py` `finished_message`) and the desktop (attention.js) word as every run's end; they no longer tell the
plain end (`run_finished`) of a run a schedule started, while a campaign's session, also started by the scheduler, is
told as before. A run started by hand (**Run now**) is told at every level (`BY_HAND_LEVEL`): the owner asked for it. At
`never` a missed firing is not told either; a question, an approval, a draft and item 458's switch-off are told at every
level, and a failure whose row cannot be read (the write failed, the schedule is gone) is told at `problems`. The row
shows a schedule's own level as a chip, and the form's default names the configured one (`StatusView.schedule_notify`).
Item 458's last nit taken: the chip of the switch-off limit shows only on a repeating schedule. Yolo is untouched: the
level changes what is told, never what a run does or asks, and a yolo schedule's run is told as any other's. Tests:
`tests/unit/test_schedule_notices.py` (the level, the rule, a second failure that needs the owner told),
`tests/unit/test_push.py` (the phone's message for `schedule_run_ended`; a schedule's run left to its schedule, a
campaign's session told), `tests/smoke/test_schedule_notify.py` (a real scheduler and the routes: one notice at `always`
for a yolo schedule; at `problems` the first failure, not the second, not a run done, but a run done that needs the
owner; none at `never`, one for its run by hand; a schedule deleted while its run works tells once; a failure to tell
does not keep a schedule at its limit on, nor a failed switch-off its end untold, checked to fail with the old order;
the page at 1280 px light and at 390 px dark as a touch screen: the row's chip, the form's levels, no limit chip on a
one-time schedule, checked to fail without the fix). Docs: `docs/memory-and-schedules.md`, `docs/daemon.md`,
`defaults.toml`.
