### 1133. A refused text step says where the field's ref comes from, and a bare wait is left out

**Status: done (2026-11-12).** Dot's record: `browser_act` refused its arguments nine times in a week. The model's own
mistakes made most of them, each answered by the refusal and corrected on the next call: an action that is no action
(`click la a ref:f11e127`, `focus`), a `click` with a stray `text`. Two had a cause on Dot's side. Runs clicked a form
field by its pixel (`click_at`) and then wrote `type` or `insert` with no ref (`run_mw80ksri`, a form of fifteen steps;
`run_mw9g683y`, a mail reply), and were told only "type needs a ref". One found the field with `browser_find`; the other
filled the form with a `browser_eval` script, past the checks a step of a batch makes at each field. A `fill`, `type` or
`insert` with no ref now says where the ref comes from (`browser_find`, for example `textbox`, or the snapshot, a field
inside a frame too) and that a `click_at` gives the steps after it no field. A bare `{"action": "wait"}` after a click
(`run_mvsnlh3f`) is left out of the batch as a step that only looks is (item 755): the batch waits for the page to settle
and shows it.

Not done: typing into whatever a `click_at` focused. The policy weighs a step by the element its ref names (a password
or card field, a send button next to it), and a field known only by its focus would be written unweighed.

Checked: `test_browser_step_meaning.py` (both new tests fail without the change), `test_tool_errors.py`.
