### 1381. Dot for iPhone: every screen redrawn on the design system, row by row

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** The owner asked for every line and area to be fixed one by one. Item 1380 has the audit and
the system; this is each screen on it. Nothing a screen does changed (the same calls, the same identifiers the simulator
tests tap, the same strings the tests read), only how it is drawn and laid out.

**What was done, screen by screen.**

- **Chat list.** A large title that collapses, a soft glow of the accent under it, and the agents as glass chips (the
  avatar with a ring that turns while the agent works and is amber while it waits for the owner, its name, and "Working",
  "Needs you" or "Paused" under it; Dot wears the app's own mark). The chats are in three groups, **Needs you**, **Working**
  and **Chats**, each with a count, so what a run waits on is the first thing: a row has a 40-point avatar with the same
  ring, the title, the last words (two lines when idle), the age, a pill ("Needs you" with a pulsing hand, "Working" with a
  moving waveform), the loop and unread marks; a swipe right reads a chat (`markSeen`); the separators start under the
  words. The new-chat button floats as accent glass at the bottom right (the pencil left the toolbar; the identifier
  `new-chat` moved with it). "Reconnecting" and "key refused" are a glass pill under the title. An empty list is an
  illustrated state with a button. At the accessibility sizes a row stacks, and the chips and the button stop growing.
- **Chat.** The transcript runs under floating glass: the queue, the status line and the composer are over its foot (the
  transcript is softened at that edge, `scrollEdgeEffectStyle`), and the title has a subtitle (Working, Waiting on you).
  Dot's words are plain text at `.callout` with 4-point line spacing; the owner's message is a raised box (18-point
  radius); a code block has its own ground, SF Mono and a copy button; links stay plain and brighter. Spacing is by what
  follows what (steps close together, the owner's message apart). A step is a quiet line with the symbol of its tool
  (`ToolGlyph`), pulsing while it runs, the verb in the text colour and what it did in SF Mono, a check when done and a
  small red mark only on failure. **An approval is the loudest card**: an amber-tinted card with a pulsing hand, "Waiting
  for your OK", the tool's name as a headline, the command in a mono box, and Deny and Allow as large glass buttons
  (Allow prominent, haptic on the answer); a question has its field and Skip and Answer; a secret says to give it on the
  Mac; an answered one folds into a line ("Allowed · Shell"). The status line is a glass pill (a moving waveform and the
  seconds while it works, an amber bell when it waits, the subagents with Details and Stop); the queue is a glass panel,
  numbered, with up and down arrows and a remove button of 40 points; the composer is one glass bar (+, the field, the
  microphone, and a round button that is the arrow, the queue mark or the stop square, one symbol changing in place);
  jump to the newest is a glass circle. The tool's detail and the select-text sheets use the same cards.
- **Run inspector.** A status card (the run's state in its colour, the steps), the model rows, the plan with a progress bar
  and a symbol per step, the helpers as a branch under the run with a pill each, and the schedules and loops with
  switches, all as cards; empty ones are a quiet line with a symbol.
- **Settings, models, schedules.** The same card language: the Mac and whether it answers first, then Dot (Models,
  Schedules), Phone (Notifications, Change address, Forget this phone's key) and the web UI, each row with a tinted
  symbol; the model lists have checkmarks and a haptic; Schedules has a switch each and an illustrated empty state.
- **New chat and the model picker.** A sheet with a hero (the agent's avatar, or the sparkles), the model as a glass pill
  and the composer as glass bar; a refusal is a card with its button; the picker is cards with checkmarks.
- **Pairing and address.** The app's mark with a glow over a soft mesh, the two steps as a numbered card, the code in a
  glass field in SF Mono, a prominent Pair; the load error is an illustrated state.
- **Both looks, the largest type, and Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion by the helpers**: see 1380.

**Checked.** The same runs as 1380 (unit and simulator tests, the screenshots of every screen in both looks, at the
largest accessibility size and in the material look). The simulator's tests that tap these screens (the ten of slice 2)
pass unchanged.
