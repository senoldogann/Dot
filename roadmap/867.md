### 867. Agents picked on their cards make a team

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner: "kendimiz agents sayfasından ajanları seçerek bir takım
oluşturabilelim onlardan da".

The Agents page had **New team** (item 610), a form that lists the owner's agents to tick. The owner looked for a way
to pick them on the grid itself.

- `views/agents.js`: **Select** at the head of Your agents. It is shown with two agents of the owner's or more.
  - It turns their cards into boxes to tick (`role="checkbox"`): a click, or Space, ticks one, and leads nowhere. Dot's
    own card is none.
  - A bar under the grid counts the picked agents, and has **Make a team** and **Cancel**.
  - Make a team opens New team's form with them ticked, first, in the order picked. Cancel leaves the cards as links.
- `teamform.js`: `chosen`, the agents a new team starts with.
- `app.css`: the mark of a card to tick at its top right, a picked card ringed in the accent, the bar as a pill that
  stays at the foot of the window while the grid scrolls.
- `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_team_pages.py::test_agents_picked_on_their_cards_make_a_team_in_the_order_picked` (in
Chrome):
- Select gives three boxes for three agents, the bar counts the picked ones, and a picked card leads nowhere.
- Make a team lists them first, ticked, in the order picked, and the team keeps that order.
- Cancel brings the links back.
