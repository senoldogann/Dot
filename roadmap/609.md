### 609. The Schedules page leads with your schedules; the brief and the watchers fold to a line

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The owner, with a screenshot of Schedules: "the Schedules page seems too crowded; this
area confuses me". The page opened with the Morning brief's whole card (item 570): its switch, time, days, its own
lines, its status and two buttons. Then came the Watchers panel (item 571) with a long line and a large picture of an
empty list. Only under both stood the owner's own schedules, the page's subject.

**As built.**

- **The order.** The page leads with **Your schedules** (and New schedule, the page's one primary button). The brief
  and the watchers come under them.
- **The brief is a line.** Its name, when it writes (*Every day at 08:00*, *Weekdays at 07:30*, or *Off*) and its
  switch. An arrow at its right opens its settings (the line on what it does, the time, the days, the owner's lines,
  when it fires next, Write one now and Save). The page keeps them open or folded as the owner left them
  (`dot.brief.open`), and the brief row's Edit in the list opens them.
- **The watchers.** With none, their panel is its head, *None yet*, and one line of what a watcher does: the picture of
  an empty list is gone. **New watcher** is a quiet button, so New schedule is the page's one primary action. The line
  no longer gives the hourly cap, which `docs/proactive.md` keeps.
- **The code.** `briefcard.js` (`briefSummary`, the fold), `watcherspanel.js`, `views/schedules.js` (the order) and
  `settings.css`. `docs/proactive.md` and `docs/memory-and-schedules.md` say where the brief and the watchers stand.
