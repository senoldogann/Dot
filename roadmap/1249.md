### 1249. The composer gives the words their own line

**Status: done (2026-11-19).** The owner's word of 2026-11-19: make the composer more usable and more professional,
plain and clean. Seen on a throwaway daemon: the box at rest was one line of the "+", the words, the choices, the
microphone and the send (item 833), so a long message wrapped a few words a line between them, on a phone most of all.

On a Mac the words now have a line of their own across the box and the controls the line under it: the "+" and the
choices at the left, the microphone and the send at the right, on the 18 px corner of a box of two lines. On a phone the
box stays one line of the "+", the words and the send until the words wrap; then the words take the whole line and the
buttons go under them. Whether the words wrap is measured in the one-line box (`composer.js` takes the mark off,
measures, and puts it back as it found), so the box does not jump between the two layouts at every key typed or deleted,
as a measure in the layout it was in did (the pre-push review).

Checked: screenshots of a Mac's window (light and dark) and a phone's, empty, short and long; `npm run check`; the
browser tests of the composer, the microphone (`test_voice.py`, which measured the one line) and the phone's layout.
