### 719. The step is scored where the text can make a difference

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Asked by the owner:** "the self-improvement does not work at all". It could not
adopt anything: its ten scoring tasks are passed under any text (the base passed 20 attempts of 20 on 2026-10-24), and a
candidate has to pass `step_margin` attempts more than the base, so every night came out `keep`, at the price of
millions of tokens. docs/harness.md said so ("before the step can adopt on evidence the scoring set needs tasks with
headroom ... which is left for later").

**What must hold.**

- A task has *headroom* when the version in use failed one of its newest `[harness] headroom_window` attempts (6),
  counted from every attempt of that version that started (item 717), of any step and label; one it passed in all of a
  full window is *saturated*; one with fewer attempts is not known yet (`harness/headroom.py`).
- `scoring_tasks` are the guards, played every step: the task of honesty and the one with an instruction hidden in a
  file. Besides them a step scores the base and the candidates on at most `headroom_tasks` (6) tasks with headroom, the
  lowest pass rate first, and on `regression_tasks` (2) saturated ones, those tried longest ago, so that a text that
  breaks what works still loses. All come from `pool_tasks`, the eval tasks safe to repeat that read nothing of the web;
  a task of the final set or a guard is left out of it. The most a text is scored on is ten tasks, as before, so the
  budget's arithmetic (`test_harness_docs.py`) holds.
- With fewer than `headroom_least` (2) tasks with headroom known, nothing is proposed: no proposer's tokens, no
  candidate that nothing could judge. The base tries `calibrate_tasks` (6) tasks of the pool it does not know yet, the
  untried first, and the step is `calibrating`, its decision saying how many tasks have headroom and how many are left.
  A pool known whole with too few is said (`nothing to learn on the pool`) and plays nothing.
- Every task of the pool is withheld from the models that write a candidate (`withheld_tasks`), as the scoring set is.
- The step's record says which tasks it scored on (`scored_on`); the page's *How it learns* names the guards and the
  tasks with headroom, or how much of the pool is tried while too few are known, and a night that only tried reads
  *trying tasks*.
- A scripted check scores on the guards alone: the references pass everything, so no task would have headroom.

**What only asks.** Whether a pass rate measured under an older text says anything of the one in use: it is not used.
A new version in use starts its own record, from the probes it played as a candidate.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_harness_headroom.py`: records, the pool, calibration of the least known, the scored set
of a ready step, ten tasks at most. `tests/smoke/test_harness_step.py`: a step with nothing known proposes nothing and
tries the pool, and the next one scores the candidate on the guards and the two tasks the base failed, and adopts on
evidence; a pool known whole says there is nothing to learn. The defaults (`test_harness_scoring.py`) and the page
(`test_harness_page.py`) follow.
