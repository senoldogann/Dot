### 1336. The settings are one plain shell: a search and groups of rows in the nav, a breadcrumb in the bar, and every page that configures Dot a row of it

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The owner's word of 2026-11-22: Dot's design is "completely like a toy"; make it plain and
professional (a settings screen with a left nav in groups and a right pane of titled cards), on the phone and the Mac
alike, and move Schedules into the settings.

The settings nav in the sidebar (`settingsnav.js`) is a search box (`/` takes the focus while the settings are open,
Escape clears it, Enter opens the first match; the search reads each page's name, its line and its words), the groups
General, Agents, Connections, Maintenance and About under small names, and a row of 16 px mark and name each, with Back
to chats at the foot. The phone's sheet (`views/settings.js`) has the same search over the same groups, each row a page
that pushes (item 338's Back and edge swipe are the route table's `parent`).

Every page that configures Dot is a row: besides the fifteen tabs that were, Scheduled tasks, Agents, Campaigns,
Connectors, Plugins, Skills, Diagnostics and Open source licences are pages of `#/settings/<page>` (`settingstabs.js` lists
them with their groups). The old addresses lead there (`MOVED` and its patterns in `main.js`), with the query they carry:
`#/customize/...`, `#/connect`, `#/agents` (an agent's own `#/agents/<id>` stays: it is its conversation), `#/campaigns/<id>`,
`#/diagnostics`, `#/licences`, `#/schedules`. A page of a page (a plugin, a campaign) opens at `#/settings/<page>/<id>`.

The page's name is the bar's breadcrumb, "Settings / General" (a third part names a page of the page); a phone's bar shows
the last part. The page under it opens on its line and its sections; `settingsHead` no longer draws a big title. A
module that heads itself with `pageHead` (Campaigns, Diagnostics, Licences, Agents) is hosted: its title goes to the
breadcrumb and its subtitle to the line (`hostPageHeads`). `customize.js` is gone, its three tabs being pages of the
settings.

Not covered: the pages' own content (item 1337 for Scheduled tasks, 1338 and 1339 for the rest). The sidebar's own head and
foot are the shell's (items 1327 to 1330: no Schedules icon, the agents' menu leads to Settings › Agents and Campaigns).
Connect apps leads to `#/settings/connectors` itself and stands for that page while it is open (`data-current`,
`aria-current`, read from the address, since Connectors is a page of the settings route and no route of its own).

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_settings_nav.py` (groups, search, `/`, breadcrumb, redirects, drawer, phone), `tests/unit/test_ui_map.py`,
`tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py`, `tests/smoke/test_connect_apps.py` (Connect apps' mark while Connectors is open), `npm run check`.
