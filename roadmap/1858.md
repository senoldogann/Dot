**Status: done (2026-11-25).** Taken from the study of Hermes Agent (`reports/refs-2026-11-25/hermes-agent.md`, item 5:
its `scripts/ci/prompt_surface.py` and `tests/ci/test_prompt_surface.py`); the owner approved it on 2026-11-25. Dot

renders it); a change of a word of the system prompt or of a tool's schema fails the unit tests until the files are
written again; the diff shows the change and the size in tokens.

  and shows the start of the diff, and renders again with another home, user and time zone to show none reaches it.
