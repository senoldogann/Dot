### 617. A picture opens in a frame that fits it, and a press shows it whole

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The owner sent a screenshot of a file's picture in the dialog of a page without the
panel: "neden bu sekilde gereksiz alti beyaz büyük bir ekranda gösteriyorda sadece fotografin botunuda gostermiyor
tikladigimizda" — why it is shown on such an unnecessarily big white sheet, and does not show the bottom of the picture
when it is clicked. The picture, a 2000 × 1179 screenshot of Chrome, was drawn at 728 × 429 at the top of a 760 × 812
sheet: 319 px of empty surface under it, and a press did nothing.

**Why.** The dialog of a file (item 269) had one height for every file, `min(860px, calc(100dvh - 64px))`, and the
picture's view only put the `img` at the top of a body that scrolls under the head, with `max-width: 100%` and nothing
that made the sheet follow the picture. A press was bound nowhere in `imageView`, while the screenshots of a run open
the lightbox (`showImage`).

**As built** (`static/js/filepanel.js`, `static/css/run.css`, `static/js/ui.js`).

- **The dialog of a picture is a frame around it.** It takes `width: fit-content` and `height: fit-content`: the
  picture's width up to the window, at least 420 px for the head, and its height up to the window less the 24 px it
  stands in at each side. The picture is drawn whole, scaled down to fit both sides, with a hairline of `--border`.
- **A press shows it larger** over the page, in the lightbox the screenshots of a run use, and Escape closes it: as
  large as the window allows (96vw by 94vh), which for the owner's 2000 × 1179 picture is about 976 px wide at
  1017 × 876. The picture is a button, named for a screen reader (`<name>, show larger`); one that fails to load says
  why, disabled.
- **Escape answers the lightbox first** (`ui.js`): the panel a picture was opened from closes on Escape too and took
  the key before the lightbox saw it, leaving the picture open over what it showed.
- **In the side panel** the picture is centered in the body and is never taller than the panel; it was pinned to the
  top with the empty body under it.
- `docs/runs.md` (what the panel shows of an image) says it.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_file_panel.py`, the image part:

- in a dialog the body is the picture's height, and no more than the 32 px of its padding taller;
- a press opens `dialog.lightbox` with the file's blob and Escape closes it.

The same file passed whole (2 tests). Measured: 760 × 527 with a 726 × 429 picture at 1017 × 876 (was 760 × 812), and
358 × 290 at 390 × 844; Chromium and WebKit, light and dark, read by eye. `npm run check` (Biome, `tsc`, 142 JS
tests), the CSS tokens and static UI units (42).

**Not done, and why.** The lightbox is shared with the screenshots of a run, whose labels still say "at full size"

(items 195 and 269) and are left as they are.
