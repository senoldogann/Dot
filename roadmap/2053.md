### 2053. Preview long prose in browser snapshots

Full browser views preview paragraphs with refs at 120 characters. They drop a text line without a ref when the
next field at the same depth already gives that label. Use the paragraph ref with `browser_read_text` to read the rest.
The browser keeps the captured accessibility tree for refs, search and action checks. Same-page deltas
still compare the unshortened snapshot and show the changed lines, including edits after the preview.

Headless Chrome on local fixtures, estimated tokens using Dot's 3.2 characters per token, including the tool
output headers. The act column measures a change on the same page.

| Fixture | Snapshot before | Snapshot after | Act before | Act after |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Existing home and list | 904 | 904 | 147 | 147 |
| Existing form | 112 | 104 | 132 | 132 |
| Existing dropdown | 237 | 222 | 289 | 289 |
| Long news page | 2,166 | 1,095 | 160 | 160 |
| Job board | 776 | 776 | 160 | 160 |
| Webmail inbox | 2,652 | 2,652 | 159 | 159 |
| Travel grant form | 975 | 633 | 148 | 148 |

The act that opened the news page from the inbox went from 2,234 to 1,162 estimated tokens. The act that
opened the existing form from the job board went from 178 to 170. Both returned full views. The job board
and inbox keep each row's labels, metadata and refs. An experimental rendering with only interactive roles
used 81, 373, 1,201 and 202 estimated tokens on the news, job board, inbox and grant form respectively. It also
lost the article, job locations, senders and timestamps, and form section labels. The tool description grew by
14 characters, around four estimated tokens in the full prompt surface.

`dotx eval check` passed all 54 references before and after, including all nine browser tasks. Across the
same check, nine browser page results went from 2,970 to 2,942 estimated tokens in total. The four act
results used 1,001 estimated tokens in both runs.

Read-only aggregates of successful tool results from real runs, excluding evals and reviews, put the recorded
mean at 1,076.7 estimated tokens for six snapshots, 769.3 for 39 acts with a view, and 1,250 for one text read.
Two snapshots, two acts and the text read reached the 4,000-character event cap, so these are lower bounds.
The persisted tool messages also had their older results shortened. Only counts and lengths were queried.
