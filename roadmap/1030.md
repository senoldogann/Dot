### 1030. A capture taken while the page connects reaches the box

**Status: done (2026-11-07).** **Found** by `tests/smoke/test_snapshots.py::test_the_box_takes_a_capture_that_waited_
and_one_that_comes_while_it_is_open` in the nightly `browser` runs (2 of the last 7: 5 chips of 9): three captures taken
right after the page was loaded again, and one of them came in.

**Cause.** The box claims what waits when it is made, and again on each `snapshot_taken` event. A capture taken after
the box was made and before the page followed the daemon's stream sent its event to nobody (the stream replays
nothing), and it waited in the daemon until the next capture. Reproduced on this Mac by holding the page's stream: 3
chips of 9.

**Built.** `state.js` `onStreamLive(listener)`: run each time the lifecycle stream is live, at its first connection and
after each break. `snapshots.js` claims what waits then too. Docs: `docs/daemon.md`.

**Checked.** The test holds the page's stream until the three captures are taken, then expects all of them: it fails
without the change (3 of 9) and passes with it; the file passes (4 tests); `npm run check`.
