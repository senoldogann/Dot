### 847. Trading is the Trader agent's

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, as stage 5 of the design plan: Trading moves out of the settings to
a Trader agent, as everything in Grok Bot is an agent with its chat.

- `agent/templates.py`: a ready-made **Trader**. It watches the desk and the trend engine (item 818) and reports the
  day; its suggested schedule is every day at 09:05, added switched off. Its tools:
  - read the account, the engine, the orders, the prices and what moved;
  - pause and resume the engine;
  - none places an order, closes a position or changes a limit: the engine trades by its rule, within the limits in
    code.
  Its tools are the trading tools, which a Dot has only with trading on. With trading off, its Use is refused and says
  which tools are missing, as for any template.
- `views/agents.js`, `agentpanel.js`: the agent named Trader opens the trading desk, `#/settings/trading`. This works
  from its page's bar (**Trading desk**), from its chat's panel (**Desk**), and from the Trader's card (**Open the
  trading desk**), where trading is switched on before the agent can be made. The agent is found by its name, as the
  agent page finds a template by its agent's name (item 722). The settings' nav no longer lists Trading (item 845).
- `docs/daemon.md`, `docs/trading.md`, `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` say so.

Kept: the desk itself, its settings, its key and every limit, all as they were; no trading code changed.
