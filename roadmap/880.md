### 880. A schedule's run that found nothing new is not pushed to the owner

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner, after the review of Dot against an agent-template article
(0xRafy, "GrokBot Templates"): "nothing new, nothing told"; a bot that pings with nothing to say is one the owner
learns to ignore.

What happened (the owner's database, read only): the "Müşteri postası" schedule ran every two hours on 2026-10-30,
seven times, and each run said that no client had answered ("Müşterilerden cevap yok"); at `[scheduler] notify =
"always"`, the default, each end was pushed to the phone and the desktop.

- `tools/meta.py`: `finish` takes `nothing_new` (false by default), for a run a schedule started.
- `agent/prompt.py`: a schedule's run is told to finish with it when nothing changed for the user since its last run.
- `store/db.py`: `last_ok_call_of(run_id, tool)`, the newest call whose own result (the first after it with its
  id) went through; `scheduler/notices.py`: `said_nothing_new` reads that finish call, not one refused, and
  `tells` at `always` leaves out a done end that found nothing new and needs nothing. A failure, an end that needs the
  owner and a run started with **Run now** are told as before; the row, the History and the run's page show it all.
- `docs/memory-and-schedules.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_schedule_notices.py::test_at_always_a_done_end_that_found_nothing_new_and_needs_nothing_is_not_told`,
`tests/smoke/test_schedule_notify.py::test_a_run_that_found_nothing_new_is_not_told_unless_it_was_started_by_hand`
(a real scheduler and a stand-in model).
