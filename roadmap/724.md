### 724. A campaign stops after sessions that keep failing, and a claim after Stop leaves it stopped

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Asked by the owner:** look deeply into Campaigns. Read with an agent and checked
against the code; nothing loses an owner's action in the usual path, but:

- `_follow_session` followed a failed session as any other, after `campaign_gap_minutes`, and `campaign_max_sessions`
  is 0 by default: a campaign whose sessions kept failing was tried for ever, its page saying its next session.
- `campaign_achieved` checked only that the campaign was not achieved, then achieved it unconditionally: a session still
  working after the owner's Stop (a `dotx` run, or one the Stop had not reached yet) could turn the stopped campaign
  achieved and tell the owner so.

**The change.** `[agent] campaign_failed_sessions` (3; 0 never): that many failed sessions in a row stop the campaign
with a ledger note that says why, told as `campaign_max_sessions`' stop is. `achieve_campaign(..., only_active=True)`
achieves only an active campaign in one conditional write; the tool refuses a claim on a campaign that is not active,
before the checker's model call and after it. The owner's Mark achieved is unchanged.

**Not changed.** A follow-up that the store refuses still leaves an active campaign with no session planned until the
daemon starts again, and the page does not say that the emergency stop holds a session back; both noted by the review.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_campaigns.py`: failed, failed, done, failed, failed keep it active, the third failure in
a row stops it with its note; a claim on a stopped campaign leaves it stopped, on an active one achieves it.
