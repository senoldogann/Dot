### 1153. A press says at once what lies in front of its element, and goes through the element's own label

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** The owner: browser use, computer use and every tool are to be fast and truly robust.
Measured read-only in the owner's store over the last 7 days (1,075 `browser_act` calls): the step model is quick
(DeepSeek flash, 1.7 s median), and the clicks' tail is long (median 0.42 s, 95th percentile 7 s). Among the failed
steps, 24 presses waited the whole `action_timeout_s` (8 s) to be told that something lies in front of their element
(Playwright's "intercepts pointer events"): page dialogs and portals (X, Notion, TikTok, Workday), cookie banners, a
sticky navbar, an editor container, and several times the element's own label (Shopify's Polaris radios, a
`<label for="development">`, Bugcrowd's "Remember this device's MFA"), whose field a person would press through it.

What must hold: nothing is sent by the look, a cover that goes or changes within the two looks (3 s) still lets the
press go, a press is never sent to another control than the one named, and what was done is read back as before. A
cover that stays longer than the looks and goes before the press's 8 s (a slow loading mask) is told now where the
press went before: the run presses again once it went.

- `driver.py` `_through_own_label`: before a click, double click, right click, check or uncheck, Playwright's own trial
  of it (every actionability check, the cookie banner's locator handler among them, nothing dispatched) looks twice for
  at most 1.5 s (`_COVER_LOOK_MS`, `_COVER_LOOKS`). The same cover named on both looks raises the existing covered
  failure at once (not sent, its name and the advice to close it, as after 8 s before). Any other end of a look (the
  element takes the press, is not yet shown, still moves, or the cover went or changed: an animation, a toast, a
  loading mask, a banner its decliner closed between the looks) leaves the press to its own time, less the looks'
  (`_LEAST_PRESS_MS`; other steps keep their whole time). Anything else that ends a look (the page went on, the
  element left it) is a step not sent (`StepNotSent`), since a look sends nothing.
- A cover that is the element's own label (`_OWN_LABEL_SCRIPT`: the label whose `control` the element is, the point's
  own control being no other link, button or field) has a click, check or uncheck sent with `force`, which lands on the
  label as a person's press does; check and uncheck read the box back as before. A double or right click never is.

Decided after an independent red-team read (Sonnet, one question): its blocker, a cover of 1.5 to 8 s turned into a
failure by one look, is why a cover is judged on two looks and only when unchanged; its label rule was narrowed from
"the label contains the element" (a label of several controls, a link of the label's text) to `label.control` with no
other control at the point, and to the presses a label takes; the press keeps only what is left of its time. Measured
before: a trial costs about 30 ms on a plain button, 1.5 s on a covered one (whose log names the cover); an overlay that
goes after 0.7 s passes the trial at 0.86 s.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_browser.py` (a dialog's cover told in under 6 s, not sent; a styled checkbox under its own
label checked through it and read back, a link inside a label not pressed; a mask that goes after 2 s lets the press
go), which fail without the change but the mask's; the browser smoke files of steps, effects, verified steps, sign-in
and secret fields (98 tests).
