### 569. A reply the answer repeats with a few lines edited shows once

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** Item 567 dropped a reply that the run's answer repeats when only its first line was
reworded; read against a week of the owner's runs (372 replies of runs with an answer), it dropped 48 of the 49 replies
the answer repeats nearly word for word. The one left showed a plan of 9,590 characters twice: the finish had written
two paths out whole (`.../oulu-meta-ads-plan-2026-10.md` became the path under the workspace) and made a comma a
semicolon.

**As built.**
- `dom.js` `repeatsAnswer`: a reply goes when every line of it is held in a line of the answer, in order, the answer
  free to add up to three lines before each (`heldIn`): the words of the line are words of the answer's line in their
  order, the marks of a sentence and the quotes at a word's ends aside (`bare`; a sign, a unit or a currency stays part
  of the word), and a path cut short (`...` or `…` at its head) stands for a word of the answer's line that ends as it
  does. The reply's first line may still be the answer's first line reworded (`rewordedFrom`, item 567). The reply holds
  twenty words and more after its first line, and at least 80% of the answer, so a note that is a small part of the
  answer stays. A changed word, number or "not" leaves a line the reply's own, and the reply is shown.
- `docs/daemon.md` says it.

**Tests.** `tests/js/dom.test.js`: the plan with two paths written out and a semicolon goes, and so does a reply the
answer adds a line before or after; a reply with "do not run" where the answer says "run", "Week 30" for "Week 3",
"Turku" for "Oulu", or a reply that is a third of the answer stays; the cases of item 567 are unchanged. Measured with
the real `dom.js` over the 372 replies of the week: the replies the answer repeats nearly word for word (49) all go,
against 48 with item 567's rule and 47 with the exact comparison before it, and none of the 323 others does. A sign or
a unit changed, two words that traded places, a "not" that moved, a short path that ends otherwise, and a first line
reworded from a line the answer added before it keep the reply.

Not built: an answer that rewrites the reply (three of the week, the same report in other sentences) shows both, as a
reply may hold what the answer left out; drawing such a reply folded under the answer is a choice for the owner.
