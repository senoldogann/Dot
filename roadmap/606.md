### 606. Every screen of the web UI fits a phone

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The owner uses Dot from the iPhone over Tailscale, and sent a screenshot of Customize ›
Connectors: the Popular cards were "very ugly", their words pushed to the right under a 48 px mark, starting at a
different place on each card. That card is item 604's (the `.plugin-section[data-section="popular"]` rules), and the
composer's microphone and bar are item 605's, and this item leaves both alone, as it leaves the Schedules page's Morning
brief and Watchers (item 609) and, but for one card below, the Agents page's list of agents and team cards (item 610),
which other items rebuilt while this one was made. This item looks for everything else of that kind. The audit was made
on `d7347e52`; the item is rebased on `main` at `9675acdb` (item 612), and its tests and a new audit of
every screen were run again there.

**The audit.** A daemon of its own in a throwaway home, seeded (runs through the scripted model: one done with a long
answer, one with a plan and a helper, one that asks, one that waits for an approval, one at work, one that failed;
notes, lessons, a note in the trash, a schedule, a campaign, agents, rules, a harness version in use and one saved, two
nights and a lesson, a plugin put there by hand), shot every route at 390 by 844, a touch screen (`has_touch`,
`is_mobile`, so that `(pointer: coarse)` holds), a screen at a time (a full-page shot loses the touch media query), the
pages in WebKit and in Chrome, light and dark, and what a tap opens in WebKit, light: the home, a chat (done, planning
with a helper, asking, waiting for an approval, at work, failed), the details' five tabs, the sidebar, Schedules,
Campaigns and a campaign, Agents and an agent, Team (Handoffs, and a team's page, since item 610), Settings and each of
its twelve tabs, Customize's three tabs, a plugin's page and a service's, Diagnostics, the licences, the controls, the
sign-in, and what a tap opens (the composer's menus, a chat's tools, the reactions, the forms and dialogs, the model
list, the file beside a run), and the 22 pages of a Dot with nothing in it, as a first opening shows them. Each shot of
the light pages in WebKit was read, the others where they could differ, and each page measured: the page's side scroll,
a box past the window that no box scrolls, a box squeezed under its own words, a touch target under 32 px, and a text an
ellipsis cut while its box had room.

**What was wrong, and what was done.**

- *Customize › Plugins cut the name of a plugin* ("Creative Desi…", "MCP Build…", "Customer Supp…") with 60 px to spare
  on its line. WebKit sizes a box of its own (the name is a flex item) by the width of its text with the space of the
  variable font's regular weight, and draws the bold name a third of a pixel wider a space: the box was a hair short,
  and `text-overflow: ellipsis` took the last letters of every name of two words. `.gallery-card-name` has a hair of
  negative word spacing (`-0.03em`), which nobody sees and which leaves the text a hair shorter than its box.
- *A sentence squeezed by a button*: the token this browser keeps (Settings › General) stood in a column of 150 px, six
  lines, beside "Forget the token here"; Appearance's "These two follow Neutral…" the same beside "Follow the theme". On
  a phone the row wraps and the sentence has at least 15 rem before the button goes under it.
- *Touch targets under 32 px*: the tabs of the details not chosen (31 px wide), the name of a file the run changed (22
  px high), the link from an approval to a rule (20), Self-improvement's "Write your own" and "Texts and notes" (30),
  the head of a licence's notice (22), a model's star (28) and Change in the list of models (28). Each box grows to 32
  or more on a touch screen of a phone's width, the names and the link by padding and an equal negative margin, so that
  the line they stand on does not move.
- *A toast over the composer*: the toasts stood at the bottom right of the page, over the composer, its send button and
  the stop button of a run at work, three seconds and a half, and nine for an error. On a phone they stand under the
  bar, across the page, and a toast that offers an action (item 616's Reload) has its button at the right of its words.
- *The file beside a run*: the head of the file's panel gave "Open on the Mac" 170 px and left the name "flights…" and
  its facts "Text, 5…". On a phone the button is its icon (its words stay for a screen reader).
- *A forgotten note in Memory* had Restore and Delete for good in a column at the side of its words, which stood in 140
  px and three or four lines; the buttons now stand in a row under the words (the proposals' Keep and Forget as well).
- *A version's row in Self-improvement* slid the page 9 px sideways on a phone 320 px wide: its three buttons (the
  texts, Use, Set aside) were a line that does not wrap. On a phone they wrap.
- *The two buttons of a service's key form* (Customize › Connectors › GitHub › Add) stood against each other, Add and
  Cancel with no space between them. Item 615 made the form a dialog, whose foot keeps its buttons 8 px apart, before
  this change merged: nothing is changed here, and a test guards it.
- *A ready team the owner has already* (Agents › Add ready-made agents, item 610, merged after the audit and shot again
  on the rebased tree): its "In your teams" badge stood beside its name and took 100 px of the 253 the card's head has,
  so the description stood in 141 px, seven lines high. On a phone the head wraps and the badge stands under the words,
  which have the head's width: three lines. A phone-layout rule only, as was asked of the Agents page; a wide window
  keeps the badge beside the name.
- *How long ago a chat ended* ("3m ago", "just now") broke in two lines in the list of chats a skill can be saved from,
  beside a long goal; and *a version in the list of Python packages* of the licences broke in the middle of a number
  ("0.143." over "1", "2026.1" over "0.19"). The time keeps a line; a version leaves a place to break after each of its
  dots (`licences.js`, the one change of a script of this item, `<wbr>`) and breaks nowhere else; and the cells of that
  table keep 8 px at their sides on a phone, not 16, so that a name has room. (A version that never breaks, `nowrap`,
  was tried first: the longest, "2026.9.29.20261017", then took 178 px of the table and the page grew by a fifth.)
