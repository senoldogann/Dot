### 1111. A failed call is answered with the lessons about its tool

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** The next step the owner asked for after the self-repair night (item 1110): the lessons
should reach more runs. Measured in the owner's Dot on 2026-11-10: 448 lessons kept, and in the last day 3 of 60
runs were handed one; in two days 6 lessons were handed at a run's start of 842 notes, 73 by a search, none on a site.
Two causes:

- A run's notes are chosen at its start by the words of its goal. A lesson is about a tool or a site
  ("`browser_act` takes an action from its list"), and its words are rarely a goal's; many are in another language
  than the goal (Turkish, Finnish, English).
- 432 of the 448 were written by a run that had read outside content, as most runs read pages: such a note taints the
  run that reads it, so it goes to no run that has not read such content itself.

Now a call that fails is answered, after its error (added after the error is cut to the run's room), with the lessons
that name its tool, chosen as a site's lessons are (item 308): visible to the run's agent, not resting (expired,
retired, unused or of no help lately), not handed to it yet, and never one saved after outside content (item 384: such
a note is handed to no run with no reader in between; only `memory_search` finds it). The most used first, two at most
(`agent/failure_lessons.py`), framed as a site's lessons and rendered as the notes are (id, author, date), counted as
handed (`failure`). A tool whose name is a plain word (`shell`) is named only in backticks. Nothing is handed with
lessons off.

What this reaches today is small: of the owner's 16 lessons not saved after outside content, one names a tool that
failed the day before. The 432 others wait on the owner's choice of whether a lesson the second look kept may lose its
mark (asked 2026-11-10); this item is the way they would reach a run.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_failure_lessons.py` (which lessons are chosen and in what order), `tests/smoke/
test_failure_lessons.py` (a failed `read_file` of a run is answered with the lesson, not with one saved after outside
content, and the same lesson is not handed twice; on the old code the error came alone).
