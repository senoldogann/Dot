### 1111. A failed call is answered with the lessons about its tool

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** The next step the owner asked for after the self-repair night (item 1110): the lessons
should reach more runs. Measured in the owner's Dot on 2026-11-10: 448 lessons kept, and in the last day 3 of 60
runs were handed one; in two days 6 lessons were handed at a run's start of 842 notes, 73 by a search, none on a site.
Two causes:

- A run's notes are chosen at its start by the words of its goal. A lesson is about a tool or a site
  ("`browser_act` takes an action from its list"), and its words are rarely a goal's; many are in another language
  than the goal (Turkish, Finnish, English).
- 432 of the 448 were written by a run that had read outside content, as most runs read pages: such a note taints the
  run that reads it, so it goes to no run that has not read such content itself.

Now a call that fails is answered, after its error, with the lessons that name its tool as a word, that hold (not
replaced, merged, resting or expired), that may be handed to the run (Dot's or its agent's own) and that it was not
handed yet: the most used first, two at most (`agent/failure_lessons.py`), counted as handed (`failure`). A lesson
written after outside content is handed only to a run that has read such content itself, so that no run is marked
untrusted by an error. Nothing is handed with lessons off.

On the owner's lessons, the tools that failed most the day before are named by 25 (shell), 37 (browser_eval), 20
(browser_wait_text), 17 (browser_act), 21 (read_file) and 12 (edit_file) of them.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_failure_lessons.py` (which lessons are chosen and in what order), `tests/smoke/
test_failure_lessons.py` (a failed `read_file` of a run is answered with the lesson, not with one written after outside
content, and the same lesson is not handed twice; on the old code the error came alone).
