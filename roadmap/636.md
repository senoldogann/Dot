### 636. In Dot.app, a microphone macOS refuses opens System Settings, and an old Dot.app says it is the old one

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner pressed the composer's microphone in Dot.app and read, twice for two presses,
the red toast of item 593: "macOS has not given Dot the microphone or its speech recognition. Allow Dot in System
Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and Speech Recognition; …". In their words (Turkish): instead of showing this
error, it should open the permission page on the computer directly, because the permission looks given but it still
shows this.

**Why, read on the owner's Mac without touching it.** `/Applications/Dot.app` is 0.1.0 (bundle `dev.dot.desktop`,
Developer ID team 79DZ4AA4DW, hardened runtime), built on 2026-10-20. Its `Info.plist` has no
`NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` and no `NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription`, and its signature carries no entitlements,
so no `com.apple.security.device.audio-input`. All three came with item 594 on 2026-10-23
(`desktop/src-tauri/Info.plist` and `Dot.entitlements`). WebKit refuses the microphone and speech recognition to an app
that does not name why it wants them, before macOS's permission is consulted, and System Settings can show Dot switched
on all the same. **For this app, opening System Settings fixes nothing.** Only a newer Dot.app does; its updater reads
`https://github.com/senoldogann/Dot/releases/latest/download/latest.json` (the latest release is v0.1.0) and offers the
next release when one is out. So the owner's request is right for a Dot.app that asks macOS for the microphone, and
wrong for the one they have: the page has to be able to tell which it is in, and the daemon, which can read the app,
tells it.

**As built.**
- `src/dotagent/voice/desktopapp.py`. `DesktopApp.state()` finds the Dot.app that runs
  (`NSRunningApplication.runningApplicationsWithBundleIdentifier_` for `DESKTOP_APP`) and says whether it *listens*:
  `Contents/Info.plist` gives both reasons in words, and its signature has no hardened runtime or carries
  `com.apple.security.device.audio-input` true. The flags come from `codesign -dv` (`flags=0x10000(runtime)`), the
  entitlements from `codesign -d --entitlements - --xml` (the plist on stdout, nothing when there are none; asked only
  of a hardened app). An app that is not signed at all has no runtime to keep the microphone from it. The answer is
  `ready`, `old` or `unknown` (none runs). Two apps that run: one that listens is enough, since the page cannot be told
  from the other. What was read of an app is kept per path while its `Info.plist` and the seal of its signature
  (`_CodeSignature/CodeResources`, which every signing rewrites) keep their modification times, so that a Dot.app the
  updater replaced is read again at the next press of a daemon that has run for days. The programs and the apps found
  are given to `DesktopApp` (`run`, `running`) and the opener to the router (`voice_router(desktop_app, opener)`), so
  that a test needs no real app and opens nothing; `DOT_OPEN` names the `open` of a daemon under test.
- `POST /api/voice/allow` (`server/routes_voice.py`; the models in `server/views.py`): `{"pane": "microphone" |
  "speech"}`, from this Mac's own address only (`on_this_mac`, refused as `routes_vault.py` refuses: 422), behind the
  token. For an `old` app it opens nothing and answers `{"opened": false, "app": "old"}`; otherwise it runs `open` with
  `x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.preference.security?Privacy_Microphone` (`…?Privacy_SpeechRecognition` for
  `speech`) and answers `{"opened": true, "app": "ready" | "unknown"}`. An app or a `codesign` that cannot be read is a
  `DesktopError` (500) with what was asked and what came back, never an answer.
- The page, in Dot.app only (`voicePlace(...) === "app"`, `voice.js`): a dictation that fails with `not-allowed` or
  `service-not-allowed`, and a recording whose microphone is refused (`NotAllowedError`, `PermissionDeniedError`,
  `SecurityError`), come with `{ allow: pane }` in the `offer` of `problem(words, offer)` (`problemOffer`,
  `captureOffer`; the pane is `allowPane`: Speech Recognition when the error's message names speech recognition, else
  Microphone). `microphone.js` posts `/api/voice/allow` (`macvoice.js` `allowAtSettings`) and says one toast per press,
  whose words are `allowToast`'s: opened, "System Settings is open at Microphone: switch Dot on there, and under Speech
  Recognition too." (or at Speech Recognition, naming Microphone), as the calmer `ok` toast since it is help; old, "This
  Dot.app was built before Dot asked macOS for the microphone, so macOS gives it none, whatever System Settings shows.
  The next Dot.app asks for it, and Dot offers it as soon as it is out.", as an error; both with the sentence about
  macOS Dictation that the refusal always had. When the ask fails, or the answer says neither, the words of item 593
  stand. The sets of refusals (`RECOGNITION_REFUSED`, `CAPTURE_REFUSED`) are shared by the words and the offers, so that
  they cannot part. A browser on a Mac and a phone keep their words and ask for nothing.
- `docs/daemon.md` (the composer's microphone, the desktop app's, the API table), `docs/safety.md`,
  `docs/development.md`.

**What the route lets happen.** It runs `/usr/bin/codesign` (twice at most) on the bundle path `NSRunningApplication`
gave and `open` (or what `DOT_OPEN` names) once with one of two fixed addresses; both by an argument list, with no
shell, a closed stdin and ten seconds. Nothing of the request but the pane's name reaches them. Its output is read into
models before it is used (the flags by their pattern, the entitlements and the `Info.plist` as property lists); anything
else is an error. It changes no setting and sends nothing out, and the log has the path of the app and four booleans.

**Measured, on this Mac.**
- The owner's Dot.app, read with the real seams: running (pid and path found), hardened, not entitled, no reasons:
  `old`.
- `NSRunningApplication.runningApplicationsWithBundleIdentifier_` is live in a process with no run loop, as the daemon
  is: a throwaway background app launched with `open -g -j` was seen at the next poll (every 0.25 s) after it started,
  and gone at the poll after it ended 20 s later, while `NSWorkspace.runningApplications` saw neither (what
  `desktop/apps.py` says of it).
- `codesign -dv` writes to stderr (`flags=0x2(adhoc)`, `0x10002(adhoc,runtime)`, the owner's `0x10000(runtime)`); the
  entitlements go to stdout as one line of XML, nothing when there are none, `<false/>` for a key set false; an unsigned
  bundle says `code object is not signed at all` and ends 1. Read from throwaway bundles signed ad hoc.
- `Privacy_Microphone` and `Privacy_SpeechRecognition` are the anchors macOS 26's own System Settings names (its Privacy
  extension; read, not opened). System Settings was not opened, here or by a test.

**Not changed, and what to know.**
- `desktop/src-tauri` (item 594 stays as it is): a Dot.app that asks macOS for the microphone needs the next release.
  Until it is out, the owner's Dot.app says it is the old one and shows no pane.
- The `ok` toast of an opened pane stays the 3.5 seconds `ui.js` gives it, while System Settings comes to the front at
  once and Dot.app's window, with the toast, goes behind it. The words are for the moment before that, and the pane
  itself is the help. A longer stay would be a change to `toast()`.
- The words of `old` name the usual cause (a Dot.app built before item 594). `desktop/release.sh` refuses a bundle with
  neither reason or without the entitlement, so a release cannot ship one that is old in another way.
