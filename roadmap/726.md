### 726. The passwords command lines take as words of their own are masked

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** From the outside review of 2026-10-27 (its finding 2): `redact_secrets` masked
`--password=…`, `PGPASSWORD=…`, an Authorization header and the password of a URL, but left `curl -u admin:hunter2`,
`--user`, `--proxy-user`, `docker login -p`, `mysql -pSecret`, `sshpass -p`, `gpg --passphrase`, `htpasswd -b`, `DB_PASS=`,
`pw=`, `sessionid=` and `aws configure set aws_secret_access_key …` as they stood, in the step's events, its approval
card and the logs, when the value was not one Dot holds (those are masked wherever they stand, item 551).

**The change.** `core/text.py`: the forms of the command lines that take a password as a word (`curl -u`/`-U`,
`--user`/`--proxy-user` of any tool, `docker`/`podman`/`nerdctl`/`buildah`/`helm login -p`, `sshpass -p`, the `-p` of
the MySQL and MariaDB clients with the password joined to it, the last word of `htpasswd -b`, `aws configure set` of a
secret), each keeping what stands before the password; and `passphrase`, `session id` and, after no letter, `pass`, `pw`
and `pwd` among the names of a secret. A path given to `pwd` or `PWD` is the working folder, not a password, and stays.
The stream mask (item 551) probes with the same masks, so a reply that streams such a line waits for its value.

**The reviews of the first push.** The pre-push review: the masks of `curl`, `docker login` and the MySQL clients looked
for the tool's name, then for the option anywhere after it, and a long line of the names alone took time that grew with
its cube (500 repeats of `docker login ` took 2.5 s, a thousand more than 20 s), on text a page or a tool's answer can
hold. Those masks, with `sshpass`, `aws configure set` and `htpasswd`, now read each command of a line once
(`_mask_command_passwords`, split at `|`, `;`, `&` and line ends), and a test holds long lines of each to a moment. The
independent review: the forms joined or clustered (`curl -uadmin:pw`, `-su`, `docker login -pX`), an option before the
password's (`sshpass -v -p`), `htpasswd -nb` (no file) and `-C 12` (a cost read as the file), and `aws` with its options
before `configure` were not masked, and `MYSQL_PWD=/x` was kept as a folder: each is read now, and only `PWD` and
`OLDPWD` name a folder.

**The review of the second push:** `sshpass`'s `-p` was looked for anywhere after its name, so the port of the command
it runs (`ssh -p 2222`) was masked: its own options are read alone, up to that command. And `{"PWD": "/Users/x"}` was
masked whole in a JSON value while the same line kept it: a field named for the working folder given a path stays there
too.

**The review of the third push** (a pass, with nits taken): `pass` among the names of a secret masked the words of a
sentence (`All tests pass: tests/…`, Go's `--- PASS: TestX`): a short name (`pass`, `pw`, `pwd`) names a password given
with `=` or in quotes, not after `: ` where prose writes.

**The review of the fifth push:** the exemption of the third push took in a name with a short name at its end (`DB_PASS:
Tr0ub4dor&3`, `SMTP_PASS: …`, as YAML writes them), whose value was no longer masked: only `pass`, `pw` and `pwd` alone
are a sentence's after `: `; a name with a prefix is a password's there too, as before.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_redaction.py`: each form masked with the words around it kept, and the twins that give
no password left alone (`curl -u admin`, `git push -u origin main`, `mysql -u root -p shop`, `docker login
--password-stdin`, `PWD=/Users/…`, `bypass=on`, `passes: 3`, `session_id: 42`); the long text of short words is still
masked in a moment.
