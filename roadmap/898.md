### 898. The office: one chat that goes on, names that glide, a bigger room

**Status: done (2026-11-01).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-01): "the names over the agents' heads move jerkily;
every message I send makes a new chat, which is wrong; the office could be bigger and wider; we should start a chat
there and go on in it fluently."

- **One chat.** `startComposer` takes `follow`, which says where the next message goes: the office's says the agent's
  newest chat when it is one of the owner's (no parent run; not a schedule's, a watcher's, Dot's own initiative's or
  a notice's), and the run at work there while it works or waits for the owner (blocked); else a new chat. A message
  to a run at work waits in that chat's queue (`POST /threads/{id}/queue`), or with **Steer** chosen under Settings ›
  General › Messages (item 230) steers it (`POST /runs/{id}/steer`), as a message in the chat does. The chat keeps where the owner scrolled it while the run works, and
  goes to its foot when it was there or a turn came. **New chat** in the panel starts the next one afresh. The panel shows that chat's last six turns
  (`GET /runs?thread_id=`, the runs of the chat at the top), the owner's words at the right and the answers at the
  left, read again when the agent's newest run or its state changes; the run at work shows its steps and words live
  under them, as before. What the owner sends the run at work stands in the chat at once, then as the run's `steer`
  events say it (the owner's, which vouch; Dot's notes do not), with a word that it was queued or sent, and is let go
  once the run read it or ended; the run a message started is taken with its chat at once, so the next message goes
  on in it; the steer's key names its run, and a steer keeps the picks for the next new task and says so (item 259).
- **Names that glide.** A tag that must stand above another glided nowhere: it jumped at the frame two agents passed,
  and its place was rounded to whole pixels. Its lift now glides toward where it must stand, its place is not rounded
  (`translate3d`), and its size is read when its words change, when it is chosen, on a resize, and one tag a frame in
  turn, rather than every tag at every frame (which laid the page out at each frame).
- **A bigger room.** Three pods across instead of two, and 20 by 15 metres at least instead of 14 by 11.
- `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` says so.
