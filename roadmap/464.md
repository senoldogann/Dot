### 464. A template's suggested schedule becomes real, switched off

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The last part of the schedule-as-agent design (items 462 and 463 were its core and its
chat carry): a template's card named a schedule in words ("Weekdays at 9:00: ten new prospects") that nothing made. The
critique asked the owner to read the ten goals a scheduled run of an agent would follow before they ship; the owner
approved them with the rest on 2026-10-21 ("Onaylıyorum, hepsini birleştir"), and they are in the item's report.
**As built.** `AgentTemplate` (`agent/templates.py`) gains `cron` and `task`, empty exactly when `schedule` is: the
ten that suggest one carry a cron expression that fires when the card says and the whole goal of one run, which the
agent's instructions then guide (the drafts-only rule stays in the instructions alone, on the critique's word).
`Store.add_schedule` takes whether the schedule is switched on; `create_schedule_off` (`scheduler/plans.py`) checks a
new schedule's options, agent and time as any schedule's but not the room for one more switched on, which switching it
on checks (`enable_schedule`); `POST /api/schedules` takes `enabled` (true by default) and adds one switched off for
false. The Agents page: a card whose template suggests a schedule offers **Also add this schedule, switched off until
you read its goal** (a 40 px row on a phone); ticked, Use template makes the agent, then the schedule as the agent's,
with the template's time and goal, switched off, and a schedule that could not be added leaves the agent made and the
toast says why. An agent's page lists the schedules its runs run as under **Schedules** (read with `GET
/api/schedules`, as the delete dialog counts them), each with its switch, its name, its goal in full, when it runs and
**Edit** on the Schedules page. Yolo is untouched: nothing here asks or refuses anything, and a schedule's approvals
are its agent's (item 462). Tests: `tests/unit/test_agent_templates.py` (each suggested schedule fires when its card
says, keeps the scheduler's least gap and fits a schedule's request; both empty where none is suggested),
`tests/smoke/test_scheduler.py` (a switched-off schedule takes no room, its time is still checked, switching it on
checks the room), `tests/smoke/test_agent_templates.py` (the offer on a card that suggests a schedule only; ticked, the
agent and its switched-off schedule with the template's time and goal; the agent's page shows the goal and switches it
on; 1280 light and 390 dark). Docs: agents-and-campaigns.md, daemon.md.
