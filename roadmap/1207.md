### 1207. A model whose account ran out of money tells the owner, once a day for each provider

**Status: done (2026-11-16).** The owner's scorecard of Dot's real runs (175 runs, 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-16, read from
their store) found that a spent balance stopped work without a word: OpenCode Go answered "HTTP 402: Insufficient
balance" on 2026-11-11 and three schedules failed before anyone looked. The router already paused such a model for
`helper_cooldown_s`, and the pause only steered helper and standby choices.

What holds:

- **Told when the pause begins, not at every failed call.** `ModelRouter.on_money_pause` listeners are called when a
  model that was answering (no pause, or one that ended) begins a pause for one of `MONEY_REASONS` ("out of credits",
  "quota exhausted", "not in the plan"). A rate limit, a refused key, a missing key or a transport error tells nothing.
- **Once a day for each provider.** `notify/money.py`'s `MoneyTeller` keeps when each provider was told, in memory; a
  restart tells once more. The tell goes through `tell_owner` (phone, desktop, messaging apps) with the provider's own
  words (its message, secrets already redacted by the connector, no debug context) and opens `#/settings/models`.
- **The call's error stays the caller's.** A listener that fails is logged (`money_pause_not_told`), as a failed
  record of the call is, so the run sees the provider's 402, not the notice's failure.

Not done here: Brave Search's own 402 ("Prepaid credit balance is insufficient", 18 results in 5 runs) is reported
inside the `web_search` result by the dotagent-web-research package, which skips to the next engine; telling it needs
a change there.
