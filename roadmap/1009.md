### 1009. Dot in the iPhone's Share sheet

**Status: done (2026-11-06).** **Why.** The next step of item 997 the owner asked for on 2026-11-06: send a page from
Safari, a text or pictures from another app to Dot without copying them over.

**Built.** iOS: a share extension `DotShare` (`dev.dotagent.phone.share`): a page's address, a text and up to four
pictures (JPEG, 2,048 px on the longer side at most) go to Dot as a new chat, with the owner's words of what to do with
it ("Look at this." when none); each picture through `POST /api/attachments`, then `POST /api/runs`, with the phone's
key; nothing is sent until Send. The daemon's host moves to the app group's defaults (`HostStore`, an install's host
moved there once), which the extension reads with the key of the shared Keychain access group. `DaemonClient` returns
the daemon's answer, and keeps a file and starts a chat. Docs: `docs/phone.md`, `ios/README.md`.

**Checked.** Built and signed for iOS (the extension's App ID made by Xcode's automatic signing).
