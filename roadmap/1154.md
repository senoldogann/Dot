### 1154. A wait for a text finds it as the page view names it

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** The owner: browser use is to be fast and truly robust. Measured read-only in the owner's
store over two weeks: 69 `browser_wait_text` calls did not find their text, and 17 of them (415 s of waiting, several
the whole 15 to 60 s) had their words in the very page view they returned: `textbox "Subject"` (a field's
placeholder), `button "Select image 6"` (an icon button's `aria-label`), `img "Gistsmith gallery image"` (an alt text),
`button "Gönderiyi planla"`. The run reads the page view, which names elements by those; the wait looked only for
visible words, so it waited for what was already on the screen.

- `driver.py` `_shown`: what a wait for a text (`wait_for_text_or_dialog`), its look into frames (`text_in_frames`) and
  the check of a step's `expect_text` (`text_shown`, item 1145) find: the visible words, or a visible element named by
  them as the view names it, by `get_by_label` (an `aria-label` or a label's words), `get_by_placeholder` and
  `get_by_alt_text`. Hidden elements still do not count. The tool's description is unchanged (its words are the view's
  already, and the first step's token budget has a margin of a few tokens).

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_browser.py::test_a_wait_finds_words_the_page_view_shows_as_names` (a placeholder, an
aria-label and an alt text that come after half a second are found, a hidden region is not), which fails without the
change; the wait and verified-step tests of the browser smoke files.
