**Status: done (2026-11-20).** From the web UI audit of 2026-11-20, each proved by a search of the whole tree (the
JavaScript, the HTML, the tests, the Rust shell) and by the class names a template string could build.

  (the titles of Watchers, Your schedules and the Evening check-in). It uses `--tracking-title` now, as the other titles
  do: a difference of 0.01em.

  `--on-brand`, `--scrim-strong` and `--shadow-card` (nothing reads them). `--mark-green` stays, in the family of marks
  a template may name.
