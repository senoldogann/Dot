### 858. The apps, skills and Plan first a message went with are words of its text in their colours, with no chip

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, with two screenshots. The first showed a sent message with a
chip under it, "rea-mcp" in a bordered pill. The second showed another coding agent's composer, where a skill stands in the line as
"⬡ Agent Reach" in violet, with no border: "mcp leri, skilleri ve pluginleri böyle etiketlemek yerine yazının içinde
böyle kenarlıksız şekilde farklı renkte verelim".

- `picks.js`: `sentPicks(data, inline)` draws the picks of a sent message as a `span`, words of its line with a space
  between them, so that a selection reads them as words.
- `messages.js`: `showMessagePicks` puts them at the head of the message's first paragraph. A message that opens with
  no paragraph (a list, a quote, code) has them on a line of their own over it.
- `app.css`: a pick is its mark and its name in the colour of its name, with no border and no ground, under the box and
  in the text alike. In the bubble, which is dark in both schemes (ink in the light one), the colours take their dark
  scheme's tones and Plan first takes the bubble's ink, so each reads on it. A server's mark keeps its service's ink on
  its tile (item 530).
- `outline.js`: the outline's line of a message is its words, without the picks at their head.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_composer_picks.py` (in Chrome): the picks stand in the first paragraph, before the goal's words,
  and a message that opens with a list has them in a paragraph of its own over the list. Its screenshots were looked at, at 1280 and 390 wide, light
  and dark: the picks stand in the message's line, readable on the bubble in both schemes, and under the box with their ×.
