### 833. The box is one line, as a messaging app's: the "+", the words and the microphone, the run's choices behind the "+"

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner ("uygulamadaki tüm ekranları ve tasarımları incele, olabildiğince
minimal ama eksik olmayan, yumuşak, Grok Bot gibi yap"), with screenshots of that app. The second of the six stages of
the design review of 2026-10-30, after the bubbles of item 832: the other app's box is one line of a "+", the words and
a microphone, where Dot's was the words on a line of their own and under them a bar of five pills, a meter and a send,
with words in the empty box about what the at sign and the slash open.

- `composer.js`: the box is `data-quiet` until the owner asks for the run's choices, and the "+" menu, where a box has
  choices, says **Model and options**, which shows them, then **Hide the model and options**; the choice is kept in that
  browser (`dot.composer.choices`) for every box made after it. What the at sign and the slash open is the box's
  tooltip.
- `app.css`: while it is quiet, the words share the line with the "+" and the controls; the choices go, but YOLO stays
  as its pill (with no tint, item 500), since the run then asks nothing, and so does the context meter once the chat is
  near full; with a microphone, the send waits for words or a file, and the microphone stays after words, so that more
  can be dictated (items 574 and 605). A Stop is always in sight (item 120). `run.css`: one line on a phone too.
  `choices.js`: the options pill marks YOLO for the rule.
- `runs.js` and `run.js`: the empty box says "Message Miro", the name the owner calls Dot by (item 420), and "Follow up"
  in the box of a run; a run under way keeps its "Steer this task (or /btw)" and "Queue a message (or /btw)".

Kept as they were: every choice, one press further away, and the whole bar as it was once the choices are shown; the
"+" menu's files and teaching; the Stop and the queue; the phone's layout with the choices shown (item 374), the
microphone under the words.

**Checked.** The owner's Dot read in a headless Chrome of its own with these sheets and scripts served in their place:
the home and a chat, at a desk's width and a phone's, light and dark, empty and with words, the "+" menu open, and the
choices away and shown. `tests/smoke/test_composer.py`: the box is one line at a desk's width and a phone's, the
microphone stands alone until words come and the send then comes beside it, the "+" shows and hides the choices and
keeps that, and YOLO stays in sight with no tint. `tests/smoke/test_voice.py`: the microphone is right of the words and
left of the send on one line, and where it stood with the choices shown. The tests that read the choices show them
first, as the owner would; `tests/smoke/test_first_chat.py` reads the box's new words and its tooltip.
