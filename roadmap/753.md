### 753. A tab that answers nothing says so

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Measured:** on 2026-10-26 a Slack tab of the owner's run stopped answering:
`browser_open` of app.slack.com took 32 s and failed with "waiting for the page to settle failed ... the document was not
parsed within 30000 ms", though the page had loaded (`goto` had seen its DOMContentLoaded); then a snapshot of it, and of
another site in the same tab, failed after 30 s each, and a new tab could not be opened. Found by the review of browser
use: the 30 s bound covered the whole wait for the page's script context, so a page that answers nothing at all read as
one still loading; and the wait for the page to be quiet had no bound of its own.

**The change.** `driver._wait_quiet` first asks the page for `1`, within the navigation timeout: a tab that does not
answer fails with "the tab did not answer within 30 s: its page is stuck, or Chrome is overloaded. Close this tab
(browser_close_tab) and open the page in a new one"; the parse has what is left of the bound. A first cut gave the
probe 10 s; the code review found that a page busy for 10 to 30 s (a heavy app starting on a loaded Mac), which passed
before, would then be told to be closed, so the probe has the whole bound, as the parse had. The wait for the quiet
window gets a bound of its own (its `max_ms` and the navigation timeout), saying the tab stopped answering.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_stuck_tab.py` (stand-in pages, a bound of 0.2 s): a page whose script never
answers fails saying the tab did not answer and to open the page anew, where the code before said the document was not
parsed; a page that answers after 0.3 s within a bound of 2 s settles.
