### 755. A batch step written as plainly meant is read so

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Measured:** in the owner's runs of the last 30 days, five `browser_act` and
`browser_snapshot` calls failed on their arguments, each a model round trip to write the batch again: a `click` with `x`
and `y` and no ref (meant: `click_at`), a batch ending in `{"action": "snapshot"}`, and a batch with an `upload` step.

**The change.** `Step` reads a `click` with `x` and `y` and no ref as `click_at` (one with a ref and a point still
fails, as it is not plain). `ActArgs` leaves out a step that only looks (`snapshot`, `screenshot`, `look`, with no other
field), since the batch's result shows the page; a batch of nothing else still fails as before. An `upload` step fails
with "upload is not a step: call browser_upload with the ref of the upload control and paths". What a step does once
read, and how it is judged, is unchanged: the policy sees the `click_at` it became.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_step_meaning.py`: the three cases of the owner's runs, the click with a ref and a
point that still fails, the batch of looks only.
