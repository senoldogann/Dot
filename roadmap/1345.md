### 1345. The side panel of a run is an inspector: a head that stays, a body that scrolls and a small sticky head for each section

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** The owner's word of 2026-11-23: a new layout for the right panel of a chat, as a
professional agent tool's inspector has it: clear sections with small sticky heads, tight rows, a tab or segmented
switch if it helps, readable on a desktop and as a sheet on a phone-sized window, on the 8 px scale and the tokens the
sheets already have; every function it has stays (the Lineage with its subagents, the Automations with a pencil, a play
and a switch, the details), and it stays closed by default (it is: the choice of `dot.inspector` starts off).

What it was (screenshots under `workspace/shots/drawer/before-*`): a card whose tab strip was five small underlined
labels and whose every section had its own head and its own scroll (`max-height: 40dvh`, the Automations' list cut at
four rows), so that a tab of three sections scrolled in three places and a head went out of sight with its list.

What it is (`after-*`): `inspector.js` builds the card in two parts, the head (grabber, tabs, Close) and
`.inspector-body`, which holds the tabs' panels and is the one place that scrolls. The tabs are a segmented switch: a
track on `--surface-2` with the chosen tab lifted out of it on a fill of its own and a hairline (`--surface` in light,
`--surface-3` in dark), its name shown and the others' icons and counts as before (the container query that cuts the
names is the old one), a finger's height on a touch screen. A section's head (`panelSection`, `panelFold`, the
Automations') is a band, `--surface-2` on a hairline, 28 px, its title in the small size, what sums it up on the same
line at the right with the section's buttons (no longer wrapped under the title), and it is `position: sticky` at the top
of the body, so the head of the section in sight stays while its rows scroll and the next head takes its place. The bodies
are tight (8 px over 12, 16 at the sides); the per-section scrolls and the Automations' own scroll are gone, the rows
(Automations, 40 px with a hairline between them; Lineage as it was) sit on the 8 px scale, the row buttons are 28 px
(36 on a touch screen, with the ring that makes 44). Only the sections that are direct children of a tab get the band: the
sections inside More and the ones in Dot's computer's full view keep their own heads. A phone's sheet keeps nine tenths of
what is seen, its grabber over the head. No function, id or ARIA role changed: the tabs are a `tablist` still, with the
arrow keys.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_panel_layout.py` (closed until asked for; head and body as two parts; the chosen tab lifted
out of a track; the Lineage rows and the Automations' pencil, play and switch present; the sticky head stays at the top of
the body while it scrolls and the head of the panel stays where it was; a column inside the window wide, a sheet as wide
as the screen on a phone; Chrome at 1440x900 and WebKit at 390x844, light and dark), `test_side_panel.py` (the tab
scrolls as one, in the body), the panel, Lineage, Automations, changes, context, file and agent smoke tests, the phone
layout, fit, screens and return tests, `npm run check`.
