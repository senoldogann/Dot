### 495. The composer picks no mode: each message's words do

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Asked by the owner: instead of choosing Task, Chat or Research in the composer, the
system should tell by itself, and the box gain the room. A task already ends at once when it answers without a tool
(`loop.py`), as a chat does, so Auto settles a task. The words narrow to research, which reads and changes nothing,
only when they ask only to look. The owner keeps a way to force a mode.

**As built.**
- `agent/mode_words.py`: `resolved_mode(asked, goal)`.
  - Research for an ask to only look, in English or in Turkish, with the Turkish letters or without them: "read-only",
    "don't change anything", "change nothing", "just research", "salt okunur", "hiçbir şeyi değiştirme", "sadece
    araştır".
  - A task otherwise. The patterns are strong phrases alone: "don't change the colours, only the font" and "sadece
    araştırıp düzelt" are tasks.
  - A mode named is the mode.
- `ChatMode` (store/models.py) is a run's mode or `auto`: what a message asks for and what a chat keeps.
- Where Auto is settled:
  - `POST /api/runs` and a queued message take `mode: "auto"` and settle it before the run starts; the API's own
    default stays a task.
  - A message sent on Auto keeps Auto as its chat's mode, since a chat's first run keeps the mode it ran in.
  - A campaign sent on Auto is a task.
  - The chat's background follow-up and its loop settle Auto by their words.
  - A schedule a chat on Auto makes is a task.
- `choices.js`:
  - `AUTO_MODE`, and `MESSAGE_MODES` for the home's and a chat's composers;
  - the mode pill is hidden on Auto, and its menu leads back to Auto;
  - an agent's editor keeps the three modes (`MODES`), since an agent's mode is kept as a run's.
- The home's box and a chat's dock both offer `/auto`, `/task`, `/chat` and `/research`; the dock had no mode commands
  before. A new chat starts on Auto, and a saved Task, which was the default before, is read as Auto.
- The placeholders say "@ for apps": the at menu offers the accounts too (item 496).

**Tests.**
- `tests/unit/test_mode_words.py`: the words in both languages, and the lookalikes that are tasks.
- `test_composer.py`, `test_chat_choices.py`, `test_dock_choices.py` and `test_new_chat_choices.py` name a mode with
  its command where they clicked the pill, and expect no pill on Auto.
- `test_composer.py`'s popover test and `test_first_chat.py` fail on main before this change, for causes of their own
  (the Options popover's cap, and Return on a touch screen); dot-68's item 486 fixes them.
