### 492. Connect apps: the services in a dialog, from the foot of the sidebar

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Asked by the owner after Grok Bot's "Connect apps": a plain entry always in sight, a
modal catalogue, more categories. Dot had the services (item 316's Customize › Plugins, 65 of them), but on a page
under Campaigns and Schedules, with 29 of them in one Development section, and each tile carrying two chips and a
"+".

**As built.**
- `sidebar.js`: **Connect apps** stands at the foot, over Settings. It is a pill with the plug and the marks of the
  first three MCP servers that are ready (`GET /mcp/servers`, read again when the dialog closes). On a phone it is a
  round button between Settings and the search.
- `connectapps.js`: the dialog.
  - A search, a chip for each section, and **N connected**.
  - **Suggested for you** (the popular services), then each section's first six tiles with **See all**.
  - A tile has the mark, the name over one line of what Dot does, and one thing at its right: its first step (Add, Sign
    in, Allow, Open System Settings), where it stands once connected, or Not tried yet.
  - A tile opens the service's page in the dialog with a way back, which returns the focus to the tile. A service that
    takes a key asks for it there.
  - **Customize** leads to the page.
  - In the desktop shell it keeps 52 px clear of the traffic lights; on a phone it takes the screen.
- `catalogue.js`: what the page and the dialog share, out of `views/plugins.js` (state, steps, polling, key forms,
  tools dialog). The page draws its tiles and its service pages from it as before; `serviceParts` is the service page
  both show.
- `connections/directory.py`: the sections are popular, code, design, data, research, communication, productivity,
  business, support and mac. Design holds Figma, Canva, Miro, Webflow and Wix; Data holds PostHog, Postman, Hugging
  Face and Mapbox; Research the web search and reading services; Support Intercom.
- A drive-by: item 491's `full !== null && full.forUse` is `full?.forUse`, which Biome asked for.

**Tests.**
- `tests/smoke/test_connect_apps.py`:
  - the button over Settings, with the mark of a ready server;
  - the sections and their counts, and See all;
  - a chip, Connected, and the search keeping its focus;
  - a tile's page and Back with the focus on the tile, and a keyed service's Add opening its key form;
  - Escape and the close;
  - at 390 px dark: the round button and the dialog over the whole screen in one column.
- `tests/smoke/test_customize_plugins.py` and `tests/unit/test_plugins_directory.py` take the new sections.
