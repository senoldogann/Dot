### 919. Claude Code as a worker of Dot's, on the owner's own plan

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-02). They develop Dot and their other projects in Claude
Code under their own Team plan. They asked that Dot hand its own problems to that Claude Code, and later reviews and
tasks too, rather than work every change out with its own model (`deepseek-v4.1-flash` on the owner's Mac).

**What may be done.** Claude Code's legal page lets a user sign in to the unmodified `claude` binary with their own
plan. What it rules out is a product that offers Claude.ai sign-in to its users, or that routes requests through a
plan's credentials. Dot runs the owner's own `claude` and never reads its token.

**Measured** on the owner's Mac (2026-11-02, two one-word sessions):
- `claude -p --output-format stream-json --verbose` streams `system/init` (the session's id; `apiKeySource: none` with
  the plan), `rate_limit_event` (the plan's window: `five_hour`, its status, `utilization` 0.38, `resetsAt`) and
  `result` (the answer, the turns, the tokens, `total_cost_usd` as the API would have charged).
- With `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set, `-p` bills that key instead of the plan. Dot's environment holds one (its review model).

**Built.** `repair/claude.py`:
- `read_account` reads `oauthAccount` from `~/.claude.json` (the address and the organisation, no secret).
- `worker_env` builds the session's environment:
  - without the names that bill an API or a cloud;
  - without any `*_API_KEY`, `*_TOKEN`, `*_SECRET`, `*_PASSWORD` or `*_KEY` but GitHub's own;
  - with `DOT_HOME`, `DOT_LOG_DIR` and `DOT_CHROME_DIR` in a throwaway folder.
- `claude_argv` runs `--permission-mode auto --permission-prompts none` and refuses `DENIED_TOOLS` outright: a merge, a
  forced push or one to main, `dotx service`, `launchctl`, `security`, `pkill`, `sudo`.
- `outcome_of` reads the stream into a `ClaudeOutcome`.
- `run_claude` runs a session in its own process group, ended with everything it started after its time.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_repair.py`, with the lines the owner's `claude` printed.
