### 1343. A Google refresh token that ran out says why: an app still in Testing signs in for 7 days

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** Self-repair found it in Dot's record: `mail_search: Google refused to refresh the
sign-in (sign in again in Customize › Connectors)`, three times in three runs (2026-11-10 twice, 2026-11-20). The run
whose events are still kept (`run_mwojijeg_46929e`, a morning schedule) shows Google's answer: status 400,
`invalid_grant`, "Token has been expired or revoked.", for both Google accounts at once; the owner signed in again that
afternoon. Ten days apart, the same week-long life each time.

The cause is the setup Dot's own docs gave: `docs/extending.md` said to add your address as a test user under Google's
Audience, which leaves the app in Testing, and Google lets the refresh tokens of such an app run out after 7 days
(for any scope beyond name, email and profile). Every sign-in so made dies a week later, and "sign in again" only
starts the next week. Dot cannot read the app's publishing status, so it cannot warn at sign-in.

- A refresh Google refuses with `invalid_grant` says the sign-in ran out or was taken away, that a Google client whose
  app is still in Testing signs in for 7 days only, and to publish the app at Google Auth Platform › Audience before
  signing in again in Settings › Connectors. Any other refusal, and Microsoft's, keep the plain "sign in again".
- `docs/extending.md` says to press **Publish app** under Audience, why, and that an unverified published app still
  works for the owner past Google's warning.
- The owner publishes the app once at Google; the accounts already signed in keep their 7-day tokens until signed in
  again after that.
