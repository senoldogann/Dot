### 1444. The hand-off card: "Dot is waiting for you in the browser on <host>"

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** When a run parks at a step only the owner can do in the browser (a login, a 2FA step: the
policy hands a credential to the owner), the daemon lists it as a `handoff` row of `GET /api/needs-you` (item 1423) under a
fixed line and nothing of the step, the page or the credential. The chat showed nothing of it: the run just waited. Research of
2026-11-23 (an agent app's phone: the run pauses at a login, a card says where it waits and gives the way to take over;
nothing the owner types goes to the model).

What changed: `Core/Handoff.swift`. `HandoffCard.make(from:)` makes the card of a `handoff` row, and `card(forChat:in:)` the
card a chat shows (its own, the newest: the daemon lists one for a chat). The headline is "Dot is waiting for you in the
browser on `<host>`", or without the host when the daemon's line names no page ("Dot needs your help"); **the host is said
only when it is plainly a host** (letters, digits, `-`, `.`, `_` and `:`, at most 253, with a letter or a digit in it), so a
line that carried a path, a user, a query, a space or a word of the page's would put none of it on the card: no credential and
no page text can come to it through the line. A short line says Dot cannot go on until the step is done and that Take over
(the run page's control, `docs/daemon.md`: it works from a phone's screen) does it from the phone. The button, **Open in
Safari**, opens the run's page of the web UI on the daemon's own address the app already holds
(`routeURL("#/runs/<id>")`, checked to be the daemon's origin, `nil` for a run id that is not plainly one) in the system's
browser; `dotagent://run/<id>` still opens the chat. `ChatModel.handoff` reads it from what the app keeps of what waits on the
owner (so it is drawn at once from the last read, and the transcript follows its coming), `Transcript` draws
`HandoffCardView` after the chat's messages, and the inbox's row of a hand-off (item 1442) now says the card's words and has
the same button. The step is not answered with a tap on the phone (the brief names a button that opens the web UI): the run goes on when the owner hands the
browser back or answers in the web UI, so the card promises nothing it does not do.

Checked: `ios/DotTests/HandoffTests.swift` (10 cases: the host named, no host, ten lines that carry a path, a credential, words,
markup, a newline or nothing and none of it comes to the card, hosts that are plain, only a hand-off makes a card, a chat's own
and newest, the web address and a run id that is no path, the deep link, the inbox row's words and no way to answer it, the
card's voice) and `NeedsYouTests` (the row's new words). `xcodebuild test` on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5), the whole suite: 368 tests, 0 failures, this item's 10 new cases among them.
Not checked: that Safari opens the page signed in (a browser that never paired shows the web UI's own sign-in screen, which
asks for a code from Settings › Phone); Take over from a phone against a real parked run (the daemon's own side, item 470,
has its tests); a tap on the button (none was driven). Seen in simulator screenshots against a canned stand-in for the app's
routes: the card in a chat (`handoff-chat-dark`) and the inbox's row (`inbox-dark`); VoiceOver reading was not checked.
