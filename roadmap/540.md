### 540. A change takes fewer pushes and reruns

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** One evening's items (531 to 538) took many hours each, and the owner asked for it to be
shortened without cutting a check. Where the time went: every push waits on a review of several minutes and the gate of
ten or more; the guard's rules were closed one spelling at a time, eleven rounds for 531 and for 534; reviews of a merge
of `main` that had changed nothing of the branch's own; the independent security review started after the rounds, not
beside them; item numbers that two sessions took at once (535 became 537); pull request bodies written again by hand;
four tests that failed on a busy runner and passed on a rerun; and an icon list kept in two places, which turned `main`
red when item 539 added one icon to the interface.

**As built.**
- The pre-push hook does not review a push that only brings `main` in: when the change the branch makes against `main`
  is the same patch (`git patch-id --stable`) as at the tip already on the remote, there is nothing of its own to
  read; a merge that resolved a conflict by editing the change is a different patch and is reviewed. After three
  earlier reviews of the branch that BLOCKed it says so before the next one: reconsider the approach (REVIEW.md
  already asked). Tested with a stand-in for `claude` in `tests/unit/test_pre_push_hook.py` (first push, clean merge,
  own fix, merge with a conflict, the round notice).
- `scripts/next_item.py` gives the next free item number from `origin/main`, the open pull requests and the pushed
  branches, and `scripts/pr_body.py <item> [--title]` makes the pull request's title and body from `roadmap/<item>.md`
  (`tests/unit/test_next_item_and_pr_body.py`).
- CLAUDE.md has the rules of a short change (one push per round, the approach before the code for the policy, the guard
  and the sandbox, the security review beside the review, one concern per pull request, the number and the body from
  the scripts, merging `main` at the end, rerunning a failure that is not the change's), and REVIEW.md the brief of the
  independent security review.
- The four tests that failed on a busy runner no longer do, each for its cause: the lesson merge's event-loop bound is
  a second (it is still asserted that the notes are compared in another thread), Quick Look is given 120 s in its test
  (the product keeps 30), the rules page's test scrolls to what is on the page when the script runs (the page draws
  the row again as the rules change; it failed half the time on a Mac too) and the phone's sidebar gets 15 s to slide
  in.
- `skills/icons.py` reads the interface's icon names from `icons.js` instead of keeping a second list, so an icon added
  to the interface cannot turn a test red.

Not built: faster or split gate jobs (the workflows need a human's look, and the gate's wall-clock is mostly the
runner's start); a hook that skips reviews of prose-only pushes (the reviews of the roadmap and docs found wrong
claims twice in item 534; they are batched into one push instead).
