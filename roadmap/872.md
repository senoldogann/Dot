### 872. A waiting approval card says each thing once

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner with a screenshot of a card that waited for them. The card read
"Run: cd "…/mywebsiterepo" && vercel build --prod 2>&1 | tail -25 (runs vercel; vercel puts a project's change live)
— On its own initiative, Dot asks before it sends a message for you, posts in your name or puts a project's change
live, in every mode". Under that came the chips "Runs a command" and "Puts a project's change live", then the same
command in its box, then "In a chat Dot opened on its own initiative, Dot always asks you about this". On a phone
Approve stood alone on a line at the left. OpenMausBot's approval card is quiet: what is asked, said once. This is part
of the design foundation of that study.

**What holds.**
- The words above a waiting card's box are the policy's summary without the target the box shows. When only the
  brackets are left, the words are what the brackets say, capitalized ("Runs vercel; vercel puts a project's change
  live"). When nothing is left, there are no words. A summary that does not hold its target is kept as it is.
- Why Dot asks (the summary after " — ") is said once. The line under the box replaces only its own sentence: the
  policy's `_INITIATIVE_REASON` or `OWNERS_REASON`, which `tests/unit/test_static_ui.py` holds equal to the card's
  `FIXED_REASONS`. Any other reason stays in a quiet line under the words: a payment's amount and caps, a guard's why.
  What the review model passed on to the owner stays there too ("The review model passed this on to you: …").
  - The first push left out every reason of a card under a fixed line. The pre-push review found that this dropped
    the review model's warning on a message in `auto`, and a pre-approved payment's amount and caps.
- A chip whose words the text says already is not shown again, and only when the text is the step's own words, with
  the command cut out of them. A summary kept whole holds the reason, whose words name every kind of step, so no chip
  is dropped for it. The words must stand whole: a short chip inside a longer word stays.
- A card with no target loses only the line's own sentence off the end of its summary. Its action may hold " — "
  itself ("Click 'Save — draft'"), so it is never split anywhere else. These two are nits of the third pre-push
  review.
- The target is taken out only where it is the action's whole object: it starts right after the label ("Run: ") and
  ends at the brackets, the reason or the end of the action. Anywhere else the summary is shown whole, as before this
  item.
  - The first fix looked for the target anywhere after the label. The second pre-push review found an MCP call whose
    target, its server `gmail`, is also inside its arguments. The card would have read `"to":"ali@ .com"`: a message
    with its recipient shown wrong.
- Nothing the owner approves is hidden. The box holds the whole target, as item 242 has it, and a question, a decided
  card and a card of a secret are unchanged.
- On a phone (600 px or less) the note and "Don't ask again in this chat" each have a row, and Deny and Approve share
  the next row half and half.

- `static/css/app.css`: the card's actions on a phone.
