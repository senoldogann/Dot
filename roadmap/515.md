### 515. A picture Pillow cannot read is one that is not readable

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** The review of Dependabot's Pillow 13 update (#465) found that Dot's image helpers caught
only `OSError`, while Pillow raises a `ValueError` for a chunk cut short (Pillow 13, where 12 raised an `OSError`) or a
text chunk that inflates past its limit (12 already), and a `DecompressionBombError` for a picture larger than it draws
(12 already): an MCP server's answer with such a picture failed its whole call on an error Dot did not expect, after the
server had acted, `read_file` of such a file the same, and an attachment answered 500. **As built.** `UNREADABLE_IMAGE`
(`core/images.py`) names what Pillow raises for data it cannot read, and `encode_image`, `jpeg_of_size` and the
application icons (`connections/app_icons.py`) catch it: the run is told the data is not a readable image, as of any
other. Tests: `tests/unit/test_unreadable_images.py` (three such PNGs, made in the test, on Pillow 12.3 and 13; without
the wider catch two fail on 12.3 and three on 13).
