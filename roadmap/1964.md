### 1964. DotNext deletes many chats in one request, gone from the list at once

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner, on build 8: deleting every chat at once (Select › all › Delete) took very
long.

Cause: Select › Delete put one doing in the outbox per chat, and the outbox sends one at a time: twenty chats were
twenty requests in a row, each written to the outbox's file twice, and a chat left the list only when the Mac told
its deletion. The daemon's `DELETE /api/threads` takes many ids at once (all or none, 409 naming a chat at work).

What was built (DotEngine): the outbox doing `deleteChats(threads:)`, one `DELETE /api/threads` for all of them;
`deletesUnderWay` (pure) and `EngineSnapshot.shownList`, which every screen of the app reads: the chats a delete is on
its way for are hidden at once and show again, each with the Mac's words, if it refuses; once the Mac answered, they
are held deleted in the list (`ListReducer.deletedHere`) before its notice comes on the stream, so they never come
back in between. The Mac deletes the chats sent together all or none: its refusal says none of them went. The list's Select › Delete
sends one.

Measured (a throwaway daemon, 20 chats): the Mac's side, 20 requests in a row 1.29 s against one of 20 in 0.11 s on
this Mac's own link, before a phone's round trips, which were twenty; the app, the list of 20 chats empty 1.98 s after Delete was tapped, the test's own polling included, against twenty
round trips one after the other before (`BulkDeleteLiveUITests`).

Checked: `SyncTests.testChatsDeletedTogetherGoInOneRequestAndLeaveTheListAtOnce` (one request; hidden while the link is
down) and `testChatsWhoseDeleteTheMacRefusesShowAgain`; `OutboxTests.testChatsTheMacDeletedStayOutOfTheListBeforeItsNotice`; `BulkDeleteLiveUITests` on a throwaway daemon
(`scripts/e2e_bulk_delete.sh`).
