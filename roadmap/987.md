### 987. The night reads the day's real runs

**Status: done (2026-11-05).** **Why.** The owner's word of 2026-11-05: the self-improvement has given nothing since
the first day. Dot's record showed why: no version was ever adopted from a night (four proposed, four set aside); six
of the last eight nights failed on a role's answer (a summary past 120 characters, an experience not an object, a task
writer's habit past 120 characters), and those that ran could not tell a better text from a lucky one, since Dot passed
101 of 104 synthetic tasks; the runs that went wrong were left out as soon as they had read a page; and a night spent
1.6 to 2.5 million tokens of the main model. The owner chose to have Dot learn from its real use. A red-team review of
the plan said adopt it with changes: count with SQL first, propose from signatures in a closed vocabulary and not from
page words, need a pattern across chats and days, try a version on real runs half and half, keep the version in use by
default.

**Built.** This item is the first part: the night stops playing synthetic tasks and keeps a digest of the day's real
runs. `harness/digest.py` reads every tool's result of the 24 hours before the night (`Store.tool_outcomes`, the
evaluation's runs left out) and how the runs ended (`Store.run_tally`, with those handed a lesson apart), and keeps,
for each way a tool failed (its signature: the tool and the first sentence of what it said, as the repair night names a
problem, `repair/problems.py` `first_sentence`), the failures, the runs it failed in of those that used the tool, the
chats, the days, the sources and the sites; the night is a step in `keep` with the digest in its record and a line that
says how the runs ended, how lessons fared and the three most widespread failures. The scheduler runs it in place of
`run_step`, which stays for `dotx harness step`. The page says a night costs nothing and reads real runs, and names a
digest's row so. Docs: `docs/harness.md`.

**Next.** A weekly proposer that writes a version from the signatures alone, for a pattern across at least two chats
and three days, never toward the shell or around an approval; a trial on real runs, half with the version and half
without (by the run's id), kept until each half has at least 40 runs and stopped early when the version does worse;
the version in use kept unless the trial shows the new one better.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_harness_digest.py` (a failure in two chats counted once a run, ids taken out, the
evaluation's runs left out, a lesson's runs apart, the night's line), `tests/smoke/test_scheduler.py` (the night at the
page's time), `tests/smoke/test_harness_page.py` (the page's words and a digest's row).
