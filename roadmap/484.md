### 484. Steps kept for the owner in a campaign's sessions

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The third slice of the parked approvals (design `parked-campaign` with the critique's
fixes; items 482 and 483 kept the steps of a chat). A session of a campaign runs in a chat of its own, so a step kept in
its chat would be found by no later session, and an answer would start a run there that the campaign never reads.
**As built.** A kept step now belongs to a scope: the conversation the owner talks to it in and, for the sessions of a
campaign, the campaign (`scope_thread_id`, `scope_campaign_id` on `approvals`; a store from before gives a kept step
the chat of its run). `Parking` carries them: a chat's run keeps in its chat, a session in the campaign's conversation
and the campaign (`parking_for` keeps sessions now; yolo, delegated runs and evals still keep nothing), and the same
step, the owner's yes and their Skip are found there (`open_kept`, `count_open_kept`, `take_permit`, `skipped_kept`;
a Skip is taken back by a run of the owner's in the campaign's conversation or a message of theirs to a session). The
answer to a session's step reaches that session while it works, never another run of the conversation; once it has
ended, no run is started: the step is decided (an Allow once's yes stored first, as in item 483) and the campaign's
next session comes forward (`_bring_session_forward`). Its brief has a section of the steps kept for the owner and
what became of them (`BriefKept`: waiting, done by the owner, allowed once and not used yet, allowed and done, the yes
lapsed, skipped, lapsed), so a later session may do an allowed step once and tries no other again. A session that kept
a step still unanswered is followed after `campaign_wait_hours`, whatever it said it needs (`next_session_at`'s
`kept_open`). The card of a session's kept step says the answer reaches the session or the next one. Yolo is
untouched. Tests: `tests/unit/test_campaign.py` (the wait, the brief's lines), `tests/unit/test_parking.py`,
`tests/unit/test_store_kept.py` (the campaign's scope, the Skip taken back there, the scope given to a step kept
before), `tests/smoke/test_kept_campaign.py` (a session keeps a question, the next waits a day, Allow once brings it
now, its brief says so, it does the step once on that yes, the same step again is kept as the campaign's). Docs:
agents-and-campaigns.md, autonomy.md.
