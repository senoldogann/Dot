### 1042. Two more ways to speed up the browser: measured, and not shipped

**Status: rejected (2026-11-08).** After items 1038-1041, the owner chose three candidates on the code's side, measured
in this order: what `browser_open` itself takes, several pages read in one reply, and the level of thinking by step.

**`browser_open`'s own time.** 857 opens in 30 days, median 1.93 s, p90 6.3 s, 50.6 minutes in all (LinkedIn 19.3,
the webmail 11.5). A probe on a throw-away Dot (never the owner's sessions) timed each phase of `_load` on public pages
(LinkedIn's job search, Reddit, Hacker News, Wikipedia), twice: an open took 0.5-1.3 s (3.1 s the first, Chrome
starting), most of it the navigation; Dot's own part, the settle (0.07-1.0 s) and the snapshot (0.05-0.3 s), about 0.3 s
an open, some 4 minutes a month. The settle keeps a page from being read half drawn: not worth it. The owner's
signed-in pages (LinkedIn 3.5 s on average) could not be timed without their session.

**Several pages in one reply.** The loop already runs several browser calls of one reply in turn, each with its result
(`agent/executor.py` `_group_calls`), and 17% of the main model's replies carry more than one call (mostly `web_fetch`,
`shell`, `read_file`), but it seldom opens pages so. A sentence in `browser_open`'s description and the prompt's Browser
section asked it to open the pages it knows the addresses of in one reply (102 tokens more in every run). The speed set,
the owner's model, 3 attempts each, against item 1040's baseline: 15/15 both, 6.3 → 6.2 model calls a task,
`speed-daily-check` 9.0 → 8.7, single-step acts 50% → 42%: within the 0.3-0.5 calls runs of the same code move by. Not
shipped. (A first run of it played the default model of a throw-away home, not the owner's: discarded.)

**The level of thinking by step** (a click's next step at `medium`, a plan or a text at the level chosen) cannot be told
apart on the speed set, where the model thinks little at any level (item 1041); its cost in passes shows only on the
owner's real work, so it is the owner's to try, as the week of `medium` on the seven schedules at `max` is.
