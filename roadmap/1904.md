### 1904. DotNext shows a screenshot whole and zoomable, a failed run as one plain row, a fold without counts, a stored thought as its word alone, and its waiting states centred

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner, on build 5 on his iPhone, asked for four fixes in the chat and the shared
states.

What changed (the app only):
- A tool step's screenshot opens whole: fitted inside the sheet (aspect fit, never cropped) on a dark backdrop; a pinch
  zooms up to four times and the picture pans; a double tap zooms to two and a half times at the tapped point, or back
  to fitted (`ZoomableImage`, UIKit's own scroll view). It was a scroll view of a picture at least 320 pt wide, which
  showed it zoomed and cut off on the right.
- A run that ended other than done is one row like the work log's (the run-end view of items 1815-1818, restyled): a
  small muted symbol, one line "The run failed · <reason>", and Continue and Retry as plain text buttons on the row (44
  pt; under the line at the accessibility sizes). No card, no red title.
- A finished run's fold says "Worked for 4m 34s" and nothing more; its chevron opens the steps. The tally of kinds
  ("read 1 file, ran 6 commands, 21 web steps…") and its words are gone.
- A stored step's thinking folds to "Thought" and the chevron, nothing more: no first sentence (the owner reads it
  opened). While it streams the row says "Thinking". No duration: the transcript keeps none for a thought.
- A loading, empty or failed state that stands alone on a scrolling page is as tall as the page's visible area less
  the page's padding (`fillsScrollPage`, `containerRelativeFrame`), so it is centred both ways between the navigation
  bar and the bottom: Settings' and Activity's pages while they read ("Reading your Mac…"), and the empty Memory,
  Scheduled tasks and Watchers. States outside a scroll view (the chat list, a chat, Connect apps) already filled their
  place and were centred.

How it was checked: `SettingsUITests.testALoadingPageIsCentredInTheVisibleArea` on a new fixture whose Mac never answers
(`settings-loading`, Usage) measures what is drawn (the spinner and its line) against the middle of the visible area, between the navigation bar
and the tab bar (or the bottom's safe area), and passes within 1 pt on an iPhone 17 (before the change the state was a 240 pt box at the top of the page, as the
owner's screenshot shows; not measured by the test);
`ChatUITests.testAFailedRunIsOnePlainRowAndTheFoldSaysOnlyHowLong` on `n2-failed` (the row's text, Retry 44 pt on
its first line, the fold without counts, the stored thought's row says "Thought" and shows its words only once opened;
the fixture's step now has a thinking); `ChatWordsTests` for the fold's words; the strings check (1055 keys, the 18
tally words removed). Screenshots: `ios-rewrite-2026-11-24/shots-d2/loading-centred.png` and `failed-row.png` in the
owner's workspace. The screenshot viewer has no fixture with a picture, so its zoom was not driven by a test.
