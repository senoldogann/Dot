### 1340. wait_until reads the names a model on the Claude plan guesses for it

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** Self-repair found it in Dot's record: `wait_until: Invalid arguments for wait_until`,
six times in five runs on `claude_code:sonnet` between 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-23.

On the Claude plan Dot's tools are deferred (item 1053): the model sees their names only, until it looks one up. It
called `wait_until` without the look-up, by its name and the shell's description ("wait with wait_until, not sleep"),
and guessed: `condition` for the command (twice), and the shell's own `timeout_s`, in seconds, for how long to wait
(five times); each call was refused and sent again, a turn lost. Once the call came with no argument at all, a model's
own slip.

  `within_minutes` or is no number; past an hour it is refused as a `within_minutes` past 60. The schema still offers
  the tool's own names alone.
