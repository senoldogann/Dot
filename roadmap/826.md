### 826. Dot's own initiative waits out a provider's dearer hours

**Status: done (2026-10-29).** **Asked** by the owner ("indirimli saatler evet, on its own için daha iyi bence"), after
item 825's reading of the costs: Ollama Cloud bills by token since October 2026, and its price outside 12 to 18 UTC on
weekdays, and all weekend, is half (`deepseek-v4.1-flash`: $0.15 a million uncached, $0.003 cached, $0.60 out, against
$0.30, $0.006 and $1.20). Dot's own runs are work nobody waits on at an hour; the schedules keep their times, as the
owner set them for an hour (the client mail every two hours of a working day).

- `[initiative] peak_hours_utc` (empty by default; the owner's Dot `[12, 18]`): in those hours of a weekday, in UTC,
  none of its runs starts, and it does not look for work, but an urgent thing of the owner's, as while the Mac is in
  use (`in_peak`, `dotagent/proactive/initiative.py`); a run under way goes on. Two hours, the first before the second,
  or it is refused as the config is read.
- The card on Schedules says the hours in the Mac's own time (`peak_hours_utc` in `GET /api/initiative`).

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_initiative.py`: a Wednesday's 13:00 UTC is in `[12, 18]`, 11:59:59 and 18:00 are not,
nor a Saturday's 13:00; `[18, 12]` is refused; in the hours a thing to do waits and the owner's urgent one starts; on a
Saturday the thing to do starts.
