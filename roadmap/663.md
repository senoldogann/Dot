### 663. The work told in one line

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** The owner asked for what another agent app shows while it works: no thoughts and no
commands, only a few words that change as it goes ("preparing", "cooking", "almost done"), with the steps behind a
switch in the settings.

**The change.**
- Settings › Interface › **Work in a chat**: **Every step** (the default, as before) or **What it is doing**. The choice
  is `dot.work` in the browser, put on the page as `data-work` before anything is drawn (`boot.js`, `layout.js`).
- With **What it is doing**, run.css folds every group of steps and every thought out of the conversation, the earlier
  turns' too, and `workstatus.js` keeps one line under it: Dot's three dots and the words of the run's latest event,
  in present tense, by the kind of its call (`tools.js` `callKind`): a thought, a command, a file read or written, a
  search, a page, the browser, the Mac, a schedule, a note, a message to an agent by its name. The words for the same
  work turn every four seconds. Nothing invented is said ("almost done" would be a guess): while the run writes a
  message or waits on a card, the line is gone.
- The line is a button: pressed, it shows every step of the chat, and again folds them; once the run is over it says
  **Show the steps**, and a run without a step offers nothing.
- `docs/runs.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_work_status.py`: a scripted run whose command takes six seconds says "Running a command…" with no
  step in sight, shows and folds the steps on a press, offers them once it is over, on a phone's width as wide; with the
  default, the steps show and the line does not.
