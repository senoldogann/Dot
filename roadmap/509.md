### 509. Steering at once, one Add, and a plainer Agents page

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The owner asked for these one after another, to go in one pull request.

- **Steering at once.** A steered message waited for the step at work to end ("Waiting for the next step"), which,
  with a model thinking at length, could be minutes. Now the step's model call races the run's inbox
  (`agent/loop._complete_unless_steered`, `attachments.RunInbox.arrival`, which waits without taking): a message that
  arrives first cancels the call, and the loop reads it and asks the step again. The loop takes a message from the
  inbox only once it is in the thread, so a stop while it is read leaves it for the run's end to keep, as before. A
  step past the run's limit is not raced, since it ends with its finish. A tool already at work finishes first. The
  waiting note says "Sending to the run".
- **One Add.** With a service's key form open, the form takes the row button's place (`views/plugins.wayRow`), and
  `.plugin-actions` keeps its buttons at their own height.
- **Connect apps in the sidebar.** The pill sits on the sidebar's own ground, as the cog does, and shows three ready
  servers' marks, chosen afresh every 6 s when there are more (`sidebar.showMarks`).
- **Agents page.** It has two parts, **Your agents** (with its count) and **Add ready-made agents**, which shows
  **Teams** or **Single agents** behind a segmented switch; the switch's choice is kept while the app is open. A
  template's facts are a line each, with an icon in place of a label.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_queue.py` (a steer cuts the waiting step short, on the pages too), `test_run_limits.py`,
`test_left_questions.py` and `test_kept_campaign.py` (an answer cuts the held step short),
`test_connect_apps.py` (one Add) and `test_agent_templates.py` / `test_agents.py` (the two parts, the switch).
