### 1184. An answer lists the pages its turn read on the web

**Status: done (2026-11-15).** The owner asked what Dot could take from DeepSeek's chat demo, then for whatever the chat
needs to be clean. A search assistant's answer shows its sources; Dot's showed none, though its turns read pages all the
time (252 `web_fetch` calls in the owner's store). Under an answer whose turn read pages there is now one quiet line:
the first letter of a few of their sites and how many pages; opened, each page's title over its site, a link out.

- `sourcelist.js` (pure, node-tested) reads the pages from the run's `tool_call` and `tool_result` events: the address
  `web_fetch` was given or the one its fence names after a redirect (http(s) only), the addresses `web_extract` read (it
  succeeds when one source was read, and lists the others under `Not extracted:`, which are left out), the title from
  the fetch's own `Title:` line, each page once, 12 at most and the rest counted. A failed call and a search read none.
  Calls made together emit their events in any order (`executor.py`), so the list follows the order they started.
- `sources.js` draws the row; `timeline.js` keeps a run's page reads in its scope and puts the row in when the run ends,
  `history.js` when an earlier turn's events are read, its place kept only when there is a row to add.
- No colour and no logos: a site's letter is a mark (item 837 keeps colour for what asks the owner to look), and no
  picture is fetched from anyone's server.

Checked: `tests/js/sourcelist.test.js`, `tests/smoke/test_chat_sources.py` (Chrome at 1280 px and WebKit as the owner's
iPhone, the run's own page and the turn under a later message), screenshots in both schemes.
