### 1275. Chrome's own probe is no longer a line of the log

**Status: done (2026-11-20).** `browser_request_refused` for `chrome-extension://invalid/`, an address Chrome asks of
every page to find out which extensions are installed, was 24,600 of the 43,000 lines of two days (57%). The log keeps
two files of 10 MiB, so the seven days it is meant to keep were two, and a count of what went wrong in a week could not
be made (the operations and the usage audits of 2026-11-20 both ran into it).

The request is refused as before; the refusal of **that address** is logged at the debug level, and a refusal of any
other address (a `file:` or a `javascript:` one, a blocked host) stays an `info` line, which is the record the safety
contract asks for.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_vault.py::test_the_probe_chrome_sends_of_every_page_is_refused_without_a_line_in_the_log_and_any_other_refusal_has_one`
(the probe is refused with no `info` line, `file:///etc/passwd` is refused with one).
