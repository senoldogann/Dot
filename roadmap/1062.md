### 1062. Every face of the type has the same three settings: its font, its weight and its size

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** The owner, on 2026-11-09: "apperace sayfasında font size font family, font weight vs tüm
alanlarda her zaman aynı şeyler yok ya da farklı … bunlar da önemli bunları da çöz". Settings › Appearance had four
faces, each with other settings: the interface a font of two and a size in a card of its own (Text), the answers a font
of three as buttons and no size, the message box and the code a font of three and a size; no face had a weight.

Now one Type card holds the four faces, the interface, the answers, the message box and the code, each with the same
three rows, a font, a weight and a size, and a line drawn in it:

- The fonts are the same list for every face: its own (Default, named), System, Inter, Serif, Geist Mono, SF Mono,
  Menlo, or families typed; the answers and the message box may also take the interface's or the code's
  (`--family-interface`, `--family-mono`), which the interface and the code cannot take of each other (a circle). The weights: Light, Regular (each face's own), Medium, Semibold; every font Dot carries is
  variable (100 to 900) and the system's has them all.
- The answers' size is new: until set it is a message's size scaled with the interface's (as before), set it is its own,
  12 to 24 px, its line keeping the message's ratio (1.6, 1.375 on a phone). Its default moves with the interface's, so
  it is painted by whether a size is kept, not by what the default is now: a size kept stays when the interface's
  brings the default to it (the first review: it was dropped at the next reload).
- The answers' size reaches all of an answer: its first heading a step over its text and its inline code a step under
  (as 17 and 13 are to 15 on a Mac, 19 and 15 to 16 on a phone, so that at its own size an answer is drawn as before), and inline code has the code's weight but in a heading or in bold, where it keeps theirs.
  The interface's weight moves the label's and the title's with it, a step and two over it, and the answers' weight
  their headings', so that the order holds in both.
  A family typed for the answers goes before the theme's voice (`--theme-voice`: Paper's serif), not the interface's.
- Each setting is a token of `app.css` (`--weight-text`, `--answer-size`, `--answer-weight`, `--composer-weight`,
  `--code-weight` beside the families and sizes there were), which `boot.js` puts on the page before the first paint as
  `appearance/settings.js` does after it.
- What a browser kept before reads as what it is now: the answers' "theme" is their own and "interface" the
  interface's, the message box's "mono" the code's. A browser that kept the interface's "system" before item 1057 now shows
  System, the same face.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_interface_settings.py` (every row of every face on the page and before the first paint,
the kept values of before) and `tests/smoke/test_phone_text.py` (the interface's size on a phone, in WebKit).
