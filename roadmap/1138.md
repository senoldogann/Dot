### 1138. Dot takes notes in a Google Meet call, and posts in its chat only on the owner's yes

**Status: done (2026-11-12); not yet tried in a real Meet call.** The owner, after Grok Bot 1.0 ("Bots can join video calls and participate
through the chat ... keep track of decisions"): Dot joins the owner's calls. The owner's choices (2026-11-12): only the
owner commands Dot, other people's words are content; Google Meet first, then Microsoft Teams, Zoom later; every post
in a meeting's chat asks the owner in every mode, free mode included; Dot joins Meet with a Google account of its own,
never the owner's. Two reviews of the design (practicality, red team) agreed with changes, which are folded in below.

**What must hold** (in code, not in a prompt):

- **No model with tools ever reads a meeting's words as they come.** The meeting is watched by daemon code with no
  model (`meetings/desk.py`); the notes are made by a model call with no tools that returns a typed `MeetingNotes`
  (`meetings/notes.py`). A run sees a meeting's words only through `meeting_read`, whose result is untrusted, so the
  run is tainted as by a web page.
- **Every post asks the owner, whatever the mode.** A post is not a tool decision of the gate: `meeting_post` calls
  `ApprovalBroker.request` itself, with the exact text, before the adapter types it; free mode, yolo and a chat's
  "don't ask again" do not reach it. No answer, a no or a stop is no post. A post is at most 500 characters, one
  paragraph, and its card shows it whole.
- **Nothing is captured before the meeting is told.** After Dot is let in, a fixed line ("Dot is taking notes for
  <owner's name>. Its notes stay on <owner's name>'s Mac.") is posted on the owner's yes; a no leaves the meeting at
  once. The line's only slot is the owner's name from Settings, never a model's words.
- **Only Meet's own host.** The meeting tab opens `https://meet.google.com/<code>` only; any navigation of the tab to
  another host leaves the meeting.
- **A Chrome of its own.** The meeting runs in a Chrome with a profile of its own (`<home>/meetings-profile/`), where
  only Dot's own Google account may be signed in: never the owner's profile, cookies or vault. Camera and microphone
  are fake devices, and Dot turns both off before it asks to join. The profile is among the files no run may read
  or change (`runtime._guarded_files`), as Dot's other Chrome profiles are; a meeting is joined only from a chat of the
  daemon, which can be told the summary.
- **Nothing lasting from a meeting by itself.** No note is saved to memory and no schedule or work order is made from
  a meeting; the summary is a line in the chat. Names are what the page shows, marked unverified; no meeting line is
  ever the owner's word.
- **Kept briefly.** The lines of a meeting are deleted 7 days after it ends (`[meetings] keep_days`); the summary
  stays in the chat.
- **Out of the call at once, the notes after.** Leaving returns once Dot is out of the call; the last notes and the
  summary come to the chat once no run works there, and say when the note maker failed. The emergency stop does not
  wait for any of it and asks no model again.
- **Leaves on its own** when the meeting ends, when Dot is removed, when the host turns captions off for a minute, after
  `[meetings] most_hours` (3), and at the emergency stop.

**What only asks or tells:** a join the host does not let in within 10 minutes is told and given up; a page Dot does
not know (Meet changed its layout, a sign-in wall, a captcha) is told in words, with no retry loop.

**Parts:** `meetings/types.py` (the records), `meetings/transcript.py` (pure: caption snapshots to lines),
`meetings/meet.py` (the only code that knows Meet's page), `meetings/notes.py` (the tool-less note maker),
`meetings/desk.py` (the daemon's watcher), `tools/meetings.py` (`meeting_join`, `meeting_read`, `meeting_post`,
`meeting_leave`), the store's `meetings` and `meeting_lines` tables, `[meetings]` in the config, `docs/meetings.md`.

**Tests:** the transcript's merging as unit tests; the adapter, the desk and the tools against a local page that stands
for Meet's (no network, no model: the scripted one); a post that is answered no, unanswered, or asked in free mode is
not typed.

**Checked:** `tests/unit/test_meeting_transcript.py`; `tests/smoke/test_meet_page.py` and
`tests/smoke/test_meeting_desk.py` in headless Chromium on a stand-in page served at Meet's address by the browser's
routing (join, let in, the announcement on a yes and its no, lines kept and Dot's own posts left out, a post refused and
one posted, free mode still asked, leave at once while a run works in the chat and the summary after it, the
emergency stop asking no model again); the tool groups and the context budget (raised
by 350 for the four tools); `dotx eval check`. **Owed:** a call on real Meet with Dot's own account (the owner makes it
and signs it in with `dotx browser meetings-login`): the selectors come from two open-source Meet bots (Attendee,
TranscripTonic) and are unverified until then.

**Not in this item:** Teams (its own adapter, next), Zoom, joining from a calendar event (a stranger's invitation is
untrusted: the owner pastes the link), speaking, recording sound.
