### 576. Settings on a phone and wide: tags as labels, the box's button at its place, the health checks fold

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** The owner sent three screenshots of Settings on their phone (the Neutral theme, light):
the Remember button and a note's tags looked bad, the health checks were a wall of text (the skills check names every
skill, more than a hundred), and Permissions, About this Dot and Daemon stood folded as bare lines, without the ground
the groups of the health checks have. They asked for those and any other fault of the phone and wide views.

**As built.**
- **Memory.** A note's tags are labels, not buttons: the rule that gives a chip a thumb's 36 px on a touch screen leaves
  them out (`.note-meta .chip`), and they keep the height of their words, the badge's beside them (24 px, where they
  were 36 and round). On a phone the box's tags take a line of their own and **Remember** stands at the right of the line
  under them, beside "One fact per note"; it had wrapped alone to the left. A touch screen is not offered ⌘↵. The four
  choices keep one line that scrolls sideways when it does not fit, where the last stood alone under the other three.
- **System.** The **Health checks** are a card that folds (`system-health`, open by default, kept in the browser), its
  head saying how many checks passed; the filter and **Run again** stay in its head, under the name when both do not
  fit. A finding of more than 180 characters shows its first two lines with **Show all** and **Show less**
  (`healthDetail`), and a row's name and pill stand at its first line, not in the middle of a tall one.
- **Cards that fold keep their ground.** The rules that set a card's head over it as a section's name (item 562 wide,
  item 330 on a phone) leave a card that folds out (`.card.fold`): folded, Daemon, Permissions and About this Dot are
  rows with the card's ground, as the groups of the health checks are, and their heads line up with the rows.
- **The tabs not redrawn.** On a phone the name over a card of General or Appearance reads as a section's title, bold
  and flush with the cards, as on the redrawn tabs and wide, not a muted line set in.
- **Providers.** Where a key stands ("No key yet", "Optional · no key") is said in the page's face; a key's last
  characters alone are set in the mono face.
- `docs/memory-and-schedules.md`, `docs/daemon.md` and `docs/phone.md` say it.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_memory_page.py`: on a touch phone a tag is no taller than 28 px and as tall as the badge,
⌘↵ is hidden, **Remember** stands under the tags at their right, the choices keep one line and nothing scrolls
sideways. `test_about_pages.py`: the four folds (the Health checks with "N of M ok" at their head), each with a ground
and its head on it; a finding of 132 skills is clipped under 60 px and opens past it with **Show less**; the checks stay
folded after a reload. `test_providers.py`: the key line is in the page's face. Both new checks fail without the change
(a tag 36 px tall beside a 22 px badge; no Health checks fold).

Not changed: **Remember** keeps the theme's primary colour, ink in Neutral and terracotta in Paper, as every primary
button of the app does.
