### 1756. DotNext streams a step's thinking and words, its capsule clock ticks on its own and says what the run does

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** The owner's report from his iPhone the night DotNext was installed (wave N2, #1091):
"when I send a message the seconds of the Working capsule freeze and jump, the thinking and the output do not stream;
after a reload I see the answer." His chat ran on `opencode_go:deepseek-v4.1-flash` in task mode, with "Tool calls in
chat" set to Zen.

What was measured before:
- The daemon and `tailscale serve` were not the cause. One run read at once on the chat's stream through the tailnet
  address, the same stream on the loopback, and the old run stream: 14 deltas each, delay from the event's `ts` to its
  arrival p50 3 ms, p95 16 ms, at most 17 ms on all three; the longest gap between two deltas 47-67 ms.
- The engine was not the cause: the real `Supervisor` and `SyncStore` against the daemon over the tailnet published a
  snapshot for every delta, about one per 20 ms, the words growing in each (`writing` 14 → 221 characters in 0.6 s).
- The capsule's clock was drawn from `Date.now` when the screen was drawn, and the screen is drawn when a snapshot
  changes. On the simulator, the owner's own chat (a run in a long command) showed "18 dk 48 sn" for over a minute; in a
  test chat the clock went 0, 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 11 s: it moved only with events.
- The engine dropped the `thinking` deltas (`ChatProjection.writing` takes `text` alone), and the transcript drew no
  thinking. DeepSeek thinks 2-9 s before each step of the owner's chat (`thinking_chars` 115-813), so in every step
  nothing moved: no word, no row, no clock. In Zen the tool rows and the passing words are hidden too, so the chat stood
  still until the run's answer came at its end, which read as "the answer appears after a reload".
- The Mac was overloaded at that hour (the chat itself says load average 900), which can delay everything; that was
  not reproduced.

What changed (all in the app; nothing in the daemon):
- The capsule's status and clock are drawn by a `TimelineView` every second, its spoken label too; the chat's title
  says "Working" without a clock (it would freeze for the same reason).
- The engine keeps the thinking of a run's open step (`ChatProjection.thinking`, live only, never cached), with the
  open-step rules of the words (a reset drops it, a stored step ends it, a break drops it). The transcript draws a
  "Thinking" row streaming its last lines while it is written and a folded "Thought" from the step's stored `assistant`
  event afterwards, under one id; in Show all, Hide successful and Hide all, never in Zen (its meaning: the answer
  only). "Response streaming" off hides it while written.
- The capsule says what the run does now (`activity(of:run:)`): "Thinking", "Writing", or the running tool's line
  ("Running sleep 6; echo done"), so a chat in Zen shows a sign of life between its words.
- The screen builds the transcript once per drawing instead of six times (measured 0.7 ms per build for the owner's
  109-event chat on the Mac).
- PLAN §24, from the owner in the same session: the copy · reaction footer is drawn under a run's final answer alone,
  once the run ended, with the time it ended; quiet glyphs of one weight close together. The `approval_auto` rows the
  policy allowed with nobody asked ("İzin verildi · yolo: …") are not drawn in any mode; a refusal still is. The web
  UI's timeline still shows them.

How it was checked: `swift test` (FakeDaemon: 50 deltas, 25 thinking and 25 words, each awaited in the transcript
before the next, the capsule's activity checked at each; the reducer's thinking rules; the mode table with thinking;
the footer; the allowed asks in every mode), red once without the reducer's line; a UI test on the `n2-chat` fixture,
whose run gets no event at all, that the capsule's label changes twice within 2.5 s each, red once with the clock on
`.everyMinute`; a daemon smoke test that 40 deltas (thinking and words) reach the chat's stream in order, each within
100 ms (largest measured well under it). On the simulator against the owner's daemon over the tailnet, a task run with
a 6 s command: the clock went 0 → 13 s second by second through the command, the capsule said the command while it
ran, the thought folded, the footer under the answer alone.

Not verified: the owner's iPhone itself (installed for him to try); a long thinking phase drawn live on the simulator
(DeepSeek's thinking came and went within a second in the probes; the tests cover the live row).
