### 532. Clearing a profile signs every site out of it

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** The audit of how Dot keeps the browser's cookies, passwords and history (the owner asked
whether it is sure) found that **Clear** in Settings › Browser said it signs the sites out and did not: it deleted the
cookies and the caches only. A site that keeps its sign-in in local storage, IndexedDB or a service worker (many do,
with a token the cookie only points at) stayed signed in; the logins Chrome's own password manager had saved, and the
autofill, stayed in `Login Data` and `Web Data`.

**As built.**
- `browser/profiles.clear_site_data` deletes, from the profile's `Default` folder, besides the cookies and the caches it
  deleted: what the sites stored (`Local Storage`, `Session Storage`, `IndexedDB`, `Service Worker` with its cache
  storage, `Shared Dictionary`, the quota database `WebStorage`, the older `File System`, `databases` and
  `blob_storage`, and `Trust Tokens`, `Reporting and NEL` and `Device Bound Sessions`, which hold what a site handed the
  browser), and what Chrome saved for the sites (`Login Data` and `Login Data For Account`, `Web Data` and `Account Web
  Data`, the autofill and cards, and the `Affiliation Database`), each database with its journal and write-ahead log.
  The names are those a Chrome 155 on macOS wrote into a profile a page had filled with every kind of storage (a probe,
  2026-10-22). The history, the settings, the extensions and their data stay, as Settings and the docs said.
- A link in the profile is deleted as a link, never followed; nothing there is nothing to delete.
- The button's text and the dialog say what goes (the cookies, what the sites stored, the logins and autofill Chrome
  saved, the cache) and what stays (history, settings, extensions); `docs/browser-and-computer-use.md` says it too.
  Chrome is still let go of first, and Clear is still refused while a run is active or a Chrome window has the profile.

Checked by `tests/unit/test_browser_profiles.py` (every name above deleted from a profile that holds them and the
profile's own files left; a link; nothing to clear) and, with a real headless Chrome, by
`tests/smoke/test_browser_clear.py` (a page fills a cookie, local storage, IndexedDB and cache storage, Chrome closes,
the profile is cleared, Chrome starts on it again and the page finds nothing, while an uncleared control finds all of
it; the history is still there). They fail without the change.

Not built: Dot's own Chrome blocks service workers (`service_workers="block"`), so none comes of its pages; the
`Service Worker` folder is deleted for the profile the sign-in window and an imported profile can fill, and tested by
name only. The history (`History`, `Top Sites`, `Favicons`) is not deleted: a profile's history is not a site's sign-in,
and Settings has always said it stays.
