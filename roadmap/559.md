### 559. The web UI: a service's key form can be clicked, failed reads and switches say so, and the accessibility and dead-code nits

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** From the audit of 2026-10-23 (`96e8c750`): the findings of the web UI that a second
reader confirmed in the code. Each is named here by its number and title.

**As built.**
- F41, returning to a kept live run replays its stream from 0: `static/js/views/run.js` follows a run opened again from
  the newest event stored among those read, not from the id of the last message read, which is 0 when that message is
  the words of a reply being written (stored nowhere). Item 568 made the same change in another session and landed
  first, so its line is the one in `run.js` and `docs/daemon.md`; `tests/smoke/test_kept_live_run.py` stays as a
  second check of it (a run left and opened again while it writes, its stream followed from a stored event).
- F42, a service's key form in its Connectors tile could not be clicked: `static/css/app.css` raises
  `.plugin-tile .plugin-secrets` over the link whose `::after` covers the tile, as the tile's step and actions are.
- F120, failed `/status` and `/approvals` reads only logged: `static/js/state.js` reads the two apart
  (`Promise.allSettled`), shows each answer that came, and tells a failure through `readFailed`, as the pages tell
  theirs.
- F121, queue reads with no newest-wins guard: `static/js/queue.js` numbers its reads and the requests whose answer is a
  queue, and an answer older than the one shown is dropped.
- F122, any `#token=` link replaced and then cleared the saved token: `static/js/main.js` and `api.js` try the token
  of the address in memory and keep it (`setToken(value, keep)`) once the daemon has proven that it holds it; one it
  does not hold gives way to the browser's own token, with a toast. The tab keeps the token it is trying in its
  session storage until it is proven or refused, so that a page loaded again meanwhile (the address no longer has the
  token) tries it again: `test_phone_settings.py` loads the page again as it opens, and lost the token without this.
- F123, a card for a change of Dot's own files offered "Make a rule of this": `static/js/approvals.js` adds
  `self_change` to the flags no rule decides.
- F124, listeners added in `beginSession` never removed: both take the session's signal in `main.js`.
- F125, earlier turns read from the newest 200 runs of a chat: `static/js/history.js` says so when a chat has more runs
  than it read (`cut`): "Earlier runs of this chat are not shown", with no count and no first run to lead to, and a run
  older than all of them says it too, where it showed nothing.
- F126, "Save your lines" before the lines are read or after a failed read: the box and Save were kept off until the
  lines were read, with Try again after a failed read. Settings' new look (item 566), which landed first, draws what
  Dot knows about you only once the lines are read, so a save can come neither before them nor after a read that
  failed: this item takes its page as it is, and `test_page_guards.py` checks that what it guards holds there.
- F127, "ok" toasts for a switch that failed: the Browser, SnapShots and Phone switches (`views/system.js`,
  `snapshots.js`, `phone.js`) say what the change was in `write`, once the daemon has made it; `onToggled` only
  repaints.
- F128, a key typed in a service's form stays after Cancel: `static/js/catalogue.js` has Cancel and `dispose()` call
  `stopAsking()` (no longer exported: nothing else used it), and a form that closes because another opens forgets what
  was typed in it.
- F129, Skills shows a load failure without Try again: `static/js/views/skills.js` passes a retry for the page and for
  the Found on this Mac part.
- F130, the New schedule form keeps the last schedule's choices: `static/js/views/schedules.js` makes the form anew once
  a schedule is added.
- F131, the Rules page understates what a yolo run keeps: `static/js/views/rules.js` adds that one that has read
  untrusted content still has a secret refused on the way out and a step that content may be after judged by the review
  model (item 525, `docs/safety.md`).
- F132, the Controls page's approval cards do post: `static/js/views/controls.js` says so in its header.
- F133, CSS selectors no markup uses: `.activity-list`, `.activity-children`, `.pop-note`, `.truncate` (`app.css`) and
  `.rule-order` (`settings.css`) are gone.
- F134, exports only their own module used, and two pref helpers: `dropSkill`, `shareSkill`, `withAgentNames`,
  `changeFound`, `importFound`, `foundSection` (`views/skills.js`) and `galleryMark` (`views/plugins.js`) are no longer
  exported, and `clearPref` is gone for `removePref` (`prefs.js`, `composer.js`). `tests/unit/test_static_ui.py` now
  counts a script imported whole as used for the names its importer reaches through its alias (a page for its `mount`),
  so the next helper of a page that only the page uses is found.
- F135, stale or orphaned comments: the orphan comment above `buildNameCard` (`views/system.js`) is gone and the
  `scheduleForm` comment says that both callers pass `onCancel`.
- F136, the look picker's radio groups named by machine ids: `static/js/lookpicker.js` names them Shape, Expression and
  Colour.
- F137, Connect apps not current to assistive technology: `static/js/sidebar.js` gives it `aria-current="page"` beside
  `data-current` while Customize is open.
- F138, QR code blob URLs that came after the tab was left: `static/js/views/phone.js` lets one go as it is made when
  the tab is gone.
- F139, the providers list a listbox without listbox keys: `static/js/views/keys.js` makes it a group of buttons, the
  open one `aria-current="true"` (`settings.css`), and Back focuses the provider that was open.
- F140, the Team arrow's label on a plain span: `static/js/views/team.js` writes "to" as text a screen reader reads
  (`.visually-hidden`).
- F155, Customize's header comment: `views/customize.js` (and `sidebar.js`'s) say it is reached from Connect apps.
- `docs/daemon.md`, `docs/runs.md`, `docs/extending.md`, `docs/memory-and-schedules.md`, `docs/safety.md` and
  `docs/development.md` say what changed where they describe it.

**Left.**

  Reading up to the opened run needs a `before` or `parent` filter on `GET /runs`, a change of the daemon's API that
  belongs to its own item.
- F137 leaves Connect apps a button (`aria-current` is the finding's point); a link that opens in a new tab is a design
  choice of items 492 and 545.
- F139 takes the plain list of buttons the finding offers, not arrow-key roving over a listbox.
- F132 takes the comment, not a `decide` hook on `approvalCard`: nothing links to the page.
- F41 keeps the words of a reply in `kept.messages`: a run left mid-reply is redrawn with them, as when it was live.

**Tests.** New: `tests/smoke/test_kept_live_run.py` (a run left while its reply streams and opened again follows its
stream from a stored event, and each event is drawn once); `test_customize_connectors.py` (a keyed service's tile takes

read is told while the approvals still show, a link with a token the daemon does not hold leaves the saved one, a page
loaded again while its token is being proven tries it again, the listeners of a session end with it, Your lines are not

`test_thread_turns.py` (a chat of more than 200 runs says its earlier runs are not shown, from its newest run and from
its first); `test_rules.py` (a card for Dot's own files offers no rule); the refused-switch tests of
`test_browser_settings.py`, `test_snapshots.py` and `test_phone_reach.py`, and the QR test of the last;
`test_customize_skills.py` (Try again on both failures); `test_schedule_agent.py` (the New schedule form opens on the
defaults after one is added). Checks added to existing tests: the Rules note (`test_rules.py`), the providers list and
Back's focus (`test_providers.py`), the look picker's groups (`test_character.py`), Connect apps' `aria-current`
