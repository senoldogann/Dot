### 778. The design defects an audit of every page found, fixed

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner: "diğer sayfalarda da tasarımsal sorunlar varsa hallet" (fix the
design problems of the other pages too). An audit looked at 27 routes of the owner's own Dot, read only, at 1280 px and
at 390 px with touch, and listed 25 findings; these were fixed:

- **Settings' nav**: a long value ran over the tab's name ("Modelsdeepseek/deepseek-v…"): the name keeps its width
  (`.settings-nav-label`), and the value ends in an ellipsis beside it.
- **Models › Fallbacks** on a phone: a model's name and its service broke mid-word ("gemma4:cl / oud", "Olla / ma"):
  they keep one line each, the service going under the name where both do not fit, a name too long ending in an
  ellipsis.
- **Models › Every model, by service**: the reasoning menu was cut by the card's edge at 1280 px and the chat size out of
  sight: what a model can do goes under its name (`.model-cell`), and the table has three columns.
- **Vault**: a username broke mid-word on a phone: it keeps one line, ending in an ellipsis; the four tabs stand two by
  two on a phone, the last no longer against the edge.
- **Connectors › Accounts**: an address broke mid-domain beside **Sign out**: the button goes under it where both do not
  fit.
- **Memory** on a phone: the four choices ran off the edge ("From outs…"): they stand two by two; a note's pin, edit and
  forget squeezed its text to half the card and broke its code mid-word: they go under the text.
- **Schedules**: a time broke at its date's hyphen ("2026-10-" over "28 05:15"): **Next** and **Last** keep one line.
- **The composer**: the model's name ended in an ellipsis at 200 px beside room it could fill: up to 20rem, still giving
  way where the bar is short.
- **Markdown**: a bold whose words hold code (`**LinkedIn `x@y.z` 'a gidiyor**`) stood with its marks, the code cutting
  it in pieces: `parseInline` finds it across them and marks them `strong`, which `prose.js` draws bold.
- **Handoffs**: the previews of messages and replies printed their markdown (`**Files:**`, backticks): they show the
  words (`plainText`).
