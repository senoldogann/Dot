### 2036. DotNext: a long chat's tables are measured once, and the scroll of a long chat is measured

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** Measured on the simulator (iPhone 17 Pro Max, iOS 27.2, Debug built with `-O`); the
owner's iPhone had UI automation off and was locked, so its numbers come later with the same scripts.

The owner, 2026-11-27: "Scrolling up and down feels like it freezes and stutters." The chat had no long chat to measure,
so this item adds one and the means to measure it: the fixture `n2-long` (24 turns of tool rows and answers with a
heading, a list, a table, a code block, a quote and links), `UITests/ChatScrollUITests.swift` (16 flicks up and down in
each tool mode; the chat screen may be drawn at most twice meanwhile; XCTest's scroll metric), the frame meter's line per
scroll (`-DotNextScrollMeter 1`), `-DotNextAutoScroll 1` for a device whose UI automation is off (the transcript glides
to its top and back twice at 3000 points a second), `scripts/scroll.sh` and `scripts/autoscroll.sh`.

What it found, with Time Profiler attached to the autoscroll: the chat screen's body ran once in 16 flicks (the scroll
state was not the cause: moving it out of the body changed nothing, so it was not kept). Of the main thread's work in the
scroll's own layout, 47 % was the tables' `TableGrid.plan`: each table that came into sight measured every cell on one
line and again at its column's width, for each of its two layouts (before and after it knew its width), through TextKit
(its text is selectable). A cache of the layout did not help (682 → 628 ms), because the lazy transcript makes a row's
layout again when it comes back into sight.

What changed: a table's sizes are kept by what its cells measure by (`TableMeasureKey`: the cells as drawn, each run's
words with its bold, italic, code, link and reference mark, the text size, Bold Text and how many of the table's own
sites have their icon at the size its cells draw it) in `TableMeasures`,
for the app's life, at most 200 tables' sizes and 400 plans, behind a lock (layout runs outside the main actor's
isolation): a cell is measured on one line once, only a cell its column wraps is measured again at that width, and a
table seen again measures nothing.

Measured on the simulator, the same chat, at a load average of 5 to 16 (other sessions' work on this Mac): Time
Profiler over 4 glides at 3000 pt/s, and the frame meter over 32 flicks and the glides. "Before" is the build without
this item; "after" is the build of this item alone, measured while its key still held only the cells' characters.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `TableGrid.plan` on the main thread | 682 ms | 113 ms |
| hitch time while flicking | 21.6 ms/s | 15.5 ms/s |
| longest frame while flicking | 588 ms | 333 ms |
| hitch time gliding, steps shown / hidden | 107 / 109 ms/s | 83 / 65 ms/s |

The build of items 2036 to 2038 together, which was pushed, is measured in item 2038 (`TableGrid.plan` there: 102 ms).

Checks: `ChatScrollUITests` (the screen drawn once in 16 flicks in both modes), `scripts/scroll.sh`, the DotNext unit
tests.
