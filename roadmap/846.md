look, model, rules, memory) in the panel at the right of its chat, as Grok Bot's panel shows the agent, rather than on
a page of their own.

  - its model, approvals and actions, and what it learned;
  - its instructions;

  its look, the editor one field a row, and the section keeps its height over the helpers.
