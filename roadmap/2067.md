What it shows: **no bot is overloaded today by its orders or routines.** No work order is open (the nine ever made were
accepted, the last on 2026-11-24), two of 43 routines are on (a reminder of JobHunter's and Dot's monthly prune), one
objective is active. The load is in the runs: Dot did 167 runs of its own and DotGelistirici 107 at 102 steps each, and
three bots end a third or more of their runs failed (JobHunter 3 of 4, Creator 3 of 9, ParaBOT 6 of 19). Dot's done
share fell from 97 % to 78 %, 19 points, just under the 20-point fall the mark reads as falling; the other bots are
younger than two weeks (the store was started afresh on 2026-10-28 and most bots made after 2026-11-19), so they have
no week before to fall from. Eight bots ran nothing this week: the manager review (item 2068) finds those.

**What changed.** The store counts what each bot carries (`Store.bot_load_rows`, one query, 8 ms on the owner's 913 MB
store): its open work orders (one escalated to the owner aside), its routines on and in all, the active objectives its
open orders serve (Dot's: every active one), and its runs of the last seven days and of the seven before (a bot's order
attempts and the parts its runs hand out are its own; Dot's helpers are parts of Dot's runs), done, failed and their
steps. `agent/team_load.py` (pure, no model) reads each row: a bot is overloaded with more than 5 open orders, more
than 4 routines on, or a done share that fell 20 points or more from the week before over at least 5 runs in each.
`GET /api/agents` and `GET /api/agents/{id}` give each bot its `load` (counts, `done_share`, `steps` and `reasons`,
each `{kind, count, limit}`). The Agents page's card shows a quiet "Overloaded" badge, its reasons in its title; the
bot's page says it under its role with the reasons. The phone's home row wears a quiet gauge beside the name (VoiceOver
reads "Overloaded"), and the bot profile a notice, "Overloaded: …" with the reasons, in the web's words, Turkish in both
catalogues.
