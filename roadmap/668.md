### 668. The agents' words in the conversation

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** In the owner's screenshots of another agent app, agents pass work between themselves in
one thread: "Sending to Chief…" while it goes, and "Messages from Account Manager and Chief" over what they said. Dot's
agents answered each other already (`message_agent`, item 87), but what an agent answered was inside its delegated row,
a click away, and nothing showed the part on its way.

**The change.**
- `agent/manager.py`: the run that answers a message to an agent names it in its `run_queued` event (`agent_name`); a
  helper an agent's own run delegated to carries the agent's id (item 553) and no name, and stays a helper.
- `timeline.js`: a `message_agent` call shows "Sending to Scout…" with Dot's three dots until the agent's run starts (its
  row takes over) or the call ends without one; an agent's run that ends done puts its answer in the conversation that
  handed it the part, as its message, under "Message from Scout" (the next agent's joins: "Messages from Scout and
  Editor"), its face and name over its words, cut at 1,200 characters with a way to the rest.
- `run.css` draws the pill (`agent-handoff`: `handoff` is the class of a step handed to the owner, item 434, whose card
  the pill's style widened past a 320 px screen on the first try), the head and the bubbles.
- `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_agent_said.py`: a scripted run hands a part to Scout; Scout's run carries its name, and its answer
  stands as "Message from Scout" with its face, name and words, wide with every step and at a phone's width with only
  the status line; nothing is left on its way. A helper Scout's own run delegated to has no name in its event, is
  "Helper 1" and puts no message of Scout's in the conversation.
