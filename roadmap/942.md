### 942. The daemon reads a request's body only within its limit, and the browser's settings are kept before taken

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Found** by an independent review of the server (2026-11-02):
- No body was limited: a sender of several gigabytes to `POST /api/phone/pair`, the one route besides the health check
  and the proof that takes no token, grew the daemon's memory until macOS ended it.
- `POST /api/browser/debug-port`, `/browser/mode`, `/browser/look` and `/browser/profile` changed the running daemon
  first and wrote `config.toml` second: a file that could not be written answered 422 with the change live (a DevTools
  port open that the page said was not set), and `/browser/look` could leave two of its five settings written.

**Built.**
- `server/bodylimit.py`: an ASGI layer inside the guard (a refused request is never read) that reads a body whole only
  within its limit, 4 KB on what the guard lets through without a token (the open routes, the pages, the files: the
  pre-push review) and 32 MB on every route that needs it (an attachment of 20 MB in base64), and
  answers 413 to one past it, declared or streamed.
- `server/routes_browser.py`: those four keep the setting first, then take it, and put the file back when the daemon
  refuses it, its own keys and no other (`kept_then_taken`, `config_edit.browser_values`/`restore_browser_values`: a
  port that is taken is not kept for the next start, and a setting kept meanwhile stays);
  `config_edit.set_browser_settings` writes the five settings of how pages look in one edit.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_open_paths.py`: 413 for a declared and a streamed body past the open route's limit and
for 33 MB with the token; a body within it reaches its route; a guarded route without the token is refused first.
`tests/unit/test_config_edit.py`: the five settings are written together, and none to a file that does not parse.
`tests/smoke/test_devtools_port.py`: a port that is taken is refused and the file keeps the port before it.
