### 619. The iMessage chats stand in a section of their own at the foot of the sidebar

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The owner: "The iMessage chats would be better somewhere else in the app, under iMessage,
and we could close that at the bottom as a collapse." The chat the router makes for a channel (item 572) stood among the
days like any chat, and in the day groups it took the place of chats the owner made themselves. This item takes the
chats of a channel out of the days into a section of their own at the foot of the list, which folds.

**What it is.**
- A chat belongs to a channel when its thread's `source` is a channel's id, as `ChannelRouter._thread` makes it
  (`create_thread(TITLES[channel_id], channel_id)`); `ThreadSummary` carries `source` (it already did; item 610 added
  `team` beside it), so the daemon changed nothing here. `sidebar.js` knows the channels by `CHANNEL_SECTIONS`
  (`imessage`: its name and mark); a thread of any other source, or of a channel it does not know, stays among the days.
- The section: `iMessage 1`, a green speech bubble (`--ok`) before the name, the count after it, at the foot of the
  list, below the days and their "Show older chats" note, and outside the `side-runs` list, so that folding Chats does
  not fold it and its fold does not fold Chats. Its chats are rows as any chat's is (`keptEntry`, the same menu, face
  and age; Select and the arrow keys reach them).
- It folds on a click of its label, and the fold is kept between visits as the others are (`dot.sideFolds`, key
  `imessage`); open until the owner folds it. The label is made once and kept from one painting to the next, so that
  the focus stays on it after a fold. Folded over a chat nobody has read, an accent dot stands after the count (`role
  img`, `aria-label` unread).
- A search shows what it finds in the section whether it is folded or not, as it does for Working; choosing chats to
  delete shows the section open, as it does for Chats.
- A section with no chat is not there, and takes no room.

**What it decides, and why.**
- *A chat at work stands under Working, as every chat does (item 202), and comes back to its section once the run ends.*
  The brief reads "a chat that works or waits there shows it as any chat's row does": the row is the same row (the
  dashed circle, Working and the time) wherever it stands, and Working is where what works stands, folded sections
  included; a run from the phone must not vanish into a folded section. A chat that waits for the owner stays in its
  section with its waiting face, and what it asks stands under Waiting for you, as for every chat.
- *A pinned chat of a channel stays under Pinned.* Pinning is the owner's choice to have it at the top; the section
  holds the chats they have not put anywhere.
- The messages of the daemon about a channel's chat are unchanged: `GET /api/threads`, the lifecycle events and the
  push do not know of the section.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_side_channels.py` (new, browser, 1280 and 390 wide, a real server, scripted runs): no
section while no channel has a chat, and no room taken; the section at the foot, below the days, with its mark and
count, the chat as any chat's row (its menu, its last words, opened it is the chat on the page), the page fits; the
fold, the dot over an unread chat and its label for a screen reader, the focus staying on the label, the fold kept over
a reload (in the browser's own storage), Chats and the section folding each alone; a chat at work under Working with its
time and back in its section when the run ends; the search finding the chat folded or not, "no chat matches" and Enter
opening the first found, with the fold back as the owner left it; a pinned chat under Pinned and back in its section
once unpinned; Select reaching the section's chat and counting it, and the arrow keys walking into it and back.
The existing sidebar and phone tests were run with it: `test_side_working`, `test_side_scroll`, `test_side_panel`,
`test_side_agents`, `test_unread_chats`, `test_chat_menu`, `test_chat_creatures`, `test_activity`, `test_working_rows`,
`test_reconnect`, `test_phone_return`, `test_phone_rows`, `test_phone_lists`, `test_phone_layout` and `test_phone_pages`
(three failed once under the load of `-n auto` and passed alone).

**Left out, and limits.**
- Only iMessage has a section, because only iMessage is a channel; another channel is a line in `CHANNEL_SECTIONS` and
  its icon.
- The section does not say that one of its chats waits for the owner while it is folded (the Waiting section does).
- A chat the owner moved by pinning it, or one that is at work, is not counted in the section's number.
