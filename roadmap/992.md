### 992. Pushes to an iPhone are signed with a subject Apple takes

**Status: done (2026-11-05).** **Found** by the review of the owner's question of 2026-11-05 (a native iPhone app or
not): the owner's log held 253 `push_failed` lines, every one a 403 from `web.push.apple.com`, so no notification
reached the iPhone. `notify/push.py` signed every push with the VAPID subject `mailto:dot@localhost`; Apple refuses a
subject it cannot reach with 403 `BadJwtToken` (other projects fixed the same 403 by dropping a localhost address), and
the daemon logged the status alone, never Apple's answer, so the reason stayed unseen.

**Built.** `notify/push.py`: the subject is the project's page (`SUBJECT`, an https: address, RFC 8292), and a refusal
keeps the first 300 characters of what the service answered, which `push_failed` logs. Docs: `docs/phone.md`.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_push.py` (the claims a push is signed with, and a 403's answer kept). On the owner's
iPhone once deployed: a notification arrives, or the log names Apple's reason.
