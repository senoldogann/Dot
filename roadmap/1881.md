### 1881. DotNext's home is the owner's bots: no tab bar, Dot large on top, one row per agent, a new bot made from "+"

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** The owner's order of 2026-11-25 10:00 ("I want a system in Grok Bot's logic", shots of
another agent app's home, chat, menu and settings; plan `ios-rewrite-2026-11-24/PLAN.md` §27): bots, not tabs. Dot and
each of the owner's agents is a bot, a face and one ongoing conversation.

What must hold:
- The tab bar is gone; the home is the only top level. Every chat belongs to one bot: the agent whose face it wears
  (`actor_id`), Dot for every other chat (its own, a schedule's, a code review's, a campaign's, a team's), so no chat of
  the old list is unreachable.
- A bot's row says what its latest chat says, marks a question waiting on the owner, and shows an unread dot unless
  the owner muted it; the floating "N unread" pill counts the bots with something unread and jumps to the next one.
- A new bot is the daemon's own agent (`POST /api/agents`), then its first meeting (`POST /api/agents/{id}/welcome`,
  item 993) in the phone's language; the phone opens its chat. A meeting that does not start leaves the agent made.
- A bot whose chats are older than the list's window (the newest 100) still opens its latest chat: the daemon's home
  chat of each agent (`GET /api/agents`, found however many chats are newer) stands among its chats, and is read again
  after Mark as read and on each return to the home, so its unread dot goes.
- A new bot speaks first: its first meeting's goal (Dot's words to the agent, source `welcome`) is not drawn as the
  owner's message, as on the web (item 993).
- Faces are the web's characters (`character/skins.js`: the eight shapes as radial profiles, the twelve colours, the
  id's hue when no look was chosen), so a bot has one face on the web and on the phone.
