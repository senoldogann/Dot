template at a time. **As built.** A team is a few templates whose agents work side by side, and nothing besides

Reproduction, Reviewer, Release QA), the Growth team (Competitor Watch, Paid Media, Product Performance, Sales Outbound)

a team**, each card with what the team does and its agents' faces and names; **Use team** (`POST
/api/agent-teams/{id}/use`) makes each agent the owner lacks as its template's Use makes it, keeps an agent of the
owner's with a member's name (read without regard to case, as the team tools read it), checks every agent to make before
it makes any, so a team one of whose agents the home cannot make makes none, and answers with the agents it made and
kept. A card whose agents suggest schedules offers to add those of the agents it makes, switched off, as a template's
card does (item 464). Dot works with a team as with any agents: the owner asks Dot, and Dot hands each agent its part
