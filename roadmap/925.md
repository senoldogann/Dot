### 925. A watchdog outside the daemon restarts one that does not answer and tells of a run that makes no step

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner, the third of the gaps they took from the DeepSeek Harness
"Super-agent" article: a watchdog outside the system that catches what is no crash, a process alive with a loop that
makes no progress. launchd's `KeepAlive` starts the daemon again when it exits; a daemon that hung (its health check
unanswered) stayed hung until someone saw it, and a run whose loop stopped without an error worked on in the list with
no event. The progress watchdog (`[agent] stall_limit`, `failure_streak`) and the checkpoints of item 909 are inside the run's own loop, and stop
with it.

- `watchdog.py`: one look a minute, a process that ends. Three looks in a row with the daemon's job alive and its
  health check unanswered restart the daemon (`restart_service`) and show a notification of the Mac; a restart that
  waits for the runs (item 441) and a job launchd is starting are left alone. With the daemon answering, a run
  `running` with no event for longer than a model call and a command may take (at least 30 minutes), and inside no
  call that its own limit still allows, is told once; `agent/executor.py` puts each call's `timeout_s` in its
  `tool_call` event for it (the pre-push review: a run waiting 40 minutes on its helpers was told as stuck).
  State in `watchdog.json`; the database read only.
- `launchd.py`: `dev.dotagent.watchdog` (`StartInterval` 60), installed, inspected and removed; `cli_daemon.py`:
  `service install` loads it once the daemon answers, `service uninstall` removes it first, `service status` has its
  line.
- `POST /api/runs/{id}/stuck` (`routes_runs.py`): the owner is told "A task makes no progress" with the run's page,
  when the run still works.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_watchdog.py` (the stuck runs of a real store's file, not one with a fresh event, one
waiting for the owner or one ended; the bound past the longest step; three unanswered looks restart once and notify;
a stuck run told once; a run inside a helper's call within its limit is not stuck, past it is; a failed tell keeps the runs told before it; a failed restart is told and tried again three looks on), `tests/smoke/test_run_stuck.py` (the route tells the owner with the page, not of an ended run)
and `tests/unit/test_service_install_from_a_terminal.py` (the watchdog's list loaded with the service, every minute,
from Dot's home; never launchd's in a test).
