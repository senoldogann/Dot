### 1097. Free mode refuses no step for the secret it carries

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** Asked by the owner, with a screenshot: a run that had read outside content measured the
length of a Claude OAuth token in a `python3 - <<'EOF'` (nothing sent anywhere) and was "Blocked by Dot's safety
rules: this run read outside content, and the step would send a secret out of the Mac". The owner wanted no such
refusal in yolo, full access and free mode.

Unlike item 1094 this was no false alarm: the command held a secret Dot holds, word for word. The refusal (item 525)
is what keeps a page's hidden instruction from having a run send the owner's keys away, and nothing else stops that.
Offered three ways, the owner chose (2026-11-10) to lift it in free mode alone (recommended: free mode is the owner's
own switch, and "asks nothing" by item 922), over yolo and free, and over every mode as before.

Now `ActionGate._refuse_carried_secret` lets the step through while free mode is on, recorded as an approval free mode
answered (the secret masked, as the other waived questions are) and logged as `secret_egress_free` (the run and the
tool, no word of the step). The records still mask the secret. In yolo and every other mode it is
refused as before.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_secret_egress.py` (a yolo run that read a page still has its step refused; in free mode the
same kind of step runs, its record masked), `test_taint_check.py`, the whole unit suite.
