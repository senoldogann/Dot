### 1911. DotNext makes a scheduled task, and shows each schedule's history

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** Backlog G1(a) of the iPhone rewrite's parity gaps: the phone could edit, run, switch and
delete a schedule, but not make one, and a schedule's runs were seen only on the Mac.

What was built, on routes the daemon already has (`POST /api/schedules`, `GET /api/schedules/{id}/firings`; neither
refuses a phone key, no daemon change):
- Settings › Scheduled tasks has a + (and the empty page a button) that opens "New scheduled task": its name and what
  Dot does; when (every day, weekdays, one weekday at a time of day, every hour, once at a date and time, or a cron form
  typed by hand), with the rhythm said in words under it; the agent its runs run as; task or research; the model and its
  reasoning on the one model picker; the approvals and goal mode. A choice left on Default is left out of the body, so the
  Mac's setting (or the agent's) stands when it fires, as the web form does. The time zone, history, chat, notification
  and check-first choices stay on the Mac's form.
- A repeat is read in the Mac's time zone (the footer says so); a single run is sent with the phone's offset, so it
  fires at the moment picked on the phone whatever the Mac's zone.
- A schedule's menu has History: its last 20 runs, newest first, each with how it went, when, and the first line of its
  answer; a run opens its chat.

DotEngine: `ScheduleRhythm` (the cron or `at` of each choice), `ScheduleCreate`, `ScheduleFiring`, and the
`createSchedule` and `scheduleFirings` calls.

Checked: `ScheduleCreateTests` (each rhythm's cron, the offset of a single run, the keys of the body, a firing decoded);
`SettingsUITests.testAScheduleIsMadeAndItsHistoryOpensARunsChat` on the fixture daemon (+, Add disabled until a name and
a goal, the rhythm in words, the new row, History, a run opening its chat). The local Xcode lock was held past ten
minutes, so the build and the UI test ran on GitHub's `ios` job.

Next slice: the time zone, notifications and history choices of the Mac's form, if the owner wants them on the phone.
