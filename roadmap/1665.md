### 1665. DotNext, the iPhone and iPad rewrite: its target, the design system, the components, the gallery and the shell

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Wave N0 of the rewrite planned in the owner's workspace (`ios-rewrite-2026-11-24/PLAN.md`,
sections 9-18 binding; the owner's decisions of §15: the ink as the only accent, SF Symbols, a flat graphite icon).

What was built:
- `ios/Next/` and two XcodeGen targets in `ios/project.yml`: the app `DotNext` (`dev.dotagent.phone.next`, iOS 26.0,
  iPhone and iPad, its own app group `group.dev.dotagent.phone.next`, its own Keychain access group, URL scheme
  `dotnext`, no push entitlement: push and the Live Activity stay with the old app until the swap) and `DotNextTests`.
  The old app's targets are unchanged.
- The palette (`Design/Palette.swift`): started from the old app's graphite `DotPalette`, four palettes (light, dark and
  each with Increase Contrast), surfaces ground / card (also the grouped section, review S3) / nested-pressed / sheet /
  opaque glass for Reduce Transparency, no strokes; inks text, secondary, tertiary and a glyph tier; the primary action
  is the ink; amber only for what waits on the owner, red only for a failure. `ContrastAudit` is a pure function that
  lists every rendered pair (each text ink on each surface ≥ 4.5:1, glyphs and the action ≥ 3:1, the action's label,
  the amber ink on its fill, the tab badge) and the test asserts none fails in any of the four palettes.
- Type roles on the system font scaled with Dynamic Type (body 16/23 through a tested line-spacing function, monospaced
  code 13/19, tabular numbers); the reading column measured from the font (`ProseColumn`, 68 characters; TextKit's own
  line breaking keeps it in 60-75 at three text sizes); spacing, radius (20/24/27/capsule, continuous) and metric
  tokens; motion tokens (120/200/150 ms, none under Reduce Motion); haptics; the stream-tick gate (only for visible,
  followed, live output, at most every 320 ms); glass only on floating chrome; one SF Symbol weight per context.

How the design system was checked: `xcodegen generate`; `xcodebuild build` and `test` of `DotNext` on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator
(iOS 27.2): its 17 tests pass (19 with the two of item 1666). Each new test was made to fail once: one build with 18 deliberate defects (an ink under 4.5:1,
a surface left out of the audit, a light card darker than its ground, a dark nested step below the card, a hue in a
neutral, a wrong luminance weight, Increase Contrast ignored, an unscaled line height, body at 17, a 90-character
column, the status order swapped, the question not amber, the composer threshold moved, replays ticking, Reduce Motion
ignored, links not underlined, an unknown symbol, a shimmer past 1) failed all 17 design tests, and was reverted. The
old app built and its tests ran unchanged.

The components, the gallery, the shell and the icon:

- System chrome first: inline navigation titles, the four tabs Chats / Agents / Activity / Settings in the system's
  floating tab bar (never minimised, the Chats badge amber, not the system's red), sheets with detents and the grabber,
  ✕ to close an inspector, ✕ and an ink ✓ for an edit (not dismissible while dirty), menus, swipe actions, a confirm
  for the emergency stop.
- Components in their states: the list row (49 / 63 pt, the 44 pt hit floor, the detail under the title at the
  accessibility sizes, one VoiceOver label), the grouped section (radius 24, no stroke, rows told apart by spacing),
  radio and switch rows (the switch is drawn in the ink: the system's white thumb vanished on a light track in the dark
  look), primary / secondary / destructive buttons (red words on a neutral fill), chips, the status word (its order is
  the plan's priority), the empty state (`ContentUnavailableView`), loading, notices (announced), the toast with Undo
  (glass capsule, announced, swipe or escape to dismiss), the glass composer shell (radius 27, compact at rest, field
  and toolbar split when focused or long), the floating working capsule and the scroll-to-bottom circle in one glass
  container (segments stack at AX sizes), the tool-call row (one quiet line, shimmer while running and still under
  Reduce Motion, a red ✕ and "failed" for VoiceOver, tap to open the detail, hold to copy) under a 44 pt group
  disclosure, the helper group with each helper's current step, the speaker line (Dot's face, another agent's disc),
  the owner's bubble (≤ 85 %, radius 20), the answer text (links in the ink, underlined), the code block (language,
  Copy, sideways scroll).
- From the owner's reference shots (plan §18): the helper row (status dot and word after the title, a live tabular
  clock, the engine line, a chevron, a round 44 pt Stop with a red glyph on the nested fill that turns the row to
  "Stopping…"), the background-work row (icon, title, kind, chevron), the Agents and Background work sheets, and the
  chat header with a subtitle (agent · Mac) and a trailing glass capsule of quick panels.
- The SF Symbols sample sheet and a gallery of eight pages (debug builds); the shell's placeholder tabs with sample
  content for every role Dot plays (assistant with computer use, coding, teacher, employee, a failure).
- iPad decided: the tab bar stays the top level; inside Chats and Agents a compact width is a stack, a regular width a
  split view sharing one selection; grouped pages at most 700 pt wide, prose at the measured 68-character column.
- The flat graphite app icon (light disc, two dark eyes; dark and tinted variants) for DotNext only,
  `ios/Next/scripts/make_icon.swift`.

How it was checked: the gallery and the shell were photographed with the harness of item 1666 (every scene in light and
dark, at Large and AX3, on an iPhone 17 Pro and an iPad Pro 11-inch simulator, in Turkish) and looked at beside the
owner's reference shots; what the shots showed was fixed before the last set: the switch's thumb in the dark look, the
red tab badge, the status word wrapping in the capsule, tool lines cut at AX3 (they wrap now), the tab bar hidden in an
iPad split, a focus ring on the selected row, settings stretched across an iPad, a full-width button that was not,
a texture that made the chrome page unreadable. The shots are in the owner's workspace (`shots-n0/`).

Not checked: the real phone (Proma), VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch Control, a hardware keyboard and landscape were
not driven; glass over live scrolling content is seen only in still shots; AX5 was not shot (AX3 was).
