### 530. A server picked in the composer wears its service's real logo

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** The owner's word of 2026-10-22, with a screenshot: the MCP servers should show their real
logos when they are picked and tagged in the composer too. The menus (item 447) and Customize already drew each server
with the mark of its service (item 477), but the chip a pick became under the box, and the chip under a sent message,
drew the plug for every server (`picks.js` gave a `plugin` pick one icon): an `exa` chip showed a plug though Exa's
logo is in `static/icons/plugins`.

**As built.**
- A server's chip wears the same mark as its row in the at menu: the service's logo, else its icon or letters
  (DeepWiki's magnifier, Context7's C7), on the tile's light ground, so that a dark logo reads in the dark scheme too;
  the plug, tinted as before, for a server of no service. `.pick-mark[data-mark="service"]` in `app.css` is the tile (18
  pixels, the logo at 12) and the chip gives up some of its left padding for it.
- The marks come from the palette (`GET /api/palette`, the `mark` of each server), which a new module `palette.js`
  reads once and keeps (it was `loadPalette` of `composer.js`) with the marks by server name. A chip drawn before the
  palette has arrived (a draft kept, or a message sent: they name their servers before any menu was opened) wears the
  plug, asks for the palette once, and every chip that names a server is drawn again when it arrives, the chips under
  sent messages too. A palette that cannot be read leaves the plug and is told in the console; the menu says it to the
  owner when it is opened, as before.
- Accounts keep the mail icon on their chips (their menu rows wear the provider's logo): left as it was.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_composer_picks.py` with a real Chrome, wide and light and on a phone and dark: a server
picked from the at menu leaves a chip with the mark of its row (a logo, an icon, letters, and the plug for the fixture
server), on the light ground; and a chip restored from a draft before the palette arrives wears the plug and then the
logo. Both fail without the change. `npm run check`.
