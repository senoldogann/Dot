### 680. An action too long for the review to read whole comes to the owner

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** The whole-project review of 2026-10-26 found that in `auto` the review model read the
start and the end of an action's target longer than 12,000 characters, with a mark of what was left out between
(item 242), and was not told to refuse or escalate what it could not see whole. A run that had read a page written to
lead it could pad a long command and put its harmful part in the middle, where the review model approved it unseen.

**The change.** An action whose target is longer than the review reads whole is not sent to the review model: the
review escalates it, saying why, and the question comes to the owner as in `ask`, with the whole command in its box.
Nothing else of `auto` changes; `yolo` asks nothing, as before.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_review_target.py`: a command of 9,000 to 12,000 characters is read whole and approved; a
longer one is escalated with no call of the model.
