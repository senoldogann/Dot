### 1047. A schedule's state holds against late writes

**Status: done (2026-11-08).** Part of the owner's ask to make the implicit state machines explicit (item 1045).

- `record_schedule_skip` wrote the next time computed from the schedule as `_fire` read it, after several awaits: an
  edit of its cron or time, or a switch, made meanwhile was overwritten, and the schedule fired at the old time. It now
  moves on only while `next_run_at` is still the `due` it read, as `claim_schedule_fire` does.
- `set_schedule_status("running")` and `record_schedule_hand_run` ran after the run's watch had started: a run that
  ended at once had its outcome overwritten by `running`, shown until the next firing. `running` is now written only
  while the run works (`mark_schedule_running`, and a `CASE` in `record_schedule_hand_run`).
