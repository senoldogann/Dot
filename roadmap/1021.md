### 1021. Two tests that failed on CI's first attempt are fixed at the cause, and a third says why when it fails

**Status: done (2026-11-07).** **Found** by the CI/CD session: since 2026-11-03 nine gate runs were run again, each
after `browser (phone 1)` or `browser (pages)` failed on the first attempt and passed on the second, each rerun about
7 minutes of a macOS runner.

**Fixed at the cause.**
- `test_browser_eval.py::test_refs_a_script_cannot_be_given_are_told_and_the_page_stays_usable` waited for "the refs
  are in different frames" and got "the page did not answer in time": to tell two refs' frames apart, the driver asked
  the page for each element, and a loaded runner's page took over the 2 s a count may wait. A ref names its frame
  (`f2e7`, `e7` for the page's own), so `browser/driver.py` now tells refs of two frames by their names before it asks
  the page anything: the answer is the same on a busy page, and it costs no round trip. The test now asks while a
  script holds the page; on the old driver that gives the very error CI gave.
- `test_phone_rows.py::test_a_runs_rows_stand_close_on_a_phone` measured the rows as the group opened and once got a
  pitch of 39 px. It now waits for the chat's page to say it is shown (`dot:page-ready`, views/run.js) and for two
  measures two frames apart to agree, a condition instead of a time. What gave 39 px was not reproduced (a font that
  came 3 s late and a page without `chat-shell` gave 34 and 50).

**Not found yet, now said when it fails.** `test_phone_screens.py::test_how_long_ago_a_chat_ended_stays_in_one_line_
beside_a_long_goal` (4 of the 9): after the click on Save from a chat no dialog was in the page. The dialog opens in
the click's handler, and neither this Mac (15 runs, 12 with every request slowed) nor CI alone (25 runs in one job,
PR #819) showed it. The test now records what the page does after the click (the click and where it landed, a page
left or loaded again, a dialog added or removed, the page drawn again, a script error) and puts it in the failure.

**Left.** `test_browser_boundary.py` (Chrome did not hand over a new tab within 8 s) and `test_phone_all_pages.py`
(an HTTP read of the test's own set-up timed out at 5 s) failed once each; no cause was shown, and neither is changed.
