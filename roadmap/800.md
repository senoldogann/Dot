### 800. A new tab is told by its id, a wait reads iframes, and what went through is said when the page cannot be read

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner ("2c için başla"): the browser points item 792 left for later.
Measured first in the owner's store (30 days, read-only): two `opening a tab failed` (`Timeout 8000ms exceeded while
waiting for event "page"`, 2026-10-27 11:22 and 2026-10-28 01:02, both while Chrome was overloaded) and ten reads of
the page that failed after a step, a new tab or an open.

- **A new tab matched by its address once.** `_background_tab` took the page Playwright reported only if its address
  was already the tab's mark; one reported before the address committed was let pass and the call waited out
  `action_timeout_s`, and the tab Chrome made stayed open with no chat to close it. The page is now told by the id
  `Target.createTarget` answers (`page_target_id`, else the mark as before), another call's new tab is passed over, and
  a tab not handed over in time, not made blank or whose run stops while it waits is closed through CDP; one whose
  colour scheme cannot be set in the owner's Chrome is closed too.
- **A tab left behind when its registration failed.** `_create_tab` closes the page when `_register` raises: no
  thread holds it yet.
- **`browser_wait_text` read no iframe.** The snapshot shows the text of a page's frames, the wait looked only at the
  main document. Between its half-second slices it now looks in the first ten frames below the main one
  (`text_in_frames`, side by side, a second at most).
- **Two downloads of one name at once.** Both were handed the free name and the second overwrote the first;
  `unique_download_path` now takes the name by creating the file, and a download that fails removes the file when it
  is still empty.
- **A read after an action that itself fails.** `browser_act` and `browser_upload` raised the read's error alone, so a
  run took the click or the upload for not done. The error now names the steps that went through (the upload) and
  says not to repeat them; a forbidden address and a batch where nothing went through raise as before.

**Not changed:** an approved click or upload not checked against the approved page once it runs. In yolo nothing is
asked, so there is nothing to check; in the other modes it needs the call to know it waited on an approval, which is
the gate's (`agent/approvals.py`, "Always needs a human") and is an item of its own.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_new_tabs.py` (a tab found by its id before its address commits, another call's
tab never taken, a tab not handed over, not made blank, stopped while it comes or not dressed is closed, two downloads
of one name, an empty failed download removed), `tests/unit/test_browser_unsettled.py`
(the error names what went through, nothing went through raises as before, a forbidden address),
`tests/smoke/test_browser.py` (a wait sees a text come inside an iframe).
