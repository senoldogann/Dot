### 456. A no is not asked again in the same run

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Part of the ninth of the nine ideas taken from OpenBot (item 449 has the plan): "a
declined action is not asked again in the same run". Today a run whose action the owner refused may put the same card to
them again a step later, and in `auto` the review model, which reads the owner's instructions but not their no, may
approve it. **As built.** `agent/declined.py` keeps the owner's no by the action as its card showed it (tool, summary,
target and amount, exactly: a browser element keeps its ref while its role and name stay the same, so another Reply
button is another action; the design's normalising of refs, which would have merged them, is left out on the critique's
word). It never keeps one for an action whose card shows less than all of it: a kind whose card says not what it writes
or types (a file's text, a field's), and a card that cut its text wherever the cut falls (`…` of the tools' clips, an MCP
call's arguments among them; `[N chars omitted]`, `[N more chars]`), told a text by its length (`(42 chars)`, what a
desktop or browser step types) or masked a secret (`***`), in its summary or its target: two actions such a card cannot
tell apart are not one. `RunGate` keeps it when the owner denies a question (`denied`, decided in the web UI or the
terminal; an approval nobody answered is no answer, and the review model's or the eval harness's no is not the owner's)
and looks it up in `_authorize_decision` after the yolo waiver and after a refusal of the policy, before the route, so
the same action is refused at once, with no card and no wait, before the owner and before the review model of `auto`
alike; the policy's event is the refusal's record, as it is of a step the owner refuses (item 474). The run is told it
stays declined until the owner writes, and that it can carry on, ask with `ask_user`, or finish. A message of the owner
(a steer, the answer to a question, `add_instruction`) releases it. Nothing is allowed by it: an action the policy
allows runs as before, and yolo, where nothing is asked, keeps nothing, so a no given in another mode does not carry into
it (the owner's word of 2026-10-21: "YOLO modundaki serbestliğe dokunma"). The kept refusals live with the run's gate: a
resumed or delegated run asks once more. Tests: `tests/unit/test_declined.py` (the key exact, the kinds never kept,
every partial card as the tools write it, the release, the words), `tests/smoke/test_approval_modes.py` (the real gate:
the repeat refused within a second with no card, its policy event recorded, another action asking, a message reopening
it; an expired question, the eval harness's and the review model's no not kept; in `auto` the review model not asked
again after the owner's no; yolo untouched). Docs: `docs/safety.md`, `docs/autonomy.md`.
