### 1024. The phone fit tests measure a field once the browser reports a touch screen

**Status: done (2026-11-07).** **Found** by the CI/CD session: `tests/smoke/test_phone_fit.py::test_no_page_slides_
sideways_or_lets_the_phone_zoom_into_a_field` turned main red on 2026-11-07 (Pixel 7 `#/settings/schedules`:
`Search chats 14px`, `Check-in at 14px`, `A thing for Dot's list 14px`), and was run again rather than fixed; it did
the same on 2026-10-23 (Pixel 7 `#/runs`), and `test_phone_dialogs.py` once ("Edit note": `Note 14px`, `Tags 14px`).

**What the failures show.** Each time every field of the page had the size it has without a touch screen, the app's own
14 px: app.css gives a field 16 px under `(pointer: coarse)` alone (no script decides it but on an iPhone, which is
not measured so), so at that moment the browser did not yet report a touch screen to the page. The test measured after
a fixed pause of 300 ms. A probe of 40 fresh Pixel 7 contexts on this Mac reported `(pointer: coarse)` at once every
time: the lag is CI's, not reproduced here.

**Built.** `test_phone_fit.py`: `_settled_fit` measures a page on a phone other than an iPhone once
`matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` is true at a frame, and once two measures a frame apart agree, in place of the
300 ms pause; a browser that never reports a touch screen, or a page that never stands still, fails saying which (about
5 s). `test_phone_dialogs.py`: the same wait before a dialog's fields are measured.

**Checked.** Both files pass (10 tests); the page loop no longer sleeps.
