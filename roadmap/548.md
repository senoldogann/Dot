### 548. A phone's push subscription is taken as iOS sends it

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** Asked by the owner: after the notification permission is granted on the phone, the page
shows "the request is not valid (expirationTime: Extra inputs are not permitted)", so the phone cannot subscribe.

**As built.**
- `src/dotagent/server/views.py`: `PushSubscribeRequest` takes the browser's `expirationTime` (a time in milliseconds,
  or null) as an optional aliased field and keeps nothing of it; iOS includes it in `PushSubscription.toJSON()` and the
  frozen model refused it. Every other unknown field is still refused.
- `docs/phone.md` says the subscription is taken as the browser sends it and that Dot keeps nothing of
  `expirationTime`.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_phone.py` subscribes with the keys of a browser and `expirationTime: null`, as iOS sends
it, against a real server, and the subscription is accepted; the push that follows reaches its service. The push
smoke test and the unit tests pass.
