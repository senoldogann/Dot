### 927. Fan-out: the independent steps of a plan run at once as helpers, by the plan rather than the model

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner, the second of the gaps they took from the DeepSeek Harness
"Super-agent" article, whose sharpest measurement is that a model left to decide hands no work to its helpers (5 tries
of 5, its p95 doubled) and does when the architecture decides. Measured on the owner's Mac (2026-10-28 to 11-02): 17 of
84 runs of their chats took more than 10 minutes, 6 more than 30, and 4 runs of 127 were helpers. Item 915 already
starts a request of the owner's in a chat of its own while one works, so the chat is not held; what was left was the
parallel work itself. The owner chose this over a bound of minutes, knowing it spends more tokens.

- `tools/meta.py`: a step of `update_plan` may carry a `brief`, a complete instruction for a run that cannot see the
  conversation. When two or more pending steps with one wait for nothing open, they run at once as helpers
  (`tools/fan_out.py`: `ready_steps`, `fan_out`), at most `[agent] fan_out` (3), as `delegate` runs them; the plan shows
  them in progress, then each done or failed with its report as the evidence; the reports come back in the plan's
  result, fenced and untrusted when a helper read outside content. One ready step stays the run's own; a helper's plan,
  a chat turn, a run with no spawner and an agent whose role leaves `delegate` out fan out nothing. The plan tool may take as long as a delegated run may.
- `docs/runs.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_fan_out.py`: the ready steps (pending, a brief, waiting for nothing open); a real run in
the loop whose plan has two such steps and one of its own: two helpers of the run with the briefs as their goals, both
steps marked done with their reports, the third open; one ready step alone starts no helper.
