### 1977. The web UI speaks Turkish on every screen, and a check fails for a line of English that is not in the catalogue

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner, with `dot.uiLanguage` Turkish, read most of the web UI in English: all 23 pages of Settings, the first conversation, the greeting, the composer, the cards that ask and the notes between steps (2026-11-26 review, C-ux item 1). Only 50 of 230 modules used `i18n.js`; the others wrote their words in their code.

What was built: a pass over every module that reads each string and template of the interface with acorn and moves it to the catalogue (`js/lang/en-app.js`, `en-views.js`, `tr-app.js`, `tr-views.js`, 5,000 keys, beside the core `en.js` and `tr.js`), with the Turkish written by hand, in the register of the existing catalogue (polite, "alt ajan" for a subagent); the sentences that were assembled from English fragments (plurals, "it/them", lists joined with "and") became whole sentences with a key for each form, or `listAnd` (`Intl.ListFormat` in the interface's language); the counts through `tCount`. What the daemon matches by its own English words (the initiative and owners' reasons of the policy, the outbox's 409 text, the stopped run's result) stays as it was, and the modules that run before the catalogue (`boot.js`, `appearance/rules.js`) are left out. `index.html` preloads the new files.

Checked: `tests/js/i18n-literals.test.js` (acorn reads every module; a line of English in a place that reaches the screen, with no entry in `tests/js/i18n-exempt.json`, fails; so do an exempt line no module holds and a sentence kept in English as its own Turkish), `npm run check`, and `tests/smoke/test_turkish_interface.py` in Chrome (every page of Settings wide and on a phone: its title in Turkish, no console error, no sideways scroll, no line of English among Dot's own words). What the daemon sends as data (the agent templates', search engines', connectors' and plugin gallery's descriptions, one macOS message) is English still: those pages are left out of the line scan and named in the test.
