### 939. Self-repair shows what Claude Code works on now, step by step, with Stop

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-02): "self-repair sayfasına daha iyi bir tasarım … sonuçta
bir model çalışıyor". The page told only how sessions ended. **Researched**: GitHub's Copilot agent sessions (a log per
session, an icon a tool call), Cursor's agents window (a group of what needs you), ReUI's live run feed and Liveblocks'
activity feed (what an agent does now kept apart from what it did), Cloudscape's agent timeline: a pinned card of the
work under way above the history.

**Built.**
- `repair/claude.py`: `steps_of` reads the steps a line of Claude Code's stream shows (a tool call each: read, edit,
  write, run, search, plan; a path in the checkout named from it, a command by the description Claude Code gave it),
  and `run_claude` hands each to its caller as it is seen.
- `repair/night.py`: a session and a second look keep what they work on now in kv `repair.live` (`LiveSession`: the
  problem, its kind, its round, the account, model and effort, the newest 40 steps masked of the secrets Dot holds),
  deleted when they end.
- `scheduler/runner.py` `stop_repair`, `POST /api/repair/stop`: the night's task is cancelled, as the emergency stop
  does.
- `views/repair.js`: the card of the work under way above the overview (its time by the second, the newest four steps,
  all a click away, Stop after a question), read again every four seconds while a night works and the page is in sight,
  the card alone drawn again; *Claude Code* folded at the page's foot to its model and effort.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_repair.py`: the steps of a line, cut and named from the checkout; the live state keeps
forty, masked. `tests/smoke/test_repair_night.py`: the live state is gone after a night. `tests/smoke/test_repair_page.py`:
the card with a night at work (its round, problem, model, four steps of five, its time), a step seen meanwhile read in
by the page, Stop after its question; on a phone's width.
