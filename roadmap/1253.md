### 1253. A text step with no field after a click names the clicked field, and a wait for words names browser_wait_text

**Status: done (2026-11-20).** Dot's record: `browser_act` refused its arguments in growth and mail runs
(`run_mwk33lk4`, `run_mwlu71zd`, `run_mwn8cu08`), three shapes of call:

- a `click` on a field's ref and then `type` with no ref (a LinkedIn post's text). Told only where a ref comes from
  (item 1133), the run wrote the post with a `browser_eval` script (`execCommand('insertText')`), past the checks a
  step of a batch makes at its field. A `fill`, `type` or `insert` with no ref and no point right after a `click` or
  `double_click` on a ref (a look between them left out) is now refused naming that ref: `write "ref": "f1e3654" in the
  type step too`.
- a `{"action": "wait", "text": "x"}` before a click. Only a bare `wait` is left out of a batch (item 1133), so this one
  was refused with the list of actions, and the run sent its click alone twice before it found `browser_wait_text`. A
  `wait` step now fails naming `browser_wait_text`, as an `upload` step names `browser_upload` (item 755).
- `press Meta+a`, `press Backspace`, `insert` with no ref anywhere in the batch: the model's own mistake, answered by the
  refusal of item 1133 and corrected on the next call. Unchanged.
