### 1338. Every settings page draws in plain rows and cards: a hairline card, a title and its muted line at the left, the control at the right

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The owner's word of 2026-11-22: make Dot plain and professional, "every line and
area fixed one by one"; a settings screen of a left nav in groups and a right pane of titled cards of rows is the
target, on a phone as on a Mac.

What the settings pages share is restyled once, in `css/settings.css` and the shared builders (`panels.js`), instead of
page by page:

- A section is a name of 14 px (medium) over a card with a hairline round it (the shadow and the soft frame are gone); the
  sections stand 32 px apart in a column of 800 px; a row is 12 px over 16 px with a hairline between rows, its title and
  the muted line under it (12 px on a Mac, a phone's 15 px) at the left and its control at the right, centred on both lines.
- The cards and rows that pages draw by hand (`.card`, `.card-pad`, `.setting` with its `.row` and `.hint`, a range with its
  value) take the same look through one block of rules, so General, Appearance, Models, Browser, Memory, Rules, Skills,
  Vault, Phone, Channels, the harness and the rest need no markup of their own for it; on a phone a control that wants room
  (a box, a menu, a choice) goes under the title at the full width, a switch stays at the right.
- The cards that fold (System's Health checks, Details and Permissions) have the same hairline and radius; System's three
  tiles are one card with hairlines between them, and the emergency stop is a plain row with a small red mark in place
  of the round pink tile. Scheduled tasks' rows and its form (item 1337) use the same builders.
- `settingsHead` draws the page's line and its buttons only: the page's name is the breadcrumb of item 1336, which is the
  page's one h1.
- The nav's rows are 28 px with a 16 px mark, so that the whole nav stands in a window of 860 px; the phone's sheet has
  the same search.

Not covered: Plugins' and Connectors' galleries of tiles and the Agents page's cards keep their own shapes (item 1339
restyles what is left).

Checked: `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py` (no literal colour, size, radius or spacing off the grid outside the tokens),
the smoke tests of the settings pages, the Models pages' menus (a card does not clip its popover), the phone's
type and layout tests; screenshots in Chromium 1280x860 and WebKit as an iPhone, light and dark.
