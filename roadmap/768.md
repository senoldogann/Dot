### 768. Settings › Vault: delete ticked items together; the list scrolls in its card

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** The owner imported Google's saved passwords (a CSV of a few hundred logins) and wanted
the ones they do not need gone without deleting them one by one, and the list to scroll by itself instead of **Show
more**.

- **Select** (beside New item, as the sidebar's Select for chats) turns each row into a tick; the whole row is the
  tick's label, a shift-click ticks every row from the one ticked last, and a bar over the list says how many are ticked
  and has **Select all** (every item the tab and the search let through, the rows not drawn yet too) / **Clear**,
  **Delete** and a way out. A ticked row that a search or a tab hides is unticked, so that Delete removes only what is
  in sight. Delete asks first ("Delete 12 items?"), says what goes (the Keychain items, and that Dot can no longer sign
  in with them) and ends the choosing.
- `POST /api/vault/items/remove` (`{"ids": [...]}`, at most 20,000): `VaultItems.remove_many` removes 40 at a time, the
  batch's Keychain items first in one `security -i` (`LoginKeychain.remove_many`) and its records after, as a single
  delete does, under each item's lock; an id the vault does not hold is passed over. A line of the prompt that fails (an
  item the Keychain lacks fails its line) has each account asked again alone, where one that is not there counts as
  removed. A Keychain that refuses ends the removal and says how many went: the accounts asked alone before the refusal
  are gone from it, so their records go too (`PartlyRemoved` names them; the pre-push review found records left without
  their secrets), and so do a contact's, which has none; the others stay whole, to be removed again.
- The list is its own scroller (at most 70% of the window's height, 800 px) and draws the next 60 rows when its end
  comes within 600 px of sight; the place is kept when the list is painted again after a save or a delete.

**The change.** `vault/items.py` (`remove_many`, `Removal`), `vault/secretstore.py` (`remove_many` on the protocol and
the Keychain), `store/db.py` (`delete_vault_items`), `server/routes_vaultitems.py`, `views/vault.js`,
`css/settings.css` (`.vault-rows`, `.vault-select-*`, `.vault-pick`, `.vault-check`), docs/daemon.md. A batch's
records are read by their ids (`Store.get_vault_items`), not the whole vault again for each.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_vault_items.py`: 91 items in three batches, an id twice and one never held passed over, a
contact asks the Keychain nothing, no lock left; a Keychain that refuses the second batch leaves 55 whole and answers
40; one that refuses within the first batch after 10 takes their records and leaves 85 whole.
`tests/smoke/test_vault_items_routes.py`: the route, an empty list refused. `tests/smoke/test_vault_keychain.py` (a
keychain file of the test's own, run on CI): several removed in one prompt, one the keychain lacks does not stop the
rest. `tests/smoke/test_vault_page.py` (headless Chrome): Select, a row's words tick it, shift-click ticks a run, Select
all and Clear, a search unticks what it hides, Cancel on the question keeps all, Delete removes the ticked items and
their Keychain items; 150 logins draw 60, then 120 on a wheel, then 150, no Show more, the place kept after a delete,
Select all ticks the 149 not all drawn and deletes them.
