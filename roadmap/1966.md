### 1966. DotNext speaks the language chosen in it, whatever the iPhone's

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner, on build 8: Chats, Dot's profile and the routines said "No chats yet", "All
chats", "Routines", "Your assistant", "New chat", "Select", "Search chats", "Next run in…" in English.

Cause: every one of those words is in the string catalog with its Turkish ("Henüz sohbet yok", "Tüm sohbetler"…); the
app speaks the language iOS gives it, the iPhone's (English on the owner's), and offered no choice of its own, while
the web chooses its language in Settings › General. "Your assistant" is Dot's built-in role, the daemon's English
default (`store/models.py`), shown as it came.

What was built: Settings › General › Language: Same as the iPhone, English or Türkçe, kept where iOS keeps an app's own
language (`AppleLanguages` of the app's domain, which iOS Settings › Dot Next › Language writes too), so every string
of the catalog, the dates and the relative times ("Next run in…") follow it from the next start; the page says so.
Dot's built-in role is shown in the app's language (`shownRole`).

Checked: `ChatListTests.testTheAppsLanguageIsReadFromWhatIOSKeepsForIt`; the strings check (every key with
Turkish); on the simulator, English chosen in a Turkish app and the app started again in English.
