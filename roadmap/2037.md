### 2037. DotNext: a whole answer is parsed once, not each time its row is drawn

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** Measured on the simulator with item 2036's scroll check; the owner's iPhone later.

`AnswerView` parsed its markdown (`markdownBlocks`) and the words of each block (`answerInline`) in every drawing of its
body, and the lazy transcript draws a row again each time it comes into sight: 13.6 % of the main thread's work in a
long chat's scroll. Now a whole answer's parsed blocks (`DrawnBlock`) are kept by its words in `DrawnAnswers` (an
`NSCache` of at most 400), so an answer is parsed once while the app runs; words still being revealed are parsed as they
show and never kept. `PerfCounters.answersParsed` counts the parses.

Measured (4 glides at 3000 pt/s, Time Profiler, against item 2036's first form): `AnswerView.body` 204 → 25 ms,
`answerInline` 186 → 17 ms on the main thread. The simulator's frame times move by half between runs of the same build
on this Mac, so the profile is what this change is judged by.

Checks: `PerformanceTests.testAWholeAnswerIsParsedOnceHoweverOftenItsRowIsDrawn` (a second drawing parses nothing; the
table's cells are the parse's), `ChatScrollUITests`.
