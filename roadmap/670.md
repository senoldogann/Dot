### 670. Each paired phone holds a key of its own, which Settings › Phone lists and removes

**Status: done (2026-10-26), waiting on the owner's look and the independent security review (guard.py).** From the
outside review of 2026-10-22 (its O2): a phone paired with a code got the installation's one token (`<home>/api-token`).
A phone that was lost could be cut off only by taking it off the tailnet, or by a new token for everything, which signed
every browser and phone out and needed a restart.

**The approach (written before the code).** What must hold:

- **A phone's key is its own and is never the installation's token.** `POST /api/phone/pair` gives a new key,
  `phone.<id>.<secret>` (the id 12 hex characters, the secret 32 random bytes in base64url). The daemon keeps its id, a
  name, when it was paired and last used, and the SHA-256 of the key, in `<home>/phones.json` (readable by the owner
  only, written whole through a temporary file). The key itself is kept nowhere on the Mac, and is never logged.
- **The guard takes the installation's token or the key of a phone that is listed**, compared in constant time
  (`hmac.compare_digest` of the hashes). A phone that is removed is refused at its next request (401), and its streams
  that are open end at once: the guard ends a stream it let in with a phone's key as soon as that phone is removed, the
  way a client that went away ends it.
- **A phone's key cannot open or close a way into Dot.** Pairing codes (`POST /api/phone/pairing`), the list of
  phones (`/api/phone/phones`, `DELETE /api/phone/phones/{id}`), and the iMessage channel's switch, code, confirmation
  and removal (`/api/channels/imessage/{switch,code,confirm,remove}`, item 572) answer the installation's token alone; a
  phone's key gets 403. So a phone that is taken cannot open another way in to stay in after it is removed, nor close
  the owner's. (The second pre-push review found the iMessage numbers.) What a key does besides, while it is good, it
  does as the Mac's own key would; removing the phone does not undo it, and docs/phone.md says what to look over.
- **The daemon still proves itself before a key is sent** (`GET /api/proof`, item 559): for a phone's key the page asks
  with its id (`&phone=<id>`) and both sides key the HMAC with the SHA-256 of the key, which is what the daemon holds. An
  id that is not listed is answered with a proof under a random key, so the answer tells nothing of which ids exist, and
  the page signs in again.
- **Runs can neither read nor write the list**: `phones.json` is among Dot's guarded files (`runtime._guarded_files`,
  beside `api-token`), so a run's commands and file tools are kept from it in every mode but `yolo` (item 531); a row
  written there would otherwise be a key the guard takes at the next start. (The pre-push review found it missing.)

What only asks, or is left as it is:

- A phone paired before this item holds the installation's token, which is not listed; Settings › Phone says so, and
  the way to cut such a phone off stays the one docs/phone.md gives (a new token for everything).
- The installation's token is unchanged: the desktop app, `dotx open` and the signed-in address of `dotx url` use it.
- Twenty phones at most; a pairing past them is refused with what to do (remove one).
- A phone's name is read from the User-Agent of its pairing (iPhone, iPad, an Android phone); the list shows when it was
  paired and last used (written at most once every ten minutes).

**Built.** `server/phones.py` (the list, its file, the keys' hashes, the event a removed phone's streams end on);
`server/guard.py` takes a listed phone's key beside the token, refuses it on the token's own paths
(`needs_main_token`) and ends a phone's request when the phone is removed (its `receive` tells the app the client went
away); `routes_phone.py` gives the key on a good code and lists and removes phones; `GET /api/proof` takes `phone`;
`api.js` proves the daemon with a phone's key's hash; Settings › Phone lists the phones with Remove, and a phone's own
page says that pairing is the Mac's.

**Checked.**
- `tests/unit/test_phones.py`: the hash alone kept, mode 0600, read again; another secret, a key cut short, the token's
  shape refused; removal sets the event and refuses the key; twenty at most; last use written every ten minutes at most;
  a file that cannot be read refused; the names; the token-only paths refuse a phone's key with 403.
- `tests/smoke/test_phone_pairing.py`: a code traded for a key that is not the token; the key reaches `/api/phone` but
  gets 403 on pairing and the list; the list shows `iPhone` without a key; `phones.json` holds the hash at 0600; the
  proof with the phone's id, and another for an id not listed; removed, its open `/api/stream` ends at once, its key gets
  401 and a second removal 404. In Chrome, at 1280 and 390, light and dark: the phone signs in with the code and holds
  a phone key, its Settings › Phone has no Show code, the Mac lists it, Remove takes it off, and the phone is back at
  the sign-in screen.
- The whole gate, the phone and settings browser tests.
