### 819. The trend engine goes on to its target, and is measured against holding

**Status: done (2026-10-29).** **Asked** by the owner after item 818 ("ne gerekiyorsa sen yap, bana bırakma"): what was
left to them was to set the engine's paper result beside holding Bitcoin after some weeks, by hand.

- **What the owner's engine did on its first day.** Its first look (2026-10-29 10:31) bought 100 USDC each of BTC, ETH
  and SOL, the most `max_order` lets an order be, for targets of 25%, 17% and 16%; at the next looks it placed nothing,
  as each share was then within the band of 10 points of its target (BTC 20%, ETH 10%, SOL 10%). The account held 40%
  in the coins for a rule that wants 58%. The backtest of item 818 sets a share to its target in one step once the band
  is crossed, so the engine as it ran was not the rule that was tried.
- `steps_toward` (`dotagent/trading/trend.py`) takes the products on their way to a target (`chasing`) and trades them
  until they are within a point of it (`REACHED`) rather than the band; each step says whether it brings its product
  all the way (`Step.whole`). The engine keeps the products a step did not bring there, capped or refused by a limit,
  in its state, and trades them on at the next look; one a look cannot judge stays on its way, and a look that trades
  nothing (paused, off paper, the market unread) keeps them.
- **The measure.** The engine keeps what the paper account was worth at its first look on it, with the bid of the
  first of `engine_coins` (`trading:engine:start`), and at each look what the account made since beside what holding
  that coin would have made (`EngineState.result`); a paper account started again starts the measure again. Settings ›
  Trading › Trend engine shows it in a line, `trading_engine` says it, and so the daily supervisor reads it. It is the
  whole paper account's, so a run's paper orders count in it.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_trend.py`: a gap within the band is left unless the product is on its way, a step on its
way buys or sells to its target and is whole; the engine with a band of 25 points and 100 an order buys five times to
half the account and stops within a point of it (three times, at 30%, without the chasing); the result is 0 at the
start, Bitcoin's 10% beside the account's half of it less the fee an hour on, the same while Bitcoin's price cannot
be read (the pre-push review: it was lost for that look), and 0 again after the paper account is
started again. `tests/smoke/test_trading_settings_page.py`: the line on the page.
