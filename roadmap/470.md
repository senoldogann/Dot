### 470. A held run's page is used from the screen that shows it

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The seventh of the nine ideas taken from OpenBot (item 449 has the plan): take over Dot's
browser from the phone. Take over brought a tab to the front of the Mac, which a phone cannot use, and was refused for a
browser without a window. This is its server half; the phone's controls are item 475. **As built.**
`browser/remote_input.py`: the events (`move`, `down`, `up`, `wheel`, `key`, `text`) with their bounds (places as
fractions of the picture, a wheel within 4,000 px, a key as Playwright names it, at most 500 characters of text, 64
events a batch), the point a fraction names in the page's CSS pixels (`viewport_point`, from the size the page measures
at any zoom or with its window filled), Playwright's mouse and keyboard doing them, and a tally of counts only.
`BrowserSession.send_input` starts no Chrome, waits up to two seconds for a step under way on the thread's lock, then
checks the run is still held (the critique's race: input sent before the owner handed the run back must not land after),
and turns any failure of the page into a `BrowserError` with the address and the kinds of the events, not chained, so no
error or log carries what was typed. `RunManager.take_over_on_screen` holds the run as `take_over` does but brings
nothing up, so it works with `[browser] headless`; its `takeover` event says `by: "screen"` and `via_reach_name` (a hint
from the request's Host, never a decision); `send_input` counts what the owner did, a batch the page failed part way
too; `hand_back` lets go of a button left down, then records `inputs` {clicks, scrolls, typed}. A lock of the run's
orders the two: a batch under way ends, counted, before the run is handed back, and none starts after. The screencast
now comes from the browser the run works in (`browser_of`), as Take over and the input do, which a campaign in the
owner's Chrome needed. `[browser] remote_input` (on by default) is switched in Settings › Browser (`POST
/api/browser/remote-input`). Not done: a tab a tap opens is not followed by the picture (the run finds it at its next
look), and no WebSocket (it would mean `server/guard.py`). Yolo is untouched: the hold is the owner's act and the gate's
own. Tests: `tests/smoke/test_takeover_input.py` (a real headless Chrome: taps, a wheel, text and Enter reach the page
at its set size, at 125 % and filling its window; input waits out a step and lands only while held, and the switch
refuses it; a failing page keeps the typed text out of its error and the logs; a run held from the screen in a browser
without a window takes input, refuses it before and after, records counts only, tells the call that waited, and keeps
the typed text out of every event, record and log; a hand-back waits for a batch under way and counts it, which fails
without the lock; 401, 403 and 421 for both routes; a campaign's cast and input in its own Chrome),
`tests/smoke/test_browser_boundary.py` (a page cannot post input either). Docs: `docs/daemon.md`, `docs/safety.md`,
`docs/browser-and-computer-use.md`, `defaults.toml`.
