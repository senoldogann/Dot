### 477. The composer's menus wear the marks of the Customize page

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The owner's word of 2026-10-21: the servers and the skills the composer lists after `/`
and `@` should wear the mark their Customize page wears. Item 447 gave a server of the at menu its service's logo, but
a service whose tile wears an icon or letters (DeepWiki, Context7, Jina) was drawn with the plug, the tools of the slash
menu wore icons of their own, and a skill its own icon where its Customize card wears the download arrow (imported) or
the brain (learned). **As built.** One rule for a server's mark: `mark_of_service` (`connections/directory.py`) gives the
listing's `ServiceMark` (logo, glyph, monogram, colour), which `GET /api/palette` and `GET /api/mcp/servers` carry as
`mark` (the latter's `logo` is gone); one drawing of it: `marks.js` (`markContent`, `markInk`), which the Plugins page's
tiles, a hand-added server's tile and the composer's menus all use. In the menus a server and each of its tools wear it
on the tile's light ground (white in both schemes, as on the page). A palette skill says whether it is `imported`, Dot's
copy of another agent's skill (`copied_skill_names`, its folder's marker), and `skillMark` gives the mark its Customize
card wears, for the card and for the menu alike. Tests: `tests/unit/test_palette.py` (each sort of mark reaches the
palette, a server of no service has none; only a marked copy of Dot's folder is imported) and
`tests/smoke/test_composer_picks.py` (the daemon's palette and servers list say it; the at and slash menus draw the
marks the Customize page's tiles and cards draw, on a white ground in the dark scheme, wide and light and on a phone and
dark). Docs: daemon.md (the menus, the servers route), extending.md.
