### 1383. A phone's dock is one capsule of glass over the composer, and each segment opens its detail in a sheet

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The owner's word of 2026-11-24, with a screenshot of a phone: end the design where the
dock stacks panes above the composer. On a phone the line that waited on the subagents (item 1331), the bar of the run's
time and helpers (items 297 and 401), the queue (148), the loops (281) and the commands at work (229) lay one upon the
other, tucked under the composer's edge and drawn at different widths (the queue 8 px from the screen's edge, the loops
and the commands 16): five layers, over a conversation a phone has little room for. The model named is the mobile app
of a developer tool: one capsule of glass floating just above the composer, its segments divided by hairlines, a round
way down beside it, and each segment opening its own sheet; nothing is ever stacked.

What changed, for a window up to 720 px wide (the phone layout; a wider window keeps what it had):

- **One row** (`dockbar.js`, `dockwords.js`): a capsule of glass (`backdrop-filter` with a solid ground where the
  browser draws no blur, the page's own `--glass-*` tokens) whose segments are the run's status with its Stop (**Working
  · 36 s** with its turning mark, **Waiting for you**, **Waiting to start**, **Stopping…**, and **Reconnecting…** once
  the page lost the run's stream, which the note under the top bar said and a phone no longer draws), the plan (**Plan
  2/5**), **2 helpers**, the loops (**every 10 min** for one, **2 loops**), **1 queued** (**1 paused** while a stop
  holds the queue) and **1 running** for the commands at work. A segment is there only while it has something to say;
  with none the capsule is not drawn. Each is a button at least 44 px tall with a focus ring, a name and
  `aria-expanded`; the way down is a round 44 px button beside the capsule, not a layer over it. `dockSegments` (pure,
  `tests/js/dockwords.test.js`) says which segments there are and their words.
- **It fits, and never scrolls.** The row says fewer words until it fits (`fit`: every segment in words; the counts of
  the secondary ones alone; the Stop without its word; the status's time without "Working", and under 360 px a
  narrower segment) and, when even that does not (320 px with everything to say, or the way down beside it), keeps the
  status and its Stop on one line, in words, and the other segments, equal and divided, on a second. The accessible
  names stay the long ones.
- **A sheet for each segment** (`docksheet.js`): one at a time, from the foot of the screen over a dim; it closes by a
  tap outside, Escape (not one that answers a confirm dialog over it), its handle, and a swipe down; the focus goes back
  to the segment. The queue, the loops and the commands at work are not redrawn: the pane itself (`queue.js`,
  `loops.js`, `background.js`) stands in the sheet while it is open and goes back to its place in the dock when it
  closes, so its rows, buttons and calls are the same: the queue's arrows (44 px, never a drag), pencil (which closes
  the sheet, the edit being made in the box), **Steer**, × and **Resume**; the loops with how often and when next,
  wrapped, and **Stop repeating**; the commands with their words wrapped whole, how long they ran and **Stop**, all
  asking first as before. The helpers' sheet says what the task waits on, what each helper does now, and has the
  **Details** and **Stop** of the line it replaces; the plan's (`planContent`, shared with the bubble a computer keeps)
  its steps and the way to the Plan tab. The status opens the panel's Task tab as before. A sheet whose segment goes
  (the last queued message started) closes.
- **What Dot asks is its own card**, directly over the capsule and never in it, as `asksdock.js` draws it; the capsule
  goes on saying the run waits for you, and the loops and the commands no longer give way to the card, there being
  nothing for them to lie over. The Continue line of a task that was cut off is a card of its own over the capsule as
  well, a line in the dock's flow (item 120), not one of the panes the capsule replaces.
- The panes the dock stacked (`.wait-line`, `.status-bar`, `.queue`, `.chat-loops`, `.background-work`) and the note of
  a lost stream are not drawn on a phone; the modules tell the capsule what they hold (`onChange` of the queue with its
  held and unread states and a message on its way, of the loops with their rhythm, of the commands at work) and their
  calls, the queue's reorder, the stops and the loops' deletion, are untouched. The fold of the loops
  (`dot.fold.chat-loops`) is kept and read on a computer, and a sheet shows the rows whatever it says (`present`). The
  legacy phone rules of the bar went.

A computer's window keeps the panes: on 1280 px the same pile has equal widths and reads as tabs under the composer's
edge, so it is left alone; the capsule is the one media query away (`@media (max-width: 720px)`) should it be wanted
there too.
