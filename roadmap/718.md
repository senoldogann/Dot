### 718. A proposer that fails in one scope no longer loses the night

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** On 2026-10-24 a step failed with `the review model did not call propose_harness`: the
review model (Claude Haiku 5.5) answered the proposer's question without its tool, on the first try and again when the
review module asked it once more (`_decide_checked`), and the whole night was lost, the other scope never asked.

**What must hold.** A proposer whose answer is no proposal in one scope (`ProviderProtocolError`, after the review
module's own second try) leaves the other scopes of `step_candidates` to be proposed and played; the step is recorded
`failed` with the error (`the proposer failed in the invariant scope: ...`) and adopts nothing, whatever `adopt` says.
Failing in every scope, its reason is `the proposer failed`. A refusal or a provider that cannot be reached fails the
step at once, as before. Asking a third time was not taken: the module asks twice already, with a note that says how.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_harness_step.py`: a proposer that answers in words twice in the first scope leaves the
second scope's candidate probed, the step failed and nothing adopted; failing in both, the step says the proposer
failed.
