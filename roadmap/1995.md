### 1995. A bot's work on the phone: its work orders, objectives and hand-offs to read, and Pause and Resume in its profile's menu

**Status: in progress (2026-11-27).** The owner's rule since 2026-11-26 is that what can be done on the Mac's web can
be done from the phone (PARITY-GAPS, Agents). The profile of a bot (item 1884, 1963) had no way to see what Dot
handed it, what the owner wants moved forward, or who it talks to, and no way to pause it.

What the web does (read before building): the Agents page lists work orders (`GET /api/work-orders`), objectives
(`GET /api/objectives`) and today's messages between agents (`GET /api/team`) and **writes none of them**. Work orders
are handed out and judged by the lead (Dot) in its chats; objectives are kept by Dot; a hand-off is a message with no
accept or decline. The only write is the agent's page's Pause and Resume (`POST /api/agents/{id}/pause|resume`,
item 465). No route is added: a daemon that took new writes for them would let a phone key hand work to agents, which
the web does not. The phone therefore reads the three lists and does the one write.

What must hold: the phone's key is accepted by every route used (checked on a throwaway daemon: the three GETs 200, pause
and resume 200, Dot's pause 422 as the daemon says); `server/guard.py` and the daemon are not touched.

What was built:
- DotEngine: `WorkOrderView`/`WorkOrderRecord`, `ObjectiveRecord`, `TeamMessage`/`TeamView` decoded from the daemon's
  own answers, `botOrders`, `botObjectives` and `botHandoffs` (pure) scoping them to a bot. The web scopes nothing (it lists every order, every active
  objective and every message of the day), so this is the phone's own: Dot has every order, objective and message it
  sent or was sent, an agent the orders given to it, the objectives those serve and the messages to or from it, within
  the newest 60 orders and 60 objectives the daemon sends (`_WORK_ORDERS_LISTED`), so an old item of a bot can be
  missing from its page while other bots' items fill the cap; `DueWords` (the web's `span`/`dueWords` as numbers the app says in the owner's language); `workOrders()`,
  `objectives()`, `team()`, `pauseAgent`, `resumeAgent`.
- DotNext, a bot's profile: a **Work** group (Work orders "1 open", Objectives "1 active", Hand-offs "2 today") under
  Library and Computer; each opens a list (open before finished) and one row opens its page: a work order's state, its
  agent, tries and due time, brief, what done means and last note; an objective's why, how it is judged, next step and
  dated progress; a hand-off's message and reply. Words written after Dot read content from outside are marked as the
  web marks them: on the list row and as a notice on the page of the order or objective. An agent's profile has a ⋯ menu with **Pause bot** / **Resume bot** and a notice while it is paused;
  Dot has no such menu (the daemon refuses to pause Dot).
- `StateRow` (the campaigns' row, now shared) puts the state under the title at the accessibility sizes.

How it was checked: `WorkTests` in DotEngine (real answers of `e2e_daemon.py --seed-work`, the scoping, due words,
pause and resume requests); `BotsLiveUITests` on that throwaway daemon (the three pages with their rows, a work
order's, objective's and hand-off's page, Pause and Resume, the daemon side says Scribe is paused; edges and widths of
the rows measured; Turkish dark and AX3 variants), `check_strings.sh`.
