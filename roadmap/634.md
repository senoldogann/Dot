### 634. The list of your schedules scrolls inside past its third schedule

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner: "on the Schedules page, Your schedules should scroll inside itself after three
schedules." Every schedule stood in the page, so the Morning brief and the Watchers under the list went down as the
schedules grew.

**The change.**
- `views/schedules.js` `cutAfterThird` cuts the list under its third row, plus 32 px of the fourth, which says that
  more lie under them, and the list scrolls inside (`.schedule-list.cut`, `overscroll-behavior: contain` as item 218's
  long lists).
- **The cut is measured where the rows stand.** A goal's lines and a schedule's opened History make rows of differing
  heights, so no fixed height is three rows.
  - It is measured again whenever one of the three rows changes size (the window, History opened).
  - It is never taller than 60% of the window, since a phone's rows are taller than a third of it.
- Three schedules or fewer are not cut.
- **A list painted again keeps where it was scrolled to inside.** A switch, or a run starting or ending, paints it anew;
  without this it would jump back to the top.
- `docs/memory-and-schedules.md` says it.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_run_now.py`, with six schedules, on a wide window (light) and on a phone (dark):
- the list is cut and scrolls;
- on the wide window it shows the three rows and the top of the fourth, not the fifth;
- on the phone it is no taller than 60% of the screen;
- scrolled to its end and switched off there, it stays where it was;
- with three schedules, it is not cut.
