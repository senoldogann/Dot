### 1242. Text a page, a mail or a site wrote is read in time that grows with its length

**Status: done (2026-11-19).** The code review of 2026-11-19 timed every module-level pattern of Dot on long hostile
text: 51 of 868 grew with the square of the text or worse, and a sweep traced which of them a third party's text
reaches. Python's `re` holds the interpreter while it matches, so a slow match stops the whole daemon, its API, every
run and the scheduler, whether it runs on the event loop or in a thread. Fixed here, each measured before and after:

- `web/text.py` and `browser/snapshot.py`: the trailing blanks of each line (`[ \t]+\n`) went back over a run of tabs
  or spaces that no line break ends from each of its places: 40,000 tabs in a `<pre>` took 3.3 s, a megabyte would
  take half an hour (findings WEB-01, X2-02). `core/text.py` `without_trailing_blanks` strips each line instead;
  200,000 tabs take 0.01 s.
- `browser/network.py` `hidden_text`, which masks secrets in a network answer a run reads: a name was looked for from
  every letter of a long word, 16,000 letters took 12 s (B2-06). The name is taken whole from its start; it masks
  the same values as before (20,000 random strings compared), and 200,000 letters take 0.09 s.
- `desktop/codes.py` `_LEADING` (a one-time code in a mail or a message): `\W*` went over the line breaks after a line
  start too, 16,000 of them took 5 s (D2-01). It stays within its line.
- `video/watch.py` `_TAG` (a site's caption file), `tools/recon.py` `_TITLE` and `_GENERATOR` (a page `recon` reads),
  and `channels/text.py` `_LINK` (an answer sent to iMessage): an unclosed `<` or `[` was read to the end from each of
  them (MT-01, WEB-02, X2-05). A tag holds no `<`; a link's words may hold pairs of brackets
  (`[see [1]](…)`, which the pre-push review asked to keep) and no unpaired one.
- `agent/continuity.py` `_heading`, which checks that a compaction's summary has its sections (the pre-push review
  found it, built inside a function where the scan of module patterns did not see it): two runs of `\W*` side by side
  took a time that grew with the cube of a run of blank lines, 1,000 of them 5 s; 200,000 take a moment now.

Left as it is: `channels/text.py` `_CODE_SPAN` on a line of many unpaired backticks (the model's own answer, 16,000
characters in 1.7 s), and the patterns of `policy/` (`elements.py`, `shell.py`), which need the owner's look.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_linear_text.py` (each reader on 200,000 hostile characters under 2 s, and what it read),
the tests of the readers changed (240).
