### 1079. On a phone, Dot works as an iPhone app does

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** Asked by the owner: everything they can do on the desktop (permissions, actions) they
should be able to do on the phone, which should behave and look as an iOS app as far as it can; and, before, that they
could not manage the notification settings on the phone although they had allowed notifications.

An audit of the UI (static) found no screen the phone leaves out: settings, approvals, agents and Connect apps all
draw there. What a finger could not do, or did badly:
- A chat's menu opened by a right-click only, which an iPhone does not send for a long press; the "…" was the one way.
- Menus were small popovers by the pointer, made for a mouse.
- A queued message moved only by a drag (which an iPhone does not do on a row) or the arrow keys.
- A Home Screen app has no Back of the browser and no swipe back of Safari.
- Settings › General › Notifications read what the browser allowed once, when it was drawn: allowed later in the
  iPhone's Settings, the switch stayed off and said to reload the page, which a Home Screen app cannot.

Now:
- On a phone (no hover, at most 700 px wide) a menu is a sheet from the foot of the screen, as an iPhone's action
  sheets are: its name, its choices in groups, Cancel at the foot (`menu.js`, the message sheet's look). Its choices
  are still a menu of menu items to a screen reader, as the pointer's menu is.
- A finger's hold of a chat's row (450 ms, within 10 px) opens that sheet; its click opens no chat, and the row asks
  for no preview of its link.
- A swipe from the screen's left edge (within 24 px, past 64 px, mostly sideways) does what the button at the top
  left does: back, or the list of chats.
- A row of Settings and a chat's row darken while pressed; the browser's grey box over a tap is gone.
- A queued message has a Move up button on a touch screen.
- The Notifications card reads again when the page comes back into view, and on an iPhone's Home Screen app says
  where iOS keeps the switch (Settings › Notifications › Dot).

Not done: the iPhone app (item 997) keeps its one button to the iPhone's Settings; whether that was the owner's
screen is asked.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_chat_menu.py` (the sheet from the "…" and from a hold, the edge swipe and a swipe from
inside that does nothing, on a 390 px touch screen) and `tests/smoke/test_queue.py` (Move up hidden to a mouse, its
press moving a message, disabled on the first).
