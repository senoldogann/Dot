### 479. The end of a hold from the screen waits on nothing

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** A follow-up of item 470 (one class: the owner's input from the screen outliving its
hold). Item 470 let a hand-back wait under a per-run lock for a batch of input under way, and a run that ended held
left a button the input had pressed down for the chat's next run; a first attempt (the held branch of item 476's
number, before item 476 went to the sidebar's faces) bounded each page call under that lock and met a new hang at every
review, since a lock held across the page's calls is where they come from. The owner approved the other way on
2026-10-21: no waits at the end of a hold at all, and the last to finish cleans. **As built.** `ScreenHold`
(`browser/remote_input.py`) is one hold, shared by the run's manager, which starts and ends it, and the browser session,
which does its batches: what the input did (a batch is counted once it may begin, before the page is touched), how many
batches are being done, and whether the hold is over. The manager keeps no lock: `hand_back` and the end of a run mark
the hold over, so that no batch begins after, and count what it did, the batch under way included; what the input left
pressed is let go once (`take_release`): by the last batch under way, under the page's lock before releasing it, so
that the run's next step finds no button down, or else at once, in a task of its own whose first step takes the page's
lock, ahead of the run's next step, which the hand-back lets go on after it. The page is given 3 s to measure itself
and take a batch and 1 s to let go, and a page that does not answer fails its batch (`TimeoutError`) or is logged
(`screen_input_release_failed`), holding nothing up; the daemon's shutdown waits for the releases, which are bounded.
A run held on the Mac (Take over) has such a hold too, since a screen may use its page (item 475). `RunGate.waiting`
counts the tool calls waiting at the gate, which the test of a held run waits for instead of a count of results. Yolo is
untouched: this is the owner's hold, in every mode. Tests: `tests/smoke/test_takeover_input.py` (a batch under way when
its hold ends is done, counted, and lets go of its press before a step waiting for the page reads it; a page that does
not take a batch or does not measure itself fails it after a moment and is free after; Return control during a batch
returns at once, refuses the next batch, counts the one under way and its press is let go; a run that ends held ends at
once and its press is let go; the held run's test waits at the gate). Docs: daemon.md.
