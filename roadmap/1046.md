### 1046. A campaign's end holds

**Status: done (2026-11-08).** Part of the owner's ask to make the implicit state machines explicit (item 1045).

What must hold: an achieved campaign keeps its outcome, and an ended campaign gets no next session. Every write of
`campaigns.status` and `next_session_at` was `WHERE id = ?` after a read across awaits:

- `set_campaign_status("stopped")`, by the owner's Stop or by `_follow_session`'s rules (failed in a row, the session
  limit, an emergency stop), after a session's `campaign_achieved`, turned the achieved campaign stopped and wiped its
  evidence.
- `set_next_session` by `_follow_session`, `start_session` or the `campaign_schedule` tool after a Stop or a Mark
  achieved put a pending session on an ended campaign (the tool said it was planned).

The store's writes now name the status they change from (`_update_campaign_in`) and say whether they did:
`stop_active_campaign`, `reactivate_campaign`, `set_next_session` and `plan_campaign_sessions` change an active (or,
to go on, an ended) campaign only. `set_campaign_status`, which switched by a parameter, is gone.
