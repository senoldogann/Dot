### 1880. DotNext puts the run's work in one pill: plain words, Stop, the plan and the helpers; the stream keeps no plan or helper card and folds its thinking

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** The owner, on build 3 on his iPhone, with another coding app's own screens as the
reference: a step's "Thinking" block collapsed by default, also while it streams; the plan card ("Plan · 1 of 5 done")
out of the stream; the "3 helpers · 1 working" card out of the stream; the capsule never showing a raw tool name like
"message_agent"; an Agents sheet like the reference's; and "we should fit everything here": one pill at the bottom for
everything about the running work, thin dividers between its segments.

What changed (the app only):
- `ThoughtRow` is one line in every state: "Thinking" with its shimmer while the step's thinking streams (no live
  preview of its words under it), its first sentence once it is stored; a tap opens the whole text in a box that
  scrolls in itself (item 1769's).
- The stream draws neither the plan nor the helper group (`streamItems`, a folded run's included).
- The working capsule is one pill: what the run does and its clock | Stop | Plan 1/5 | queued | helpers done/total
  (`person.2` with "1/2"). Each segment is a 44 pt button: the status opens the run inspector, Stop stops the run (asking
  first when Settings › Confirmations says so), the plan opens its checklist in the inspector sheet (medium and large
  detents, ticking as the steps are done), the people segment opens the Agents sheet. It stays one line at the
  default text size: in words where they fit (`ViewThatFits`), else Stop, the plan and the queue as their glyphs with
  the numbers and "Working" as its spinner before the clock, which fits the smallest iPhone with every segment shown.
  At the accessibility sizes the segments stack in a rounded card, as glyphs with the numbers.
- The status follows the main run's own state, as the reference's does: "Working 1m 50s" (the turn's clock) while the run takes a
  step of its own, "Waiting on 2 helpers" (those still working, no clock) while its step only waits for or talks to its
  helpers, and "Working" again once they finished and it goes on; never a tool's raw name. (Item 1756's "Thinking",
  "Writing" and the command's line in the capsule are gone: the stream says those.)
- The plan and people segments belong to the chat's newest run: its plan, and the helpers it started (done/total, never
  an older run's). Both stay once the run ended, in their finished state, until a newer run starts: the last plan and
  helpers are one tap away where they were while the run worked, without scrolling back to their step; a newer run
  without a plan or helpers shows neither. In Zen, which filters the plan out of its transcript, the pill reads it from a transcript of
  every row, so the plan is still one tap away. (The web's dock shows helpers only while a run works; on the phone the
  stream no longer has the helper card that kept them reachable, so the pill keeps them.)
- The Agents sheet's rows (status dot, title, coloured state, clock, model line, Stop for a working one) add what a
  working helper does now in plain words (`stepWords`: "Read notes.md", "Running npm test", "Waiting for a helper",
  "Working" for any other tool), and a tap opens the helper's own chat inside the sheet. No branch
  names: Dot is not a coding harness.
- A helper's own chat shows its work: the chat's runs are those no run of the chat started (`ownRuns`, pure, in
  DotEngine), where the screen and the transcript had skipped every run with a parent, so a helper's chat was empty.
  The fixture `n2-chat` has the first helper's chat (`thr_h1`), and the UI test taps its row and finds its words.

How it was checked: `ChatUITests` on the `n2-chat` fixture asserts that the plan segment says "Plan, 1 of 3 steps
done", that no plan or helper card is in the stream, that the status, Stop, plan and helpers segments stand on one line
in that order, at least 44 pt tall, centred on the same y within 1 pt and inside the screen, that a tap opens "Plan · 1
of 3 done" with its steps, and that the people segment says "Helpers, 1 of 2 done" and opens the Agents sheet, whose
working row says what it does and never the tool's name. Its first local run, of the pill in words only, measured it
too wide: 12 pt past the edge of an iPhone 17 (402 pt) and 26 pt past an iPhone SE (375 pt), with the fixture's clock
at 41m and two messages queued; that is why the pill falls back to glyphs. The fallback was not measured locally (the
Xcode queue of the Mac was over an hour); GitHub's `ios` job runs `ChatUITests` on the pull request. `swift test`: 104
tests, one timing test failed once under a load average of 48 and passed alone; this change does not touch the engine.
