### 688. A part of the scheduler's tick that keeps failing no longer ends the daemon

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** The whole-project review found that `_tick_forever` ended the loop, and with it the
daemon (`server/daemon.py`), after `FAILED_TICKS_TO_END` (20) failed ticks whichever part of the tick had failed. A
record of the reflection or of the harness's step that could not be read (`last_reflection` raises `StoreError` for it)
made every tick fail, so the daemon restarted about every five minutes and every long run was marked interrupted, for
something that has nothing to do with firing schedules.

**The change.** `Scheduler.tick` keeps the parts that fire schedules (`_fire_due`) and start a campaign's sessions
(`_start_due_sessions`) apart from the others. The failures of the first two are raised together as before and count
towards the end of the loop; a failure of the reflection, the harness's step or the check of how learning fares is
logged at every tick as `scheduler_auxiliary_part_failed` with the part's name and the traceback, and never raises.
`docs/memory-and-schedules.md` says which parts count.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_scheduler.py`: with a reflection record that cannot be read, the loop still runs
after more than 20 ticks and each tick logged the part by name; the existing tests of a tick that fails once and of a
scheduler whose scheduling keeps failing (it still ends after 20 ticks) pass unchanged.
