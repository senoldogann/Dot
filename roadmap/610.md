### 610. A team is a team: made as one, shown as one, and a chat with it works as one

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The owner, on the Agents page: "When I add a team with + Use team, why is it not added as
a team, but its agents one by one?", "The Your agents area could have another design: now it keeps growing downward" and
"When we add a team and start a chat with that team, shouldn't it keep working as a team?" Item 504 made a team a
gallery of templates: Use team made the agents the owner lacked and nothing recorded the team, so to work with one the
owner asked Dot, which handed each agent its part with `message_agent`.

The approach. What must hold: a team's runs are Dot's runs, with Dot's tools, approvals and policy, and the team's
context adds no permission and no tool (nothing under `policy/` or `agent/approvals.py` changes, and no path under
"Always needs a human" in REVIEW.md); the context is the owner's own configuration, made from the stored team as each
run starts and never from what a run learned, so a member taken out or deleted is not named in the next run; a team
holds nothing but its record, so removing it keeps the agents and the chats. The members' own bounds apply to their own
runs, through `message_agent`, as before. What only asks: nothing; a run may still message any agent, as item 87 says.

**As built.**
- **The team is a record.** A `teams` table (`store/db.py`, `TeamRecord` in `store/models.py`): its id
  (`team_<time>_<hex>`, never an agent's `agt_` nor a campaign's `cmp_`), name, purpose, the ids of its agents in order
  (JSON), when it was made, and `ready_team`, the ready team (item 504) it came from, `NULL` for one the owner put
  together: a second Use finds the record by it, whatever the owner has named the team since. `create_team`,
  `update_team`, `get_team`, `find_team`, `list_teams`, `find_ready_team` and `delete_team`; the agents must be agents
  the owner has (Dot is none), each once, checked inside the write that stores them. `delete_agent` takes the agent out
  of its teams in the same write; the team stays with the agents it has left.
- **A chat with a team is a thread that names it.** `threads.team_id` (an added column, `NULL` for every chat there
  was). `POST /api/runs` with `team_id` (a new chat only: not with `thread_id`, `agent_id` or `standing`, 422) starts a
  run of Dot's (`RunManager.start_for_team`) in a thread made with the team; a team with no agent left refuses it (422),
  an unknown team is a 404. The run's `agent_id` stays `NULL`. Every later run of the thread, whoever starts it (the
  chat's box, the queue, a resume, a loop's firing, a reaction's follow-up), is told the team, since the loop reads the
  thread's team as each run starts (`_team_brief` in `agent/loop.py`): the chat's team is not copied into its runs. Only
  a run of Dot's leads it: an agent's run in the chat (`agent_id`) and a delegated run do not.
- **What the run is told.** The prompt opens with the team (`_lead_section`, `agent/prompt.py`, where an agent's own
  voice stands for an agent's run): that it leads the team "<name>", the owner's agents working together, and every
  message of the chat is for the team; what the owner made it for, which it follows within the rules below as it follows
  an agent's instructions; the agents by name and role, one line each; and to hand each the part of the work its role
  fits with `message_agent` (several at once work in parallel), put their answers together and answer itself only what
  no agent's role covers. A team with no agent says there is nobody to hand a part to; a run without `message_agent` is
  told to do the work itself. A name or role is put on one line, and an agent that a run of Dot's made (item 307) is
  marked "made by Dot": its role was written by a model, which is the one thing in the text the owner did not write.
- **The summary of a chat and a run name the team.** `ThreadSummary.actor_id` is the latest run's agent, else its
  campaign, else the team (`COALESCE`), and `ThreadSummary.team` and `RunDetail.team` carry its id, name and agents
  (`ChatTeam`); `delete_team` unlinks its chats (they stay, as Dot's) and `GET /api/agents` gives each agent the title
  of its newest chat (`latest_title`).
- **Routes** (`server/routes_teams.py`): `GET /api/teams` (each with its agents as they are and its newest chat), `GET
  /api/teams/{id}` (with its chats), `POST /api/teams`, `PUT /api/teams/{id}`, `DELETE /api/teams/{id}`; a team is made
  and changed with at least one agent and ends with none only by the deletion of its agents.
- **Use team** makes the agents as before, then the record, or finds the team of that ready team and puts back, at its
  end, the members it lacks (one the owner took out, or deleted, and the route made again); the name and purpose the
  owner gave stay. `TeamUse` also says the `team`, whether it was `created` and the names `added`. The toast says it
  ("Content team is under Your teams.", "Research Desk is back in the Content team."), the gallery card says "In your
  teams", and the list scrolls to the new card. Item 504's checks (every agent checked before any is made) stand.
- **The Agents page.** **Your teams** above **Your agents**, which is a grid of compact cards (`.agent-grid`, two to
  four across by the page's width, one on a phone): the face, the name over the role on one line, and the title of the
  newest chat with its age. A team's card: its agents' faces over one another (four, the rest counted, `teamFaces` in
  `actors.js`), name, purpose, how many agents and its newest chat; a click opens `#/teams/<id>`. **New team** puts
  agents of the owner's together (`teamform.js`, the same form that **Edit** opens). The ready teams and the templates
  stay below. The button and page of today's messages between agents (`#/team`), called **Team** until now, are
  **Handoffs** (the review of item 610: "Team" beside **New team** and **Your teams** named two things).
- **A team's page** (`views/teams.js`): its faces, name and purpose, its agents as links, a box ("Message Content team",
  the one an agent's page has, from `startComposer`, which starts on Auto with Dot's own bounds) and its chats. With no
  agent left it says so and has no box. A removed team's page says it is gone. **Edit** and **Remove** are in the bar.
- **The chat shows the team.** The sidebar's row wears the faces of two of the team's agents (`teamDuo`, one up and left
  of the other in the square of a creature) and puts the team's name ahead of its last words; the bar of the chat, and a
  phone's line under the chat's title, name the team; the Details name it. The sidebar is told when a page changes a
  team or an agent (`TEAMS_CHANGED_EVENT`, as it is told of a schedule's change), since the daemon sends no event for
  it.
- `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` (the page, Use team, Your teams, the chat with a team, what its runs are told, the
  API), `docs/daemon.md` and `docs/phone.md` say it. `roadmap/504.md` stays as written.

**Decisions to look at.**
- The chat belongs to the team through its thread, not through each run, so that no path that starts a run in the chat
  (its box, the queue, a resume, a loop's firing, a reaction's follow-up, the note of a command that ended) has to
  remember it, and a change of the team applies to the next run at once. The price: a run says nothing of the team in
  `runs`; only its thread does.
- `ready_team` is a column the brief did not name: without it a second Use finds the team by its name, which the owner
  may have changed, and makes a second record.
- A team that lost its last agent stays, says so and takes no new chat; a chat already begun with it goes on, told that
  it has no agent, as a run of Dot's. It is not deleted behind the owner's back.
- No restriction on `message_agent`: a team's run may message an agent outside the team, as any run may.
- The face in a row is two agents' faces and the card's and the page's a stack, not one face of the team's own: a team
  with no look of its own to choose, and the faces say who is in it. A team with no agent wears a face from its id.
