### 990. A photo goes smaller, and a message sent while its files upload goes once they are there

**Status: done (2026-11-05).** **Found** by the owner on 2026-11-05, on a phone: the photos attached in the composer
uploaded slowly, and each press of Send meanwhile showed "Wait until the files are uploaded" once more (three on the
screenshot). The page sent each file whole, as base64 in JSON (a third larger): the owner's photos and screenshots of
1 to 2.4 MB went at 1.3 to 3.2 MB each over a mobile network, and Send refused to go until they were all there.

**Built.** `shrink.js`: a JPEG, PNG or WebP past 1 MB whose longer side is past 2048 pixels goes as a JPEG of 2048 on
that side (quality 0.88, clear parts white), when that is smaller; a model reads a picture at about that size at most.
HEIC (which Chrome on a Mac cannot read) and GIF go as they are. `attachments.js`: each upload is kept as a promise
(`settled()`), and `filesUploaded` waits for them at the top of each send (`run.js`, `runs.js`, `agents.js`), before
the box is read: while files upload it says once "Goes once the files are uploaded", waits for them and those added
meanwhile, and the message goes with the words and files of the box by then; a press while one waits sends nothing
more, and nothing goes when an edit of a queued message was opened or closed meanwhile, since the box then holds
something else. A phone's photo keeps its turn (`imageOrientation`). A picture within the edge goes as it is, whatever its weight, so a screenshot's text stays sharp (the pre-push
review). Docs: `docs/daemon.md`.

**Checked.** `tests/js/shrink.test.js` (the size and the name), `tests/smoke/test_composer.py` (a 3000 × 1800 picture
of several megabytes goes as a 2048 × 1229 JPEG, smaller; two presses of Enter while it uploads give one toast and one
run with the file and the words typed while it waited), `tests/smoke/test_queue.py` (an edit saved while a file uploads goes once it is there, with no other
press; one cancelled while it waits sends nothing, neither the edit nor the draft).
