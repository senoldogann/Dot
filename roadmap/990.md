### 990. A photo goes smaller, and a toast is not shown twice at a time

**Status: done (2026-11-05).** **Found** by the owner on 2026-11-05, on a phone: the photos attached in the composer
uploaded slowly, and each press of Send meanwhile showed "Wait until the files are uploaded" once more (three on the
screenshot). The page sent each file whole, as base64 in JSON (a third larger): the owner's photos and screenshots of
1 to 2.4 MB went at 1.3 to 3.2 MB each over a mobile network.

**Built.** `shrink.js`: a JPEG, PNG or WebP past 1 MB whose longer side is past 2048 pixels goes as a JPEG of 2048 on
that side (quality 0.88, clear parts white, the photo's EXIF turn kept), when that is smaller; a model reads a picture
at about that size at most. A picture within the edge goes as it is, whatever its weight, so a screenshot's text stays
sharp; HEIC (which Chrome on a Mac cannot read) and GIF go as they are; one the browser cannot read fails on its chip.
`ui.js`: a plain toast (no button, no details) whose kind and words are on the screen already is not shown again; the
one there stays for its whole time again. Docs: `docs/daemon.md`.

**Not built.** Sending the message by itself once its files are there: the pre-push review found, round after round,
that a send that waits races everything the box can do meanwhile (words typed, files added or removed, an edit of a
queued message opened or closed); after three rounds the structural option was to keep Send refused while files upload,
as before, with its toast shown once.

**Checked.** `tests/js/shrink.test.js` (the size and the name), `tests/smoke/test_composer.py` (a 3000 × 1800 picture
of several megabytes goes as a 2048 × 1229 JPEG, smaller; three presses of Enter while it uploads show one toast; once
it is there the message goes with it).
