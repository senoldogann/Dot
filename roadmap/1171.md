### 1171. A window turned or narrowed lays out the earlier turns again at its new width

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** **Found** by a test before the change: a skipped earlier turn (item 1165) keeps the
height it was last laid out at, which is of the width it then had. A phone turned from upright (390 px) to on its side
(844 px) left the far turns as tall as upright until each was seen: the conversation was 27,939 px tall where its turns
laid out were 25,795 px, and each message stood off its place until the reader came to it. A window narrowed from 1280
to 800 px changed no turn's width, and nothing.

**Built.** `skipWhenSized` (`history.js`) watches the width of the earlier turns: at a new width it lets every turn be
laid out again, in the next frame (not in the `ResizeObserver` callback, whose change of sizes the browser reports as
a loop, a page error), and skips the far ones again two frames later, once their new heights are kept.

**What it does not do.** A turn is laid out again once a width changes, not as it changes: a window dragged to a new
width lays out all of them at each width the drag passes.

**Also, a test of `main`.** `tests/smoke/test_chat_switch_scroll.py` failed on `main` since item 1131: the words a run
writes on its way to its calls fold into its steps, so the chat at work it opens was no taller than the window and
had no foot to stand at (CI's last whole run of the browser tests was before item 1131). The test now shows the steps
open (`dot.work`, item 830), as the other tests of a chat's work do, and the chat at work is tall again; what it checks
is unchanged.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_history_skips_offscreen.py`, in Chromium and WebKit: a phone turned on its side and a
wide window narrowed keep the conversation exactly as tall, and each turn where it is, as with every turn laid out, and
the mark of the oldest message goes to it alike. The phone failed before the change; the page's errors are none.
`tests/smoke/test_chat_switch_scroll.py` passes (it failed on `main`).
