### 840. A line of a review model's answer past its bound is cut there, not refused, so the night's step does not fail

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner ("ekteki bu problemi gördüm. Sorun ne ise araştır çöz"), with a
screenshot of the self-improvement page: the step of 30 Oct at 04:00 *Failed*, "the proposer failed in the domain
scope: the arguments of propose_harness are not a valid verdict (summary: String should have at most 120 characters)".

The review model (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash) wrote the candidate's one-line summary past its 120 characters, was asked once
more with the problem, wrote it past again, and the step failed: a candidate it had written whole was lost for a line
shown on the page. `dotx.log` holds the same class the same week: a learned skill's description past its 200
characters, twice in a row, and the skill was not written.

- `agent/review.py`: the bounded texts of the verdicts (`Text120`, `Text200`, `Text300`: a reason, a summary, an edit,
  a cue, a lesson, a moment, a piece of evidence, an owner line, a skill's description) may be cut at their bound with
  an ellipsis. A verdict is read plainly first, so a text past its bound is refused as before; when that is its only
  fault (`string_too_long` alone) and each such text, whole, passes the strictest gate of what is learned
  (`learned_text.gate` with links and account names, against the values Dot holds, `known_secrets(filled=False)` as
  the refusing gates read them), it is read again with the texts cut (`_cuttable`, the validation context `_CLIP`).
  The cut is a prefix of a text that passed, so it holds nothing the gates after it refuse; a text that fails is left
  whole and the verdict is asked again (the pre-push review: the first push cut first, which could keep the start of a
  secret, a path or an address that straddled the bound where no gate then knew it). The tool's schema still tells the
  model each bound. A skill's kind (its name, 60 characters) is never cut: cut, it would name another kind of work.
  The habits' and the tool guidance's bounds (item 389) are rules of the harness and are refused as before.
- `docs/harness.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_review_lenient.py`: through the reply as the review reads it, a summary past its 120
characters is kept as its first 119 and an ellipsis and a skill's description past its 200 as 200; read plainly the
type still refuses them, and the schema still says 120. A summary past its bound whose tail is a key of a known form,
a path in a home folder or an e-mail address is not cut but refused, and so is one with a value Dot holds, and a
verdict wrong in another way too; a skill's kind past its 60 characters is refused as a protocol error, which is asked
again (the independent security review found no vulnerability, and that a kind past its bound came out as a bare error
of the type, which the retry let by).
