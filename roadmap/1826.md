### 1826. DotNext's Activity tab, native: while you were away, Ready drafts, Dot's proposals, what is in motion, and the ways Dot comes to you

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** Package W2 of the iPhone rewrite's P1 audit (`ios-rewrite-2026-11-24/P1-gaps.md` §8),
under the phone scope of PLAN §26 (the owner, 2026-11-25: the phone shows what the owner needs away from the Mac to
direct, watch, answer or stop Dot's work; setup is the Mac's). Before, the tab was a "Watch the Mac" web row and an
empty state (`ios/Next/App/Tabs.swift`): nothing Dot did on its own reached the phone's Activity.

What must hold:
- Real daemon routes only, the web's words and rules (`away.js`, `awaywords.js`, `briefcard.js`, `checkincard.js`,
  `initiativecard.js`, `watcherspanel.js`, `views/harness.js`); the one route missing, the drafts that wait, is item 1825.
- The first visit from any device marks "while you were away" and shows nothing; Mark as read posts the card's `until`.
- A draft never goes out without the owner's Send, which asks first; a 409 (answered elsewhere) drops it from the list.
- A proposal from content others can write asks before Use; one the checks refuse offers no Use.
- Watchers: read and switched on or off only, the rest of their choices sent as they are; brief and check-in: their
  switches with their time and days as the Mac set them; no configuration screen (§26).
- A part that fails leaves the others on screen, with one notice (unreachable with Diagnose, key refused with Pair
  again, too old, unreadable); a switch is put back when its write fails; a reload that fails keeps what was shown.
- An ask is answered in its chat, with its whole card (the dock), not from a one-line summary in the list.

ActivityWords), `App/AppModel+Activity.swift`, the engine's `Contract/ActivityModels.swift` and
