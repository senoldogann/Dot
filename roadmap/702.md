### 702. A run that acted on the desktop gives the front back

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** A run that clicked or typed in an application left it in front when it ended: the
owner found their own window behind it.

**The change.** `DesktopSession` notes, by chat, the application in front before its run's first act or open, and the
one each act left in front. The end of the run (`RunManager._drive`) calls `end_of_run`: the first comes forward again
when the second is still in front. Nothing moves when the owner went elsewhere, when the application quit, while
another run has acts under way on the desktop, or while a batch waits for the owner. `[desktop] give_front_back` (on).

**Checked.** `test_desktop_front.py` (a stand-in Mac): the front comes back once, also after two acts; not after the
owner moved, nor once the app quit, nor with the setting off, nor from under another run.
