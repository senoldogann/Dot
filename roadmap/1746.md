### 1746. The run's account: what it did by its record, on the run page and in its detail

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The community's first complaint ("finish what it starts", "says it did it, didn't")
asks for an end report that matches what happened. The run's recap (item 536) is written by a model from the record,
and the Changes tab's line (item 1404) counts steps outside the undo journal with no word of what they came to.

**Built** (`agent/account.py`, `server/routes_runs.py`, `server/views.py`, `static/js/runchanges.js`,
`static/css/app.css`):

- `run_account`: from the run's `tool_result` events (read without their text) and its newest plan, with no model:
  its calls and those that failed; the files its file tools changed and the commands it ran; what it sent for the
  owner or in public (the `message_send`, `public_post`, `external_send` and `release` flags); its act steps summed
  (item 1744's tally); its plan steps done, those done with no tool result that shows them (item 1745) and those left
  open.
- `GET /api/runs/{id}` (the phone's run detail) and `GET /api/runs/{id}/actions` carry it as `account`.
- The Changes tab's card says it under the line of item 1404: how many steps were seen to hold and how many were not,
  what was sent, the steps marked done that nothing shows and those left open. Nothing to say, nothing shown.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_run_outside_journal.py` (the account through the API: a file written, commands run,
none sent; the run detail carries it), `tests/unit/test_plan_proof.py` (the account of a plan with a step not shown),
`tests/js/runchanges.test.js` (the card's words).

**Not done.** The iPhone app's own view of it: the detail carries it, no screen shows it yet. The answer itself is left
as the model wrote it, as every channel sends it.
