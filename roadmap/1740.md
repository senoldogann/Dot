### 1740. `search_chats`: a run finds what was said in any chat, by its words

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The market research of 2026-11-24 (M2, idea 1; Hermes Agent's session search, Letta Code's
`/search`) found that Dot's runs have no way to search what was said in a past chat: `recall` reads back only the records a
compaction named inside one run, `chats` finds a chat by the words of its title, and the notes (the only cross-run memory)
hold what a run chose to save, which 96 % of lessons never reached. On the owner's store: 208 chats, 16,140 messages and
27,286 more kept aside for rewinding, 530 runs; none of it findable by a word.

What must hold:

- (a) A run finds a thing said in any chat by words in it (Turkish case, accents and the dotless i folded as the notes'
  index does), with no model call, in three modes after Hermes: `find` (hits with chat, when, whose words, a snippet),
  `around` (the messages next to a hit), `read` (one run: goal, end, answer).
- (b) The index is never stale and no writer has to remember it: a message appended, a history replaced, a chat rewound or
  deleted, a run ending with its answer, all leave `find` right.
- (c) Taint: what a chat said is fenced and marks the reader untrusted when a run of the chat ever read content third
  parties can author (`chat_ever_tainted`, the rule of `read_chat`, item 974), a watcher's chat always; `read` of a run uses
  `run_ever_tainted`. A closing tag in the text cannot end the fence; the secrets Dot holds are masked.
- (d) Answers are bounded (8 hits, 2 messages each side of a hit, 7,000 characters at most).
- What only asks: nothing. A read-only tool of risk `safe`; a yolo or free run asks nothing new and is denied nothing.

Measured before: nothing on the store finds a chat by what it says (`chats` takes title words only).

What changed (`store/db.py`, `store/models.py`, `tools/search_chats.py`, `runtime.py`, `agent/loop.py`, `tools/groups.py`):

- Schema version 4 (the convention for a new table, item 1268; the database is copied first): `chat_index` (one row per
  indexed text: a user's or an assistant's message of a chat's history, or a run's answer, with an integer id) and `chat_fts`
  (FTS5, `unicode61 remove_diacritics 2`, the Turkish `ı` written as `i`), kept by five triggers on `messages` and `runs`,
  so that the eight places that write or delete messages (append, replace, rewind, delete a chat) need no change. The step
  writes what the store holds into the index in its own transaction. A tool's output is not indexed (a page's or a file's
  text); the `message_archive`, which keeps replaced history for rewinds and repeats the same early messages version after
  version, is not either (the answers of runs are, so that what a compaction replaced is still found by what it came to).
- `search_chats` in the `office` group and in every agent's own tools beside `chats` and `read_chat`.
- Context budget raised by 200 (total) and 150 (tools): one tool, five short fields.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_search_chats.py` (find, Turkish folding, a tool's output not found, fenced + untrusted on a
tainted chat and not on a clean one, a closing tag cannot end the fence, around and read, a secret masked, bounded
answers, the index follows append/replace/delete; the two taint tests fail with the check taken out),
`tests/unit/test_store_schema.py` (a version 3 database is brought to 4 after a copy and its chats are found; the pinned
digest), `test_freedom_contract.py` (a row for the tool), `test_known_secrets_callers.py`, `test_tool_groups.py`,
`test_agents.py`, `test_context_budget.py`; `dotx eval check`.

Not done: the archive's older versions are not searched; no ranking by recency (BM25 only); no filter by chat or date in
`find` (the answer names the chat, `around` and `read` follow it).
