### 1164. An expectation that cannot tell what a step did costs no round trip of the model

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** **Measured** after items 1145 to 1151 and 1158: the speed set took 7.2 model calls a task where
it had taken 6.0 before item 1145, and the late form of the state set a call more on both of the owner's models. The
traces say where the call went. A model that names what a step expects gets it wrong in ways item 1145 refused
whole, and each refusal was a round trip of the model:

- words the page shows already (`received`, `application`, on a page with a link of that name): six of some 45
  traced attempts, two of four with `glm-5.3-flash`;
- words within the text the batch writes (`lamp` after typing `lamps`), `expect_text` given to the call instead of
  the step, and a guess in the model's own words given to a step: once each, two of them in three attempts at the
  shop search. The second of these is item 1161's, found in its own traces the same morning and built there
  (`expectation_on_last_step`); this item is built on it and does not repeat it.

Every time, the model sent the same step again with nothing to expect. The refusal bought no better expectation:
only the round trip.

**Built.** The step goes, and says what was not checked (`browser/session.py`, `core/outcomes.py`,
`tools/browser.py`):

- Words or an address the page shows before the step, and words the batch itself writes, are not looked for
  afterwards (`_sights_of`): seen, they would tell nothing. The step is sent once, carries no check from them, and
  its line says `what it expected was not looked for (…)` with which of the three it was. Nothing calls it verified.
- Each thing a step names is judged by itself: what can tell is still looked for beside what cannot (the
  pre-push review: an address held already took words only the result shows with it).
- A batch with steps after a step of which nothing could be looked for stops there (`gate_lost`): they were to run
  once that sight had shown.
- A guess in the model's own words given to a step (`expect`) is the call's, after the call's own and none twice
  (`guesses_to_the_call`, a pure function of the arguments, read after item 1161's placing of the words a call
  names beside its steps). A step's guess is cut to the 300 characters the call's may have, and a further one is
  added only while the whole still fits: one that does not fit is left out, which the result does not say, since
  a guess is an estimate for the typed judge and checks nothing.
- `browser_act` no longer refuses a call whose step expects words the batch writes: the session says it of the step.
- A page that does not answer the look before the step still refuses the step: that is the page's state.
- The scripted second click (item 781) still leaves such a click alone, as every click that named what it expects.

**This reverses a choice of item 1145.** Its two design readers had both wanted the step sent (one as uncertain, one
with a note); the step was refused instead, "since nothing was sent, so correcting it repeats nothing". Correcting it
repeated a model call. Of the two readings the note was taken: nothing was looked for, so the step is not checked,
which is what its line says, where uncertain would say that a result was looked for and not seen.

**Not changed.** The desktop's `expect_text` is refused as before: every observation above is of the browser, and the
desktop's tests need a Mac nobody is using. A count of how often the words show, which could verify a step whose
words showed once before and twice after, is not built: there is no measurement that asks for it yet.

and every attempt passed. The tree read words given to the call as its last step's by code of this item's own,

The fast model's form costs what it cost before item 1145: nine of ten attempts took three steps, no call was
