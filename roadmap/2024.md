### 2024. The chat header keeps the bot's face and name; only a lasting link problem shows, in the capsule

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner's shot of 2026-11-27: the title pill (Dot's face and "Dot") must not say
"Syncing messages…" or "Reconnecting to <Mac>…" (item 1931 put the link's states in the capsule and the freshness line
under the name).

- A bot's header pill is always its face and name: no line under it. A plain chat's subtitle says the link only for a
  refused key or an old Dot, which no wait mends.
- The capsule says the link only when it is a lasting problem: *Offline* or *Can't reach <the Mac>*, once it lasted five
  seconds (`linkLine`, `linkLineDelay`), gone at once when it mends. A reconnection that mends and a chat being brought
  up to date show nowhere (*Syncing messages…* is gone). Why the capsule and not a dot on the face: a dot on a bot's face
  reads as the bot's presence (as in Messages or Slack), while the problem is the phone's link; the capsule over the
  composer is where the chat's state already lives, beside the send that the link affects, and it holds one quiet word.
- The web already told only a lasting break (after `QUIET_BREAK_MS`, and the grace after the page comes back) and never
  in the chat's title; its words are now the same: *Can't reach Dot* (the bar's pill, the chat's note, the phone's line
  under the bar and the capsule).
