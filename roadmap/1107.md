### 1107. A paper buy is kept with its stop in one write, and an order left pending is settled at the start

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** From the review of the implicit state machines, two gaps of the trading orders and
exits:

- A paper buy with a stop was written `filled`, then its exit in a second commit: a stop of the daemon between the two
  kept a position with no stop under it. Now `Store.settle_trading_buy` writes both in one transaction.
- An order is written `pending` before it reaches the market and settled after; a process that stopped in between
  (a crash, a kill) left it `pending` for ever. It stayed counted against the day, the safe side, but never said what
  it was. Now the daemon's start (`RunManager.recover`, beside the drafts left sending) marks such an order `unknown`
  with a note that it may have reached the market (`unknown_pending_orders`), still counted; the order of a run still at
  work in a command that lives is left to it.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_trading.py` (the order of a gone process is unknown, a working run's stays pending; the
exits' tests), the trading smoke tests.
