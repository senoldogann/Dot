### 665. A schedule a run made is a line of the conversation

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** In the owner's screenshot of another agent app, "Send it. Run this every week." is
followed by "Created routine · Overnight outbound" and "Done.". Dot's `schedule_create` showed only as a step among
the others, which a reader folds away, and which item 663 takes out of the conversation.

**The change.**
- `tools/schedule.py`: `schedule_create` emits `schedule_made` with the schedule's id, name, cron and the daemon's words
  for when it runs.
- `messages.js` `scheduleLine`: a quiet line of the conversation's lane, the calendar, "Made a schedule", the name in
  the text's colour and when it runs in the words the Schedules page uses (`rhythm.js` `loopRhythm`), a link to
  `#/schedules?schedule=ID`; drawn by `timeline.js` where the event comes and by `history.js` in an earlier turn.
- `views/schedules.js`: the row a link names is marked once, in the accent's light that fades, and scrolled into sight.
- `docs/memory-and-schedules.md` says so.

**Checked.**

  outbound · on Mondays at 09:00", in sight with every step shown and with only the status line; the link opens the
  Schedules page with that row marked and in the viewport; the follow-up's page keeps the line on the earlier turn.
