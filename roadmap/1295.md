### 1295. `browser_captcha` never reads a page it could not read as a page that passed, and presses only a pixel that is the check's own

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** Two gaps in `browser_captcha` (item 1288). `find_checks` swallowed a probe that timed out
or a Playwright error with `continue` and returned an empty tuple, which `_cleared()` and the hold loop read as "the
check passed" and "the button went away": a frame that was being torn down, or a page too busy to answer, was a pass.
And the press went to a pixel read a moment earlier, with nothing to say that the widget was still there or that the
element at that pixel was still the widget.

`find_checks` now returns a `Reading` (viewport, checks, `unreadable`). A frame that fails to answer makes the reading
unreadable when it is the main frame, a frame that showed a check earlier in the call or a frame whose address is
hCaptcha's, reCAPTCHA's or Turnstile's own; an unrelated frame (an ad) that hangs does not, so a page with one still
passes as fast as before. `_poll` reads an unreadable page again until the time is up and never takes it for an end; a
call that ends so answers `unfinished` with "could not read the page, look at it" (`HumanCheckOutcome.unreadable`),
before a press as well, where "no check here, do not look for one" would have been false. A hold whose page stops
answering goes on holding rather than ending as "the button went away". The probe reads `textContent` before
`innerText` (a node with more text than a label is never laid out), reads a body of more than 20,000 characters as no
body, and looks at the first thousand and the last two thousand candidate nodes of a page of more than three thousand.

Just before the mouse goes down or clicks, the page is read again (`_confirm_press`): a check that moved is waited for
(up to six more reads of 120 ms) and pressed where it stops, one that never stops is not pressed, one that is gone or
passed is reported as such, and the element under the pixel must be the check's own (`document.elementsFromPoint` in
the frame it lives in, and in every document above it the `<iframe>` itself must be on top). A press that is not made
says what covers it. The guard of item 1252 (`_refuse_a_flagged_press`) is unchanged and weighs the pixel pressed, which
is now the verified one. A page whose check stands still pays one more read (a few milliseconds).

Checked: `tests/unit/test_human_check_press.py` (fifteen tests on a scripted page: an unreadable main frame, widget
frame and ad frame; a press followed by an unreadable page is `unfinished`, and the same page unreadable for one read
passes; a moved check is pressed where it stopped and weighed there; one that never stops, one covered and one gone
are not pressed; a refusal of the guard stops the press; a hold whose page stops answering; the note of an unreadable page after a hold or a press reads as one sentence), and
`tests/smoke/test_browser_captcha.py` on real Chrome (a hold under a cookie wall and a widget under a promo are not
pressed; a hold that slides into place and a hold at the end of a twelve-thousand-row page pass), with the earlier
checkbox, hold, grid and wait tests unchanged.
