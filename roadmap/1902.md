### 1902. `write_file` reads its content by the name `file_text` too

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** **Found** by Dot's own repair of what its record showed (seen 6 times, kind
`tool_error`): on 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24 Claude models (`claude_code:sonnet` four times,
`anthropic:claude-sonnet-5-5` once) sent `write_file` its content as `file_text`, the name Anthropic's text editor tool gives a new file's content.
Each call was refused ("content: Field required; file_text: Extra inputs are not permitted") and sent again.

`WriteFileArgs.content` reads `file_text` too (`AliasChoices`), as `edit_file` reads `old_text`/`new_string` since item
879; the schema the model reads still says `path` and `content`, and a call naming the content twice is refused. One
other refusal in the same record sent `path` and `file_path` together; it is left as it is: a call that names one
argument twice stays refused, as for `edit_file`.

Checked by `tests/unit/test_record_fixes.py::test_a_write_reads_its_content_by_the_name_file_text_too`.
