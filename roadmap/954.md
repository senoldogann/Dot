### 954. An open whose page answers and does not settle shows it as still loading

**Status: done (2026-11-03).** **Found** by Dot's repair night in its own record (problem 88dbcf592efae58a, seen three
times, `tool_error`): `browser_open` failed with "waiting for the page to settle failed". On 2026-10-31
`https://www.linkedin.com/in/senoldogann/` failed after 58 s ("the document was not parsed within 30000 ms"), and the
`browser_snapshot` the run took 7 s later showed the whole profile; `https://x.com/senoldoganx` the same. On 2026-11-01 a
LinkedIn activity page failed with "the tab stopped answering while the page settled", and a script read it 2.7 s
later. The navigation had gone through each time: only the wait for a quiet page ended, and the open threw away a page
that could be read, costing the run a call and a minute. After a step, a script or an upload, a page that does not
settle has been a note since the review of every module; an open was left out. The third sighting (Search Console on
2026-11-02, "the tab did not answer within 30 s") was a page that answered nothing for minutes, which nothing could
have read: it still fails, saying to open the page in a new tab (item 753).

**Built.** `driver.wait_dom_quiet` raises `TabSilentError` (a `BrowserError`, in `core/errors.py`) for a tab whose
page answers nothing within the navigation timeout, and a plain `BrowserError` for one that answers and does not
settle. `BrowserSession._load` (`browser_open`, `browser_new_tab`) settles through `_settle_loaded`: a page that does
not settle is captured as it is, with the note "the page is still loading; what is shown may be incomplete", and
without the second, longer settle a nearly empty first view gets; a silent tab and a forbidden address still fail.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_browser.py::test_an_open_whose_page_answers_and_does_not_settle_shows_it_as_still_loading`
(a real headless Chrome; a page that goes on, once loaded, to one whose end comes after 20 s, with a navigation timeout
of 2 s) fails before the change with the settle error and passes after it, in about 10 s.
`tests/unit/test_browser_stuck_tab.py`: the stuck page raises `TabSilentError`, a page still parsing a `BrowserError`
of another kind.
