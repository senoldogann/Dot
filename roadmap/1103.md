### 1103. A run's status is moved between its working states alone

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** From the review of the app's implicit state machines the owner asked for: a run's
status was a `Literal` of six values with its moves spread over the store, and `Store.set_run_status` took any of them,
so a caller could write `done` with no end time and no `run_finished` event. Its callers pass `running` and `blocked`
only. Now it takes a `WorkingStatus` (`queued`, `running`, `blocked`), which mypy holds every caller to: an end is
written by `end_run` alone, with its time and its event.

Checked: `mypy src`, the unit suite.
