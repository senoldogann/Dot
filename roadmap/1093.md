**Status: done (2026-11-09).** Asked by the owner, with a screenshot of the lock screen: another coding agent app shows one card on glass,
"2 active agents" and a row for each with its status in colour, where Dot showed a black card for each run, which

Now:
- The daemon (`notify/apns_watch.py`) keeps one Live Activity per phone (`DotFleetAttributes`, its token kept under
  `fleet`): a row for each of the owner's runs (title, agent, status, what it does now), the runs at work first, then
  those that ended last, four at most, and how many are at work. The first run starts it by push-to-start; a run that
  starts before the app sent the activity's token opens no second one (60 s); every change goes to its token; an ended
  run stays a row 15 minutes and the activity ends and goes once no row is left. A step still goes at most every four
  seconds, with the priority that spares the battery; a start, a wait and an end at once.
- A token older than the eight hours iOS keeps an activity is dropped and a new activity started; the app forgets the
  token (`DELETE /api/phone/apns/activity?token=`) when the owner swipes the activity away, only while
  it is the token kept (an older activity's end leaves a newer one's), and any when it opens with none on the phone;
  one swiped away is started again with the next run, not before. A start APNs took and whose token does not come is
  not sent again until it comes or no row is left (a warning after 60 s); one it did not take goes again at the next
  change. A daemon that starts again takes the runs still at work as rows of the activity it finds, and ends it when
  none is. An activity of one run, from an app before this, is
  refused (404), and its tokens are forgotten once the phone registers the new one.
- The app (`ios/`): the lock screen's card on glass (a clear tint), Dot's mark, "Dot" and "2 at work" / "All done",
  each row its title, what it does or who worked on it, and Working / Waiting / Done / Failed / Stopped in colour; the
  Dynamic Island shows the count in Dot's yellow, or a check once all are done, and up to three rows expanded.
