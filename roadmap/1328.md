### 1328. The sidebar's head, search row and foot as the reference app's: the agents a menu instead of faces, four small buttons at the foot, no Schedules icon

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The owner's word of 2026-11-22: Dot's design is "completely like a toy"; make it plain and
professional like the reference app on this Mac, on a phone and on a Mac in one design language. Item 1327 made the rows;
this is the rest of the sidebar. The drawer of a phone is this same component: its head, its search row, its rows, its
dock and its foot are the Mac's, a finger's room larger.

- **The head** is the button that folds the sidebar and the title ("Dot", or the name the owner gave it). On a phone the
  title stands alone (the drawer has no X on a touch screen, item 888).
- **The search row** is one field of the ink's tint with no frame, and three small icon buttons at its right: the agents'
  menu, a new agent (item 1259) and a message to Dot, which leads to the home with the cursor in its box: Dot is one
  conversation (item 1279), so the button is named "Write to Dot", not "New chat". The phone had its search at the foot,
  under the thumb, and your initials at the head; both are gone, the search is at the top as on a Mac.
- **The agents** were a column of coloured faces on a Mac (item 835) and rows with a large face on a phone (item 1259). They
  are a menu now, behind the first button: each agent a badge of its two letters, its name and how many chats it has, to its
  conversation (item 1233), else its page, the open one in the heavier weight; All agents, New agent and Campaigns under
  them. Every function of the faces is there: an agent's page or chat, every agent, a new agent. The menu reads the
  agents again as it opens, so that an entry leads to the conversation as it is now, and waits for the first reading on a
  press that comes before it.
  `menu.js` takes an entry with a `mark` of its own for an icon, a `detail` at its right and `current`.
- **The foot** is four small icon buttons: Settings (named for the owner to a screen reader), Connect apps (item 545, which
  no longer wears the logos of the services), Watch the Mac (item 1264) and Models, at the right. **There is no account
  usage page in Dot**, so the fourth button leads to Settings › Models, where the models and the roles they work in are
  chosen; it is not named Usage.
- **The Pages group is gone.** Watch the Mac moved to the foot, Campaigns to the agents' menu, and the Schedules icon is
  removed from the sidebar (item 855 had put it in the head): the page and its route `#/schedules` are untouched, and
  Settings holds the way in, a Schedules row beside Connect apps in `SETTINGS_GROUPS` (`settingstabs.js`), on a Mac's nav and
  on a phone's sheet (the pre-push review found that no row of Settings led to the page).
- The phone's list no longer hides an agent's conversation from the days: a chat is a row on a phone as on a Mac.
