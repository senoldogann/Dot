### 620. A reply the finish writes again in other words shows once

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The owner: "showing the same thing twice in the chat still happens now and then: find
the problem and fix it." Their screenshot showed it in the chat of a run that described the Mac's screen:
- the description stood as a reply;
- then a thought ("I should call finish with the answer. But the reply was already given as text…");
- then the description again as the answer, every line reworded.

**What it was.**
- A task run that used tools and answers in a plain reply is told to call finish (the loop's note, item 460): such a
  reply may be a step announced, not an end.
- DeepSeek V4.1 Flash then calls finish and writes the answer again in other words.
- The page drops a reply the answer repeats by comparing their words line by line (items 460, 567 and 569). That holds
  a first line reworded or a few lines edited, not a reply whose every line was said anew.
- The words alone cannot tell such a reply from one that holds a line the answer left out (a warning, which must stay:
  the review of item 567). But the loop knows the sequence: the finish came right after its note on the reply, and
  nothing else did.

**As built.**
- **The loop** (`agent/loop.py`) keeps the text replies it told to finish since the run last acted. A step that calls
  anything but finish clears them: they were steps announced. The finish that ends the run takes them, and the run's
  outcome names the steps of those its answer restates (`RunOutcome.restated`), which the manager writes into the run's
  `run_finished` event (`restated`) once the run's inbox is shut. One sent back (a failed call, open plan steps,
  missing paths, the goal check) starts them anew. The review of the first push: an event sent from the loop there
  awaited between the check that the user sent nothing and the run's end, where the loop must await nothing, or a
  message sent in that moment is lost.
- **What restating is** (`agent/restating.py` `restates`): the answer is at least half the reply's length, so one that
  only points at it ("see above") leaves it to be read; each line of the reply that holds words has at least half of
  them in the answer; and the answer holds each number of the reply (by its digits: 16:17 is 16.17) and each word that
  turns its sense ("not", "değil", "yok"), as the comparison of words keeps such a reply. Words are in lower case,
  without the common ones and those of one or two letters; a word with a digit always counts. A reworded line keeps its
  names and the words that carry it; a line the answer left out shares next to none, so its reply stays.
- **The page** drops the replies of those steps: the run's timeline (`timeline.js`) as the run's end comes, and an
  earlier turn of the chat (`history.js`) from the run's `run_finished` event. The comparison of words stays for the
  other cases.
- **The docs.** `docs/daemon.md` (the message that only says the answer again).

**Tests.**
- `tests/unit/test_restating.py`: the owner's own reply and answer (Turkish, every line reworded) restated; a reply with
  a warning the answer left out, an answer that only points, a number or a "not" the answer lost (English and
  Turkish), the same words, a reworded first line, lines of marks.
- `tests/smoke/test_answer_once.py`, in a browser on a scripted run:
  - the reply and a finish in other words show the answer alone, the run's end naming step 2;
  - a finish that only points at the reply, and a reply after which the run worked on, keep the reply;
  - an earlier turn of the chat leaves the restated reply out.

  The first and the last fail against the code before; the second passes on both, as it holds what did not change.
  Item 567's test of a reply that says more than the answer still passes.
