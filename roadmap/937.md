### 937. Memory › Notes folds each note to its first two lines

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-02): "memory sayfasında notes da collapse uncollapse
olabilsin". **Measured** on the owner's Mac: 301 notes, each open with its whole words, every tag and its lines of who
saved it and how it was used, made Settings › Memory 11,000 pixels long on a phone (22,000 at its scale of two).

**Built.** `views/memory.js`: a note is folded to its first two lines, two of its tags and how many more, its badges
(pinned, merged, a run's wish that it go everywhere, from outside content) and its age; a press on its words or its arrow
opens it whole (every tag, who saved it, how it was used), and **Open all** opens every note until a press folds one
again (the choice kept while the list is drawn again). The "Show more" of a long note is gone: opened, a note is whole.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_memory_page.py`: a long note shows two lines, `zyxwv`, `invoices`, `+2`; it opens and
folds; Open all opens it. The other memory page tests open the notes they read whole.
