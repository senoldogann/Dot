### 2091. Evals from real runs graded on trajectory, skill ablation, cost per call

**Status: done (2026-11-28), the commands; a first measured suite is left to the owner's run.** Owner 2026-11-26, from
Uber Engineering's "How Uber Evals 1 Million Agent Skill Invocations a Month": Dot's self-improvement never adopts
because its synthetic tasks saturate (a 100 % pass hides a change; a 70-90 % suite shows one).

Measured first, read-only on the owner's store (2026-11-28): 255 runs keep their events (230 of them code reviews of the
pre-push hook) and 1,110 more are rows of the run ledger, whose chats were deleted with their events. Runs of the owner's
own words (chat, command line, schedule, resume; no parent run) with events: 14, of them 5 failed and 1 marked wrong,
5 with a trajectory. The `skill` tool was never called in them. Every tool call of the record: 6,045, about 134 M
tokens charged as below; `read_file` about 23,000 tokens a call, `browser_act` about 139,000.

What must hold:
- The owner's store is opened read-only (`?mode=ro`); nothing of it is written or copied out of the Dot home.
- A replayed goal is the owner's words to a run on their Mac: an attempt plays it in a throw-away home and an empty
  workspace, held to that workspace as a task the improvement loop wrote is (the balanced preset at least, every
  question declined: `runner.held_to_workspace`, ids `real-` and `owner-`). Only the owner's own words are taken: no
  delegated run, reply to someone else, code review or evaluation.
- The judge never knows which arm gave which answer; each answer is fenced with a nonce (it may quote a page). A judge
  that does not pick stops the ablation rather than leave a pair out.
- No kind of case fills in for another (no fallback): a suite short of a share is shorter and says so.
- Tests call no model: the pure parts with fixtures, the readers on a store made in a temporary folder.

What changed:
- `evals/real_runs.py`: the suite (5 % golden catalog tasks, 80 % real runs, 15 % the owner's `evals/real/owner.json`),
  real runs that went wrong first (failed or cancelled, then marked wrong or asked again); a trajectory (acting tools
  in order) from a run that ended done unmarked; an attempt passes when it ends well and follows half the trajectory in
  order; the skills its run read are seeded and checked with `skill_read`.
- `evals/ablation.py`, `evals/ablation_play.py`: arms with, without, without again (the noise band), and with
  `--parts` each `## ` part left out; the blind judge (`pick`, purpose `harness_select`); verdicts helps / hurts /
  within the noise / too few, with the change in tokens.
- `evals/call_costs.py`: a call's cost is the run's tokens just after it less just before it (from the step that asked
  for it to the step that read its result, side calls included, a step shared by its calls), per day, `skill:<name>`
  for a skill.
- `evals/runner.py`: `play_attempt` with notes saved in the attempt's memory first; the held prefixes.
- `dotx eval suite`, `dotx eval real`, `dotx eval ablate`, `dotx eval costs` (`cli_eval_real.py`); docs in
  `docs/development.md` "Evals from real runs".

Left: the owner's first `dotx eval real` on a model, to see where the suite's pass rate sits; with 14 real cases the
suite is small until more runs keep their events.
