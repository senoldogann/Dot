### 1946. A goal that is reached is never ended failed for a better result in reach

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** B-code M3 of the review of 2026-11-26 (K3-04 of the code review of 2026-11-19). Item 809
had the goal checker's "Outcome Maximization" rule say `continue` when the goal is reached but a clearly better result
is in reach (a better price, a missing detail). `continue` is also "not reached": after `goal_patience` (4) rounds of
it the run ended **failed**, "the goal is still not reached", though it had reached it. The owner saw a false failure,
and the tokens of four rounds were spent on it.

What must hold:
- Item 809 stays: a reached goal with a clearly better result in reach is still sent back to better it, bounded by the
  same `goal_patience`.
- A run whose goal is reached never ends failed for that: when its rounds run out, or at a run's limit, it ends done
  with its answer.
- A goal that is not reached still ends failed after `goal_patience` rounds, as before.
- Nothing about the gate changes: the checker's words are no instruction of the owner's, and every step it leads to is
  judged by the gate as before.

What changed:
- `agent/review.py`: the goal verdict has a decision `improve` ("reached, and a clearly better result is in reach");
  the checker's prompt says improve, never continue, for a reached goal, and why.
- `agent/loop.py`: `_after_verdict` sends an `improve` back with a note that says the goal is reached (`_improve_note`)
  while rounds are left, and ends the run done once they run out; `_after_limit_verdict` ends it done.
- `cli_ui.py` shows the verdict; a campaign's claim the checker gives `improve` is accepted, as its targets are reached
  (`tools/campaign.py` says so, and `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md`). A run sent back to better a reached goal did
  nothing wrong, so its lessons do not count it among the times the goal check sent it back (`agent/lessons.py`).
- `docs/autonomy.md` lists the verdict.
