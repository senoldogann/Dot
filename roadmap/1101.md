### 1101. A phone's chat names whom it is with in the middle of its bar, and its bubbles round as a messaging app's

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** Asked by the owner with a screenshot of Grok Bot's chat on an iPhone: "its bubbles take
their shape from their length, the radii are not all the same; and Grok Bot shows in the very middle: let us do it so".

- The bar of a chat on a phone shows, in its middle on glass (`--glass-surface`, `--glass-border`, `--glass-blur`), the
  face and name of whom the chat is with: Dot, an agent or a team. The chat's title and the model, which it showed on
  the left since item 295, are in the panel's Details. A helper's page keeps its goal. The name gives way with an
  ellipsis before it reaches the way back or the tools.
- A message's bubble, yours and Dot's on a phone (item 1091), is rounded 20 px (`--radius-bubble`): a bubble of one
  line, 38 px tall, is a pill, and a longer one a box whose corners stay as round, where 24 px made a long one look
  swollen.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_phone_layout.py` (the name in the bar's middle within a pixel, clear of the way back and of
the tools; the screenshot looked at), `npm run check`.
