### 848. Quiet pages: one action a row, the rest behind "…", and the paragraphs behind an (i)

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, as stage 6 of the six-stage design plan they approved on
2026-10-30. Agents, Schedules, Customize, Memory and Vault were to show one action on each row, keep the rest in a "…"
menu, and put the paragraphs that explain a page behind an (i).

Measured on the owner's Dot before the change:
- A note's row had four buttons: pin, Edit, Undo the merge and Forget.
- A login's row had two: Edit and Delete.
- A schedule's row had three beside its switch: Run now, Edit and Delete.
- Schedules opened on two lines of explanation, and Agents on four paragraphs above its teams, its agents and the
  ready-made ones.

What changed:
- `menu.js`: `moreButton({ name, items })`, a row's "…" that opens the shared menu (item 130) under it and closes it when
  pressed again. A menu choice may carry a `title`.
- `panels.js`: `aboutToggle(text, label)`, an (i) and the paragraph it shows or hides. `pageHead` puts a page's line
  behind an (i) beside its name in the top bar. This covers Agents, Schedules, Campaigns, Handoffs, Diagnostics and the
  licences.
- `views/memory.js`: a note's "…" holds Pin it (or Take the pin off), Edit, Undo the merge and Forget. The badge still
  says Pinned. `views/vault.js`: an item's "…" holds Edit and Delete.
- `views/schedules.js`: a schedule's row keeps its switch, and its "…" holds Run now, Edit (Edit the brief's settings
  for the brief) and Delete. Run now's choice still says, where the pointer rests, that it does real work under the
  approvals the row's chips name. The page's note on when schedules work is in its (i).
- `views/agents.js`: what Your teams, Your agents and the ready-made teams and agents are for, each behind an (i) beside
  its name. `views/plugins.js`: what the nightly look does and found, behind an (i) beside Proposed for you.
- `app.css`, `settings.css`: the (i), and the head of a page whose line is folded. The pin's pressed style goes.
- `docs/daemon.md`, `docs/memory-and-schedules.md`, `docs/proactive.md` say so.

Kept:
- Every action and every word, a press away.
- The warnings that stand where they are: the scheduler paused, a list that could not be read.
- A settings tab's own line under its name.
- A chat's bar keeps the face and name its agent had when the page opened, after an Edit in its side panel (item 846),
  until the page opens again; the panel and the sidebar show the agent as saved (the pre-push review asked; left so).
