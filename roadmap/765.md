### 765. A run trades with its reasons, its indicators and the whole of its account

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner: when Dot trades it should research what it buys and why, and
decide fast and well; and the open nits of items 763 and 764.

**The change.** `trading_order` takes a `reason` (20 to 300 characters: what the prices and the account showed, what the
run expects, what ends the trade), kept in the order's note and shown in `trading_history` and Settings › Trading; an
order with none is not placed. `trading_market` gives, with a product's candles, what they come to
(`trading/indicators.py`, pure: the change over the window, high and low, SMA20 and SMA50 with where the last close
stands, Wilder's RSI14, the average candle range in percent of the close; `None` where the window is too short, never a
guess), and with the overview each product's last day as Coinbase sums it (`market.day`: the change in percent, the
volume in the quote). The account's worth prices every currency held, not only the allowed products (the pre-push review
of 764: a product allowed while it was held read as a gain of the day, which loosened the day's loss limit by its
worth); one Coinbase gives no price of is said (`Portfolio.unpriced`) and counted as nothing, and a day's start is not
taken from a worth with a hole in it: until the day's first whole look, no buy is placed, a sell still is (the pre-push
review found that a start taken from each look read every loss as none, and then that a currency Coinbase has no product
of in the quote, a dust balance of a coin only traded in USD, would have held buys for good: it is worth nothing to the
account at every look alike, no hole, and only a price that could not be read this time holds). The review also found
the currencies past the priced count cut from the wrong list (one priced at nothing unsaid, the quote reported
unpriced), the run's reason replaced by what went wrong in a failed or unknown order's note (both are kept now), and the
overview failing whole when Coinbase's sum of a product's day could not be read (its numbers are left out instead). The
page's product pattern is the daemon's. On the owner's Dot a paper shift runs every hour (`Kağıt trading vardiyası`,
docs/trading.md says its order of work): the measure of what Dot's trading comes to before any money is on it.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_trading_indicators.py` (a short window gives what it can, the averages and the RSI of a
window worked out by hand, a window that only rises); `tests/unit/test_trading.py` (the reason kept with the order, an
order with none refused; a currency held outside the allowed products priced, one Coinbase has no price of said and the
day's start waited for a whole look, with no buy until then; the currencies past the count said unpriced; a failed
order's note with the reason and what happened; the overview standing without the day's sum). Not measured: what the
paper shift makes; the first ran on 2026-10-28 00:15.
