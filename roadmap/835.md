### 835. The owner's agents are faces at the top of the sidebar, each to its page, as a messaging app's people

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner ("uygulamadaki tüm ekranları ve tasarımları incele, olabildiğince
minimal ama eksik olmayan, yumuşak, Grok Bot gibi yap"), with screenshots of that app. The second part of the third of
the six stages of the design review of 2026-10-30, the plain sidebar: the other app's sidebar opens on its agents'
faces, each with its name; Dot's had a row, Agents, that led to a page of them.

- `sidebar.js`: under the search, a row of the owner's agents (`GET /api/agents`, Dot itself left out, as its chat is
  the "+"): each its face over its name, a link to its page (`#/agents/<id>`), where a message to it starts, its role in
  the tooltip, the one whose page is open marked (`aria-current`); the last tile, All, is the page of every agent and
  team, and the Agents row gives way to it while the owner has an agent of their own. The agents are read as the page
  opens, once a run ended (a run can make one), when a page of it changed one, when the stream comes back and when the
  page comes back into view; the same agents read again leave the tiles standing, so that a focus or a pointer on one
  stays (the pre-push review: every run's events drew them anew), and a list that did not load leaves the row as it
  was.
- `api.js`: `AGENTS_CHANGED_EVENT`, sent once a request of the page made, changed or deleted an agent (a new one, an
  import, a template's or a ready team's, a change, its look, a pause, a delete), which the sidebar reloads on.
- `shell.css`: the tiles (a face of 40 px over a name of two lines at most, so that "Competitor Watch" reads whole, at
  the reading size of the sidebar's rows, 15 px on a phone, on tiles of 88 px there: CI's `test_phone_type` found the
  names at 13 px took a phone's sidebar under nine characters in ten at 15 px or more, item 329's rule),
  the row scrolling sideways with no bar and never squeezed by the list under it, the open tile a surface step up as a
  row is (item 74). The classes are `side-agent-*`: `side-face` is the frame of a chat's face and its loop's mark.

Kept as they were: the Agents page and every way to it, Campaigns and Schedules; a chat's row and the faces of what it
delegated (items 198 and 507).

**Checked.** The owner's Dot read in a headless Chrome of its own with these sheets and scripts served in their place,
wide and on a phone, light and dark: five agents, three tiles and a part of the fourth in the desk's sidebar, four and
a part on a 375 px phone; "Product Performance" is the one name cut there, at its second line. Measured there as
`test_phone_type` measures, on phones of 440 and 375 px: 99 % of the sidebar's characters at 15 px or more, nothing
under 13 px and no target under a finger's size. A first look showed the
row squeezed by the list to a sliver on a phone, and names cut at six letters, both mended before the tests.
`tests/smoke/test_side_agents.py`: two agents made, their tiles and All, the names whole, the links and the tooltip,
the tile a finger's size, the Agents row hidden; on a desk the open agent's tile marked, then All's, the focus on a
tile kept through the agents read again, and an agent renamed from a page shown at once; the agents deleted from the
page, the row gone and the Agents row back.
