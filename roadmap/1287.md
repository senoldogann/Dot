### 1287. Meet's microphone, camera and caption toggles are found by the start of their label

**Status: done (2026-11-21).** Both real tries to join a Meet call (2026-11-12) failed with "Dot could not turn the
microphone off before joining" and none was made since. The toggles were looked for by their exact label
(`[aria-label="Turn off microphone"]`), taken from two open-source bots and tested only on a stand-in page that used the
same words. Meet ends these labels with the machine's keyboard shortcut ("Turn off microphone (ctrl + d)", "(⌘ + d)"
on a Mac), which community scripts for Meet show, so an exact match cannot find them. This is the likely cause; no real
call has been joined to prove it.

What changed: the mic, camera and caption selectors match the start of the label (`^=`). A look for a toggle, the
join button or the chat button that fails now puts the labels of the buttons the page does show in the error's
`page_labels`, so that the next change in Meet's labels is mended from the record. The desk logs the context of a join that failed
(`meeting_join_failed`) and of a chat that would not open. Reading the labels is best effort (a page that is gone gives
none, so the error being built is not replaced), each label is cut to 120 characters without control characters, and the
mic and camera selectors match a button only. The stand-in's labels carry the
shortcut like Meet's. `docs/meetings.md` says the join is still unproven.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_meet_page.py` and `test_meeting_desk.py` (the stand-in with shortcut labels would have been
refused before the change); a toggle labelled otherwise ("Mikrofonu kapat (ctrl + d)") is refused with that label in
`page_labels`, in the error and in the desk's log; labels are cut, and a closed page gives none. Not checked: a real Meet page, which needs the owner's Meet link and `dotx browser meetings-login`.
