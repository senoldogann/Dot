### 1382. Dot for iPhone: an app icon, a launch screen and a Live Activity on the app's own tokens

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** The icon was a placeholder (a yellow circle with two eyes on white, drawn by
`scripts/make_icon.swift`), the global accent was yellow, and the Live Activity used the system's orange, green and red.

- **The icon**: a luminous blue dot (a radial gradient lit from the top left, a rim light and a soft highlight) with two
  dark eyes, on a near-black navy ground with a glow of the accent: three looks as iOS 18 asks (`icon-1024.png`,
  `-dark.png`, `-tinted.png`, a grey dot on black). `scripts/make_icon.swift` draws them; the same dot is the app's mark
  (`DotGlyph`, which the pairing screen, Dot's avatar and the load error draw) and the launch screen's.
- **The launch screen** is the dot on `LaunchBackground`, which is the app's ground in both looks (the first frame no
  longer flashes white). `AccentColor` is the app's blue (a lighter one in the dark).
- **The Live Activity** reads `DotPalette` (compiled into the extension: working is the accent, waiting the warn colour,
  done and failed the good and bad ones, the island's keyline the accent) and draws the same dot. Its layout and its
  links did not change.

**Not verified:** the simulator draws no Live Activity and no Dynamic Island content, so the extension builds (with the
app) and its colours are measured as the app's, but its look on a phone is not seen.
