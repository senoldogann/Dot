### 2032. The search's chips: All, Messages, Bots, Files, on the phone and the web

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner's ask of 2026-11-27, with Grok's app: tapping the search opens it with chips on
top (All, Messages, Bots, Group chats, Files) that filter what it finds. Dot has no group chats apart from its chats (a
chat with a team is a chat), so that chip is left out.

What was built:
- Daemon: `GET /api/threads/search/files?q=&limit=` (`Store.search_chat_files`): the files the owner attached to the
  chats' messages (the `attachments` and `steer` events) and those a run wrote or edited with `write_file`/`edit_file`
  in a call that did its job, newest first, once per chat, a helper's under the chat at the root of its line, every word
  of `q` folded in the name or path; `GET /api/threads/search` now says each find's `run_id` (the run the message is of),
  which the web opens the chat on.
- DotEngine: `FileHit`, `searchFiles`, `HomeFinds.files`, `SearchScope` and `scopedFinds` (All three of each kind, a chip
  every find of its kind), `cutShort` (All's See all), `markedWord` (the word a message find opens its chat at).
- DotNext: the home's search shows the chips while it is open (`SearchChips`, a row that scrolls sideways); the finds
  under their headings (Bots, Chats, Messages, Files, with See all under All); a message opens its chat with Find in chat
  on its marked word, at the find whose words hold the snippet's (`findStart`); a file opens its chat.
- Web: chips under the sidebar's search while it has words (`searchfinds.js`, `searchscope.js`): All, Messages, Agents
  (the web's word for the bots), Files; the chats found by their titles stay the list's rows, and above them the
  messages (the words found in bold), the agents and the files; Agents and Files hide the list; a message opens its chat
  on its run scrolled to it with a ring that fades (`chatfind.js`).

How it was checked:
- `tests/smoke/test_thread_sync.py`: the files search finds an attached file and one a helper wrote under the owner's
  chat, folded, newest first, not a refused write; the chat search says the message's run.
- `FaceAndSearchTests` (DotEngine), `tests/js/searchscope.test.js`: each chip's finds, See all, the marked word, the bots
  by every word folded.
- `RowStatesLiveUITests.testTheSearchChipsShowEachKindAlone` against the throwaway daemon: the chips, All with the chat and
  the file a run wrote, Files and Messages alone, Bots finding nothing, a find opening its chat.
- `tests/smoke/test_living_face_and_search.py` in Chrome at 1440x900 and 390x844, light and dark, English and Turkish:
  the chips, All's three kinds, Files and Agents alone hiding the list, a message opening its chat at that message.
