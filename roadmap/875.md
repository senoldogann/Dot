### 875. An agent says what it never does, and its runs stop there

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner, after the review of Dot against an agent-template article
(0xRafy, "GrokBot Templates"): a good agent has "a defined job, and defined anti-jobs"; knowing what a bot will not do
is what makes it safe to leave running.

What Dot had: the edges of a template's job were words inside its instructions (12 of 15 had one, 3 only the drafts
rule), and an agent the owner or Dot made had no place for them. On the owner's Dot a rule the owner gave ("never touch
the thesis files", 2026-10-30) lived in memory alone, and Dot's own initiative still planned that work three times on
2026-10-31; that part goes to the initiative in its own item, after item 868.

- `store`: `agents.refuses` (a line each; `''` for every agent before), `AgentRecord.refuses`, `REFUSES_CHARS` 2,000;
  `create_agent(..., refuses)`, and `update_agent(..., refuses=None)` keeps what is saved, so trimming tools or an
  older client never clears it.
- `agent/prompt.py`: `AgentBrief.refuses`; the agent's section adds "What you never do, whoever asks (...): when the
  work leads there, stop that part and say so in your answer instead." and the lines (`refusal_lines` drops bullets and
  blank lines). `agent/loop.py` passes it.
- `agent/templates.py`: `AgentTemplate.refuses` for all 15 (Sales Outbound: no inbound leads or existing deals, no
  CRM, nobody written to twice, no invented person or fact, no send); Use template gives them; `AgentFile.refuses`
  (a file made before has none), checked for secrets with the name, role and instructions.
- `tools/team.py`: `create_agent` takes `refuses` (eight lines at most).
- `server`: `AgentRequest.refuses` (`None` keeps it); create, change and import carry it.
- `views/agents.js`, `app.css`: **Never does** in the editor; a template's card lists them a click below its actions
  (its own area on a phone).
- `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_agent_refuses.py` (the store keeps it through a change that does not name it; every
template has two to five; the file carries it), `tests/unit/test_agents.py::test_the_prompt_says_what_the_agent_never_does_after_its_instructions`;
`tests/smoke/test_agent_templates.py` (every card's Never does count and lines; a template's agent and its file in
