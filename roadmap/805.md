### 805. Dot's own lines on a channel are in the owner's language

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner, who reads Turkish: every line the iMessage channel wrote itself
was in English (*Working for 45 s*, *Queued*, *The run was stopped.*, *Dot does not read attachments yet*), around
answers the model wrote in Turkish.

- `channels/words.py` holds every such line in English and in Turkish (`Words`, `ENGLISH`, `TURKISH`): intake, the
  iMessage channel's notices and pairing code, the router's lines (queued, stopped, could not start, how a run ended,
  a wait and where to answer it, tells folded, pictures not sent), the cut of an answer, the line of what was left
  out with its reasons, and progress (its lead, the count of earlier steps, the units of time, a phrase per step).
- `[channels] language = "auto"` (the default; `en`, `tr`) picks them. `SpokenLanguage` hears each message of the
  owner, in the channel before its intake and from the owner's own words alone (the words the router is handed may end
  in Dot's English notes to the run, item 806): `language_of` tells Turkish by the letters only it
  has (ğ, ı, İ, ş) or by more of its common words, English by more of its own, and nothing for a link, a number or a
  single word of neither; the language told is kept in the store (`channels.language`) and read at start.
- A run's progress speaks the language of the message that started it. The run's own English end (*The run was
  stopped.*) is said once in the owner's words, not after them. What a run is told (`UNREAD_NOTE`) stays English.
- The module's English constants stay (`TOO_OLD`, `QUEUED`, …) as the English entries, for what reads them.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_channel_words.py` (the language of messages, followed and kept, a setting that holds
one, a kept value that is no language, every line and step in both with the same placeholders, intake, progress,
the router's lines, the cut and the left-out line in Turkish); the channel tests as they were, in English.
