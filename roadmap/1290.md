### 1290. An account a run makes keeps one login in the vault, and a sign-up that failed gets a new password under it

**Status: done (2026-11-21).** The owner's word of 2026-11-21: when the agent signs up somewhere, its email and
password must be kept in the vault properly and be there to sign in with later. `browser_create_login` already made
the password, filled it and kept the login; what it left open was the second call. A sign-up the site refused (a taken
username, a failed check) and tried again kept a second login for the same account with a password that had never
been used, and a later sign-in could fill the wrong one.

`browser_create_login` now looks in the vault for a login of the same site and username (the username in any case)
before it makes a password. One that stands is not added to: the call is refused with what to do, which is to sign in
with `browser_fill_login` when the account exists. When the sign-up never went through, the call is made again with
`replace` true, and the login that stands gets the new password under its own record, its earlier password in the
notes; if the fill then fails, its old password and notes are put back, as a new login is removed. Only a login Dot made
(its note begins "Made by Dot") can be replaced: one the owner saved or imported is refused with `replace` too, and the
refusal names the login's id. The approval says when the call replaces one. The system prompt tells the run to look in `browser_logins` before it
makes an account, to pass the email or username it signs up with, and to use `replace` after a failed sign-up.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_vault_items_fill.py` (a second call for the same account is refused and keeps one login;
`replace` changes its password under the same record and keeps the old one in its notes; a login the owner saved is
never replaced; a fill that fails during a replace gives the old password and notes back), the vault, context-budget and lean-prompt tests.
