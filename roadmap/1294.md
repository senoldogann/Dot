### 1294. `browser_create_login` finds the owner's login by its site, keeps every earlier password, and takes turns

**Status: done (2026-11-21).** Three defects of item 1290, which made a login for an account once and kept one.

(a) The existing login was found by `item.website == website`. The vault keeps the website as the owner typed it
(`github.com`, `localhost:3000`, `HTTPS://GitHub.com/login`), while `website_of(url)` returns `https://github.com`, so the
owner's own login was not found and a second one was made beside it. `vault/hosts.py` has `site_key` (the host as
`website_hosts` reads it, and the port the website names, none for its scheme's own) and `same_site`, and the call
compares the host and the port, and the username without case and blanks. A login for another port, or of another
username, is not the same account and still gets its own.

(b) A replace kept only the password that stood. The notes are now the new `Made by Dot` header and a line for each
password the login had, the oldest first (`Replaced on <date>, its earlier password: <password>`), read from the old
notes, those of the first form of item 1290 included; past the notes' 1,000 characters the oldest lines go first, and the
header and the newest line stay. Every earlier password is held as a secret, as the one that stood was.

(c) Two calls for one account that started together both passed the check of the vault. The run of a call is now under
a lock for (host, port, username in lower case), taken before the vault is read and held until the login is made and
filled or taken back; a lock nobody holds is let go.

Nothing asks or refuses more: a call whose account the vault does not hold works as it did.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_vault_items_fill.py` (the owner's login saved without a scheme, in capitals with a path, with
`www.` and with the other scheme is found and refused, with `replace` too, and no second one is made; another port or
username still makes its own; three signs-ups keep both earlier passwords in order; two calls with a vault that answers
late make one login, and fail without the lock), `tests/unit/test_login_notes.py` (the notes, the old inline form, the
limit), and the rest of `tests/smoke/test_vault_items_fill.py`.
