### 970. A restart waits past another program that answers at the daemon's port

**Status: done (2026-11-04).** **Found** by Dot in its own record: `restart_when_idle_failed` nine times on
2026-11-03, each "something other than the Dot daemon answers at the address" with a 404 page of Python's
`http.server`, although the daemon came up each time. A `python3 -m http.server 8765`, left running since that morning,
listened on `*:8765`; the daemon binds `127.0.0.1:8765`, which wins the connections while it listens, but in the moment
between the old daemon's end and the new one's bind the health check reached the other server, and
`restart_service` raised on that first answer instead of waiting for the new daemon.

**Built.** `cli_daemon.probe_starting_daemon`: a look at a starting daemon whose health check failed (another program
answered, the answer broke off) is no answer yet; `restart_service` (so `service restart`, `restart_when_idle` and the
watchdog) and `wait_for_daemon` (`service install`) look again until their 60 seconds are up, and then raise the last
look's error, or say that nothing answered.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_restart_past_another_listener.py` (fails before the fix), `tests/unit/test_watchdog.py`,
`tests/unit/test_service_install_from_a_terminal.py`.
