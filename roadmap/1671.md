### 1671. The Mac does not idle-sleep while Dot works

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Dot kept the Mac awake only for the phone, while it charges and Settings › Phone asks
(`caffeinate -s`, item 271). On battery, or with that setting off, idle sleep froze a long run, a night schedule or a
meeting in the middle of a step.

What changed (`awake.py`, `config.py`, `defaults.toml`, `runtime.py`, `server/daemon.py`, `server/routes_system.py`,
`server/app.py`, `server/views.py`, `cli_daemon.py`, `evals/runner.py`):

- `[daemon] keep_awake` (on by default) and `keep_awake_after_s` (60): `keep_awake_while_working` looks every 2 s
  whether Dot works (a run of its own or of a `dotx` command it relays, a meeting: a schedule's run is a run) and holds
  `caffeinate -i -w <daemon pid>` (`runtime.work_awake`) from then until 60 s after the last work (`holds_awake`).
- Chosen over an IOKit power assertion made through PyObjC/ctypes in the daemon: the same child `caffeinate` as the
  phone's hold (`KeepAwake` now takes its assertion, `-s` or `-i`), `-w` lets it go when the daemon dies however it
  dies, `pmset -g assertions` names it, and a test points `DOT_CAFFEINATE` at a stand-in that keeps nothing awake.
- `GET /api/health` and `GET /api/status` carry `awake` (`holding`, `idle`, `off`); `dotx service status` prints it in a
  line. An eval's config turns it off. A `caffeinate` that cannot start ends the task, logged as
  `keep_awake_while_working_ended`.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_work_awake.py` (the rule of the grace; the state of the setting; a stand-in `caffeinate` is
started with `-i -w <pid>` once work begins, held through the grace and let go after it, and at the end). Without the
grace, two of its tests fail.
Not done: the real `caffeinate` was not started by a test; `pmset -g assertions` on the owner's Mac was not looked at.
