### 1991. DotNext: the jump-to-end button comes whenever the owner has scrolled up, and the expanded composer names the model

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** Two reports of the owner's: scrolled far up in a long chat with the capsule ("Plan 5/5")
showing, the round "↓" beside it did not come; and the expanded composer's chip (the reference: T3's) should say the
model, not only its mark.

Rules:
- The chat follows new words, and hides the "↓", only while it is at its end. The owner's own scroll away stops the
  follow; a height that moves by itself (a lazy stack estimating rows out of sight) does not. The old rule read the owner's
  scroll only at the moment the end moved away, so a chat whose end had moved away first, by itself, was never seen to be
  left: the follow stayed on and the button never came. The owner's scroll now counts when it begins too
  (`followsAfterScroll`, pure and tested). The button leaves at the end, and a tap scrolls to the end and follows again.
- One-line capsule: the chip is the provider's mark and a chevron. Expanded (more than a line, a file, or an
  accessibility size): the mark, the model's name without a provider's name in front of it, its level and "Fast" (fast mode)
  in grey, and the chevron, on the one centre line of + the microphone and send. At the accessibility sizes the name may
  truncate, the grey words and the chevron give way, the mark stays. VoiceOver reads "Model: name · level Fast" in both.
- The web UI moves with the phone (the owner's standing rule): its way down (the computer's arrow in the bar, the phone-width
  round button beside the capsule) came only while a finished chat's answer start was below the window, so a reader scrolled up
  past it in a long chat that is over had none, and a press in a working chat led to an earlier turn's answer. It now shows whenever
  the end is away from the window (a working run's: whenever the reader left the follow), and a press leads to the answer while its
  start is below, else to the end and follows again; the press also holds the follow while the page's own shift of the scroll
  (older turns drawn above) would otherwise read as the reader leaving. The web's composer chip, name and grey level with Fast is
  item 2015's, done in step with this one.

  separate finding.
