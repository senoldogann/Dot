### 1008. Approve and Deny on the iPhone's lock screen

**Status: done (2026-11-06).** **Why.** The next step of item 997 the owner asked for on 2026-11-06: answer what a run
waits for from the lock screen, without opening the app.

**Built.** `notify/apns.py` `approval_payload`: an approval's alert with the app's category `DOT_APPROVAL` and the
approval's id, never what it asks. `notify/apns_watch.py`: only a step of low or medium risk goes so. A step of high
risk (money, a message or a post sent for the owner, a credential), a question, a secret and a handoff go as a plain
alert that opens Dot: the alert says nothing of what it asks, and the owner must see the amount or the receiver first
(the pre-push review). iOS: Approve and Deny each require Face ID or the passcode (`authenticationRequired`); the app
posts `/api/approvals/{id}/decision` with the phone's key, as the app's page does, with no note and no "don't ask
again"; an id from the payload must have the daemon's shape; an answer that does not reach the daemon is said in a
notification of its own, and a 409 (no longer open) is let go. No change to the approvals: the phone's key already
decides them. Docs: `docs/phone.md`, `docs/safety.md`.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_apns.py` (a medium step's category and id, no summary; a high step, a question, a secret
and a handoff plain); the app built for iOS.
