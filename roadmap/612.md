### 612. Watchers tell as they judge, and small fixes of voice, names, reactions and shown pages

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The final review of main found, outside the channel, seven things in items 571, 574, 585,
586 and 600, each with a probe that reproduced it: a look of a watcher judged every mail before it told any (so a look
that was cut told and kept nothing, and the next look asked about the same mails and was cut again), and Check now had
no busy state; the Watchers row on a phone left a long name a sliver and let a long address out of the row; reading
aloud went on over the page the owner opened next; `spokenText` was quadratic; a skill's name lost every letter outside
a-z, so "Şirket yıllık raporu" was `irket-y-ll-k-raporu`; `step-1`, `step-01` and `step-0001` were three messages to the
claim of a follow-up; and a shown page named files it did not write in while its answer said nothing was left out, and
wrote a repeated picture once for each row.

The approach. No path under "Always needs a human" in REVIEW.md changes, and nothing of the policy, the guard or the
approvals: what must hold is held by the code that does the work (a look writes down each item as it deals with it; one
message has one key; the reading ends with its page), and nothing here asks the owner anything new. Item 615 moves the
watcher's form into a dialog after this lands: nothing here touches where the form opens, and the new tests do not use
it.

**As built.**

1. *Watchers (571).* `proactive/watching.py`. `_choose`, which judged every item before any was told, is `_to_decide`
   (the newest `judged_per_look`, oldest first) and `_decide` (the model's verdict, or what the look found for the
   calendar and a GitHub watcher without words); `_look` deals with one item at a time: judges it, tells it (`_tell`
   writes it down as it did) or, when it is not the owner's, writes it down (`_keep`), and only then asks about the
   next. The look's limit (`LOOK_TIMEOUT_S`, 180 s) is no longer one `asyncio.timeout` round the whole look but a moment
   (`_until`) that the reading of the provider and each question are held to; what is being told is told and written
   down first, so a look is cut between items and never in one (the old loop could be cut after a tell's push and before
   its key was kept, which told it again at the next look: the test of a look cut while it tells shows it on the code
   before). A cut look says how far it came ("it had dealt with 3 of the 7 new
   ones (2 told) and the next look takes the rest") on the row and in the outcome (`found`, `told`), its cursor stays,
   and the next look, five minutes on, asks only about what is left. The question of a watcher has a limit of its own,
   `JUDGE_TIMEOUT_S` (30 s), under the look's and not `[agent] model_call_timeout_s` (420 s, which is for a run's step
   and which one stalled answer could outlast the look with, so the "told unjudged, not dropped" path was out of reach
   under the defaults): a question that is not answered in 30 seconds is a mail the model could not judge, told as such,
   and the look goes on. Check now (`watcherspanel.js`): while a look goes on the row's button shows a spinner
   (`aria-busy`, a title that says it can take minutes), is disabled, and the panel keeps the state through every
   drawing of the list; `check` does nothing for a row that is looking.
2. *The Watchers row on a phone (571).* `settings.css`, `watcherspanel.js`. The address chip has its text in a
   `.chip-text` that ends in an ellipsis inside the row (the chip's title has it whole), so no address runs out of the
   row at any width; the kind chip wraps under a long name (`flex-wrap`) instead of squeezing it; on a phone the name
   spans the row's width from the switch (it had 80 px at 390 and 14 px at 320, between the switch, the kind chip and
   the three buttons), the buttons stand at the end of the row's last line beside the when-line, the switch stays at the
   name's first line, and the kind chip keeps the height of its words as the other chips of a row do (item 436). The
   page's phone grid is said again for the watcher rows only, by selectors that outweigh it.
3. *Reading aloud stops with the chat (574).* `views/run.js`: the page's disposer calls `stopReading()`, which the
   router runs when the owner leaves the chat and when a message sent in it takes the chat to the page of its next run.
4. *`spokenText` in linear time (574).* `voice.js`. Three patterns read to the end of a line from each of a run of their
   characters: the link (from each `[`), the table's rule (two runs of spaces that could share a line's) and the pipes
   (from each space of a run). The link is one pattern of three alternatives, the link and the two stretches that look
   like one and are not (a `[` with no `](`, a `[text](` whose address does not end in `)`), the latter taken whole so
   that no `[` inside them starts a scan of the same stretch again; the rule has its spaces only after a pipe; a pipe
   takes the spaces before it from where their run begins. 80,000 characters of each of 13 shapes take under 5 ms each
   (the `[` run took 4.3 s in the review and 9.3 s here). On 300,000 random Markdown-like texts, the rule and the pipes
   read exactly as they did (0 differences in 600,000 strings of their own characters) and the link only differs where a
   failed link's address run holds a `[` that would have started a link of its own, which is garbage text either way.
5. *Names in Turkish (585, 423).* `agent/playbooks.py` `slug`: the text is lower-cased, the letters no decomposition
   turns into ASCII are spelled (`ı` i, `ß` ss, `ø` o, `æ` ae, `œ` oe, `ł` l, `đ` d, `ð` d, `þ` th), NFKD splits the
   others into a letter and a mark, and the marks go: `learned-sirket-yillik-raporu`, `learned-musteri-sikayet-yaniti`,
   `taught-fatura-duzenleme-cikti-alma`. A skill written before keeps its folder (a name is kept with its skill, never
   recomputed), and a run of its kind finds it: `same_kind` slugs the kind on both sides, so "Şirket yıllık raporu"
   finds the skill stored as `learned-irket-y-ll-k-raporu` and updates it where it is, and now so does the same words in
   capitals or without their marks. "Ödeme" and "Deme" were one name; they are two.
6. *Reaction keys (586).* `agent/reaction_words.py` `MESSAGE_PATTERN` is `answer` or `step-` and a number from 1 written
   as it is (`[1-9][0-9]{0,5}`), which the routes and `is_message` read: `step-01`, `step-0001` and `step-0` are 422, so
   a message has one key and one reaction on it starts one follow-up.
7. *Shown pages (600).* `tools/page_files.py` (rewritten around the same seams), `tools/pages.py`.
   - The pictures of a `srcset` (of an `img` and of a `source`) are written in, read as the HTML standard reads one (an
     address is what comes up to a space, a comma inside it, as in a `data:` address, does not end it, and a comma at
     its end does; descriptors stay), and so is the icon of the page (`link` whose `rel` has `icon` in it).
   - A frame, an object and an embed are named in `PageFiles.framed` and in the answer ("Not loaded, as a shown page
     loads no frame, object or embed"), since the policy of a shown page has no frame-src, object-src or child-src and
     its default-src is none: nothing, a `data:` address included, loads in them.
   - A picture that three `img` tags or more name by their `src` alone is written once (`_repeated` counts them;
     `shared` makes the entry): a table `{"1": "data:…"}` in one script at the end of the body (the end of the page when
     it has none, in a function so that it declares nothing the page's scripts could meet, its `</` escaped), and each
     tag keeps its other attributes and takes `data-dot-pic="1"` in place of its `src`. A thousand rows with one icon
     were 494,465 characters with 640 rows broken; they are 57,280 with every row. A picture named fewer times, or by a
     tag with a `srcset`, is written where it is named.
   - Each file is found, read and encoded once however often it is named (`_places`, `_contents`, `_addresses`; the
     names of a file still go in `written`, `left_out` and `too_large` as many times as the page names it, as before):
     22,000 `image` tags took 36 s and take 0.10 s, one `style` with 22,000 `url()` 26 s and 0.05 s, 13,000 linked
     sheets 21 s and 0.05 s.
   - Writing the files in has `WORK_S` = 10 s, checked where a file is looked for the first time. After it the rest of
     the page is shown as written, what the tag in hand had counted is taken back (`mark`, `undo`), `PageFiles.cut` says
     so and the answer repeats it.
   - `docs/runs.md` says all of it; the tool's description says that a frame, an object or an embed loads nothing.
8. Docs: `docs/proactive.md` (the look's order and limits, the question's limit, Check now, the phone row, the outcome

**Findings left.**
- The review also suggested that `readAloudButton` read the reading in progress, so that a speaker found again shows it.
  With the reading ending with its page a speaker is never found with one in progress, so it is not built.
- The sharing of a repeated picture is for `img` tags with a `src` alone. A `srcset`, a `source`, a `video` poster,
  SVG's `image` and a `url()` repeated by the thousand are written where they are named: their time is fixed (each file
  is read once), their size is not, and what does not fit is named as too large.
- Chrome draws a `srcset` whose candidates are `data:` addresses from its last candidate, whatever the screen (measured:
  with files it chooses by density, with `data:` addresses it takes the last at one pixel to the point too). In a real
  `srcset` the picture is the same one at twice the pixels, so its size does not change.
- The links of `spokenText`: a failed link's address that holds a `[` which starts a link of its own is read with the
  failed one.
- A look that is cut is tried again after the retry floor (five minutes), not at once; the items past the hour's limit,
  which are said in one line at the end of a look, are written down with that line, so a look cut before it leaves them
  for the next.
- A reaction stored under a key written before this change (`step-01`, which only a script could send) stays where it
  is; nothing of the page writes one.
- A look does not look at the emergency stop between two mails (nor did it before): the stop is read when a look starts.
- An `img` tag inside a `template` that is one of the three or more tags of a picture has the mark and no `src`: the
  script of the table does not reach the content of a template, so a copy made from it has no picture.
