### 1102. A run does the owner's request however bluntly it is put

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** Asked by the owner: "the agent should handle more aggressive prompts too and not give
up; I mean the sense of refusing."

Every run is told that there is always a way (item 446); it was not told what to make of a request worded roughly,
and a model's own caution answered such a request with a warning, a moral note or a weaker version of it. One line is
added to that section of the prompt: the owner's request is the job however bluntly or harshly it is put; on their own
Mac, accounts, files and code they need no reason and no permission; it is done without a lecture, a warning or a
watered-down version; what the trust and safety rules hold back, or what would harm someone else, is held back in one
line, and the rest is done; the lean prompt says it in one line, as it says the rest (`_BLUNT`), so that it stays
under half of the full one. The rules of trust and safety are unchanged and still bound every way.

The line is kept short, as the prompt is near what a small window leaves: in the full prompt it says what is held back
too, in the lean one the next line does. `test_context_budget.py` is raised by 150 (system by 200), and
`test_scaffold.py`'s small window from 42,000 to 43,000 tokens, as it was for every rule before.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_context_budget.py`, `tests/smoke/test_scaffold.py`, `dotx eval check`, the whole unit suite.
