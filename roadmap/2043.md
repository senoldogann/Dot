### 2043. Show when the Mac was last reachable

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** After a lasting outage, DotNext shows “Mac asleep or unreachable since HH:MM” in the
chat capsule and the chat list. The time comes from the most recent successful contact with the Mac, including
stream keepalives, and is kept with the phone's cache. Before the first contact the line has no time. The web app
shows the same wording in its connection line on desktop and phone. English and Turkish use the same wording
across the two clients. An interrupted chat stream can still be reported separately when the daemon is reachable.

Verification covers the capsule and chat list in `ChatUITests`, daemon outages at desktop and phone widths in
`test_phone_line.py`, and the outbox's automatic delivery after a daemon outage in `SyncTests`.
