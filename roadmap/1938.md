### 1938. A floor of free space: the owner is told once, and large writes wait

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The review of 2026-11-26 (wave 1, #5; A-runs §4): on 11-25 the Mac's disk filled, two
copies of the database failed half way ("No space left on device", "database or disk is full"), a schedule's run died
on "the database refused the write", and nobody was told until things broke.

What changed:
- `[store] min_free_gb` (5 GiB; 0: no floor) and `store/space.py`: pure `short_of_space` and `space_change`,
  `ensure_space` (raises the new `DiskSpaceError`, a `StoreError`, with the free, needed and floor bytes), and
  `watch_space`, a daemon task that looks every five minutes and tells the owner once when the disk falls below the
  floor (`owner_told`, "Your Mac's disk is nearly full", opening Settings › Diagnostics), logs `disk_space_low` and
  `disk_space_recovered`, and tells again only after a recovery.
- A copy of the database (the daemon's loop and `dotx backup now`) checks room for its uncompressed and compressed files
  before it begins; refused, the loop logs `backup_skipped_low_space` and tries again ten minutes later.
- An upload (`POST /api/attachments`) is refused before it is written; the web answers `DiskSpaceError` with 507.
- docs/configuration.md says it.

Runs and the database's own writes are not stopped: what fills the disk was not the store, and a refused run would
lose more than a late copy.

How it was checked: `tests/unit/test_disk_space.py` (the floor, a refused write leaves nothing written and maps to
507, the owner told once while low and again after a recovery), with the backup, config and server tests.
