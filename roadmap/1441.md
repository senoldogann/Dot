### 1441. The iPhone app's Notifications switches: which pushes Dot tells this phone

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The daemon sorts every push into three kinds (`needs_you`, `ready`, `done`, item 1421) and
tells a phone only those it switched on (`GET` and `PUT /api/phone/push-settings`), but the app had no switch: the only way
to quiet a kind was the iPhone's own page of this app's settings, which cannot tell a finished task from a question Dot asks.
Research of 2026-11-23 (the push policy of an agent app's phone: a switch for "when it needs me" and one for "when it is
done").

What changed: Settings has a **Notifications** group of three switches, Needs you, Ready and Done, each with one line of
what it means in the app's own voice (`PushKind.words`; no engine words: a test fails on "push", "daemon", "token", "run"…).
`Core/PushSettings.swift`: `PushSettings` is the three booleans as the daemon writes them (`needs_you`, `ready`, `done`);
`PushSwitches` is the rule, with no clock and no network. A flip **shows at once** and all three are written together; one
write is on its way at a time, and a second flip meanwhile waits and goes after it carrying the latest of all three (the
owner's last word stands, also when they flipped a switch back); a read that arrives while a write is on its way does not
undo the flip; a write the daemon does not keep (a refusal, no answer) puts the switches **back to what the daemon held**
(the last thing it kept, also when the failed write was a follow-up) and says so in a plain line (a 401 and a 404 say to
forget the key and pair again; no answer says Dot could not be reached; anything else that Dot could not keep that). With
no answer to the read, **the last switches the phone knew are shown dimmed** with a line saying so, and do not move (a
switch that cannot be kept is no switch; a flip with no link says so the same way instead of waiting 45 s for a refusal).
`AwarenessModel` (one per `NativeStore`, `Data/AwarenessModel.swift`) reads on appearing (`task(id: online)`: again when
the link comes back), writes in order, and keeps what the daemon held in `awareness.json` of the cache folder
(`AwarenessSnapshot`, in the chat cache's layout; a field a later build adds reads as none), so the switches are drawn at
once from disk. `APIClient.pushSettings()` and `setPushSettings(_:)`. The row of Settings › Phone that opened the iPhone's
page of this app's settings is now "iPhone notification settings" (it is where banners, sounds and the badge are allowed).

Checked: `ios/DotTests/PushSettingsTests.swift` (14 cases of the rule: dimmed until read, a flip shown at once and written as
three, a second flip waiting and carrying both, a flip back, a refused write rolled back also after a follow-up, a read
during a write, the plain words for each failure, no jargon in the three lines, the JSON), `AwarenessModelTests` (6 cases
against a request stub: the read and its file, the last known state drawn from disk with the daemon out of reach, a flip
written as all three and kept, a 500 and a 404 rolled back with their words, two flips in order) and
`AwarenessRequestTests` (the paths, the methods, the phone's key and the keys of the body). `xcodebuild test` on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5), the whole suite: 368 tests, 0 failures, this item's 22 new cases among them.
Seen in a simulator screenshot against a canned stand-in for the app's routes (dark, with one switch off:
`workspace/ios-aware-shots-2026-11-24/settings-dark.png`).
Not checked: on a phone or against Dot's own daemon; a tap on a switch (none was driven: the flip, the dimmed look and the
rollback are checked by the model's tests, not on a screen); the light look; VoiceOver reading of the three rows.
