### 966. A settings tab names itself once on a phone that turns

**Status: done (2026-11-03).** **Found** by `tests/smoke/test_phone_back.py`, which failed three times on CI on
2026-11-03 with two `h1` after the phone turned: a settings tab names itself in its head (`.set-title`), and on a phone
in the bar instead. The script chose the bar's name as the page was drawn (`PHONE.matches`, then again on its `change`
event) and the style hid the head's by the width (`@media (max-width: 720px)`): two choices made apart, which a turn of
the phone could find out of step for a moment.

**Built.** `views/settings.js` always puts the tab's name in the bar (`h1.stage-h1.set-phone-title`) and no longer
listens to the width; `settings.css` hides the bar's title on a wider window (`.stage-title:has(> .set-phone-title)`)
by the very width that hides the head's on a phone, so the style alone chooses. The wide window's rules are written
`not all and (max-width: 720px)`, the complement of the phone's, so that no width between 720 and 721 px shows both
or neither.

**Checked.** `test_phone_back.py` four times in a row, `test_settings_nav.py`, `test_phone_settings.py` (both now say
the bar's title is hidden on a Mac's window, not absent), `test_phone_screens.py`, `test_channels_page.py`,
`test_rules.py`, `npm run check`.
