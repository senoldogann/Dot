### 1893. DotNext lets go of a reset Dot's chats instead of syncing them for ever

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** Found by the live check of the iPhone app (L1, backlog item A2, which the owner approved on
2026-11-25): after the Mac's Dot was reset at the same address, DotNext's chat list said "Syncing…" for ever and a new
chat stayed "Sending…".

Why: revisions are the database's. A reset daemon starts them again below what the phone's kept list holds, and the
list's reducer rightly takes no view older than the one it holds, so the list never went live; a new chat is sent only
on a live list. The cache was dropped only on another daemon version, and a reset keeps the version.

What must hold:
- A restart or an update of the same Dot is no reset: the outbox is kept, nobody is told.
- After a reset no copy of the old daemon's chats is used, and nothing the outbox kept for them reaches the new one.
- No word the owner wrote is lost in silence: the unsent messages' words and the old chats' drafts go into the new
  chat's draft.
- The owner is told once.

What changed:
- The daemon: `GET /api/status` says `store_id`, the id its database is given the first time it is asked (a `kv` row,
  `Store.store_id`), which a restart and an update keep and a new home does not
  (`tests/smoke/test_server.py::test_the_status_names_the_database_by_an_id_that_a_restart_keeps_and_a_reset_changes`).
- The engine keeps the daemon it heard (`DaemonIdentity`: version and store id, `daemon.json` beside the copies).
  `DaemonRules.change` tells the same daemon, an update (copies dropped, as before) and a reset (another store id):
  the unsent messages are kept first (`DaemonReset` in `reset.json`, each by its outbox key, so a kill before the next
  step tells none twice), then the outbox drops its doings for the old chats and runs with their photos
  (`DaemonRules.afterReset`; a new chat is kept and its photos uploaded again), then the copies, the accepted messages
  and the started chats are dropped. The notice stays until the owner closes it.
- The outbox's own gate (`Outbox.maySend`, from `SyncStore.maySend`) sends nothing before the daemon's status was heard
  in the session, whatever starts the sending (a launch, a doing, a retry), so an old doing never reaches a new daemon;
  a new chat goes as soon as the list is live rather than at its next five-second look.
- The app puts the words, and the drafts of the old chats, into the new chat's draft (the new chat sheet now keeps its
  draft like a chat does; a mark kept with the draft puts them there once) and shows on the chat list "Your Mac's Dot
  was reset; the old chats are gone from this phone. Your unsent words wait in New chat." (and "Photos of unsent
  messages were not kept." when there were some) until it is closed.

Checked: `SyncTests.testAResetDotAtTheSameVersionDropsTheOldCopiesAndOutboxAndKeepsTheUnsentWords` (red without the
change: the list never goes live; and red without the outbox's gate: a doing made while the Mac is slow to answer is
sent to the new daemon), `testTheSameDatabaseHeardAgainUnderAnotherVersionIsNoReset`, the whole engine suite,
the smoke test, the app's build and the String Catalog check. Not done: a reset of the e2e script's throwaway daemon
in a UI test (a reset home forgets the phone's key, so it needs the daemon's database reset alone; the next slice).
