### 1913. Several chats under Dot or one agent: the audit, and a chat shown twice stays open

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner asked whether several chats inside Dot or an agent mix anything up.

Checked on a throwaway daemon (`ios/Next/scripts/e2e_daemon.py`, a phone key; two chats under Dot and two under one
agent, runs at work in three at once; 21 checks, all held): each chat's stream carries its own run and none of
another's; a follow-up queued in one chat is that chat's only; an approval names its run and shows in its chat's view
alone; stopping one run leaves the others as they were; marking one chat seen leaves the others unread; an agent's
chats wear the agent and Dot's none. In the app's code: the transcript, the pending messages, the asks dock, the
Working capsule, Stop, the live player, the model and approvals choice (kept on the daemon per chat), the draft and the
scroll place (`ChatScrollMemory`) are each the chat's own; a chat's screen is rebuilt per chat (`.id(thread)`).

Found: the engine's open and close were not counted and arrive as separate tasks, so a chat shown twice (a switch
back to it before its old screen went) was closed by the old screen's close while on screen: its stream ended and it
was no longer marked seen. Fixed: the `Supervisor` counts the screens holding a chat and ends it when the last one
leaves, whichever order the calls arrive in.

Checked: `SeenTests.testAChatShownTwiceStaysOpenUntilBothLeave`, red without the change.
