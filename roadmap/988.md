### 988. A version is proposed from a week of real runs and tried on them half and half

**Status: done (2026-11-05).** **Why.** Item 987 made the night a digest of the day's real runs; this item is
the rest of the owner's choice of 2026-11-05 ("learn from real use"), as the red-team review of the plan shaped it.

**What must hold.**

- A proposal comes from a pattern, not from one run: a way a tool failed (a signature of the digest) in at least three
  runs, in at least two chats, on at least three days of the last seven. One run, one chat or one bad day proposes
  nothing.
- The proposer is shown the signatures and their counts, never a run's text. A signature seen only in runs that read
  what others can author is shown as its tool and a class from a closed list (`timed out`, `not found`, `refused`,
  `invalid arguments`, `page did not load`, `other`), since a third party's error may be quoted in its first sentence.
- A candidate goes through the gate of every text Dot learns (`create_version`), and a candidate whose text names the
  shell, the terminal, an approval, a permission or asking is set aside unused: a habit must not steer runs toward a
  tool with fewer guards or around a question.
- At most one proposal a week and one trial at a time; while a trial runs no proposal is made.
- The trial: each run of Dot's own agent that starts while it is on takes the candidate or the version in use by its
  id (`sha256(run_id)`, even or odd), and records which (`harness_trial` event). The version in use stays in use.
- What the trial measures: the share of runs in which one of the signatures it was proposed for failed (lower is
  better), and the share of runs that ended done (a guard).
- When it ends: with at least 40 ended runs in each half, the candidate is *better* when its share of failing runs is
  lower with p < 0.1 (one-sided test of two proportions) and its share of done runs is not lower by a quarter; with at
  least 20 in each half, it stops at once as *worse* when its done share is lower than the other half's by a quarter or
  more; after 21 days without a decision it ends *unclear*. A better candidate is kept for the owner (`adopt = "owner"`,
  the default) or adopted (`"auto"`); a worse or unclear one is set aside. The version in use is never replaced unless
  the trial says better.

**Built.** `harness/trial.py` (pure): the half of a run (`arm_of`), the closed list of classes (`class_of`), the
patterns of a digest (`patterns_of`) and the lines the proposer is shown (`pattern_lines`, `shown_signature`), the words
that keep a candidate out (`steering_words`), the halves of a trial (`halves_of`, `failing_runs`) and the judgement
(`judge`, a one-sided test of two proportions). `harness/seat.py`: the text a run takes as it starts (`text_for_run`),
which `agent/loop.py` calls in place of `adopted_text`, recording its half (`harness_trial`). `harness/weekly.py`: the
week (`week`: weigh the trial under way, or propose from a week's patterns and begin a trial, telling the owner) and the
night (`night`: the digest, then the week, in the step's record as `week`), which the scheduler runs. `Store.trial_runs`
reads a trial's ended runs. `prune_records` moved to `harness/service.py`, which the digest and the step share. The page
says the week's word under a night's row, and what a night and a week cost. Docs: `docs/harness.md`. With it, the nits
the pre-push review left on item 987: the step's section of `docs/harness.md`, `harness/schedule.py`,
`server/routes_harness.py`, `server/views.py` and `docs/configuration.md` say a step or a night where each is meant, and
`OUTCOME_ERROR_CHARS` no longer stands between a comment and its constant.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_harness_trial.py` (the judgement's four ends, a pattern across chats and days, a
tainted signature by its class, the closed classes, the steering words), `tests/smoke/test_harness_week.py` (in the real
runtime with the scripted proposer: a week's pattern proposed for, a trial begun, each half's text, the trial ended as
worse with the candidate set aside and the version in use kept; a quiet week proposes nothing; a proposal that names
the shell is set aside unused), `tests/unit/test_harness.py` (who may read a stored version),
`tests/smoke/test_harness_page.py`, `tests/smoke/test_scheduler.py`.
