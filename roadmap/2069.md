### 2069. In yolo, Dot asks only before a payment

**Status: built (2026-11-28), waiting on the owner's look (it touches `src/dotagent/policy/`).** The owner decided it on
2026-11-28, in the lead chat: "Yolo modda hiçbir şey sormasın, sadece ödeme işlerinde sorsun". The run that brought it,
`run_mwzkfimb_635142` (the owner's chat, yolo), filled four job applications and left two questions for a `browser_act`
"press Enter" that submitted a form: the step was read as a message sent for the owner, which item 860 kept for their
yes in yolo, and the run ended without sending any of them.

## Approach (written before the code)

What must hold, in the gate and the engine (no model, no rule of the owner's and no review answers it):

- In a yolo run, a step that carries `financial` (a payment, as the classification reads it today, unchanged) is the
  owner's, in a chat the owner opened (item 860, as before) and in a chat Dot opened on its own initiative (new: the
  owner's word names payments as the one ask of yolo, and item 850 never held them there). It is handed to the owner by
  the money stop, as in every mode that asks; one within the caps they pre-approved for its site (`[policy] spend`) is
  their question instead. No review model answers it, in a schedule's run of the owner's chat neither (as before).
- Nothing else asks in yolo: a message or mail sent for the owner, a public post and a release go through unasked in
  every chat, the owner's and Dot's own, and are recorded as yolo's with why the policy would have asked. The review
  model no longer judges a schedule's message or post in the owner's chat in yolo (item 860's `judged`): it goes.
- Outside yolo nothing changes: in `ask`, `auto` and `supervised` a chat Dot opened on its own initiative still asks
  before a message, a post or a release (item 850), and item 429's guard asks before a message or a post everywhere.
- Free mode is unchanged: it asks nothing, a payment neither (item 922).
- What refuses is unchanged: a research run, the pace of a site, the review model's check of a tainted yolo run
  (`[policy] yolo_taint_check`, item 525), the refusal of a secret sent out in yolo (item 1097), the desktop's keep-away
  from password managers, the sandbox of a run nobody started (item 2016), the emergency stop and sealed paths.
- What asks past the gate is unchanged and named in `KNOWN_ASKS`: a post in a meeting's chat (item 1138) and a join that
  is not the owner's word (item 1150) ask in every mode, free mode included; a tainted run's leave of a meeting asks in
  yolo (item 1138); the budget's cap (item 1731). The owner's word covers yolo's gate: these are asks of their own,
  reached by any mode, and dropping them is their own decision, not taken here.
- A run nobody watches (a chat a schedule or a watcher opened) asked nothing in yolo and still asks nothing, a payment
  neither: the owner's two chats of 860 and 850 are where yolo asked, and only there a payment still does.

What only follows from it:

- A note from a run of Dot's own initiative to a run of another chat is refused when the reader would do unasked what
  the sender asks, unchanged: in yolo the sender still asks a payment, so its note goes to no schedule's or watcher's run
  in yolo, which would pay unasked. The first draft lifted the refusal for a yolo sender; the independent security
  review found the payment that let through, and it was put back (`RunManager.note_chat`, its words now naming the
  payment). The security review also noted that a model can write the wait's mark at the end of an answer the goal
  check accepts, so that a done run reads "Waiting for you" (item 2070): a label only, as a schedule's firing could
  already be marked so (item 1952); no decision reads it.
- `claude_code/cut.py` (`outward`) and `agent/resume.py` (`OUTWARD_FLAGS`) read item 860's class (a payment, a message, a
  post) to keep a run whose such call was cut from doing it a second time. That is a class of what reaches other people,
  not of what yolo asks: it keeps its three flags, now named there and no longer the engine's ask set.

**Where.**

- `agent/gate.py`: in yolo the held guard is `payment_guard` for a chat the owner opened or one of Dot's own initiative;
  outside yolo `initiative_guard` for a chat of Dot's own initiative. The review model's judgement of a schedule's
  message (`judged`) goes.

- The card's words (`cardwords.js`, `approvals.js`), the YOLO hint and the Rules page say what yolo asks.
