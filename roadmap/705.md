### 705. A campaign's chat and its sessions wear a mark of their own, and a session at work looks like a chat

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** **Asked by the owner** with a screenshot: under **Working**, a later session of a
campaign stood as a plain activity row (a state dot, the title, "a session of the campaign") between chat cards with
creatures, and a campaign's conversation looked like any chat, while a schedule's chat wears the violet loop.

**The change.** `ThreadSummary.campaign_id` names the campaign whose conversation a chat is (the newest one on that
thread, read through a new index `campaigns_thread`), so the sidebar puts a still target on a disc of orange and pink at
the left foot of its creature, the loop's mark staying at the right; both can show. A run at work with no chat of its
own (`workingEntry` without a chat: a later session, a delegated run) is drawn as a chat's card (`workRow`): its
creature, its title over its detail, and the working clock at the right, a session with the campaign's mark.

**Checked.** `test_activity.py`: the Working section holds two chat cards, the session's with its creature, the
campaign mark, the working clock (kept under the pointer, as the row has no menu to give it to) and "a session of the
campaign" said once to a screen reader; the screenshots at 1280 and 390 pixels, dark and light, show it beside a chat
at work. `test_campaigns.py`: the conversation is marked while the campaign is there, no other chat is, and the mark
goes with the deleted campaign.
