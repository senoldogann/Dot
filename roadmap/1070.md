### 1070. A tap on a notification on the phone opens Dot at the chat it is about

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** The owner, on 2026-11-09: "telefonda approval iletisi geldiğinde ya da bildirim
tıkladığımda hedef sohbete gitmiyor ya da hiç açılmıyor".

Why: the service worker's tap called `WindowClient.navigate` on the window of Dot it found, then `focus`. For a
home-screen app of an iPhone in the background, `navigate` did nothing, or failed; a failure ended the promise before
`focus`, so the tap opened neither the chat nor Dot.

Now (`sw.js` `openAt`): the window found is brought to the front first, while the tap still allows it, and then told
the route (`dot:open-route`) with a channel to answer on. Dot's own page opens the route itself and answers (`main.js`:
a message of the worker of this origin, a route of the page's own, `#/…`, and nothing else); a window that does not
answer within 1.5 s is sent to the route, as the page of a Mac out of reach, which is served at Dot's own address and
runs no script of Dot's, never answers (the pre-push reviews: told by its address, it was taken for Dot's page and kept
the owner on it). With no window of Dot open, one opens at the route.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_phone.py` (the real worker tells the page a route, which it opens; a message of another
kind, or a route of no page of Dot's, changes nothing; it fails without the page's listener). A tap itself cannot be
played in a test: bringing a window to the front needs one. On the owner's iPhone: a tap on an approval's notification.
