### 1726. DotNext's chat, whole: the work log, the ask dock in the composer's place, the composer, the queue and its edit, the inspector

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Wave N2 of the iPhone rewrite, the app's part (`ios/Next`), on item 1725's engine.

What was built:
- The transcript: the owner's bubble (85 %, folded at twice its folded height with "Show full message"), Dot's answer as
  markdown (tables and code scroll sideways, code has Copy, links underlined in the ink), tool rows as one tight 28 pt
  block (shimmer while running, red ✕ when failed, a tap opens arguments, output and screenshots, a long press copies),
  the fold line ("2 dk 14 sn çalıştı · 3 dosya okudu, 2 komut çalıştırdı"), the plan card, the helper group with each
  helper's step, Dot's notes as Dot's rows, the asks' anchors, the failed-run card with Try again, the usage-limit
  card, reactions with their meaning (👍 👎 ✋ 📌 🔁 ❓) and Copy under each answer; where the chat starts (who, which Mac,
  when) so a short chat stands at the top; per-chat scroll memory, following only near the end, a debounced jump circle.
- The ask dock (PLAN.md §13 B1, §20) in the composer's place: count and Previous/Next for several asks, fold back to the
  composer, Stop in it while the run can stop, approvals (the whole summary and target, Allow once / Always allow in
  this chat / Deny), questions (options, several, free text, Send answer / Skip), hand-offs (what to do on the Mac),
  secrets in a secure field (`POST /api/approvals/{id}/secret`, at least 8 characters, a short code as a message; the
  value is kept nowhere on the phone), the phases' words, the draft and its photos kept beneath it.
- The composer: compact glass pill at rest, field and toolbar when expanded (always at the accessibility sizes), + menu
  (Photos, Camera, Files, Scan a document, Paste image), the model · effort chip from the daemon's models (the chat's
  next follow-up), on-device dictation (moved from the old app), Send / Queue / Steer / Stop with the other way on a long
  press and as a VoiceOver action, ⌘↩, `/` and `@` suggestions, per-chat drafts; the "Follow-up while Dot works"
  setting (Queue, the default, or Steer).
- The queue sheet as the owner's reference shots: rows with a "Send now" ink pill, a tap edits in the composer under
  "Editing queued message · Cancel" with a ✓ (files kept; Cancel brings the live draft back), swipe Remove with Undo,
  drag and the VoiceOver actions Move up / Move down, a held queue with Resume queue.
- Header and inspector: "Dot · <Mac>" or the phase ("Waiting on you", "Working · 41 dk 15 sn", "Failed") under the title,
  the glass capsule (changes and files, the agents, Stop with a confirmation); the inspector with the run, the model of
  this chat, tokens, context used, the plan, the helpers, the steps no undo can take back, the changed files (QuickLook of
  each diff) and the chat's loops.
- States: cold, syncing, cached, reconnecting, offline with nothing saved, key refused, deleted, failed run, usage limit.
- §19: helper titles on one line with the state on the second, short titles from goals, one duration shape with no
  abbreviation dot, the iPad badge in amber, the Response streaming switch on the radio rows' inset; Settings' Emergency
  stop calls the daemon's failsafe after a confirmation and says whether it reached the Mac.
- Debug-only fixtures of the chat (`-DotNextScene n2-…`) for the shots and the UI tests; the e2e daemon's new script
  words (`approve:`, `question:`, `secret:`, `tools:`, `fail:`) and longer waits.
