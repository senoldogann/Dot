### 1161. An act's expectation written beside its steps is the last step's

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** In the speed set with traces (item 1159), two attempts of fifteen wrote `expect_text`
beside the steps of a `browser_act` call rather than on a step; each call was refused ("Extra inputs are not
permitted") and written again, a model round trip spent for nothing.

- `core/outcomes.py` `expectation_on_last_step`: a top-level `expect_text` or `expect_url` of an act call is moved onto
  its last step when that step has none, the plain meaning of words the page is to show after the batch.
  `browser_act` and `desktop_act` read their arguments through it. When the last step has its own, the call is left
  as written and refused as before: two expectations are not merged.

`dotx eval check`, and the speed set before and after
(`deepseek-v4.1-flash`, five tasks three times): calls refused for their arguments went from 2 in 15 attempts to 0,
15 of 15 passed both times, 6.1 model calls an attempt both times; the wall time moved within the run-to-run noise
(26.7 s and 29.5 s, the model's first token 3.3 s and 4.3 s).
