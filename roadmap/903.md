### 903. A moment with a link no longer loses the night's change

**Status: done (2026-11-01).** On 2026-11-01 the nightly step failed with "the proposer failed in the domain scope: a
moment cannot be recorded: a link": the proposer wrote a link in one moment of its proposal three times in a row, and the
change its other moments explained was lost. A moment only says why a change is made; no run reads it. `proposal_check`
(`agent/review.py`) now keeps the moments that may be recorded and leaves out the others, and asks the proposal again only
when none may be. What may not be recorded is still never stored: the moment left out is dropped, not cut.

alone; one with no clean moment is refused as before.
