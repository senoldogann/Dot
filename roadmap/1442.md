### 1442. The iPhone app's inbox of what waits on the owner, with its badge

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** A run that waits for the owner (an approval, a question, a secret, a hand-off) was told only
inside its own chat and by the chat list's "Needs you" group of chats: the owner had no one place to see everything Dot
asks and answer it, and the app's icon showed no number. The daemon now reads all of it in one list (`GET /api/needs-you`,
items 1422 and 1423); this is the app's side. Research of 2026-11-23 (an agent app's "Needs you" list: decisions with the
recommended option marked, permission requests, the answer where the ask is) and the design review of 2026-11-24 (a row
that carries the ask, not the title twice).

What changed: `Core/NeedsYou.swift` reads the daemon's list (`NeedsYouList`: an item that does not read, or a kind this build
does not know, is left out or kept as `other`; `count` still says how many wait, so the badge is right) and makes the rows
(`NeedsYouRules.rows`: newest first, who asks, the chat's title, what is asked in words). An **approval** says what Dot wants
to do by `ApprovalPresentation` (a line that starts "Run: " is a command; any other is "Dot wants to do this"), with
**Allow once** and **Deny**; a **question** shows its options as buttons, the recommended one marked, or a field for words
when it offers none, with Skip; both answer through the outbox as `.decision` (`POST /api/approvals/{id}/decision`, the route
the chat screen's card uses), so an answer given with no link is kept and sent later, and the row says "Your answer is on its
way" meanwhile. A **secret** is never answered on the phone (the daemon's decision): its row says "Dot needs a secret: enter
it on your Mac", `decision(...)` is nil for it whatever is asked, and it has no field or button that takes a value; a
hand-off and a kind this build does not know are answered in the chat. **Allow once is not offered for an approval whose
line is as long as the daemon cuts a title** (`TITLE_CHARS` 120, cut with no mark): that line may be the start of a longer
one, and the row says to read all of it in the chat (Deny stays; this was added because a list is the one place a command
is allowed from without the chat's whole card, and the review of item 1392 had caught a card that cut a command). `AwarenessModel` keeps the last list in
`awareness.json` of the cache folder and draws it at once, reads it on start, on foreground and link, on a pull to refresh
(`NativeStore.refreshEverything`), when an alert arrives (`willPresent`) or a push wakes the app (`refreshInBackground`),
and on the stream's notices that change it (`approval_requested`, `approval_decided`, `question_left`, `run_finished`;
others leave it), after each outbox outcome, one read at a time; a read that fails leaves the list and says in the inbox that it
was read before. The app icon's number follows `count` after every read (`UNUserNotificationCenter.setBadgeCount`; the
daemon also sets `aps.badge` on every alert; a read that failed leaves the number). The chat list has one flat card with a
hairline at its top while anything waits (`NeedsYouEntry`: the count on a badge and what the newest asks), above the chats'
own "Needs you" group, which stays; it opens `NeedsYouView`, whose rows open their chats (`pendingRunId`).

The pre-push review found two things, fixed in a commit after it: the guard counted characters (graphemes) where the daemon
cuts by code points (`plain_line(...)[:120]`), so a line of combining marks or an emoji sequence that was cut reached the
phone as fewer than 120 characters and passed for a whole one (it counts `unicodeScalars` now); and an option was known by its
label, so two options that say the same words drew and answered as one button (each has the place it holds in the list as its
identity now).

Checked: `ios/DotTests/NeedsYouTests.swift` (21 cases: the daemon's own example from `docs/phone.md`, an unknown kind and an
