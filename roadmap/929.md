### 929. The On its own card folds to its head

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner ("On its own alanını collapse uncollapse olmasını sağlar mısın"):
the card on Schedules is long (the lead, the model that decides, the list, the box), and the page has no way to put it
out of sight.

- `server/static/js/initiativecard.js`: the card's title is a fold button (`aria-expanded`, a chevron, as the other folds
  of the pages), the count and the switch beside it outside the button, so that the switch never folds the card; the
  rest folds away. The choice is kept per browser (`dot.fold.initiative`), open the first time.
- `server/static/css/settings.css`: the title button set in the head as the panel's title, the folded rest keeping the
  panel's gaps, and no hairline under a folded head.
- `docs/proactive.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_initiative.py::test_the_card_on_schedules_switches_it_and_keeps_the_list`, at 1280 and
390 wide: the title folds the list and the box away while the switch and the count stay in sight, the chevron turns,
the fold is kept through a reload and opens again. The two screenshots were looked at.
