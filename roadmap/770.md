### 770. A paper buy has its stop and its target, watched day and night

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner, after Dot's own trading run told them that it could place no
stop: a trade's stop waited for the next hourly run, up to 59 minutes, which is no stop at all. "If the agent does not
work 24/7, what is the point?"

**The change.** `trading_order` takes `stop_price` (required for a buy) and `target_price`, judged against the prices
when the buy is placed (the stop under the bid, the target over the ask). On the paper account the buy's fill is kept as
an exit (`trading_exits`: the size bought, the stop, the target). `scheduler.runner.Scheduler` ticks
`trading.watch.TradingWatch` with its other parts, every 15 seconds: it reads the quotes of the products with open exits
(one request a product) and, once the bid is at an exit's stop or target, places a market sell of what the exit holds
through `Trading.place`, then tells the owner (`notify.owner.tell_owner`). An exit's sell (`OrderRequest.closing`) is
held by none of the limits (`limits.refusal(closing=True)`: a stop a cap could hold protects nothing), counts against
the day like any other, and need not be of an allowed product any more.

Exits are the paper account's only. Eight pre-push reviews each found an edge of Dot selling real money by itself (a key
not checked for a moment, a buy whose end was not known, a run stopped while it bought); the owner chose to keep this
item to paper and to have what Dot sells by itself on their Coinbase account come as an item of its own, reviewed on its
own, before they switch to live. A live buy keeps no exit: its question says Dot does not sell at its stop on the
Coinbase account yet, and so does its answer to the run.

What holds an exit's sell, the exit open for a later look (`ExitHeld`): trading off or switched to live, the emergency
stop, and Coinbase not taking orders of the product, even when one comes while the watch reads the prices (the pre-push
review: it was failed for good, never watched again once the owner released the stop). A refused sell leaves it
`failed`; a sell that may have gone out without saying what it did is not tried again (it could sell twice): `failed`,
the owner told. One that never went out (no order of it kept: the prices could not be read first) leaves the exit open
for the next look, and the look goes on to the other exits; held at every look for five minutes (`HELD_TOLD_S`), it is
told to the owner once, and again if it is held again after being released. A sell by hand takes from the product's open
exits, oldest first. Every change of an exit (its sell and how it ended, a move, the paper account's start closing it)
is made under the order lock, from the exit as it stands then (the pre-push review: a move that waited for the prices
wrote back an exit the watch had sold meanwhile, open again to sell a later buy's holding): an exit's sell sells what
the exit holds when its turn comes, at the stop or target it has then, never what a sell by hand took meanwhile;
starting the paper account again closes its exits. `trading_exit` moves an exit's stop or target, judged as a buy's are;
`trading_portfolio` lists the open exits; the prompt's Trading section says every buy names its stop, sold at on the
paper account, and a stop is never moved away from the price.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_trading.py`: a stop over the bid and a target under the ask refused; a buy's exit kept,
nothing sold until reached, sold at its stop when what it holds is worth more than one order's cap and the day's cap is
used up; a target taken; sells by hand taking from the oldest exit and closing it; no sell while the emergency stop is
engaged (the exit stays open) nor while live is in use (it waits, and sells once paper is in use again); a sell that
never went out leaving its exit open while another product's exit sells, told once after five minutes, and selling once
the prices can be read; an exit's sell read before a sell by hand taking only what that left, and nothing once one
closed it; the paper account's start closing its exits, a later buy's holding not sold at an old stop; the emergency
stop, trading off, the account switched and Coinbase halting the product, each coming while the prices were read,
holding the sell and leaving the exit open, sold once nothing holds it; a held sell told once after five minutes, again
after it was released and held again, and again after the emergency stop; a move and the watch's sell of one exit
waiting for each other, in either order, the sold exit never reopened and a stop moved under the bid not sold at; an
exit moved within the prices and refused past them; the watch telling the owner once, and not looking again before its
time; the order tool refusing a buy with no stop and showing the stop in the question; a live buy keeping no exit, its
question and answer saying so.
