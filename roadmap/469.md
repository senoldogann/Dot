### 469. A rule is tried on past steps before it is added

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The sixth of the nine ideas taken from OpenBot (item 449 has the plan), its second part:
"try a rule on past steps before adding it". Until now a rule, written by hand or drafted from a sentence, was added
blind. **As built.** The design's preview (its "c1"). Its usage line on every rule ("c2": `GET /api/rules/usage`)
follows as its own item. The shadow state, a rule that only watches, is not built: it needs a fifth behaviour through
the policy, the drafting and the page, all on a human path, for what the history of the steps already shows of any tool
Dot has used. The design leaned on a refactor of `policy/engine.py` that the "strictest" pull request owns: the try-out
matches with the engine's own `match_rule`, read, never changed, so no human path is touched, and
`tests/unit/test_rule_replay.py` checks that the rule it finds deciding, and its answer, are what `decide_past_review`
gives, so that the strictest change (a hand-off rule never shadowed, which the design's "a hand-off candidate wins"
assumed) fails it until the try-out follows. The store finds where the days start by halving the event ids
(`first_event_id_since`; the events have no index on the time and are never pruned by themselves, so no read walks the
table) and reads the steps (`tool_steps`: the `tool_result` events whose action reached the policy, newest first, with
the flags of their action, whether their run was in yolo, whether it was tainted when the gate decided them, as
`gate.tainted_after` reads it, and for a step the gate did not let through, the source and verdict of the `policy` event
of its call, looked for among the run's own last 50 events) by pages of 1000, at most 20,000 steps and two seconds, each
page counted in a worker thread, so that the event loop, which serves the pages and the runs, takes its turn between two
matches (one match, which keeps the interpreter while it runs, it still waits out); `partial` says when steps were left.
`server/rule_replay.py` counts what the rule fits; which of those an earlier rule decides first; the steps of yolo runs
apart, as yolo asks nothing whatever the rules say; the steps settled before any rule (the always-on review, a research
run, Dot's own files, money and credentials, the guard of messages and posts, a spend within its caps, the pace), but
for a hand-off rule over the guard or a spend, which the policy lets take their place; and, against what the policy said
then, whether it would ask more, less or the same, with the taint of that moment. A step with no decision on record
(held by another run, refused by the review model, from before item 468, or an OpenCode agent's, whose results do not
keep one: a follow-up) is counted as such. It keeps the newest 20 steps it fits with their summaries masked and cut; a
pattern that takes longer than the daemon allows on a past step stops it with the problem said, as the daemon would
refuse the step. `POST /api/rules/preview` writes nothing; a pattern that is not one is 422 as for a rule added. The
form says it in a few lines (`rulepreview.js`) with the steps leading to their runs; a drafted rule is tried at once,
and a change of the rule, or its adding, puts the try-out away, an answer that comes after a change being left unshown.
Measured: on a copy of the owner's store, the 8,284 steps of the last 30 days in 0.12 s, a page of 1000 in 9 ms; 20,000
steps that all stopped at the gate with no call to find, in 0.50 s. 7,966 of the owner's steps ran in yolo, so a try-out
that counted them as decided would have told of questions no yolo run asks. Yolo is untouched: nothing a run does or
asks changes. Tests: `tests/unit/test_rule_replay.py` (fits, an earlier rule first, yolo and the checks before the rules
apart, read from a step's record or its policy event, the comparison for a hand-off, an allow and a pre-approval, the
taint of the moment, the rule and answer the policy gives, the newest twenty, a count ending at its deadline, a pattern
that takes too long), `tests/smoke/test_rule_preview.py` (the store's start of the days; the route on two seeded runs
with their calls and policy events, nothing written, a bad pattern and no days refused; the Rules page at 1280 px light
and 390 px dark as a touch screen: the lines and the steps, no "null", a change puts it away, a step leads to its run),
`tests/smoke/test_rule_preview_load.py` (20,000 calls read, or the newest said, while the daemon answers; a pattern slow
on every step stopped at the budget, the daemon answering meanwhile), `tests/smoke/test_rules.py` (a drafted rule tried
at once, put away once added; both checked to fail without the change), `tests/js/rulepreview.test.js`. Docs:
`docs/safety.md`, `docs/daemon.md`.
