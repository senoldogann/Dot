### 1236. Every write of the store runs under its write lock

**Status: done (2026-11-19).** The store has one connection for all its writers, and a write runs alone on it under
`@_writing`, so that a unit of several statements is committed whole. The code review of 2026-11-19 (findings S2-01,
X3-05, A4-02) found three methods that executed and committed without it: the guarded update of a campaign
(`_update_campaign_in`, behind its plan, its next session, its stop and its resume), a campaign achieved while still
active (`achieve_campaign` for the session's claim), and the harness steps closed at start. Their commit, made while
another writer's unit was part way, committed that half unit. Each now writes in a method under the lock; a read that
follows stays outside it. A campaign that is not there is still an error when it is
achieved, as for the other guarded updates.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_store_write_lock.py` (every method of the store that commits holds the lock, or is called
only by methods that do, and none that holds it calls another that takes it; it named the three before the change), the campaign and harness-step tests (42).
