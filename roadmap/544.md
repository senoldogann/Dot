### 544. The side panel's sections scroll on their own, and Files and Details leave the Run tab

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** Asked by the owner: each area of the right panel should scroll within itself, and the
Files and Details parts go if they are not needed. They were not: Files listed the conversation's files, which open
from their chips and paths in the conversation, and the files the chat wrote are Artifacts' (item 539); Details held
the run's state, which the top bar's face shows, the model, reasoning and approvals the composer shows, and its work
and time, which a glance does not need.

**As built.**
- `static/js/views/run.js`: the Files card and the observer that kept it up to date are gone; the Run tab is the
  Recap, the context of item 539 and More, closed the first time, which holds the run's facts, how it was set up and
  its cost. `dom.js` keeps `fileSource` to itself and drops `FILE_BUTTONS`, which nothing reads any more.
- `static/js/details.js`: the facts are one list (`#panel-run-details`, "Details" to a screen reader), the state, the
  agent, the model, the reasoning, the approvals, the goal mode, the campaign, the work, the tokens, the context, the
  time, the parent, the mode, the start and the id, under More.
- `static/css/run.css`: in a tab of several sections each body scrolls on its own once it holds more than two fifths
  of the screen (`max-height: 40dvh`), its head staying over it; a tab of one section (Changes, Plan, Agents) has the
  panel to itself, and the Screen tab keeps its own size (`inspector.js` marks each tab's panel with `data-tab`).
- `docs/daemon.md` and `docs/runs.md` say where the facts stand and that the files open from the conversation.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_side_panel.py`: the Run tab shows Recap, Skills & MCP and More, the facts hidden under
More until it opens and the run's state and id among them, a section of the Run tab scrolls on its own and the Plan
tab's one section is not held to a share of the screen. `tests/smoke/test_file_panel.py`: the Run tab lists no files,
and a file opened from its chip stays beside the conversation as the chat goes on. `test_agents.py` and
`test_quiet_compaction.py` pass as they were: the facts keep their id under More. `test_side_panel.py` passes again: it
failed on `main` since items 536 and 539 put Recap and Skills & MCP over the old Details and More.
