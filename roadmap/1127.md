### 1127. Showing or folding the steps leaves the conversation where it was

**Status: done (2026-11-11).** The owner: pressing Show the steps or Hide the steps threw the chat up on their iPhone.
The steps of every turn open or fold above what was pressed, and Safari has no scroll anchoring, so the conversation
moved under the finger. The page now keeps what was pressed (the line of what the run does, or an answer's button; the
last one pressed in the conversation, as iOS focuses neither) where it stood: it measures it before and after and
scrolls the conversation by the difference, with the browser's own anchoring (Chrome's) put off for that move, which
would count it twice (`views/run.js`).

Checked: `test_work_status.py`, `test_chat_switch_scroll.py` and `test_phone_layout.py`; on the owner's iPhone after
the deploy.
