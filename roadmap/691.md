### 691. "Read-only" or "salt okunur" in a task no longer turns it into research

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** On Auto (item 495) the words of a message settle its mode, and `mode_words.py` read the bare
`read[- ]only` and `salt okunur` as an ask to only look. "Make config.toml read-only", "create a read-only API key" and
"dosyayı salt okunur yap" ran as research, which cannot do the task, and the run could only say so. The module's own
comment holds that only an ask to change nothing at all, or to research only, is read there.

**The change.** The two bare alternatives are gone. English, as an ask to the run is put:
`stay|remain|work|operate|run|go|keep things` (optionally `in`) before `read-only` where a sentence or a clause starts
(the start of the message or of a sentence, or after a comma, a semicolon, a colon, a dash, a bracket, "and", "then"
or "but": "look into the build and stay read-only", "investigate the crash; work read-only"), and `keep it` only where
the message opens with it; in both, what follows `read-only` is a mark, the end or a word that goes on with the
sentence, never a noun ("run read-only checks" is a task). After a task names a thing, "Keep it read-only", "and keep
it read-only" or "the DB must stay read-only" are about the thing. The code review of this item took four rounds over
these phrasings; the rule is now fixed by who the verb's subject is and by one class of the places a clause starts,
not by more spellings. Turkish: `salt okunur` followed by `kal`, `çalış`, `ol`, `bak` or `incele`, or by `olarak`,
`şekilde`, `biçimde`, `modda` or `modunda` and one of those verbs or `araştır` or `oku`, with the Turkish letters or
without. And "read-only" (also "please", "be", "mode") or "salt okunur" as a label that opens a sentence or the message
and is followed by a punctuation mark, "please" or "lütfen" or the end ("Read-only: find out why the build is
slow"), where it cannot be about a thing the task makes. `only_looks` returns the earliest words of either.
`docs/daemon.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_mode_words.py`: the asks above, in English and Turkish, are research; "make config.toml
read-only", "create a read-only API key", "give the new hire read-only access", "set the file to read-only", "turn on
read-only mode in the app's settings", "dosyayı salt okunur yap", "config.toml dosyasını salt okunur olarak ayarla",
"salt okunur bir API anahtarı oluştur" and "klasörü salt okunur modda bağla" are tasks; the earlier cases still hold.

**Not done.** A task whose sentence starts with the label ("Read-only: ..." followed by a change) is research; the words
cannot tell it from the ask, and research says so when it cannot do the task.
