### 1312. Signing out of Google keeps the token until Google took it back, and the page says when it did not

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The review of the server found that `Accounts.forget` forgot the account's tokens from
the Keychain before it asked Google to revoke the refresh token, that a revoke Google refused was only logged
(`sign_out_not_revoked provider=google status=400`, its body dropped) and that the page said "signed out" all the same.
A grant that Google did not end stayed listed under the owner's account, with a refresh token that nothing could end any
more.

Signing out still always works on the Mac: the account leaves the list and its place in the Keychain whatever Google
answers. What changed is what happens to the token. Before the revoke it is parked under a place of its own in the
Keychain (`pending-revoke-<id>`) with a record in the store (`oauth.pending_revokes`, no token in it). Google taking it
(200), or calling it `invalid_token` (400: already revoked or expired, nothing left to end), clears both. Anything else
(a network error, a 5xx, another refusal) leaves them, and the daemon asks again as it starts and every hour
(`keep_revoking`, a task of the daemon: a round that raises anything is logged with its traceback as
`sign_out_revoke_retry_crashed` and the next hour's still runs) until Google takes it or seven days passed, when the tokens are forgotten and
`sign_out_revoke_given_up` is logged. The log of a refusal now carries Google's `error` field. A sign-out that cannot park
(the Keychain or the store refuses) goes on with the one try it has. Microsoft has no revoke call for a client like Dot's,
so its tokens are forgotten at once and its sign-out says it was not revoked.

`DELETE /api/accounts/{provider}/{address}` answers `{revoked, notice, providers}` instead of the bare list; `notice` is
"Signed out here; Google may still list Dot: remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions" when the provider did not end
the grant (Microsoft: `account.live.com/consent/Manage`, not checked against Microsoft's pages), and the Accounts card shows it in
place of "is signed out".

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_oauth_accounts.py` against a fake HTTP transport and a throw-away keychain file (a refused
revoke keeps the token parked, names Google's error in the log and not the token, and the next round takes it;
`invalid_token` is revoked and keeps nothing; a Google that cannot be reached is asked every round for six days and let go
after seven; Microsoft is signed out at once; the route answers `revoked` and the notice; the daemon's loop settles a
pending sign-out and stops with the daemon), and the existing sign-in, refresh and sign-out tests.
