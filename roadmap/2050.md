### 2050. The old iPhone app's dictation loses no word on a pause either

**Status: done (2026-11-28).** Item 2048's cause in the old app (`ios/Dot`), which stays installed until Dot Next takes
its place: `Dictation` took each result of `SFSpeechRecognizer` as the whole text of the go (`heard = words`), and
since iOS 18 a pause closes the utterance inside the task (a non-final result with `speechRecognitionMetadata`) and the
next results hold only the words after it, so a pause replaced the box's dictated words with the new ones.

What changed: `Core/DictationRules.swift` gets `DictationTranscript` (the closed utterances in order and the live one;
the same rules as DotEngine's of item 2048, here as the old target does not link DotEngine), and `Dictation` passes
`isFinal || speechRecognitionMetadata != nil` and hands the box `transcript.text`. What was typed before dictating, and
typing that stops it, stay as they were (`DictationText.update`).

How it was checked: `DictationRulesTests.testAPauseKeepsTheWordsSaidBeforeIt` with the results measured for item 2048
(English with a pause, Turkish with two, an empty final after a pause, words said again, a revision), run on a
simulator with `xcodebuild test-without-building -only-testing:DotTests/DictationRulesTests` after one
`build-for-testing` of the `Dot` scheme (`ios.yml` builds and tests Dot Next only).
