### 1348. A queued message is moved by an up and a down arrow, not by a drag

**Status: done (2026-11-23).** The owner's word of 2026-11-23: on his phone the queue is reordered by dragging a row by
hand, and the drag takes the swipe that scrolls the chat.

The row of the queue (`queue.js`) was `draggable` with a grip: item 148 drag and drop with the arrow keys on the grip, item
1079 an up arrow on a touch screen only, since an iPhone does not drag. A row that is `draggable` on a touch screen takes the
press that begins a swipe (a long press lifts the row instead), in a list that is itself a scroller in the dock.

- Each row has an up and a down arrow (`.queue-arrow`, `aria-label` "Move queued message up" / "…down", the title "Move up" /
  "Move down"); the one that points past an end of the queue is disabled. They are buttons, so Enter and Space press them;
  the focus stays on the message when it moves, going to the row's other arrow, then its pencil, when the arrow it held
  is disabled at the new place. They call the reorder as before (`PUT /threads/{id}/queue`, `{ids}`).
- The hit area is 32 px square with a pointer and 44 px on a phone (`@media (pointer: coarse), (max-width: 720px)`, where the
  row is 44 px tall and its list may be 14 rem or 30 % of the screen); the icon stays 15 px.
- The drag is gone: the grip, `draggable`, `dragstart`, `dragover`, `drop`, `dragend`, the drop line and the 1079 button.
  The list does not chain its overscroll to the dock (`overscroll-behavior: contain`).

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_queue.py` (the arrows' labels, sizes and ends, Enter and Space, the focus, the order in the store;
no row draggable; at 1280 and 390 wide, light and dark), `tests/smoke/test_chat_one_scroller.py` (WebKit with touch at 390 and
430: 44 px arrows, taps reorder the queue in the store, nothing draggable); `ruff`, `npm run check`.
