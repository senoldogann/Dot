### 1323. A deleted chat's runs leave their numbers in a run ledger, so that the statistics outlive the chats

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The owner asked on 2026-11-22 which loops to change so that Dot gets better. A read of
their store found the measurement under every loop thinning out: deleting a chat deletes its runs, so the 146 runs
counted for 2026-11-16..21 on 2026-11-21 were 78 a day later (most of the gone ones were review chats the owner
tidied), and the scorecard, the nightly counts and any later "did this change help" read only what is left.

Deleting a chat still deletes its runs with their words. In the same transaction, before their events go, each run
leaves one row in the new table `run_ledger` (schema version 3, copied before the step as every version is): its
source, model, effort, status, end reason, times, steps, tokens, parent, agent, campaign, the schedule of its chat, the
harness version it took, its tool calls, how many failed or did not parse, its stalls and compactions, and when it was
deleted. No goal, no answer, no event.
`dotx stats` (`collect_statistics`, `collect_run_costs`) and the token total of `collect_spend` read the runs and the
ledger together; the time, site and failure-cause tables cover only the runs still kept, as their events are gone.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_run_statistics.py` (a deleted scheduled chat leaves a row of numbers with its schedule and
tool counts, no text column, and the statistics count it), `tests/unit/test_store_schema.py` (version 3 pinned; a
version 2 database takes the ledger after a copy), `tests/unit/test_store_writes.py`.

Not done here, on purpose: the other proposals of that read (a recipe loop for repeated schedules, lesson counters, a
step budget for unattended runs) wait on numbers this ledger now keeps; schedules already write and read playbooks.
