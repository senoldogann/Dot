### 1840. Take a run's page over from the iPhone, and hand it back

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** As the web's "Use it here" (items 470, 475), from Dot Next's full-screen live view.

What must hold: Take over holds the run at its next step without bringing anything up on the Mac; the owner's taps,
drags, presses and keys reach the page where they are on the picture; Hand back lets the run go on; whatever the daemon
refuses (`[browser] remote_input` off, a step still going, a hold ended elsewhere) is said as it is. No daemon change:
the input path (`browser/remote_input.py`, the manager's hold) is used as the web uses it.

What was built (the code shipped in item 1839's commit, as the full screen draws it; this commit adds its end-to-end
check and its record):
- Engine (pure): the input events as the daemon takes them (`RemoteInputEvent`: move, down, up, wheel, key, text; a
  place is a fraction of the picture), the batches of at most 64 events and texts of at most 500 characters, the scroll
  of a drag on the page's own scale (`scrollFor`, within the daemon's 4000 CSS pixels), `runHold` from the run's
  `takeover` and `handback` events; `DaemonAPI.takeOverOnScreen`, `sendInput`, `handBack`.
- App: `RunControl` sends the events in order, one batch at a time (moves, scrolls and text wait 50 ms to go with the
  next, as `control.js` does), takes the page's width from each answer for the scroll's scale, and follows the run's own
  record (a hold that ended on the web or the Mac ends here; a run held elsewhere shows Hand back). On the canvas while
  held: a tap clicks (a second within 0.35 s and 24 points is a double click), one finger's drag scrolls, a press held
  0.45 s drags (and is never left down), two fingers move and zoom the picture. The keyboard button types on the page
  (Return is Enter, ⌫ Backspace; no autocorrection). A success haptic on taken and given back.
- Refusals: `remote_input` off says where to switch it on (Settings › Browser › "Use the page from the screen while I
  hold the task"); "still going" says "Dot is finishing a step; try again in a moment."; "not taken over" returns to
  Take over; a phone refusal says the Mac does not let a phone do it.

Checked: `RemoteInputTests` (the events' JSON, the limits, the scroll), `WatchAPITests` (input answer, a refused
takeover's words), `WatchLiveUITests` + `scripts/e2e_watch.sh`: the test taps the play page's button twice and its
field once on the picture, types "hi", hands back; the script fails unless the run's `handback` says it was held `by:
screen` and the page itself reports 2 clicks of its button and "hi" in its field (it sends its state to its own
server, `e2e_daemon.py`), not only Dot's count of what was sent.
