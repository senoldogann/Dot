### 886. The nightly step reads a field the review model left out as what leaving it out says

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner, with a screenshot of Self-improvement › Nightly learning, where
30 and 31 Oct both read *Failed*: "Self improvement sistemi sürekli error alıyor. Bir türlü çalışmıyor doğru şekilde.
Bu konunun da üstüne git ve problemleri düzelt, en iyi seviyeye getir."

What the store and `dotx.log` showed. Three nights since the fresh start of 2026-10-28:
- **29 Oct:** the base tried six tasks of the pool. That is the calibration item 719 does until enough tasks have
  headroom, and it worked as meant.
- **30 Oct:** the invariant scope's candidate was played on four tasks, and the base was kept because the candidate
  was unclear. The domain scope's proposer wrote its summary past 120 characters, twice. Item 840 cuts such a summary,
  and it is live. Here, though, the night was recorded *failed* for that scope.
- **31 Oct:** the review model, `ollama:glm-5.3-flash:cloud`, answered the domain scope twice without the fields the
  verdict requires:
  - the first time without `keep` and `tool_guidance`, with its summary past 120;
  - the second time without `habits`, `tool_guidance` and `moments`.

  Item 840's cut applies only when a text past its bound is the verdict's only fault, so it did not apply. The night
  failed in 7 seconds.

**What holds.**
- `HarnessProposal`:
  - `habits` or `tool_guidance` left out is no new text, and `moments` left out is none;
  - `keep` left out is whether no text was given;
  - a keep needs no summary, and is "keep";
  - a `keep: false` that gives no text is still asked again (`proposal_check`, item 587), since the text it meant may
    come then. A first draft read it as a keep, and `tests/unit/test_harness_roles.py` held it to the ask.
- `HarnessComposition`: `keep_base` left out is whether no text was given. Keeping the base needs no texts, decisions
  or summary, since the step does not read them then. `composition_check` takes a kept base with no texts and gives
  back the base's. A kept base given other texts is still asked again.
  - The first push filled the texts in blank, which the check then refused against a base that had text: the night
    would still have failed once a version was adopted. The pre-push review found it.
- `ExperienceDraft`: `entry` left out is none.
- What a change cannot do without is still asked again, and refused at the second answer as before: a proposal's
  summary, and a composition's texts. Item 840's cut now also applies to the first answer of the 31st, since its
  summary was its only fault left.
- The tools' schemas are unchanged: they still ask for every field, so the model is told what to send.
- A night on which a role failed stays recorded as failed and adopts nothing (item 588). Fewer nights fail now, since
  the answers that failed them are read.

**Where.** `agent/review.py`: `_blank_text`, and a `mode="before"` validator on `HarnessProposal`,
`HarnessComposition` and `ExperienceDraft`. `docs/harness.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_review_lenient.py`:
- the 31 Oct answers: the first is read as a change of the habits, its summary cut at 120; the second as a change with
  no text, which the check asks again;
- a keep with nothing else is a keep;
- a change with no summary is refused;
- the schema still requires every field;
- a composition that keeps the base with nothing else, or leaves out `keep_base` and gives no text, keeps it;
- one that changes the habits with no tool guidance is refused;
- `tests/unit/test_harness_roles.py`: against a base with text, a composition that keeps it and leaves the texts out
  comes back with the base's texts, and one that keeps it with other texts is asked again;
- an experience with no entry has none.

On main's `review.py` the proposer test fails.
