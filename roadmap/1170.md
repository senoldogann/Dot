### 1170. A page's read of a run leaves out the loop's snapshots

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** **Measured** on the owner's Dot, read-only: the loop stores a snapshot of a step's
whole context (`loop_state`, what a resumed run picks up) as an event of the run, and `GET /runs/{id}/events` and a run's stream
gave them to the page, which draws none (`static/js` reads no `loop_state`). Of the six earlier turns a 40-turn chat
reads as it opens, they were 647 of 1,295 KB, 17 of 128 KB on the wire (they pack well); a run at work sent one with
each step.

**Built.** `Store.list_events_without` leaves the kinds it is given out in the query itself, so that a page of
`limit` events is full whenever more are stored (the page stops reading at a short one). `UNREAD_BY_PAGES`
(`server/stream.py`) holds `loop_state`: `GET /runs/{id}/events`, the stored events of a run's stream and of the
streams of several runs leave it out, and the live ones skip it. The loop reads its newest snapshot back from the store
(`load_loop_state`, `Store.last_event`), not through the API; no other reader of the API or the streams reads it.

**What it does not do.** The snapshots are stored as before, and `dotx` and the evals read them from the store.

**Measured** as above: the six turns' events 1,295 KB before, 648 KB after; on the wire 128 KB before, 111 KB after.
The time a phone takes to parse what is left was not measured on the phone.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_unread_events.py` (no browser): five steps with a snapshot each, read three events at a
time through `GET /runs/{id}/events`: every step comes, every page but the last is full, no snapshot; the run's stream
has none; the store still gives the newest snapshot. `tests/smoke/test_chat_open_payloads.py`, in Chromium and WebKit:
the events a chat reads as it opens hold no snapshot while each turn draws its work. Both failed on `main`.
