### 717. An attempt that never started is the probe's failure, not the candidate's

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Asked by the owner:** "the self-improvement does not work at all". On
2026-10-21 the step played a candidate whose 16 attempts each ended `failed` in under a second, with no step and no
token: its runs never started. The contrast read them as sixteen failures and called the candidate worse on eight tasks;
the step recorded `keep`, as if the text had been tried and lost.

**What must hold.**

- An attempt that ended `failed` with no step and no token did not start (`Scorecard.started`). It is no evidence for
  or against the text: it is left out of the scores, and a task without its repeats of attempts that started is
  `unclear` (it never wins, it never loses).
- Why it did not start (the run's own words, cut to 300 characters) is kept with the card and said in the contrast the
  owner reads (`contrast_detail`), never in what a model reads (`contrast_text` says how many, not why).
- A step with an attempt that did not start, of the base or of a candidate, is `failed`, its error says so with the
  first reason, and how learning fares counts it.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_harness_step.py`: attempts that never started leave the candidate unclear, never worse,
with the reason in the owner's detail and not in a model's; a card tells a run that never started from one that failed.
`tests/smoke/test_harness_step.py`: a step whose candidate never started is `failed`, says why, and sets the candidate
aside as not judged.
