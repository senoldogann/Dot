### 930. The evening check-in: at the end of the day, what got done, what waits and the next step

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-02): of the ideas taken from Codync (an open source Grok
Bot alternative), its lead bot's check-in at 09:00 and 18:00, which follows up on what is open and offers the next
step: what the owner asked for as a CEO's status report and a Dot that thinks of the next step for them. The morning
brief (item 570) is the 09:00 half; the evening was missing.

**Built.**
- `proactive/checkin.py`: the brief's twin. A schedule of its own (`research`, notice always, its own chat "Evening
  check-in"), set by `GET`/`PUT /api/checkin`. Its firing reads Dot's record in a fence (`record_parts`: waits,
  objectives, work orders, failed runs, campaigns) and the runs of the owner's day that ended done since the last
  check-in (`done_lines`, their taint taken). It writes what got done, what waits, and one to three next steps for
  tomorrow, each with why; its first line is told as *Your evening check-in* (Turkish *Akşam yoklaman*).
- The scheduler fires it as it fires the brief and tells its end in its own words.
- Schedules › Evening check-in: a card of one line, its time and its switch (`checkincard.js`).

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_morning_brief.py`:
- the card makes a read-only schedule; its firing reads "Done today" with the day's run, in the fence, before its own
  goal, in its own chat, and is told in its own words;
- in a browser, on a desktop and on a phone, the card's time and switch set it, and the line stays inside the screen.
