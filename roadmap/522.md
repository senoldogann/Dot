### 522. A payment is the button that takes the money: "Pay $19.90", "Buy", "Order now", "Confirm and pay"

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** Found by the whole-project review.

- **What was missed.** `_FINANCIAL_ACTION` (`policy/elements.py`) knew "Pay" only as a whole label and "Buy" only in
  "Buy now". "Pay $19.90", "Buy", "Order now", "Confirm and pay" and "Rent HD $3.99" passed as plain clicks: no money
  hand-off, and the pre-approved caps never read their amounts.
- **What counts now.**
  - A call to pay at the start of a label or of a sentence in it, after a stop, a dot, a bar, a dash, a colon or a new
    line: "Pay $19.90", "Buy", "Order", "Order now", "Pre-order", "Öde", "Maksa", "Osta", "Lamp — $9.90 — Buy".
  - A call to pay anywhere in it: "Place order", "Confirm and pay", "Agree & pay", "Complete checkout", "Proceed to
    pay", "Place bid", "Add funds", "Şimdi öde", "Ödeme yap", "Onayla ve öde", "Vahvista tilaus", "Tilaa ja maksa".
  - A verb that offers something for an amount its label names (`_PRICED_ACTION`): "Rent HD $3.99", "Subscribe for
    €4.99/month", "Upgrade · 99 TL". The amount is read as the caps read it (`spend.find_amounts`).
  - "Order", "Book" and "Reserve" with the amount right after them or after words that only say when or what
    (`_BOOKING`, `_BOOKING_WORDS`: "now", "this room", "a table"), where an amount must start
    (`spend.starts_with_amount`): "Order · €23.50", "Book now · $199", "Reserve a table — €10". A row of past orders,
    an order's summary or a book's title has a number or other words there and is no payment ("Order 4521 · $45.00 ·
    Delivered", "Order summary · $45.00", "Book: Dune · $9.99").
  - `asks_to_pay` decides for a click's label and for a dialog (`dialog_is_financial`) alike.
- **What does not.** The way to the payment page: "Checkout", "Proceed to checkout" and "Continue to payment" stay
  plain clicks, so the run fills the order and the button that pays is the one handed to the owner. Nor does a label
  that only names money: "Payment methods", "Where to buy", "Order history", "Orders over $35 ship free", "Subscribe"
  without an amount (still a follow), "Ode to Joy".

Checked by:
- `tests/unit/test_spend.py`: the labels that ask for money are high risk and `financial`, in English, Turkish and
  Finnish; those that name money or lead to the payment page are not.
