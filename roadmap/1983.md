### 1983. DotNext's Memory: add a note, pin it, and bring a forgotten one back from the trash

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The phone's Memory page could read, search, edit and forget a note, but a forgotten note
could not be brought back from the phone, and no note could be added or pinned (PARITY-GAPS, G1b). The phone now does
what the Mac's web page does with them, on the daemon's own routes; nothing in the daemon changed.

What changed (the app and the engine only):
- `+` on the Memory page opens a sheet for a new note: its words and tags separated by commas (`POST /api/memories`,
  the owner's own note). When Dot already held a note with these words (`created: false`) the page says so.
- A note's sheet has Pin / Unpin (`PUT /api/memories/{id}/pin`); a pinned note never rests, and its row says Pinned.
- Memory › Trash (`GET /api/memories/trash`): the forgotten notes, newest first, with when each was forgotten; a tap
  opens the note with when it is deleted for good and Restore (`POST /api/memories/trash/{id}/restore`). Forget's
  confirmation now says the note can be restored. Delete for good, merge undo and the typed kind of item 1975 (open) are
  not on the phone yet.
- Rows show their whole words at the accessibility sizes.

Rules: every route is one the daemon already lets a phone's key use (none of them is in `refuse_from_a_phone`'s list);
a refusal would show as the page's failure banner with its Mac place. Nothing is deleted for good from the phone.

How it was checked: `MemoryNotesTests` (each call's method, path and body; the trash and the saved note decode);
`SettingsDesignUITests` on the new `settings-memory-trash` fixture (insets, 44 pt rows); `SettingsLiveUITests` against the
throwaway daemon with a phone's key: a note added with tags, pinned, forgotten, restored, and `e2e_settings.sh` read the
daemon's side (the note with its tags, the trash empty again); screenshots light, dark, Turkish and AX3. Not checked: the
small (SE) and Pro Max screens, and whether a pin survives the trash (the daemon decides).
