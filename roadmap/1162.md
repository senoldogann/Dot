### 1162. A task run's words stream as an update from the first word

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** The owner saw the words a run writes on the way to its calls come in as a message and
then go into the steps (item 1131 folds them there once the step ends with calls). The daemon's stream of words said
nothing of what they were until the step was over. In the owner's runs of the week to 2026-11-13, 443 of 498 steps with
words went on to calls, 53 had no call (sent back to finish, or a first step's answer) and 2 came with finish alone; all
151 runs were tasks.

- `agent/streammask.py` `delta_event`: each `delta` event says `update`, true outside a chat, where a step's words are
  not the answer (finish brings it, or OpenCode's last reply) unless the step ends with no call. Dot's own loop,
  Claude Code's runs on the owner's plan and OpenCode's all send it, the plan's being the owner's main one.
- `timeline.js`: the live message of a step whose deltas say `update` is drawn as an update from its first word
  (`sayKind`), hidden with the steps under "What it is doing" as the folded ones are; the step's end makes it a message
  when it calls nothing but finish. A live message ended by another step's words keeps its kind.
- `workstatus.js`: an update's words do not step the line aside, as a message's do: the line says the work while they
  stream.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_opencode_run_ends.py` (an OpenCode task's words are an update),
`tests/smoke/test_kept_live_run.py` (a task run's words stream as an update and end as a message when their
step calls finish alone), `tests/smoke/test_work_status.py` (an update's words keep the line, a message's step it aside),
`npm run check`.
