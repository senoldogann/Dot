### 1947. The review model reads the owner's instructions whole

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** B-code M8 of the review of 2026-11-26 (K3-05 of the code review of 2026-11-19).
`render_instructions` in `agent/review.py` gave the review model the twelve newest of the owner's instructions, each cut
to 1,500 characters from its middle. A pasted brief of 3,000 characters that says "never mail the client, only draft"
in its middle reached the model without that line, and an `auto` run's send of the mail was approved. In a long chat
the first instruction, the task itself, was dropped as well.

What must hold:
- In `auto`, the review model decides a step only from instructions it reads whole: none is cut in its middle, nor at
  its head or tail; when one does not fit, the owner decides. Head and tail with the constraints kept was weighed and
  not taken: what is a constraint cannot be told from its words reliably, and a whole instruction needs no guess. Only
  yolo's check of a tainted run, which asks nobody, is also shown the rest cut as before, so it never reads less than
  it did.
- The newest instruction is read whatever its length. The rest are read as they fit in a bound, so that a chat of many
  long messages does not grow every question without end: the first, then the others newest first.
- What the model did not read cannot approve a step: when an instruction is left out, an `auto` step comes to the owner
  without asking the model. A yolo run asks nothing new: in yolo the review model judges only a schedule's message or
  post in the owner's chat, whose "what it cannot tell goes to the owner" is the exception the freedom contract names,
  and yolo's check of a run that read outside content (which asks nobody) reads the instructions as they fit, its
  refusal standing as before. Free mode asks nothing and reads no instruction.

What changed:
- `agent/review.py`: `_read_instructions` takes the newest, then the first and the others newest first, each whole,
  while they fit in `_INSTRUCTIONS_CHARS` (60,000 characters, about four times what twelve cut instructions were);
  `render_instructions` numbers them as they stand, shows the newest twelve of the rest cut to 1,500 characters from
  their middle as before, marked `[cut]`, and names any others; `review_reads_instructions` says whether every one is
  read whole. The independent security review found that, without the cut ones, a long newest message left a short
  "never email anyone" out of yolo's check of a tainted run, which asks nobody: the review now reads at least what it
  read before, every instruction of the old view in it, whole or cut as it was.
- `agent/gate.py`: `_review` hands the step to the owner without the review model when an instruction is left out, with
  why on the card (`_INSTRUCTIONS_UNREAD`).
- `docs/autonomy.md` says how the instructions are read, and the limit it brings: once the owner's messages to a run
  pass 60,000 characters together, every step the review model would have answered comes to them for the rest of the
  run.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_review_instructions.py` (a 3,000-character brief with "Never mail the client" in its middle
reaches the review model whole, and the model denies the send; which instructions are read and named when they do not

review),
