### 1905. DotNext marks an opened chat seen, so it leaves "unread" in the list

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner's test of build 5: a chat stays "unread" after they opened it and came back to
the list.

Cause: the phone never told the Mac. The web's sidebar sends `PATCH /api/threads/{id} {seen: true}` for its open chat
(`sidebar.js` `seeOpenChat`); DotEngine had `DaemonAPI.markSeen` and no caller. The daemon side holds (`seen_at` moves
the chat's revision, so the list's stream tells the new row).

What was built (DotEngine): `chatsToMarkSeen` (pure) gives the chats open on the phone whose row says unread, once per
revision of the row; the `Supervisor` marks them seen when a chat opens, after each look at the list (a frame or a read)
and when the app comes back to the screen, never while it is away; a mark that fails is tried at the next look. A run
that ends while its chat is open is so marked seen too, as on the web.

Checked: `SeenTests` (an opened chat is marked and its row reads read; a run that ends while the chat is open is marked
again; a closed chat keeps its unread), red without the change; DotEngine's 138 tests.
