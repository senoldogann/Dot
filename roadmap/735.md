### 735. What a run's first step carries is measured, and a fresh home's is held under a budget

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Asked by the owner:** do in Dot what another agent's maker did with its own tools
("your agents use the tools 4x better while using 79% less context": an eval made of real failures, a hill climb of the
tool descriptions per model, then a trim with no regression). The first part: measure. On 2026-10-27 a fresh scheduled
run of the owner's sent 50,000 to 61,000 tokens to its model before any work (49,000 two weeks before), a fresh home's
about 20,000, and nothing said where the rest came from (the owner's 8 MCP servers and 125 of their tools, 46 skills and
158 of plugins, 55 learned skills, 17 agents, notes).

**The change.** `agent/context_parts.py`: the first step of every run records a `context_parts` event, its request in
characters, the system prompt by the heading of each section, the tools' schemas by group, the conversation by role,
and the whole in Dot's estimate of tokens. `dotx stats` ends with the medians of the newest 200 runs of its window, the
five largest sections and groups named. `tests/smoke/test_context_budget.py` plays a fresh home's task run and holds
it under a budget: 20,500 tokens in all, the system prompt 6,900, the tools 13,700 (measured 20,221, 6,692 and 13,502;
the core tools' schemas 20,406 characters, the browser's 11,440).

**Next.** An eval of tool use made of the failures the owner's store holds (two weeks: `browser_wait_text` failed 30%
of 237 calls, `browser_upload` 15%, `web_fetch` 11% of 865, `shell` 164 of 3,692), the descriptions climbed on the
main model (OpenCode Go, the owner's keys), then trimmed under a lower budget with no regression.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_context_parts.py`: a request is parted by section, group and role; the stats name the
medians and the largest parts; a line in a fenced block (a skill's `# install` comment) is no section, so that its words
are never kept as a section's name. `tests/smoke/test_context_budget.py`: a fresh home's first step is recorded once and
is within its budget, the safety rules among its sections.
