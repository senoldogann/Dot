### 1742. The prompt cache says why it missed, and a chat that comes back within the hour keeps its Claude cache for an hour

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The market research of 2026-11-24 (M4 topic 8, TOP 10 #3; audit ARC-04 and ARC-05) named the
prompt cache the best-evidenced cost lever (41 to 80 % of cost, 13 to 31 % of the time to the first token in a
cross-provider study) and asked for three things: a prompt prefix that is byte-stable, a longer cache time for schedules
and watchers that repeat within the hour, and the share of a run's input read from the cache, with the cause of a cold
step. Most of the first was done by items 404, 1217 and 1219; this item measured what is left before building.

**Measured first** (the owner's store, read-only, 14 days to 2026-11-24, 10,236 answered calls, from the `model_call`, `context_parts` and
`tool_groups` events and the runs' chats):

- Step input read from the cache: 94.3 % of 1.04 billion tokens (OpenCode Go DeepSeek 9,391 calls 94.3 %; Anthropic 38
  calls 86.9 %; Antigravity 31 calls 89.8 %). The side calls (progress, goal check, lessons, recap, compaction) are asked
  once and read 0 to 12 %, by design (item 1010).
- Of the 59.2 million step tokens that were not read from it: first steps 12.4 million (21 %), later steps 46.9 million
  (79 %). First steps: 69 of 72 after a pause of an hour or more were cold, 22 of 34 after 5 to 60 minutes, 3 of 17
  within 5 minutes, 57 of 60 in a new chat. Later steps: 90 fully cold (8.5 million), 322 above 20,000 tokens at full
  price; by the events just before them, a tool group turned on 49 steps (4.0 million: the tools stand before the
  conversation, so a new group is a cold step), a compaction 33 steps (2.9 million), no event 235 steps (10.8 million:
  mostly a large tool result newly added, some a cold cache nothing explains).
- Same chat, consecutive runs (123 pairs, by the sizes of the parts `context_parts` recorded): the system prompt or the
  tools differed in 81 (66 %), and 74 of them started cold; of the 42 with equal sizes 20 started cold, 11 of the 13 with a
  pause of 1 to 6 hours. The sections that changed most: the tools (61: a run starts with the groups its chat used, which
  grew since the previous run's first step, so this overstates), Team 30, Skills 29, Tool groups 25, Environment 24 (the
  day's date), What you remember 20, Character 11 (a deploy). Sizes miss a change that keeps the size, so the real
  number is higher; nothing said which words changed.
- What the prompt order costs: a probe of the builder shows a change of mode (task to chat) keeps the first 18 % of the
  prompt, a campaign session 20 %, approvals 74 %, one skill more 85 %, one tool group more 83 %. A mode flip is rare
  inside a chat (`How to work` changed in none of the 123 pairs), so it was left where it is: moving the lines that end a
  run into the Environment section would change what the model reads first, for a gain nothing measured.
- The pause is the biggest cold-start cause on this traffic, and DeepSeek's cache is the provider's own: Dot cannot
  lengthen it. Anthropic's is five minutes, or an hour for a write that asks for it.

What changed (`llm/types.py`, `llm/codec_messages.py`, `llm/calls.py`, `llm/router.py`, `agent/cache_report.py`,
`agent/context_parts.py`, `agent/loop.py`, `store/db.py`, `store/stats.py`, `cli_stats.py`, the run's Cost card):

- `Usage.cache_write_tokens` (Anthropic's `cache_creation_input_tokens`, which the codec folded into the input and
  dropped) goes into the `model_call` event and `TimeStats.cache_write_tokens`.
- `ChatRequest.cache_ttl` (`5m` or `1h`) and `cache_ttl_for(gap)`: a run whose chat was last called between five and
  fifty-five minutes before writes its cache for an hour (`cache_control: {"type": "ephemeral", "ttl": "1h"}`, GA, no
  beta header), else the provider's own five minutes. `MessagesOptions.long_ttl` is true for Anthropic only; OpenCode
  Go's messages route is never sent the field. No schedule is parsed: the chat's own last call says its rhythm, so the
  first return after a pause writes the hour and the following ones read it.
- `context_parts` carries a fingerprint of each system section and each tool group (`system_hashes`, `tools_hashes`),
  so a changed prefix is told by name, not by a size that may not move.
- `agent/cache_report.py`: `cache_report(...)`, pure, from a run's step calls, its tool-group and compaction marks and its
  parts against its chat's previous run's: the share read from the cache, what was written, why the first step was cold
  (`new_chat`, `prefix_changed` with the parts, `after_pause`, `unexplained`) and each later step above 20,000 uncached
  tokens with what came before it (`tool_group`, `compaction`, `other`). Read in `RunCosts.cache` (`GET
  /api/runs/{id}/stats`, `dotx stats --run`, the Cost card's **Cache misses**).

Not done, and why: the prefix was not reordered or moved (above: the part a change of mode costs is rare, and what
would move is words the model reads first); a tool group turned on mid-run stays a cold step (the tools stand before the
conversation; 6.8 % of the uncached tokens, for a run that needs the group); DeepSeek's own cache time is not ours to
set; nothing was measured on Anthropic traffic (38 calls in 14 days), so the hour's effect on real traffic is a
prediction, and the research's 41 to 80 % does not apply to a provider mix that is 94 % cached already.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_cache_report.py` (the TTL rule at its edges, the hour sent to Anthropic and never to the
gateway, write tokens parsed, fingerprints move with a change that keeps the size, the report's causes);
`tests/smoke/test_cache_report.py` (a scripted model records the requests of three runs of one chat: the system message
and the tools are the same bytes, the third run after an away of fifteen minutes asks for the hour, the reports say new
chat, warm and no change; fails without the rule); `tests/js/usagewords.test.js`; `uv run dotx eval check` and
`tests/unit/test_freedom_contract.py` (the loop is touched only to choose the TTL).
