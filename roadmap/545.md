### 545. Customize reads as one page: the head of Plugins on Skills and Connectors, and Connect apps leads there

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** Asked by the owner: Plugins looked fine but Skills and Connectors did not, and Connect
apps at the foot of the sidebar opened a dialog of its own that had nothing in common with them; they wanted one look,
a luxurious one.

**As built.**
- `static/js/customizehead.js`: the head the three tabs share, which Plugins had alone (item 542): a card lit by the
  theme's accent with the tab's title, a line on what it holds, its search and its controls, and a few of its marks
  scattered beside them, which a phone leaves out. Plugins scatters its featured plugins and logos, Connectors the
  logos of the popular services, Skills the marks of its skills.
- `static/js/views/connectors.js`: the head with Connected and All and Check again; the accounts side by side; the
  sections as chips, one showing its section alone, as the dialog had them; Popular standing up as New & noteworthy
  does, four a row; each card its first step in words on the line of its name (Add, Allow, Sign in, Connect) where a
  "+" was, lifting under the pointer; each section titled with its count. A chip with the search or Connected that
  finds nothing says so, as the dialog did, rather than leave the page empty.
- `static/js/views/skills.js`: the head with Personal and System, Save from a chat and Import from other apps; each
  skill's mark light on a tile of its colour, the colour its name picks (`tintMark`), as a plugin's is.
- `static/js/sidebar.js`: Connect apps opens Customize › Connectors and stands for it while it is open, wearing the
  marks of the servers that are ready, which it reads again on the way back from Customize, as the dialog's close did;
  `connectapps.js` and the dialog's styles are gone.
- `docs/extending.md` and `docs/daemon.md` say where Connect apps leads and what the tabs share.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_connect_apps.py`: the button at the foot of the sidebar wears the ready server's mark and
opens Connectors with no dialog, standing for the page; Popular stands up, a section's title counts its cards, a chip
shows its section alone and All every one, a chip and a search that find nothing say so, the search keeps its focus
and a card says its step in words, and on the way back the button reads the ready servers again; on a phone
the round button opens the same page and nothing is wider than the screen. `test_customize_connectors.py` reaches the
page by the button; `test_plugin_gallery_page.py` reads the shared head; the tests of Skills, the composer's marks, MCP
sign-in and tool choice, 1Password, the application icons, the tour and the phone pass as they were. The unit tests
(3130), `npm run check` and `ruff` pass.
