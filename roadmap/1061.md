### 1061. A run that waits on the owner says so, not "The task failed"

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** Found in a look through the owner's Dot on 2026-11-08: five of the eleven newest
failed runs had ended with `finish(success=false, needs_from_user=…)`, a code, a sign-in or a choice only the owner
could give, and each said "The task failed" in red, with what Dot needed in a box under it.

A run's status stays `failed` (the store, the schedules' tells and the campaigns read it so); the page reads such an
end as its own outcome, `waiting` (`outcomeOf` in `messages.js`): "Waiting on you" in the warning's colours, with a
hand, and the face blocked, not failed. The same in a chat's earlier turns (`history.js`, from the turn's
`run_finished` event) and in the run open at the bottom (`timeline.js`). The line that offers Continue under such a
run says that Dot waits on what it asked and that a message below answers it, not only that the task stopped.

An answer's class names its outcome as `answer-<outcome>`: as `answer waiting` it took the shell's own `.waiting` state
(`shell.css`), fixed over the whole page.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_waiting_answer.py`: a chat of a waiting run, a failed one and a waiting one at the bottom,
in a browser; and a turn older than those read at once, whose box goes in above what is read without moving it in a
browser without scroll anchoring (Safari, the phone; item 708). Both fail without the change. A helper run that ended
so keeps "Failed": what it needs goes to the run that asked it, not to the owner.
