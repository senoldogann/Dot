### 1968. A voice call with a bot is designed, and its first slice, a local speech prototype with measurements, is in

**Status: done (2026-11-26): the design and the measured part of slice 1. The call itself is not built, and the phone-side half of slice 1 is open (below).** The owner asked for a voice conversation with a
bot (speak while Dot speaks, live captions, both sides in the chat). [The design](voice-mode-design.md) compares hosted
speech-to-speech, a local streaming pipeline and an on-phone pipeline, costs each, and splits the work into five slices.

**Decisions (2026-11-26).** The first version is local: streaming speech-to-text on the Mac, Dot's own model, streaming
text-to-speech on the Mac. A limited paid realtime comparison comes only later, if Turkish naturalness or barge-in misses
the target, and only after the lead has been asked for a spend cap. The first release is the app in the foreground with
one selected bot. A task that finishes during the call is announced in one short sentence at the first pause, and its
full result is written in the chat through the normal run path.

**Slice 1.** `tests/smoke/voice_conversation_probe.swift` feeds paced 16 kHz PCM to a prepared `SpeechAnalyzer` and renders
a fixed reply with `AVSpeechSynthesizer.write`, calling no model; `test_voice_conversation_probe` (marker `live`) runs it
for Turkish and English and checks the owner's last word survives with no, 200 ms and 800 ms synthetic tails;
`test_voice_transport_with_fifty_millisecond_batches` sends twenty 50 ms batches through the existing authenticated
`/api/voice/streams` routes and prints the round trips. On a quiet M4 Mac, prepared: endpoint to final text 22 ms
(Turkish) and 50 ms (English) median, synthesis to first audio 15 ms, the first turn of a call about 0.5 s slower for the
cold synthesizer; the last word was right in all 20 turns; loopback upload 1.3-7 ms a batch. Under heavy load the same
probe gave up to 5.5 s, so the p95 under load and the paired phone are the open measurements. The tables are in the design.

Not done: the daemon voice session, the downlink, DotNext's call screen, the Mac/web call, per-bot voice settings
(slices 2-5); a media trace from a physical iPhone; real recorded speech instead of `say` fixtures.
