### 1932. DotNext's chat settles when nothing happens: the asks scene no longer holds the phone at 100% CPU

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The Lead's report: the `n2-asks` scene pinned the app at 100% CPU, a battery bug, and
main's `tests (chat)` failed on it (the dock's and the live line's tests timed out evaluating the screen).

The root cause: the chat followed its end whenever the transcript's content height changed (item 1848). A
`LazyVStack` estimates the height of rows it has not drawn; scrolling to the bottom draws them, which changes the
estimate, which scrolled again. The chat re-rendered about 15 times a second forever: Time Profiler showed
`ChatScreen.body` on top, `Self._printChanges()` showed `_position` changing 77 times in 10 s, and the content height
changed 172 times in 10 s on a scene where nothing moved.

What changed (the app only):
- The chat follows its end when what stands there changes (its last row, the line that says a run starts or works),
  never when its height does: a few scrolls over half a second, then none. One scroll lands short of the end while the
  stack learns the true heights; the later passes reach it. Opening a chat does the same.
- While the owner is asked something, the live line says "Waiting for you", even when the run's last call is a wait
  (`wait_until`): it waits on them, not on its command (the line had said "Waiting · test -f …" since item 1909).
- The work line shimmers only while work moves: "Waiting for you" and "Stopping" are still text. The shimmer draws at
  30 frames a second, not at the display's rate.
- Two UI tests had gone stale with item 1909 and failed on main beside the loop: the live line's steps are now one
  grouped line ("Started 1 helper and opened 1 page"), and the Agents sheet's row is told from the helper's row in the
  work log under the sheet by its model.

Measured (simulator, `ps` CPU of the app after it idled): `n2-asks` 98% before, 3.5% after (7.6% with every step
shown, where the working line still shimmers); `n2-chat` 0.1%.

How it was checked: `ChatUITests.testTheAsksSceneSettles` launches `n2-asks` with a debug render counter
(`-DotNextRenderMeter 1`) and fails if the chat drew again over four idle seconds; `ChatWordsTests.testOnlyWorkShimmers`;
`ChatUITests` locally (12 of 13; `testTheWorkLogGroupsToolsShowsHelpersAndSaysWhatItWaitsOn` fails on this simulator
on origin/main too and passes on GitHub's runner); the streaming e2e (`testAStreamingRunIsMeasured`) still sees the
run's end.
