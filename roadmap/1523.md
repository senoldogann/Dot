### 1523. What is done on the phone with no link is kept in an outbox and sent once, in order, when the link is back

**Status: done (2026-11-24); what was checked in a browser, and what was not, is listed at the end.** A message typed with
no link failed with "Could not reach Dot" and stayed in the box for the owner to press Send again; an answer to an approval
and a Stop were lost the same way. The iPhone app keeps them (item 1371 (c)); the web app now keeps the same things by the
same rules. The daemon is not changed: the idempotency contract was read first (`server/sending.py`, `send_once`: each
message of the pages carries a key, kept a day, and a repeat is answered with what the first made, 409 while the first is
under way) and `POST /api/runs`, `POST /api/runs/{id}/steer` and `POST /api/threads/{id}/queue`, the three routes the
composer sends a message by, already take `key`.

What changed:

- `js/outboxrules.js` (pure, `tests/js/outboxrules.test.js`, 19 tests): an entry (its key, kind, chat, request and place
  in the order), one send for one key, which entry is due (none without a link or with the page out of view; a chat's
  entries in the order they were made, the one that waits holding the ones behind it, those `left` or `refused` or on
  their way not holding; the oldest head first), what the daemon's answer means (no answer, 408, 429, 5xx and a 409 on a
  message wait and hold the order; a 404 or 409 on an answer is told as expired or answered; a Stop or a queue change that
  finds nothing is done; any other refusal of a message stays with its words and holds nothing), the day limit, Send again
  under a new key, what waits (the line's count) and what a killed page leaves (an entry on its way waits again).
- `js/outbox.js`: the entries in IndexedDB (the store `outbox`, which made the database's version 2; a cache dropped with a
  forgotten key leaves it alone, as it is the owner's own words), written whole in one transaction before anything is asked of the
  network; sent by one loop when the phone is online and the page in view (`online`, `visibilitychange`, the stream coming
  back, and a wait that grows from 0.5 s to 15 s while Dot cannot be reached); `viaOutbox(entry, send)` tries `send` and
  keeps the entry when there is no link or Dot cannot be reached, and goes behind what waits in the same chat without being
  tried, so that the order holds; Retry now, Edit (takes the message back), Send again and Discard; a message that waited
  more than a day is `left` (the daemon forgot its key). A browser that cannot use IndexedDB keeps the entries in memory.
- `js/outboxrows.js` and `css/run.css`: under the conversation, a message as a card of its words with *Waiting to send*,
  **Retry now** and **Edit** (a finger tall each), an answer, a Stop and a change of the queue as one line that says they
  wait; *Not sent* with its reason, **Send again**, **Edit** and **Discard** for a message left or refused (English and
  Turkish).
- `views/run.js`, `approvals.js`, `queue.js`, `api.js`: the composer's three routes (a next run, a steer, a queued message),
  the Stop, the answer to an approval or a question, and a queued message's edit, removal, order and Resume go through
  `viaOutbox`; a write on a phone gives up after 30 s as a request with no answer does. `/models` is kept like the other
  lists, because the composer waits for it before it sends and must not wait on a link that is gone.

Not done: a new chat started with no link (the sheet says it cannot reach Dot and keeps the words, as the iPhone app does:
a chat that does not exist has nowhere to be shown); the other boxes that send (the agents' page, the home's) and a queued
message's Steer; files that are still uploading when the link goes (a message with a file that was never uploaded is not
sent offline: the box keeps it); a card answered with no link shows its note and not more; the card of an answer that
expired stays on the page until the page's next read of the approvals.

Checked: `tests/js/outboxrules.test.js`; `tests/smoke/test_phone_outbox.py` (a message made with no link is a row *Waiting to
send* with Retry now and Edit, the box is empty, the daemon's queue is as it was, the page is killed and opened again with no
link and the row is there, the link is back and the queue holds the message once and the row is gone; a message whose answer
was lost after the daemon took it is sent again with its key and the queue holds it once; Edit takes a message back and
nothing is sent; a message made 25 hours old is *Not sent*, is not sent, and Send again sends it once; an answer given with
no link to a question answered on the Mac meanwhile says that it was answered already); `npm run check`.
Not verified: a real phone's link; a page killed by iOS while it sends (the test closes the page, which the browser does
politely); two pages of the same phone sending the same outbox at once (the daemon's key makes it one message, the test
does not run it).
