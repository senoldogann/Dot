### 1420. The app's alerts stay quiet while it shows Dot: an activity lease per phone

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** From the research of 2026-11-23 on the mobile agent apps (a good agent app's phone: its client-activity lease,
`mobile-notifications.md`: "ordinary alerts stay quiet while the mobile app is in the foreground"). The app asked for
every alert while it was open: its `willPresent` shows banner and sound for all of them, so a run that ended under the
owner's eyes still rang the phone.

What changed: `POST /api/phone/activity` (the phone's own key; the Mac's token is a 403) takes `{state: "foreground" |
"background", chat?, ttl_s?}` and keeps a lease for that phone, in memory (`notify/leases.py`, one per daemon, the clock
injected). `foreground` holds `ttl_s` seconds (45 when left out, at most 90) and the app says it again every 25 seconds;
a lease that is not renewed ends by itself, and `background` ends it at once. While it holds, `ApnsWatch` does not send
that phone the alert of a run's end, of a schedule's run's end or of a campaign's end (`push.quiet_while_shown`: the
three ends the app shows itself); every other alert goes whatever the lease says: what asks something of the owner (an
approval, a question, a question left, a secret, a hand-off), a draft, what Dot tells the owner, and Dot's own warnings (a
schedule that did not run or was switched off, learning that stopped, a restart: the app shows them nowhere, which the
pre-push review of this item found). So does every Live Activity update; another phone's alerts are not touched. A phone
that is removed loses its lease. `chat` is only recorded with the lease and returned (the pre-push review of item 1421 found it read by nothing): it
names the thread on screen, and the quiet holds for it as for every other chat while the app shows Dot, so it changes
nothing yet; the spec's "and not at all for the chat it shows" is a part of the quiet that holds for all. The reply says the lease as held (`ttl_s` as capped,
`expires_at`). `docs/phone.md` has the contract of the call (path, method, auth, body and reply examples).

Checked: `tests/unit/test_phone_leases.py` (the time a lease holds, the 45 s default and the 90 s cap, renewing, background,
one lease for each phone), `tests/unit/test_apns.py` (a watch with a fake clock: the news is quiet for the phone that shows
Dot and goes to another, every kind of ask goes whatever the lease says, the Live Activity is told, the lease ends at its
45th second and at `background`, a watch with no leases sends all), `tests/smoke/test_phone_activity.py` (the route on a
served daemon: 403 for the Mac's token, the reply, the cap, background, 422 for a body of another shape, removal of the
phone), `ruff format --check`, `ruff check`, `mypy src`. The new tests fail on the code of before.
Not checked: on a phone (no APNs key was used and no push was sent: the sender is the test's stand-in); the app does not
call the route yet (`ios/` was not touched), so until it does every alert goes as before. Web push to a browser is not
changed.
