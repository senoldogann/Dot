### 656. Every row of a run's conversation reads down one edge

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner put a screenshot of a Dot chat beside one of the coding app they use and
asked why Dot's was not geometrically clean when the other's was. Measured in Chrome on a real run, the conversation's
column was centred, but its rows started in three places:
- what the run said and its answer started 36 px in;
- a group of steps and each step in it started 34 px in, behind a mark in a box of 18 px;
- a change of the memory started 27 px in, its mark 15 px wide with a gap of 8.

The marks stood 1 to 3 px off the centre of the face's 28 px column. The eye reads that as a ragged edge.

**The change.**
- `app.css` `.tool-state` and `run.css` `.steps-state`: the mark's box is 20 px. After the head's 4 px, its centre is
  14 px in, the face's, and with the 12 px gap the words start 36 px in.
- `app.css` `.timeline > .note`: a quiet line of the conversation has a gap of 12 px. Its first mark is 16 px with 2 px
  (`--space-half`) on each side, so it is centred 14 px in and its words start 36 px in. The rows in a step, the thought
  rows and the answer take the same lane.
- One rule puts every quiet line of the conversation's column on the lane: `.run-thread .note`, outside a card,
  wherever it stands. That covers an earlier turn's step kept, a turn cut off and continued, a background turn's goal,
  the earlier turns not shown, and the messages waiting to be read. The reviews of the first three pushes found such
  lines off it one parent at a time; the rule names the column, not the parents.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_conversation_lane.py`, new. A scripted run says a message, runs a command, keeps a note and
  answers, at 1440 and 390 px. Every row's words start within 1 px of the answer's, and every row's mark is centred
  within 1 px of the face's. So is every quiet line of the column outside a card, with probe lines put straight into
  the column and into a list there.
- It fails on the styles before: the group of steps' words at 506 for 508.
