### 747. A desktop step whose run stopped stops typing

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Found** by the review of computer use the owner asked for: stopping a run, or its tool
running out of time, cancels the coroutine that waits for the desktop's worker thread, but not the thread. A `type` step
writes its text key by key (no length limit), and went on typing after the run was stopped, into whatever then had the
keyboard, possibly while the owner typed; only the `STOP` file and the corner were looked at between keys (item 683).

**The change.** `DesktopSession._call` gives each call to the worker thread a stop (`threading.Event`) that the thread
names as its own (`actions.set_call_stop`) and that a cancelled call sets. `actions.check_failsafe`, which a text typed
key by key, a scroll, a drag and each step of a batch look at between their events, ends the step on it as on the
`STOP` file, never between a press and its release. The next call has a stop of its own.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_desktop_stop.py`: a call that "types" (checks between keys) cancelled after 0.2 s ends
its thread within a few keys, where it went on for its whole text before; the next call of the thread is not stopped.
