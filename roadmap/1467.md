### 1467. Dot for iPhone: the helpers list puts the ones at work first

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The owner (2026-11-24, 10:44, with screenshots of the Run sheet): "in the agents list the working ones are
at the very bottom". The Run sheet (Run › Helpers) listed the helpers in the order the app held them, the oldest first: sixty that
had finished, then the five that still ran, below the fold. Nothing told the owner to scroll.

What changed:
- `Core/HelperOrder.swift`: `HelperOrder.groups(_:)` splits a chat's helpers into **Working** (queued, running, waiting on the
  owner, ending) and **Finished**, each newest first by the time the daemon queued the helper; `HelperOrder.title(_:count:)` is the
  header of a group with its count ("Working · 5", "Finished · 12"); `HelperOrder.badge(for:)` is the daemon's own word on a row
  ("Running", "Done", "Failed", "Cancelled"), as before. A helper whose time this state has not heard stands where it came (the
  later arrival is the newer).
- `Helper.createdAt` (Core/ChatState.swift): the state keeps when the daemon queued each helper, from `GET /api/threads/{id}/helpers`
  (`created_at`) and from the `run_queued` event that announced it, which is what the order is made of.
- The run inspector draws the two groups, Working first, each a card with its count in its header; with none working only
  Finished stands, with none at all the line "No helpers in this chat." stays. The capsule's helpers segment opens this same sheet
  and scrolls to its first group, so that the helpers at work are what it shows. There is no other list of helpers in the app.

Checked: `ios/DotTests/HelperOrderTests.swift` (9 cases: working first and finished after, each newest first; the screenshot's
case of sixty finished and five working; queued, blocked and ending count as at work; an unknown time keeps the arrival order; an
empty list; the headers with their counts; the badges; the state keeps the times from the list and from the events; a helper that
ends moves from one group to the other). `xcodebuild test` on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5), `DotTests`: see item 1466's
