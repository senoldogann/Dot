### 1985. DotNext opens a bot's chat at once while its chats are being deleted

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner, on build 9: while every chat was being deleted, going back to Dot's chat
showed a full-screen "Syncing…" until every delete finished.

Cause: a bot's screen took a chat as gone only once that chat's own stream said it was deleted. While the delete was
under way (item 1964 hides the chats in the list from the first moment), Dot's screen still opened the chat it held,
one of those being deleted, and drew it empty and syncing, as its view and stream waited on a chat the Mac was
deleting.

What was built: `BotChatScreen` takes a chat as gone as soon as a delete of it is under way (`goneChats`, the list's
and the outbox's), so it opens the bot's latest chat that stays, or its new-chat page when none does; nothing waits on
the delete. The throwaway daemon holds a delete of chats with `--slow-deletes <s>`, as a far or busy Mac answers.

Checked: `BulkDeleteLiveUITests` on a throwaway daemon that takes 8 s to delete (`scripts/e2e_bulk_delete.sh`): the
twenty chats leave the list at once, and Dot's new-chat page is there at the first look after the tap back, with no
"Syncing…", and stays while the Mac deletes; red without the change (the screen showed the chat being deleted).
