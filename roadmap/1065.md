### 1065. A work order is accepted only on its evidence, and one that came back twice starts afresh

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** The owner, on 2026-11-09, shared the lessons of managing coding agents (give the agent
a way to check its work, and take no "looks done"; after two failed corrections, clear the session and fix the prompt
rather than add a third) and said to start on the recommendations put to them (A and B).

What held before: an attempt was asked to end with a `Verification:` section, but `review_work` accepted any answer;
and a return carried the earlier answer and the lead's notes up to the third attempt, each one more correction on the
same context.

Now, in code:

- **Evidence (A).** `accept` is refused unless the newest attempt's answer has a Verification section with something
  in it (`core/text.has_verification`: the line that names it, as a heading, in bold or plain, and at least a few
  characters before the next heading; "none" is not one). The lead returns the answer asking for the check, or
  escalates it. An order past its due time whose run answered nothing yet cannot be accepted. `delegate` reads reports
  by the same rule (item 802's mark of a claim), which now also knows `**Verification:**`.
- **Afresh (B).** Once an order came back `FRESH_AFTER` (2) times, a return must rewrite its brief (`review_work`'s
  `brief`): the store keeps the new brief, and the next attempt's goal holds it and what done means alone, not the
  answers sent back and the notes on them. A return after that goes to the owner, as before (`MOST_RETURNS`).
- The prompt's managing line and `review_work`'s description say both; `assign_work`'s `acceptance` asks for the
  checks whose output proves it.

Checked by `tests/unit/test_work_orders.py` (no evidence refused, "none" refused, a late order with no answer refused;
notes on a third answer refused and the fresh attempt's goal holding the rewritten brief and no earlier answer) and
`tests/smoke/test_work_orders_for_real.py`.
