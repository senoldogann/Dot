### 580. A chat that loops wears the loop's mark in the sidebar

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** The owner asked for a mark on the chats that loop, in the sidebar, "something really
nice, that glows, or whatever fits best": a chat with a loop (`/loop 30m <message>`, item 281, or a schedule a run of it
made, item 291) looked like any other in the list.

**As built.**
- **The daemon.** A chat of `GET /api/threads` says its loop: `loop_cron`, the rhythm of the switched-on repeating
  schedule whose runs go on in the chat that fires next, and `loop_next_at`, when (`null` for a chat with none). A
  schedule that fires once, one switched off and one that starts a chat of its own at each firing are no loop; one that
  makes no choice of its own goes on in its chat by `[scheduler] same_chat`, which the route passes to the store.
- **The mark.** At the foot of the chat's creature, a disc of violet and blue whose arrows (the icon of `/loop` in the
  slash menu) turn slowly, once in `--motion-turn`, in a glow that breathes with `--motion-breath`, cut out of the row
  by a ring of the row's ground (the open row's surface too). Under reduced motion it stands still in its glow. Its
  title, and the row's name for a screen reader, say how often and when next ("Loops every 30 minutes, next 15:30";
  `loopRhythm`, shared with the loop line over the composer).
- **It follows.** The daemon tells of no change of a schedule, so a request of the page that changes one (`/schedules`,
  a chat's new loop) sends `dot:schedules-changed`, and the sidebar reads its chats again: a loop stopped takes its
  mark at once, from the loop line, the Schedules page or an agent's page.
- `docs/daemon.md` and `docs/memory-and-schedules.md` say it.

**Tests.** `tests/unit/test_store_threads.py`: of a chat's schedules, the switched-on repeating one that goes on in it
and fires next gives its rhythm and time; one switched off, one that fires once, one that starts its own chat and
another chat's give none, and one with no choice of its own counts by `same_chat`. `tests/smoke/test_chat_loop.py`: a
chat given a loop with `/loop` wears the mark at the right foot of its creature, the glow moving and, under reduced
motion, still; its row says the loop; Stop takes the mark away without a reload.
