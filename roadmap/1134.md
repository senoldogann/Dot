### 1134. A wait for a text waits out a page that holds its main thread

**Status: done (2026-11-12).** **Found** by Dot's own record (a repair, seen 6 times, `tool_error`): `browser_wait_text`
failed with "the page did not say in time what it shows" on `x.com/<profile>` and on Google Cloud's console, among
others, with only the page's address as context.

**Cause.** `wait_text` first counts the dialogs open in front of the page (`no_dialog_open`), so that one opening during
the wait ends it (item 698). A count that the page does not answer within 2 s (`_COUNT_TIMEOUT_S`) raised, and the whole
wait failed, whatever time the model had given it: a heavy single-page app holds its main thread for seconds while it
builds a view, which is exactly when a model waits for a text on it. Item 1035 handled the same silence for a page under
navigation only. The count of the matches, after the text or a dialog showed, raised the same way.

**Built.** `browser/driver.py`: `no_dialog_open` returns `None` when the page does not say in time, and
`wait_for_text_or_dialog` ends its slice as its time does when a count is not answered. `browser/session.py`
`_text_or_still` asks the page again until the wait's time is spent, as it does for a navigation under way; a page that
stays silent ends the wait as its time does, and the reading after it fails or shows the page as before. Docs:
`docs/browser-and-computer-use.md`.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_browser.py::test_a_wait_for_text_waits_out_a_page_that_holds_its_main_thread`: a page
whose script holds its main thread for 3.5 s, then shows the text; it failed without the change (the record's error,
after 2 s) and passes with it.
