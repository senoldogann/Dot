### 1027. A break that began while a phone's page was away waits for the grace of its return

**Status: done (2026-11-07).** **Found** by `test_phone_return.py::test_a_phone_back_from_the_background_keeps_its_chat_
still_while_the_network_wakes`, which failed on 4 of the last 6 nightly `browser` runs: back from the background on a
network that takes 4 s to wake, the chat said "Connection lost. Reconnecting…" 2 s after the return, though the page
says nothing for 8 s after a return (item 328, `RETURN_GRACE_MS`).

**Cause.** The wait of a break was computed once, when the break began (`showAfter(node, quietBreak)`). A break the page
saw while it was still away got the short wait (`QUIET_BREAK_MS`, 2 s), as the return had not come yet, and the timer
fired 2 s later whatever the return said. On this Mac the break is mostly seen after the return; on a slow runner (and on
a phone) before it.

**Built.** `dom.js` `showFrom(node, dueAt)`: the time a note is due is asked again when it comes, so a later one, as the
grace of a return that came meanwhile, holds it back. `state.js` `breakToldAt(since)`: the later of a short break after
`since` and the end of the grace. The chat's note (`views/run.js`) and the shell's pill (`shell.js`) use it.
`showAfter` stays for a fixed wait.

**Checked.** `tests/js/dom.test.js` (a break begun before the return is not told inside the grace), `npm run check`,
`test_phone_return.py`, `test_phone_back.py`.
