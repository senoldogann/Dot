### 722. A team's agents the owner had get their suggested schedules, and an agent's page offers its own

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Asked by the owner:** "on Agents I switch on *Add its schedule too, switched off* again
and again, but they turn off; is something wrong?". Their store held no schedule of any agent, though the six agents of
templates they had made (the Growth team on 2026-10-22, Chief of Staff and Expense Manager on 2026-10-16) each suggest
one.

- `useTeam` (views/agents.js) added the schedules of the agents Use team *made* alone. An agent of the owner's with a
  member's name is *kept*, so a team used again, its agents there already, added none, the toast said only "You have
  every agent of the team already", and the cards drawn anew showed the switch off: it read as a switch that would not
  stay on.
- The switch is for one Use, not a setting, and its words ("switched off") read as its own state.
- An agent that lacked its template's schedule had no way to get it but making the agent again.

**The change.** `addSuggestedSchedules` adds the schedule a template suggests, switched off, to each agent given, the
kept ones of a team too, unless the agent has that schedule already (its agent and its goal), so that a second Use adds
none twice; the toast says how many were added and which had theirs. An agent's page whose name is a template's that
suggests a schedule, and that lacks it, shows *Suggested by its template* with **Add it**. The switch reads "Also add
its schedule; you switch it on on the Schedules page after reading its goal".

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_agent_templates.py`: the Growth team made without schedules, used again twice with the
switch on, gets its four schedules once, switched off, and says so; an agent whose schedule was deleted is offered it on
its page, which adds it switched off, on a phone's width. Item 464's test of a template's schedule still passes.
