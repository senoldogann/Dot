### 905. The office's chat flows as the chat page does

**Status: done (2026-11-01).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-01, with a picture of Dot's panel): "Chat alanını lütfen
düzelt, daha akıcı, güzel görünümlü, normal sohbetteki gibi akıcı hale getir. Performans sorunları da varsa onları da
çöz." The picture showed an answer with its Markdown as marks (`##`, `**`), cut at 1,200 characters, the whole goal
said again over the chat ("Working: …"), the run's steps in a box under the chat, and two places that scrolled: the chat
in a well of 240 pixels and the panel round it.

**Built.**
- The turns are written with the chat page's own renderer (`prose`): the owner's words in a bubble, the answers across
  the panel, rendered and whole.
- Over the chat, what the agent does is a word with its state's dot (Working, Waits for you); the goal is the chat's
  first message, not said twice.
- What the run at work does now (its newest six steps and the words it writes, rendered) is the last part of the chat,
  whoever started the run; a run that is no chat of the owner's (a schedule's, a watcher's, Dot's own, a helper's)
  keeps its goal in the state line. The words are cut between the blocks the chat page's reader finds
  (`office/tail.js`, `parseSourcedBlocks`), never inside a code block, a list or a paragraph; a code block still being
  written shows its opening line and its newest lines. The feed keeps the whole answer being written (64,000
  characters at most, `office/feeds.js`) so that the cut is made there and nowhere before it. The live part stands
  after the chat's log, outside it, so a screen reader reads a new turn and not every step.
- The chat takes the panel's height and is the part that scrolls; many help offers scroll in their own part, and the
  panel scrolls as a last resort (a phone's keyboard, a box of many lines), so the box is never out of reach. What the
  owner sends brings the chat to its foot.
- **Performance.** A repaint (every event of the run at work, at most four a second) made the whole chat again: every
  turn's nodes, its scroll read and set. Now a turn's node is made once for its words and kept, the chat log is kept
  and from the first node that changed on, only what changed is taken out and put in (a step swaps the live part
  alone), and the nodes of turns no longer shown are let go, and the live part is made again only when a step or a word
  changed. The scroll stays where the owner left it, or follows the foot when they were there.

**Checked.** `npm run check`; `tests/js/office-tail.test.js` (whole blocks, an open code block, tilde and list fences);
`tests/smoke/test_office.py`: an answer's bold is rendered as bold, the state line is the
word alone, and the chat log is the same node after the panel is painted again with a new message in it. The office's
browser tests ran on CI: on the owner's Mac (load 40 to 50 from other work) WebGL did not start, for main's code too.
