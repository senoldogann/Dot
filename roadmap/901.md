### 901. The office: a clean chat in the panel, full screen, and names written as names

**Status: done (2026-11-01).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-01), with two shots of the office: a free agent's panel
held its last run's steps and its long answer ("the agents' chat screens should be clean"), and the office stood in a
small square with the window free ("with this much room, why a small square? It could be full size when we want").

- **A clean panel.** A free agent's panel is its chat alone: its steps and its words live show only while it works or
  waits for the owner. With no chat of the owner's with it (none yet, or its newest is a schedule's or a helper's),
  the panel is an empty chat: "No chat yet. Write to start one."
- **Full screen.** A button at the top right corner shows the office over the whole window (`.office-full`), the panel
  wider and its chat taller; the button again or Escape takes it back. It stands over the sidebar and under what its
  own panel opens, the composer's menu and the toasts, so a send that fails still says so; on a phone its panel stays
  across the foot. An Escape that a menu took, or that closes a dialog (a file, a question), leaves full screen as it
  is; a browser that draws no room, or a room that could not be drawn, has no button.
- **Names written as names.** An agent a run made was named "Community &amp; Launch": the model wrote it for a page.
  `create_agent` and `create_team` read a page's escapes out of the name and role a run gives, the cards say them so,
  and `find_agent` reads names the same either way, so the owner's agent of that name is found by "Community & Launch"
  too (its stored name is the owner's to change on its page).
- The tags' place is rounded to whole pixels again: on a phone a tag between two pixels lost part of a pixel of its
  height, under a finger's 32 (`test_phone_type`); the glide of item 898 keeps it from jumping.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_office.py::test_a_free_agents_panel_is_its_chat_and_the_office_goes_full_screen` (the
empty chat, no steps, full screen at the window's size, the composer's menu over it, back, Escape), `tests/unit/test_team.py` (a name found
across escapes); the unit suite.
