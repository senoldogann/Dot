### 542. A plugin gallery on Customize, the services under Connectors

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** Asked by the owner, with Qoder's page beside ours (Plugins | Skills | Connectors):
Customize › Plugins listed services alone, looked plain, offered none of the plugins people use (Superpowers, UI/UX Pro
Max, Frontend Design, design and product skills), said "Not tried yet" of services nobody could add, and drew letters
where a logo was missing.

**As built.**
- `plugins/gallery.py`: a gallery entry names a GitHub repository, the commit Dot installs, the skill folders it keeps
  and the hash of their files. Install downloads the commit's archive (`codeload.github.com`, at most 100 MB in two
  minutes, written off the event loop) and unpacks the named skill folders alone into a hidden staging folder of Dot's
  plugins: a member whose path leads out of it, a link, or anything that is neither a file nor a folder is refused, a
  hidden one skipped, and each skill needs its `SKILL.md`. The files are hashed (`files_hash`: each path, whether it
  runs and its SHA-256, in order) and compared with the pinned hash before the folder takes the plugin's name, with a
  `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` and a `gallery.json` marker; a refusal leaves nothing behind.
- `plugins/gallery_list.py`: 22 plugins of 201 skills, six featured (Superpowers, UI/UX Pro Max, Frontend Design,
  Product Design, Product Management, Engineering), the others Anthropic's and those of the makers of Apollo GraphQL,
  Redis, MongoDB, Supabase, Cloudflare and Hugging Face, each pinned by a script that read the repository, its licence
  (MIT or Apache 2.0 alone: Vercel's skills name none and Anthropic's document skills may not be passed on) and its
  stars on 2026-10-22. Qoder's own plugins (Architecture Visualization, Cloud Agents, Phone Use) are not to be had;
  Product Design and Engineering do the design review and the architecture work.
- `plugins/catalog.py` `PluginShelf`: the runtime's plugins as a shelf that `read_again` refills after an install or a
  Remove plugin, so the runs that start after read the new skills with no restart; the MCP servers Dot started keep
  their plugins, and those of a plugin that came while Dot runs start at its next start.
- `server/routes_gallery.py`: `GET /api/plugin-gallery` (each plugin with `available`, `installed` or `taken`, and the
  plugins Dot reads that are not the gallery's) and `POST /api/plugin-gallery/{id}`, one install at a time; a refusal
  is a `GalleryError`, 409.
- `static/js/views/plugins.js`, the gallery after Qoder's: a head lit by the theme's accent with the search and a few of
  the gallery's marks, New & noteworthy (three a row; a row that scrolls sideways on a phone), All plugins with All,
  Featured and Installed and Popular or Newest, cards with the mark, the name and the check of a plugin from its maker,
  the publisher, the kind and the skills, Install or Installed with Remove (after a confirmation, to the Trash), and a
  page per plugin with its skills and where it comes from. `views/connectors.js` is the old services page under its new
  tab; `#/connect` and a service's old `#/customize/plugins/<id>` lead there.
- `connections/directory.py`: Canva, Asana and Box, which no way of Dot's reaches, are gone, and with them "Not tried
  yet" and the note on Google's and Slack's servers; a listing's letters (`monogram`) are gone too. `marks.js`: a mark
  with no logo is the interface's icon or the plug in white on a tile of its colour lit from its top left, one with no
  colour taking one of six (`--mark-*` in themes.css) by its id; the gallery's puzzle piece takes its kind's.
- Every "Customize › Plugins" that meant the services says Connectors: the runs' messages (`agent/picks.py`,
  `mcp/signin.py`, `oauth/accounts.py`, `tools/browser.py`), the sign-in callback page and the docs.

**Tests.** `tests/unit/test_plugin_gallery.py`: the entries (unique ids, each skill folder once in its plugin, the
logos there, the plugins the owner asked for, open licences alone, the homepage at the pinned commit), unpacking a local
archive (the named skills alone with their scripts still runnable and the manifest written; a link, a path out, an
absolute one, a skill without `SKILL.md`, the size limit and what is no archive refused; a hash that follows the
contents, the names and the modes), install (done; files that differ from the pinned ones, a plugin Dot has already and
a download that fails leave nothing), the download of the pinned commit through a stand-in transport (a missing or too
large archive refused), and the shelf, which keeps the servers a plugin had at the start.
`tests/smoke/test_plugin_gallery_routes.py`: a real server installs from an archive made here and the skills are
offered at once, Remove takes them back, a tampered plugin is a 409 that leaves nothing, an unknown one a 404, one put
there by hand listed apart. `tests/smoke/test_plugin_gallery_page.py` (browser): the page wide and light and on a phone
and dark, the marks, the check, the order, the search, Install, Installed with the plugin put there by hand, Remove
after its question, a plugin's page, and a service's old address under Connectors.
`tests/smoke/test_gallery_reachable.py` (`-m internet`): every entry's commit downloaded from GitHub unpacks to the
files pinned (22 of 22 on 2026-10-22). The Connectors tests, the phone tours and the composer's marks follow the new
tab, addresses and marks; the narrowest phone keeps the three tabs' words and leaves their icons.

**The gate.** `ruff format`, `ruff check`, `mypy src`, `npm run check`, the unit tests (3097) and `dotx eval check`
pass. The smoke suite with the browser tests, run whole on the Mac, passed 984 and failed five, none of them this
