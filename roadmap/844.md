### 844. A reaction's follow-up draws no message of yours in the conversation

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, with a screenshot of a chat after a 👎 on an answer: under it, a
bubble of theirs, "My reaction 👎 to your answer above: that is not right. Reconsider it and change what is wrong; ask
me if you cannot tell what.": "reaction verdiğimizde mesaj gitmesine gerek kalmasın, ajan zaten görsün bunu". A
messaging app sends no message for a reaction; the chip on the message says it.

A 👎, a 🔁 or a ❓ on a message of a run that ended starts a follow-up run (item 586), whose goal is Dot's words for the
reaction. The conversation drew that goal as the owner's message, on the run's page and in the turns before a later run.

- `store/models.py`, `store/db.py`, `agent/loop.py`, `agent/manager.py`: the run of a reaction's follow-up keeps the
  reaction it follows up (`RunRecord.follows_reaction`, a column of `runs`, NULL for every other run and for runs from
  before), set as the run is stored, so that no page reads the run without it. A follow-up whose words the owner
  changed in the queue, or that they put a file on there, is their message and keeps none (the pre-push review).
- `views/run.js`, `history.js`: a run with `follows_reaction` has no message of yours, on its page or in the turns
  before a later run; the chip on the message reacted to stays. Dot's own reaction (item 664) stands, on the live
  page as once it is read again, on the run's own messages only, never on one of an earlier turn (the pre-push
  review).
- `docs/runs.md` says so.

Kept: the run, its goal as the model reads it, the queue a follow-up waits in while a run works (it shows there, to
edit, take out or send on), and a queued follow-up sent on with Steer, whose words the owner may have changed and which
still stands as their message.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_reactions.py`: a follow-up started at once and one that waited in the queue keep `wrong`,
and the runs they follow keep none; one whose words were changed in the queue, or that was given a file there, keeps
none. `tests/smoke/test_reactions_page.py`: after a 👎 on an answer, the page goes on at the follow-up and draws no
bubble of the reaction's words, the first message stays and the chip on the answer is pressed (run here in Chrome for
both looks, WebKit standing in for Chrome in a copy of the file, as this Mac lacks WebKit; CI runs it as written).
