### 1920. DotNext's rows say where each chat and bot stands

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner, on build 6: a row must show the chat's state, each distinct, in the app's
plain look: working, needs you, waiting on helpers, waiting on something in the background, unread, pinned.

What must hold: no polling; the states come from the list's rows, which the phone reads again whenever a chat's
revision moves (`thread_changed`). A run's steps move no revision, so a row says "Working…", not the step.

What was built:
- Daemon (`server/thread_sync.py`): `ThreadRow` carries `helpers` (how many of its helpers its run waits on, at work)
  and `background` (its view's `BackgroundWork`: helpers let go, background commands, its loop). Both are read only for
  a chat whose latest run works, that let a helper go, or that has a loop, so the list at rest costs what it did; a
  helper's start and end, one let go, a background command and a loop each already move the chat's revision. The
  contract schema (`docs/schemas/thread-sync.schema.json`) is regenerated; older phones ignore the fields.
- DotEngine: `RowState` and `rowState` / `botState` (pure): Needs you (an ask, or a blocked run), Waiting on N helpers,
  Working, Waiting · what, or at rest; a bot takes the strongest of its conversations (Dot's own work does not make it
  look busy, item 1912), and any ask of any of its chats.
- DotNext: `RowStateLine` (Needs you in the accent and semibold; Working… and Waiting on N helpers… shimmering as the
  work log's live line; Waiting · what quiet), `RowTrailing` (a small three-dot live mark in place of the time while a
  run works, still under Reduce Motion), `BotStateRing` on Dot's face (the accent while something waits on the owner,
  a slow arc while a chat works). The home's bot rows and the chat list's rows use them; the unread dot and the pinned
  bots on top with their pin (swipe and context menu) stay as they were.
- `e2e_daemon.py`: "waithelper:" (a run that waits on a 5-minute helper); a helper's goal is matched inside an agent's
  preamble.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_thread_sync.py` (a row's helpers and background, at work, let go, at rest);
`RowStateTests`; `RowStatesLiveUITests` on a throwaway daemon (`scripts/e2e_row_states.sh`): one agent per state, each
row's words, the live mark, the unread one, a bot pinned from its menu going to the top, the Turkish words.
