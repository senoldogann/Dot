### 1976. A chat told as a conversation: Dot's short messages of what it turns to, and the live line with its face and time

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner's order of 2026-11-26 (GF1), with three shots of Grok Bot: in the chat with
only the messages (item 1915), the agent tells the owner in short plain messages what it does as it goes ("First I'm
looking at which fields the form wants…", "Found the form…"), no tool rows, and one live line under them with the bot's
face, what it does now and for how long ("Gistsmith.com inceleniyor · 1 dk").

Measured before (the owner's store, the 279 top-level runs that ended in the two weeks to 2026-11-27): the models wrote
words before their calls in few steps: 200 of 3,285 acting steps on Claude Code (6 %), 66 of 4,983 on OpenCode (1 %),
4 of 37 in Dot's own loop. So the model's own words cannot be the conversation alone; and 213 of the runs were Claude
Code's and 63 OpenCode's, 3 Dot's own loop's, so a line made in Dot's loop alone would have told almost nothing.

What must hold: no model call and no token per line; the model never reads a line; the steps stay one tap away; the
modes that show steps (Every step, What it is doing on the web; the four tool modes on the phone) look as they did.

Decision (justified): a typed `narration` event, not ordinary assistant text. Whether a chat hides its steps is the
reader's choice on each client (the phone keeps it per chat, the web per browser), so the daemon cannot know when to
send text; assistant text would also enter the conversation the model reads again (tokens at every later step) and
show twice beside the steps. An event costs no token, and each client draws it in its own language with the words its
live line already has for the call.

What was built:
- `agent/narration.py` (pure) and its one adapter `tell_call`: beside a call's `tool_call`, in every harness (the
  executor for Dot's tools in Dot's own loop, on Claude Code and on OpenCode; OpenCode's own tools where it emits their
  `tool_call`), a `narration` event `{call_id, tool, args}` when the call turns the run to other work. A call of a kind
  one of the run's four newest lines told is not told again, but for a search of other words or a page on another site.
  The line names a page by its site, a file by its name, a search by its words, a helper by its work, never a command's
  text; masked of the secrets Dot holds. One read of the run's newest lines in the store; nothing waits.
- Web: Settings › Interface › Work in a chat › **Messages only** (`data-work="messages"`): steps and thoughts fold out as
  with What it is doing; the run's words on the way to its calls are drawn as its messages and stay; a `narration` is
  one of Dot's short messages ("Looking at gistsmith.com", "Searching the web for “…”"); the live line keeps the face and
  says the time ("· 2 min"). A line after the step's own words is not drawn; it ends no group of steps.
- Phone (DotNext): *Show steps* off builds `conversation(...)` (DotEngine, pure) instead of filtering the transcript:
  Dot's words stay where they were said, the lines are Dot's messages, and a finished run's steps fold into one
  *Worked for* before its answer; the live line wears the bot's face (`BotFace`) and the time ("· 1 dk").

How it was checked:
- Replayed the owner's runs of two weeks through `narration_of` call by call (the store opened read-only): 279 runs,
  796 lines (median a run: 1 on Claude Code, p90 2, max 17; 6 on OpenCode, p90 14, max 43 for a run of 226 steps);
  80.8 KB of events in all; 0 tokens added to any model call. The first rule (one line per change of kind and target)
  made 2,231 lines (OpenCode median 11, max 226): the four-line window and the site/search rule are what cut it.
- `tests/unit/test_narration.py` (what is told, what a line names, masking, the event in a real store);
  `ConversationTests` (DotEngine: the conversation of a working and a finished run, no tool mode draws a line);
  `tests/js/narration.test.js` (the words and the time); the executor's unit tests and code mode's with the line;
  `tests/smoke/test_work_status.py` in Chrome (a silent run: two lines and "· 1 s" with Messages only, none with What it is
  doing); the DotNext build and its String Catalog; `dotx eval check`, `test_freedom_contract.py`.
