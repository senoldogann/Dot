### 511. A team works as one, and lines that stay one line

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Reported by the owner.

- **Use team made only Competitor Watch.** Paid Media, Product Performance and Sales Outbound were the owner's already
  (made on 2026-10-16), and Use team keeps an agent of a member's name, as item 504 built it. Only a toast said so.
  The team's card now marks such members **Yours**, read from `GET /api/agents` as Use team reads them.
- **A member's chat delegated to helpers and never called its team.** The templates named `delegate` but not
  `message_agent`, so a member's run had no Team section and could only hand parts to generic helpers. Now:
  - Every template names `message_agent` (`_WORK` in `agent/templates.py`).
  - **Use team** gives it to a kept member whose tools lack it, after the checks, and says which (`joined`).
  - The prompt's Team section (`agent/prompt._team_section`) names the run's team (`templates.team_of`, by a member's
    name) and its teammates first, each agent with its team. It says a part an agent's role fits goes to that agent,
    not to `delegate`, and the Delegation section says so too when there is a team.
  - A run that answers a message still messages no one further (item 504).
- **One line.** The steps head no longer wraps (`run.css`). Its title, and while it works the count of what it did so
  far, give way with an ellipsis, while the time, the failed badge and the chevron keep their width. A tool's name
  ellipsizes at half the row. The control pill's words are one line, and its buttons are one group: under 440 px of
  panel (a container query on `.computer-body`) the words stand over the buttons.
- **All tools.** The agent editor's tools have a box that ticks them all. All ticked saves `tools: null` (every tool,
  any added later too), an every-tool agent opens all ticked, and none ticked is refused in the form.
