### 511. The list of plugins stands still under a finger

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Dependabot's update of playwright (#506) failed its phone tests, and the owner asked to
fix it. The failures were not the update's: `test_phone_back` failed with playwright 1.63 as well (2 runs of 5 on this
Mac), and `test_phone_reach` read a picture before it had loaded. A run that logged every pointer and resize event
showed why a tap on a plugin's tile was lost: Customize › Plugins drew the tiles, the Accounts card above them got its
providers' rows (from 123 to 259 px) between the press and the release, the tiles moved 136 px down, and the click went
to the grid between two tiles. **As built.** The list waits for the card's first read (`accountsCard().read`), so the
card and the tiles show together and nothing moves under a finger; a service's own page has no card and waits for
nothing. The tests that read a picture's size waited for nothing either (`test_phone_reach`, `test_browser_boundary`,
`test_composer`, `test_customize_plugins`, `test_file_panel`, `test_sign_in_page`, `test_queue`, `test_takeover`): they
read it once `decode()` says it is ready, and the check for broken pictures waits for every picture to load or fail.
Tests: `tests/smoke/test_customize_plugins.py` (with the accounts read a second late, a tile stands where it first
showed; it fails without the wait). Docs: extending.md.
