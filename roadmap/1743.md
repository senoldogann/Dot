### 1743. What the owner does with Dot's tells is recorded and shown per watcher and per kind

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The market research of 2026-11-24 (M4 topic 7, TOP 10 #9; M2 idea 8) found the one gap in
Dot's proactive side: the caps (`tells_per_hour` 20, "never told twice", one line of 140 characters) are set with no ground
truth of whether the owner looked, and ProAgentBench's finding is that the history of what a person did with earlier
suggestions is what improves the timing of the next. Dot recorded the owner's reaction to nothing it told.

Design (no schema change: the events table, under the pseudo-run `_tells`, holds both kinds of line, so no table or column
is added):

- `tell_owner` takes the kind of tell and its subject (every one of its 22 callers says: the brief, the check-in, a watcher
  and its id, the initiative, a schedule's end, or other news of Dot's), and `notify/tell_ledger.py:watch_tells` records
  each tell as the phone is told it (the same `notice_message` and `push_message` rules, so the ledger holds the pushes and
  nothing else) as `tell_sent`.
- The answers: the daemon sees a chat opened itself (`PATCH /api/threads/{id}` with `seen`, which every device sends when
  a chat is opened, marks the tells of that chat's runs from the last half hour as opened); a notification closed unopened
  is reported by the page (desktop notifications) or through a window of Dot that is open when the worker sees the phone's
  push closed (`notificationclose`), as `POST /api/tells/dismissed`. What the owner did next is not recorded but read: a run
  their own message started, or a reaction, in the chat of the tell's run within the half hour is *acted on*.
- `notify/tells.py` is the pure count (acted, opened, dismissed, ignored, pending, unmeasured, precision), `GET /api/tells`
  its answer for 30 days, and Scheduled tasks shows it (a line in each watcher's row and a panel under the initiative).

Decided and not decided: nothing changes in what Dot tells or in a watcher's caps; the research's other two steps (digest
low-priority tells into the brief, suggest muting a watcher below a threshold) wait for numbers. The window of half an hour
is a constant, not a setting. A push closed on a phone with no window of Dot open is not seen: it counts as ignored, like a
push never looked at, so on a phone alone `dismissed` undercounts and `ignored` overcounts, and the share of tells opened or
acted on is not affected. Tells are kept without a limit (a line of about 120 bytes each, at most 20 an hour a watcher).

Checked: `tests/unit/test_tells.py` (the states and their order, the share, the rows), `tests/smoke/test_tell_ledger.py` (the
real store, bus and routes on the scripted model: a tell of Dot's own and a run's end are recorded, a chat opened answers
them once, a closed notification once, the owner's follow-up makes them acted on, `GET /api/tells` counts), 
`tests/smoke/test_tells_panel.py` (a browser on the real daemon, desktop light and phone dark, nothing past the width),
`tests/js/tellwords.test.js`, `tests/unit/test_owner_tell.py`; `dotx eval check` was not needed (no tool, policy, gate or
loop path changed).
