**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner ("uygulamadaki tüm ekranları ve tasarımları incele, olabildiğince
minimal ama eksik olmayan, yumuşak, Grok Bot gibi yap"), with screenshots of that app. The last part of the third of
the six stages of the design review of 2026-10-30, the plain sidebar, as the owner was told: "satırın içinde
'çalışıyor' ya da turuncu 'seni bekliyor'". The other app's list says in a chat's own row that it waits ("Waiting for
you: Sign in to …", in orange, with a dot); Dot's had two folding sections above the chats, **Waiting for you** and
**Working**, whose waiting rows were shorter rows of their own beside the chat, which kept its place among the days.

- `sidebar.js`: what waits (`GET /api/activity`'s waiting rows, oldest first) is placed as what works is (item 202,
  `placeWaiting` beside `placeWork`): a row whose run is a listed chat's latest stands as that chat's own row, with its
  menu, once however many of its rows wait, first above what works and out of its day; its last line says "Waiting for
  you:", what has waited longest and how many more wait ("(+1)"), the dot of a run that waits stands in its age's place,
  and its name for a screen reader names each thing and how it waits. A pinned chat stays under Pinned, saying it there.
  A chat that waits while its run goes on (a step kept for the owner, item 482) stands once, with what waits, wearing
  what it delegated, and not again with what works (the pre-push review found it drawn twice); a chat that waits wears a
  waiting creature whatever its run did (a question left on a run that failed looked sad, and a step kept while the run

  waits is read oldest first whatever order it comes in; Select is not offered while every chat waits or works, nor in
  the menu of a chat that waits; whether a chat is in the list is asked of one set of the chats that stand above it,

  listed chat's is a chat-shaped row (`waitingRow`) in place of the old activity row, which is gone, and its run does
  not stand again with what works. Neither group has a heading or a fold; each is named for a screen reader. A search
