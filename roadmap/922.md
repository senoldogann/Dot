### 922. Free mode, and a send nobody answered kept as a draft for the owner's yes rather than refused

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner: "Dot bizden cevap beklerken zaman aşımına uğrayıp error veriyor.
Zaman aşımı olmasın; ayrıca mailleri draft olarak kaydedebilsin ve gönderileri de aynı şekilde ben geldiğimde onayı
verdikten sonra halletsin, yayınlaması gerekeni yayınlasın" and "Dot'un bize sorup sormamasını arayüzden de
ayarlayabilelim, ayar düğmesi ile istediğimizde her türlü serbest mod yapabileyim ya da kısıtlı mod".

On the owner's Mac, 34 questions in five days ended as "timeout" after `approval_timeout_s` (900 s): 11 in their
chats, 10 in chats of Dot's own initiative, which nobody watches, so their mails and releases failed. Approval
behaviour could be chosen per chat, but no single switch turned off every question.

- A message, a post or a release whose question nobody answers in `approval_timeout_s` (900 s) is no longer refused:
  it is kept as a card with its draft, in every chat, whatever `[policy] unanswered` says (`RunGate._ask_user`). The
  first versions made the question wait as long as the owner took (`approval_timeout_s = 0`); six pre-push reviews
  found what an endless wait holds up (a site's pace and spending locks, the desktop, a run's minutes, the parent of a
  delegated run, a schedule's next firings), so the question waits as before and what it would have refused waits as a
  card instead, which holds nothing.
- A message, a post or a release of a run of Dot's own initiative (nobody there: `owner_there` false) is not waited
  for (`RunGate._keep_send`). It is kept as a card in its chat for 30 days (`parking.SEND_KEEP_S`), marked a kept send
  (`kept_send`, `exact`). The run is told to keep its draft as it is to go out (`kept_send_text`), to go on, and to say
  where the draft is. The same step again is that card.
- The owner's yes stores no permit: it reaches one run, the one at work in the chat or the one it starts
  (`RunManager._carry_kept`), whose gate lets that step through once, exactly as the card showed it: tool, kind,
  summary, target, amount and every flag (`allow_send_once`, `kept_digest`). A step of a page (a Send button) is never
  met again as it was, so it keeps a fingerprint of its kind only, and the run told to open the draft is asked once
  more for it. Another send, the same with a flag more, and any other run of the chat ask. This is the independent
  review and the pre-push review of item 922: the first version stored a permit of the kind, which any run of the chat
  could spend on any send. Exact means a command of the shell or an application's script (`_EXACT_TOOLS`); a
  terminal's line is not, as its card names a session by a number a restart gives again (the independent review's
  second pass). A yes that reaches the run at work has it ask the owner before such a step rather than keep another
  card (`owner_answers_sends`, the second pre-push review). A run of the owner's own in that chat asks, and keeps a send nobody answered in time.
- Free mode, `[policy] free` (off by default), with `GET|PUT /api/policy/free` and the first section of Settings ›
  Rules, "How Dot asks". On, every run's next step is decided as in yolo, with none of the owner's questions of items
  850 and 860 (`RunGate._mode_now`; the broker holds the switch for every gate). A refusal still stands. A phone may
  switch it off, not on (item 678: it would outlast the phone's removal; `docs/phone.md`).
- A public action's question is no longer asked under its site's pace lock, which every other run's public step on the
  site waits for: the pace is read before it is decided and again after, under the lock (`RunGate.authorize`), a refusal
  then saying the pace filled while it was decided. In free mode a secret field is typed as in yolo, on a page, a
  window or in a terminal. Past the caps of kept steps (20 in a chat, 50 in all) a send is refused, its draft kept, as
  the docs say.
- A send of a run that read outside content, or whose question the policy raised for what the run read or for
  Dot's own files (as the policy raised it, before a guard took it over), is kept but never exact: the run the yes
  reaches asks for the step itself (item 483's line; the ninth pre-push review). A campaign's send is never exact
  either: its session ends with the card, and the next asks for the step (the tenth).
- `docs/safety.md`, `docs/proactive.md`, `docs/autonomy.md`, `docs/phone.md`, `docs/daemon.md` say so.
