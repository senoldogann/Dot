### 1306. A model call's retries have a budget, a provider's Retry-After is held within it, and a long rate limit goes to the run

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The connector used a provider's `Retry-After` raw: up to 120 s each time, five pauses
(`attempts = 6`) came to 600 s, past the run's `model_call_timeout_s` of 420 s, so the loop cancelled the call, the router
recorded it as cancelled, and the rate-limit wait of the loop (`quota_wait_s`, standby models) never saw the 429. A hint
of 0 retried at once.

- `retry_async` holds a hint within the policy's delays (`hinted_delay`: at least the base delay, at most the longest,
  lengthened by up to 20 % by chance), and takes a `deadline_s` of the policy: a pause that would end past it is not
  taken, no further try is begun, and the error that came is raised. Only the pause is checked: a try that began before
  the deadline runs to its own timeout. It takes a clock and a sleep, for a test.
- The connector gives the retries of a model call (`retry_with_reset`, `post_json`, `get_json`) 300 s after which no
  pause or try is begun (`connector.RETRY_DEADLINE_S`), below the call timeout. Other users of `retry_async` (OAuth, web, connectors, phone) keep
  the policies they had, whose pauses are seconds.
- A rate limit whose hint is above 30 s (it was 120 s) is no longer retryable in the connector: it is raised to the loop,
  whose rate-limit wait, standby models and the router's pause handle it as they handle one above 120 s today.

Not changed: `defaults.toml` (attempts, timeouts), the policy of the loop, the shape of the pause the router makes. Not
budgeted either: the duration of a try. The read timeouts of the providers are 300 to 600 s (`defaults.toml`) and a read
timeout is retried, so one try begun at the end of the 300 s may still run past the call timeout of 420 s and be
cancelled by the loop, as before. Cutting each try to what is left of the budget would cut a slow reply that would have
come, which an unattended run pays for more than a late cancel; the pauses, which were the 600 s, are what the budget
bounds.
The 300 s is a constant, not a setting; an owner who sets `model_call_timeout_s` under it gets the old order of the two.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_retry_budget.py` (the hint within bounds with jitter, 0 is not instant, a long one is cut to the
longest delay, the deadline stops the retries and raises the error, a try that began in time is not cut and its retry is made when
its pause ends in time, no deadline retries as before, a 429 of 90 s reaches
the caller after one request, a short 429 and a 503 are retried and the answer comes, the deadline is below the call
timeout in the defaults), `tests/smoke/test_wire_codecs.py` (a burst of 60 s is for the loop, one of 20 s is retried).
