### 785. Trading's paper account and day, and a market wake, as the docs say

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner ("Dot sistemindeki her bir modülü, özelliği tek tek incele ve
problem varsa ya da mantık hatası varsa hepsini düzelt"). A review of `trading/` and `scheduler/` against
docs/trading.md and docs/memory-and-schedules.md found, each checked in the code:

- **The paper account's money came from the settings at every look.** `PaperBroker.balances()` started from
  `paper_start` as it stands now, so changing it in Settings took money from the account or added it at once, without a
  start: from 1000 to 100, the day read as a loss of 900 and held every buy for the day's loss limit. What the account
  started with is kept at **Start again** (`trading_paper_start`), and an account started before it was kept reads
  `paper_start`; "since the start" on the desk is counted from it (`paper_started_with` in `GET /api/trading`).
- **Start again kept today's traded sum.** The day's orders before the start still counted against `max_day`, so a
  day that had reached it refused every order of the new account until midnight. The paper account counts what was
  traded from its start, as its day's loss already did.
- **A buy's fee was not counted against `max_day`.** A buy of 25 filled at 24.85 and a fee of 0.15 counted 24.85; it
  counts 25, what it spent.
- **A move of the market started a paused agent's schedule.** A schedule's firings skip a paused agent (item 465), and
  Run now is the owner's hand; `fire_for_signal` checked neither, so a market wake started the trading shift as the
  paused agent and counted the wake. It is refused as a run of the schedule at work is, and counts nothing.
- **A store that failed between a one-time firing and its run lost the schedule.** The firing switched it off, and
  the read of the chat's last run that came before the `try` raised past the code that keeps a one-time schedule due.
  The read is inside it.

**Not changed**, with why: the products past the three a wake names are rested with them (with the 30 minutes between
wakes, the next one would be held and rested anyway); a firing written over the owner's edit of the same few
milliseconds (a conditional write would be a change of the store for a window that small); a pinned-date cron on the
night the clocks go forward, which the review reported and a probe did not reproduce (the firing comes after the gap).

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_trading.py` (the fee counted, the traded sum after a start, `paper_start` changed between
starts), `tests/smoke/test_agent_pause.py` (a market wake refused for a paused agent, no chat made), the scheduler's and
the trading settings' tests.
