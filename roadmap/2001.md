### 2001. The question card is one panel on the phone and on the web: "User input needed", a pick per card, one Submit

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner's references of another coding agent's question card: one card docked above
the composer with a small-caps "USER INPUT NEEDED" and a title, each question with its small-caps topic and its words,
the options as full-width cards (bold label, grey description), a written answer, one "Submit answers", and several
questions paging in the card with a "1 / 3" count. Phone and web move together (the owner's rule of 2026-11-27).

What changed:
- DotNext (`AskDock`): the eyebrow says "User input needed" ("Senden bir cevap bekleniyor"); the count is "1 / 3" and
  stays on one line. An option is a full-width card whose words are whole at every text size; a pick is an ink ring
  and a check (a box for a question that takes several), never a colour alone. "Submit answers" is enabled once every
  question has an answer, and sends them all.
- Web (`asksdock.js`, `approvals.js`): the panel shows one card at a time with the same eyebrow, title, "1 / 3" count
  and arrows. A tap picks an option (one, or several; a second tap lets it go) instead of sending it, and the panel's
  one Submit sends every question's answer once each has one. A question card outside the panel keeps its own Send,
  enabled once it has an answer.
- The data and the answers' path are the ones of before (`ask_user` options, `multiple`, descriptions; the decision
  call with the answer as text).

Not done: the "+" that attaches a file to an answer. An answer is text on the decision call; a file needs a path of its
own on the daemon.

How it was checked: DotNext `ChatUITests.testThreeQuestionsPageInOneCardWithSingleMultiAndWrittenAnswers` (single
select, multi select with a pick let go, a written answer, three questions paging, Submit enabled only once all are
answered, picks kept while paging) and `testTheQuestionCardGrowsAtAX3InTurkishAndDark`; the web's
`tests/smoke/test_question_choices.py` in Chrome at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, light and dark (single and multi select,
the run reads the answer; three questions page and go together).
