### 866. "Don't ask again in this chat": the owner's yes to a step holds for its kind in that chat

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner, with the card of a webmail's Send and Dot's line "Şimdi 5 kişisel
basın e-postasını yazıp gönderiyorum": "approve'dan hariç bir de checkbox olursa, don't ask again diye, böyle
işaretlendiğinde o sohbete özel artık o tür şeyleri yaparken sormasın".

**What must hold.**
- Only the owner gives it: a yes on a card of the page, the box ticked (`POST /approvals/{id}/decision` with
  `remember`). No rule, no review model, no run and no channel's answer makes one.
- It holds in that chat alone, the chat of the run whose card it was. It covers the steps that run and the chat's
  later runs, and their delegated runs, as their parent's chat. It never covers another chat.
- Its kind is the step's tool, its flags, and where it reaches:
  - the site of a page step (its host);
  - the server of an MCP tool;
  - the exact line of a command and the folder it runs in;
  - the application of a desktop or AppleScript step;
  - the target of any other step.
  A step of another kind is asked as before, the same tool on another site or with another flag among them.
- A command of the shell tool holds for its exact line with its summary, which names the folder it runs in: the same line in the same folder, and no other. What a line does is not
  told by its program. It may be a script the run can rewrite (`./publish.sh`), an interpreter under any name, or a
  program that runs another from its arguments (`find -exec`, `git -c`, `pip install`).
  - The first draft read the program by its first word. The pre-push review found `sendmail a; msmtp b` through a yes
    for `sendmail`.
  - The second draft refused chains and named wrappers and interpreters. The independent review and the next pre-push
    review found more programs that run another, a list that would never close.
  - The kind is now the step itself, as CLAUDE.md asks of a structural fix.
- A page's site is its ASCII host. An address whose host is not ASCII, or cannot be read, holds for itself alone, and
  targets are lowered, not case-folded: a `ß` folded to `ss` would make two domains one (the independent review).
- The permit is kept once the owner's yes is stored, so that no step goes on a yes that was not (the pre-push review).
- A run that has read outside content uses a permit and gives one, as any run does: **the owner's choice**. The
  pre-push review found that a page, a mail or a tool's answer marks nearly every run of the owner's case, so the box
  would hold for none of their sends. The owner was asked to choose between a review by the review model, asking
  each time, and no check, and chose no check ("okumuş olsa da sorma"). The risk they took is said in
  `docs/safety.md`: a mail the run read could have it send from the permitted webmail to whoever the mail names,
  unasked. A question about Dot's own files (`own_files`) is asked each time, as a kept step's yes
  (`agent/parking.py`) never answers it either. `REVIEW.md` names the check of what is never answered.
- A permit answers before the review model would: in `auto`, and for a schedule's message in the owner's chat (item
  860), the review model's pass-on had shown the box and then ignored the yes (the pre-push review).
- Lines typed into a terminal session or into Terminal from the desktop, and an AppleScript that reaches a shell, are
  never remembered, as their record does not name the folder they run in. The card shows no box on them.
- A step answered from a permit reads "You allowed this kind in this chat" in the conversation, not the review
  model's line (the pre-push review), and `docs/autonomy.md` lists `chat-permit` among who answers.
- A command's yes holds for its line, not for what the line reads: a script it runs, or a file it takes a recipient
  from, may change between two runs of the line. A list of such programs would never close (CLAUDE.md), so
  `docs/safety.md` says it under what the box does not guard, beside the owner's choice above (the pre-push review).
- A yes is kept until its chat is deleted; a rewind does not take it back. A step whose record masks a secret Dot
  holds never matches its live kind, and is asked.
- It is never given, and never answers, for:
  - money (`financial`), a trade (`trade`);
  - a credential or a password manager's fill (`credential`, `vault_fill`);
  - Dot's own files (`self_change`);
  - a hand-off, a question of the run's (`ask_user`), a secret's card (item 857);
  - a step of a chat Dot opened on its own initiative (item 850), which waits for the owner each time.
  The route refuses `remember` on such a card, and the page shows no box on it.
- A step it answers is recorded as answered, with who: `chat-permit`, the yes it stands on, and the step as recorded.
- A refusal that is no question still refuses: a research run, a secret sent out, a site's pace.

**What only asks.** Within the kind, the step is not judged again: five mails sent from one webmail in the chat go,
whoever they are to. A new chat asks again.

**Where.**
- `policy/chat_permits.py` (new): `permit_key(action)`, the kind; `may_remember(...)`, the exclusions.
- `store/db.py`: the table `chat_permits` (thread, kind, the approval, when), and `add_chat_permit` and
  `chat_permit` (the approval it stands on, or none).
- `agent/gate.py`: before a step goes to the owner, a permit of its chat for its kind answers it.
- `server/routes_runs.py`, `server/views.py`: `remember` on the decision, kept only after the yes is stored.
- `approvals.js`: the box "Don't ask again in this chat" beside Deny and Approve, on the cards it may hold for.
- `docs/safety.md` and `docs/autonomy.md` say so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_chat_permits.py`, on a real server and a scripted model, in yolo in the owner's chat:
  - the first mail asks, and is approved with the box;
  - the same line again goes unasked, recorded as `chat-permit` with the yes it stands on;
  - the same program to another recipient is asked;
  - another chat asks again.
  - The route refuses the box on a payment's card, and leaves it open.
  - On the gate, with a permit of the kind planted in the chat, a payment is still handed to the owner and a mail of a
    chat of Dot's own initiative still asked; without the initiative the mail's permit answers it, in a run that read
    outside content too.
  - The kind: one webmail's two compose pages are one kind, another site or another set of flags is not. A command, a
    script by path hold for their exact line in their folder; an AppleScript that reaches no shell holds for its
    application, as any desktop step; what the roadmap names is never remembered.
- `tests/unit/test_static_ui.py`: the card's list of what shows no box is the policy's.
