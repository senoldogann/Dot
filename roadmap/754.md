### 754. An upload reaches a see-through file field over its button, and one inside a web component

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Found** by the review of browser use the owner asked for, from the uploads of the
owner's runs that still failed after items 704 and 745 (`browser_upload` failed 8 times in 60 calls in 30 days): a page
with several file fields whose visible button has a see-through `<input type=file>` laid over it as its sibling (no
label, no `aria-controls`) failed, as Playwright's click met "`<input type=file>` intercepts pointer events", the
forced click of item 745 was not made (the element on top is not the button), and the page has more than one field. And
a control of a web component never found the field its `aria-controls` names inside the component's shadow root, nor
passed the "is it on top" check there: both looked in the page's document, not in the element's own root.

**The change.** `_CONTROLLED_FIELD_SCRIPT` takes, after the label's field and the `aria-controls` one, the file field a
click at the element's middle reaches (`elementFromPoint`): the one a person's click there opens, so no guess. It and
`_ON_TOP_SCRIPT` look ids and points up in `element.getRootNode()`, the shadow root of a web component's control. A
field beside the button that nothing lays over it is still not taken (item 704's rule).

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_browser.py` (real headless Chrome): a page with two fields, one see-through over its
button, and a web component whose button names its field by `aria-controls` in its shadow root each take the file;
the button under a layer that takes a click of its own still fails without pressing the layer. Both new pages fail on the
code before.
