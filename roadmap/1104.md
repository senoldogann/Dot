### 1104. A campaign whose session's end could not plan the next is planned again

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** From the review of the implicit state machines: a session's end plans the next session
(`RunManager._follow_session`); when that failed (`campaign_session_not_followed`, a `DotError` logged) the campaign
stayed `active` with `next_session_at` NULL and no session working, a state with no way out until the daemon started
again (`resume_campaigns`, at its start only).

Now the scheduler's tick, before it starts the due sessions, plans such a campaign (`replan_stalled_campaigns`) once
its last session ended `CAMPAIGN_STALL_S` (10 minutes) ago: its next session starts `[agent] campaign_gap_minutes`
after. A session that ended less long ago is its end's to follow, so a tick between the end and its plan does not start
a session early.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_campaigns.py` (none planned before the 10 minutes, one at them, at the gap after, and none
twice), the campaign tests.
