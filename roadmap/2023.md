### 2023. Chat settings as native inset groups, and a capsule that says "Waiting on N helpers" only while the run waits, with no spinner

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner's two shots of 2026-11-27 from their iPhone:

- *Chat settings* (the composer's model chip) was loose rows: a segmented control of six levels that cut its words
  ("Default…", "Extra h…"), a greyed Fast mode switch saying "This model has no fast mode.", a Plan first switch with a
  dark knob on a grey track that read as on, a "Default" badge and a column of empty stars. Now it is inset groups the
  way iOS draws its settings (`InsetSection`): the recent models, *Options* (Reasoning as a row whose menu holds the
  model's own levels in full words, `MenuPickerRow`; Fast mode only for a model that has it; Approvals; Plan first), the
  providers as folding groups, *Manage hidden models*. Each model row wears its provider's mark, "Default" under the
  daemon's main model, a ✓ on the chosen one and a quiet ★ on a favourite; a long press (or VoiceOver's action) stars it.
  The switch's knob is white on both sides, as the system's. Settings › Models' picker shares the catalog, the group and
  the level menu.
- The capsule's status took far too much width: "Waiting on 1 helper" sat in a 200 pt frame. It keeps to its words now.
- "Waiting on N helpers" stood in the capsule whenever the newest step's first open call was a helper tool, even while
  another call of the same step ran. The rule is now the engine's (`waitsOnHelpers`): the main run has stopped for its
  helpers only while it writes and thinks nothing and every call of its newest step with no result yet waits on a helper
  (`delegate` without `wait: false`, `message_agent`, a CLI's `Task`/`Agent`); N counts the live helpers it still waits
  for (`blocking`). Otherwise the capsule says Working and the helpers keep their own segment.
- No spinner in the capsule: the words and the ticking clock say the run works.
- The web, the same in the same pull request: its phone sheet's Options are one card in the phone's order and words
  (Reasoning with full words, Fast mode as a switch only for a model with it, Approvals, Plan first; Mode and How Dot
  works below), the desk's Reasoning menu ends with the Fast mode switch only for a model with it; the capsule (desk) and
  the dock (phone) say "Waiting on N helpers" by the same rule (`waitsOnHelpers` in `waitcounts.js`, the root run's open
  calls kept by the timeline) and draw no turning mark, and the line over the desk's bar says what the first helper does.

How it was checked: DotEngine `swift test` (`WorkLogTests.testTheRunWaitsOnHelpersOnlyWhileEveryOpenCallIsAWaitOnOne`);
`node --test` (`waitcounts`, `dockwords`); `tests/smoke/test_chat_settings_sheet.py` (WebKit 390x844 and Chrome
1440x900, light and dark), `test_subagent_rows.py` (a run that only waits on two delegates says "Waiting on 2 helpers",
no mark), `test_phone_dock_capsule.py`; the phone's UI tests `ChatSettingsUITests` (no cut level at the default size and
AX3, fast mode hidden for a model without it, the design bar) and `ChatUITests.testTheCapsuleSaysWaitingOnlyWhileTheRunWaitsAndSpinsNothing`
(the new n2-helpers scene and n2-chat) run on GitHub's iOS job.
