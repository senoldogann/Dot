### 1972. Swipe a message to quote it

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner's reference (Grok Bot): a message pulled to the right becomes a chip above the
composer's text, and the message sent carries it.

Rules:
- iPhone: any message of the owner's or an answer, swiped to the right, follows the finger as a rubber band (it slows
  towards 72 pt), a reply arrow shows behind it, a light tap is felt at 44 pt, and on release past that it is quoted; it
  snaps back either way. The swipe starts at least 32 pt from the screen's edge (the back gesture's) and counts only when it is
  more across than down, so scrolling is not taken. Quote is also on the long-press menu and a VoiceOver action.
- The chip above the text shows the quote's first line with a cross; a quote is the message's words, folded and cut at 280
  characters. Sending puts each line of it behind `> ` and a blank line before the words: that is all the model gets, no daemon
  field (the web has none for replies, and a new one would be a contract to keep). A message that starts with `> ` lines
  shows them as a quote above its bubble, on the phone (`QuoteBlock`) and on the web (markdown).
  The chip shows the quote's first line; the whole quote (up to 280 characters) is in the message sent and in its quote block.
  A long message is cut at the same length on both sides, counted in the platform's own units (characters on the phone,
  UTF-16 units on the web), so with emoji the cut may fall a few characters apart.
- The web has a Quote button in the foot of a message and of an answer (and so in the sheet a hold opens); it puts the same
  `> quote` at the start of the box, replacing a quote the box began with.

Checked: `QuoteTests` and `tests/js/quote.test.js` (the same rules both sides), `BotsLiveUITests` (swipe, chip, send, the quote
shown on the sent message) and `e2e_bots.sh` (the run's goal starts with the quote); string catalog check.
