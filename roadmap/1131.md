### 1131. What a run says on the way to its calls is its work told, in one line

**Status: done (2026-11-11).** The owner's screenshot: a run's chat on their iPhone was ten messages one under another
("Checking the gh login now.", "Listing the public repos.", …), each its own bubble, and asked for what the coding apps
of 2026 do. A step's message whose step calls a tool is now the run telling what it does next: it folds with the steps
(with *What it is doing*, the default), and the line under the conversation says the newest of it, shimmering, while
the step's calls run and the model is asked again (`js/workstatus.js`). Shown with the steps, or with *Every step*, it
reads as a quiet note with no bubble (`css/run.css`). A message with no call after it stays a message, and the answer
stands alone under the question; earlier turns draw it the same way (`js/history.js`, `js/timeline.js`).

Checked: `npm run check`, `test_work_status.py` (the line says the step's words, which fold; no bubble on a phone),
`test_conversation_lane.py`, `test_reactions_page.py` (on *Every step*), `test_streamed_message.py`,
`test_answer_once.py`, `test_card_arrives.py`.
