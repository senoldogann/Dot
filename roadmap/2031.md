### 2031. A living face while a chat works, on the phone and the web

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner's ask of 2026-11-27, with Grok's app: while a bot works, its avatar in the chat
list and the chat's header keeps moving (it bobs, the eyes glance left and right, now and then it flips) and rests when
the work ends. Dot's faces do the same, in Dot's own look, calm.

What must hold: it moves only while a chat of it has a run at work (or waits on its helpers) and rests at once when the
run ends, waits on the owner or fails; Reduce Motion turns it into a slow glow; nothing draws off screen or while the app
or the page is not in front.

What was built:
- DotEngine `livingPose(at:seed:)` (pure, `Face/FaceMotion.swift`): a 1.8 s bob of 6 % of the face's size, a glance to
  one side every 4.2 s (the side alternating), a blink every 5.3 s, a flip about the upright axis every 16 s with a small
  hop; each face offset by its id's hash, so that two faces at work never move as one. `stillPulse(at:)` for Reduce
  Motion.
- DotNext: `LivingFace` draws any face in a pose inside a `TimelineView(.animation(minimumInterval: 1/30))` paused while
  the face is at rest, off screen (`onAppear`/`onDisappear`) or the scene is not active; `LivingBotFace` and
  `DotFaceDrawing` give the eyes the pose's glance and blink. It lives on the home's rows and Dot's large face
  (`botState(...).isLive`), Dot's rows in a chat list (`ChatRow`; an agent's rows there wear the neutral avatar, no
  face), a bot's profile, the chat's header pill (`capsuleStatus` working or starting) and the live line of item 1976.
- Web: `actors.js` `liven(face, alive, seed)`: the face's engine moves (its blink and breath) and its eyes glance every
  3.6-4.8 s through `setLook`; the bob and the 16 s flip are CSS on the compositor (`.avatar[data-living]`, a delay per
  face from its id); an `IntersectionObserver` stops the engine and the CSS off screen, the ticker stops while the page is
  away, and a face the page took out stops at its next glance. A working row of the sidebar wears the face in its badge's
  place (`livingBadge`), and the chat's bar shows the face it keeps inside its badge while the run works; at rest both go
  back to the badge's letters. `prefers-reduced-motion`: the engine draws one frame and the face glows (`face-glow`).

How it was checked:
- `FaceAndSearchTests` (DotEngine): a minute of poses bobs, glances both ways, blinks, flips 3-4 times and turns less
  than 6 % of the time, within its bounds; two bots never move as one; the pulse spans its range.
- `RowStatesLiveUITests.testWhileABotWorksItsFaceLivesAndRestsOnceStopped` against the throwaway daemon of
  `e2e_row_states.sh`: the working bots' faces live, a resting one is still, the chat's header face lives and rests once
  Stop is tapped.
- `tests/smoke/test_living_face_and_search.py` in Chrome at 1440x900 and 390x844, light and dark, English and Turkish: the
  working row wears the living face (its CSS animations `face-bob` and `face-flip`), the resting row its badge, the bar's
  badge shows the face while the run works; stopped, both rest; under reduced motion it glows.
- CPU of the DotNext process on the simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.2), the home with three bots at work, sampled
  every second (`ps`): 0.3 % of one core over 30 s with the faces living, 0.3 % with the app in the background, 0.2 %
  with every bot at rest; the drawing itself is the system's render server's, which `ps` of the app does not count.
