### 969. The Mac writes down what is said as it is said, on every device

**Status: done (2026-11-03).** **Found** by the owner: the composer's voice message was slow to start, on the Mac, in
a browser and on the iPhone ("I press, I start to speak, but the first 1-2 seconds do not flow"). Measured on this Mac
with real Chrome: the microphone itself opens in 0.14 s to 0.43 s and gives its first sound in 0.26 s to 0.67 s; the
browser's own recognition, the way of every computer's browser and Dot.app, starts its engine for a second or two, and
what is said meanwhile is lost. The iPhone's way, a recording the Mac wrote down after the tick, heard from the start
but waited for the Mac at the end (0.4 s to 1.4 s for 5 s to 12 s of Turkish). The owner chose, of three ways offered:
the Mac writes down, streamed while the owner speaks, on every device.

**Built.**
- `transcribe.swift` reads 16 kHz mono 16-bit samples on its standard input and writes them down as they come
  (`SpeechAnalyzer` started on an `AsyncStream` of `AnalyzerInput`, the samples converted when the model wants another
  format), and answers once its input ends: 0.04 s to 0.08 s after 12 s of Turkish fed in real time. The file mode is
  gone: nothing writes a recording to a file any more.
- `voice/helper.py` `Transcriber`: `open` starts the program for a stream, `feed` hands it numbered pieces (out of turn
  or past five minutes drops it), `end` gives it 0.8 s of silence (the model drops a last word without it), ends its
  input and returns the words, `drop` kills it; two streams at once at most (`busy`, the place taken before the
  program starts, so that two opens at the same moment cannot both pass), and one no piece came to for 20 s (two
  minutes before its first, while the browser may ask for the microphone) or open past the longest recording is
  dropped by a watch. The sound goes through a pipe, never a file.
- Routes `POST /api/voice/streams`, `POST /api/voice/streams/{id}/audio`, `POST /api/voice/streams/{id}/end`,
  `DELETE /api/voice/streams/{id}`; `POST /api/voice/transcribe` and the WAV reading (`voice/wav.py`) are gone.
- The page: `voicestream.js` sends the samples a quarter second at the least and two at the most, one piece at a time,
  and a stream that could not be opened or a piece that could not be sent ends the recording at once, saying why;
  `recording.js` opens the stream at the press, while the microphone opens, so the program is ready by the first word;
  `voice.js` `listeningWay` records wherever the Mac can write down, on every device, and keeps the browser's
  recognition where it cannot.
- Docs: `docs/daemon.md` (the composer's microphone), `docs/safety.md` (where the voice goes: to the Mac alone where it
  can write down; to the browser's makers only where it cannot).

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_voice_helper.py` (a stand-in program that reads its input: pieces in turn, silence after,
no file, out of turn, too long, two at once, idle drop, timeouts, errors), `tests/unit/test_voice_wav.py` (the page's
samples, rate, five minutes and largest piece are the daemon's), `tests/smoke/test_voice_routes.py`,
`tests/smoke/test_voice_recording.py` (Chromium and WebKit: the stream opens at the press and is fed before the tick, a
computer's browser records too), `tests/smoke/test_voice.py` (the dictation where the Mac cannot),
`tests/smoke/test_voice_live.py -m live` with the real program on this Mac (Turkish 0.04 s and English 0.11 s after the
end), `tests/js/voicestream.test.js`, `npm run check`.
