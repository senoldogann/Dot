### 465. Pause one agent without losing it

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The ninth of the nine ideas taken from OpenBot (item 449 has the plan), one of its small
ones: "pause one agent without losing it". Until now stopping an agent's unattended work meant switching each of its
schedules off, or deleting it. **As built.** `AgentRecord.paused_at` (a column; NULL while it works, as every agent of
an older database), set by `POST /api/agents/<id>/pause` and cleared by `/resume` (`Store.set_agent_paused`; Dot is 422:
its schedules are switched off one by one); the editor's Save (`update_agent`) does not write the column, so it keeps
the pause, and so does a restart. The pause holds where unattended work starts, `Scheduler._fire`, after the emergency
stop: a repeating schedule of a paused agent is skipped to its next time with "skipped: <name> is paused", and a
one-time one waits, still due, with "waiting: <name> is paused" and fires once the agent is resumed; a pause is the
owner's, so it counts no failed run (item 458), tells no missed firing and makes no chat. The agent is read before
anything starts, so one that cannot be read fails the firing ("failed to fire") and starts nothing. Not gated, on
purpose: what the owner sends the agent, Run now of its schedules (the owner is there), and `message_agent` from other
runs: the critique dropped the design's gate on delegation, which would have refused a step of a yolo run. The agent's
page has Pause and Resume in its bar (a new `pause` icon, in `icons.js` and `skills/icons.py`), says "Paused" over its
composer with what still goes through, the list says "Paused", and a schedule's row "<name> is paused"
(`ScheduleAgent.paused_at`). The design's "forget what this agent learned" is not done: it is a destructive feature of
its own, and the page has Forget and Drop for each lesson and skill. Yolo is untouched: the pause is the owner's switch
on the agent's schedules, as switching a schedule off is, and no run is asked or refused anything. Tests:
`tests/unit/test_store_agent_pause.py` (kept by a Save and a restart, cleared by resuming; a store from before the
column), `tests/smoke/test_agent_pause.py` (the routes, Dot refused, the Save keeping it; a paused agent's repeating
schedule skipped with its status, its next time moved, no failure counted, no chat made, no missed firing told, Run now
and the owner's message still starting runs as the agent, and after resuming its firing as the agent; a one-time
schedule waiting due and firing once resumed; an agent that cannot be read firing nothing; checked to fail without the
scheduler's check; the agent page at 1280 px light and 390 px dark as a touch screen: Pause turning into Resume, the
note, the list's badge and the schedule row's). Docs: `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md`, `docs/memory-and-schedules.md`.
