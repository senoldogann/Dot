### 841. What a run changed is in the side panel's Changes tab, with its Undo, not under the answer

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, with a screenshot of the line under an answer ("Changed a file
job-applications.md · Review · Undo"): "bunu da kaldıralım sohbetten. Dot bir kodlama IDE'si değil, personal agent,
yani otonomi, Grok Bot ve diğerleri gibi her şeye entegre olabilen harika bir sistem." Since item 597 every answer of a
run that wrote a file carried that line, an editor's habit in a conversation.

- `views/run.js`: the line is made once for the page's run and stands at the head of the side panel's Changes tab
  (`changesSection`'s new `head`), read as the answer comes, as it was; the answer holds nothing of it.
- `runchanges.js`: a line with no `onReview` has no Review, as it stands in the tab Review opened; its files open
  beside the conversation as before, and its Undo asks, takes back and says so as before (item 597).
- `changes.js`: the section takes a node over its files. `app.css`: the line's margins in the tab.
- `docs/daemon.md` and `docs/autonomy.md` say where the line and its Undo are.

Kept: the Undo itself and all it checks (item 597), the files and their diffs in the Changes tab (item 368), a file
opened beside the conversation from the line.

**Checked.** The owner's Dot read in a headless Chrome of its own with these sheets and scripts served in their place:
a chat whose run wrote three files has no line under any answer, and its Changes tab opens on "Created a file and
changed 1", its two files, Undo, and the three files with their diffs under it, with no error on the page.
`tests/smoke/test_run_changes_card.py` reads the line in the tab (no Review, the files under it), its Undo and what it
says after; `test_answer_streams.py` that no answer holds it; `test_phone_screens.py` opens the tab on a phone for the
files' size and for a file opened from them. Those three launch Playwright's own browsers, which this Mac lacks; CI
runs them.
