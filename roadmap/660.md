### 660. A message still streaming when its run ends is not shown twice

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner sent a message from the phone to a chat on `glm-5.3-flash-free`, a Zen free
model whose runs go whole to OpenCode's agent (item 283). The answer showed three times over:
- the streamed message to its last word but one, still with its caret;
- that last word alone, with a caret of its own;
- the answer under Dot's face.

**Why.** The stream holds back the word being written until the step ends (item 551). OpenCode's run (`opencode/run.py`
`step_ended`) counted the step first and let the tail go after. The tail came under the next step's number: the page
opened a second live message with it. The step's `assistant` then found no live message of its step, so the first
stayed streaming, and the answer was drawn under both. Dot's own loop lets the tail go before it counts.

**The change.**
- `opencode/run.py` `step_ended`: the stream's tail goes out before the step is counted, under the step's own number.
- `timeline.js` `settleLive`, the net behind it: a live message that loses its place before its step's end reaches
  it ends as a step's end ends it. It is drawn whole without its caret and counted among the replies (an empty one
  goes), and an answer that repeats it leaves it out. A live message loses its place when another step's words open
  a message, when a step's end comes for another step, or when the run ends (`closeLive`). The first two left a caret
  on the page before; they were the owner's case.
- When a step's end comes, a live message of the next step whose words end the step's own text is the step's tail come
  under the wrong number: it goes. The step's own message, if it was ended when another took its place, is drawn
  again where it stands rather than in a second message.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_opencode_run_ends.py`, a new test, with OpenCode's events of a one-line answer (a step started,
  its part, its words, its end): every piece and the held tail come under the step's number, and their words are the
  step's `assistant` text. Before the fix the tail ("olabilirim?") came as step 2.
- `tests/smoke/test_streamed_message.py`, two new tests of the net, on a scripted model that streams a message that is
  its answer, with the page open.
  - The run's `assistant` event left out: the page ends with no live message, no caret and the words once.
  - OpenCode's old order of events: the stream's last piece held back and sent under the next step's number just
    before the step's `assistant`. The page ends with no live message, no caret, no message beside the answer and its
    words once. With the net of the first push only, two live messages were left, as on the owner's phone; with that of
    the second, the tail stayed as a message of its own.
- The reviews found what the net missed, one round each:
  - alone, it left the cause on the server and cut a one-line answer short;
  - it caught only the run's end, not a message that lost its place to another step;
  - it then drew that message again and kept the tail as a message.

Left as it is: the old order together with a step whose end never comes leaves both pieces of a one-line message beside
the answer. That takes two faults at once, and the run's fix removes the first.
