### 476. The chats keep their creatures after Select

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** A fix, reported by the owner: after deleting some chats from the sidebar, every chat in
the list stood without its creature. Choosing chats (**Select**, which deleting several goes through) drew each row to
tick with the chat's kept face (item 277's `faceOf`), and a node is in one place only, so the face left the chat's kept
link (item 328's `keptEntry`). The choosing over, the kept links came back as they were kept, without it, until the page
was opened again. **As built.** `sidebar.js` gives a row to tick a face of its own (`pickFaceOf`); the kept face stays in
the kept link. The whole class was looked for: the kept face is put in a chat's link only, and a chat at work, whose
link is in Working, is not in the list. Tests: `tests/smoke/test_chat_menu.py` checks that each chat has its creature
once Select ends; it fails without the fix.
