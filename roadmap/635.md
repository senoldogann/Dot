### 635. Chats chosen for deletion keep the mark of their loop

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner: "when I click Select in the sidebar, the loop icon on the chat cards should show
in that state too."

**The defect.** `sidebar.js` drew a chat's row at rest with `loopFace`, the creature with the mark of its loop at its
foot (item 580). It drew the row to tick while chats are chosen (`chatPick`) with the creature alone, so the mark went
the moment Select was pressed.

**The change.**
- `chatPick` wears the same `loopFace`.
- On a ticked row, the mark is cut out of that row's ground (`shell.css`), as at rest it is cut out of the list's.
- `docs/daemon.md` says it.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_chat_loop.py`, in light and dark (the second with reduced motion):
- after Select, the looping chat's row to tick wears the mark with its words;
- ticked, it still does;
- it fails against the sidebar before.
