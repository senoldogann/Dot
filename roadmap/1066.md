### 1066. An agent's page says how its work orders went

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** The owner, on 2026-11-09, shared the lessons of managing coding agents, one of them:
measure the rework and the reverts, not how much the agents wrote, and said to start on the recommendation put to them
(C): for each agent, accepted the first time, sent back, came to the owner.

Nothing showed it: the work orders were kept, each with its attempts and its end, but no page counted them.

Now an agent's page has **How its work went**, under **What it used**, when the agent has work orders: how many, how
many were accepted on their first attempt, after a return, wait for the owner (escalated; one the owner drops is
cancelled, and the store keeps no mark of an escalation past it, so the row says "waiting for you", not "came to you":
the pre-push review) and are open, and the returns in all
(`agent_work`, a count of `store.agent_work_orders`; an order reassigned counts for its newest agent). `GET
/api/agents/{id}` gives it as `work`.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_agent_design_pages.py` (three orders of an agent, one accepted the first time, one after a
return, one escalated: the API's counts and the row on the page).
