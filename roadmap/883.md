### 883. The morning brief names a goal started again and failing, so the owner can stop it

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner ("benim kararımı bekleyen şeyleri değerlendir, mantıklı ise
implemente edelim"), after item 881 left the weekly fleet-health tell for them to choose.

What it answers: `dotx stats` (item 881) marks a goal started again in other chats whose runs keep failing, but only
for one who runs it. A tell of its own would need a threshold of "too much" and one more notification; the brief is
read every morning already and says what failed since the last one. So the brief names those goals, without a new
setting or notification.

- `store/stats.py`: `SPEND_SQL`, `chat_spend` and `ChatSpend.retried` (two chats or more, two failed runs or more)
  are shared; `ChatSpend.newest`, when its newest run was created. `cli_stats.py` reads `retried` from there.
- `store/db.py`: `Store.goal_spend(since, limit)`, the same query on the daemon's store; `tokens_since`, the week's
  tokens in all, which a goal's share is of (not of the goals read); `newest_run_of_goal`, the newest run of any chat
  of the goal, rewound or not, whose taint the brief takes; `retried_goals`, every goal `retried` (`RETRIED_SQL`,
  by `HAVING`), however few tokens it spent.
- `ChatSpend.opened`: the chats a schedule did not open. A schedule opens a chat of its title at each firing, so two
  failed firings looked like a goal started again; `retried` counts `opened`, in the brief and in `dotx stats`.
- `proactive/brief.py`: `_retried`: the goals of the last 7 days that are `retried` and ran since the last brief,
  each with its chats, runs, failures and share of the week's tokens; the newest run of each is quoted, so the brief
  takes its taint, as for every part of its record. Nothing is said when there is none.
- `docs/proactive.md` says so.
