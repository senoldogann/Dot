### 832. A chat drawn as a messaging app draws one: soft bubbles, an ink one for you, the actions where you look

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner ("uygulamadaki tüm ekranları ve tasarımları incele, olabildiğince
minimal ama eksik olmayan, yumuşak, Grok Bot gibi yap"), with screenshots of that app. The first of the six stages a
design review of every screen of Dot set out on 2026-10-30 (40 screenshots of the owner's Dot beside eight of the
other app's): its chats read as texting, one grey bubble a thought and yours in an ink pill, with the time and the
actions only where the pointer rests, where Dot's answers were text on the page beside a face, with full paths, a
boxed card of the changes and a foot that never hid.

- `themes.css`: `--msg-me` and `--on-msg-me` (your message: the text's colour in light, one surface raised in the dark)
  and `--msg-agent` (Dot's and its agents' words: the second surface), made of each look's own colours.
- `app.css`: what Dot and its agents say, the answer and the lines along the way, in a soft bubble (`.answer > .prose`,
  `.bubble.say > .prose`), one that holds a table, code or a picture as wide as the column; your message in the ink one;
  where a pointer hovers, the foot of an answer that ended well and of your message shows on the pointer or the focus
  (a reaction that stands, a failure and a stop stay in sight); what a run changed is an attachment in the answer's
  fill rather than a boxed card.
- `messages.js`: an answer that ended well says no word for it; a failure and a stop still do. `prose.js`: a file a
  message names is a chip of its name, its whole path in the chip's title and for a screen reader; `clipboard.js`
  copies the path as it was written, in the markdown and the HTML of a copy.

Kept as they were, as the review found them better: Stop, Undo and Review one press away; the YOLO mark in sight; the
faces and their states. The rest of the stages: the one-line composer, the plain sidebar, the soft tokens, the agent's
side panel with Settings cut to seven sections, the quiet pages.

**Checked.** The owner's Dot read in a headless Chrome of its own with these sheets and scripts served in their place,
two chats wide and on a phone, light and dark. `tests/smoke/test_markdown_copy.py`: a copy keeps the path a chip shows
the name of, and the code's copy button over its block is read in one frame (a scroll still moving the page between
two reads put them apart once the bubbles made the page taller). `tests/smoke/test_file_panel.py`: a chip opens its
file by its name. `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py`: the new colours are tokens of the themes.
