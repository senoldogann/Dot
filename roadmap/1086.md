### 1086. On a phone nothing stays tinted after a tap, and a sheet's first choice is not ringed

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** The owner's screenshots of the phone: a chat's "…" stayed tinted after it was pressed,
"everywhere", and the sheet a menu opens drew a blue line under its first choice.

Why: iOS keeps `:hover` on the last thing a finger pressed, and 105 rules of the stylesheets tinted what a pointer
rests on outside `@media (hover: hover)`; the sheet (item 1079) let `showModal` focus its first choice, which the phone
drew with its ring.

Now every rule with a `:hover` selector stands inside `@media (hover: hover)` (moved by a script; a rule's other
selectors, `[aria-expanded="true"]` and `:focus-visible`, stay where they were), and
`tests/unit/test_touch_hover.py` fails on any that does not. The sheet takes the focus itself, as the message sheet
does, and closes however its dialog closes; Escape on a phone goes to a dialog open over the drawer, not to the
drawer (the nightly run of 2026-11-09, `test_plain_words.py`: the drawer closed under the sheet, which stayed).

Checked by `tests/unit/test_touch_hover.py` (fails on the stylesheets before), `tests/smoke/test_chat_menu.py` (on a
390 px touch screen the sheet holds the focus, the pressed "…" keeps no tint, and Escape closes the sheet and leaves
the drawer; fails before), `test_plain_words.py` and the browser tests
that hover with a mouse (activity, vault, reactions, helper card, rules, unread chats, phone screens and layout).
