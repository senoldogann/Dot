### 1440. The iPhone app says it is in front of the owner: the activity lease

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The daemon holds a lease for a phone whose app says it shows Dot and keeps the alerts of a
task's end from it (`POST /api/phone/activity`, item 1420), but the app never called the route, so every alert rang the
phone under the owner's eyes. Research of 2026-11-23 (a good agent app's phone: its client says "I am here" every 25 s
with a 45 s life; the server quiets ordinary alerts while it holds).

What changed: `Core/ActivityLease.swift`. `ActivityLeaseRules` is the rule with no clock and no network (every decision takes
the time): the scene becoming **active** says `foreground` at once and again every 25 s while it stays so (the lease is
45 s, so one renewal lost on a poor link does not end it); **inactive** (a system sheet, the app switcher, Face ID) and
**background** say `background` at once, once (a second one after it says nothing new, and a scene that never held a lease
says none); the **chat on the screen** changing says `foreground` at once with the new thread id (while the app is not
active it is kept and rides the next `foreground`); an **unpaired** app says nothing at all, not even `background`; a chat
id the daemon would refuse (empty, over 64 characters) is not sent, as it would refuse the whole report. `ActivityLease` is
the scheduler on the main actor (an injectable clock, wait and sender, so a test plays minutes in milliseconds): one
report is on its way at a time and only the newest waits behind it, so a link too poor to answer never piles requests up
and `background` is never overtaken by an older `foreground`; the renewal counts from the last report said, whatever
caused it. The sender never throws: `NativeStore` logs a report that did not get through and shows nothing, since a lease
that is not said ends by itself in 45 s and the alerts go (the safe side). `APIClient.report(activity:)` is one try with
an 8 s wait on a session of its own (`makeLeaseSession`, made like the app's session but not waiting for a link: a report
that waited would reach the daemon late and say where the app was). `NativeStore` owns the lease (a store exists for a
paired phone only), knows the chat on the screen from the chat sessions it holds (`chatOnScreen`; the newest open one while
a navigation settles) and `AppModel.sceneChanged` tells it the scene's phase (`DotApp`'s `scenePhase`), keeping the phase so
that a store made later (pairing done while the app is in front) says it at once. The brief's "(active, inactive and
background ...)" is read as: active says `foreground`, inactive and background end the lease with `background`.

Checked: `ios/DotTests/ActivityLeaseTests.swift` (12 cases of the rules, 6 of the scheduler with a manual clock: the 25 s
renewal, the chat said at once, an unpaired app, a slow send that does not pile up, `background` not overtaken, stop) and
`AwarenessRequestTests` (the path, the method, the phone's key, the body's keys, `chat` as null, a 422 left to the caller,
the lease's session). `xcodebuild test` on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5), the whole suite: 373 tests, 0 failures, this item's 22 new cases among them.
Seen on the simulator against a canned stand-in for the app's routes (not Dot's own daemon): the app posted
`{"state":"foreground","chat":null,"ttl_s":45}` when the list showed and `{"state":"foreground","chat":"thr_3","ttl_s":45}`
when that chat opened.
Not checked: on a phone or against Dot's own daemon (no push was sent; the daemon's own side is item 1420's tests); that
the report of the `inactive` phase reaches the daemon before iOS suspends the app (the store's background grace keeps the
process alive for the first seconds, and a report that is lost ends by itself in 45 s).
