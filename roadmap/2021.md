### 2021. A routine's message is one compact row, not the owner's bubble

**Status: in progress (2026-11-27).** The owner's words of 2026-11-27, with another coding app's "Automation" row as the
reference: a message a schedule, watcher or hook sent into a chat showed as a big owner's bubble.

What must hold:
- A run whose source is `schedule` (a hook fires a schedule or runs as `watcher`) or `watcher` opens with one compact
  row on the owner's side: the calendar-clock mark, "Routine" ("Rutin", the phone's word: a bot's profile lists its
  Routines), the routine's title cut at its end, a chevron; a tap unfolds the whole message. Never the owner's bubble.
- The title is the name of the chat's schedule that sends those words, else of the chat's one schedule, else the words'
  first sentence. The phone reads the chat's schedules from its view (`schedules`, already sent); the web reads
  `/threads/{id}/automations` once per chat for the title and keeps the first sentence while it reads.
- At least 44 pt / px tall, the title wraps at AX3 rather than leaving the screen.

How it was checked: DotEngine `RoutineMessageTests` (every tool mode, the conversation, the title rule, the daemon's
shape), `tests/js/routine.test.js`, `ChatUITests.testARoutinesMessageIsOneCompactRowThatUnfoldsItsWords` (L and AX3),
`tests/smoke/test_work_status.py::test_a_routines_message_is_one_compact_row_that_unfolds_its_words` (1440x900 and 390x844).
