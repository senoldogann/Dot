### 1202. A phone's composer opens the chat's settings as a sheet, as another assistant's thread settings do

**Status: done (2026-11-16).** The owner asked for the phone's composer to be as plain as another coding assistant's, with that app's
thread settings as the picture: a sheet from the foot of the screen with Close and Done, the models grouped by
provider in sections that fold, and the options as rows. Dot's phone box had the model's menu over the box with tabs
(Model, Reasoning, Options, Mode; items 1121 and 1122), a rail of providers inside the Model tab, and a pill that cut
the reasoning level ("deepseek-v4.1-flash L.").

- `js/sheet.js`: `phoneSheet`, a sheet over everything that answers the calls of `anchoredPopover` (open, close,
  toggle, repaint, isOpen, dispose): Close, the title and Done at its head, the body scrolled, a foot that stays;
  Escape, a tap on the dim and a drag of the head down close it.
- `js/modelpicker.js`: `modelSections`, the models for the sheet: the default's row with Change (item 209's making of
  the default, Cancel back), the favourites, a section per provider (the chosen model's open, the others saying how
  many they hold), a tick and a star per model, a search at the foot that makes one list across providers.
- `js/choices.js`: on a phone's width the model's pill opens the sheet ("Chat settings"); under the models the
  Options card holds Reasoning, Mode and Approvals as rows that say their value and open in place, and How Dot works
  with its switches. A pick keeps the sheet open. A window that crosses the phone's width takes the other menu. The
  tabs and their CSS are gone.
- `js/composer.js`: a touch in the sheet, and the sheet being open, keep the box's line, as a menu inside the box does.
- `css/run.css`: the sheet; the model's pill gives the ellipsis to the name, so the level stays whole.
- `docs/phone.md` says so.

Checked: the browser tests of the composer, the model picker, one-message choices, fast mode, the composer's picks and
the phone pages, at a phone's width on a touch screen too; `npm run check`; screenshots of the sheet at 402 px in
WebKit, dark.
