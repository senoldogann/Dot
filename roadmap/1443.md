### 1443. "While you were away": what Dot did on its own, as a card at the top of the chat list

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Dot runs schedules, campaigns, watchers and its own initiative while the owner is away, and the
phone said nothing of it when they came back: a finished run showed only as an unread dot on one chat. The daemon now
lists what it did and keeps the moment the owner last looked for every device (`GET /api/away`, `GET` and `POST
/api/away/read`, items 1356 and 1424); this is the app's side. Research of 2026-11-23 (what an agent app shows when you
come back: what finished, what failed, what waits).

What changed: `Core/Away.swift` reads the daemon's answer (`AwayView`: the counts of every run Dot did on its own, the
newest fifty, the approvals it answered for the owner added up; a key left out reads as none and a run that does not
read is left out of the card, not the whole card) and makes the card (`AwayRules.card`: "Dot did 5 things on its own",
"3 done · 1 failed · 1 at work", the three newest by their end, how many more, "Dot answered 2 approvals for you"; **no
card when Dot did nothing**, and approvals alone are no news worth a card). `AwarenessModel.refreshAway` reads the mark
(`GET /api/away/read`): **at the first visit from any device it marks the moment (`POST` with the phone's now) and shows
nothing**, so the card never pours out history; otherwise it reads `GET /api/away?since=<mark>` and keeps the view when
there is something to say. `AwayCardView` is one flat card with a hairline at the top of the chat list: its rows each open
their chat (`pendingRunId`, as a notification's tap does) and a close button, **putting it away marks the card's `until`
read** (`POST /api/away/read`; the daemon never moves the mark back, so the Mac and every phone share it) and hides it at once.
A mark that does not get through (no link, no answer) is kept in `awareness.json` (`awayMarkPending`) and sent first by the
next read, so a card put away offline stays away and is not drawn again from disk. The card is read on start, on foreground
and link, and on a pull to refresh (`refreshEverything`), one read at a time, and is drawn from the last read before
the network answers; a read that fails leaves the card the phone held. Words are the app's own ("task", "stopped", "at work";
a test fails on "daemon", "token", "run", "yolo", "reviewer").
