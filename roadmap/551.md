### 551. Secrets stay masked in a step's events, cards and streams, and a failed call that read outside words taints the run

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** Found by the audit of 2026-10-23 at `96e8c750` (F12, F16, F47, F49, F142, F13, F17), each
read again in the code by a second reader. Item 525 promised that the events of a step mask the secrets Dot holds; the
call's arguments and the text of its result did, and the rest of what a step records did not. Nothing leaves the Mac
by these, but a secret that sits in a command or an address stood in the database, the events API, the timeline, the
approval card, the push to the phone and the stream a screen share shows.

**Fixes.**
- F12, *tool_result stores the action's summary and target unmasked*, and F16, *the same in the policy event, the
  approval records and the cards*: `gate.masked_action` is the step as its records show it (summary and target through
  `redact_known`, the secrets Dot holds and keys of a known form). The executor writes the `tool_result` event with it
  (summary, target, site); the gate writes every record of a step with it: the `policy` event (`_emit_policy` takes only
  masked arguments), the approval of a question, of a review, of a waiver in yolo and of a step kept for the owner, so
  the `approval_requested` card and the push to the phone, and the context of each error a refusal raises. The reason
  of the policy, which quotes the summary for a hand-off and for the review's refusal, is masked with it, and before
  `step_decision` cuts it to its size, so that a cut leaves no half of a secret. The policy, the review model, the
  digest of a step kept for the owner and the key of a step the owner refused are given the step as it is. The
  OpenCode path (`opencode/run.py`, `step_result`) writes its `tool_result` event the same way.
- F47, *streamed text and thinking go out unmasked*: `agent/streammask.py` (`StreamMask`) masks the stream of a step as
  the text so far, so a secret split between two deltas is found whole. What may still become a part of a secret
  waits, and nothing else: the word being written, the end of the text where it is the start of a secret Dot holds,
  up to four words that introduce a secret whose value is still to come (found by asking the mask whether a value in
  the form of a key after them, with or without `:` or `=`, would be masked: `Bearer`, `password =`,
  `Authorization: Basic`, `--token`), the header of a private key's block, text that ends inside a secret the next
  characters would lengthen (a private key is shown once its end comes), and a cut that a secret stands across.
  Plain words are shown as they come, so a reply streams as before and its end adds no more than the word it was
  writing: holding a fixed 64 characters, as first built, made the end of each message jump by lines
  (`test_streamed_message`) and the browser tests caught it in the review. `flush` shows the rest when the step
  ends, before its `assistant` event. The loop and OpenCode's follow both stream through it. The stored `assistant`
  event and the single-action event now mask the secrets Dot holds too (they masked keys of a known form only). The
  one gap, written in docs/safety.md: a quoted secret with spaces in it, introduced by more than a few words, can
  show its start while it streams.
- F49, */btw answers are stored and sent unmasked*: `answer_aside` masks the answer before the event and the returned
  `AsideAnswer`.
- F142, *the step router sends the goal and the last results unmasked to TypeSafe*: `step_state` and `last_result`
  take the secrets Dot holds and mask the goal and the results before they are cut to size; "What leaves this Mac"
  names the step router in TypeSafe's bullet.
- F13, *a tainting call that fails with an exception carrying outside words does not taint the run*: the executor
  taints the gate when a call whose action is of a tainting kind fails with an `McpError` or a `WebError` that is not a
  failure in transit (`_may_hold_outside_words`). A refusal, a bad call and a timeout in transit leave the run as it
  was.
- F17, *an MCP server's error reaches the model unfenced*: a call the server answers with a JSON-RPC error is raised
  as `McpRejected` (new in `core/errors.py`, a kind of `McpError`; `mcp/connection.py` raises it where it raised
  `McpError`), and `tools/mcp.py` returns its words as an error result, fenced as data with the call's nonce and
  `untrusted`, up to 2,000 characters; a timeout and a lost connection, whose words may be the client library's, are
  raised as before (the executor still marks the run untrusted for them, `McpError` being among what may hold outside
  words).

**Left.** Nothing of the item's findings. The audit rated F12 critical and F16 high; both readers found them medium, as
nothing leaves the Mac by them, and so the fix is the masking and not a new check. Not done on purpose: the model's own
context (its tool results, the messages of the thread) is not masked, as before; the main model reads what its tools
returned, and only the records and the screens are masked. Rule replays (`tool_steps`) match the policy event and the
result of a step by their summary and target, which are masked alike, so a step whose command held a secret is replayed
from the masked words.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_step_records_masked.py`: in yolo, ask, auto and a refusal, the events, approvals, cards,
decisions and error contexts of a step that carries a held key and a key of a known form hold neither, and the review
model still gets the step as it is (five tests, failing on the code before). `tests/smoke/test_secret_egress.py`: the
whole `tool_result` event of the refused fetch is checked, not its text alone; a shell step that ran keeps `true # ***`
as its target; what a streaming model says arrives masked, whole, and the stored message masks both kinds of secret;
a side question's answer is masked. `tests/unit/test_streammask.py`: a secret of every form split at every size, a
private key, a secret longer than the wait, text that streams while it comes and the end of it at the flush, text
without spaces, thinking and text each masked, a reset and a new step. `tests/smoke/test_failed_calls_taint.py`: an MCP
error and the errors of a search taint the run in the real loop; a failure in transit, a bad call and a call that
reached nothing do not. `tests/unit/test_mcp_pure.py`: a call the server rejected comes back from the tool fenced and
untrusted, and a timeout and a lost connection are raised. `tests/unit/test_stepmodel.py`: the judge's state is masked
before it is cut. `tests/unit/test_opencode.py`: OpenCode's `tool_result` masks the summary, the target, the title and
the text. `dotx eval check`, `ruff` and `mypy` pass.
