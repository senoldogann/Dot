### 474. A step keeps the taint the gate weighed, and an OpenCode agent's steps keep their decision

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** A follow-up of items 468 and 469. Item 468 kept with every step what decided it, but not
whether the run was tainted when the gate decided, and an OpenCode agent's steps kept nothing of it; item 469's try-out
had to read the taint from the run's events before the step's result, which for a call that ran beside read-only ones
may hold the taint their results brought after the gate decided (469's review, round 2). **As built.**
`StepDecision.tainted` is the taint the policy weighed (`RunGate._decide_spent` reads it once for `decide` and the
record; `None` in a record from before), and the `policy` event of a question, a hand-off or a refusal keeps `tainted`
too: the gate passes the taint it weighed down the paths of a question, so that one the review model passes on after it
took its time, while a read beside the step tainted the run, still keeps it, and the review model reads it too.
OpenCode's session follower authorises a permission with `authorize_recorded`, keeps the decision with the call, and its
`tool_result` (`step_result`, a function of its own now) carries `decision` and `flags` as the executor's does: `None`
for a call the gate did not let through. The try-out reads the kept taint first, the step's or its policy event's, and
the run's taint before the result only for a step from before. An OpenCode call the gate stopped still counts with no
decision on record: its policy event comes before its `tool_call`, which OpenCode reports once the tool runs. Yolo is
untouched: nothing a run does or asks changes. Tests: `tests/smoke/test_step_decisions.py` (the decision and the
question's policy event keep the taint; a question the review model passed on keeps the taint weighed though the run was
tainted meanwhile, which fails without the change), `tests/unit/test_action_steps.py` (a record from before 474 reads
with no taint), `tests/unit/test_opencode.py` (an OpenCode call let through, stopped and never asked),
`tests/unit/test_rule_replay.py` (the kept taint before the run's), `tests/smoke/test_rule_preview.py` (the store reads
the taint a stopped step's policy event kept). Docs: `docs/safety.md`, `docs/daemon.md`.
