### 860. In yolo, a chat of the owner's asks before a payment, a message or mail sent for them, and a public post; nothing else

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, after a team's chat asked again and again: "Şimdi de yolo da çok
fazla soru sormaya başladı. Sanki yolo'nun bir anlamı kalmadı gibi hissediyorum … bizim bahsettiğimiz sadece e-posta
gönderme ya da ödeme yapma gibi şeyler idi, bana sorsun demiştim."

That chat was not in yolo. Its runs were in `ask` (Auto-accept edits). A new chat starts in the mode the screen kept,
or else in the daemon's `[policy] approvals`, which was the default `ask` on the owner's Mac. Dot.app keeps its own
browser storage, and it had kept no choice. In `ask` the aggressive preset asks for every step that sends outside, and
every call of an MCP server is one (each of rea-mcp's tools asked). Asked, the owner chose:
- in yolo, their own chats ask before a payment, a message or mail sent for them and a public post;
- new chats start in yolo.

**What must hold.**
- In a yolo run of a chat the owner opened, a step that raises `financial`, `message_send` or `public_post` is the
  owner's question. No rule, review model or waiver of yolo lets it through. A refusal or a hand-off that is stricter
  stands, as in item 850.
- A payment is handed to the owner, as in every mode that asks (the money stop of item 429 is stricter than a
  question, and it stands): their yes is that they paid, and the run does not pay. One within the caps they pre-approved
  for its site (`[policy] spend`) is a question instead, theirs, as in `ask`. The first draft let it through unasked,
  which no mode does; the pre-push review found it.
- Every other step of yolo asks nothing, as item 443 has it: commands, MCP tools, files, the web, a credential typed
  from the vault, a trade within its limits, Dot's own files.
- "A chat the owner opened": its opener is neither one nobody watches nor one of Dot's own (a schedule, a watcher, a
  notice, the reflection, an eval, Dot's initiative), whatever started the run in it. A delegated run, an agent's run
  among them, takes its parent's. The first draft read the run's source too. The independent review found that a run
  could put a mail off until a command in the background ended, and the run that tells the chat so sent it unasked.
  The third pre-push review found that a run could put it off to a schedule it made in the chat (`schedule_create`
  goes on in the same chat), whose run sent it unasked.
- Whether the owner is there to answer: a run of theirs is, and so is the one that tells their chat its command
  ended. A run a schedule, a watcher or a notice started is not. In such a run of the owner's chat the review model
  judges a message or a post, asked by the owner after the third review: "e-posta … legal ise, tehlike arz
  etmiyorsa ve bir amaç içinse yollasın; aslında burada ajanın niyeti önemli". It judges against their words, and
  the message must be lawful, harmless and called for. Its yes sends it, its no refuses it, and what it cannot tell
  goes to the owner. A payment is still handed to them.
- A question of the owner's nobody answers in time expires and refuses the step, as yolo keeps no question (item
  850's chats keep theirs, as a run that asks does).

**What only asks.** Which steps raise those flags is the classification's, as in `ask`: a mail sent with `osascript` or
`sendmail`, a Send in a page or in Messages, an MCP tool whose name sends, a payment form. A step that sends and is
classified as none of them goes through, as it does in `ask`. A draft of `draft_message` sends nothing; the owner's
press of Send on its card does.

**Where.**
- `policy/engine.py`: `owners_guard(info)`, the question of `financial`, `message_send` and `public_post`; its source
  is `owner_asks`.
- `agent/gate.py`: `RunGate(owners_chat=…, owner_there=…)`. In yolo the guard is held as item 850's is, so the step
  is never waived. It is asked of the owner, or judged by the review model when the owner is not there and it is a
  message or a post. The initiative's guard goes first when both apply.
- `agent/manager.py`: for a root run, `owners_chat` from its chat's opener and `owner_there` from its source; a
  delegated run takes both from its parent.
- `agent/review.py`: the review model's rules say that a message, a mail or a post must be lawful and harmless, whatever
  the instructions say. The rule holds wherever the review model answers, in `auto` too (`docs/autonomy.md` says so).
- The pages that said yolo asks nothing say what it asks: `docs/proactive.md`, `docs/memory-and-schedules.md` (Run
  now), `docs/autonomy.md` (a no holds), `docs/phone.md`, `docs/daemon.md` (the pill), `docs/safety.md` (outside
  content in yolo), and the Schedules page's toast after Run now, which said "it asks nothing".
- A schedule's run resumed after a restart is taken as the owner's, there to answer (its source is `resume`): stricter,
  its message waits for them. The words the review model judges such a run's message against are the schedule's goal,
  which a run of the chat may have written; the rule of lawful and harmless holds whatever it says, and `docs/safety.md`
  says so.
- The card says so ("In yolo too, Dot asks you before …"), as item 850's does.
- The owner's `config.toml` (not committed): `[policy] approvals = "yolo"`, so that every screen that kept no choice
  starts a chat in yolo.
- `docs/safety.md`, `docs/channels.md`, `docs/extending.md`, `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`'s goal, `defaults.toml`'s
  comment, the YOLO hint of the mode picker (`runmodes.js`) and the Rules page (`views/rules.js`) say so.
