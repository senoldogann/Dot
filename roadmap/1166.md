### 1166. Words that may be the answer stream in sight

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** Item 1162 streamed every word of a task run as an update, folded with the steps under
"What it is doing" until its step ended. A step whose words were the answer then showed nothing while it wrote and the
whole answer at its end. On the owner's main harness that is most answers: 45 of 68 of the owner's Claude Code tasks
in the week to 2026-11-13 ended with a reply that called nothing, never having called finish. No model says before its
words are written whether calls follow them, so what the words are is known while they stream only where the run's own
rules say a reply with no call is not the answer.

- `agent/streammask.py` `Said`, `said_in_loop`, `said_in_harness`: a `delta` event says `says`: "update" in Dot's own
  loop once a task or research run used a tool, short of its limit and of the last time it is told to finish (a reply
  with no call is sent back to finish then); "message" in a chat; "either" else: a first step, and every step of a task
  on Claude Code or OpenCode, whose last reply is the answer.
- `timeline.js`, `run.css`: words that are "either" stream in sight as a quiet note (`data-either`), not as a message;
  their step's end makes them the message in place or folds them with the steps. An update streams folded, as in 1162.
- `workstatus.js`: words in sight step the line aside, as a message's do.

What this does not do: a progress update of a Claude Code or OpenCode task, or of a first step, is still in sight while
it streams and folds with the steps when its step ends with calls, as a quiet note rather than a message. Keeping it
folded from its first word would hide the answer while it is written; no harness tells the two apart before the end of
the step.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_streammask.py` (what the words are, by mode and the loop's state),
`tests/smoke/test_opencode_run_ends.py` (an OpenCode task's words are either), `tests/smoke/test_work_status.py` (the
line and what is in sight for each), `tests/smoke/test_kept_live_run.py` (a Dot loop step after a call streams as an
update), `npm run check`.
