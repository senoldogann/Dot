### 1990. A terminal session whose program ended leaves room for another

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** Dot's record held `terminal_open: This chat has 3 terminal sessions open (…), the most
it may have` five times, in five runs. The daemon's log shows the pattern: a run opened short commands (a `git push`, a
build, a test run) in terminal sessions, each ended by itself, and when the run did not read the session once more
after its end, the session stayed, holding no program, until its 30 minutes of idleness ran out. Three of them refused
a fourth `terminal_open` in that chat, though nothing ran.

What changed: the limit of a chat's sessions (`[shell] terminal_sessions`) counts sessions whose program still runs
(`running_sessions` in `dotagent.terminal.session`). One whose program ended stays as before, to be read, its exit
status with it, until a read, `terminal_close` or its idleness closes it. The refusal names the sessions that run.

How it was checked: `tests/smoke/test_terminal.py::test_a_session_whose_program_ended_leaves_room_for_another` fails
without the change and passes with it; the file's other tests, `tests/smoke/test_terminal_input.py`, the freedom
contract, `dotx eval check`, ruff and mypy pass.
