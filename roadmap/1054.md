### 1054. The Claude plan's models by their names, the older ones too, and fast mode where they have it

**Status: done (2026-11-08).** The owner's word of 2026-11-08: "claude kendi üyeliğimizi kullanıyoruz ama model
isimleri doğru görünmeli, legacy modeller görünmeli ve her modelde eğer fast mod destekliyorsa görünmeli".

Measured on Claude Code 2.1.319, with `claude -p --model <name>` at the lowest and the highest effort:

- The aliases name Fable 5.1, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 (the `model` of the program's first line).
- The plan answers on Fable 5, Opus 5, 4.7, 4.6, 4.5, 4.1 and 4, Sonnet 5 and 4.6 and Haiku 4.5; not on Sonnet 4.5
  or 4, Haiku 5 or the Claude 3 models, with or without their dates.
- Fast mode could not be measured on the owner's plan: every model answered `extra_usage_disabled`. The plan's fast
  models are those of Anthropic's API that have it (`FAST_MODE_MODELS`): Opus 5.5 by its alias, and Opus 5.

The aliases stay what a run names (a choice of the newest Opus follows Claude Code); the older models are named by
their ids. Every model has a name as people know it (`model_label`), which `GET /models` gives with each model and
`GET /status` gives for the plan's, so that every page names a model so from its first paint, before the slower
listing comes (the models page's count of the schedules' and agents' models was drawn under the ids). The model menu's
search reads a model's id too, so `claude-opus-4-7` finds Opus 4.7.
