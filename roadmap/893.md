### 893. A run saves how the owner told it to do a kind of work as a skill (`skill_save`)

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-10-31), with a guide to Grok's bots ("tell your bots to
create skills for itself as you give it instructions on how to do tasks. this lets them do them more consistently even
over long conversations"), to make Dot's team better by it.

What Dot had: skills it learns from its own runs at night (`learned-`), and skills the owner teaches by doing the task
once in Dot's browser (`taught-`, item 423). Told in a chat "do it this way from now on", a run could only save a note.
The rest of the guide Dot has already, or keeps out on purpose: an agent designer (`create_agent` and the templates,
items 873 and 876), routines on a schedule and on an event with a filter (schedules, watchers), the vault and the
connectors, voice with dictation on the Mac (item 626); its own e-mail address and accounts are credentials, which
Dot asks the owner about (docs/safety.md).

- `tools/skill_save.py`: `skill_save` (group `skills`): kind, description, the owner's steps, and `replace` to rewrite
  one saved so. Refused in a chat any run of which has read content others can write (`ever_tainted`, new on
  `ToolContext`, for the run, and `Store.chat_ever_tainted` for the runs before it: the owner's next message clears the
  taint, not the page's words, which the chat's history keeps) and in a chat Dot opened on its own initiative; the
  secrets Dot holds are masked, in the skill and in the owner's words its new version notes.
- `agent/playbooks.py`: `said` on a taught skill and its record (its own header), `save_said_skill`,
  `revise_said_skill` (a new version, the old one kept, `revised` with the owner's words; only one said, of the same
  agent); `skill_proposals.py` keeps `said` when a rewrite is accepted.
- The prompt's note on `taught-` skills says they may be told in a chat; `docs/extending.md` says how.
- The budgets: 23,900 in all and 16,500 for the tools (23,823 and 16,407 measured).

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_skill_save.py` (saved with the header and no secret, rewritten as version 2 with the old
one kept, refused when tainted earlier in the run or in an earlier run of the chat, and in a chat of Dot's initiative,

the tool group and secret-caller tests; the unit suite; `dotx eval check`.
