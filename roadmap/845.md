### 845. The settings in seven sections

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, as stage 5 of the six-stage design plan they approved on
2026-10-30 ("Grok Bot kadar sade fakat hoş"): the settings were fifteen tabs in four groups, where Grok Bot's are a
short list.

- `settingstabs.js`: seven sections, each one or more of the tabs: **General** (General and Snapshots),
  **Appearance**, **Models** (Models, AI services and Web search), **Rules and memory** (Rules, Memory and
  Self-improvement), **Browser and Vault**, **Phone and channels** and **System**. Trading is in none: it moves to the
  Trader agent (item 847).
- `settingsnav.js`: the sidebar's nav is the seven sections, with no group titles, each with what it holds (the main
  model, the notes, the vault's items, a dot when a phone reaches Dot), the section of the open tab marked.
- `views/settings.js`: the page's head is its section's name with the row of the section's tabs under it (a segmented
  row of links, the open one marked; none for a section of one tab), then the tab's line; a phone's list is the seven
  sections in one card, a row opening its section's first tab under the bar that names the section.
- `app.css`, `settings.css`, `shell.css`: the row of tabs as Customize's; the group titles' rules go.
- `docs/daemon.md`, `docs/phone.md`, `docs/harness.md` say so.

Kept: every tab, all they hold, and their addresses (`#/settings/keys` still opens AI services, in Models).
