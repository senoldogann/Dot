### 1422. One list of what waits on the owner (`GET /api/needs-you`), and its count on the app's badge

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Comes after items 1420 and 1421 in the same pull request, whose watch it edits. From the research of 2026-11-23: Devin's "Needs you" list (a decision with the option it recommends marked,
a permission, a review) and Claude's Projects ("which one needs you"). The approvals and questions of a run were cards
scattered in its chats; the web UI's Activity listed them for the web only.

What changed: `GET /api/needs-you` (any token; a phone's key too) answers `{count, items}`, newest first, every item
`{id, kind, run_id, thread_id, agent, title, since}` plus `choices` and `recommended` on a question that has them (the
keys are left out otherwise). `kind` is `approval`, `question` (`ask_user`: its options' labels, and the label of the
one the run marked `recommended` in the tool's own options, which the tool already has: no schema change), `secret`
(`ask_secret`: the id and who asks, a fixed title, never the card's words or a value) or `handoff` (the policy's
hand-off, kept steps included). The code is `notify/needs_you.py`; the Activity's "waiting" rows read their cards through
the same `open_asks` (and `live_runs`, moved out of `activity_view`), so the two lists cannot drift; the decision routes
are untouched. Every alert the daemon sends through APNs now sets the app's badge to the count (`aps.badge`, a number,
`apns.badged`; the watch is given a count by the daemon; a count that cannot be made leaves the alert as it was; the Live
Activity carries none; the lock-screen text is what it was). `docs/phone.md` has the contract.

A step kept for the owner whose time is over (`kept_until`) has lapsed and is no longer listed or counted, as the answer
route refuses it, though the Activity's rows still list it as before. A question whose stored options cannot be read (a
pydantic `ValidationError`, which is no `DotError`) is listed with no choices, and the watch's `_badged` passes over such an
error too, so that no record can end the APNs task and with it every push (the pre-push review).
