### 870. Graphite: Grok's neutral greys as a theme of Dot's

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner, who asked to read milind-soni/OpenMausBot line by line and take
from its design what Dot can: "TASARIMDA ÇOK örnek alacağımız şeyler var". OpenMausBot's Midnight skin is "pixel-sampled
from the real Grok app". This is the first part of the design foundation of that study.

The owner's own look is Clean in dark (canvas #0a0a0a, a blue action), as their screenshots measure. So a change to the
neutral look's greys would not reach their screen. Grok's greys come as a theme of their own instead, which they pick
in Settings › Appearance.

**What holds.**
- Graphite in dark has OpenMausBot's layers:
  - the sidebar #0e0e0e, the page #121212, a card, the composer and a menu #1f1f1f, and a hover #2c2c2e;
  - the ink #f5f5f5, its muted tier a grey of it, and a white action;
  - colour only for an error and a warning.
- In light it is the same plan on white: a near-black ink and a near-black action.
- It is held to Dot's contrast rule like every palette (`appearance/tokens.js`), and the browser test measures it in
  both schemes as the page paints it.
- Its name is taken: no theme of one's own is saved as `graphite` (`themes.py`).

**Where.** `appearance/palettes-more.js` (the palette and its card's words), `themes.py` (`RESERVED_THEME_IDS`),
`docs/daemon.md`.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_themes.py`: every theme, Graphite among them, reads in both schemes as the browser paints it.
- `tests/unit/test_theme_folder.py`: the fifteen themes the page ships are ids that a theme file may not take.
- A preview of the owner's own chat on a phone-sized headless Chrome, in Graphite and in Clean.
