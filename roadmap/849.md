### 849. Every icon draws about the same area at its size

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner: "sohbetteki hoparlör ikonu, composerdaki artı ve mikrofon ikonları
diğer ikonlarla aynı boyutta değil … tüm ikonlar aynı boyut olsun yerine göre". The screenshot they named did not reach
the session; the icons were measured instead.

Measured on the owner's Dot: the icons of an answer's foot and of the box were all 15 px boxes, the same as their
neighbours'. Their shapes fill the 24 grid unequally, though:
- the copy and the reaction draw 20 by 20;
- the speaker draws 20 by 14, and the microphone 14 by 20;
- the "+", the arrows and the stop draw 14 by 14, and the × 12 by 12.

So in boxes of one size the speaker, the "+" and the microphone read smaller. The same held across the app: 95 shapes,
from 12 by 12 to 20 by 20.

- `icons.js`: each shape is seen through a frame of its own (`FRAMES`, a square of the 24 grid around its middle). The
  frames were measured from the shapes' drawn boxes, so that each draws about the area of an 18.5 by 18.5 square, its
  longer side at most 22. The chevrons, a fold's mark, keep theirs.
- `app.css`: the stroke is divided by the frame's scale (`--icon-scale`), so every icon's line is as thick on the
  screen. The icon draws past its frame, so that a close frame does not cut the stroke's half-width.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so.

Kept: every icon's box size where it stands (15 px in a small button, 16 in a link, 19 in the sidebar's head); this
evens what is drawn inside the box.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_icon_sizes.py` (new, Chrome) draws every icon as the page does and holds four things:
  - its area;
  - its longest side;
  - its middle;
  - its stroke on the screen, and that the stroke is not cut.
- With the frame written wrong, which the first draft did (three numbers for a viewBox), the test fails.
- Seen on the owner's Dot, at three times the size, before and after: the speaker as wide as the copy and the reaction
  beside it, and the "+" of the box as large as its microphone.
