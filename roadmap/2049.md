### 2049. The plan's sheet: a head with the count, where the run stands and a bar; the steps on a line; a failed run's step says so

**Status: done (2026-11-28).** The owner's shot of 2026-11-28: the Plan sheet was a plain list ("Plan · 1 of 6 done",
circles) in a tall, mostly empty sheet, and the run had failed while its plan still showed step 2 in progress.

- The plan as its run left it (`planAtEnd`, `PlanEnd`, `planCurrentStep` in DotEngine; `planwords.js` on the web, the
  same rules): once the run failed, the step in progress shows failed; once it was stopped, stopped. At work or done
  the plan stands as the run wrote it.
- The head: "2 of 6 done", where the run stands ("Working on step 3", "Failed at step 2", "Stopped at step 2", "The
  run is done", "Finishing") and a thin bar of how far it came, red once the run failed.
- The steps on a vertical line that joins their marks: a done step quiet with ✓, the step at work in ink and weight with
  a filled ring (no spinner, no pulse), later steps dimmed, a failed one red with ✕, a stopped one with its stop mark.
- The phone's sheet takes the height its content needs, up to the large detent (`FittedSheetChrome`); the mark grows
  with the text (Dynamic Type, AX3).
- The web, the same: the bubble over the desk's composer, the phone's sheet and the Plan tab draw the head and the line,
  and follow the run's end.

- Found by this branch's browser run, from item 2023 (main's nightly run fails the same three): the web sheet's *Plan
  first* switch did not turn on screen when tapped (the picks tell the composer, not the sheet, which is now drawn
  again); `test_fast_mode.py` reads Fast mode as the sheet's switch; `test_helper_wake.py` reads "Waiting on 1 helper"
  in the bar's own part.

How it was checked: DotEngine `swift test` (`WorkLogTests.testAFailedOrStoppedRunsPlanShowsItsStepFailedOrStopped`);
