### 1090. The iMessage tests' channel stands at the database's newest row from the start

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** `test_channel_routes.py::test_a_message_of_the_word_stop_stops_the_run_at_work` failed
on the gate twice ("not in 30 s: the run is at work": #791 on 2026-11-05, #861 on 2026-11-09) and passed alone.

Cause: the tests' `_reading` left the channel's cursor unset, so its first poll set it at the newest row and read
nothing, and each test slept 0.4 s before its first message to let that poll come. On a slow runner the poll came after
the message, set the cursor past it, and the message started no run. With the sleep taken out the test fails at once
on the old helper, every time.

Now `_reading`, and the four tests that start their channel themselves the same way, store the cursor at the
database's newest row before the channel starts (`_from_now`), as `start_from_now` does when the owner switches the
channel on; the first message is read whenever the first poll comes, and the eight sleeps that waited for that poll are
gone. The test of the first poll itself (it sets the cursor and reads nothing before) keeps its unset cursor. Dot itself
is unchanged.

Checked: the stop test with its sleep taken out fails on the old helper and passes on the new; `test_channel_routes.py`
whole (33), three times over three workers.
