### 584. Memory merges its near-duplicates and forgets only with the owner's yes

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** The owner asked whether memory deletes what is not used and whether it is smart. Read in
the code (the numbers of their own store were read by another session, read-only, and are not checked here): nothing
deletes a note by itself (`docs/autonomy.md`: "nothing the reflection does deletes a note"); a note only rests, after
`[memory] rest_days` (90) unused, `[agent] lesson_rest_days` (60) for a lesson, a negative score, a `valid_days` that
ran out, a fold of two lessons that say the same the same way round and the cosine of their embeddings is 0.92 or more
(no model asks), or at most twelve pairs a night that the review model calls outdated; a resting note stays on the page
for ever, and only the owner's Forget (page, `dotx memory forget`) or the agent's `memory_forget` deletes. Near
duplicates that differ in their words pile up, since the fold asks for 0.92. This entry was written before the code, as
item 540 asks: what must hold, where it is held, and what only asks. The code follows it.

**What must hold, and what only asks.**

| What | What must hold | Held by | What only asks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nothing is forgotten by itself | the reflection deletes no note: it rests, merges (the sources stay) and proposes | the store: the reflection's code calls no delete; `Store.trash_memory` is reached by the owner's press, the CLI's command and the agent's `memory_forget` alone; a test counts the notes across a night of merges and proposals | the proposal: a note that rested for `forget_after_days` waits on the Memory page for **Forget** or **Keep** |
| The owner's Forget is not the end | a forgotten note stays in a trash, whole (its vector too), for `[memory] trash_days` (30), and comes back awake on **Restore** | `memory_trash`, a table of its own: a trashed note is in none of the tables a search, a list or a count reads, so no query needs a condition to leave it out; the reflection purges what is older, and only that | **Delete for good**, which is the owner's press too |
| A merge keeps what it replaced and can be undone | the sources rest (`merged_into`) and stay whole; the merged note names every source with its run, dates, tags, uses and mark (`merged_from`) | `Store.merge_memories`, one transaction that adds the merged note and rests the sources; `unmerge_memory` wakes the sources and trashes the merged note | the review model picks what is merged and which wording stays; **Undo merge** is the owner's |
| Taint carries through a merge | the merged note is "from outside content" if any source was | the store takes the mark from the sources, never from the caller; the nightly candidates never pair a note saved while reading outside content with a clean one (item 557: page content does not put a trusted note aside) | - |
| What is the owner's is not touched | a note the owner wrote, a lesson they saved, a standing (`core`) note, a pinned note and a journal are never merged, rested by the reflection or proposed; an agent's notes stay its own | one function, `mergeable`, and the same exclusions in `to_retire` and the fold | - |
| An undone merge stays undone | the sources are tagged `keep-separate`, which no later night merges | a reserved tag the candidates leave out; the owner can take it off | - |
| The model writes no new text | a merge keeps the wording of one source, which the judge picks | the verdict names a number, `keep`, and the note's words are that source's; nothing a page put in a note is rewritten by a model | the wording picked |

**Approach.**

- **Merging** (`agent/reflection.py`, `memory/service.py`). After the fold of exact twins (item 306, unchanged) and the
  outdated check, the reflection lists the notes that may be merged (`mergeable`: Dot's own, awake, not expiring, with a
  vector, not pinned, not tagged `core`, `journal` or `keep-separate`, not saved by the owner), cuts each group to the
  notes close to every other one (cosine at least `[memory] merge_similarity`, 0.80; the same agent's, the same mark of
  outside content, the same way round, so the opposite-meaning case stays the outdated check's) and asks the review
  model, in one call for the clean groups and one for the groups of outside content, which notes of each group say the
  same thing and whose words say it best (`judge_merges`). At most `[memory] merges_per_night` (20) merges, the closest
  groups first. A group the model leaves alone stays as it is.
- **The outdated check first reads the closest pairs.** `outdated_pairs` takes the twelve pairs with the highest
  similarity, not the twelve newest notes' pairs: a pair that high is the likeliest to be a repeat or a correction.
- **Forgetting.** `forget_proposals` is computed, not stored: the notes that have rested for `forget_after_days` (90) by
  `rested_since` (`rested_at`, which the reflection and the merge write when a note rests; for a note that rested before
  this item, the last use or write plus `rest_days`, the latest it could have started), leaving out the owner's, pinned
  and standing notes and the lessons the owner saved. `GET /api/memories/proposals` lists them;
  `POST /api/memories/proposals/forget` and `/keep` take the ids the page showed (never "all now", so a note that became
  a proposal after the page loaded is not forgotten unseen); Keep wakes a note, so it rests again, and is proposed
  again, only after it goes unused for as long as before. The reflection counts the proposals into its record and purges
  the trash.
- **The trash and the owner's Forget.** `forget_memory` (the page's, `dotx memory forget`, the agent's `memory_forget`)
  moves the note to `memory_trash`; `dotx memory restore ID`, `POST /api/memories/trash/{id}/restore` and the page's
  **Restore** bring it back awake (its rest tag, `merged_into` and `replaced_by` cleared, written anew).
- **Use, visible.** The note's line says when it was last used and how often (as before) and, resting, since when. The
  list takes `order` (`changed`, `used`, `most_used`, `least_used`) and the page offers it as a Sort control placed
  outside `.note-tools`, whose rules item 578 changes.
- **For item 586.** `pin_memory` and `unpin_memory` (a pinned note never rests: `resting`, `to_retire`, the outdated
  check, the merge and the proposals all read `pinned_at`; pinning wakes a resting note), and `propose_lesson` (the
  owner's words checked as a lesson is, then proposed on the run they are about, as one more lesson of the run's
  `lessons_proposed`, waiting for **Save** or **Dismiss** as the reflection's lessons do under `propose`; `RunManager`
  has it under the lock of the owner's decisions).

**Data.** Additive, in `Store.open`'s own migration: `memories.merged_into`, `merged_from` (JSON), `pinned_at` and
`rested_at`, all NULL for the notes there are; the table `memory_trash` (`CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`). An older Dot
reads the same file and ignores them. Nothing of a note is changed by the migration. The reflection's record in `kv`
gains three fields with a default (`to_forget`, `purged`, and item 585's `skills_proposed`): a Dot from before cannot
read a record written after them (the models refuse unknown fields: it says "the last reflection cannot be read"), as
with item 306's `outdated`, so a rollback would need that record deleted.

**As built.**
- `store/db.py`, `store/models.py`: the columns above, added by `_ADDED_MEMORY_COLUMNS` (so an older database opens),
  and the table `memory_trash`; `Store.merge_memories` (one transaction: the new note, the sources resting with
  `merged_into`; the mark of outside content, the tags and the use counts are computed here, from the sources),
  `unmerge_memory`, `trash_memory`, `restore_memory`, `trashed_memories`, `delete_trashed`, `purge_memory_trash`,
  `rest_memory`, `retire_memory` (which now stamps `rested_at`), `wake_memory`, `set_memory_pin`, `pinned_memory_ids`,
  `resting_candidates` (which leaves the notes of a source out: the reflection's journal) and `list_memories(order)`.
  Saving the same words again finds the note a merge did not put aside first, and the words of a source that was merged
  away lead to the merged note.
- `memory/lifecycle.py` (new): `rested_since`, `forget_proposals` (no journal, by its tag or by the reflection as its
  source, either alone), `proposable`, `forget_proposed` and `keep_proposed` (by the ids the caller names, never "all"),
  `pin_memory`, `unpin_memory`, `restore_note`, `trash_of`, `trash_total`, `delete_for_good`, `purge_trash`,
  `merge_notes` (refuses a note the owner saved or pinned, a `core` note and a journal), `undo_merge` (tags the sources
  `keep-separate`) and `notes_gone`, which maps a chat's quoted notes to what stands for them now. `forget_memory` moves
  to the trash. The rest of a lesson that repeats an adopted habit is `rest_repeating_habits`.
- `agent/consolidate.py` (new): `mergeable`, `merge_groups` (groups of at most four notes, each close to every other,
  one agent, one mark, one way round), `fingerprint` (a group the judge left alone is not asked about again until one of
  its notes changes, kept in `kv`), `rest_repeating_habits` (which remembers, under `HABITS_KEY`, the adopted version of
  the harness, the closeness bar, the embedding model and the lessons it found to repeat no rule, each as its id at its
  last change, so that a night with nothing new embeds nothing: the lessons and the rules were embedded every night) and
  `merge_near_duplicates`: one question of the judge for the groups of notes saved while reading outside content and one
  for the others (a group's notes all have one mark; a note's words could name "GROUP 1" and steer the verdict on a
  clean group in a question that held both), and a group whose notes the owner pinned or forgot while the judge answered
  is skipped (`notes_merge_skipped` in the log) and the night goes on. `agent/review.py`: the judge (`MERGE_TOOL`, one
  call for the groups of one mark, a verdict names `keep`, a number, so no model writes the new words) and the habits
  block of the lesson writer. `agent/reflection.py`: the outdated check takes the closest pairs, the pinned notes are
  protected like the owner's lessons, and the night runs the steps above in this order: fold, retire, outdated, habits,
  merge, purge, proposals, owner section. `Reflection` records `to_forget` and `purged`; the line of `dotx reflect` says
  how many notes wait to be forgotten.
- `agent/loop.py`, `agent/lessons.py`, `agent/manager.py`: a chat that quoted a note a merge put aside is handed the
  merged note once and told the old one is gone (the harness audit's finding: the ids of forgotten notes were not mapped

- `server/routes_data.py`, `server/views.py`: `GET /api/memories?order=`, `rested` on a page,

**What is left for later.**
- The merged note keeps one source's words. A model-written wording of two notes that each hold a detail needs its own
  check of what the model adds (it reads notes that may come from a page), and a place for the owner to read it first.
- A note saved while reading outside content is merged with another such note only; merging it with a clean one needs
  the owner's reading first.
- Notes the owner wrote are never merged or proposed; a page of "these two of yours say the same" is for later.
- The score-based rest (a negative balance) has no date, so such a note is proposed once the reflection has tagged it.
