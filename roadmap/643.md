### 643. A chat that opens shows three dots, and only after a moment

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner asked for the animation shown while a chat opens to be simpler and nicer.
Three options were drawn for them; they chose **three dots**.

**The change.**
- The loader of item 502 had five things moving at once: a turning ring round a breathing dot, its glow, the line
  "Opening this chat" in a band of light, and the shape of a conversation with a light crossing each of its lines.
- `panels.js` `chatLoading` and `run.css` now draw three dots of Dot's yellow that rise in turn in the middle of the
  room under the bar, and nothing else.
- They fade in only once the read has taken 300 ms, so a chat that opens at once flashes nothing.
- A screen reader is still told "Opening this chat" (its `role="status"` and label), and with reduced motion the dots
  stand still, a little dimmed.
- `docs/runs.md` says so. `docs/daemon.md` and the test of what repeats forever (`test_css_tokens.py`, item 645) no
  longer name a light over the words of a chat being opened: the dots only rise and fade, which the compositor draws.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_chat_loading.py`, new, over a static page with the page's own files:
  - in the first moment nothing is seen;
  - then three dots move, with no words;
  - with reduced motion they stand still.
  - It fails on the loader before: seen at once, no dots, "Opening this chat" in words.
