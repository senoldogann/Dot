### 507. A chat at work wears its helpers' faces in its row, not a list under it

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** Asked by the owner, with a screenshot of the sidebar: the runs a chat delegated hung under
it as a block behind a "1 delegated run" line, so the Working section read as a tree; they should stand as small icons
inside the chat's own row, laid over one another, as the connected apps' marks do in the foot.

**As built.**
- `js/sidebar.js`: `helperFaces(children)` draws the runs a row delegated — up to three, each a run's own character (a dot
  of its state for a run without one), the rest counted with `+N`. A chat's row wears them on its second line, where its
  last words stood while nothing worked, so its name keeps the width it had; the row's name for a screen reader says how
  many there are ("Check the shop's books (working, 4 helpers)"). A row of Working that is no chat's wears them before its
  time as well.
- The list under the row and its fold are gone: `withDelegated`, the `.side-family`, `.side-children` and
  `.side-children-fold` block, and the fold kept in this browser (`dot.sideFolds.runs`) went with them. The same runs
  stand in the chat's conversation and in its Agents tab, and the row opens the chat.
- `css/shell.css`: `.side-helpers` — 20 px faces laid 4 px over one another, each ringed in the sidebar's own ground so a
  hovered row does not show through, the count in the same size.

**Checked.** `npm run check` green (43 tests), and the browser tests of the sidebar and the chats in Chrome:
`tests/smoke/test_activity.py` (1 passed — the row wears one face for the one run the chat delegated, nothing hangs
under it, and its name says "1 helper"), `test_side_working.py`, `test_side_agents.py`, `test_side_scroll.py`,
`test_chat_creatures.py`, `test_chat_menu.py`, `test_unread_chats.py`, `test_phone_lists.py`, `test_phone_return.py` and
`test_reconnect.py`. Clicked through a chat that delegated four runs, at 1 280 and 390 px, light and dark: three faces and
"+1" under the chat's name, no nested rows, the title as wide as it was.
