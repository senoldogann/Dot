### 767. A helper's closed row keeps its steps out of the page until it opens

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Measured:** item 734 left it: the owner's chat of a run that had delegated to several
helpers held 75,340 elements when it opened, as `timeline.js` drew every helper's steps into its row, which is closed
from the start (item 123) and mostly never opened.

**The change.** A helper's row (`childScope`) draws its run's steps into its `inner` as before, and `inner` joins the
row the first time the row opens, then stays: at a click on its summary, which comes before the browser opens the row,
so the steps are in the frame that shows it open; in `reveal` (an approval the helper asks for), before it opens the
row; at the row's `toggle` for any other way it opens. A closed row is its line alone, kept up to date by its events as
before: the ring, the last line, the time, the card, the head of its group. The handlers are unchanged but one: the
assistant handler drew a step's message again where it stands when the message was on the page (`isConnected`), and
now when it stands in its scope, on the page or not. The browser's find no longer reaches into a row that never opened.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_closed_helper_rows.py`, in Chrome in a wide window and in WebKit at a phone's width: a
stored chat at work with three
helpers of 200 steps each (one done, one failed, one at work) opens with 1,424 elements, against 13,545 before the
change and 13,645 once every row is open (the test holds it under a fifth, and failed before the change with all 600
steps in the page); each closed row shows its ring, its last line and its time and no step; the helper at work takes
steps while its row is closed (its line says step 203), opened shows all 203 and takes two more; the rows that ended
closed open on their 200 steps and their answers. The time from the chat's route to the chat drawn, 1280 px wide,
three tries each on the Mac: Chrome 80 to 99 ms before, 66 to 69 ms after; WebKit 134 to 142 ms before, 102 to 115 ms
after. The browser tests of the chat, the helpers and the phone: 315 of 319 passed; of the four others, three fail with
main's `timeline.js` as well (`test_watchers`, `test_channels_page`, `test_approval_target`) and one passed again with
fresh folders. `npm run check`.

**Not done.** The steps of a closed row are still built when the chat opens, off the page: the nodes and the memory
they take are as before (Chrome's count of nodes and its JS heap did not move), and so is most of the time to draw
them. Building them at the first open means holding a closed row's events and replaying them through the handlers
then; the handlers paint the row's line and tell the page of a helper's screenshots (`onImage`) as each event comes, so
they would be split first.
