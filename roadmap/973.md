### 973. What Dot asks stands at the composer's place

**Status: done (2026-11-05).** **Asked** by the owner, with a screenshot of another agent app's "User input needed" panel: on a
phone and on a computer, the cards that wait for their answer should come up from the composer, and several should
scroll inside it. They stood in the conversation (an approval, a question, a step handed over, a secret asked for) and
under "Dot asked you" after it (the questions a run left), where a long chat put them out of sight.

**Built.** `asksdock.js`: one panel in the dock, right above the composer, headed "Your answer is needed", with the count
of its cards when there are several and a chevron that folds it to its head; its list scrolls in itself, never taller
than half the window. While it stands open with a card in it, the composer gives it its place (`app.css`, by the
panel's own state), as the cards are where the answer is given; folded, the composer is back. `timeline.js` hands each
card that waits to the page as it arrives (`onAsk`) and takes it back once it is answered (`onAnswered`): a mark keeps
its place in the conversation meanwhile, so what was decided (a refusal, a question's answer) stands where it was asked.
`views/run.js` puts the questions a run left in the panel too, in place of the list under the conversation. A new card
unfolds the panel. Docs: `docs/daemon.md`, `docs/autonomy.md`.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_approval_target.py` (the card stands in the panel and not the conversation, the composer
hidden while it is open and back once it is folded or answered, a refused card back in the chat; on a phone and wide),
`tests/smoke/test_chat_follows.py` (two questions a run left: counted, the list scrolling in itself within half the
window), the other card tests (`test_question_choices`, `test_card_arrives`, `test_owner_asks`, `test_kept_handoffs`,
`test_dock_choices`, `test_ask_secret`, `test_initiative_asks`, `test_chat_calm`), `npm run check`, screenshots at 390
and 1280 px.
