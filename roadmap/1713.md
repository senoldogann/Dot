### 1713. The day's room for lessons is counted by time and shared fairly between kinds of tasks

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The audit of 2026-11-24 (A2, D4/D3b) found two faults in the cap of item 1247 (`[agent]
lessons_per_day`, 40): it counted the runs' `lesson_saved` events, which are deleted with their chat, so deleting chats
gave the day its room back; and it was first come, first served: 82 lessons were dropped by it in a fortnight whatever
they were worth, while the reflection writes most of its lessons at 03:00 in one burst.

What changed (`store/db.py`, `store/models.py`, `agent/lessons.py`, `defaults.toml`):

- A list in the `kv` table (`lessons.saved`, no change of the tables): `auto` records each lesson it saves as a new
  note, with the run's source and whether it had read outside content; a record older than the day the count reads
  goes as the next is written. The day counts these, not events, so it does not shrink when chats are deleted.
- `day_full(saves, source, tainted, cap)`: the cap as before; one source (`ui`, `schedule`, `review`, ...) takes at most
  half of it (`ceil(cap / 2)`); a lesson of a run that read outside content is left out once three quarters are taken,
  keeping the last quarter for clean lessons, which are the ones later runs start with (measured on the owner's Mac:
  72% of clean lessons were handed to a later run, 31% of the others). Each reason says which limit it met; the run's
  page still offers Save anyway.

Not done: ranking lessons by value across runs (holding them back to compare) — every decision is still made as a run
ends, which this keeps; the shares are what makes it fair across sources.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_lessons.py` (the cap, the half for one source, the quarter for clean lessons; they fail
without the change), `tests/smoke/test_lesson_counts.py` (the count stays when the events are deleted, old records go),
`tests/smoke/test_lessons.py`, `test_failure_lessons.py`, `dotx eval check`.
