### 871. An approval card stands alone, with no line above it that says the same

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner with a screenshot of a chat that waited for them. Above the card
an orange line read "Needs approval: Run: cd … (runs vercel; …) — On its own initiative, Dot asks …", and the card
under it said the same words again. OpenMausBot's approvals are quiet: one card, and history settles into one line.
This is part of the design foundation of that study.

**What holds.**
- The policy line that asks for a card ("Needs approval", "Handed to you") is taken out of the chat once its card
  stands right after it. This holds live and when the chat opens again. The card carries the same summary and reason.
- A line no card follows stays: a refusal of Dot's rules ("Blocked by Dot's safety rules").
- A card allowed leaves the chat, as item 434 has it. A card refused, expired or answered settles in place and says
  so.
- The pending card itself is unchanged: what it shows, its buttons, its rule and its "Don't ask again in this chat".

**Where.** `timeline.js` (`approval_requested`, `approval_decided`), `docs/daemon.md`.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_approval_target.py`: at 1280 and 390 px, a waiting card stands with no "Needs approval" line.
  Allowed, the chat shows neither the card nor the line. Refused, the card alone says Denied. Opened again, it reads the
  same.
- `tests/smoke/test_card_arrives.py`: each card stands within 3 s of the run asking, in WebKit, now measured from the
  pending approval, since the line it was measured from is gone.
