### 698. A wait for a text ends when a dialog opens in front of the page

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** **Measured:** 27 % of `browser_wait_text` calls in the owner's month ran out (10 to
15 s each), several with "a dialog is open" in the page they showed: the text could not come until it was answered.

**The change.** `driver.wait_for_text_or_dialog` waits in one Playwright wait for the text or, when no dialog was open
as the wait began, for a dialog (`role=dialog`, `alertdialog`, `dialog[open]`, `aria-modal`) that is not a cookie
banner. A dialog that brings the text is the text. `TextWait.ended_by` says why a wait ended early and the result
says to answer the dialog first. `wait_for_text` is gone.

**Checked.** `test_browser.py`: a dialog 400 ms after a click ends a 10 s wait within 2 s with the reason; with that
dialog open, another wait is for the text alone; a dialog that holds the text is the text. The other wait tests pass.
