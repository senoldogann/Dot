### 1999. A message sent in a chat is in it at once, with a quiet "Pending" and "Starting…", as DotNext does it

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The web side of DotNext's items 1919 and 1927 (gap table: item 1998). On the web, a
follow-up sent in a chat whose run had ended kept its words in the box, with the send disabled, until the request that
starts the next run answered; on a busy Mac nothing moved for that long.

What changed (the web UI only):
- From the send on, the words leave the box and the message's bubble stands under the conversation, as it will stand,
  with a quiet **Pending** (Turkish **Bekliyor**) at its right edge, the one place it shows, and under it the agent's
  typing dots and **Starting…** (**Başlıyor…**), shimmering as the work line does (`pendingMessage`, messages.js). The
  page of the new run, which draws the message itself, replaces it once Dot took it.
- A send Dot refuses (a model not set up, any error) takes the bubble away at once and gives the words back to the box
  (`takeWords` / `giveBack`, composer.js), before what was typed meanwhile; the error says why as before. A message kept
  for the link (no link: the outbox) leaves the bubble for the outbox's own row.
- A steer and a queued message keep their own notes (the run at work's "Sending to Dot", the queue), as the phone does.

How it was checked: `tests/smoke/test_pending_send.py` in Chrome, the page's route holding the request 2.5 s: the bubble,
"Pending" and "Starting…" within 0.5 s of the send, the box empty, the bubble's right edge on the conversation's, then
the new run's page with nothing pending, at 1440 px in English and 390 px in Turkish; a refused send gives the words
back. Clicked through on a throwaway daemon at 1440×900 and 390×844.
