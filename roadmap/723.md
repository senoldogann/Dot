### 723. A schedule switched on stays on, and one that ran once says so

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Asked by the owner:** look deeply into Schedules ("they turn off"). Read with an
agent and checked against the code; the owner's own schedules hold one-time ones that fired and a recurring one whose
firing "failed to start: a run is already active on this thread".

- A one-time schedule that fired is `enabled = 0` (`record_schedule_fire`), and its row read "switched off", as one the
  owner switched off; its switch, pressed, was refused ("no run left to wait for").
- The edit form sent `enabled` as it was when the form opened: a schedule switched on in the list meanwhile went off
  again on save, and one switched off for its failures went on with an edit of its goal.
- A one-time schedule due and still waiting (a paused agent, the emergency stop, a start tried again) was refused an
  edit of its goal or name while switched on.
- A recurring schedule whose chat was busy with the owner's own message failed to start, in grey, and was told as a
  missed firing.

**The change.** The daemon says it (`ran_once` in `ScheduleView`, `scheduler/plans.py`): a one-time schedule whose
firing at its moment started its run (`last_run_at >= run_at`, no "failed to start", not switched off for failures)
reads *ran once*, grey beside its last status (it says nothing of how the run went), with its switch disabled and a
title that says Edit gives it a new time, on the Schedules page and an agent's page. One given a new time after it
fired, or whose start failed, switched off by hand reads "switched off" and can be switched on (pre-push review round 1:
the page's own guess from a firing having happened said "ran once" of them). `PATCH /api/schedules/{id}` without
`enabled` keeps the schedule on or off as it is, and the form sends it only when its own switch was moved. An edit that
leaves a due one-time moment as it was keeps it waiting (`_edited_timing`). A recurring firing refused because its chat
is busy (`RunConflict` with a thread) is "skipped: ...", amber, counting no failure and telling nobody of a fault; a
one-time one keeps its failure, whose wait spaces the next try. "failed to start" is red.

**Not changed.** Two windows of milliseconds remain: an owner's switch-off during a one-time firing whose start then
fails is put back on by `keep_schedule_due`, and a change saved while a firing is being written can be overwritten by
it; both need a firing marker in the table, left for an item of their own.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_schedule_stop.py`: an edit that leaves the switch alone keeps the schedule on and off;
a due one-time schedule's goal is edited and it still waits; on a phone, a fired one-time schedule reads ran once with
its switch disabled, one switched off by hand reads switched off; a schedule re-timed after it fired, or whose start
failed, is not ran once. `tests/smoke/test_scheduler.py`: a busy chat skips a
recurring firing with no failure recorded.
