### 649. Dot's own learning goes on when the review model's quota is spent

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner saw "LESSONS FROM THIS TASK could not be drawn: provider answered HTTP 429:
You reached the Free usage limit" (Ollama Cloud). They asked why, and whether Dot should go on with another provider
that has some limit left, as someone may use one provider alone, or a local model alone.

**Why it happened.**
- The owner's `[models] review` is `ollama:gemma4:cloud` on Ollama Cloud's free plan. Its standby is the same model.
- A run's lessons, their vetting, the upkeep of the notes and the skills, and the playbooks ask the review model.
- Such a question went to the run's own model only when the review model *declined* it (item 249). The run's own model
  was `opencode_go:deepseek-v4.1-flash`, which had answered the run itself.
- A quota error was raised as it came, so the lessons failed.

**The change.** The checks are left alone: they may not pass on.
- `agent/review.py` `_decide_checked_passing_on`, which only Dot's own learning uses:
  - A model that answers with a quota, rate-limit, credit, key or access error (`llm.router.unavailable_reason`) passes
    the question on, as a declining one does.
  - A model the router holds paused is left out while another can be asked (item 240's reason). The question no longer
    spends the connector's retries on a provider that refused already.
  - When no model takes it, the first model's own error is raised, of its own kind, saying who was asked and why.
  - Any other error (transport, protocol) is raised as it comes, as before.
- `llm/roles.py` `ModelRoles.after_review(own)` gives the order: the run's own model, then the standby models, each
  once.
  Every learning helper uses it: lessons and their vetting, the owner's words, outdated notes, merges, skill overlaps
  and revisions, playbooks, and teach. The ones of no run had no one to pass to before.
- **Not passed on.** The goal checker (`goal_checker_for`, a campaign's claim), the `auto` approvals and the rule
  drafter
  still ask the review model alone. The first two are checks of the run, and the run's own model would judge itself.
  The third answers the owner at once, with the error.
- With one provider, or with a model on the Mac alone, the run's own model writes the lessons. There a spent quota is
  the run's too.

**Tests.** `tests/unit/test_review_passes_on.py`:
- a spent review model passes the lessons to the run's model, and the next question skips it;
- no model able to answer raises the quota error, naming who was asked and why;
- a transport error is not passed on;
- `after_review` gives its order.

Turning off the passing on fails two of the tests, and turning off the skipping of a paused model fails one. The tests
of item 249 (`test_review_declined.py`) pass unchanged.

**For the owner.** Their standby is the same Ollama Cloud model as their review model. When its quota is spent, a run
moving to standby meets the same limit. A standby on another provider, Settings › Models › Fallbacks, would carry both.
