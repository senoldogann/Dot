### 1148. A chat says what a send will do, what the task is doing and what came of it

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** The owner asked for the conversation to be dependable and plain in daily use: what a
message will do, what Dot does now, whether it needs a decision, what finished, where the files are, and how to stop.
Reproduced on a daemon with a scripted model (Chrome, 1280, 390 and 320 px) before anything changed:

- A message sent while a task worked steered it or waited in the queue by a setting and ⌘⇧Enter; only the placeholder,
  gone once a word was typed, said which. Ultrathink, Ultracode and the picks on a steer were told by a toast after the
  send (`views/run.js`). The Stop was the send of an empty box, gone while the box held words; a loop's "Stop" deleted
  its schedule.
- The line of the work turned through other words for the same work every four seconds (`workstatus.js`), and a finished
  answer was typed in again over two seconds, its copy and foot hidden meanwhile (`revealAnswer`, item 366).
- The "+" held the files, the model and options and teaching a task; on a phone teaching was a long press only.
- A chat's loops stood open as a card of about 140 px over a phone's composer.
- The Task tab put the recap, then the skills, then "Files made" with "+2" beside every file, debug files and results
  alike.
- The whole interface was English; "YOLO" said nothing of what the mode still asks; a chat the AI service stopped showed
  "provider answered HTTP 402: … https://…/billing … (ref: …)" as its answer and its sidebar line; the sign-in screen on
  the Mac itself said to get a code from Settings › Phone.

What changed (the stored values, the APIs and the policy are as they were):

- **What a send does.** While a task works, a choice of two over the box (`role="radiogroup"`, the arrows move it) says
  **Add to task** (a steer) or **After this task** (the queue); it starts from Settings › General's (`BUSY_SEND`) and
  holds for the page. The send's words, tooltip and name, and the placeholder, are the chosen way's; a hint beside it
  gives what ⌘⇧Enter does. With Add to task chosen and a choice for one message or picks armed, a note in a polite
  status region says before the send that they wait in the box for the next message, with **Send after this task
  instead**; a steer sent the other way with ⌘⇧Enter says it after, as before. Nothing armed is dropped. Not shown where
  one way alone is open (held run, campaign, run a Terminal command drives).
- **Stop.** The bar over the box ends in **Stop task** while the task works, also while the box holds words; pressed,
  the bar and the line say "Stopping…" until it ends, and the focus goes back to the box. The send of an empty box stays
  a Stop. A loop's button says **Stop repeating**, and its dialog that the task at work goes on.
- **Progress from events.** One phrase per kind of work; the words change only on an event. Waiting to start (queued
  until `run_started`), waiting for you (approval, question) and stopping are said on the line, and once each, as a
  change of state, through a `role="status"` region (never each call nor the seconds); the end is said once too. The
  line is `aria-expanded`, not `aria-pressed`.
- **Answers at once.** `revealAnswer` and its CSS are gone: a finished answer is drawn whole with its foot; streaming of
  a step's words, reduced motion and the landing (a reader of older messages is left there, with the way down) are as
  they were.
- **Updates and the answer.** A message on the way to its calls (the `assistant` event's calls, `narrates`) is a group
  named "Progress update" with a rule at its left; other messages "Message"; the answer's article "Answer". Stored words
  are untouched.
- **Composer.** The "+" adds: files and **Teach a task**; the run's choices have their own **Model and options** button
  (`aria-pressed`, same kept choice); **/teach** is in the slash menu of a chat's box. A phone's tap of "+" still opens
  the picker at once (item 1089), and a phone shows the choices while the box is used (item 1120).
- **Loops.** Folded until opened (`dot.fold.chat-loops`): one line "Repeats every 30 minutes · next 06:00"; not a live
  region (it was read out at every reload of the chat); an unread list says why as an alert.
- **Task tab.** **Results** (the files the answer names, from its file chips and words: name over folder, kind, opens
  beside the conversation) and **Outcome** (how the run ended per `run_finished`, and what it needs) come first, then
  the recap, **Sources**, **Changed files** (with their lines), Skills & connected apps, Memory updated. No file is
  classed by a guess, and "done" is not said to be verified.
- **Sidebar.** An agent's name takes two lines before it is cut (its whole name stays its tooltip and label); a chat the
  AI service stopped says "Stopped: the AI service's balance or plan" (by the HTTP status the run recorded) instead of
  the service's sentence. The loop and campaign marks are as items 580 and 705 made them.
- **Errors.** A failed answer whose words are a service's failure (`errors.js` `serviceFailure`, the format of
  `llm/provider_errors.py`) says "The AI service stopped this task", what to do by its status (key, balance or plan,
  limit, a fault of the service, a refusal), that what Dot did stays in the chat, and keeps the service's line, its link
  among it, under a closed Details. Continue is offered as before, only where the run is resumable.
- **Language.** `i18n.js` with `lang/en.js` and `lang/tr.js`: Settings › General › Messages › **Language** (English
  until chosen, kept in the browser; never the browser's, so a Mac whose Chrome speaks Turkish runs the tests in
  English); `tsc` refuses an unknown key or a Turkish catalogue without one, and `tests/js/i18n.test.js` checks both
  ways and the places. It covers the chat page (composer, intent, bar, line, answer, outcome, resume line, tabs and Task
  tab), the loops and their rhythms, the sidebar's chat list, the errors' sentences, the approval modes, the indicators
  and the sign-in screen; dates follow the language. Other settings pages stay English.
- **Approval mode.** `yolo` is named **Ask only to pay or send** ("Pay or send" on its pill), its hint naming what it
  asks (in a chat you opened a payment, a message or mail, a public post; in a chat Dot opened only a message, a post or
  a release, item 850); every sentence that said YOLO says the mode's name or "without asking"; "asking nothing (YOLO)"
  on the initiative card was wrong and says "asking only before a message, a post or a release", what `initiative_guard`
  asks. One sentence is left as it is: the card of a question only the owner answers says the policy's own reason, "In
  yolo too, Dot asks you before …" (`OWNERS_REASON` in `src/dotagent/policy/engine.py`, which
  `tests/unit/test_static_ui.py` holds the card's words to); the policy is a path that needs the owner's look, so its
  wording waits for an item of its own.
- **Sign-in.** On a loopback address the screen says to open the Dot app or sign in from Terminal (the fold open), and
  that the code is a phone's; elsewhere as before. The ways in, the key, the pairing and the checks are unchanged.

Decided with two independent reviews (conversation UX and accessibility; implementation risk): a radio group, not a
menu; "After this task", not "Queue next"; not "Few asks"; no live region on the line or the timer; the reveal's tests
replaced, not kept. Kept against them: the language work in this item (each item one commit), and the Model and options
button out of the "+" although items 833, 1089 and 1120 had put it there, as the owner's request of 2026-11-13 asks the
"+" to mean add.
