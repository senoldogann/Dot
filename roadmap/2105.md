### 2105. The capsule over the composer drops Working and its Stop

**Status: in progress (2026-11-28).** The owner's words of 2026-11-28 (iPhone, DotNext): the "Working" segment and the
Stop of the capsule over the composer said again what the transcript's work line and the composer's Stop say.

What must hold:
- A run that simply works (or starts) has no segment in the capsule, and the capsule has no Stop, on DotNext, on the
  web's phone row (`dockbar.js`) and on the web's bar over the composer at computer widths (`capsule.js`).
- Stopping the run stays one tap away: the composer's button, its field empty, is the Stop on both sides (DotNext's
  `composerButtons`, the web's item 120), and the ask dock keeps its own Stop.
- The capsule keeps what is more than work: Waiting for you (with its clock on DotNext), Waiting on N helpers (only
  while the run has stopped for them), Waiting to start, Stopping…, Can't reach Dot, Offline, and the plan, helpers,
  loops, queue and commands segments; with none of them it is not drawn.

Render cost, measured on the web with a working run (Chrome, CDP `Performance.getMetrics` over 10 s, three runs each,
before and after): 390 px wide, layouts 10-21 to 10, layout time 2.2-4.6 ms to 1.5-2.1 ms, script 17-19 ms to 11 ms;
1440 px, layouts 10-11 to 0-6, script 9-16 ms to 6-9 ms. Style recalculations (about 600, from the page's animations)
did not change. On DotNext the capsule's once-a-second `TimelineView` no longer runs while a run simply works (it runs
only beside Waiting on you); not measured on a device.

How it was checked: `tests/js/dockwords.test.js`, `tests/smoke/test_phone_dock_capsule.py` (320 to 430 px, light and
dark), `tests/smoke/test_phone_dock.py` (the composer's send is the Stop), `tests/smoke/test_pending_send.py`,
`ChatUITests.testAWorkingRunHasNoStatusOrStopInTheCapsuleAndTheComposerIsItsStop` and the capsule tests beside it.
