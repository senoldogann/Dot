### 2003. Where the seconds go: `dotx perf` and Settings › Maintenance › Speed

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner asked for production-level speed, measured the way a service is: a table of
latencies with their p99 and the count of slow requests. Dot recorded the seconds of every model call and tool call,
but nothing put them together, so the lead measured the owner's store by hand (89 runs in three days: a run's start to
its first model call p50 3.5 s, p95 13.6 s, p99 35.2 s; the time between two steps p99 21 s; DeepSeek's step calls p99
104 s).

What changed: `dotx perf [--window 24h|7d]` and Settings › Maintenance › Speed (`GET /api/diagnostics/speed`) print one
table, stage by stage, read from the store read-only: a run's start to its first model call and each read of that start
(the `start_reads` event of item 2004), the time between two steps and Dot's own part of it (less the model calls the
loop makes between them: a compaction, a goal check, a checkpoint), the model calls by model and purpose, the first word
of a step's reply by model, and each tool's running time. Each row: n, p50, p95, p99 (nearest rank), the longest and how
many took over 3 s. A gap the owner held with a ✋ and a call their stop cut off are left out. The statistics are pure
functions (`dotagent/store/perf.py`); the API's own routes are not timed, so they have no row.

The first table of the owner's store (7 days, 203 runs): start → first model call p50 3.9 s, p95 18.8 s, p99 305.6 s;
between two steps p95 2.4 s, p99 23.9 s, of which Dot's own p95 1.2 s, p99 5.8 s; DeepSeek's step calls p50 4.7 s,
p99 57.2 s; its first word p95 8.6 s, p99 34.5 s.

How it was checked: `tests/unit/test_perf.py` (the percentiles, a run's stages from a fixture of events, what is left
out, the read-only reader), `tests/smoke/test_about_pages.py::test_the_speed_page_shows_each_stage_of_the_runs_with_its_percentiles`
(the API and the page in Chrome, wide and light, on a phone and dark), the settings' rows in `test_settings_nav.py` and
`test_phone_settings.py`, `npm run check`, ruff and mypy.
