### 459. A change's changelog entry and roadmap item in files of its own

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The owner's question of 2026-10-21: why the open pull requests were not merged. All five
open then conflicted with `main`, and only in `CHANGELOG.md` and `ROADMAP.md`: each pull request wrote its entry at the
top of Unreleased and its item and log line at the end of the roadmap, the same lines as every other, so each merge left
the others in conflict, and GitHub ran no gate on a pull request in conflict. Resolving by keeping both sides also
brought back lines a later commit had rewritten (item 433's yolo line beside the one that replaced it, found by the
pre-push review of #443), and an item put by its context line could land at the top of the file. **As built.** A
change's entry is `changes/<item>.md` (`changes/README.md`), its item `roadmap/<item>.md` with its log lines
(`roadmap/README.md`); ROADMAP.md keeps the items up to 458 and says so at its top, CHANGELOG.md's Unreleased says
where the rest is. `scripts/fold_changes.py <version> <date>` makes a release's section from the entries under
Unreleased and the files of `changes/`, the newest item first, keeps Unreleased's note, removes the files, and refuses
a file that is not a list or not named for its item, a version released already and a release with nothing in it
(`tests/unit/test_fold_changes.py`). CLAUDE.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, REVIEW.md and docs/development.md's Releasing say so.
