### 644. The plan over the composer

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner asked that, while a run works and has written a plan, the line over the
composer (where it says "Working 1m 37s") show the plan too, and that a tap open it as a bubble. A mock was shown to
them before it was built, and they liked it.

**The change.**
- `capsule.js`: a part **Plan 1/3** (its name "Plan, 1 of 3 steps done"), there while the run works and has a plan.
- `planbubble.js`, new: the plan's drawing moves here from `views/run.js` (`planStep`, `planTargets`), and
  `planBubble` opens the plan over the dock, in the composer's popover (`anchoredPopover`, which takes the part's click
  itself), with:
  - how far the plan has come;
  - its targets;
  - its steps as the Plan tab draws them;
  - **Open in the side panel**.
  It follows the plan while it is open; Escape, a click outside or a second click closes it.
- `views/run.js`: the plan the timeline reports paints the Plan tab, the part and the bubble; once the part goes (the
  run no longer works), an open bubble closes with it rather than stay over nothing.
- `app.css`: the bubble is a little wider than the composer's menus and keeps within the window above the line.
- `popover.js`: a panel's place is measured on the screen against its frame. Before, it took the control's offsets,
  which count from the control's offset parent: for the plan part, the bar round the capsule (its backdrop filter makes
  it one), which put the bubble 534 px left of its part at 1280 px. The composer's own menus, whose controls' offset
  parent is the frame, stand where they stood.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_plan_bubble.py`, new. Over a static page with the page's own `capsule.js` and `planbubble.js`, at
  390 and 1280 px:
  - the part says Plan 1/3;
  - a click opens the bubble above the line and inside the window, with the targets and the three steps;
  - its left edge is at the part's, or kept 8 px inside the dock (it failed on the old placement: 127 px for 661);
  - it follows the plan to 2/3;
  - Escape closes it, and **Open in the side panel** opens the tab;
  - a run that no longer works has no part.
- The same file on a real run: its scripted model writes a plan of two steps with `update_plan` and goes on working.
  The part says Plan 0/2, the bubble shows the steps over the line and inside the window, and its way leads to the Plan
  tab. Opened again and the run stopped, the part and the bubble go.
- The browser tests of the composer's other popovers (the model, the reasoning, the modes, the chat size; 36) pass.
- On the first try a click opened the bubble and closed it again: the popover takes the part's click itself, and the
  part had a handler of its own. The test caught it, and the part now has none.
