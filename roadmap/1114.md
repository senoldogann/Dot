### 1114. A tap on a notification no longer closes the iPhone app

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** Asked by the owner: "when a run ends a notification comes; when I tap it the app closes
completely." The phone's crash reports (five on 2026-11-10, 05:12 to 09:43) all stop in
`AppDelegate.userNotificationCenter(_:didReceive:)`: an assertion of UIKit's
(`-[UIApplication _performBlockAfterCATransactionCommitSynchronizes:]`, as it updates the app's snapshot) when the
completion of the tap's handling came from a thread that is not the main one. The delegate's methods were the `async`
forms, `nonisolated`, whose completion Swift calls from its own threads.

Now both (`willPresent`, `didReceive`) are the forms with a completion handler, which read what the notification
carries at once and finish on the main actor: the tap opens the route in the web view, or answers the approval, and
then tells iOS it is done, on the main thread.

Checked: the iPhone app built for a device and installed on the owner's phone; a tap is the owner's to try.
