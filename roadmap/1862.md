### 1862. Connect apps on the phone: one sheet over the Mac's catalogue

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** Helper G2 of PLAN §27: the owner's grok-connect-apps.png in Dot's words and logos.

What must hold: one sheet lists every service of the Mac's Connectors catalogue with where it stands, finds a service
or a skill by a word, says for each how it is connected from here, and never offers a step the daemon refuses to a
phone (item 1861).

What was built (`ios/Next`):
- `Settings/ConnectAppsSheet.swift`: `ConnectAppsSheet`. ✕, "Connect apps" and an "N connected" pill that lists the
  connected services alone; the sections as chips (All first, the daemon's order); Featured (the web's Popular, at most
  eight), then each section under a header that picks its chip; a row per service (logo tile, name, one line, a Connect,
  Connected or Waiting pill); a floating glass search capsule, "Search apps and skills", over the services and the
  palette's skills. A row opens the detail: what it does, each way with how it connects, "Connected as" (accounts
  signed in, the vaults 1Password reads), Disconnect behind a confirmation. Connect starts a Mac step
  (`POST /api/connections/{id}`) and the row then says "Finish on your Mac"; a service the daemon takes from the Mac
  alone says the same at once and its detail names the Mac's page, with Copy instructions. No "For you" (no source).
- `Resources/PluginLogos.xcassets` from `scripts/sync_plugin_logos.py`: the web's 103 logo files as template vectors,
  drawn in the theme's ink as the web draws them (item 946); a glyph maps to an SF Symbol; else the initial.
- Settings: a Connect apps row and a search entry (PLAN §27 overrides §26's cut of Connectors); helper G1 opens the
  same sheet from the home's apps button and the settings sheet.
- Fixtures `settings-connect-apps`, `settings-connect-apps-detail`, `settings-connect-apps-offline`;
  `UITests/ConnectAppsLiveUITests.swift`, run by `scripts/e2e_settings.sh` against a throwaway daemon.
- Turkish strings in `translations_tr.py`, block G2.
