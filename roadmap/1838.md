### 1838. Watch the Mac on the iPhone, natively

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** The owner: "Watching my computer from the phone and following the agent in real time
matter a lot to me — and design." Dot Next's Activity › Watch the Mac opened the web page in a web view.

What must hold: the Mac's screen full screen on the phone, live with a short delay, zoomable (pinch, double tap) and
pannable, in landscape too; it says what stands in place of the picture (connecting, reconnecting with why, the daemon's
refusal with Try again); nothing is read while the viewer is closed or the app is away; a weak link gets a picture that
keeps up rather than one that stalls.

What was built:
- Engine (`ios/DotEngine/Sources/DotEngine/Watch/`, pure): the live stream's frames (`splitLiveFrames`, the 13-byte
  header of `desktop/live.py`), a key frame's `avcC` and its parameter sets (`avcConfiguration`), the frame meter (first
  frame, frames a second), the lag meter (each frame's arrival less its capture time, against the smallest seen; twelve
  frames in a row more than 1 s behind is a link too slow for HD) and the quality rule (Automatic: Low data on a link
  iOS calls expensive or constrained, or after HD fell behind). `DaemonAPI.screenLive(quality:)` reads the answer in
  the chunks the network hands over (`ChunkReceiver`, a session of its own from the stream session's configuration).
- App (`ios/Next/Watch/`): `H264Feeder` turns frames into sample buffers for an `AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer` (the
  phone's hardware decoder, each frame shown at once: the newest is the screen now); `ScreenFeed` reads on a task of its
  own and tells the main thread the picture's size, first frame and frame rate at most twice a second, so the screen
  never stutters; a broken stream is read again after 1, 2, 4, then 8 s, a 4xx is said in the daemon's words.
  `LiveCanvas` (a scroll view) fits the picture, zooms it 1x to 5x and keeps it centred. `WatchMacScreen`: black
  ground, dark glass chrome (✕, the Mac's name with a live dot and "Live · HD · 58 fps", rotate, the quality menu:
  Automatic, HD, Low data), hidden after 3 s of a live picture and back on a tap.
- Activity's row opens it natively (the web view row is gone).

Measured (2026-11-25, the owner's MacBook loaded with other helpers' simulators): the daemon's `hd` stream read by a
script over loopback: first frame 0.5 to 2.9 s (the capture starting), 27 to 30 frames a second on a quiet desktop,
0.6 to 0.9 Mbit/s. In the app on the simulator (`WatchProbe`): first frame 6.8 and 9.7 s (ScreenCaptureKit slow to
start on the loaded Mac), then 10 to 24 frames a second. Not measured: `light` and any throttled link for the screen,
because ScreenCaptureKit failed to start for every try in that hour ("did not list the displays in time", while the
disk was full); the viewer said "Reconnecting…" and tried again, as built.
