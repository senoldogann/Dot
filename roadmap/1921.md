### 1921. DotNext opens and closes a work-log row without a hitch

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner: expanding a command or tool row in a chat and collapsing it again stutters,
"like a hitch".

Measured first, on the fixture `n2-jank` (a working chat whose command rows return 600 and 400 lines of output; the
debug frame meter `-DotNextToggleMeter 1`, `scripts/jank.sh`, `UITests/WorkLogJankUITests.swift`; iPhone 17 Pro Max
simulator on a loaded Mac, so read the numbers against the short-row control below):
- Opening the 600-line row blocked the main thread for 0.80-0.98 s, every time (the longest wait of the 8 ms probe), and
  closing it for 80-155 ms with 2-5 hitches.
- The row's top jumped 217 pt up when it opened (713 → 496) and back when it closed.

Two causes:
- The row's detail was one `Text` in a `ScrollView`; a `Text` lays out the whole of its string before it draws, so the
  600 lines were laid out on the main thread on each open.
- While the owner was at the end of the transcript the follow (`scrollTo(edge: .bottom)` on every change of the content's
  height, item 1848) ran on every frame of the row's spring animation and chased the growing row.

What changed:
- `OutputBox` (`Components/OutputBox.swift`): a detail past 24 lines or 3,000 characters is a `UITextView` on TextKit 2,
  which lays out only the lines in view, selectable and scrolling in itself; shorter ones stay a `Text`. Used by the tool
  row and by a stored thought.
- `EnvironmentValues.disclosureToggled`: a row of the work log (step, thought, fold, command group) tells the transcript
  when the owner opens or closes it, and the transcript stops following (`pinned = false`) until the owner is at its end
  again; the row stays where it is, the end of the page does not chase it.
- The meter: `FrameMeter.toggles` and `ToggleMeter` (debug builds only).

How it was checked, before → after (before on the base of this branch's first commit, after on the current `main`;
the Mac was quieter for the last run, so the one-line row on the same screen is the control; main-thread wait = the
longest wait of the 8 ms probe during one second after the tap):
- Opening the 600-line row: 797-983 ms → 8-18 ms (the first open of a process, 149-310 ms across the runs, builds
  TextKit's machinery; a warm-up when the chat opens did not remove it, so it stays).
- Closing it: 80-155 ms → 9-15 ms. Hitches per toggle 2-5 → 0-1. Control, a one-line row: 5-37 ms.
- The row's top before, opened and closed: 713.3 / 713.3 / 713.3 (before: 713 → 496 → 713) in all 6 rounds
  (`WorkLogJankUITests` asserts it). The raw lines are in the owner's workspace,
  `ios-rewrite-2026-11-24/jank-and-chat-sheet-2026-11-26/`.
