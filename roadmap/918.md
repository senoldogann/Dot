### 918. iMessage reads plain: no mark to see, short answers, quiet progress, tells in the owner's language

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-02): "iMessage messages could be plainer and neater;
remove the round dot at the start of the agent's message". Of the DeepSeek Harness "Super-agent" article's proposals,
the channel norm (an IM reply in 100 words at most) goes with it. What the owner saw: every message of Dot's started with
`◆ `, a tell was *Dot did on its own: …* in English under a Turkish conversation, and a run's progress was up to six
messages of up to ten lines, raw shell commands (`$ ls -la …`) and a 💭 among them.

**Built.**
- `channels/imessage.py`: the mark of Dot's messages is a zero-width space (`MARKER`), which a phone draws as nothing
  and its keyboard does not type; a row that starts with the old `◆` is Dot's still (`marked`). Should Messages drop
  the zero-width space, a row of the words of a message Dot sent from five seconds before to a minute after (whole
  or all but their last word however short, as a tell whose link Messages split off, or a start of them of 20
  characters or more: `same_words`) is let go as Dot's own and logged
  (`imessage_message_let_go`, *the words of a message of Dot's, unmarked*), so that the log says it at the first tell.
  What Dot sent is kept in the store (`imessage.sent`) before Messages has it, so that a restart in between changes
  nothing (the third review: a tell of a mail's subject must never come back as a command).
- `channels/progress.py`: one message a minute at most, four in all, three steps to a message (the newest, no count of
  the earlier), a command told as *ran a command*, never its text, and the thought as *Thought: …* rather than 💭.
- `channels/words.py`: `Words.titles`, the titles Dot gives its tells in the owner's language (*Kendi başıma yaptım*,
  *Bir işi bıraktım*, *Bir iş emri seni bekliyor*, *Sabah özetin*, and the four of a trading exit); a watcher's name
  stays as the owner wrote it. A trading exit's title no longer names its product (*An exit did not sell*), which leads
  its line instead. The
  initiative's *Dot let go of a thing after N tries* is *Dot let go of a thing* (the count is on its page), so that it
  has one title to say.
- `agent/prompt.py`: a run started from a channel writes its answer as a message, in 100 words at most: the result
  first, then what the owner must know or do, anything longer in a file it names.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_imessage_channel.py` (the mark is a zero-width space; Dot's words without it, or without
their link, are let go and logged, the owner's short words are theirs), `test_channel_progress.py`,
`test_channel_words.py` (every title Dot gives has its Turkish), `test_channel_answer_style.py`, and the router's smoke
test of progress. Not checked here: that Messages keeps a zero-width space in `chat.db` (this terminal has no Full Disk
Access); the log line above says so on the owner's Mac at the first message after the update.
