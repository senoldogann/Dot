### 645. A page at rest costs next to nothing: what repeats is drawn by the compositor

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner asked for Dot's performance problems to be found and solved, in the chat, in
the background and everywhere else.

**What was measured.**
- The owner's Dot.app at rest, read with `ps`: its web process kept 21 % of a core busy, its GPU process 9 % and the
  app 4 %.
- The same page in a headless Chromium on the owner's daemon, read-only, with their longest chat open: 3.3 to 6.7 s of
  the main thread's work in 30 s at rest.
- With reduced motion the same page took 0.10 s in 30 s at rest.
- Turning the endless animations off one at a time named the cost: the loop mark's glow (`loop-glow`, item 580) animated
  a `box-shadow`. With it off, the page took 0.13 s in 30 s. The spin of the mark's arrows and the face's breath are
  transforms, and turning them off changed nothing.
- A shadow that changes is drawn again on every frame, with its style computed again. Three loops in the sidebar kept
  that going for as long as the window was open.

**The change.**
- **The glow.** It breathes in a layer of its own (`.side-loop::after`) whose `opacity` changes, which the compositor
  draws. The mark's own shadow stays still. The layer draws the ring of the row's ground over its glow, as the mark's
  shadow did, so the glow never tints the ring.
- **A working run's dot.** It sends out its ring as a disc that grows (`transform`) and fades (`opacity`). Before, it
  was a shadow that grew (`pulse`). Over the dot the disc is the dot's own colour. The ring reaches 8 px past every
  dot, as before: 2.6 times a 10 px dot, and `--pulse-reach: 3` on the 8 px dots of the sidebar, its helpers and the
  background rows.
  - Under reduced motion that dot now stands still too, as `docs/daemon.md` already said every pulse does. It had
    pulsed on.
- **Measured after,** the same page, the owner's own data, with the new sheets served in place of the old: 0.13 s in
  30 s at rest, against 1.3 to 5.5 s before.
- **The look did not change.** Both were photographed at three times the size, at the glow's peak and at rest, in the
  light and dark schemes, old beside new, and a working dot at 0, 25, 50 and 75 % of its pulse. They are the same.
- **What still redraws,** each only while something works in front of the owner:
  - the microphone's ring while it listens, and the plan step at work (`pulse`);
  - the light over the words of a thought being written, or of a chat being opened (`shimmer`, `sunshine`).
- **The guard.** `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py::test_what_repeats_forever_is_drawn_by_the_compositor` holds every
  endless animation to opacity and transform and names those three. It fails on the old sheet.
- `tests/smoke/test_chat_loop.py` reads the glow from its layer, and checks that the layer changes opacity alone.
