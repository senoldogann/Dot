### 1089. On a phone the composer's "+" opens the files at once

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** Asked by the owner, with screenshots of another assistant on the iPhone: its "+" brings
up the phone's own menu (photos, the camera, a scan, the files) at once; Dot's needed a tap of "+" and then of
**Add files**.

Now a finger's tap of the "+" on a phone opens the file picker at once, and iOS draws its own menu over it. A finger's
hold opens Dot's menu of the "+" (**Add files**, **Model and options**, **Teach a task**), as a hold of a chat row opens
its menu (item 1079), and the phone's own callout does not come up over it. A mouse's click, a key's press and a
tablet's tap open the menu as before; a press that became a scroll leaves no finger behind for a key's press.

The click that ends a hold is stopped with `stopImmediatePropagation` (`menu.js` `openOnHold`, the pre-push review):
here the click is aimed at the "+" itself, whose own `onclick` would open the picker over the sheet in an engine that
runs a target's other listeners after `stopPropagation` (Chromium does not, so the test passes on either).

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_chat_menu.py` (a tap opens the file chooser and no sheet, a hold the sheet; fails on main),
`tests/smoke/test_composer.py`, `tests/smoke/test_teach_repeat_panel.py`, `npm run check`.
