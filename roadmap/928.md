### 928. A channel's answer is cut to 100 words by the code, the rest a link away

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Asked** by the owner, the fifth of the gaps they took from the DeepSeek Harness
"Super-agent" article (2026-11-02, in this order: thread pass-through, fan-out, an outside watchdog, ids kept through a
compaction, a channel's cap). The article's channel norm is an IM reply of 100 words at most; item 918 told the model so
in its prompt, and its lesson is that what a prompt asks a model does not do every time: what must hold is the code's.

- `channels/text.py`: `first_words` (the start of a text up to its n-th word, ended at the last end of a sentence in the
  last third of them when there is one) and `cut_answer` (those words, then the line that says where the rest is, as
  `cut_with_link` says it; within the channel's characters in any case).
- `channels/router.py`: a run's answer goes through `cut_answer` with `[channels] answer_words` (100; 0 sends it whole).
- `docs/channels.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_channel_text.py` (a short answer whole; sentences cut at a sentence end within 100 words
with the link; a run-on text at the hundredth word; 0 whole; the characters still bound it) and
`tests/smoke/test_channel_router.py` (a long answer that comes back on the channel has at most 100 words before the
link).
