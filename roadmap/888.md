### 888. A phone's drawer has no X: a swipe to the left closes it

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner, with a screenshot of the sidebar open on their iPhone: "mobildeki
sol üstteki çarpıyı kaldır."

**What holds.**
- On a touch screen of a phone's width (720 px and less, `pointer: coarse`), the drawer's head shows no X. Dot's name
  stands where it stood.
- Three things close the drawer: a chat chosen, as before; a swipe to the left on the drawer, mostly sideways and past
  56 px; and Escape.
- A swipe up and down, which scrolls the chats, does not close it.
- A narrow window of the Mac, with no touch, keeps its button.

**Where.**
- `static/js/sidebar.js`: the `side-close` class, and `closesOnSwipe`.
- `static/css/shell.css`.
- `docs/daemon.md`.

**Checked.**
- `tests/smoke/test_phone_fit.py`: on a Pixel 7 in Chrome, the drawer shows no X, a swipe down leaves it open, and a
  swipe to the left closes it; a 390 px window with no touch keeps the X.
- The two WebKit iPhone tests that closed the drawer by its X close it with Escape. WebKit makes no touch of a page's
  own.
- A preview on the owner's Dot at 390 px with touch.
- The phone pages, back, layout, screens and the Mac's lights tests pass.
