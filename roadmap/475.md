### 475. A held run's page is used from the phone

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The phone's half of taking over Dot's browser (the seventh of the nine ideas from
OpenBot; item 470 is the server's half). **As built.** `pointer.js`, pure and tested in `tests/js/pointer.test.js`:
where a point falls on the picture as fractions of the page (`fractionOf`, the bands of a picture drawn whole in a box
of another shape not the page, a zoom of the screen's own taken from the box), what a touch has come to (a tap, a scroll
past 8 px, a press after 450 ms still), a double tap, how far the page scrolls for a finger's move in its own pixels,
the zoom of two fingers kept around the point between them (1 to 4 times), and the names Playwright gives keys and
chords. `control.js` puts a layer on the picture while the reader uses the page: a tap is a move, a press and a release
(two clicks for a double tap), a finger dragged sends wheel events, a press held then dragged sends a press, moves and a
release, two fingers zoom the picture by CSS variables (no inline styles), a mouse and a trackpad go as they are (hover
moves now and then, the wheel in pixels), and a field kept out of sight, 16 px high so that a phone does not zoom into
it, takes the screen's keyboard: a key or a chord with Control, Alt or Meta goes as it is, text as text (in pieces of
500), Backspace and Enter as keys, a composition once composed. Input goes in order, one batch at a time, moves and
scrolls every 50 ms, a press, a release and a key at once. `computer.js`: **Use it here** beside Take over (first on a
touch screen) posts `/takeover/screen` unless the run is held already, opens the view over the whole page and starts the
layer with a **Keyboard** button; a refusal because a step is still going says "Dot is finishing a step", one because
the run was given back elsewhere ends the layer; Return control, a hand-back seen in the timeline, closing the view and
the run's end end it. The card of a browser step handed to the owner offers it on the run page (the timeline's card,
through `createTimeline`'s `onUseHere`, as the critique found: the card of a left question never has the offer); every
other card says `onUseHere: null`. `index.html` preloads both modules. Yolo is untouched. Tests:
`tests/js/pointer.test.js`; `tests/smoke/test_takeover_input.py` (at 390 by 844 as a touch screen in the dark: Use it
here opens the view, a tap clicks the page's button, a finger dragged up scrolls the page, Keyboard types into its field
and Enter submits its form, two fingers zoom the picture and a tap on it still clicks, Return control stops it all; the
card of a browser step handed to the owner offers Use it here and it opens the view). Docs: `docs/daemon.md`,
`docs/phone.md`.
