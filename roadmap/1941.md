### 1941. A restart whose load launchd refuses tries again with waits that grow

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The review of 2026-11-26 (M10, CFG-01 of 2026-11-19): `launchd.restart_job` unloads the
daemon's job and loads it again; a load launchd refused was tried once more after 2 s and then kickstarted (item 1310),
which fails on a job that is not loaded, so launchd's busy label ("Input/output error") lasting a few seconds after the
unload left Dot down until the watchdog's next look, about a minute, with runs cut meanwhile.

What changed: `load_job` tries the load again after 2, 5 and 10 s (`_BOOTSTRAP_RETRIES_S`), logging each refusal with
launchctl's words (`service_bootstrap_failed`), before the kickstart; the error, when nothing starts the job, says how
many tries failed. The restart itself reloads the job from the same property list, so a successful later try is the
old job put back. docs/daemon.md says it.

How it was checked: `tests/unit/test_restart_pending.py` (one, three and four refusals; the waits 2, 5, 10 s; the
error naming the last load and the kickstart), with the launchd, restart and watchdog tests.
