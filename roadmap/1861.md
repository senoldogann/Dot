### 1861. DotNext's engine reads the Connectors catalogue and says what a phone may start

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** Helper G2 of PLAN §27 (Grok Bot logic): the engine part of Connect apps on the phone.

What must hold: the phone shows the same services, sections and connected state as the web's Connectors page
(`views/connectors.js`, `catalogue.js`), and offers only the steps the daemon takes from a phone's key. Measured before:
`POST /api/mcp/servers` calls `refuse_from_a_phone` (item 678), `POST /api/accounts/{provider}/start` and
`POST /api/vault/connect|token` call `on_this_mac`, and `POST /api/connections/{id}` (a Mac application's question, a
synced folder's app, Dot's browser's sign-in page) is taken from any key and answered on the Mac. So no key or token is
ever typed on the phone: the SecureField and OAuth flows the brief named have no route that takes them from a phone, and
opening one would need a change to the phone refusals of item 678, which is the owner's to decide.

What was built (`ios/DotEngine`):
- `Contract/ConnectModels.swift`: the answers of `GET /api/plugins`, `/connections`, `/mcp/servers`, `/vault`,
  `/accounts` and `DELETE /api/accounts/{provider}/{address}`, the part the sheet reads; kinds and states stay strings.
- `Settings/ConnectCatalog.swift`: `connectApps` reads them as `catalogue.js` does (a way is connected when its server
  is ready, its application allowed, signed in or synced, or 1Password reads a vault; an official sign-in connects
  Google and Microsoft 365) and gives each service its `ConnectAction` (connected, a Mac step the phone starts, on the
  Mac alone, waiting, nothing) and what Disconnect takes back; `connectSections`, `filterConnectApps`, `filterSkills`.
- `Wire/DaemonAPI+Connect.swift`: the five reads asked together, the Mac step, and the three Disconnects.

Not done: "For you" has no source: the daemon suggests no service from what is connected (the plugin scout's proposals
