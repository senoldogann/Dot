### 1072. A late answer of an opening that hung notes no newer build once the copy gave way

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** Found as `main`'s browser test of a slow network
(`test_phone_return.py::test_a_copy_of_an_earlier_build_opened_on_a_slow_network_gives_way_to_the_daemons_pages`)
failed three times in one day, each time passing on a rerun: the cache of the daemon's build was not alone within 15
s, a note of a newer build standing beside it.

Why (`sw.js`): an opening while the Mac was out of reach is answered by the copy of an earlier build, its request to the
network hanging; the late answer, when it comes, notes a newer build (`noteNewer`, item 359), so that the next opening
waits for the network. A later opening that the network answered keeps the daemon's build and takes the copy and the
note away (`keepPage`). When the hung request's answer came after that, it noted the newer build again, and that note
stood for good: every later opening of the phone waited `NEWER_WAIT_MS` (5 s) for the network.

Now `noteNewer` notes a newer build only while the copy the opening was answered from is kept: once a page of the
network's build took it away, there is nothing older to note.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_phone.py` (in the real worker: a late answer after the copy gave way notes nothing, one
while it is kept notes the newer build; it fails without the change) and `test_phone_return.py`, five runs of the test
that failed.
