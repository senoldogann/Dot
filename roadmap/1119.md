### 1119. A chat's bar on a phone has no ground: the conversation scrolls under its glass

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** The owner asked, with Grok Bot's chat as the example, for the way back to be a round pane of glass as the others are and
for the black band behind the bar to go, so that only the bar's parts have a ground. Before, the chat scrolled between
the bar and the composer (item 352), so the bar's transparent ground (item 1113) showed the page's colour, and the
way back was a bare chevron.

Now, on a phone's chat page, the bar stands over the conversation as the composer does at its foot (`run.css`): the
conversation's top is padded by the bar's height and scrolls under it, a touch between the bar's parts goes through
to the conversation, the way back is a 40 px circle of glass and the tools' pill glass too (`app.css`). A menu of the
composer opens over the bar.

Checked: screenshots of a scripted chat on an iPhone 15 Pro (WebKit, dark) before and after, `npm run check`, the
browser tests of the phone and the composer.
