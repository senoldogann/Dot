### 1135. The page's text is read on the page that replaced it while it was read

**Status: done (2026-11-12).** **Found** by Dot's own record (a repair, seen 6 times, `tool_error`): `browser_act`'s
read of the whole page failed with "reading the page text failed ... Execution context was destroyed, most likely
because of a navigation", on a job ad of careers.nordea.com and an application form of ats.talentadore.com among others.

**Cause.** `visible_text` read the whole page with one `page.evaluate`, and a page that sends itself on as it loads (a
redirect, a single-page app that replaces its document) destroyed the script's context under it. The other reads of a
page (its settling, its size, its snapshot, its title) are made again on the new document once it is parsed (item 752);
the read of its text was left out.

**Built.** `browser/driver.py`: `visible_text` reads the whole page through `_again_after_navigation`, five tries at
most; an element's read (a `ref`) is not repeated, as its ref belongs to the document it was read on. The URL rules
still hold after the read (`_read_held`). Docs: `docs/browser-and-computer-use.md`.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_redirect_chain.py::test_the_page_text_is_read_on_the_page_that_replaced_it_while_it_was_read`:
a page whose document is replaced under the first two reads; it failed without the change and passes with it.
