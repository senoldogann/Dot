### 817. A plugin's skill whose name another skill has is offered by its plugin's name

**Status: done (2026-10-29).** **Asked** with item 816 ("tüm plugins, skills ve connectors sisteminin problemsiz ve
sorunsuz olmasını istiyorum").

- `dotx skills list` on the owner's Dot named three skills **Left out**, each a skill of its own that another had the
  name of: the engineering plugin's `code-review` beside the owner's `code-review` (another skill: it reviews against
  standards and a spec), the skill-creator plugin's `skill-creator` beside the owner's older copy, and UI/UX Pro Max's
  `design-system` (tokens and components) beside Product Design's (an audit of a design system). The first read kept
  the name and the other was not offered at all.
- A skill of a plugin whose name another skill has, and that is no copy of it, is now offered as `<plugin>-<skill>`
  (`plugin_skill_name`), as a plugin's MCP server is named (`<plugin>-<server>`): `engineering-code-review`,
  `skill-creator-skill-creator`, `ui-ux-pro-max-design-system`. It is left out, as before, only when that name is taken
  too or is no skill name (more than 64 characters, or the plugin's name holds a character a skill name may not). A
  skill of the owner's folder or of `[skills] dirs` is never renamed, a copy of a skill still counts once, and a skill
  that comes with Dot still gives way to one of the owner's.
- docs/extending.md says it where it said the second was left out.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_skills.py`: the owner's three cases (a plugin's skill beside the owner's, two plugins'
skills of one name), a copy that counts once, a plugin whose name makes too long a name left out as shadowed, and what
`plugin_skill_name` makes of a plugin's name.
