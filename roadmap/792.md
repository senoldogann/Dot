### 792. The browser does not repeat what went through, and a script's selector call reads its end

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner, the second round of the review of every module ("onaylıyorum ne
gerekiyorsa yap"). A review of `browser/` and `tools/browser.py`, each found point checked in the code:

- **An action reported failed after it went through.** `browser_act`, `browser_eval` and `browser_upload` let the page
  settle after their steps; a page that did not settle in time (a slow navigation a click started) raised "waiting for
  the page to settle failed", and the steps' reports were lost, so a run took the submit, the post or the like for not
  done and did it again. The settle after an action is said ("the page was still loading …: what was done went
  through, so do not repeat it"): in the page's notes after steps and an upload, after a script with what else it
  did to the tab; the call returns what it did, and a forbidden address still refuses.
- **A risky click clicked twice.** The retry of item 781 clicks again, with a script, a click that left the page
  unchanged for half a second; a Send, Post or Pay button that acts slower got a second click, past the pace and the
  spending counted once. A batch flagged as sending, posting, paying, removing, installing or a credential is not
  retried.
- **A selector script ending in `;` or a comment.** `browser_eval` with a selector wrapped the function in a call on one
  line, so `(el) => el.textContent;` or one ending in `// …` was a SyntaxError; the refs' path broke on the two
  together. Both assign the function on lines of their own, then call it.

**Not changed**, written here for a later item: a background tab matched by its address once (a tab whose address had
not committed yet waits out the timeout), `browser_wait_text` reading no iframe, an approved click or upload not checked
against the approved page once it runs, a tab left behind when its registration fails, two downloads of one name at
once, and a read of the page after an unsettled action that itself fails (the reports are lost then too; the call's
error says the page could not be read).

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_unsettled.py`, `tests/unit/test_browser_retry.py` (a sending, posting or paying
batch is not clicked again), `tests/smoke/test_browser_eval.py` (a selector script with `;` and a comment).
