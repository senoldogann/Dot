### 792. A human check is solved, not handed back

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner, with a screenshot of a job application stopped on an hCaptcha:
"…Hcaptcha reCAPTCHA ve cloudflare gibi şeylerde ajan kendi çözmeli bana sormadan; sistem beni az çok tanımalı,
karakterimi bilmeli, ona göre davranmalı." The run had asked whether to solve the puzzle or leave it; the answer is
now the system's: solve it and go on.

- **`drag_at`.** A viewport drag from one pixel to another (`x,y` to `to_x,to_y`), for a cross-origin widget where no
  ref reaches inside the frame: an hCaptcha "drag the shape to the loop that is closed" puzzle, a slider, a canvas.
  It presses, moves in ten steps so a drag library sees a drag, lets go, and releases the button when it fails. The
  page's `_MOVING_ACTIONS` sees it, so a drag that changes nothing is reported as such.
- **The `captcha` skill.** A built-in skill for hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA and Cloudflare Turnstile: the checkbox by
  `click_at`, an image grid tile by tile, the drag puzzle by `drag_at`, Cloudflare's wait (no reload loop), three
  attempts, and the rule that a CAPTCHA farm or a solving service is not the way. It says to look again after every
  click, because the tiles move.
- **The prompt.** The full and lean prompts say a human check is part of the work, not a reason to stop or to ask: solve
  it with `browser_screenshot`, `click_at` and `drag_at`, and after three ways that fail say what is left and keep the
  tab open. `ask_user` is no longer the path for a check.
- **The owner's character.** A memory note says the instruction is standing, and that he wants the work done without
  being asked for the routine decisions.
- **Measured.** Unit: `test_browser_step_meaning` 4, `test_skills` 54, `test_lean_prompt` 54; smoke: the real
  `drag_at` moves a knob from (38,38) to (220,120) in Chrome; the skill lists and the first-step budget (22,466 of
