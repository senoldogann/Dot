### 490. Teach a task's routes are under /api

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** A fix of item 423, reported by the owner: the close button of the **Teach a task** panel
did not close it and showed "The recording did not stop: Method Not Allowed" at each press. `routes_teach.py` built
its router without the `/api` prefix every other router of the API has, so the panel's calls (`/api/teach/...`) matched
no route and the UI's pages, which answer last, refused them with a 405; starting a recording failed the same way. And
the routes, served at `/teach/...`, were outside the guard's token check, which covers `/api` only. **As built.** The
router has the `/api` prefix. The whole class was looked for: of the routers without a prefix, MCP's spells `/api` in
its paths, and the pages', the drawings' and the OAuth callback are left open on purpose. Tests:
`tests/smoke/test_open_paths.py` checks that the paths the daemon answers without its token are only those, and that
the panel's close reaches its route (404 with nothing recorded, 401 without the token); both fail without the fix.
