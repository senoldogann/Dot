### 1299. A desktop screenshot blacks out what was on the screen during the capture, and a script that times out dies with its commands

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** `shots._picture_of` blacked out the password managers' windows from a window list read
before the capture, which takes time (up to `capture_timeout_s`): a password manager opened or moved meanwhile was in
the picture and not in the list. `snapshots.capture_front_window` had the same order. And `run_osascript`, on a
timeout, killed only `osascript`, and its reap `wait_for` could raise a bare `TimeoutError` in place of the explained
`DesktopError`.

The windows are read again right after the capture (`screen.windows_on_screen()`) and what is blacked out is the union of
both lists (`safety.merged_windows`: each window once as it stood in each reading, so one that moved is covered where
it was and where it is), with the applications' bundle ids read after the capture too (in `snapshots`, the applications
of both readings). The `frontmost` the view names is still the first reading's. `run_osascript` starts `osascript` with
`start_new_session=True` and kills the process group (`os.killpg`) on a timeout and in the `finally` that follows a
cancelled run; a killed process that does not exit within `_REAP_TIMEOUT_S` is logged (`osascript_not_reaped`) and the
`DesktopError` that explains the timeout is raised as before.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_screenshot_redaction_order.py` (a manager opened during the capture, one moved during it, one
closed during it, an unchanged screen blacked out once per window, the window capture of `snapshots`, the merge; a
script that will not die raises the explained error and its group is signalled; on this Mac, a script still runs in its
own group and the command of a script that timed out is gone with it), with `test_desktop_screenshots.py`,
