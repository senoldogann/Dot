### 1169. The agents and a run's record are read once where parts of the page want them at once

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** **Measured** on the owner's Dot, read-only, with Chromium's request initiators: as Dot
opened, `GET /api/agents` (151 KB, 49 KB on the wire) was read three times, by the faces' looks (`actors.js`
`loadLooks`), the side menu's faces (`sidebar.js` `loadFaces`) and the new chat's agents (`views/runs.js`); and a run
opened that was not its chat's newest (a loop's chat opened from an older link) read `GET /api/runs/{id}` twice at the
same moment, for the side menu's lookup of its chat (`resolveThread`) and for the run's page.

**Built.** `sharedGet(path)` (`api.js`): a read of a path that is already on its way shares that request, and each
caller gets a copy of its own (`structuredClone`), so none sees what another changes in it. Nothing is kept once the
answer came, and a change sent meanwhile (any request but a `GET`) lets the next read go to the daemon again. The side
menu's faces, the new chat's agents and the faces' looks read the agents through it, and the side menu's lookup and
the run's page read a run through it. Every request still goes through `request`, the daemon's proof included.

**What it does not do.** The agents are read twice, not once: the faces' looks are read before the page is drawn
(`main.js` waits for them), so the two reads after them are never on the way at the same time as that one; sharing it
would mean keeping an answer, or drawing faces before their looks are known. `GET /api/models` was read once already
(`state.js`).

**Measured, before and after,** on the owner's 40-turn chat, opened at an older run, iPhone size, Chromium at 60 ms
latency and four times slower CPU, the branch's page files routed in, six rounds in turn: the agents read 3 times
before, 2 after; the run read 2 times before, 1 after. Time to the chat list after opening Dot: 2.94 to 3.30 s before,
2.94 to 3.12 s after; to the chat's text: 1.32 to 1.63 s before, 1.36 to 1.60 s after, and one 3.42 s that did not
come again in three more rounds. No gain in time beyond the noise is claimed: 49 KB less on the wire as Dot opens.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_chat_open_payloads.py`, in Chromium and WebKit, on a phone-sized page: as Dot opens the
agents are read twice; a run before its chat's newest is read once as its chat opens. Both failed on `main` (three
reads, two).
