### 812. The import dialog's file field is a field, and its buttons stay put while it imports

**Status: done (2026-10-29).** **Asked** by the owner, with two screenshots of Settings › Vault › Import from CSV in
Dot.app: the file field's button and the file's name stood at the top of its box, and once Import was pressed the
buttons looked as if they ran into each other.

- **The file field.** `.input` gives a field 36 pixels and no padding above its words, which WebKit lays a file field's
  button and name against the top of. A file field has 4 pixels inside its border and a button (`::file-selector-
  button`, drawn as the page's own small button) as tall as the line it stands on (`--leading-xl`, so both grow with
  the text size): both stand in the middle, in WebKit and in Chrome, in a field of about 38 pixels.
- **The busy button.** The form dialog put its spinner before the submit button's words: the button grew by the
  spinner and its gap (21 pixels), Cancel moved by as much, and WebKit, fading both to their disabled look at once,
  left traces of where they had been. The words stand in a box of their own (`.btn-label`), and while the request runs
  the button wears `busy`: the spinner over the words, which keep their room (hidden, the button's name stays for a
  screen reader), in the button's own colour. Neither the button nor the ones beside it move.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_vault_page.py` (the field is its line and its room around it; while a slowed import runs,
Import and Cancel keep their boxes, the spinner is in Import, which keeps its name); WebKit and Chromium drawn by hand
at twice the size: the button and the name in the middle, the busy button its own size.
