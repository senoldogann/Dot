### 814. Snapshots: both Command keys together, and a double tap only once switched on

**Status: done (2026-10-29).** **Asked** by the owner: a snapshot should be taken when the Command keys on both sides
of the space bar are pressed together, and not when one Command key is pressed twice (as the owner does for its
shortcuts).

- `[snapshots] double_tap` (off by default) says whether a double tap of one Command key captures as both keys
  together do; Settings › Snapshots switches it live (**Double tap of ⌘ too**, `POST /api/snapshots/settings`). The
  watch follows the tap whether it counts or not (`TapDetector`, told whether it counts), so the switch takes at once;
  a tap that does not count is no capture and keeps nothing back, so both keys pressed right after a lone press still
  capture (the pre-push review); both keys together capture either way.
- The page's words and the docs say both keys first, and the tap as the switch's.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_snapshots.py` (a double tap captures nothing while off and once while on, both keys
together capture while off, also right after a lone press), the config and settings tests as they were.
