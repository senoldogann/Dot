### 1159. An eval report keeps each attempt's trace

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** The owner asked for browser use to be made faster by measurement, before and after.
The first real-model measurement (`opencode_go:deepseek-v4.1-flash`, four browser tasks twice) passed 8 of 8 at 43 s a
pass, of which 38.6 s were model calls and 4.2 s tools, about 197 thousand tokens a pass; in the owner's runs a
quarter of the acts are followed by a look at the page. Which steps a change of the harness saves or adds could not be
told: a report kept the totals of an attempt alone, and its events went with its throw-away home.

- `evals/results.py` `TraceStep` and `TaskOutcome.trace`; `evals/metrics.py` `run_trace`: each attempt keeps its model
  calls (purpose, seconds, input and output tokens, whether it failed) and its tool calls (tool, seconds, whether it
  went through, the first line of its result), in order, at most 400; an act's line names the actions of its steps and
  never what they typed. A report saved before has no trace and reads as before.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_evals.py::test_a_trace_keeps_the_steps_and_calls_in_order_and_never_what_an_act_typed`, the
eval unit tests, `dotx eval check`, and `dotx eval report` of a report saved before.
