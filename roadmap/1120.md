### 1120. A phone's composer is one line until it is used

**Status: done (2026-11-10).** The owner asked that the composer show the model, the mode, the reasoning, the context meter, the approvals and the
microphone only once it has the focus, as another assistant's app does. A phone's box is now the "+", the words and the send at rest;
a touch in its words (once the touch ended: opened as the finger came down, the words moved up and the touch ended on
the model's pill) or a key typed there brings the line under the words (the "+", the model, the meter, the microphone
and the send), and a touch elsewhere while the words do not have the focus puts it away (`composer.js`: a touch inside
keeps it while iOS gives a pill no focus; a menu of the box keeps it while open). The focus alone does not, so a page
that focuses the box as it opens leaves it at rest; a touch on the "+" or the send at rest does not move them under
the finger. The **+** menu's Model and options is a Mac's. The model's menu on a phone scrolls as a whole and stands
over the bar.

Checked: screenshots of a scripted chat on an iPhone 15 Pro (WebKit, dark) before and after, `npm run check`, the
browser tests of the phone and the composer.
