### 1957. DotNext shows a run's helpers as one group row, a tap away from a card of each

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** The owner's references of another coding agent's subagents (collapsed: two marks,
"2 subagents", "2 working", the time and a chevron; expanded: a card of one row per subagent).

What changed (the app only; the daemon's helpers view already carries each helper's provider, model, effort, state and
times, so nothing new is asked or polled):
- Where a run started helpers, the stream shows ONE row in place of a row per helper: their providers' marks
  overlapping, "2 helpers" ("2 yardımcı"), under it what they do in their states' inks ("1 working · 1 done": blue while
  working, amber while waiting on the owner, red once failed, grey once ended), the group's time (ticking while one
  works) and a chevron.
- A tap opens an inset card: per helper a dot in its state's ink, its title on one line, "· Working" in that ink, its
  time and a chevron that opens its chat; under them, in grey, its provider's mark and engine in words ("Claude Code ·
  Opus 5.5 · Medium", the model's label from the daemon's list). At the accessibility sizes the title takes three
  lines and the time its own.
- The palette gains `live` (blue), used only for a working helper; every ink stays at 4.5:1 on every surface (the
  contrast audit covers it). The Agents sheet's engine line is in words too (it showed the daemon's ids).
- The capsule's helpers segment stays.

Not done: a helper's branch or worktree on its second line, which the daemon's helpers view does not send.

How it was checked: `ChatUITests.testTheHelpersAreOneRowThatOpensACardOfEach` (folded label "2 helpers, 1 working,
1 done", 44 pt, no per-helper rows; open: two rows, the engine in words; a row opens the helper's chat) and
`testTheHelpersGroupHoldsAtAX3InTurkishAndDark`; `ChatWordsTests.testTheHelpersGroupTalliesAndSpan`;
`DesignSystemTests` (the audit); `StreamingLiveUITests.testAHelperIsOneGroupRowWithACard` against the throwaway daemon (a real `delegate` helper: the
group folded, then its card).
