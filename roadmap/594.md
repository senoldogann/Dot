### 594. Dot.app asks macOS for the microphone and speech recognition

**Status: done (2026-10-23); needs the owner's look (`tauri.conf.json`), and reaches the owner with the next Dot.app
build.** The owner pressed the composer's microphone (item 574) in Dot.app and it was refused (item 593 made the words
right). The app's web view has WebKit's speech recognition, but macOS gives an app the microphone and speech
recognition only once the app says why it asks, and an app under the hardened runtime the microphone only once it is
entitled to it. `/Applications/Dot.app` (built 2026-10-20) has neither: no `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` in its
`Info.plist`, no entitlement in its signature.

**As built.**
- `desktop/src-tauri/Info.plist`, which Tauri merges into the app's own: `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` and
  `NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription`, each saying in a sentence why (the speech one that macOS may send the voice to
  Apple).
- `desktop/src-tauri/Dot.entitlements`, named in `bundle.macOS.entitlements`: `com.apple.security.device.audio-input`
  alone. The hardened runtime stays on; no camera, no JIT, no exception to library validation.
- The web view's own question goes to the shell (`on_permission_request`, `permission_for`): it gives the microphone
  only to a page of an address the shell gave its commands (`Granted`, the daemon's own once it proved itself), and
  nothing else, to no page. macOS gives the microphone to the app, not to a page, and wry grants every request no one
  answers (wry 0.57, `requestMediaCapturePermissionForOrigin`): a page a link led the window to could have listened.
  The handler is told what is asked, not by which frame (wry passes the kind alone), and judges the window's page: a
  frame of the daemon's own address would be judged as its page is. The UI's frames (a page a run showed, a drawing of
  a file) are sandboxed without `allow-same-origin`, so their origin is opaque and WebKit refuses them before the shell
  is asked; the UI writes no markup (`test_static_ui.py`), so it makes no other frame.
- With the microphone, the daemon's page could record sound too (`getUserMedia`, which WebKit offers once the app says
  why it asks for the microphone); Dot's page takes only the words of the dictation. The docs say so.
- `desktop/release.sh` refuses a bundle it signed whose `Info.plist` lacks either reason or whose signature lacks the
  microphone entitlement, the defect this item mends, which no check would have caught.
- `docs/daemon.md` says it under the desktop app, and `docs/safety.md` names Dot.app among what may send the voice
  out (Apple's recognition, as Safari's).

**Tests.** `tests/unit/test_desktop_microphone.py`: the `Info.plist` holds the two reasons and nothing else; the
hardened runtime is on and the entitlements file grants the microphone and nothing else, so that a new entitlement is a
decision of its own. `lib.rs`'s tests: the daemon's page is given the microphone and neither the camera nor where it
is; a page of another address, another port, `localhost` for `127.0.0.1`, the shell's own down page and no page are
given nothing. `test_desktop_microphone.py` also reads the shell's one window, which asks `permission_for` through
`on_permission_request` (without it wry grants every request), every frame the UI makes, sandboxed with
`allow-scripts` alone and handed no permission, and the release's check of the bundle. `cargo fmt --check`,
`cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo test` pass.

Not changed: the installed Dot.app, which only a new build (the release, `desktop/release.sh`, signed with the owner's
identity) carries; the installer, and the release beyond its check of the bundle, which item 575 changes.
