### 2013. Several tasks in one chat, slice 3: the tasks on the pages and the phone

**Status: built (2026-11-27), behind `[agent] chat_tasks` (1 by default).** Slice 3 of [item 1982](1982.md)'s design,
after slices 1 and 2 ([item 1984](1984.md), [item 2012](2012.md)). With it the owner's Dot can set `chat_tasks = 5`
without a screen that hides where a message went or which task works. The same words on the pages and in DotNext (the
owner's rule of 2026-11-27: phone and desktop in lockstep).

**Built.**
- The daemon: `ChatTaskView` and `TasksView` (`server/views.py`), `tasks_view` (`server/thread_sync.py`) from
  `RunManager.tasks_of_chat` (the chat's own task and each lane's: its title, newest run, its status, and whether it
  works, waits for the owner or ended; none while the chat has no lane), in the chat's view (`ThreadView.tasks`, also
  a lane's, which names its chat) and at `GET /api/threads/{id}/tasks` (with `cap`, the configured `chat_tasks`). A
  chat's revision is the newest of its own and its lanes' (`Store.thread_revision`, `thread_revisions`), and a lane's
  change is told as its chat's too (`thread_revisions_after`), so the phone's view of the chat moves with its tasks.
  No trigger and no schema change.
- The pages: `tasks.js` (the rows over the composer, read again as runs start and end), `taskwords.js` (pure: a task's
  state words, the open count, the routed toast's words), a `tasks` segment of the phone's capsule (`dockwords.js`,
  `dockbar.js`) and its sheet, the line of a task's own page, the composer's Stop of a chat with tasks
  (`POST /threads/{chat}/stop`), the routed toast with Open and Move (`queue.js` `onRouted`), and every message of a
  chat that may hold tasks sent through the queue's route. Words in `lang/en.js` and `lang/tr.js`.
- DotNext: the engine decodes `TasksView`, `ChatTaskView`, `ThreadView.tasks`, `QueueView.routed` and
  `ThreadRecord.lane_of` (`Contract/Models.swift`), says a task's phase and the routed notice as pure functions
  (`Sync/ChatTasks.swift`), and calls `POST /threads/{id}/stop` and the two moves (`Wire/DaemonAPI+Tasks.swift`); a
  message sent online keeps the queue's answer for its routed notice (`Outbox`). The app (`App/ChatTasks.swift`,
  `ChatScreen`, `Composer`): the capsule's "N tasks" segment and its sheet (title, state, Stop, open), the line under
  a task's own title ("A task of …", "Back to the chat"), the composer's Stop of a chat with tasks stopping every task,
  and the routed toast with Open and Move ("Move to": the chat's other tasks and New task). Words in
  `Localizable.xcstrings` through `translations_tr.py`, the same as the pages'.

**The pre-push review** (first round, BLOCK): Move was offered for a message steered into a run already at work,
which would have stopped that run and sent its own goal elsewhere; now Move is offered, on both, only for a queued
message, a continued run and a new task (`moveSource` on the phone, `routedWords` on the pages; no "New task" for one
that is a new task already). `tasks.js` swallowed every failure of its read: now a deleted chat clears the rows, a
link that is down keeps them, and anything else is told. A lane's revision is its family's (its chat's and the other
lanes'), as its view shows the chat's tasks; the composer's Stop reads the tasks again after any stop.
The second round asked for `docs/runs.md` to say both (done), and, as nits, that a lasting failure of the read is
told once until one works, and that a chat left tells nothing (the page now disposes of the tasks as of its loops).
The third round asked for `docs/daemon.md` and `docs/phone.md` to name the capsule's tasks segment and its sheet,
and for the reason `chat_tasks` is 1 (here and in `defaults.toml`) to be the owner's switch, not the pages; done. Its
two nits are answered in the pull request: a routed toast whose answer comes while another chat is on show is shown
on the next visit to that chat, and a phone reaches a chat's ended tasks only from a task's own screen.
