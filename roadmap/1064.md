### 1064. On a touch screen a message shows no buttons, and holding it opens its sheet

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** The owner, on 2026-11-09, with two screenshots of a messaging app on their iPhone (a
conversation with nothing under its messages, and the sheet a hold opens: a grid of reactions over Reply, Copy, Select
text): "mesaj geldikten sonra hiçbir ikon vs göstermesek üzerine basılı tutarsak alttan böyle bir pop up açılsa ve
seçenekler orada görünse".

Now, on a screen with no pointer to rest (`hover: none`: a phone, an iPad without a trackpad; an iPad with one keeps the
buttons that show where it rests, the pre-push review):

- Under a message (yours, Dot's answer, what Dot said between steps, live or in an earlier turn) no button and no time
  show; the reactions that stand on it stay, as chips. The buttons are out of sight, not out of reach: VoiceOver reads
  them and a keyboard reaches them, and one with the keyboard's focus shows. A link keeps the phone's own menu of a
  long press (the pre-push review).
- Holding it (450 ms, a finger that moves less than 10 px; not on a link, a button or a block of code) opens a sheet
  from the foot of what is seen (`messagesheet.js`): when it was sent and how long its work took, the seven reactions
  of Dot's messages (a press puts one on or takes it back, through the same daemon call as the palette:
  `reactionsOf`), and a row for each control of the message's foot (copy, read aloud, show the steps, edit from here,
  open the task), which presses that control, so the sheet does what the foot does, and Select text, which selects the
  message's words (a hold no longer selects them: the hold is the sheet's, and iOS's own menu of a long press is
  kept off).
- A message whose foot is kept back everywhere (yours while its turn's run works, item 205) lends the sheet none of
  its controls, only Select text: an Edit from here would be refused then (the pre-push review). The focus goes back
  to the message as the sheet closes.
- A press outside the sheet, a swipe down of it or Escape closes it. The click a hold ends with is the sheet's, for
  400 ms at most and only until the next touch, so that no later press is taken for it (the browser tests found a
  press of a chip right after a hold eaten).

A Mac's window keeps the buttons under a message, shown on hover, as before.

Checked by `tests/smoke/test_message_sheet.py` (WebKit as a phone: no buttons, a hold of the answer and of your
message, a reaction from the sheet standing as a chip, Select text, a short press opening nothing, and the buttons in
a Mac's window) and by the phone cases of `test_reactions_page.py`, `test_message_foot.py` and `test_chat_calm.py`,
moved to the sheet.
