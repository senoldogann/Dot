### 1276. Dot tells you when its settings do not read and when a restart did not happen

**Status: done (2026-11-20).** Two ways for Dot to be wrong without anyone knowing, from the operations audit of
2026-11-20:

- **The watchdog.** `watchdog.look` read the settings first, and settings that do not validate (`config.toml` after an
  upgrade that tightened a field, or a tree that moved under the daemon) ended the look as `watchdog_failed`: four times
  in two days, nobody told, at the moment the daemon could not start either. Now the look says it once, in a
  notification of the Mac ("Dot's settings cannot be read", with the reason, `watchdog_config_unread` in the log), keeps a
  mark in `watchdog.json` so that it is once and not every minute, and forgets the mark when the settings read again.
- **`dotx service restart --when-idle`.** When a run is still at work after two hours, the job gave up with one line in
  the log and unloaded itself: today's wait (12:27 to 14:27) left merged code not live for about three hours, found out
  by chance. When the job ends with no restart (it gave up, failed or crashed) it shows "Dot was not restarted" now, the
  reason in a line, as the watchdog does for what it cannot restart.

Found by the pre-push review: the banner is shown before the job unloads itself, which ends the process (a test fails on the other order).

Checked: `tests/unit/test_watchdog.py` (a notification once for two looks with unreadable settings, the mark gone when
they read, told again when they fail again) and `tests/unit/test_restart_when_idle.py` (a banner after a wait that ran
out, after a restart that failed, none after one that went through; no test shows a real notification: the banner is
recorded). `docs/daemon.md` says both.
