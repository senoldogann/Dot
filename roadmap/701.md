### 701. A screenshot of the desktop comes with the text on it and where to click it

**Status: done (2026-10-26).** The owner's main model (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash) sees no image: a screenshot reaches it as
another model's description, and a window that shows nothing to accessibility (a canvas, an Electron app without a
tree) left it nothing to click by.

**The change.** `desktop/ocr.py` reads the lines of text of a picture with macOS's Vision (`VNRecognizeTextRequest`,
accurate, no language correction; `pyobjc-framework-vision` added), on this Mac. `shots._text_of` reads the picture
the model is shown, after the windows of password managers (and Dot's own while its vault shows a secret) are painted
black, and gives each line the x, y of its centre in the coordinates `desktop_act` takes, for a full screenshot and a
zoomed region. The caption lists them fenced as screen content. `[desktop] read_screen_text` (on) turns it off; a
failure of Vision costs the text, not the screenshot. The note for a window with almost no elements points at it.

**Measured:** 261 ms for the first read of a 1366 × 860 screen, 50 ms after.

**Checked.** `test_screen_text.py`: Turkish letters read; a 2x picture and a zoomed region give the coordinates of the
whole screen within 6 units; a blacked-out secret is not read; off reads nothing; the caption fences the lines.
