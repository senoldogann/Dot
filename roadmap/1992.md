### 1992. DotNext shows the photos, videos and files of a message: tiles in the composer and in the chat, a player on a tap

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner attached a video on the phone and sent "Bu videoda ne oluyor?": the sent bubble
showed the words alone (item 1930 sent the file and drew nothing of it), and the composer's model button was a plain
white block.

**The data.** Nothing new on the wire: the daemon already tells what a message carried, `{id, name, kind, size}` per file,
in the run's `attachments` event (its first message) and in each `steer` event. The transcript's owner row now holds
those files (`TranscriptItem.owner(..., files:)`). One route is new, `GET /api/attachments/{id}/media`: a video or a sound
as a `FileResponse`, so `Range` works (206) and a player starts at once and seeks; it is found with `located_file` as the
image route is, serves the type the name says (never the stored one: an attachment is anyone's file), and refuses any
other kind (422). It needs no change of `guard.py`: a phone's key reaches every `/api` path but the ways in.

**The phone.** One tile (`MediaTile`) is drawn by the composer and by the chat: the picture, the length at the foot, a
play glyph over a video, a ring while it is made ready. A video added is on show at once with its first frame read from the
source, and its ring fills with the share of the picture re-encoded (`VideoTranscoder`'s progress); a × takes it away and
gives the export up. In the chat the owner's message draws its files above the words: a photo is its picture, a video a
tile that plays full screen (`AVPlayer`, the Mac's route with the phone's key as the header of AVFoundation's requests,
`AVURLAssetHTTPHeaderFieldsKey`), a sound or other file a chip. The first frame and the length of a message's video are
read from the Mac's copy (the file is served with its index first) and kept for the session.

**The model button.** The chip showed OpenCode Go's mark for `opencode_go:deepseek-v4.1-flash`, and OpenCode's mark is a
white block with a hole: it was drawn right, and read as nothing. A model of a family the app has no mark of (DeepSeek,
Kimi, GLM, Qwen, MiniMax) that OpenCode Go, OpenRouter or Ollama hosts now wears a neutral symbol; the others keep their
provider's mark.

**The web.** The same tile on the web UI (the owner's rule of 2026-11-27: phone and desktop move together):
`attachments.js` draws a video as a tile in the box and over a sent message, with its first frame and length (a `<video>`
that is never shown, fed the file on the page, or the first 4 MB of the daemon's copy by a `Range` read), the play glyph,
a ring that fills with the share of the upload (`XMLHttpRequest`, as `fetch` tells no upload progress), the × at its
corner, and a click plays it full screen with the browser's controls (Escape, the × or a click outside closes it). The
tile keeps the web's own 56 px picture size (the phone draws 96 pt); words are the phone's: "Play video NAME", "Remove NAME", "0:14".
Reading a frame and playing need `media-src blob:` in the page's Content-Security-Policy (`server/guard.py`,
`SECURITY_HEADERS`), which had no `media-src` at all under `default-src 'none'`. `media-src blob:` is added, that source and no other
(the owner approved the line on 2026-11-27; `guard.py` is under "Always needs a human"): a blob can only be made by the page's own script, which
`img-src blob:` already trusted the same way. `tests/unit/test_ui_csp_media.py` pins it; an independent security review of the line found nothing.

**Not done.** A message still waiting in the outbox, and one the daemon took before its event reached the phone, draw their
words alone; a run older than the newest the phone holds has no events to read its files from until the chat is opened.

**Checks.** `tests/smoke/test_server.py` (the route: 206 with `Content-Range`, 422 for a text file, 404), `ReducerTests` (the
files of a goal and of two steers), `ChatSettingsAndVoiceTests` (the model mark), `ChatUITests` (the composer's tile) and
`StreamingLiveUITests` against a throwaway daemon (send a short clip: the tile in the bubble with its length, a tap opens
the player).
