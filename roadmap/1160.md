  for its 8 s at Playwright's `performing click action` (the page had stopped answering a moment before: a 1.5 s

  8 s`), and two tests of `test_browser_eval.py` could not either, in another Chrome that no test had left stuck. The

chats and in the same chat. Each was handed over, the slowest in 0.5 s. Item 1002 had tried every core busy, a
window minimised and no window, with the same result. The cause is still not known.

**Built.** What the next record needs to tell the browser's side from the page's (`browser/session.py`):

`Browser.getWindowForTarget`, which the browser itself answers, and the rest of the page. The error of a tab not

the check that starts Chrome with a profile through an `httpx` client left at its 5 s, and Chrome took 6 s to start
on the runner. The client waits 60 s.
