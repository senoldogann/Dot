### 1056. A full-screen viewer for what a run shows: the whole screen, zoom, and a way out

**Status: done (2026-11-09).** The owner's word of 2026-11-08, with a screenshot of a CV a run showed, full screen on
the iPhone: "ne zoom yapılabiliyor ne net gösteriyor ne geri kapatabiliyoruz … sohbet içinde çok fazla amatör şeyler".

Measured on the owner's chat in WebKit at the iPhone's size: the page dialog covered 32 to 707 of 739 pixels, the chat
and the composer showing under a scrim below it; a page's frame cannot be pinched on iOS; a tap on the page or beside it
did nothing (Close did, at the top corner); and the CV was a 1400px JPEG at 75 % of a PDF (`sips -Z 1400`), which no
viewer makes sharp. The picture viewer (`showImage`) had no zoom either, and closed on any click.

Now one viewer (`viewer.js`, its arithmetic in `viewmath.js`) shows pictures and pages: the whole screen, opaque, the
safe areas clear, its title and tools (−, the level, +, Fit, Close: 44px each) over the stage. It zooms from 1 to 5
times with two fingers, a double tap (to 2.5 and back), the trackpad's pinch or Ctrl and the wheel, and its buttons
(and `-`, `+`, `0`); zoomed, the content is dragged, its edges kept within the stage. A page stays in its sandboxed
frame: fitted, it is its own (links, choices, scrolling); zoomed, a clear layer over it takes the gestures. A picture
closes on a tap beside it or a swipe down too; Escape closes the viewer before the panel it was opened from.

`show_page` tells the run to show a document as its text, not a picture: zoomed, text stays sharp (one line, 24 tokens
of the first step's budget, raised by 50).
