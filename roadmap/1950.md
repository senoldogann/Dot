### 1950. A schedule that goes on in its chat reads what was said there, not every step of its earlier runs

**Status: done (2026-11-26).**

The run review of 2026-11-26 (problem 6) measured the first model step of a schedule run at 107.9 k input tokens
(ui 90 k, review 21 k), the first call 15 s, with a cache hit of 9.5 k: all 17 enabled schedules but two go on in
their chat (`same_chat`), where the digest of earlier answers is not used, so the run replayed the chat's whole history,
the tool calls and results of every earlier firing too. "Müşteri postası (2 saatte bir)", a mail check of 6 steps,
started with about 114 k tokens, 33 times that week.

`scheduler/history.py: condensed_chat` turns the chat's history into one message of at most `history_chars` (4000)
characters: the owner's messages and the final answers, newest first, the newest answer up to half the budget and an
older line cut at 600 characters, a goal the chat repeats word for word said once (the firing's own message says it
again), and nothing a message says can close the block (`<` escaped, as the digest does). `agent/loop.py` applies it to
a run whose source is a schedule, after the tool groups were read from the full history; the chat itself is unchanged.
A firing in a chat of its own has no history yet, so nothing changes for it.
