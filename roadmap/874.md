### 874. An agent's page says what its runs used, and takes away the actions none called

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner, after the review of Dot against an agent-template article
(0xRafy, "GrokBot Templates"): "no leftover tools", since an unused tool is a way for an agent to go sideways.

What the owner's Dot showed (2026-10-28 to 2026-10-31): agents from templates hold 22 to 48 actions and their runs
called 5 to 22 (Bug Reproduction 12 of 48, Reviewer 5 of 25, Paid Media 13 of 43), and nothing in Dot said so: tool
counts were per model (`store/stats.py`), never per agent.

- `store/db.py`: `agent_tool_use(agent_id, runs)`, the newest ended runs of an agent: their count, tokens, newest
  start and the calls of each tool (`tool_call` events). `AgentToolUse` in `store/models.py`.
- `server/routes_agents.py`: `GET /api/agents/{id}` carries `usage` (`AgentUsage`): the newest 20 ended runs, and once
  three ended, the actions the agent names that none called, but for the core tools and `message_agent`.
  `POST /api/agents/{id}/trim-tools` takes them away; for an agent with every action, keeps the ones called that are
  tools here, and `message_agent`; 422 before three runs and when nothing would change or nothing be left.
- `views/agents.js`: **What it used** on the agent's page: runs, tokens, the most called; "Not called in its last N
  runs" with **Remove them**, or "It has every action" with **Keep only these**, each on a confirm.
- `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_agent_usage.py` (the store's window and counts, a run still working left out; the
unused list; trimming). `tests/smoke/test_agent_design_pages.py` (in Chrome): one run tells no unused action, three
do, Remove takes them away and the record keeps `web_search` and `message_agent`; an agent with every action keeps
what its runs called; the phone width does not scroll sideways. The screenshots, desk and phone, were looked at.
