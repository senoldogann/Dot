### 1644. Dot for iPhone: the new look seen on every screen, before and after

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The standard's tenth rule: "verify with your eyes and a second pair". Every screen the audit of item
1640 listed was photographed again with the same canned daemon and the same launch arguments, from the build of this branch, and each
was looked at; what the shots showed was fixed in items 1642 and 1643 (below) before the set was taken once more.

What was looked at:
- 25 screens (the chat list and the list offline, seven chats: done, at work with a queue and a loop, an approval, a question, a hand-off,
  a failed run, five helpers at work; the Queued sheet, the Run sheet at its large and medium heights, a step, the inbox, Settings and its
  Models and Schedules pages, the new-chat sheet and its model picker, the pairing and the address screens, the design gallery) in dark and
  light, at the default text size and at AX3, on an iPhone 17 Pro; the lists, the sheets and a chat again on an iPad Air 11-inch in dark
  and light. `workspace/ios-pro-shots/before/` and `after/` hold them (`<screen>-<look>-<size>.png`, `ipad-<screen>-<look>-large.png`),
  `pairs/` puts each before and after side by side, `contact/` shows the whole set of each look and size, `README.md` lists them.
- The queue's gestures were driven in the simulator (an XCUITest written for the run and not kept): the swipe to the left with Remove,
  the "Removed from the queue · Undo" line, Undo, the swipe to the right with Send now, Reorder with its handles, and the press and
  hold menu (Send now, Move up, Remove): `after/interactions/`.

What the shots found, and was fixed: the Queued sheet's cards ran to the sheet's edges (a `List` row's background ignores its insets:
the list has content margins now); at AX3 the capsule's loop and queue segments had lost their words (they stand over each other with
their words now), a tool line was cut to "Opene…xample", the approval's command broke "bank.exampl-e", the inbox's count badge stood tall,
a glyph was pushed out of its card, the gallery's buttons broke "Al-low onc-e"; in the light look the inbox's count badge was a brown
disc (the amber ink as a fill): it is the amber fill (`warnSurface`) with the amber ink now; on an iPad a line of an answer ran past 100
characters (the words keep to 720 points).

Not checked: the look on the owner's phone and the real glass over live content (the shots sample a still ground); the Live Activity
and the share sheet were built and read in the source, not seen on a screen (the simulator cannot start an activity here); the
reorder drag itself (the handles show; the order's rule is a tested function, `queueOrder(_:moving:to:)`, and the daemon call is
the existing one); VoiceOver, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and landscape; the pre-iOS 26 look beyond `-DotMaterialLook`.
