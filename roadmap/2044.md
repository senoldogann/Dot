### 2044. Keep the Mac awake for an upcoming routine or watcher

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** Dot's existing idle-sleep assertion now starts within three minutes of an enabled
routine or watcher being due, and continues while a routine starts, a watcher checks or a task runs. The existing
grace period still applies. The emergency stop keeps upcoming work from requesting a hold. The daemon page
records the macOS assertion and explains the limits of an idle-sleep hold.

Verification covers active work and the grace period in `test_work_awake.py` and upcoming work in
`test_scheduler.py`. The assertion adapter was checked on macOS with `pmset -g assertions`.
