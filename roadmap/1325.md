### 1325. The generic controls are one restrained set: 32 px, a hairline, a calm blue, no pills, no bounce

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The second step of the redesign (item 1324 gave the tokens): every generic
control in `app.css` is drawn on them the same on a phone and a Mac, so every page inherits it.

- **Buttons** are 32 px tall on an 8 px corner (28 the small one, 36 the large; `--control-h`, `--control-h-sm`,
  `--control-h-lg`), a hairline (`--button-border`, the strong border) on a 3% tint of the ink, 7% under the pointer;
  the primary is the accent blue with white on it and a 12% mix toward the page under the pointer; the destructive is a
  red outline (or filled with `.solid`, on `--bad-fill`, `#dc2626` in dark and `#b91c1c` in light, with white on it:
  not the salmon that reads as text on black); the ghost has neither frame nor fill; an icon-only button is as wide as it is
  tall. On a phone what is tapped takes `--touch-target` (36 px, with the 4 px ring of an icon button that makes the
  44 a finger hits).
- **Fields** (`.input`, `.select`, `.textarea`) are 32 px, on `--field` (a hair over the surface in dark), and take the
  accent's border and a 3 px ring at a quarter of it in focus; a button's focus ring is 2 px, the accent itself
  (3:1 against every surface of both looks and schemes, which a test measures). Checkboxes and radios are 16 px and take the accent; the text selection is the accent's tint.
- **Switch**: a plain toggle, 32 by 18 px with a 14 px knob and an off track of `--control` (solved to 3:1 against the card in every look), the same on a phone (in a
  44 px box a finger hits; it was 40 by 24 there and 34 by 20 on a Mac). Segmented controls have 28 px segments and
  the chosen one a hairline over the surface instead of a shadow.
- **Badges** are 6 px corners, not pills (a running one is the accent's tint, no longer a solid blue); `.pill` is an 8 px
  corner; the empty state's mark is a 36 px rounded square, not a ringed disc, with a title in the label weight.
- **Menus, popovers, toasts, dialogs, sheets**: a menu is 10 px corners with 28 px rows, a hairline ring and fades in from
  98% in 120 ms (it rose 6 px from 96%); a toast is a raised surface with a hairline (red on a red tint when it is an
  error), no longer the inverted slab; a dialog is a 16 px corner with a ring, its title in the title weight; a sheet
  rises 16 px (it rose 32).
- **Scrollbars** are thin (6 px: `scrollbar-width: thin` and a thumb of the ink at the alphas of `--scroll-thumb` and `--scroll-thumb-hover`; the WebKit
  pseudo-elements where that is not supported) wherever a pointer scrolls; a touch screen keeps the system's overlay.
- **Quiet**: the spinner's arc is the text's own ink (no blue), the plugin gallery's popular tiles lose their blue
  gradient, and the text links in `app.css` that were the accent are `--accent-strong`, the blue that reads on black.
- The composer's "+" and microphone take the control height (`--control-h`) as the small button now is 28.

Not covered: the tooltip is the browser's own (`title`) and no skeleton exists in the interface, so neither has a
component to draw; sidebar rows, the stage bar, the composer's own pills, the run page and the settings pages keep their
own rules, which the other items of this batch redraw (those that name `--radius-pill` keep working on the tokens above;
the six that set text in `--accent` (`.side-working`, `.file-code-language`, `.file-code-number` and `.file-code-literal`,
`.note-more`, `.follow-texts > summary`, `.harness-write`) take `--accent-strong` now, and a test fails for any
`color: var(--accent)` left in the four sheets).
