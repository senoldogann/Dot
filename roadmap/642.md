### 642. Dot's mark is Obsidian: a ball of obsidian with eyes of champagne gold, on every device

**Status: done (2026-10-25).** The owner asked for a more luxurious logo, "like Grok Bot and the other autonomous
harnesses", on every device. Five marks were drawn for them, each at the size of an app icon, a browser tab (32 and
16 px) and a menu bar; they chose **A, Obsidian**.

**The change.**
- `desktop/icons/render.py`:
  - the mark is a ball of obsidian lit at its upper left, with the eyes of the character's attentive pose in champagne
    gold, a soft light and a hairline of gold round it;
  - the tile of the app icons is night (#232326 to #050506);
  - the menu bar's icons have a wider hairline, so the ball holds on a dark bar at 22 points;
  - it now also draws the maskable icon (the mark inside the circle a phone may cut it to) and `icon-96.png`, which no
    script drew before.
- Every file it writes is drawn again: the favicon, the web app's icons, the iPhone's touch icons, the app icon and the
  menu bar's three states. `npm run icons` drew the bundle's set (`icon.icns`, `icon.ico`, 32, 128 and 256 px).
- `static/index.html`: the wordmark's o is the same ball. Its gradients sit in one hidden `.mark-defs` that every copy
  takes them from (the sign-in card's copy too); it is not under `display: none`, where a browser may not paint them.
- `static/offline.html` (the page an iPhone keeps for a Mac out of reach) and the page a sign-in comes back to
  (`callback_page.py`'s `WORDMARK`) carry the same gradients, light and rim in their wordmark. `app.css` gives the
  ball's and the eyes' fills a plain colour (`--mark-ball`, and the gold of `--mark-rim`) that a copy without the
  gradients would still be drawn in.
- `desktop/ui/down.html` and `down.css`: the same ball, with its gradients in its own drawing.
- `tests/unit/test_mark.py`: the favicon and every copy (the page, the offline page, the callback page, the down page)
  wear the same three gradients, the ball the obsidian and the eyes the gold; every copy the daemon serves has the
  light and the rim; and no file of `src` draws the mark but the copies the test holds.
- `tests/unit/test_compression.py`: the file it serves whole, being under the 1024 bytes gzip starts at, is now the
  chevron icon; the favicon, which was that file, grew past them with its gradients.
- `docs/daemon.md` says so, and that a phone keeps the Home Screen icon it took until Dot is added again.

**Checked.**
- The test of the mark and the manifest's.
- The icons were looked at:
  - the web app's (192 px);
  - the maskable one;
  - the menu bar's waiting state at 44 px, the ball, its gold and the blue pastille readable.
- The sign-in card's wordmark was looked at in light and dark, and so were the offline page's and the callback page's.
- On the first push the review found those two pages drawing no ball: they took `app.css`'s new fills without the
  gradients, and an SVG fill whose gradient is missing paints nothing. The test of the mark now holds every copy.
- The app icon of Dot.app changes with its next build.
