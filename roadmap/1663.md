### 1663. The owner chooses the models of the pre-push review, and a review that cannot be done stops on its own

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Measured by the lead on 2026-11-24: 191 review runs in seven days, 79 that day, three
minutes on average; two reviews of one diff were refused by Opus ("Claude Code's session failed: API Error: Opus 5's
safeguards flagged this message"), and the hook's fall back to DeepSeek then ran past 22 minutes. The owner kept the
review and asked to choose its models himself, with an ordered fallback, a model of its own for a small change, and a
review that stops on its own.

What was built:
- `[code_review]` in `config.toml` (`CodeReviewConfig`): `model` and `effort` (by default `claude_code:opus`, medium),
  `fallback` in order and `fallback_effort` (by default `opencode_go:deepseek-v4.1-flash`, high), `small_model` (none by
  default), `small_max_lines` (150) and `max_minutes` (15). The defaults keep the review as it was.
- `dotx review plan --lines N --changed FILE --guide REVIEW.md` (`dotagent.code_review`, pure): `size`, `limit` and one
  `try <model> <effort>` line per model in order; a change of at most `small_max_lines` changed lines that touches no
  path of REVIEW.md's "Always needs a human" goes to the small model first.
- `.claude/hooks/pre-push-review.sh` reads that plan rather than naming the models: it tries each model in turn while one
  gives no verdict, and stops the push with "could not be reviewed: …; push again, or push with --no-verify" when none
  gives one, when the review ran past `max_minutes` in all (the run is sent SIGTERM, on which `dotx run` asks Dot to stop
  it, and SIGKILL 30 seconds later), or when the plan cannot be read. A commit that passed is still not reviewed again.
- Settings › Models › **Code review** (review model, small change model, small change lines, time limit) and **Code
  review fallbacks** (the list the task fallbacks use, with words of its own), through `GET` and `PUT /api/code-review`,
  which writes the table whole or not at all; a phone's key reads it and cannot change it.

How it was checked: `tests/unit/test_pre_push_hook.py` (a stand-in `dotx`: a refusal hands the change to the next model;
two refusals stop the push and name both; a review past a two-second limit is stopped and stops the push; a plan that
cannot be read stops it), `tests/unit/test_code_review_plan.py` (the defaults, the small model, REVIEW.md's paths),
`tests/smoke/test_code_review_settings.py` (the route on a served daemon, the Mac's token and a phone's key) and
`tests/smoke/test_standby_models.py::test_settings_choose_the_models_of_the_code_review` (the page, wide and light and
on a phone and dark).

Not checked: a real review on a model other than the defaults; the hook on the owner's checkout, which reads it only
once that checkout pulls main.
