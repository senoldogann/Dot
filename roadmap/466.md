### 466. While you were away: what schedules and campaigns did, on the Home

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The ninth of the nine ideas taken from OpenBot (item 449 has the plan), one of its small
ones: a digest of what ran without the owner. Until now the runs of schedules and campaigns, and the approvals the
review model and yolo answered in them, were read run by run. **As built.** `GET /api/away?since=` (`server/away.py`:
one read, pure builders) gives `AwayView`: the scheduler's top-level runs (a schedule's firings and runs by hand, a
campaign's sessions; `source` `schedule`, no helper, still in their chat) that ended at or after the moment or work,
counted by how they ended with the campaigns' sessions apart, the newest fifty with what names them (the campaign's
title, the schedule's name from its chat, else the goal's first line), their agent, steps and the first line of their
answer, masked by `redact_secrets` before it is cut to 200 characters (the store masked it once already); the approvals
`reviewer` and `yolo` decided since the moment in those runs and in their helpers, by who and how, and the ten runs with
the most; and the drafts they wrote that still wait. It reads no summary and no arguments of an approval. The moment is
kept within the last week and not after now. The critique's points taken: no extension of `GET /api/approvals`, which
nothing would read; decisions counted only in those runs and their helpers, not in the owner's own chats; the cost of
the queries measured on the owner's store (195 runs) at under a millisecond, so no index is added. The Home shows it as
a card over the Today strip (`away.js`; its words in `awaywords.js`, tested in `tests/js/away.test.js`): the headline,
the newest two runs and the two runs with the most decisions, each list with its **Show N more**, and **Mark as read**,
which keeps the moment in this browser (`dot.away.read`); the first visit marks it and shows nothing, so the card never
pours out the past, and the card is hidden while there is nothing to tell. It reloads on the lifecycle stream as the
strip does, and keeps only the newest answer, so a slow one never overwrites it nor gives Mark as read an older moment.
Yolo is untouched: it only reads the records item 443 keeps. Tests: `tests/unit/test_away.py` (the moment, the masked
and cut answer, what names a run, the counts and the ranking), `tests/smoke/test_away.py` (a store seeded as schedules,
a campaign and the owner's chat leave it: the counts, the rows and their names, the decisions of a run and its helper
counted and the owner's own answer, one from before the moment and one in the owner's chat left out, the waiting draft
of a schedule's run counted and the owner's not, no summary, argument or secret in the answer, the week's bound; the
Home at 1280 px light and at 390 px dark as a touch screen: nothing on a first visit, then the headline, two runs and
two decision rows each opened by its Show 1 more, Mark as read, and nothing after a reload), `tests/js/away.test.js`.
Docs: `docs/daemon.md`, `docs/autonomy.md`.
