### 1151. A field's readback asks the page once before the step and once after it

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** **Measured** after item 1145 went live: the readback of a step that writes into a field
asked its page three more times (what the field held, a turn of its event loop, what it holds), each a round trip to
Chrome. On a local page a batch of eight fills made 77 calls into its page where it had made 53, and a form of eleven
steps took about 0.2 s longer on a quiet Mac, more on a busy one. A click costs what it did (a single click: 141 ms
before, 136 ms after).

**Built.** One answer of the page where a step asked two (`browser/driver.py`):

- Before a `fill` or a `select`, the marking its secret check reads and what the field holds come together
  (`_MARKED_READ_SCRIPT`). The marking is `_FIELD_MARKING_SCRIPT`'s own, run as it is; a reading that throws answers
  null beside it, so the secret check never depends on the reading, and an answer that is not the script's fails the
  step rather than pass for a field with no marking.
- After the step, the turn and the reading are one call into the field's own frame (`_TURN_READ_SCRIPT`), given the
  time a step is given rather than the 1.5 s of a probe: a page at work is slow, not unreadable.
- A box is read with its turn (`_TURN_TICKED_SCRIPT`); Playwright is asked only for a state the script does not know.

Eight fills now make 61 calls (8 more than before item 1145, not 24), a `select` 26 for 25, a `check` 24 for 23, a
`type` 27 for 25.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_readback_calls.py`, a stand-in for the element that counts what it is asked:
one question before and one after, the marking kept when the reading fails, no second reading of a field that holds
what was asked, Playwright asked for a box only when the script does not know. The readback's own smoke tests
(`tests/smoke/test_browser_verified.py`) and the secret fields' (`tests/smoke/test_secret_fields.py`) pass unchanged.
The counts above are of `Locator.evaluate`, `Page.evaluate`, `ElementHandle.evaluate`, `Locator.count` and
`Locator.is_checked` during one batch on a local page, on the code before item 1145, after it and with this item.
