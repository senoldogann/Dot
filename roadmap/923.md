### 923. A partial view waits for the whole snapshot that timed out

**Status: done (2026-11-02).** **Measured:** Dot's own record (problem `3e041d189be13fa1`, three times):
`browser_open` failed with "taking the page snapshot failed ... Page.aria_snapshot: Timeout 8000ms exceeded" on
bsky.app and x.com searches and a webmail's compose page, and each time the log has `browser_partial_snapshot_failed`
with `error="timeout"` just before: the partial view of item 743 never got its part.

**Root cause.** A Playwright snapshot that times out is not stopped in the page: its script runs on to its end, and
whatever is asked of the page next waits behind it. The naming of the part (`_PARTIAL_ROOT_SCRIPT`) was given the same
time as the whole, so on a page whose whole snapshot took more than about twice that time it timed out waiting for the
first one to end, every time. Measured in a headless Chrome on a list of 60 000 links: a whole snapshot of 4.4 s given
1 s kept the page from answering anything for 1.4 s more; `capture_page` with 1 s failed. Item 743's test made the
whole fail at once without running it, so it never met this.

**The change.** `driver._partial_snapshot` lets the naming of the part wait `_PARTIAL_WAIT_TIMES` (2) times the
snapshot's time, so a page whose whole snapshot takes up to about three times `action_timeout_s` still gives a part;
the part's own snapshot keeps the same time as the whole. A page busy longer still fails with the first timeout, as
before; the longest such call grows from about 24 s to 32 s at the default 8 s.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_browser_partial_snapshot.py`: a page of 60 000 links whose whole snapshot is given a
third of the time it takes, in a real headless Chrome, now gives a part; with the old wait it fails. (Item 984 gives up
on timing the page for this, which lost two pull requests: the test holds it for a known time instead.)
