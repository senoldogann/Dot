### 1421. Three kinds of push, each with a switch for each phone

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Based on item 1420's branch (`item-1420-phone-activity-lease`), which it edits the same
function as. From the research of 2026-11-23 on what the mobile agent apps push (Claude's two switches, the sixth
rule of `agent-design-bar.md`: push only when a decision is needed, something is ready, or it is done). The app's pushes
were all one switch, iOS's own.

What changed: the push kinds of `PUSH_KINDS` now name a group each (`notify/push.py`, `PUSH_GROUPS`, `push_group`;
`PUSH_KINDS` is made from the map, so a new kind cannot join without one, and the `needs_you` entries of the asked kinds
come from `_ASKED_KINDS`). `needs_you`: `approval_requested` (every severity: an approval, a question, a secret, a
hand-off), `question_left` and Dot's own failures (`schedule_missed`, `schedule_stopped`, `daemon_restarted`,
`learning_stalled`: the pre-push review of this item found that a phone that switched `done` off would stop hearing the
only warnings that Dot's own work broke). `ready`: `draft_created` and `owner_told` (what Dot tells the owner on its own).
`done`: `run_finished`, `campaign_ended` and `schedule_run_ended`. Each phone has three switches, all on until it says
otherwise (`GET` and `PUT /api/phone/push-settings`, the phone's own key; the Mac's token is a 403; the three booleans are
given whole, a missing or non-boolean one is a 422): kept in the store's `kv` table under the phone's id
(`notify/settings.py`), forgotten when the phone is removed, and read by `ApnsWatch` for every alert it sends, the lock
screen's approval alert included (an approval is `needs_you`). A row that cannot be read is logged and read as all on, so
that a phone hears more rather than miss what waits for it. The Live Activity is no kind and goes as before. A schedule's
level of notices (item 461) decides upstream whether its run's end is an event at all; the `done` switch only decides
whether a phone hears it. Web push to a browser is not changed. `docs/phone.md` has the contract.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_push.py` (every push kind names one of the three groups, the map in full, the asked kinds are
`needs_you`, the alerts of item 1420's lease are all `done`), `tests/unit/test_push_settings.py` (defaults, one row for each phone, forget, strict
booleans, an unreadable row), `tests/unit/test_apns.py` (a watch with three phones: one wants to be asked only, one never
said, one wants all but being asked; the lock screen's approval; the Live Activity still told; a schedule's levels still
decide which ends are events and the `done` switch holds the heard ones back), `tests/smoke/test_phone_push_settings.py`
(the routes on a served daemon: 403, defaults, set and read, a phone's own, 422 for a body of another shape, removal),
`ruff format --check`, `ruff check`, `mypy src`.
Not checked: on a phone (the sender is the test's stand-in; no push was sent); the app does not call the routes yet
(`ios/` was not touched), so every phone is told all three kinds until it does. `owner_told` as `ready` and the failures
of Dot's own work as `needs_you` (the spec's `done` says "finished, failed") are my reading of "map what exists": say if a
kind belongs in another group.
