and the caret, and the owner had to leave the chat and come back to read it; and "Goal check: the goal is reached — …"
still stood in the conversation above it.

- The stop: an answer that ends a run under the reader's eyes is written in a few words a frame (`revealAnswer`, item
  366), drawn by the same `drawLive` as a step's live message. Item 1091 had `drawLive` mark a step's bubble blank
  while its model sent only spaces (`live.node`); the answer's writing has no such node, so its first frame threw after
  drawing its first words, and nothing drew again. `tests/smoke/test_answer_streams.py` failed so (the answer's foot
  never came), and runs at night only. Now the step's live message is drawn by `drawStreaming`, which marks it, and the
  answer by `drawLive` alone.

  draws a line: the answer says what came of the work, a run sent back shows what it does, and one that stops says why
  in its answer. The event is unchanged.
