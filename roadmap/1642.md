### 1642. Dot for iPhone: one recipe for the card, the row, the sheet, the buttons and the state mark

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Items 1380 to 1382 had a card language in each sheet (a tinted card here, a flat one there), three
button styles in one row and a "Done" that was a pill in one sheet and bare text in another (the design review's fix 10). Each piece
now has one recipe, written once in `Features/Theme.swift` and `Features/Chrome.swift` and described in `ios/README.md › The design`.

What changed:
- **Card** (`.card()`): the surface, a 1-point hairline, radius 16, flat; a card that asks the owner has an amber hairline
  (`.card(border:)`), never a tint over its fill. The approval card, the inbox's entry and rows, the Run sheet's status card, the
  away and hand-off cards, the notices and the queue's messages are this card.
- **Row** (`CardRow`): a monochrome 22-point Lucide glyph in the secondary colour (no gradient tile), a title, a detail in the quiet
  tier, a chevron; `destructive` puts the muted red on the words of the one row that ends something. **Section** (`SectionLabel`,
  `CardGroup`): a header in the secondary colour, a footer in the quiet tier. `CardDivider` is inset to the row's words.
- **Sheet** (`dotSheet()` and `DoneButton`): the app's ground as the background on every iOS (on iOS 26 the sheet was the system's
  glass, which made a card on it darker than its sheet), the neutral tint, the drag indicator, and ONE trailing "Done" in the
  system's confirmation style (a filled glass capsule, the ink with the ground's label) in every sheet and every detent: Queued,
  Run, Step, Needs you, Settings, New chat, the model pickers, Select text and the address screen.
- **Buttons** (`dotButton(.primary|.secondary|.destructive|.quiet)`): flat capsules, 44 points (50 large, 36 small), no glass and no
  gradient: Allow once is the primary, Deny the secondary, a disabled primary a raised fill with the quiet tier's words. The composer's
  send and stop discs are the primary's colours.
- **State mark** (`StateMark`, `WorkingIndicator`): a dot, a glyph or a small moving arc and words, no capsule: running is neutral words
  with the arc (it turns once in four seconds and stands still under Reduce Motion), waiting on the owner is amber, failed is a muted red
  glyph and words, done is muted. It replaces `StatusPill` in the Run sheet's helpers, Settings and the gallery; a helper that waits on
  the owner says "Waiting on you" (the daemon's word is "blocked") and one that is ending says "Stopping". **Chip** (`DotChip`): the
  glass capsule of the model chip.
- `DesignGallery` shows the language on one screen (`-DotGallery 2`: the palette, the avatars, the state marks, the buttons, the chip, a
  group, a notice) and `-DotGallery 5` puts a real sheet over it.
