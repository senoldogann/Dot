### 1002. A new tab Chrome does not hand over says what Chrome made of it

**Status: done (2026-11-06).** **Why.** Dot's record held "opening a tab failed: Chrome did not hand over the new tab
within 8 s" five times in four runs (2026-11-02 to 2026-11-05, Dot's own visible Chrome). Once it began in a Chrome, every
new tab of the next minutes failed the same way while the tab already open went on working; a restarted Chrome made
tabs again. The cause could not be found: the handover took 0.01 to 0.15 s in Dot's own Chrome, visible or headless,
with every core busy, with a page whose script held its tab for 30 s, with the window minimised and with no window left
(probes of 2026-11-06), and the record says nothing of what Chrome did with the tab it made.

**Built.** `browser/pages.py` `unhanded_tab`: what Chrome's `Target.getTargets` lists of the tab that was not handed
over: gone, or its address (still `about:blank` without Dot's mark when its first page never committed), whether a
client is attached to it and whether it is in the browser context of the other tabs. `BrowserSession._background_tab`
asks Chrome for it (2 s at most) and puts it in the error, under `chrome`, so that the next time tells which of those
broke. Docs: `docs/browser-and-computer-use.md`.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_new_tabs.py` (the error carries what Chrome lists; the line for a tab in its own
context, in the others' context, alone and gone); a probe against a real Chrome gave "Chrome lists the tab at
'about:blank#dot-probe', attached, in the browser context of the other tabs" and, once it was closed, "Chrome lists no
such tab any more".
