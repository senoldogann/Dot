### 859. A card stands in the chat as it is asked, in Dot.app too; Dot's lines wear its face; an agent's message stays folded

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, with two screenshots of a team's chat in Dot.app. The first
showed Dot's lines along the way with no face at their left. The second showed "Needs approval: rea-mcp:
extract_artifact({}) — aggressive preset still asks for: external_send" with no card under it and "On it…" below:
"izin istiyor ama bu izin sohbetten girip çıkma yapmaz isem göremiyorum, anlık olarak sohbette göstermiyor bazen".
The owner also wrote: "subagentlerin Message from Bug Reproduction geldiğinde başka sayfaya gidip geldiğimde mesajı
uncollapse yapmasın, ben açana kadar collapse kalmalı".

**The card.** The run writes the policy line and the card's `approval_requested` a moment apart, then waits for the
owner. WebKit (Safari, the iPhone, Dot.app) hands the page the first writes that come close together and holds the rest
until the next write: the stream's keepalive, 15 s on. That is the class item 716 met in the stored replay. In
Playwright's WebKit a probe (the page open, then a command that asks under the cautious preset) had the card 15 s late
on one try of three, and the test below at its first card on two runs of two; in Chrome it came at once. The owner's store holds the cards of that chat in order,
and one of them expired before it was seen.
- `server/stream.py`: the run's stream and the lifecycle stream write a keepalive a quarter of a second after their
  last write, when no other came (`_FLUSH_S`), and every 15 s as before when nothing happens. Both are counted from
  the stream's own last write. A run's stream hears every run's events, and another run streaming its words put the
  keepalive off for as long as it streamed. That was the review's finding on the first draft, and it held the 15 s
  keepalive off before this change too.

**The face.** Item 374 kept the face for the answer that closes a turn. The owner reads Dot's lines as messages in a
chat, which show who speaks.
- `timeline.js`: the first of the agent's lines after someone else spoke (the owner, its helpers, its agents) wears
  its still face. The lines that follow line up with its words. The steps, thoughts and notes between them do not count
  as someone speaking, and neither do what it asks or shows on the way (a card, a hand-off to an agent, a page, a
  restart).
- `app.css`: a line with a face takes the face's column, as the answer does.

**The agents' messages.** Item 830 had the block open with **What it is doing** (the default), and every drawing of the
chat opened it again.
- `timeline.js`: the block is folded until the reader opens it, in either choice. One opened stays open in that tab
  (`sessionStorage`, the newest 200), and a choice of the work shown no longer opens or folds it (`layout.js` sends no
  event of the choice any more: nothing else listened to it).
- `docs/runs.md`, `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` and `docs/daemon.md` say so.
