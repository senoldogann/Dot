### 1852. A refused key's line on a phone's sign-in is chosen by the style, not by the script

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** `tests/smoke/test_phone_line.py::test_a_key_that_was_refused_is_said_on_the_sign_in_with_the_way_to_pair_again`
failed on the browser (phone 2) job of `main` (2026-11-25): the sign-in said "Dot did not accept that access key"
(the `renderSignIn(..., true, true)` of a refused key) but drew no `.link-line`. `renderSignIn` built the line only when
`phoneScreen()` (`matchMedia("(hover: none) and (pointer: coarse)")`) held at the moment it drew, once; Chromium's
touch emulation can reach the page after its first frames (the same lag items 966 and 1024 found in `test_phone_back`,
`test_phone_fit` and `test_phone_dialogs`), and then the line was never drawn. A real phone could be read so too while
its page starts.

What changed: the line of a refused key is always built on the sign-in, and `shell.css` shows it under
`(hover: none) and (pointer: coarse)` alone, which the browser applies whenever the touch screen is known; a Mac's page
still says the refusal in the card's words only. The test now also checks that a page with no touch screen does not
show the line.

Not reproduced locally (4 runs alone, the module under `-n 2`, all green before and after); the cause is read from
the CI failure: the refused path ran, the phone check did not hold.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_phone_line.py` under `-n 2`, `npm run check`.
