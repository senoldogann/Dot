### 796. Memory: a restored note's vector, a resting note's tag, a run's reserved tags, an undone merge's date

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner, the third round of the review of every module. Measured on their
store first, read only: 754 notes, 754 rows of the search index, 754 vectors of 1024 numbers, no orphans, no trashed note
recalled, no awake duplicates. A review of `memory/`, the reflection and the store found, each checked in the code:

- **A restored vector of another size.** The trash kept each note's vector when the embedding model changed, and a note
  restored then put a vector of the old size beside the new ones, which failed every search and every run's start. The
  trash's vectors are dropped with the others when the model changes, and a vector is restored only of the size in use;
  else the note is embedded again.
- **A note folded into its twin stayed awake.** The rest tag came after a note's own tags and the cap of six cut it off,
  so a note with six tags was counted put aside and stayed awake. The rest tag is kept past the cap.

  words matched an awake note's put that note to rest. The reflection's tags are taken out of what a run saves or
  updates, after the tags are cleaned as the store keeps them (`#rest` and `rest.` are `rest`), and a note's own
  (the owner's `keep-separate` after an undone merge) stay through a run's update.
- **An undone merge's notes slept again.** "Undo this merge" woke its notes with their old dates, so one unused for 60
  days rested again the next night; they are written anew, as a woken note is.
