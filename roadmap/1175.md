### 1175. A read-only field of a login is refused at once, with why

**Status: done (2026-11-14).** Dot's record showed `browser_fill_login: the field did not take the login` five times on
auth.webmail.zone.fi, each with the part `username`. That sign-in asks for the address, then for the password, and
keeps the address in its field, read-only, on the second step (its script: `readonly: activeStep !== 1`). The run gave
that field's ref with the password's; Playwright's fill waited the whole action time (8 s) for a field that takes no
typing, and the run was told nothing of why, so the password did not go in either. The model's choice of refs is its
own, but the words it got could not tell it what to change.

- `driver.py`: `fill_login_field` reads whether the field is read-only (`readonly`, as Playwright reads it before it
  types) with the rest of what it asks of the field, and refuses a read-only one at once, before anything is typed or
  held back: the field is read-only, a sign-in in two steps keeps the username so, give the refs of the fields that
  take typing (the password alone).

Checked: `test_vault_items_fill.py::test_a_read_only_field_is_refused_at_once_with_why_and_the_password_alone_then_goes_in`
fills a username and a password on a password step whose username is read-only: without the change it fails with "the
field did not take the login"; with it the run is told the field is read-only, the password field stays empty, nothing
is held back, and the password given alone goes in.
