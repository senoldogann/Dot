### 1314. A connector a key change retired is closed after its calls end, and idle connections to a provider are dropped after 30 seconds

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** `ModelRouter.retire` put the connector a key change took out of service in a list that
was closed only at `aclose`, so a daemon that ran for weeks kept one connector (with its HTTP client and its open
connections) for each key saved. And the log showed `SSLError ... BAD_RECORD_MAC` on long-lived keep-alive connections
(`keepalive_expiry` was 60 s), each retry of which sent a prompt of 28 to 51k characters again.

The router counts the calls it makes itself (`complete`, `model_info`) for each connector. A retired connector is closed
once none of them is left and 30 seconds more have passed (the grace is for a caller that took the connector from
`provider()` before the key changed and keeps it outside the count: a listing, the doctor); a key changed while a call
is under way never cuts it. `aclose` stays the final sweep: it closes what is still in service, what is retired and
waiting out its grace, and waits for a closer that is closing one, so each connector is closed once.
`llm/http.py` keeps an idle connection `KEEPALIVE_EXPIRY_S = 30.0` seconds (was 60). httpcore drops a connection whose
read failed, so `ssl.SSLError` needed no case of its own in the connector; a test through httpcore's real connection pool
shows the retry opens a new connection and sends the same prompt again.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_router_connectors.py` (a retired connector is not closed while its call is under way, is closed
after it and the grace, a connector that serves nothing is closed after the grace, two key changes close each connector
once, `aclose` sweeps one still in its grace and one whose call is going), `tests/unit/test_transport_retry.py` (the retry
after a TLS error goes out on a fresh connection with the whole prompt; the idle expiry is 30 s), and the existing
connector tests.
