### 2047. Drafts in the owner's own Gmail and Outlook

**Status: done (2026-11-27), waits on the owner's look (new OAuth scopes).** From Grok Bot's review (backlog GK2):
`mail_search` and `mail_read` read Gmail and Outlook, but an answer went only to Mail.app (`draft_message`) or the
browser.

- `mail_draft` (tool group `accounts`, tasks and chats) makes a draft in the account's own Drafts: Gmail's
  `drafts.create` (a reply gets the thread and `In-Reply-To`/`References` of the message `reply_to` names), Graph's
  `messages` or `createReply`. With `draft` it writes a waiting draft of its chat again (`drafts.update`, `PATCH`).
- The draft is the chat's draft card of item 314 (`drafts.mailbox`, a JSON column added in place): the account it
  goes from and **Open in Gmail** / **Open in Outlook** (Gmail's `#drafts?compose=<message id>`, Graph's `webLink`,
  https only), on the web (`drafts.js`) and the phone (`DraftCard.swift`). Send on the card writes the card's words into
  the mailbox's draft and sends it (`drafts.send`, Graph's `send`); Discard deletes it there (a 404 means it is already
  gone and the card is dropped all the same).
- Sending stays the owner's press, whatever the approvals: the tool is classified like `draft_message`
  (`notes_write`, `safe`, a key in it flagged `exfiltration`), no new ask in any mode (freedom contract row), and no run
  can send a draft. A write is retried only after a 429; a lost answer to a send says whether it went is not known.
- Scopes: Google adds `gmail.compose`, Microsoft `Mail.ReadWrite` and `Mail.Send`; neither provider has a drafts-only
  scope, so this reverses part of item 558 (a token read out of the Keychain could send mail, and at Microsoft change
  or delete it). The owner re-consents once per account in Settings › Connectors › Accounts; until then a draft is
  refused (403) with that advice.
- Context: the tool is in the `accounts` group, withheld without a signed-in account and off on the first step with
  one; `tests/smoke/test_context_budget.py` passes unchanged (fresh home). The full tool surface grows by ~530 tokens
  (`tests/fixtures/prompt_surface/summary.txt`).

How it was checked: `tests/smoke/test_mail_drafts.py` (a real sign-in against stand-ins of both APIs and a keychain
in memory: a Gmail reply made, rewritten and sent by the card's Send; an Outlook reply discarded; a 403 with the
