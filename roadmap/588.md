### 588. A step never undoes the owner's adoption; outside content asks first

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** The harness audit of 2026-10-23 (items 587 to 589) read the improvement loop against the
owner's own clicks and found: `run_step` ended by marking its survivor `probed` with a statement that changed any
state, so a version the owner adopted while the step ran (a step plays up to `step_minutes`, and the page lists its
candidates from the start) became `probed` and the page said Built-in; the step retired the versions it beat as
"beaten" even when the owner had adopted one of them, which took the adopted pointer with it; with `adopt = "auto"` it
replaced a version the owner chose meanwhile with a text that was never compared with it; the composer's version
carried none of its candidates' taint; adopting a version Dot wrote after a run read a page was a bare click; a step
recorded as `failed` could have adopted its survivor; twelve tainted nights in a row hid the clean experience before
them; the composer and the experience writer were shown each scoring task by its id and verdict, and a lesson naming one
was accepted into the library the proposer reads first; and `runs.harness_version` was written and never read. This
entry was written before the code, as item 540 asks: what must hold, where it is held, and what only asks. The code
follows it.

**What must hold, and what only asks.**

| What | What must hold | Held by | What only asks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The owner's adoption stands | what the owner adopted or set aside while a step ran is as they left it | the store: `set_harness_state`, `retire_unadopted_harness` and `adopt_harness_replacing` change a version only `WHERE state IN ('candidate', 'probed')`, and the last only over the version it compared with, in one transaction with the owner's own writes | the step's decision, which says what it left alone |
| A step adopts over what it compared | `auto` adopts the survivor only over the version that was in use when the step began | `adopt_harness_replacing(version, replacing)`: `None` (not adopted) when the adopted key is no longer `replacing` or the survivor is no longer open | the decision says "left to you: what is in use changed while the step ran" |
| A failed step adopts nothing | a step recorded `failed` put no version in use | `adopts_on_its_own(..., failed=)`, true when a role failed after the probes | the decision says the step failed after the probes |
| Taint is the OR of the parts | a composed version is from outside content when any candidate it merged is | `_compose` writes `tainted` in the composed version's notes from its candidates' | the owner's reading of the pill |
| Adopting a tainted version is not a bare click | `dotx harness adopt` warns and takes a yes; the page's Use asks | `cmd_harness_adopt` (no terminal and no `--yes`: not adopted) and `confirmDialog` on the page | the warning's words; `POST /api/harness/{id}/adopt` stays as it was, the question is the command line's and the page's |
| The scoring set stays out of the proposer's reach | no task id nor task verdict reaches the composer, the experience writer or, through the library, the proposer | `contrast_text` is counts only and the models are given nothing else; `experience_check` refuses an entry that names a task of either set (by its id as the catalog writes it, in any case, as a token of its own; the words of its description name nothing); the library read leaves out one already stored that does | the writer is asked again, without being told which task |

**Approach.**
- **M3.** `Store.set_harness_state` marks `probed` or `candidate` only a version that is one of the two and returns the
  version as it stands; `retire_unadopted_harness(version, now)` retires only a candidate or a probed one and says
  whether it did; `adopt_harness_replacing(version, replacing, now)` adopts in one unit only if the kv key still names
  `replacing` and the version is still open. `run_step` uses them: its survivor is marked `probed`, a survivor the owner
  already adopted or set aside is not touched and the step's own adoption is not tried (the decision says "is in use, as
  you left it while the step ran"), the versions it beat are retired only while open, with `retired` added to their
  notes only when they were, and `auto` adopts through `adopt_replacing`. No Python-side re-read: the guard is in the
  SQL, so no click can land between a read and a write.
- **L1.** `adopts_on_its_own` takes `failed`: a composer or an experience writer that failed after the probes leaves the
  step recorded `failed` with the survivor `probed` and not adopted, and the docs say so.
- **M2.** The composed version's notes carry `tainted`, true when any winner's is. `is_tainted(version)` reads it.
- **M8.** `dotx harness adopt ID [--yes]`: a tainted version prints a warning (that a line in it may come from content
  others can author, and to read it with `dotx harness show`) and asks `Adopt it anyway? [y/N]`; a no, an end of input
  or no terminal leaves it unadopted and says so (exit 1), and `--yes` adopts. The page: a *From outside content* pill
  after the summary of a version's row (the column of the states is one width, which a second pill overflows) and on
  the card of the version in use, and **Use** asks "Use a version written from outside content?" first, as **Set
  aside** does. The API's adopt does not ask.
- **L3.** `Store.list_harness_experiences(limit, untainted=, offset=)`: the library read (`experience_lines`) takes the
  untainted ones, newest first, a page at a time until it has `limit` that do not name a task, so the tainted and the
  task-naming are left out before the newest twelve are taken.
- **M10.** `contrast_text` is now what the models read: the verdict in all, on how many of the tasks the candidate did
  better and worse (`better on 2 of 8 tasks, worse on 0`) and how many could not be judged, and the tokens. The task by
  task text moved to `contrast_detail`, which the notes of a version and the record of a step keep for the owner (the
  page and `dotx harness show` read them). `ExperienceRequest` has `withheld` (the ids of the scoring and the final
  set, never shown to the writer), and `experience_check` refuses a cue, a lesson or an evidence that names one
  (`names_a_task`: the id as the catalog writes it, in any case, as a token of its own) with a message that names no
  task. The proposer's own request carries a run's digest, the harness and the experience library, and the library is
  clean by the two checks above.
- **M9.** `RunRecord.harness_version` (a field with a default, filled from the column the loop already wrote), so that
  `GET /api/runs`, `GET /api/runs/{id}` and the run's record carry it; `RunCosts.harness_version` for `GET
  /api/runs/{id}/stats` and `dotx stats --run`; `RunStatistics.harness` (`HarnessUse`: the finished runs per version,
  with how many ended done) and a **Harness** table in `dotx stats`, Dot's own text beside the versions.

**Data.** None: no column and no table is added (`runs.harness_version` was there), and every new field has a default,
so a record written before reads and one written after is read by a Dot from before the item as far as that Dot knows
its fields (the models of the API are the ones that grew).

**Review round 1 (2026-10-24).** The independent review found that the guards of 588 held (a state changes only from
candidate or probed, the step adopts only over the version it compared with, no model reads a task) and three smaller
things.

- **N8, the taint of a composed version.** `is_tainted` read the notes of the version alone, so a version composed
  before the notes carried the mark gave no warning, and the page, which read the notes itself, took notes it could not
  read for clean while the command raised. `taint_of(store, version)` (and `is_tainted`, now `async`) follows
  `composed_of` as far down as the parts go (a part that is gone is a taint too, and a loop in the notes is cut), and
  notes that cannot be read are a taint that says so. `GET /api/harness` gives the page the answer (`from_outside`, by
  version id); the page no longer reads the notes for it.
- **N12, what tainted it.** The warning said `(a page, a message, a download)` whatever the run had read (a download
  is no kind of read: what taints is a web address, a page or the screen in the browser, an answer of a tool server).
  A candidate's notes now keep what its run read (`tainted_by`: the kinds and channels of its tool results, from
  `Store.outside_reads`), a composed version's the union of its parts', and the warning, the question of the page and
  the pill's hover say it (`its run read a page in the browser`); a version written before says it does not know.
- **I4, the rest of it (item 587's round 1).** The gate is asked again whenever a text is used (`check_adoptable`, in
  587); what 588 built is where it matters most. `adopt_replacing` never adopts a survivor the gate refuses now (it logs
  `harness_adopt_refused` and the step leaves the survivor to you); `dotx harness adopt` refuses such a version before it
  asks anything, not even `--yes` adopts it, and says how to set it aside; `GET /api/harness` says which versions of its
  page are refused (`refused`, by version id, in words) and which come from outside content (`from_outside`), and the
  page wears *Refused* on such a version, offers no **Use** on it and says in the card of the version in use that the part
  that fails is left out of every run.
- **N4, the experience check.** `names_a_task` took the words of a task's id for its name (`Report failing tests`, `read
  config port` and `bump version` are what an experience says of a test run, a port and a version) and failed a step that
  was better on all ten tasks. It matches the id as the catalog writes it (`bump-version`), in any case, as a token of
  its own, and never the words of its description.

**Tests (round 1).** `tests/unit/test_harness_adoption.py`: the taint of a version by its notes, its parts (before the
mark, as far down, a part gone, a loop), notes that cannot be read, and what the run read; the task by its id and not by
its words, with the default tasks; `dotx harness adopt` says what the run read and refuses a version the gate refuses

`test_harness.py`, `test_harness_page.py` (browser): the composed version keeps what its parts read; the view says
which versions come from outside content and why, a pre-588 composed version and one whose notes cannot be read among
them; the page's pill and question on a desktop and a phone.

**Pre-push review, round 4 (2026-10-24).** `GET /api/harness` asked the gate of every version it lists (`view` in
`server/routes_harness.py`, built in round 1 above for `refused`): up to 501 a request, each seven questions over up to
six readings of both texts, synchronously on the daemon's loop, with `taint_of`'s store reads between them, on every
load of the page. The loop over the versions is a function of its own (`_refused_of`) that `view` runs with
`asyncio.to_thread`, as the codebase does for the rest of its CPU work; the store reads of `taint_of` stay on the loop.
`tests/smoke/test_harness.py` (+1): the gate is asked of each version in a thread other than the loop's, and the version
it refuses is marked all the same and the one it accepts is not.
