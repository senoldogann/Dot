### 752. A page that sends itself on through several pages is read where it lands

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Measured:** in the owner's runs of the last 30 days, ten `browser_open`,
`browser_new_tab` and `browser_act` calls failed with "measuring the viewport failed ... Execution context was
destroyed", 0.9 to 3.6 s in, on pages that send themselves on by script once loaded: Microsoft's sign-in
(entra.microsoft.com, login.microsoftonline.com, login.live.com, ciamlogin.com), Google's account chooser, the Stripe
dashboard on its way to sign-in, redfin. Two more failed with "waiting for the page to settle failed" on the same chain.
Found by the review of browser use the owner asked for: the size of the page (read on every capture, as the owner's
Dot fits pages to the window) was read with no retry, while the snapshot beside it had one; and that one retry, which
the settle had too, met the next hop of a sign-in's chain.

**The change.** `driver._again_after_navigation` (was `_once_more_after_navigation`) runs a read again while its
document is replaced under it, after the new document is parsed (`wait_for_load_state("domcontentloaded")`, 5 s at
most), five times at most; any other failure, or the fifth, fails at once with the number of tries. `measure_viewport`
reads through it too. A capture's two reads (the size and the snapshot) no longer outlive each other: when one fails the
other is cancelled, where a snapshot left running after the call had failed replaced the tab's refs under the next
call.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_redirect_chain.py` (a stand-in page, no browser): the size of a page whose
document is replaced under three reads is read on the fourth, after three waits for the new document; one replaced
under every read fails after five; another failure is not tried again. The first fails on the code before. The browser
tests of `test_browser.py` and the effect, eval and console tests pass.
