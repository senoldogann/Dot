### 1997. The pre-push hook measures the first step's budget when the prompt surface changes

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** A change to `.claude/hooks/pre-push-review.sh` applies to the owner only once their own
checkout has pulled it.

`tests/smoke/test_context_budget.py::test_a_fresh_homes_first_step_stays_within_its_budget` is a macOS smoke test, which
a pull request does not run (item 1886), so a change that grew a tool's description or the system prompt passed its pull
request and turned `main` red on the merge: four times on 2026-11-26 and 27 (items 1953, 1969, 1974 and 1988).

What changed: after the prompt surface check of item 1971 passes, the hook runs that one test when the pushed diff
touches `tests/fixtures/prompt_surface/` or the test file, in a throwaway `DOT_HOME`, `DOT_LOG_DIR` and `DOT_CHROME_DIR`,
and stops the push with the test's assertion lines (the measured total, system and tools against the budget) and what to
do. The surface fixture is what decides it: the check before it makes the fixture follow every change of what the first
step carries, so a change under `src/dotagent` that leaves the surface as it was runs nothing more. The surface check now also runs
for a push of the fixture or the budget's test alone, and the test file joins the paths that must have nothing
uncommitted for the check to read what is pushed. The throwaway home is removed on an interrupt too.

The test alone took 31 s of wall time on the owner's Mac at a load average of 96 (24 s in pytest), and 150 s on a cold first run, against a review of minutes.

Checks: `tests/unit/test_pre_push_hook.py` (a change of the surface runs the budget in a home that is removed after, one
over the budget stops the push with the numbers and no model is asked, a change that leaves the surface as it was does
not run it, a push of the budget alone runs it).
