### 946. Customize in the theme's own colours, and an account's chip named by its provider

**Status: done (2026-11-03).** **Asked** by the owner: a message that picked the Google and the Microsoft accounts
showed their addresses (they wanted the provider's mark and name, Google, Microsoft); and Plugins, Skills and
Connectors made quieter, in the colours of the theme, each service, plugin and skill with an SVG logo where one exists
and a fixed icon where none does, the logos in one colour (the owner's pick from a mockup, over their brand colours).

**Built.**
- `server/palette.py`, `views.py`: an account of the palette names its provider (`provider_name`); `picks.js` draws an
  account's chip as the provider's mark and name, the address in its title, once the palette names it (until then,
  and for an account signed out since, the mail icon and the address).
- `marks.js`: a logo is drawn through its file's shape as a CSS mask over the ink of where it stands (`.mark-logo`), so
  every logo is one colour in every look and scheme; the tinted tiles (`tintMark`, the per-brand ink, the gallery
  kinds' colours) are gone, every mark stands on one quiet tile (`--surface-2`, the ink). The account card, the first
  chat's card, the sidebar's Connect apps, the composer's menus and chips wear the same.
- `static/icons/plugins`: Simple Icons' symbol (16.36.0, CC0) for each of the 78 brands it draws, 1Password's added;
  the 24 it does not draw keep gilbarbara's file, of which only the shape shows. Cal.com's symbol is its wordmark and
  stays out.
- `customizehead.js`: the head is the title, its line, and the search with the tab's controls on one row, on the
  page's ground; the lit card and its scattered marks are gone. The skills are one list, a row each.
- The Mac applications' own icons (`connections/app_icons.py`, `GET /connections/{id}/icon`) are gone: they were the
  one coloured artwork left, and Mail, Calendar, Notes and the rest wear their glyphs. The listings' `colour` and
  `app`, which only drew them, went too.

**Checked.** `npm run check`; the unit tests (`test_plugins_directory.py` holds each symbol as one path, named in the
licence); the browser tests of Connectors, Skills, Plugins, the composer's picks (an account's chip says Google with
its address in its title) and Connect apps, and the phone pages; the three tabs looked at in a throwaway daemon, dark,
light and on a phone.
