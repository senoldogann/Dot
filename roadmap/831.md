### 831. A chat with a team is a group chat: the lead says the plan, and every agent works at once

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner, with screenshots of another agent app's group chat ("ajan
grubuyla sohbet başlattığımızda orada kaç ajan varsa hepsi aynı anda başlamalı; verdiğim görevi koordine olarak
yönetmeliler"): a PM answers first with the thesis and the parts, naming @Designer and @Engineer, who answer under their
own names, agreeing or adding. The second stage of the agent-first plan the owner chose (item 830 the first), with the
way they chose: "the lead plans, the others work at once".

What Dot had: a chat with a team (item 610) whose run of Dot's hands an agent a part with `message_agent` (several at
once in parallel, `[agent] parallel_tools`, 4) and puts the answers together; the agents' answers stand in the
conversation under their names (items 668, 733, open since 830). But nothing had the lead say a plan, name the agents or
send them all; an agent read its part alone, never the chat; and a team of more than four worked four at a time. The
owner's two teams of the old store were never once talked to.

- `prompt.py`, `_lead_section`: the chat is a group chat; the lead answers first with one short message, the plan and
  who does what as @Name, and sends every agent it names its part in that same reply, all at once; it does no agent's
  part; it ends with one short message to the owner.
- `tools/team.py`: `message_agent` sent by the run that leads a group chat hands the agent, as its opening, who is in
  the chat and that its answer is its message there, its part, and the chat's conversation (`group_conversation`: the
  owner's messages, the lead's and the agents' answers, Dot's notes left out, the newest 6,000 characters) fenced with a
  nonce as text that asks nothing of it, then how to answer, as people in a chat do. Its run's goal stays the part
  (`Spawner.delegate`'s `opening`), so that its row reads as before. A tainted lead's agents start tainted, as every
  delegated run does.
- `agent/loop.py`, `parallel_calls`: a run that leads a group chat runs as many calls at once as its team has agents, up
  to eight.

The pre-push review's three nits, taken: a compaction's summary (`[Continuity note`) is left out of the conversation an
agent reads, as it is Dot's and may restate a page or a mail; the many calls at once are only for a reply whose calls
all send agents their parts (`step_parallelism`), so that a lead's reads keep `[agent] parallel_tools`; and an agent of
the owner's outside the team reads its part alone, not a group chat it is not in.

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_team.py`: a scripted lead in a chat with a team of two writes the plan and sends both
their parts in one reply, and an agent outside the team its own; the runs started before any ended; each agent of the
team read the group, its own part alone and the chat fenced, the outsider its part alone; their goals are the parts. `tests/unit/test_team.py`: the conversation without Dot's notes and summaries
and cut to its newest; the opening's fence, which a quote cannot close; the calls at once by the team's size, and only
for a reply that sends parts.
