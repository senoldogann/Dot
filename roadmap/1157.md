### 1157. A box is judged by Dot's reading after the page's turn, also when Playwright read it first

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** **Seen** in the gate: `tests/smoke/test_browser_verified.py::
test_a_dropdown_and_a_box_the_page_puts_back_fail_their_steps` failed in the first attempt of `browser (pages)` on two
pull requests in a morning (#916 and #917, neither its cause) and passed in the second. Its box is cleared by a timer
of the page. Playwright reads a box itself right after its click and raises when it is not as asked
(`Clicking the checkbox did not change its state`); on a slow runner the page's timer came before that reading, so
the step read `failed (may have been sent)` with nothing checked, where on a quick machine the same box read
`failed (sent)` and `contradicted`. That was a flaky test, and a step told two ways by the speed of the machine.

**Built.** `_tick` in `browser/driver.py`: Playwright's own reading right after the click is not the step's. The
click went out; the page may put the box back before that reading, or set it only after it (a box a script draws,
which read as a failed step though it was ticked a moment later). Either way the reading after the page's turn
(`_ticked`, item 1145) says which: verified, or failed, sent and contradicted. Any other failure of the click is the
step's, as before.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_browser_readback_calls.py::
test_a_box_playwright_read_too_early_is_told_by_the_reading_after_the_pages_turn` with a stand-in that raises as
Playwright does: cleared again is contradicted, ticked a moment later is verified, a click that timed out still
fails the step. In a real Chrome the smoke test has a second box, cleared in its own `change` handler, which is
clear at Playwright's reading every time: without `_tick` the test fails there with the gate's own words
(`('failed', 'unknown', [])`), and with it both boxes read failed, sent and contradicted.

Log:

- 2026-11-13: item 1157, a box the page puts back is told the same way on a slow machine as on a quick one: the
  step's own reading after the page's turn decides, not Playwright's right after the click.
