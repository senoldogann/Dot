### 885. The office: Dot and the agents in a 3D room, living their day by what they do

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-10-31), with three screenshots of an app whose agents sit
in a 3D office: "a office where the agents walk, sit, work at their computers ... when a message is sent from the chat
they start working ... the most professional level, better than the screenshots". Chosen: real 3D (WebGL), on the
Agents page.

Researched first: ready CC0 character packs (rigged, with walk and idle) were found, none with a sitting-and-typing
clip confirmed, and none in Dot's look. So everything is drawn in code: no model file, no licence beyond three.js's,
the heads Dot's own looks.

- `static/vendor/three/` three.js 0.187.0 (MIT, the npm tarball's sha512 checked), unminified, loaded by `import()` only
  when the office opens; `@types/three` 0.187.0 a development dependency for the type check; Biome leaves the folder.
- `office/layout.js` (pure): the room's plan, which grows with the desks (two a pod), the lounge, the coffee bar, the
  whiteboard, the shelf, windows, the meeting table, and the spots an agent goes to.
- `office/nav.js` (pure): the walking floor as a grid, A* in eight directions without cutting corners, the path cut
  to its corners.
- `office/brain.js` (pure): an agent's mode from its newest run and the approvals that wait (working, waiting,
  free); working -> its desk, sit, type; waiting -> its desk, stand, face the room, wave; free -> a weighted pick of
  spots no other agent holds, a stay of 9 to 26 s, naps at night; getting up and sitting down eased.
- `office/scene.js`: the slab, two walls with windows onto a sky of the hour, oak floor boards, desks with monitors
  (code scrolls on a working one), keyboards, mugs and nameplates, chairs, sofa, armchair, rug, coffee bar, whiteboard,
  shelf of books, round table, plants, a lamp and a wall clock with the Mac's time, from rounded primitives.
- `office/figure.js`: the figure: the head is the look's shape extruded and bevelled in its colour, eyes on a canvas
  (blink, gaze, focus on work, wide when waiting, shut asleep, smiling over a coffee); jointed limbs eased toward the
  pose; a stride measured on the floor; clothes and an accessory (glasses, headphones, beanie, cap, bun) from the id;
  a contact shadow and a selection ring.
- `office/office.js`: renderer (ACES, sRGB, one shadow-casting sun, lamps at night), a camera that turns, zooms and fits
  the whole room by projecting its corners, tags placed over the heads and lifted off each other, the panel with the
  agent's composer (`views/agents.js` `officeComposer`: the run starts and the page stays), the lifecycle stream and a
  read every 20 s, drawing only in sight, never while Dot's window is away (`data-away`, item 662), a still frame per
  change under reduced motion (the simulation's clock only goes forward, by an offset that settling adds to), a note
  without WebGL 2; leaving the page or rebuilding the room gives back the geometries, materials and textures and loses
  the WebGL context at once (`forceContextLoss`), and the WebGL 2 probe loses its own (the pre-push review: Dot.app
  stays open for days).
- `themes.css`: the office's stage, glass and state colours as tokens (`--office-*`); `types/interface.d.ts` names the
  three.js kinds the office uses, so no `import("three")` stands in a script.
- `views/agents.js`, `app.css`: the Office part over the teams, Hide/Show kept in `dot.agents.office`; on a phone a
  near-square frame and finger-sized tags.
- `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md`, `README.md` say so.

**Checked.** `tests/js/office.test.js` (the plan's desks and size; every spot reachable from every other; the modes; an
agent walking to its desk, sitting and typing, standing to wave, going off free; spots held);
`tests/smoke/test_office.py` (in Chrome, wide and on a phone: every agent and Dot tagged, a working one says so, a click
opens its panel with the box to message it, Close, the lists under it, away and back three times; Hide kept across a
reload; without WebGL 2 the note and the lists); the Agents page's and the phone's browser tests. Looked at in Chrome at
1440 px and 390 px, with agents working, waiting and free.
