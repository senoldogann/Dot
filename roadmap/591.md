### 591. A chat's loops fold under a head, and their Stops stand in one column

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** The owner's screenshot of a chat on their phone with four loops: each row's Stop stood at
a place of its own, pushed by the words beside it, and the four rows, each cut short to "Loop: Inst…" and "on Mondays,
Wednesdays and Fridays at 18:3…", took the room over the composer. They asked for the Stops in one line and for the
loops to fold.

**As built.**
- **One column.** A loop's row is three columns, its dot, its words and its Stop (`.loop-row`, a grid): the words give
  way, never the Stop, which shrank with them in the row's flex before, so the Stops of the rows stand in one line.
- **A phone's two lines.** Under 720 px the message stands over how often it asks again and when next, each on a line of
  its own, where both were cut short on one; wider, they share the line as before.
- **The fold.** Over the rows a head, a button, says "Loops" and how many there are, and folds the rows away; folded, it
  says when the next one asks ("next 18:30"). The choice is kept in the browser (`dot.fold.chat-loops`), as the folds of
  the pages are, and stays across a reload and from chat to chat.
- **When next.** A row says its next firing as the sidebar's mark does (`nextFiring`, item 590): "next 18:30", "next
  Sat 07:00", where it said the daemon's "Sat 2026-10-24 07:00".
- `docs/runs.md` says it.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_chat_loop.py`: a chat with two loops of different words, wide and light and on a phone and
dark: the Stops end at the same place, the head says "Loops 2" and folds the rows, saying the next firing, and the fold
outlives a reload.
