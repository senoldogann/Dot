### 1247. Lessons do not pile up in memory

**Status: done (2026-11-19).** The owner's Dot held 1046 notes 22 days after its fresh start (2026-10-28), 834 of them
lessons, about 38 a day and 97 on 2026-11-19; 577 notes had never been handed to a run. Measured read-only on the
owner's store:

- Of the 834 lessons, 346 were handed to a run other than the one that wrote them, every one of them first within 20
  days of being written (146 within a day, 246 within 7, 313 within 14). A lesson no run was handed in three weeks is
  unlikely to be handed later, yet it stayed awake for `lesson_rest_days` (60).
- Repeats were not the cause: only 38 lessons (4.6%) had an older lesson at the write-time check's cosine of 0.85 or
  more, 98 at 0.80. The write-time checks (a note or a harness habit that says it already) stay as they are; dropping
  at 0.80 would drop lessons the nightly merge's model would keep apart.
- The nightly merge made 11 merges in 22 nights, never near `merges_per_night` (20), so that cap is unchanged.
- Lessons made per day ranged 3 to 97; the days over 40 had the same share later handed (42%) as the others (41%), so
  the cap is a ceiling on a runaway day, not a judge of value.

What changed: `[agent] lessons_per_day` (40): past it in 24 hours, `auto` (counting only its own saves) leaves a kept lesson out with the
reason,
which the run's page shows with Save anyway (the owner's save is not capped). `[agent] lesson_unused_rest_days` (21):
the reflection rests a lesson that no run was ever handed after that many days; one handed keeps `lesson_rest_days`.
The night's retirement now reads every awake lesson (`Store.awake_memories_with_tag`), not the 500 most
recently changed that its merge reads: on the owner's 834 lessons the 334 oldest, the never-handed ones this rule is
for, were never examined. A lesson the owner saved and a pinned note never rest for age; nothing is forgotten without the owner. No new
question is asked anywhere.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_reflection.py`, `tests/unit/test_lessons.py` (`day_full`, `to_retire` with both limits),
`tests/smoke/test_lesson_counts.py` (only auto's new saves count; 502 awake lessons all read), `tests/smoke/test_lessons.py` (a cap of 1 saves the first lesson, leaves the second out with the reason, and Save
anyway still saves it), the lesson merge and failure lesson smoke tests.
