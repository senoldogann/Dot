### 1173. The rest of a chat's words speak Turkish

**Status: done (2026-11-13).** Item 1172 left part of a Turkish chat in English because another session was changing
those files at the time, not because those words were out of scope: the run page's own words (`views/run.js`), the
sidebar's ("Connect apps", the chat menu, deleting chats), the reasoning levels' names, the side panel's Set up head,
and two texts of a message's picks.

- `views/run.js`, `sidebar.js`: their visible words, tooltips and screen-reader names through `t()` and `tCount()`
  (100 keys); the code, the requests and the lines another session changed for item 1169 are untouched.
- `efforts.js`: the levels' names and hints for a person; the level ids (`off`, `low`, ..., `max`) and the one-message
  choices' ids (`ultrathink`, `ultracode`, product names kept as they are) are what the daemon and the models take,
  unchanged.
- `setup.js`: the Set up head and its counts of repeated lines (`tCount`), and the notes/skills words `timeline.js`
  gives it.
- `picks.js`: a picked chip's tooltip and the toast when a message has too many picks of one kind, a whole sentence per
  kind.
- `state.js` `readFailed`: the toast a page shows when it could not read again what it shows ("The chat list could
  not be updated. Reload the page to see it.") speaks the page's language; each caller names what it shows by a
  catalogue key. The console line stays English and the error's own words stay the toast's details; when it reads again
  and when it tells are as before.

What stays English, and why: the words a model or a person writes (a message, an answer, a step's own line); the names
of connected apps and of MCP tools, which come from their servers; the other settings pages, which are not a chat's.

Checked: `npm run check` (both catalogues, their places, `tsc`), 68 browser tests of the composer, the picks, Set up,
the sidebar, rewind, lessons, phone pages and chat choices, 28 of the page guards and routes (the toast), and a
screenshot of a Turkish chat with the sidebar.
