### 1524. One thin line under the bar says how the link stands: offline with the saved chats, offline with what waits, reconnecting, a key refused

**Status: done (2026-11-24); what was checked in a browser, and what was not, is listed at the end.** The web app told a
bad link in four places that did not agree: the bar's pill ("Reconnecting" after a quiet time), the chat's own note, a
toast for every request that failed, and a screen of its own when the page opened with the daemon out of reach. With the
phone keeping its chats (item 1520) and what was done with no link (item 1523) there was a state that none of them could say:
*this is a copy, and this much waits*. The iPhone app says it in one line (`ConnectionBanner`, item 1371 (e)); this is the same.

What changed:

- `js/linewords.js` (pure, `tests/js/linewords.test.js`, 10 tests, both languages): `lineOf(facts)` is `null` while the link is
  well; the phone's key refused comes first (*This phone's key was refused*, with **Pair again**); with no link it says
  *Offline: 2 waiting to send* when something waits (what the owner can act on), else *Offline: showing saved chats, last
  updated 3 min ago* when a copy is shown, else *Offline*; on a link that is there and a Dot that cannot be reached for more
  than a moment, *Reconnecting…* (with what waits, if something does). `ageWords` is *just now*, *3 min ago*, *2 h ago*,
  *4 d ago*. A test holds every line to 90 characters, two lines of a 320 px phone.
- `js/connectionline.js` and `css/shell.css`: the line is a child of the bar's own header (`.stagebar`). *Offline* and a
  refused key take a row of their own, so the page begins under it (the browser test measures that nothing is under it);
  *Reconnecting…* floats under the bar over the room the conversation leaves above its first message (one line, 23 px, the
  first message stands under it), as the notes of a lost stream always did, because `test_phone_return.py` holds that what
  is read never moves when the network is lost (item 328): a first version drew every kind in a row of its own, and the
  chat moved 31 px when the network went; the line has no height while nothing is
  said; the clock that keeps "3 min ago" true (30 s) runs only while a copy is shown and is cleared with the line; a break
  of the stream is told after its quiet time (`breakToldAt`, as the bar's pill is). It wraps to two lines at 320 px rather
  than being cut, and its button is 44 px tall.
- `js/linkfacts.js` gained the facts the line reads (a copy and since when, whether Dot answered, what waits in the outbox, a
  key refused); `main.js` says a refused key and draws the line, with **Pair again** (which focuses the code field), on the
  sign-in screen the refused key leads to; `ui.js` `attempt` shows no toast for a request that got no answer while the line
  says the link is down (the toast covered the conversation the phone keeps for the owner to read; found in the first
  screenshot of an offline chat).

Looked at: the screenshots of the line over the cached chat, over a message that waits (the row *Waiting to send*), while
reconnecting, and on the sign-in with the key refused, at 390 by 844 and 320 by 640, in the light and the dark look, are in the
workspace's `pwa-shots/` (`offline-line-*.png`). Two things were cramped and were fixed: the refused key's line at 320 px
broke its words into three short lines beside the button (the button now wraps under the words), and the buttons of a waiting
message looked like plain words (they have a border now).

Checked: `tests/js/linewords.test.js`; `tests/smoke/test_phone_line.py` (the line is absent while the link is well; over a chat
drawn from a copy made three minutes old it says *Offline: showing saved chats, last updated 3 min ago*; with a message made
offline it says *Offline: 1 waiting to send* and the row stands under the chat; when the link is back the line and the row are
gone and the message was sent; the daemon stopped on a link that is there it says *Reconnecting…*; a key the daemon does not
know brings the sign-in with *This phone's key was refused* and **Pair again**; in each, at 390 by 844 and 320 by 640, light
and dark: the line is inside the bar, the page starts under it, nothing is cut, no sideways scroll, one line at 390 and two
at most at 320, every button 44 px tall); `npm run check`.
What the pre-push review found, and what changed. The line read `navigator.onLine` on every page, and on a Mac with its
network down (the Dot on that Mac still answering) it would have said *Offline*; it is the phone's alone now, and the toast
of a request that got no answer is suppressed only where the line says it.

Not verified: a real iPhone's `navigator.onLine`, which is optimistic (a Wi-Fi with no way out reads as online, and the line
then says *Reconnecting…* once Dot cannot be reached rather than *Offline*); Safari's own handling of the Home Screen app's
status bar over the line; the line on a page that is not a phone's touch screen is not shown (a test holds that it is not).
