### 1197. A locked iPhone is no longer told to the owner, and a phone going between locked and out of reach is told once until it is up again

**Status: done (2026-11-16).** Item 1196 told the owner once per state when the phone stayed locked, unclear or out of
reach for `notify_after_s` (five minutes). The first night it was live the owner got the locked notice five times
(22:48, 02:45, 02:52, 03:19, 04:01 in the daemon's log): each time they used the phone and locked it again, the state
went up, then locked, and a new state was told anew five minutes later. Locking the phone is how the owner uses it,
so the notice was noise; they asked for it to stop.

- `phone/keeper.py`: no notice for `locked`. Settings › Phone and `phone_status` still say it, so a run that needs the
  phone sees it and can say so.
- `next_state` carries `notified` over a change of kind until the phone is up, so a phone going between locked,
  unclear and out of reach (WebDriverAgent can stop answering while the phone sleeps) is told once for the whole time
  it is not up.
- `docs/phone-control.md`, `[phone]` in `defaults.toml` and the `notify_after_s` comment say so.

Checked with the unit tests of the keeper's steps and the smoke test of the keeper against the stand-in WebDriverAgent
(silent, locked, silent again: one notice; up, then silent: a second).
