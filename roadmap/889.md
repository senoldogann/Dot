### 889. The office live: each agent's steps over its head and in its panel, a message seen as a visit, and a lighter room

**Status: done (2026-10-31).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-10-31): "make the 3D system more performant, fluid and
trouble-free ... clicking an agent shows its streaming, what it is doing right then, and from there clicking another
agent runs it at the same time ... the visuals and the system behind them both matter".

Measured first, in Chrome at 1440 px (`data-draw-calls`, `data-triangles`, `data-render-ms` on the frame): 1,420 draw
calls, 615,428 triangles, 2.7 ms a render. After: 271 draw calls, 135,996 triangles, 1.7 ms, the same picture.

- `office/scene.js`: `bakeStatic` merges the still room into one mesh per material (a part marked `userData.dynamic`,
  a screen and the clock's hands, stays); small rounded pieces get fewer segments.
- `office/office.js`: the sun's shadows are drawn when the room is built and when day turns to night
  (`shadowMap.autoUpdate` off); the figures cast none (their contact shadow stays); 30 frames a second while nobody
  walks or sits down and the camera rests; the drawing's pixel ratio follows the frames, from 2 down to 1 and back, set
  only on a change; the figure under the pointer is looked for once a frame; the cost on the frame for tests.
- `office/feeds.js`: follows the eight newest working runs and the open panel's on one stream: each run's stored events
  read once (`GET /runs/{id}/events`), then `GET /runs/streams?ids=…&after=` from the newest read (the pre-push
  review: a stream for each working agent filled the six connections a browser keeps to a server, and the owner's
  message waited behind them); every event read into a feed: the calls in words (`message_agent` as "Message <name>"),
  whether each went through, the answer as written, how it ended. A call read as history moves nobody; a feed manager
  let go starts nothing more; each feed reads an event once by its id, and the stream goes on from the feed that read
  the least (the pre-push review: one global id skipped what a slower history had not reached); only the runs that
  work are streamed, the open panel's that is over read once (a run whose end was pruned made the stream again and
  again).
- `server/stream.py` `follow_runs`, `server/routes_runs.py` `GET /runs/streams` (12 runs at most, before
  `/runs/{run_id}` so that no run is called "streams"), `RunsStreamQuery`; `docs/daemon.md` lists it.
- The office: a bubble over a working agent with its step; the panel's live steps and text, repainted at most four
  times a second; a `message_agent` call walks the sender to the other's desk to talk (`brain.visit`, the pose "talk")
  for 7 s, then back to its work; tags placed with their bubbles' height; a step wakes the still office under reduced
  motion.
- `themes.css`: the bubble's tokens. `docs/agents-and-campaigns.md` says so.

**Checked.** `tests/js/office.test.js` (a run's events into a feed; a visit kept until it ends, then back to typing);
`tests/smoke/test_office.py` (in Chrome: the working agent's bubble, its panel's step marked through, fewer than 300
draw calls, the one stream opened after the history, never from the start; with a real daemon, two runs' stored events
on one stream in the store's order, 404 and 422); looked at in Chrome with two agents' streams played, one messaging the other.
