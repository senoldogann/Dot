### 592. Days together by their short names, so that a loop's time stays in sight on a phone

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** A look at the owner's Dot once item 591 was live, on a phone: a loop's second line, how
often it asks again, still lost its time ("on Mondays, Wednesdays and Frid…", "on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 09:…"),
the part that matters most.

**As built.**
- `rhythm.js` names days together by their short names ("on Mon, Wed and Fri at 18:30", "on Tue and Thu at 09:30"), a
  day alone by its name ("on Mondays at 09:00"); "every day", "on weekdays" and "at weekends" stay. The loop line over
  the composer and the sidebar's mark read them.
- `docs/daemon.md` gives the short form in its example.

**Tests.** `tests/js/rhythm.test.js`: the owner's crons and the other day forms read as above.

  phone, the Stops in one column, the times cut off).
