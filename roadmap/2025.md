### 2025. A new chat's first message opens the one chat screen, on the phone and on the web

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner's report of 2026-11-27 (a real iPhone, build 10): after the first message of a
new chat the phone showed a screen of its own, a plain title bar with the message and "Starting", the bubble with
"Pending", and nothing else: no bot header pill, no composer, no live line.

What changed:
- DotNext: the chat a first message starts is the one `ChatScreen` from the send on, by the outbox's key until the
  daemon names it, then by its thread; the screen keeps its identity across the swap (no push, nothing drawn anew). It
  has the bot's header pill, the message with its quiet "Pending", the starting line and the pill's motion at once, and
  the composer, whose button waits the second or two until the chat is named (a chat with no id yet takes no message;
  the words stay in the field). The bare `StartingChat` screen is gone.
- Web: already the one chat page: the first send waits for the run and opens the chat at `#/runs/<id>` with its own
  composer. Its test now says so.

How it was checked: `StreamingLiveUITests.testANewChatsFirstSendIsTheNormalChatScreen` against the throwaway daemon
(Dot's ⋯ › New chat: the pill and the composer with the message at once, the answer in the same screen, no navigation
bar pushed) and `testANewChatStaysOpenAndBecomesTheRealChat`; `tests/smoke/test_new_chat_model.py` (desktop and phone:
the message and the chat's composer on the page the first send opens).
