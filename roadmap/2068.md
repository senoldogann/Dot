### 2068. A manager reviews the team: stuck orders, idle, overloaded and failing bots, objectives behind, and a card per change it proposes

**Status: done (2026-11-28).** The owner's ask of 2026-11-28, after Grok Bot's note ("specialised bots like a company,
a manager bot oversees them; overload one bot and it drops balls"). Dot had bots, work orders, objectives, hand-offs and
teams, and item 2067 marks a bot that carries too much; nothing looked at the team as a whole and proposed what to
change. The numbers item 2067 measured on the owner's store say what such a review would have found on 2026-11-28: no
stuck order (none open), three failing bots (JobHunter, by its 3 failed runs of 4), eight idle bots of which several
have no routine to retire, and Dot's done share falling (97 % to 78 %).

What changed:
- `agent/team_review.py` (pure): the team as the store has it (`TeamSnapshot`: each bot with item 2067's counts, the
  open orders the bots hold, the active objectives, the routines; a title or name a run wrote after reading outside
  content withheld), the findings read from it with no model (`team_findings`: an order unmoved for two days or past its
  due time, an overloaded bot, a bot whose week's runs failed half the time over three runs or more, an objective with
  no progress for a week, a bot older than a week with no run, order or routine on; Dot and a paused bot never idle),
  the proposals' shapes and their check against the snapshot (`checked_proposals`: an order not open or not one bot's,
  a bot or routine not there, Dot as the target of a move or split, a name taken, a routine named twice or not the
  bot's, a routine already off: each raises, so the model is asked again), and when a review is due.
- `agent/review.py`: `team_reviewer_for`, one call of the manager's model (its own, or the review model; the review
  model's fallbacks when it declines) with no tool but `propose_team_changes`, the findings and the team fenced as data.
  A new call purpose, `team_review`.
- `agent/team_review_service.py`: the state in the store (`kv` key `team_review`: the manager, Dot by default, how
  often, off by default, the last try, the newest review, the last error), one review at a time, none under the
  emergency stop; a review with no finding asks no model. Accept makes the change as the owner's own: orders go on by
  the desk's `reassign` (all still the bot's and in review, or none), a split makes the new bots with the split bot's
  instructions, bounds and edges and moves the routines named, a retirement switches the routine off with a status
  saying why; one that no longer fits is refused (409) and stays waiting. Skip changes nothing but the card.
- The scheduler starts a review when one is due (the daemon's alone), and `/api/team-review` reads and sets it, runs one
  now and accepts or skips a card.
- Web: the Orders part of the Agents page holds **Manager review** above the work orders (the manager, how often,
  Review now, what it found, the summary and the cards with Accept and Skip). Phone: a bot's profile has a **Manager
  review** row (its detail: Off, Daily, Weekly, or which bot reviews) opening the same page; choosing how often there
  makes that bot the manager. The same words on both, Turkish in both catalogues.

What it does not do: it never changes anything by itself, in any mode; it adds no tool to any run, so no run's prompt
or first step changes (the context budget and the freedom contract are untouched). A tell when cards wait is left out:
the tells are English today, and the owner reads Turkish.

How it was checked: `tests/unit/test_team_review.py` (the findings, the proposals kept and the nine shapes refused, when
a review is due, a review over a real store with a stand-in model, Accept of a split and of a retirement, Skip, a
proposal decided twice, a stale one refused, a manager that is not a bot); `tests/smoke/test_team_review_routes.py`
(the routes, in the real runtime, the manager's model played by the scripted provider through `propose_team_changes`,
and each bot's `load`); `dotx eval check`, `tests/unit/test_freedom_contract.py`, `tests/smoke/test_context_budget.py`;
`npm run check`; DotNext built and its String Catalog checked.
