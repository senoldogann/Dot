### 1136. Plain screens: one list first, the rest a click away

**Status: done (2026-11-12).** The owner, after Grok Bot 1.0: "Arayüzde bir çok profesyonel olmayan feature lar,
yapılar mevcut. GrokBot gibi sade fakat arkasında içeride büyük mühendisliğin olduğu bir yapı kurmak istiyorum." Shots of
the owner's own Dot (read only) and of a throwaway one at 1440 and 390 px, and an independent read of them, found the
same five places where a screen opened on its machinery instead of its list. Nothing is removed; each moves behind one
click.

- **Agents page** in four parts under tabs, the last one kept in this browser (`dot.agents.part`): Agents (the cards,
  then the ready-made ones), Teams, Orders, Office. The 3D office, which opened the page with about 25 name tags over its
  room on the owner's Dot, is the Office part, drawn only while it is shown. New agent stands alone beside the tabs; New
  team, Import an agent and Handoffs are under its "…".
- **Schedules**: a row is its name and one line of when and how it went, about 75 px where it was 200 (six chips and two
  lines of goal on each of the owner's 28). Details at the end of the line opens the chips, the goal and the History,
  which it replaces; a failed last run still shows on the line. The list still scrolls inside past its third row (item
  634, the owner's word).
- **A model service's failure in words**: `errors.js` `serviceWords` reads "provider answered HTTP 410: … (ref: …)" as
  the service's own sentence. A failed chat's line in the sidebar and the home's Set up a model card say that sentence;
  the card keeps the recorded line under its one Details, with the other way to a model (`dotx setup`).
- **Proposed plugins**: a card is its name, source, risk, what it does and Install or Update with Skip. The review's why,
  what it needs and "Fits your work" (82 % or 100 % on every card shown) are in its Details.
- **Dot's look** in Settings › General: its face and Change, which opens the picker and Save (40 moving faces were drawn
  on every open of General).
