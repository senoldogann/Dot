### 1954. The pre-push review leaves the Claude plan when it is spent or the diff is a security one, reads only what is new, and gives DeepSeek a budget

**Status: done (2026-11-26), but for what is said under "Not done".** A change to `.claude/hooks/pre-push-review.sh` applies to the
owner only once their own checkout (`~/Desktop/Dot`) has pulled it.

The run review of 2026-11-26 (problems 1, 2 and 3) measured the reviews of one week (19 to 26 November, the owner's store
read-only): **593 review runs, 1 608 M input tokens, 28.6 % of everything Dot spent**, in 274 chats. The 319 runs that
were a later one in a chat that had an earlier one took **702 M tokens (44 %)**; DeepSeek did 61 of them (563 M, 938 s on
average), **21 of which stopped at the hook's time limit with no verdict** (179 M); Opus refused 48 diffs as a cyber risk;
the Claude plan's five-hour window ran out six times and cut 5 reviews, 4 delegate runs, 2 chats and a schedule's run.

What changed:

- **Plan usage (review problem 2).** `dotx review plan` reads the newest five-hour window any step of Dot's own runs
  recorded (`store/stats.py: newest_plan_windows`, a read-only connection, nothing written; `assistant.plan_window` of
  item 1403). When it is used as far as 80 %, or refuses calls, and has not reset (`code_review.plan_is_spent`), the
  review goes to the fallback models that are not on the plan first and the plan's model last (route `plan_spent`).
  Measured on the 532 reviews of that week on the plan: **56 started with the window at 80 % or more; of the 6 that the
  usage limit cut, 4 would have started on the second model.**
- **Security diffs (problem 3a).** A change that touches a path REVIEW.md lists under "Always needs a human" goes to the
  second model straight (route `security`), the plan's model after it, so that the refusal of a diff of the shell guard no
  longer costs a try that gives no verdict before the second model begins. The small model still leads a small change
  that needs no human.
- **Only what is new (problem 1).** The hook reads a push from the newest commit of the branch that was reviewed here
  (passed, or BLOCKed with findings), not from where the branch leaves main: a first push that BLOCKs never reaches the
  remote, so each round was the whole change again. Not while main has moved under the branch since that commit, and a
  review that gave no verdict counts for nothing. The reviewer reads only the files of the diff (their paths are in the
  `.paths` file) and REVIEW.md, and is given the earlier BLOCK's file to check that each Important finding in it is
  fixed or still stands.
- **A budget for the other model (problem 3b).** A model that is not on the plan is told: at most 40 tool calls and
  four fifths of the time left in minutes, and to write its findings and the VERDICT line at three quarters of either.
  This is words in the goal; `dotx run` has no step limit to enforce it with.

The plan prints one more line, `route small|security|plan_spent|default`, which the hook shows. Nothing of the policy or
the gate is touched (REVIEW.md's "Always needs a human" paths are not), and no yolo or free run asks anything new: the
hook is outside the runs.

**Not done.** The report also asked that runs cut by the plan's limit resume at the reset. Schedules' do already (item
1206, `scheduler/recovery.py`), and a chat whose helpers' reports wait is woken at the reset (item 1669). What is left is
the owner's chat runs (2 of the 13) and the delegated helpers (4, about 230 M tokens): resuming a helper that had spent
100 M tokens the moment the window opens would spend the new window at once, which is a cost decision of the owner's
(the memory of how Dot is run says to ask before cutting a campaign), not a default. Nothing was changed there.

Measured after the pull: the share of review tokens in the week, the reviews that start on DeepSeek, and "could not be
reviewed" stops; the numbers above are the before.
