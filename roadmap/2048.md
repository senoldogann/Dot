### 2048. A pause in a dictation loses no word, on the phone and the web

**Status: done (2026-11-28).** The owner, in real use on 2026-11-28: dictating into the composer, they paused a little
and much of what they had said was gone; the text started again from scratch.

Cause, reproduced on this Mac: since iOS 18 (macOS 26 does the same), `SFSpeechRecognizer` with partial results
closes an utterance when the speaker pauses, inside the same task and with no final result. The closing result is not
final but carries `speechRecognitionMetadata`, and the next results hold only the words said after the pause. Dot
Next's `VoiceInput` took each result as the whole text of its segment (`current = words`), so every pause replaced
what came before it, in the recorder's live line and in what ✓ put in the box (the task's final result holds only the
last utterance). A probe fed `say` recordings (English, and Turkish with two pauses) to the on-device recognizer at
real time: every pause gave `final=false meta=true` with the full sentence, then partials from one word again; a
pause right before the end gave a final with no words.

What changed:
- DotEngine `Voice/DictationTranscript.swift`: the utterances closed, in order, and the live one; a result only
  replaces the live one, a result that is final or has metadata closes it, an empty result keeps it, and a task that
  ends (a silence, a new microphone) keeps its live words. `VoiceInput` passes `isFinal || speechRecognitionMetadata
  != nil` and shows and delivers `transcript.text`; what was typed before dictating stays as before
  (`DictationText.merged`).
- The web's dictation (`voice.js` `heardOf`, replacing `transcriptOf`) keeps the settled words of a result list that
  begins again (a list whose settled results are not those seen before), where it rebuilt the text from the latest list
  alone. WebKit, which drives the same recognizer through its delegate, gets each pause's utterance as a finished
  recognition (measured: `didFinishRecognition` with `final=false meta=true` mid-task) and so turns it into a final
  result, so Safari kept its words; the guard is for a browser that starts its list again.
- The Mac's own transcription (`voice/transcribe.swift`) already joins every final result, and the old iPhone app
  (`ios/Dot`) is not changed here.

How it was checked: `DictationTranscriptTests` (DotEngine) with the probe's results (a pause in one task, Turkish with
two pauses, an empty final after a pause, a revision, a task that ends); `tests/js/voice.test.js` (a list that grows,
a list that begins again after a pause, an empty list); `npm run check`; one DotNext `build-for-testing`; the phone UI
on `ios.yml`.
