### 604. The Popular cards of Customize › Connectors are rows on a phone

**Status: done (2026-10-24).** The owner sent a screenshot of Customize › Connectors from the iPhone: "on mobile these
cards are very ugly, they should have fitting sizes". Each Popular card stood up as on a desk: a 48 px mark over its
words, which started at a different place on each card, pushed to the right.

**Why.** Popular stands up (item 545): `display: flex` in a column, with `.plugin-main` a grid of one column. A row's
rules, which every tile of the page wears (`.plugin-tile:not(.plugin-server)`), put the name in grid column 2 and what
it does in columns 2 to the end. Inside the card's main part they opened a second column beside the empty first one, so
the words took their own width at the right. On a desk the four narrow cards hid it: the words filled the width. On a
phone a card has the screen's width, and a short line ("Reads your events and adds new ones.") started halfway across.

**As built.**

- The stand-up look of Popular applies from 721 px: four cards a row, and two under 1100 px, as before. In it, the
  words of a card keep the one column of its main part, at its left, under the mark.
- On a phone, where the section is one column, a Popular card is a row, as the other sections' are: the 40 px mark
  beside its name, what it does and how it connects, and its step (**Add**, **Allow**, **Sign in**) on the line of its
  name.
- `docs/extending.md` (Connectors) says it.

**Tests.** `tests/smoke/test_phone_all_pages.py` (+1), WebKit over a served daemon:
- at 390 px each Popular card's mark is 40 px or less and its words start just right of it, the name and what it does
  at one x;
- at 1280 px the words start at the mark's edge.

It fails against the rules before: a 48 px mark at 390. The existing check that no page of a phone scrolls sideways
passes at 320 to 430 px. Shots at 390 and 1280, dark and light, were read by eye.
