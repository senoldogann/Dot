### 1643. Dot for iPhone: every sheet and screen in the new look, the queue with swipe, undo and a drag

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The recipes of item 1642 applied to every screen of the app, and the three things the review asked of
the queue.

What changed:
- **Queued** (the owner's first complaint): each message is a card on the sheet's own ground, lighter than it, never darker; a swipe to
  the left removes it and a line says "Removed from the queue" with an Undo for five seconds (the Mac is told when they have passed,
  or when the sheet goes: `QueueSheet`); a swipe to the right, or a press and hold, sends it to the run at work (Send now); a press
  and hold also has Move up, Move down and Remove; "Reorder" shows the system's handles to drag the order (`queueOrder(_:moving:to:)`
  in `Core/QueueOrder.swift`, `ChatModel.moveQueued(from:to:)`); the footer is in the quiet tier. The three icon buttons a row
  had (two chevrons and a red trash) and the blue "Send now" are gone.
- **Run**: the status card is flat (the glyph and the words, no tinted tile); "Model for this chat" and "Default models…" have the same
  plain glyph; a helper's "Running" is neutral words with the small moving arc, "Done" a muted check, "Failed" a muted red glyph; the
  plan's progress and marks are the ink; the schedule rows have no tile; a chat with no helpers and no schedules says so in one quiet
  line instead of two empty cards.
- **Needs you** and the approval and question cards in a chat: a card with an amber hairline (no tint), the glyph amber with no disc,
  Allow once the primary and Deny the secondary, the answer field a raised well, "Recommended" in sentence case; an answered card folds
  into a quiet muted line (it was green and red).
- **Step**, **Select text**, **Settings** (no tiles: plain glyphs; switches in the neutral tint; "Connected" a muted check;
  "Forget this phone's key" has the muted red on its words only and asks once: "You will need a new code from your Mac to pair this phone
  again"), **New chat** and the model pickers (ticks in the ink, the model chip is `DotChip`), **Models**, **Schedules**, **Licences**.
- **First run**: the pairing and address screens are flat (no mesh, no glow, no shadow, a plain field, step numbers in grey discs); the
  footer links are 44 points high; step 2 says "In Settings › Phone, press New code, then type it here."
- **Chat list**: no blue glow, the "Working" header and the helpers line in the secondary colour, the unread dot the ink, a waiting
  chat's row has no tint (its amber ring and the amber "Needs you" header carry it), the swipe is a graphite; **capsule over the
  composer** (item 1390): working is the ink, waiting is the one amber fill; at the accessibility sizes the segments stand over each
  other with their whole words and the time without seconds ("2m") instead of "You" and "2m…" (the review's fix 4; the dock is
  capped at AX2 like the composer); **hand-off** and **away** cards, **error banners** (`NoticeCard`: a muted red glyph and the words
  in the ink on a plain card), empty states (a quiet glyph, a title, a line: no mesh, no orb), the dictation line and the microphone.
- Not done: "Always allow…" on the approval card (the review's fix 3). The daemon takes `remember` on a decision (item 866), but the
  phone's outbox record of a decision has no such field, so a decision made offline could not carry it without a change of that
  persisted shape; the label "Allow once" says what the one button does.
