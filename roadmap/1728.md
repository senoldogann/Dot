### 1728. A memory benchmark: does a search find the newest fact when a fact changed (`dotx eval memory`)

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The market research of 2026-11-24 (M4, topic 2; M2 idea 11) found that an
embedding-only store collapses on facts that changed (MERIT: 0.30-0.95, and an agent acts on a retrieved value 55% of
the time), and that Dot had no measure of its own recall: `dotx eval memory` was planned (`MEM-P02`) and not built.

What changed (`evals/memory_cases.py`, `evals/memory_bench.py`, `cli.py`, `cli_commands.py`, `cli_eval.py`):

- 42 cases of Dot's size, one fresh store each: owner facts a run saves over time (14 with a subject, 16 without, 4
  three-step chains, 4 whose old note was handed to a run before the new one was saved, which lifts it in a search) and
  eight facts that are both true, four of them trap-shaped (the same sentence with another name or number). Each case
  asks a question and says which fact is right.
- `dotx eval memory [--embeddings scripted|configured]`: the notes are saved as `memory_save` saves them and searched as
  `memory_search` searches, the three best. Counted per group: the newest fact came first, was among the three, and an
  outdated one was among the three too. No model writes or judges; a scratch folder holds the stores.
- Scripted embeddings are made from the cosines `bge-m3` measured on these exact texts (3 places), so the offline test
  runs the search the way the real one ranks; `tests/smoke/test_eval_memory_live.py` (`live`) measures them again and
  fails when the model drifts by more than 0.02.

Measured before any change to recall, both with scripted embeddings and with the real `bge-m3` (the same numbers):

| group | cases | newest first | newest in top 3 | outdated also shown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| facts that changed | 34 | 2 | 34 | 34 |
| saved with a subject | 18 | 1 | 18 | 18 |
| saved without one | 16 | 1 | 16 | 16 |
| old note handed to a run | 4 | 0 | 4 | 4 |
| facts that did not change | 8 | 8 of 8 first | 8 | - |

Reading the numbers: in a store of two or three notes the two versions of a fact tie exactly in the fusion (each is
first in one ranking and second in the other), and the older wins the tie, so "newest first" 2 of 34 is a floor, not a
rate for a large store. "Outdated also shown" is not affected: nothing in recall tells a note that a newer one replaced
it, so both reach the run, 34 of 34. The cosines settle why a rule on the similarity alone is not enough: the facts
that changed lie at 0.68-0.97 (U05, U06 and U12 are reworded and lie at 0.68-0.82), the facts that are both true at
0.68-0.84.

Not done: what a run does with two notes (a benchmark of answers needs a model); the store is two or three notes, not
thousands.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_eval_memory.py` (the scripted vectors have the cases' cosines, the cases are well formed,
impossible cosines are refused, today's numbers above), `tests/smoke/test_eval_memory_live.py` (`live`: the cosines are
what `bge-m3` measures), `uv run dotx eval memory`, `uv run dotx eval memory --embeddings configured`.
