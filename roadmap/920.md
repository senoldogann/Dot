**Built.** `repair/problems.py`:

  problem of each defect, counted, with its newest five lines of evidence. A fixed or declined problem seen again opens
  again.
- **What it hands out.** The most seen open problem, seen at least `least_seen` times and tried fewer than twice.
- **Where it is kept.** The book is in kv `repair.book`, 200 problems at most.
