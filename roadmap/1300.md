### 1300. The count of a group's faces shows its "+"

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The head of a group of helpers in a chat shows three faces and the rest counted ("+4",
"7 helpers"), and the owner saw the count as "--4" on the iPhone. The faces breathe and pulse by `transform`, which lifts
an element above every neighbour that is not placed, whatever their order in the page; the count came after the faces and
lay under the last one, which hid its "+". The chat's row in the sidebar draws its faces and count the same way and had
the same defect.

What changed: the faces and the count of both are placed (`position: relative`), so they stack in the order they stand in,
the count last. The count is a pill that grows with its digits ("+12" no longer spills out of a circle), starts clear of
the face it leans on, and keeps its digits level (`tabular-nums`).

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_helper_group.py` opens the run's page with motion on (the test's other pages ask for none, so
the faces stood still and the defect did not show), proves the faces breathe, and asks which element lies at the count's
first quarter and its middle; it failed before the change and passes after. A bare page with the same shapes showed the
defect in Chromium and WebKit and the fix in both. Not checked: the iPhone itself.
