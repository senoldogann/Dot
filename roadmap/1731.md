### 1731. What a task will cost is shown, and caps the owner sets pause a task and ask the owner, in every mode

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** **Asked** by the owner (2026-11-24, through the improvement lead): the market research of
that day (`reports/market-2026-11-24.md`; its community study, theme "cost", the second complaint: "My first week on grok
bot cost me around 120 dollars", "$500 dollars in one day", and implication 1: "Cost must be visible and capped") says
users must see and cap what a run costs. The owner asked for budget control: a cap they set asks them even in yolo and free
mode. That is his decision, recorded here and in [Freedom](../docs/freedom.md).

Measured on the owner's Dot (read-only, 2026-11-10 to 11-24, 528 runs):
- The tokens of the 14 days by source (input + output, as `runs` holds them): helpers (`delegate`) 2,521M in 73 runs,
  the owner's own tasks and chats (`ui`) 851M in 108, pre-push reviews (`review`) 760M in 235, schedules 355M in 79,
  teams 76M; the own initiative ran no run in the period.
- By model: `claude_code:sonnet` 3,010M, `opencode_go:deepseek-v4.1-flash` 1,042M, `claude_code:opus` 520M; an API key
  billed by the token (`anthropic:`) 4.5M in one run. Everything else is a plan: the Claude plan (77 %) and OpenCode Go's
  subscription. 97.6 % of the input tokens were read from the cache. The plan's 5-hour window reached 99 % used.
- By day: 5M to 55M a day until 11-20, then 234M, 668M, 1,058M and 2,423M on 11-21 to 11-24 (the improvement work, with
  up to 10 helpers at once).
- Per run, counting what it delegated (its "family"): median 2.3M, 90th percentile 26M, 99th 270M, the most 438M. The
  costliest runs are helpers and the owner's long tasks on `claude_code:sonnet` in yolo: 200 to 370 steps over 1.5 to 3.5
  hours, each step re-reading a context of up to 0.97M tokens (mostly cached). Of the 528 runs, 36 passed 50M, 7 passed
  200M, none 500M.
- Per source and day, the most: helpers 1,599M, reviews 397M, `ui` 303M, schedules 86M.

What was there: `[agent] run_max_minutes` and `run_max_tokens` end a task at its limit (told to finish; the owner set both
to 0 on 2026-10-28, as a limit that ends long work is no use to them); `[initiative] retry_under_tokens` lets a costly
thing of Dot's own go (item 913); the Cost card shows what a task used so far, and Settings › Models › Usage the days
and the plan's window (item 1403). Nothing estimated a task before it ran, nothing capped a day or a source, and nothing
asked: a limit ended the work.

What must hold:
- A cap pauses the task and asks the owner, **Continue** (with as many tokens again) or **Stop**, in every approval mode,
  yolo and free mode included; an answer nobody gives before `approval_timeout_s` stops it. Nothing else of yolo or free
  mode changes: below the caps nothing asks and nothing waits but one read of the store per step.
- The caps are generous enough that none of the measured 14 days would have hit them; 0 turns each off.
- No money is shown: Dot has no price per model, and the owner's spend was all on plans (a share of a window, not a bill).
  A plan's window stays what 1403 shows. The unit of every cap and estimate is the tokens the Usage page counts.

The design:
- `[budget]` (`config.py` `BudgetConfig`, `defaults.toml`): `run_tokens` 500M (a run and the runs it delegated to;
  the most measured was 438M), `day_tokens` 4,000M (all runs that began today; the busiest day was 2,423M), and per source
  a day: `schedule_day_tokens` 500M (the most 86M), `initiative_day_tokens` 500M (the own initiative's 3 busiest days of
  item 910 were 213M together), `review_day_tokens` 1,000M (the most 397M), `ui_day_tokens` and `delegate_day_tokens` 0
  (the owner's own work, bounded by the run and day caps).
- `agent/budget.py`: the pure part (the first cap a run is past, the words of the card, the estimate) and `Budget`, the
  caps as they stand (Settings changes them live) with what the owner allowed past them (in memory, as free mode's switch
  is: a restart forgets an allowance, and the next step asks again). `keep_to_budget` reads the run's family, today's and
  its source's today's tokens in one query and, past a cap, asks through the approval broker (tool `budget`, severity
  `budget`), one card per cap at a time: the runs that reach the same day cap while it is asked wait for that answer.
  Continue raises that cap by itself once more (for the run, or for today); Stop, or no answer, sets the run's stop and
  refuses the step's calls, so the run ends cancelled as the Stop button ends it.
- Where: `execute_calls`, the one path of every tool call of Dot's loop, of a run on the Claude plan and of OpenCode's
  agent (bridged calls), before the calls run; a step whose calls are only `finish` and the plan tools is not held.
- The estimate (`RunBudget` in `RunCosts`, `GET /runs/{id}/stats`, the run page's Cost card and any phone reading that
  route): the median and the 90th percentile of the family tokens of the last 50 ended runs of the same model, mode and
  source in 30 days (none under 3), with what the run has used, its cap, and today's total against the day cap.

Settings and `PUT /api/budget`: a phone may lower or set a cap, not raise or take one off (item 678).

Checked: `tests/unit/test_budget.py` (which cap comes first, a cap of 0, what was let past, the card's words, the
estimate, a phone that loosens; on a real store and broker in free mode: under the caps no question, past one the run is
blocked on a `budget` card, Continue lets as many again go and asks again past that, Stop sets the run's stop; two runs
past one day's cap get one card and a Stop holds for both; the run against its caps with what like runs spent),
`tests/smoke/test_usage_view.py` (the costliest tasks in `GET /api/usage`; `GET`/`PUT /api/budget` and the config file;
Settings › Models › Caps at 1280 and 390 px; the Cost card's line; a scripted yolo task past the day's cap waits on the
card in Chromium and Stop ends it cancelled: this one fails with the check left out of `execute_calls`),
`tests/unit/test_freedom_contract.py` (the new known ask, named), `tests/js/usagewords.test.js`, `npm run check`,
`dotx eval check`.

Not done: a price table and money (above); a cap on the plan's window share (the window already refuses a call at 100 %,
and the tokens bound the rest); a helper's tokens count against its parent's run cap at the parent's next step, not while
the helper works (the helper's own run cap and the day caps hold it meanwhile); OpenCode's agent's own tools are held
at its next call of one of Dot's tools; a deleted chat's runs no longer count for the day.
