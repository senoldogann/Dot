### 1233. An agent is one conversation

**Status: done (2026-11-19).** The owner's word of 2026-11-19, after Grok Bot (whose bots are each one ongoing chat,
its app's sidebar a column of their faces): "a bot has one purpose, and its chat is used all the time", where Dot
started a new chat with each message from an agent's page. The office's panel already went on in the agent's newest
chat (item 898); the page did not, and the sidebar's face opened the page rather than the conversation.

The daemon names an agent's home chat (`Store.agent_home_chats`, one query for every agent, `home` in `GET /api/agents`
and `GET /api/agents/{id}`): the chat of the latest activity whose latest run is the agent's and that the pages or
`dotx run` started (its welcome among them), not one a schedule, a watcher, Dot's initiative, a lead's work order or
the agents' work started (their runs go on in chats of their own, item 952), a team's or a campaign's, however many
chats are newer: the pre-push review found that picking it out of the sidebar's newest 200 chats lost it once a
schedule that makes a chat per firing filled them. The sidebar's faces link to its latest run and are marked while it
is open; the agent page's box goes on in it (`follow`, as the office's), and opens the chat it reached; **New chat** in
the page's "…" starts the next message afresh, and hides **Open your chat with …**, which leads back otherwise. Chats
deleted in the sidebar tell the faces and the page (`AGENTS_CHANGED_EVENT`), so the box never goes on in a chat that is
gone. The office's panel (item 898) goes on in the same chat: it took the chat of the agent's newest run, which a
schedule's or a lead's work order made another than the page's (the second pre-push review).

The first part of the owner's plan for a plainer shell after Grok Bot (the same day): the faces as a column of their
own, the side panel shut until asked for, a plainer composer and a one-line status come next, each its own item.
