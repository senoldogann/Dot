### 537. Code in a chat is a box with a head, markdown is read as it was written, and the slash menu is drawn as a menu

**Status: done (2026-10-22).** The owner asked for three things with two pictures: the code of a chat shown as the first
picture shows it (a dark rounded box, the language at the left of its head, a switch that wraps its lines and a copy at
the right, the code copyable and well formed under it); the other faults and inconsistencies of the chat, the ones not
yet seen, found and put right; and the slash menu drawn as the second picture draws Qoder's, with line icons (a puzzle
piece for a skill, a terminal in a box for a command), sections parted by hairlines, each row a name and its muted words,
a selected row lit, and the keys under the list.

**As built.**
- `js/codeblock.js` (new) is the one box of code. In an answer, in your message and in a step's row it is the same
  `div.code-block`: a head with the label at its left (the language the fence named, or the title it gave, `text` when it
  named none; a step's boxes say Command, Arguments, Result and Error) and at its right a switch that wraps the lines of
  this block (`aria-pressed`; Settings › Appearance's "long lines of code wrapped" says how every block begins and a
  block's own switch overrules it; a step's boxes begin wrapped) and the copy of the code as it was written. The result
  of a step is cut at 6,000 characters in the page and its copy is all of it; Show all puts all of it in the box. A table
  has a head of the same make, with its name and its copy as markdown, and fills its box (a narrower table no longer
  leaves the box's right side empty); it scrolls in a holder of its own under the head. Code shows its letters as they
  are (`font-variant-ligatures: none`: no `->` or `!=` turns into one glyph).
- `js/markdown.js` reads what models write, not only the plain forms. A fence's info string is tolerated
  (` ```python title="x.py" `, ` ```js {1,3} `, ` ```python:x.py `): before, one more word after the language made
  the fence no fence, so the next block's fence closed it and the blocks after it swapped places. A fence is dedented by its
  own indent and a longer fence closes a shorter one only if as long. Lists nest as far as their items are indented
  (up to six deep), the blocks under an item (code, a quote, more words) belong to it, a numbered list keeps the number it
  begins at and goes on past a blank line, a bullet after a numbered item at its own margin starts another list, task
  items (`- [ ]`, `- [x]`) carry a box. Tables take single-dash rule cells and the alignment the rule line says. Headings
  are read to the sixth level without their closing hashes; quotes hold blocks (lists, code); strikethrough (`~~`),
  `__bold__`, `_italic_`, an address in angle brackets, `<br>`, the entities a writer spells out (`&lt;`) and a
  backslash before a mark are read; a picture written as a link is its words as a link. The composer's mirror reads the
  same fences and hands every letter back.
- The copy of a selection (`js/clipboard.js`) keeps what the page now draws: the fence with its language, a task's mark,
  strikethrough, a table's alignment, a nested list with its indentation and its numbers; the head of a box is left out
  as the line under an answer is.
- The room between blocks (`app.css`): the rule that resets the browser's own margins on a list and on a quote was as
  heavy as the one that gives the blocks of a prose their room, and took it away, so a list or a quote touched the block
  before it. The reset is `:where` now, the room stands, and a paragraph, a list, a quote, a block of code and a table are
  `0.65em` apart. A task's box is centred on its line's text in Chrome and in Safari (they sit it differently on the
  baseline; measured at 13, 15 and 18 px), and the live caret of a table being written is in its last cell as before.
- `js/tools.js`: what a step shows (command, arguments, result, error) is the same box, wrapped from the start; a step's
  result no longer has a copy button floating over its edge.
- The slash menu (`js/slash.js`, `js/composer.js`) is drawn in sections with a hairline between them and no title on the
  page (each is a `role=group` named Commands, Skills, MCP tools for a screen reader), each row a line icon, the name and
  under it its words cut by an ellipsis, the active row lit, and under the list what the keys do (Select · Enter/Tab to
  add · Esc to close; a touch screen has none, so none is drawn there). A command wears the terminal in a box (`/loop`
  its arrows), a skill the puzzle piece, a server its service's mark as before (item 530); the MCP tools of every server
  are one section, each tool named by its server. A skill is the puzzle piece as a chip and as the row of a call to the
  `skill` tool, an MCP tool's row the plug. The Customize cards keep the marks of item 477.
- `js/icons.js`: the line icons `wrapText`, `squareTerminal` and `arrowUpDown`, and the current Lucide puzzle piece.
- `js/approvals.js`: what an approval card shows of the action (the command, the path, the address) is the same box, in
  its head Command for a shell line, Target for any other; it wraps, scrolls past a dozen lines and has a copy, so that
  the owner can take what is asked. `tests/smoke/test_approval_target.py` still holds it (the box scrolls, the card
  stays in the window, wide and on a phone, light and dark).
- `js/why.js`: the aggressive preset's reason is its own name, so what a step said of why it ran under the default
  preset was "Allowed by the aggressive preset: aggressive preset."; it says "Allowed by the aggressive preset." now.

Checked by `tests/js/markdown.test.js` (each reading above, with the invariants that the mirror hands every letter back
and that a text of nested marks stays cheap), `tests/js/slash.test.js`, and with a real Chrome (wide) and a WebKit as an
iPhone, light and dark, `tests/smoke/test_chat_code_blocks.py` (the head's label, wrap, copy and size; the cascade of
fences; a nested list with a block of code under an item; numbering; tasks; the table's alignment; strikethrough, a
break, an escape; the step's boxes and the whole of a cut result; the menu's sections, icons, hairlines and the keys
under it, shown on a screen with a pointer and left off a touch screen), `tests/smoke/test_markdown_copy.py`,
`tests/smoke/test_composer.py` and `tests/smoke/test_composer_picks.py`. They fail on the page without the change.

Not built: syntax colouring (the head names the language, the code stays in one colour); an image written in markdown is
still not drawn (it stays its words as a link, which is safe: no address is loaded because a model wrote it).
