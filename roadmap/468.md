### 468. Every step keeps what decided it, and its row says why it ran

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The sixth of the nine ideas taken from OpenBot (item 449 has the plan), its first part:
"record the policy decision on every step that reached the gate". Until now a step the policy let through kept no word
of why: its `tool_result` had the tool, the summary, the kind and the target, a `policy` event existed only for asks,
hand-offs and refusals, and `approval_auto` only when yolo or the review model answered. It is also what lets item 469
try a rule on past steps. **As built.** `agent/authorization.py` (not under `policy/`): `StepDecision` (the verdict, the
source, the reason cut to 300 characters, the rule's id, who answered and the run's preset) and `Authorization` (the
owner's note and the decision). `RunGate.authorize_recorded` holds what `authorize` did, which now returns its note, so
the gate's protocol, the tools that ask in the middle of a call and the tests' stand-ins are untouched; who answered is
known from the path the question took: nobody (`policy`), the owner (`user`, also when the review model passed it on),
the review model (`reviewer`), or yolo, which asks nothing. The `policy` event of an ask, a hand-off or a refusal now
names its rule. The executor writes the decision and the action's flags into every `tool_result` (none when the call
never reached the gate or the gate raised); the steps a browser batch takes inside one call are the batch's one record,
said in the docs. `GET /api/runs/<id>/actions` gives each acting step its `flags` and `decision`, none for a step
recorded before, never a guess. The chat's tool row says **Why it ran** when opened (`why.js`, pure, tested in
`tests/js/why.test.js`): for a step that acted, or that more than the preset decided, the rule of the owner's named with
the way to the rules, the preset, or who answered and why the policy asked. Yolo is untouched: the record changes
nothing of what a run does or asks; a yolo step says "YOLO, not asked" with the reason. Tests:
`tests/smoke/test_step_decisions.py` (a real store and bus: the preset and a rule of the owner's when nobody was asked;
the owner, the review model, a question the review model passed on, yolo and supervised; `authorize` keeps its note),
`tests/smoke/test_run_actions.py` (a scripted run: the rule that let a file be written named in the API and in its
`tool_result`, the edit the preset let through), `tests/unit/test_action_steps.py` (a step recorded before has no
decision), `tests/js/why.test.js`, `tests/smoke/test_why_it_ran.py` (the opened row of a real scripted run at 1280 px
light and at 390 px dark as a touch screen: the rule named, the way to the rules). Docs: `docs/safety.md`,
`docs/autonomy.md`, `docs/daemon.md`.
