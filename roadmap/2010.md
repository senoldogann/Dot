### 2010. A chat's row says when its run waits on its helpers, and what works for it while it rests, as DotNext's rows do

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The web side of DotNext's item 1920 (gap table: item 1998). The sidebar's rows said
Working, Waiting (for the owner), the helpers' count, unread and pinned, but not that a run waits on its helpers, nor
that something still works for a chat at rest.

What changed (the daemon's `GET /api/threads/view` already says both, item 1920):
- The sidebar reads `/threads/view` beside `/threads` (which a phone keeps a copy of for when it has no link, so it
  stays the list's source), and keeps by chat its `helpers` (how many helpers its run waits on, at work) and
  `background` (a helper let go, a command in the background, its loop).
- A row whose run waits on its helpers says **Waiting on 2 helpers…** (**2 yardımcıyı bekliyor…**) in its last line, in
  place of its last words and of the helpers' count, shimmering as the work line does; its right still says Working and for how long. A chat at
  rest with something working for it says **Waiting · <the first of them>** (**Bekliyor · …**), quiet. What waits for the
  owner keeps its own line ("Waiting for you: …"), first. A screen reader hears the same words in the row's name.
- What the rows wait on is read again with the list, and on its own when a helper is let go (`helper_released`, now on
  the lifecycle stream the pages hear) or a chat's background commands change (`terminals_changed`).
- With no link `/threads/view` cannot be read: the rows say what the list alone tells, as before.

How it was checked: `tests/smoke/test_row_waits.py` in Chrome, a run that waits on two helpers in shell commands: the row's
last line at 390 px in Turkish and 1440 px in English, with Working at its right and no count beside it; one helper
stopped, the open page's row says "Waiting on 1 helper…" without a reload. `tests/js/rowwaits.test.js`: both states, the
cut, nothing for a chat at rest or a daemon from before.
