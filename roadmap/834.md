### 834. A new chat is the "+" at the right of the sidebar's head, as a messaging app's

**Status: done (2026-10-30).** **Asked** by the owner ("uygulamadaki tüm ekranları ve tasarımları incele, olabildiğince
minimal ama eksik olmayan, yumuşak, Grok Bot gibi yap"), with screenshots of that app. The first part of the third of
the six stages of the design review of 2026-10-30, the plain sidebar: the other app's sidebar has its "+" at the right
of the head, where Dot's had a row of its own under the search, "New chat ⇧⌘O", with a pencil.

- `sidebar.js`: the new chat is one link, the "+" (`icon-btn side-compose`, "New chat" for a screen reader, "New chat
  (⇧⌘O)" its tooltip), that stands at the right of the head on a desk and in the foot on a phone, moved as the width
  changes as the search is; the row under the search is gone, and the note of an empty list says to start with the "+"
  (the pre-push review). ⇧⌘O is as it was.
- `shell.css`: the "+" at the head's right; the row's styles went with it. On a phone the foot's round button is the
  same "+".

Kept as they were: the head's button that folds the sidebar away, at the left where the top bar's opener stands (item
113), and the desktop shell's drag region around it; a phone's foot under the thumb (items 294 and 295).

**Checked.** `tests/smoke/test_desktop_lights.py`: in the desktop shell the "+" stands on the line of the lights, right
of the button that folds the sidebar; in a browser it ends where the search under it ends, says its shortcut, is the
sidebar's only "New chat" and opens a new chat with the cursor in the box. `tests/smoke/test_phone_layout.py`: on a
phone it is the foot's and the head has none. `tests/smoke/test_new_chat_choices.py` reaches a new chat by it.
