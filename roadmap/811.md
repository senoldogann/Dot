### 811. A fresh Dot lists its first steps on New chat

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner after the fresh start ("bir kurulum sihirbazı yok mu?"): the
model and its keys are set up by `dotx setup`, and Dot's first conversation (item 420) names Dot and connects Google,
but nothing showed the rest of what a new Dot wants: the vault, iMessage, the phone, the connected apps.

- `GET /api/assistant/first-steps` says, for each of six steps, whether it is done: the owner gave their name, the
  vault holds an item, iMessage has a number or address paired, a phone is paired, the Connectors page has a server, a
  chat exists; and whether Dot is `fresh`, its first run not `FRESH_DAYS` (14) old or none (`Store.first_run_at`).
- New chat (`firststeps.js`, `firststepslist.js`) puts a card under the block in the middle, in a row of its own so the
  greeting and the composer keep their place and While you were away and the Today strip take the rows under it:
  each step a link to its page (none for the first chat, which is the composer, and none once done) with where it is
  done, a check once it is, and *N of 6 done*. It is put on the page only while Dot is fresh, a step is left and it was
  not hidden; **Hide** removes it for good in that browser (`dot.firstSteps`). On a touch screen a step's link is 44
  pixels high, a finger's tap (the phone's type test).
- `test_home_first_screen.py`'s new chat with nothing under the composer hides the card as the owner would; the card has
  its own tests.
