### 2015. The web composer works as DotNext's on every width: one capsule, the model as its mark, its settings in one sheet

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner (2026-11-27): desktop and mobile must be truly identical; the desktop composer did not
work as the phone's. The web drew the words on top and a bar of pills under them at every width (item 1330): the mode, the
model with its name, the reasoning as bars, the approvals under the box, each with a menu of its own; DotNext's composer is
one capsule whose shape follows what is written (items 1924 and 1991) and whose one choice, the model's chip, opens the
chat's settings.

What changed (the web UI only):
- `composershape.js` (pure): one row while the words are short (at most 32 characters, no line break, no file or pick),
  the words over the controls otherwise, the phone's `ComposerLayout.of`; `data-shape` on the composer, read by run.css.
  One row: the "+", the words, the model's provider's mark and its chevron, the chat's size (the web's own ring), the
  microphone and the send on one centre line. Open: the words on top, and under them the "+", the mark, the model's name
  with the level and Fast in grey, the chevron, the microphone and the send.
- The model's pill opens the chat's settings on every width, the phone's sheet (item 1202): from the foot of a phone's
  screen, over the middle of a wider window (560 px wide). It holds the models (favourites, a section per provider,
  search, Change for the default), Reasoning, Mode and Approvals as rows that open in place, and How Dot works. A pick
  keeps it open, as the phone's does; Done or Escape closes it. The mode, reasoning and approvals pills leave the bar at
  every width; YOLO's lock stays, as the run asks nothing in it. The desktop's own model menu in the composer (a rail of
  providers, ⌘1-⌘9 for favourites, the arrows to move) is gone with it; Settings › Models keeps that menu.
- The empty box says what the phone's says: "Message Dot" (by the name the owner calls Dot), "Ask Scout" in an agent's
  chat, "Queue a message" or "Tell the running task" while a task works (Turkish "Dot'a yaz", "Scout adlı bota sor",
  "Mesajı sıraya al", "Çalışan göreve yaz").

Not in this item: the "+" menu's items, the send's long press (Steer now / Queue), the quote as a chip, the recorder's
look; they are the next composer items.

How it was checked: `tests/js/composershape.test.js`; the browser tests of the composer, rewritten for the settings sheet
where they opened the pills' menus (`test_composer.py`: the shapes, one centre line at 1280 and 800 px and on a phone,
the settings' rows; `test_model_picker.py`, `test_fast_mode.py`, `test_one_message.py`, `test_composer_words.py`,
`test_chat_choices.py`, `test_dock_choices.py`, `test_new_chat_model.py`, `test_new_chat_choices.py`,
`test_agent_page_choices.py`, `test_composer_picks.py`, `test_phone_pages.py`, through `tests/smoke/chat_settings.py`);
clicked through on a throwaway daemon at 1440×900 and 390×844.
