### 985. A page script is ruled on and reviewed whole

**Status: done (2026-11-05).** **Found** by the security audit of 2026-11-05 (the owner's ask; run-1 lead
`dotagent/tools/browser.py:_eval_tool:classify:script-clipped-out-of-policy-haystack`): `browser_eval` runs a script of
up to 100,000 characters in the page, and its action held the first 159 characters of it in the summary and the page's
address as its target. The owner's rules are searched in summary and target, and the review model of `auto` reads
those two, so an owner rule against what a script does (`submit\(`, `fetch`) did not fit a script that did it past
character 159, and the review approved a script it had not read; item 680's escalation of an action too long to read
measured the target alone. `shell` and `apple_script` put the whole command in the target.

**Built.** `policy/types.py` `ActionInfo.body`: the whole of what a step runs when its summary shows its start alone;
`browser_eval` sets it to the script. `policy/engine.py` `haystack_of` searches it too; `agent/review.py` shows it to the
review model (`runs:`) and hands one past 12,000 characters to the owner; `agent/gate.py` records it with the step and
masks the secrets Dot holds in it, as in the summary and target; the approval card (`approvals.js`) shows it whole
under Script, as the owner answers for what the step runs (the pre-push review: a script handed to the owner showed its
start alone). A past step's record keeps the summary and target, not the body, so a rule tried on past steps
(`server/rule_replay.py`, items 469 and 473) fits a page script by its start alone; `docs/safety.md` says so. Docs:
`docs/safety.md`, `docs/autonomy.md`.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_review_target.py` (a script whose `submit()` stands past 200 characters of comment is
reviewed with it in sight and ruled on by an owner's hand-off rule against it; one past 12,000 characters goes to the
owner), `tests/smoke/test_approval_target.py` (the card shows the script whole, wide and on a phone),
