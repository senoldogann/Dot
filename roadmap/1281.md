### 1281. A chat with helpers reads cleanly on a phone: two-line rows, one status

**Status: done (2026-11-20).** The owner sent a phone's screenshot of an agent's chat at work (390 px, Paper look) and
asked that every structure and use in a chat look appropriate and clean. The Design Critic ranked what was wrong on it,
and these three are the ones that were the markup's, each shown by the screenshot:

- **Rows that said nothing.** A helper's row on a phone was one line (item 401): its name, then its goal, then its last
  line, which took up to half the row. Two helpers of the same agent read "Research Desk Gör…" and "Research Desk Göre…",
  and the last line "(a) Tarihi doğrulanmış …". The row is two lines now, as on a wide window: the name and what it is
  for, then its last line, each with its own ellipsis.
- **Two icons beside every closed row.** The way to the helper's page and the chevron stood side by side on a touch
  screen, which has no hover to hide the first (item 884). On a touch screen it shows on a row that is open.
- **The same status twice.** "1 working · 1 done" and the time in the head of the group, and the same words and a clock
  in the status line over the composer, one under the other. While a helper works the head says only how many they
  are (`data-live` on the group, `app.css`); once none works it says how they stand and how long it took. Known, left: the
  line over the composer counts the helpers of the whole chat, not of one group, and stays while the run itself works,
  so after the helpers end and before the run does both still say it; a chat with several groups reads the live group's
  "working" from the line over the composer, whose count is the total.

Left, with the reason: the stop button's colour and the English words in a Turkish chat (`helpers`, `Plan`, `Next
task`), which are the theme's and the translation's (the Turkish of Home and Agents is a task of its own); the
overlapping faces of the head; the "Message from …" line.

Checked: `tests/smoke/test_helper_group.py` now also asserts what the eye sees (the head's state and time hidden and
`data-live` set while a helper works, shown and unset once none does; on a touch screen the link to a helper's page hidden
on a closed row and shown on an open one), `test_closed_helper_rows.py`, `test_helper_fold.py`, `test_activity.py`, the
phone files; and the screenshot of the group at 390 px, dark (rows of two lines, a head of "4 helpers" only, the status
line under it).
