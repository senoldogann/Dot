### 2005. A step writes only the message it shortened, not the whole history

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** Between two steps the daemon's own time (less the model calls the loop makes there) was
p99 5.8 s over seven days on the owner's store, up to 26.7 s, and every gap over 4 s was in a run whose context was past
90,000 tokens. In those the time was spent between the `loop_state` the step's results end with and the one after
`_fit_context` (median 5.3 s, up to 24 s). Past 60 % of a run's budget `_fit_context` shortens the tool result that has
just become the seventh newest, and any change made it write the chat's whole history anew: every message copied to the
archive (the part before the run), deleted, inserted again and indexed again for the chat search, under the store's one
write lock, so every other run's writes waited too.

What changed: `Store.rewrite_messages` writes only the messages that changed when the history kept its length and each
change stands at or after the place the newest run of the history's version started; a rewind keeps the messages before
the place it goes back to as the version stores them, so those are left as they are. A change before that place, or a
history of another length (a compaction), writes the history anew as before (`replace_messages`), which keeps the old
one aside for the rewind. The changed rows are deleted and inserted, so the chat search's index follows them.

Before and after, the owner's chat of 481 messages (427 kB) in a throwaway store, 16 steps of a new 20 kB result each,
the steps whose shortening changed a message (11): the whole history anew 0.29 to 0.58 s a step (median of two passes,
the longest 2.1 s), only the changed message 0.004 to 0.008 s (the longest 0.09 s), on a Mac with a load average over 400.

How it was checked: `tests/unit/test_store_messages.py` (a change after the newest run's start is written in place and
the search finds its new words, a change before it writes the history anew and a rewind still finds the messages as
they were; an unchanged history writes nothing), `tests/smoke/test_rewind.py`'s store tests, the freedom contract,
`dotx eval check`, ruff and mypy.
