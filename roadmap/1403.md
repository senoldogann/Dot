### 1403. What the tasks used is shown: so far on a task at work, and by day in Settings

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** Found by the autonomy audit (finding 8 and the cost gap). The owner set `run_max_minutes = 0`
and `run_max_tokens = 0` (2026-10-28), so no task has a ceiling and no "left" exists to show; `limit_reached` only spoke after a
limit, the cost view was per task (`GET /runs/{id}/stats`, `js/cost.js`, time and tokens, nothing on the task's top) and the
window of the Claude plan that each step records (`assistant.plan_window`) was shown nowhere. A budget that is not capped is
read as what is spent, so that is what this item shows.

What changed.
- A task at work says what it has used so far at the head of its Cost card (**Used so far**: "2h 10m · 1.2M in · 30k out ·
  41 steps") and, on the Claude plan, the window its newest step saw ("5-hour window: 62% used · resets in 1h 19m"); the card
  was already read every ten seconds while the task works, so it is live. `RunCosts` carries `plan_window` for it (the newest
  `assistant.plan_window` of the run). A task that ended shows the card as before.
- `GET /api/usage?days=7` (`days` 1 to 92): a row for each local day from the oldest to today (zeros for a day with no task):
  the tasks that began on it, their steps, the tokens in and out and their minutes of wall time (to now for one at work), read from
  the runs and the ledger of deleted chats on a read-only connection (`store.stats.collect_usage`); and `windows`, the newest
  window of each kind (`five_hour`, `seven_day`, ...) that a step of the period recorded, the step of the latest moment of
  each kind however many came after it: its status, the share used, when it resets and when it was seen. "Per plan" is per kind of window: the events do not say which account the plan was.
- Settings › Models has a **Usage** section before the long list of every model: the windows (a bar and "62% used · resets in
  1h 19m · seen 12m ago"), the week in a line and a row for each day, today first, with a bar of its tokens against the busiest
  day, at 390 px as wide. The words are pure functions (`usagewords.js`).
- No money. Dot keeps no price for a model or a task (the Claude plan has no price per call: it has a window that fills, which is
  what is shown; Claude Code's own `total_cost_usd` is read only by the repair night), so a figure of money would be invented. The
  page and the docs say so.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_usage.py` (fixture runs and steps with their times set: the days and their sums, zero days, the run of
eight days ago out, the run still at work counted to now, the newest window of each kind, the window of one run, an unreadable
store is an error), `tests/js/usagewords.test.js` (days, lines, bars, windows, "so far"), `tests/smoke/test_usage_view.py` (the
route and its bounds; Settings › Models in Chromium at 1280 and 390: the days, the window, no overflow, no price; the Cost card of a
scripted task at work, wide and at 390, and none of "so far" once it ended), `npm run check`.
Not done: a ceiling or a warning at a share of the window (a decision for the owner: what to cap); the tokens a task's helpers used
are in the helpers' own tasks, which the usage counts as tasks of their own days; a day's minutes are wall time, a task that waits
for hours counts them.
