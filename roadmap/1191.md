### 1191. A summary's writer has a cap, so a compaction never holds its run for minutes

**Status: done (2026-11-15).** On the owner's Mac a compaction's summary was asked for with no limit on what its writer
wrote: its word limit was only checked once the reply had come. In the week to 2026-11-15 the fast model
(`opencode_go:glm-5.3-flash`) took 35 to 54 s a summary, and once it ran on for 35,060 tokens in 400 s while its run
(`run_mwhra7hb_e6d63a`) waited.

- `agent/loop.py`: `summary_output_cap` gives the writer the words of a summary at its limit and slack and 4,000 tokens
  of thinking (7,000 tokens for 1,500 words; the longest summary seen that passed was 6,434). A writer on the Claude
  plan (`claude_code`) is not held to it: the `claude` command takes no such limit. A reply cut off there is
  asked for once more, whole and under the word limit and without reasoning (a writer that thought the cap away
  would think more with some allowed); its attempts stay within the three a compaction has, and the
  `summary_cut` log line names the cap, the words and the length of the writer's thinking (a writer that thought
  away its whole cap says so, as an empty reply's line does); a summary cut off at every try is written by the run's
  own model, as one too long is.
