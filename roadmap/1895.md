### 1895. DotNext: the owner's campaigns on the phone, native

**Status: done (2026-11-26).** Backlog item A1 (owner approved the backlog on 2026-11-25; PLAN §26 keeps "Campaigns:
list, state, pause/resume/stop" on the phone). It carries S2's unpushed work of 2026-11-25 (branch
`item-settings-native-s2`, item 1780, never merged) onto main.

What must hold: the phone does what the daemon takes and nothing else. `routes_campaigns.py` has no pause apart from
stop: `POST /api/campaigns/<id>/stop` is resumable (no session starts, the working one stops), `resume` lets a stopped
or achieved campaign go on, `achieved` closes it on the owner's word; anything else is a 409 (`campaign_control.py`).
So the page offers Stop while it is active, Resume once it is not, and Mark achieved until it is achieved
(`CampaignRecord.actions` in DotEngine, tested for each state); Stop and Mark achieved ask first with the web's words.
Delete stays on the Mac. The guard lets a phone's key read and write these routes (none of them is in `_WAYS_IN` and
none calls `refuse_from_a_phone`); no daemon file changes.

What was built: Settings › Agents › Campaigns (`ios/Next/Settings/CampaignPages.swift`): the list (title, state,
goal line, sessions and whether one works or when the next starts, the last progress note and how long ago), and a
campaign's page (state, sessions, approval mode, model, tokens, start; goal, targets and how they are verified; the
questions waiting for the owner; progress notes; the ten most recent sessions; the actions). The engine's models and
calls (`CampaignModels.swift`, `DaemonAPI+Campaigns.swift`), fixtures `settings-campaigns` and `settings-campaign`, a
seeded campaign in `e2e_daemon.py` that `SettingsLiveUITests` stops and resumes and `e2e_settings.sh` reads back.
Settings' search finds it in English and Turkish. Placement: the bot grouping of PLAN §27 (#1121) is not merged, so
the row sits under Settings › Agents; it moves to Dot's profile with #1121.

Checked: `swift test` (CampaignTests), `SettingsUITests` (stop asks, then Resume; AX3: the actions reachable, 44 pt),
the String Catalog check, shots light and dark, tr and en, L and AX3 on iPhone 17 Pro Max
(`shots-settings/A1/` of the owner's workspace).
