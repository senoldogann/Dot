**Status: done (2026-11-26).**

schedules'; the same reason repeats: Fiverr's login (the vault's password is wrong, the reset mail is in an inbox Dot
cannot read, runs `run_mwudx5te_0aef10`, `run_mwux7imh_d6a963`, `run_mwvtcsq4_c394e0`, `run_mwwcnapc_b9aeba`) and
TikTok's (`run_mwvv6xbm_abb97b`). Each run spent about 8.8 M tokens at the same wall and ended failed, so the schedule
neared `stop_after_failures` for something the owner has to do.

Measured on the owner's store: of the 12 runs of that week that ended on "the goal cannot be reached from here" (blocked)
9 had said, in their own finish, what they need from the owner (`needs_from_user`, 198 to 516 characters each: "Fiverr'e
bir kez giriş yap ..."), among them 4 of the 5 schedules' (the fifth, `run_mwux7imh_d6a963`, said nothing and still fails). That is the sign used, not the words of
the check's reason: the goal check's verdict model is in `agent/review.py`, which waits on the owner, and a reason read
for words would be one more spelling to chase.

- `agent/loop.py: _owner_blocked_end` (called after the goal check, also at a run's limit): for a schedule's firing
  (`_is_firing`, resumed ones too) whose verdict is `blocked` and whose finish carried `needs_from_user`, or that began
  under the note below, the end is **done** (`owner_wait_end`), the check's words under the answer start with
  `OWNER_WAIT_NOTE` (`store/endings.py`), and the need stays in the run's `needs`, so the owner is asked once, by the
  end's existing notice (`tells`: a run with `needs` is told at `problems`, and at `always`). The schedule's row counts
  a done, not a failure. Any other kind of run fails as before.
- `scheduler/owner_wait.py: owner_wait_of` reads the wait from the schedule's runs, by time: the newest run that began in the
  last seven days, ended done and whose result carries that mark (one row, `Store.schedule_run_marked`, however often the
  schedule fired since), unless a run of the owner's (the app, `dotx` or a channel) came after it in its chat. Seven days
  after it the part is tried again (the owner may have cleared it themselves). Nothing is stored for it.
- `Scheduler._with_owner_wait` puts in front of the digest of a firing (not of a by-hand one, nor of a retry) the note of
  `scheduler/history.py: owner_wait_block`: what the run asked, quoted with `<` escaped, that this part is on hold, to do
  the rest, and when nothing is left to end at once with one line, `needs_from_user` empty and `nothing_new`. The
  waiting run is among those whose taint the firing starts with.

Freedom contract: no new ask, in any mode. The owner is told by the end's notice, as they are of any run's `needs`; no
question goes through the approval broker, and no gate decision changes (`tests/unit/test_freedom_contract.py` passes).
