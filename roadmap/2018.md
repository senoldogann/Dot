### 2018. First words in seconds: Dot says it is on it the moment a task starts, then what it looks at first

**Status: done (2026-11-27).** The owner's order of 2026-11-27 (FR1), from Grok Bot's habits: it answers within the
first seconds ("Looking. First I check X") and keeps the chat open while the work goes on.

**Where the seconds went (measured before).** The owner's store, read-only, the top-level runs of the two weeks to
2026-11-27, from their events: the message (`run_queued`), the start (`run_started`), the start's reads (`memories`, the
notes' recall with its embedding, the skills, the model's profile, read at once since item 1918), the first step's model
call (`model_call` less its `seconds`; on Claude Code the first `assistant` less its `seconds`), its first token, and
the first words a client could show (the first step's words when it had some, else the first `assistant` with text or
the first `narration`). Seconds:

- The queue is not where the time goes (a chat's run starts at once).
- The start's reads are 2 to 4 s at p50 and 10 to 30 s at p95 before any model is asked.
- The first step's own first token is p50 4 s, p95 33 s on the owner's OpenCode model; Claude Code's first step thinks
  13 s at p50 before its first call.
- The first step is a call with no words in 75 % of the OpenCode runs and in all Claude Code runs: a third of the
  owner's chats showed no words of Dot's before the answer.
- The fast model ([models] fast, the same DeepSeek flash) answers its side calls with a first token at p50 1.9 to 2.6 s
  and p95 6 to 8.5 s (`extract`, `recap`, `lessons`, n 51 to 79): a line of any model, even started in parallel with the
  start, cannot be the first words at p95 under 3 s.

**What must hold.** First visible words at p95 under 3 s after the message, on the phone and on the web; never a second
voice that contradicts the run (GF1's rules: words only at a result, a decision or a block; "done" only after the check;
the steps hidden with Show steps off); no new ask or wait in any mode (the freedom contract); no token added to the
run's own steps.

**Decision (justified).** The words come from the daemon at the run's start, not from the run's model: two parts in one
`first_words` event the clients draw as one of Dot's short messages, in every way of showing the work.
- At once, as the run starts, `{"text": ""}`, which each client says in its own language (*Looking into it…*,
  *Bakıyorum…*). It waits on nothing, so it is the measured p95.
- Then, started in parallel with the start's reads and the first step, a line of the fast model written from the owner's
  message alone ("Looking at your calendar first."), `{"text": ..., "model": ...}`, which takes the place of the first.
  It is told only while the run has said nothing (no words streamed or written, no call, no line of a call, no question,
  no end); the moment the run speaks, the call is cancelled. So the line is never a second voice beside the run's words,
  and it says what Dot looks at, never a result.
- Not taken: telling the run's model to say a line before its first call. It cannot be the first words (its first token
  is p95 33 s, and the start's reads come before it), it costs a step's tokens on every run, and item 1976 measured that
  the models rarely write words before calls even when asked by their harness.
- Not taken here: the start's reads. With the words said at once they no longer hold the first words; they still hold
  the first step, and item 2004, built the same day, cut them where they were slow (the skills read once, not at each
  start; the notes' embedding bounded in time).

**Built.**
- `agent/first_words.py`: `tells_first_words` (a run of the owner's chat, `source` ui and no parent, with `[agent]
  first_words`, on by default), `has_spoken` (pure), `first_line` (the answer's first line, unquoted, a question or more
  than 160 characters dropped, the secrets Dot holds masked), and the adapter `tell_first_words`, started by
  `RunManager._drive` beside the run as it starts and cancelled with it: it emits the words said at once, listens to the
  run's own events, and races the fast model (12 s at most, effort off, 600 output tokens, the owner's message in a
  fence with a nonce) against the run's first speech. A model that fails or times out is logged (`first_words_failed`),
  and the words said at once stay. The call is recorded as a `model_call` of purpose `first_words`.
- Web: `timeline.js` and `history.js` draw a `first_words` as one of Dot's short messages (`.say-first`), the line in
  the place of the words said at once, in every choice of Work in a chat, live and in a chat's history; `workstatus.js`
  `firstWordsPhrase`; `work.firstWords` in both catalogues.
- Phone (DotNext): `TranscriptItem.firstWords` (DotEngine), one row a run right under the owner's message, shown in
  every tool mode and with Show steps off, never in a finished run's fold; `firstWordsText` and the String Catalog's
  *Looking into it…* / *Bakıyorum…*; the chat fixtures carry one.
- Several tasks in one chat (item 1984): each task of a chat, a lane's too, is a run of the owner's chat, so each has
  its own first words; the pages draw lanes from slice 3 of item 1982 on.
- `eval_config` turns the first words off, as it does the busy judge: the script would answer the fast model's call with
  a reply meant for the run.
- Tests: a run says no first words in a test unless the test is marked `first_words` (`tests/conftest.py`). The whole
  smoke run found 23 tests whose scripted model lost a reply to the fast model's call, and tests on the defaults whose
  call would have gone to the fast model they name, a real one: a guard for every test rather than a line in each test's
  configuration.

**Measured after.**
- A throwaway runtime (mktemp DOT dirs, the scripted model, no network), 30 runs started as the owner's chat, the fast
  model held past the run: from the message to the first words p50 / p95 / p99 0.002 / 0.004 / 0.004 s.
- Replayed on the owner's 66 chat runs of the two weeks: the first words come at the queue's wait (p95 0.0 to 0.2 s)
  plus that, so p95 under 0.3 s where it was 208 s on OpenCode and unmeasurable on Claude Code (most runs said no word
  before their answer).
- The line of the fast model, measured by hand on the owner's fast model (DeepSeek flash on OpenCode Go, a throwaway
  home, twelve requests in Turkish and English): from the call to the line p50 2.3 s, p95 3.1 s, twelve lines of twelve
  ("Önce takvimine bakıyorum.", "Checking the CI build logs first."); 220 tokens in and 80 out at p50, its thinking with
  effort off counted. At 120 output tokens a third of the answers were empty or cut mid-word ("Ö"), so the call has 600
  and a reply that did not end by itself is no line. The line comes before the first step's words in most runs (first
  step p50 12 to 13 s), and is dropped in the others. Nothing is added to the run's own steps.
