### 1825. The drafts that wait for the owner, in one list (`GET /api/drafts`)

**Status: done (2026-11-25).** For DotNext's Activity tab (item 1826, P1-gaps §8 "Drafts waiting list"), which lists
the drafts a run wrote and that wait for the owner as "Ready". Before, the daemon served one draft by its id alone
(`GET /api/drafts/{id}`): a client learned of a draft from a run's `draft_created` event or the count of "While you
were away", and no route listed them.

What must hold: the list holds the drafts no one sent or discarded yet and those whose last send failed (both wait for
the owner's Send), never one that was sent, discarded or is being sent; the newest first; `limit` 1-200; a phone's key
reads it as the Mac's token does (a draft is already readable from a phone by its id).

Built: `GET /api/drafts?limit=` in `server/routes_drafts.py`, from the store's `list_drafts` of `waiting` and `failed`.
Checked by `tests/smoke/test_drafts_waiting.py` (a served daemon, a paired phone's key; it fails with 404 without the
route).
