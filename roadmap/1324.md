### 1324. The look is plain and professional: true black and zinc greys, a calm blue, an 8 px scale and dense type

**Status: done (2026-11-22).** The owner's word of 2026-11-22: Dot's design is "completely like a toy"; make it plain and
professional like the developer app the owner showed on this Mac, light and dark, one design language on a phone
and a Mac. This is the foundation of four parallel redesign items: the tokens every page inherits.

Colours (`themes.css`, the neutral look): the dark page is true black and the raised surfaces (`--surface`,
`--sidebar`: the card, popover and composer's ground) are `#111111`, `--surface-2` and `--surface-3` the steps a hover
and a selected row take; light is zinc-25 for the page, zinc-50 for the sidebar and white for the raised surfaces.
Borders are white at 8% in dark and black at 9% in light. The text tiers are zinc (`#18181b`/`#3f3f46`/`#5f5f69` in
light, neutral-100/300/400 in dark). The accent is a calm blue (`#346bf1` = oklch 0.571 0.21 264 in dark, `#1b4ed8`
= 0.488 in light) with white on it; `--accent-strong` is the blue that reads as text. The ok, warn and bad colours are
emerald, amber and red. A user's message sits on the one surface step off the page (`--msg-me` = `--surface-3`: lighter than the
page in dark, a shade darker in light; the text's own ink on it) in every look instead of an ink bubble, and `--bubble`, which was the amber of Dot's face, is the
accent's tint (`--brand` keeps the amber on the face, and Dot's computer's backdrop, `--computer-backdrop`, now
stands on `--brand` instead of `--bubble`, so its frame stays warm in every look, with `--brand-ink`, the fixed
`#1c1c1c`, as the ink that reads on it: the page's own ink is light in dark and was 1.37:1 on the amber). `--edge` and
`--edge-strong`, which nothing draws any more (the rings are `--border` and `--border-strong`), are gone. The phone's own lighter dark greys are gone: a phone draws
the same greys as a Mac.

Shape and type (`app.css`): radii 6/8 for marks and controls, 12 for a card, 10 for a menu and a bubble, 14 for the
composer, 16 for a dialog and a sheet (they were 8/10/16/24 and 12, 18 and 22 for the composer and bubbles; a phone
was rounder still, now the same); the fully round shape is `--radius-pill`, and the rules that name it are the list of what takes it. The type is
a dense scale: `--text-3xs` (10 px) and `--text-2xs` (11 px) for the metadata beside xs 12, sm 13, ui 14, md 14
(was 15), lg 16 (was 17), xl 18 (was 20), the line heights a step tighter, the messages and the composer at 14 px on
a line of 1.55, the face `-apple-system` first. Weights stay 400, 500 and 600. A card's padding is 16 px (was 20),
motion is 120/160/240 ms on a calm ease (was a springy 0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), the shadows of dark are the hairline ring
(`--box-shadow` is a 1 px `--border` ring) and the composer keeps its soft lift. New tokens `--control-h` (32 px),
`--control-h-sm` (28), `--control-h-lg` (36) and `--touch-target` (the control's height; 36 px on a phone, whose icon
buttons keep the ring that makes the 44 a finger hits) are what item 1325's controls take.

Tests: `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py` holds every contrast rule as it was; two things had to change with the look: the
neutral look's `--on-bubble` was written into the test (`#1c1c1c` on the amber) and is now measured as the other looks'
are (the text on the accent's tint), a new test measures `--brand-ink` on every stop of the warm backdrop (4.5:1) and that the
rules sitting on it name it, and the test that held the phone's own greys now holds that the neutral look has
no block for a touch screen. The muted and faint inks of light are a step darker than zinc-500 (`#5f5f69`,
`#66666f`), because Dot measures them at 4.5:1 on its third surface too.

Not covered: the other looks (Paper, Clean, Rose, Grove...) keep their palettes and take the new shape, type and
spacing tokens; the sidebar, the composer, the run page and the settings pages, which the other three items of this
batch redraw on these tokens.

Checked: `tests/unit/test_css_tokens.py`, the unit tests of the sheets and the pages, `tests/smoke/test_themes.py`,
`test_controls.py` and `test_own_themes.py`; screenshots in `/tmp/ui-foundation/before` and `/tmp/ui-foundation/after`.
