### 1641. Dot for iPhone: one neutral graphite family, no accent hue

**Status: done (2026-11-24).** The owner's order of 2026-11-24, 11:20 (item 1640): "watch these blues". The app's palette is now
the greys of the web app's Graphite theme (`static/js/appearance/palettes-more.js`, item 870), so that the phone and the web are one
product, and there is no accent hue.

What changed:
- `Shared/DotPalette.swift` has twelve roles in two looks. Dark: the ground `#121212`, a surface one step above `#1F1F1F`, a raised
  step `#2C2C2E`, the ink `#F5F5F5`, `muted` `#B0B0B0`, `faint` `#949494`. Light: a light grey ground `#F2F2F2` with white cards
  above it, raised `#E9E9EB`, the ink `#0D0D0D`, `#4A4A4A`, `#666666` (the web's greys one step across, so that a card is never
  darker than its ground). The primary action is the ink as a fill with the ground as its label (`action`, `onAction`). Colour
  is only amber for what waits on the owner (`warn`, and the one amber fill `warnSurface`) and a muted red for what failed (`bad`).
- Gone: `accent`, `accentFill`, `accentFillTop`, `onAccent`, `link`, `good`, `Theme.accentGradient` and the blue `AccentColor`. A name
  that is gone cannot come back by accident: every place that read one of them had to choose, and was found by the compiler.
  `AccentColor` (the system tint of every control that sets none: a switch, a cursor, an alert's button) is the ink in both looks,
  and `dotSheet()` and `RootView` tint with the ink, so no sheet or stack falls back to the system's blue or purple. A link in a
  message is the ink with an underline. A switch that is on has the quiet tier's grey as its track (`Theme.toggleOn`), so that its
  white knob reads on it.
- No gradients, shadows or glow: the glow of the accent under every screen (`DotBackground`) and the mesh of blues behind the first-run
  screens and the empty states (`DotMesh`) are gone, so are the shadow of Dot's mark and the amber ring's glow, and the ring that
  turns is a short plain arc in the ink. Dot's own mark (`DotGlyph`, the launch screen, the Live Activity's copy) is a flat graphite
  disc with two eyes in the ink; the launch screen's mark has a light and a dark image on the app's ground.
- The Live Activity (`DotLiveActivity`) and the share extension draw with the same palette (`DotShare` now builds
  `DotPalette.swift` too): "Working" and the working mark are the ink, "Done" is muted, "Waiting" amber, "Failed" the muted red, the
  keyline a grey; the share sheet's buttons are the ink and its error the app's muted red.
- Measured in `DotPaletteTests`: words 4.5:1 and glyphs 3:1 for `text`, `muted`, `faint`, `warn` and `bad` on the ground, the surface and
  the raised step in both looks; the action's label on its fill; the text and the amber ink on the amber fill; white on the swipe
  actions' fills; the family neutral (the channels of every grey within 3/255) with only the amber and the red warm; a card never
  darker than its ground; the primary action is the ink; `Theme` follows the appearance; the `AccentColor` asset equals the ink.

Not changed: the home-screen icon (`AppIcon`) is still the blue dot of item 1382. It is the owner's to redraw (a graphite one is
`scripts/make_icon.swift` with other colours); it is named in the pull request.
