### 771. Dot may trade the whole market, every product that trades enough a day

**Status: done (2026-10-28).** **Asked** by the owner, after Dot's own trading run told them it could trade only
`BTC-EUR`, `ETH-EUR` and `SOL-EUR`: "If the agent does not chase opportunities across the whole market, what is the
point?"

**The change.** `[trading] products` may hold `*` (`config.ANY_PRODUCT`): every product priced in `quote` that Coinbase
trades now and that traded at least `[trading] min_volume` of it over the last day (1,000,000 by default), as Coinbase's
product sums it (`approximate_quote_24h_volume`, now `ProductRules.quote_volume_24h`). `limits.not_allowed` judges it
with the order's rules, read when the order is judged, in every mode; a product named in the list stays allowed whatever
it trades. `CoinbaseMarket.whole_market` reads Coinbase's list of spot products in one request (about a megabyte, 0.3
seconds, measured) and keeps it 30 seconds; the new tool `trading_scan` ranks what a run may trade by the last day's
move, by volume, or by where the price stands in the day's range, and `trading_market` reads any product priced in the
quote (with `*` alone, its overview is the eight busiest). Widening from a phone stays refused: `*` is another product,
and a lower `min_volume` is "allow products that trade less" (`routes_trading.widens`), even while `*` is not allowed,
as the Mac's `*` would take the lower floor up unseen (the pre-push review). Settings › Trading takes `*` in the
products field and says its chip in words; the prompt's Trading section names `trading_scan`.

Measured on 2026-10-28: in `EUR` Coinbase trades 33 products, 6 of them past a million a day; in `USDC` 390, 51 past a
million and 112 past a quarter of one; a `USDC` product is Coinbase's `USD` book under another name. The owner's paper
account moves to `USDC` with `*`.

Found on the way: the config editor read a number with decimals as its integer part, so the decimals stayed behind the
value written over them, and any `[trading]` amount changed a second time from Settings › Trading made `config.toml`
no TOML (refused, so the file was kept, and the change too); `config_edit._key_value` reads such a number whole now.
And the pre-push review of item 765: a 404 at the day's first look took a holding for one Coinbase has no product of,
so the day started without its worth; a 404 is now checked against Coinbase's list of what it trades, and without the
list none is taken at its word. `changes/765.md` said buys were held while any price could not be read; they are held
until the day's first whole look.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_trading.py`: with `*`, a buy of a product past `min_volume` placed and one under it, one
of another quote and one whose volume is unknown refused; the scan ranking by each sort and leaving out what may not be
traded; the overview of `*` taking the busiest; a 404 of a listed product, or with no list, held as a price not read
until a whole look. `tests/unit/test_config_edit.py`: amounts with decimals and with an exponent changed twice, the
comment after them kept. `tests/smoke/test_trading_settings.py`: a phone refused `*` and a lower `min_volume`, with `*`
allowed or not, the Mac let through. `tests/smoke/test_trading_settings_page.py`: `*` allowed on the page, said in
words, saved, and the page inside a phone's screen with it. `tests/js/tradingwords.test.js`: `*` parsed and its words.
Not measured: a run trading the whole market for a day.
