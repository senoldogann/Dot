### 744. A desktop screenshot of a region that leaves the screen is cut to the screen

**Status: done (2026-10-27).** **Measured:** in the owner's runs of the last 30 days, 3 of 138 `desktop_screenshot`
calls failed with "the region leaves the screen; use coordinates from a full screenshot | region (0, 0) 1600x40, screen
1366x888.299": a model asking for a strip across the top of a screen it believed wider than it is.

**The change.** `shots.clip_region` cuts the region (model units) to the screen (the display's size in units), the
right and bottom edges rounded down so that they stay on it. `take_region` looks at the cut region (its left, top, zoom
and the text lines are the cut region's, so the coordinates stay true), and `ScreenshotView.clipped` says what was cut:
"The region (0, 0) 1600x40 left the screen (1366x888.299); it was cut to (0, 0) 1366x40", which the caption of the
screenshot carries. A region with nothing on the screen, or a sliver of less than the minimum 16 units on a side, still
fails with the same pointer to a full screenshot.

**Checked.** `tests/unit/test_desktop_screenshots.py` (no screen is captured: the display, the windows and the picture
are replaced): the clipping arithmetic for regions past each edge, past two, left of the screen, whole on it and with
nothing on it; the same on a display drawn smaller (scale 0.5); the failures; and a `take_region` whose view maps its
pixels from the cut region and whose caption says so. Each fails on the code before.
