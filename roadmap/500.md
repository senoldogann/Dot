### 500. A YOLO run warns of nothing in the interface

**Status: done (2026-10-21).** The owner's decision of 2026-10-21: YOLO has unlimited authority, and the interface must
not warn about it under a command. Before, the mode showed in seven places: a "YOLO, not asked" line under each step
the run took without asking, a "YOLO, not asked" line in its tool row's Why it ran, a note in the composer's Options
menu and a warning tint on the Options pill, and a warning badge on a campaign's approvals and on a schedule's.

**As built.** What a YOLO run did stays in its records and its events: the `approval_auto` event, `tool_result.decision`
and the AUTO_LINES setting are untouched, and nothing under `src/dotagent` but the web interface changed.
- `js/why.js`: `showsWhy()` answers false for a decision `answered_by` yolo, so a step's row has no Why it ran; the
  `case "yolo"` branch of `sentence()` is gone.
- `js/timeline.js`: the `approval_auto` handler draws nothing when `decided_by` is yolo. A denied line stays, and the
  review model's lines follow Settings › General › Chat › Show what ran without asking.
- `css/app.css`: the `.auto-decision.yolo` rule is gone.
- `js/runmodes.js`: `YOLO_NOTE` and the `note warn` element of the approvals picker are gone, and so its caller in
  `js/choices.js`; the mode's hint in the menu stays.
- `js/choices.js`: the Options pill wears no warn tint for yolo.
- `js/views/campaigns.js` and `js/views/schedules.js`: a yolo campaign's or schedule's approvals badge is a plain chip,
  as the others are.
- `js/composer.js` and `css/app.css`: `composerPill`'s `setTone` and the pill's warn rules are gone, as the YOLO pill was
  their only caller. The Options menu still shows the mode chosen in the warning ink, which warns before a run is started
  rather than about what it did.
- Settings › General's Chat card and the comment of AUTO_LINES say what the switch now does: the review model's lines.
- Docs: `autonomy.md` describes the pill and the timeline as they are now and says a yolo step's row gives no reason;
  `daemon.md` no longer says the pill wears the warning ink.

**Checked.** `npm run check` green (43 tests). `tests/js/why.test.js` drops the yolo sentence and keeps a reviewed step
that shows its line, adding a yolo step that shows none. `tests/smoke/test_yolo_line.py` is rewritten: a yolo step draws
no line and no line of the page says the mode, the review model's two lines read the reason once, and the switch takes
them out and in; `tests/smoke/test_composer.py` sees YOLO chosen with no tint and no note in the menu, and
`tests/smoke/test_late_helper.py` and `tests/smoke/test_chat_calm.py` keep their subject with a reviewed line instead of
a yolo one.
