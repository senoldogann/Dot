### 567. A reply that nearly repeats the answer shows once

**Status: done (2026-10-23).** The owner: "on the phone, the desktop and the web the agent writes the same things two or
three times in the chat". Read in their runs: a task run that answers in a plain reply is told to call finish (item
460) and finishes with the reply again, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash rewords its first line on the way (run of 2026-10-23
03:49: "Kusurları düzeltecek kod ajanı için, olduğu gibi kopyalanacak prompt:" became "Kusurları düzeltecek kod ajanına
olduğu gibi yapıştırabileceğin prompt:", the 5 000 characters after it the same). The chat dropped a reply only when it
was the answer word for word, so the prompt showed twice; a reply given twice before the finish showed three times.

**As built.**
- `dom.js`: `repeatsAnswer(reply, answer)`, whether a reply says nothing the answer does not, so that the answer alone
  may stand for it: the same words whatever their spacing and marks of markdown, or the same lines after a first line
  the answer rewords (at least half the words of the reply's first line in the answer's, and the line neither adds a
  word to the answer's nor has a number of its own), those lines twenty words and more. A reply with a line the answer lacks, more on one of its lines, or a first line of its own stays, however much
  of it the answer holds; a short line and a note that is part of the answer match only word for word.
- `timeline.js` (a run's page, live and replayed) and `history.js` (an earlier turn of a chat) drop a reply that
  `repeatsAnswer` finds in the answer, where they compared the exact words before.
- `docs/daemon.md` says it.

**Tests.** `tests/js/dom.test.js`: the same words with other spacing and marks, the real case of a reworded first line,
and what stays (a short line, a note of the first tenth of the answer, a text that says something else, and a reply
with one sentence more than the answer: on a line of its own, at the end of a line, in its first line, and after a
thousand lines the answer holds). `tests/smoke/test_answer_once.py`: a reply and a finish whose first line is reworded
show the words once, in the answer (it fails without the change); a reply with a line the finish leaves out stays,
that line with it.

Not built: the loop itself still asks the model to finish after a plain reply (item 460), which costs a step and lets it
reword; letting a task run end on its plain reply is a change of the loop and its evals, for another item.
