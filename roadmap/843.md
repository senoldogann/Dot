under it, further left, "••• Looking through the results…": "sohbette neden botun ikonu görünmüyor ve thinking ile

Measured in a chat at work (a scripted run that says a line and runs a command): the interim message's box was 68 px
for a bubble of 40, its foot of reactions holding a line of 32 px for a button that shows only under the pointer; the
line of the work stood 16 px under that, 44 px from the bubble, and began 36 px left of it, in the place a face would
take, with no face; and nothing in the chat wore Dot's face until the answer came.

  Dot's), as a messaging app shows someone typing; it goes with the dots once the run is over.

  start; the line stands 8 px under the message before it.
